## Statement

EC STING activation drives tumor vascular normalization and upregulates T/NK adhesion molecules (ICAM, VCAM, SELL).

## Evidence summary

Multiple murine studies showing STING activation in ECs promotes vessel integrity, upregulates ICAM/VCAM/SELL, secretes CXCL10/CCL5. Host EC STING knockout dramatically reduces CD8 T cell infiltration. STING-JAK1 direct binding amplifies pro-inflammatory programme.

## Conditions and scope

Tumor endothelial cells; partially IFN-dependent. Reverses EC anergy that excludes effector cells from cold tumors.
