## Statement

Itaconate and its derivative Octyl itaconate inhibit G6PD catalytic activity by reducing Vmax with minimal Km change, and do so through non-covalent pocket binding rather than covalent cysteine modification.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1467–1468) — mechanistic/pharmacological evidence.
  - Quote: "Octyl Ita significantly reduced G6PD activity in A549 cells without affecting protein abundance ... reduced G6PD Vmax (1.52 → 1.19 U) with a minimal change in Km (188 → 178 μM)."
  - "neither Octyl Ita nor itaconate covalently modified G6PD ... Octyl Ita inhibits G6PD through non-covalent pocket binding."

## Conditions and scope

Cell homogenate + recombinant enzyme; no covalent adduct by ITalk chemoproteomics — distinct from classic itaconate KEAP1/GAPDH covalent chemistry.
