## Statement

Competitive Ccr2-/-:Ccr5-/- (80:20) BM chimeras, lacking CCR5 in monocyte-derived cells while preserving normal DC representation, had 6.8-fold (melanoma) and 9.3-fold (adenocarcinoma) reductions in lung tumor burden versus Ccr2-/-:WT controls.

## Evidence summary

Surface metastasis / tumor quantification (Fig. 5c,d; P<0.0001). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 melanoma and KPAR1.3 adenocarcinoma.
