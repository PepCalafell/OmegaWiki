## Statement

An effector binds the target–host complex to form a strained ternary complex (THE) from which the target dissociates quickly; this facilitated pathway is faster than spontaneous dissociation via mutually exclusive competition.

## Evidence summary

"In the full facilitated dissociation process, as the concentration of added effector increases, the rate constant of facilitated target dissociation approaches koff,T:HE ... strongly suggesting that the ternary complex is an intermediate in the facilitated dissociation process" (p.3). Crystal structures of the AS1 ternary complex (Kd,T:HE = 200 nM) directly capture the intermediate.

## Conditions and scope

Designed AS1 host with peptide or 3hb effector; kinetics by SPR.

## Counter-evidence

The ternary complex is only transiently populated at working concentrations; it becomes dominant only at high concentration.
