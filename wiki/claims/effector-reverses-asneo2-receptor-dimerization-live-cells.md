## Statement

In live-cell plasma membranes, ASNeo2 efficiently dimerizes IL-2Rβ and γc, and adding the effector rapidly and completely reverses this receptor dimerization — even at a high excess of γc — with the effector-induced intramolecular conformational change directly observable by single-molecule FRET.

## Evidence summary

"Dual-colour co-tracking and mobility analyses confirmed that ASNeo2 efficiently dimerizes IL-2Rβ and γc and that adding the effector reverses this association rapidly and completely, even at a high excess of γc" (p.6); kapp ≈ 0.22 s⁻¹. Double-labelled ASNeo2 smFRET captured the effector-induced conformational change at the membrane.

## Conditions and scope

Live-cell single-molecule microscopy; engineered labelled IL-2Rβ and γc.

## Counter-evidence

Observed in a labelled-receptor system; native receptor stoichiometry/context may differ.
