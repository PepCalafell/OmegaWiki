## Statement
The promoters of regulators in the human macrophage activation TR network are uniformly accessible across all activation states, independent of which stimulus is applied.

## Evidence summary
>92% Pa across activation states for network TRs (and across all 485 TRs); enhancer marks uniform. Open chromatin at these loci is pre-set rather than signal-induced.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages; central finding of the paper.

## Counter-evidence
TRs outside the network / not expressed in any condition show much lower accessibility (see [[claims/open-chromatin-tr-loci-specific-expressed]]).

## Open questions
