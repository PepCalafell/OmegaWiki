## Statement

Adoptive transfer of Cas9+ P14 cells with `Zscan20` KO (TEXterm single-state TF) produces stronger B16-GP33 melanoma control and stronger effector marker (CX3CR1, GZMB) and cytokine expression than the multi-state TF `Hic1` KO, despite both KOs comparably suppressing TIM3+/TEXterm differentiation.
