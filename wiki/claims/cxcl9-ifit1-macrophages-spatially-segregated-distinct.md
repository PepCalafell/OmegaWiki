## Statement

Analysis of a human-tumor scATAC-seq dataset revealed distinctive chromatin accessibility at the CXCL9 and IFIT1 loci in different macrophage populations, and spatial profiling showed CXCL9+ and IFIT1+ macrophages were spatially segregated across multiple tumor samples.

## Evidence summary

Orthogonal scATAC-seq and spatial evidence. (p.4) Quote: "CXCL9⁺ and IFIT1⁺ macrophages were spatially segregated across multiple tumor samples".

## Conditions and scope
