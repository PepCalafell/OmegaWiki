## Statement

The seven CKIs that upregulated Ifnb1 also induced transposable elements — notably ERVs/LTRs — proposing drug-induced viral mimicry (dsRNA-driven interferon induction) as an off-target route to Ifnb1 upregulation.

## Evidence summary

RNA-seq TE analysis showed broad TE/ERV induction by all seven inhibitors, paralleling Trametinib-induced retroelement activation in pancreatic cancer (Cortesi et al. 2024). Links [[foundations/endogenous-retrovirus-viral-mimicry]], [[concepts/cki-induced-retroelement-activation-drives-interferon]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; correlative RNA-seq evidence only.

## Counter-evidence

The study explicitly does not identify the specific repeat elements or molecular mediators responsible; causality is not established.
