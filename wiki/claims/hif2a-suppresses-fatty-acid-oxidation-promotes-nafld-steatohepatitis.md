## Statement

In hepatocytes, HIF-2α is the dominant HIF subunit governing lipid metabolism. It transcriptionally suppresses fatty acid oxidation enzymes and induces lipogenic genes (and lipid droplet formation), promoting steatosis. Chronic HIF-2α activation (e.g., constitutive transgenic models) progresses to steatohepatitis with fibrogenesis, partly through upregulation of histidine-rich glycoprotein (HRGP). HIF-2α knockdown reverses hypoxia-induced lipid dysregulation in HCC cell lines.

## Evidence summary

Rankin et al. (constitutive activation model), Morello et al. (human NAFLD, HRGP), Qu et al., integrated by Luo et al. 2022.

## Conditions and scope

- Chronic hypoxia/HIF-2α stabilization in hepatocytes
- NAFLD/NASH/MASH disease spectrum
- Distinct from acute hepatic HIF-2α benefit in glucose homeostasis

## Counter-evidence

Acute hepatic HIF-2α elevation improves glucose tolerance (Taniguchi/Wei) — chronicity and dose matter.
