## Statement

The dominant motif-mediated mode of commensal effector–host binding is bacterial
C-terminal PDZ-binding motifs engaging human PDZ domains, quantitatively validated.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.447–448, Fig.3g): 23 PDZ–PBM
interactions; holdup assay validated 16/23 (70%) Y2H pairs; 3 needed tandem PDZ domains.
Different effectors often target different PDZ domains of multi-PDZ proteins
(specificity/functional specialization).

## Conditions and scope

Tests defined domain/peptide fragments; tandem-domain requirements can cause misses.

## Counter-evidence

7/23 pairs not confirmed by holdup (possibly untested tandem combinations).

## Linked ideas

Defines [[slim-pdz-effector-host-interface]]. Uses [[holdup-assay]], [[pdz-domain]].

## Open questions

Functional consequences of PDZ scaffold targeting (adhesion, trafficking, immune
signalling).
