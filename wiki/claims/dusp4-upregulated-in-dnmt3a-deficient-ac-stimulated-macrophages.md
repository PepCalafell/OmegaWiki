## Statement

DNMT3A normally represses Dusp4 expression in AC-stimulated macrophages; loss of DNMT3A elevates Dusp4 mRNA. Dusp1 is not similarly affected.

## Evidence summary

Differential mRNA assays (Fig. 4i-j) and ERK-pathway inhibitor controls (ED Fig. 3h) confirm specificity. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM, mRNA readouts at 1-h chase.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
