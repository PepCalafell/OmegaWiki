## Statement

Phagocytosed apoptotic-cell methionine is sufficient as the SAM substrate for efferocytosis-induced gene induction; extracellular methionine is not required.

## Evidence summary

Methionine-free media still permits AC-induced response; MAT2A inhibitor still blocks; isotope tracing in methionine-free media confirms AC-methionine origin. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only; short time scale (45 min + 1-6 h).

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
