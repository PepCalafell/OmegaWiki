## Statement

Metformin reprograms the immune TME by suppressing Treg activity and improving Teff function, synergising with ICIs in preclinical models and retrospective NSCLC cohorts.

## Evidence summary

Preclinical mouse studies and retrospective patient analyses.

## Conditions and scope

Obese / diabetic NSCLC patients on PD-(L)1 blockade.
