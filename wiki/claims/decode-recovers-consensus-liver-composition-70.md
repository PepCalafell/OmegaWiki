## Statement

DECODE's deconvolution of healthy (chow-diet) mouse liver across multiple omics recovers the established consensus composition — hepatocytes ~70%, Kupffer cells ~15% — supporting the reliability of its cross-omics estimates.

## Evidence summary

Chow-group recovery showed strong cross-omics consistency aligning with consensus (~70% hepatocytes, ~15% Kupffer; Supplementary Fig. 19a), used as an external sanity check on accuracy.

## Conditions and scope

Validation in the healthy baseline group; serves as a positive control for the cohort analysis.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
