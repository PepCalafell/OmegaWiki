## Statement

GM-CSF and lactic acid together cooperatively induce interferon-stimulated genes (e.g. Ifi44, Ifi209, Mx1) in macrophages, contributing to the ISG+ TAM signature.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2065) — mechanistic evidence. RNA-seq combinatorial analysis; ISG+ TAMs display mixed GM-CSF and lactate/PGE2 signatures.
  - Quote: "We observed that GM-CSF and lactic acid cooperatively induced many ISGs, such as Ifi44, Ifi209, and Mx1"

## Conditions and scope

In vitro BMDM; interaction inferred from combinatorial DEGs.
