## Statement

Butyrate (and to a lesser extent propionate) inhibits HDACs and acetylates NF-κB p65 to suppress pro-inflammatory gene programs in M1 macrophages while engaging Akt/mTOR/FoxO3a to drive M2 polarization and OXPHOS. Acetate behaves differently — it can abrogate M2 in airway asthma models.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized from primary literature reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]].

## Conditions and scope

- LPS-stimulated macrophages; SCFAs at physiological gut concentrations; NASH, intestinal homeostasis, muscle atrophy models.

## Counter-evidence

- Acetate in asthma models abrogates M2 polarization, indicating non-uniform SCFA-macrophage effects.
