## Statement

A TAM cluster (12_MBMMac) is highly enriched in melanoma brain metastases but not in primary GBM (Propeller q=1.03e-9), and uniquely upregulates LRMDA — a melanocyte differentiation factor — suggesting bidirectional TAM-tumour-cell signalling shapes macrophage identity at the brain metastatic niche.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/using-pan-cancer-atlas-investigate-tumour]] (Coulton et al., *Nat Commun* 2024, Fig. 3c-d).

## Conditions and scope

Comparison limited to melanoma brain mets vs primary cutaneous melanoma vs primary GBM; no perturbation data.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
