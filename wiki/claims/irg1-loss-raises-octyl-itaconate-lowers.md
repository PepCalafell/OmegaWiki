## Statement

The IRG1/itaconate–G6PD axis operates in vivo: IRG1-deficient lung tumors show elevated in situ G6PD activity, whereas Octyl itaconate treatment lowers in situ tumor G6PD activity.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1471) — mechanistic/correlational (in vivo) evidence.
  - Quote: "IRG1-deficient mice showed significantly elevated in situ G6PD activity compared with WT controls ... Octyl Ita treatment decreased G6PD activity in orthotopically implanted lung tumors relative to placebo-treated mice."

## Conditions and scope

In situ enzymatic activity staining; multiple lung tumor models, consistent with cultured-cell findings.
