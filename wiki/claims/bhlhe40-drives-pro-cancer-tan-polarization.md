## Statement

BHLHE40 is identified by single-cell transcriptome sequencing as a key regulator of pro-cancer, immunosuppressive TAN-1 neutrophil polarization in pancreatic cancer. Hypoxia further stabilizes neutrophil HIF1α, drives NET formation, and promotes CTC metastasis.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023). Mechanism flagged as incomplete by reviewers.

## Conditions and scope

Pancreatic cancer scRNA-seq evidence; generalization unclear.

## Counter-evidence

Some neutrophils have antitumor activity under hypoxia (PMN-mediated endometrial adenocarcinoma killing).
