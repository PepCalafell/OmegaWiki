## Statement

Expression of the canonical DNA-methylation machinery — Dnmt1 maintenance methyltransferase, Dnmt3a de novo methyltransferase, MeCP2 reader, and Tet1 eraser — is elevated in E13 vs E11 mouse embryonic brain, consistent with a biochemically active mid-gestation methylation-remodelling state.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 3h. Spatial mapping of log-normalised expression of [[foundations/dnmt1-maintenance-methyltransferase]], [[foundations/dnmt3a-de-novo-dna-methyltransferase]], [[foundations/mecp2-methyl-cpg-binding-protein]] and Tet1.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse embryo; mRNA level only; protein and activity not measured.

## Counter-evidence

Increased mRNA does not necessarily imply increased enzymatic activity.
