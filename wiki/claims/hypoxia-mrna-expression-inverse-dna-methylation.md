## Statement

Within hypoxia score-high tumours, genes that are differentially expressed tend to show the opposite-direction change in promoter DNA methylation, consistent with hypermethylation-driven silencing and hypomethylation-driven overexpression. This couples the hypoxia-associated transcriptional program to the epigenetic layer.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — paired mRNA/methylation comparison (Supplementary Fig. 6b,c).
- Mechanistically consistent with hypoxia reducing TET activity and causing DNA hypermethylation (Thienpont et al. 2016, *Nature*).

## Conditions and scope

- Trend-level association; gene-by-gene exceptions exist.

## Counter-evidence

- Causality (hypoxia → methylation → expression) not directly demonstrated in this study.
