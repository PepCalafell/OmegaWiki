## Statement

eFibro_CTHRC1 cells preferentially localize at the leading edge between malignant and normal regions: in 32 of 41 (78%) ST slides containing both fibroblasts and malignant cells, the CTHRC1+ signature score negatively correlates with distance to tumor.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "the eFibro_CTHRC1 fractions showed a negative correlation with the distance to tumors in the majority of ST slides (total ST slides, 32/41, 78%)" (Han 2025, p.9; Fig. 4b,c).

## Linked ideas

- CTHRC1+ CAFs as a physical barrier candidate for immune exclusion.
