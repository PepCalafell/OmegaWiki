## Statement

Across the E11→E13 developmental transition in mouse embryo brain/spinal-cord pixels, three neuronal-development genes (Usp9x, Ank3, Shank2) are upregulated in expression with concomitant loss of DNA methylation, with strongly significant Wilcoxon-test p-values for both modalities.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 3e–f. Wilcoxon p-values reported for RNA and DNAm separately.

## Conditions and scope

E11 vs E13 mouse embryo; brain and spinal cord regions; specific marker genes.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.

## Open questions

- Causal directionality (demethylation precedes expression or vice versa) at finer temporal sampling.
- Generalisation to other organs across the E11–E13 transition (heart and craniofacial data shown in Extended Data Fig. 9).
