## Statement

The NSCLC tumour microenvironment can be functionally classified into immune-rich and immune-poor (immune-desert / immune-excluded) spatial niches whose composition and interactions jointly shape progression and therapeutic responses.

## Evidence summary

Spatial transcriptomic atlases, multiplex imaging and scRNA-seq studies in NSCLC consistently identify these two dominant niche archetypes and link their distribution to ICI response.

## Conditions and scope

Classification is dichotomous in shorthand but is in practice a continuum, with multiple niches coexisting in single tumours.

## Counter-evidence

Topographical refinements show that binary categorisation may obscure clinically relevant gradients.
