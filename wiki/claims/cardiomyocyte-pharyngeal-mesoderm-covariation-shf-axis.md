## Statement

In E8.5 embryo seqFISH, cardiomyocyte Fa2 significantly covaries with pharyngeal-mesoderm Fa1 (log10P = -3.92), with negatively correlated mature cardiac markers (Tnnt2, Tnni3, Myl2/3, Acta) and positively correlated SHF progenitor markers (Nkx2-5, Isl1, Mef2c, Fgf8).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — Ridge regression on stem/TA-style covariation module recovers cardiomyocyte Fa2 ↔ pharyngeal mesoderm Fa1 dependency supported by 223 co-localized pairs (out of 853 PM / 773 CM); top-correlating gene patterns implicate cardiac progenitor → mature cardiomyocyte transition driven by pharyngeal mesoderm (Fig. 3f–g).

## Conditions and scope

Single E8.5 seqFISH dataset; cell-state interpretation correlational.
