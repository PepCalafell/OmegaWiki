## Statement
The promoters shared across the four human macrophage activation states are overwhelmingly accessible (active, Pa) rather than poised (Pp), defining a common epigenetically active cellular program.

## Evidence summary
ChIP-seq across H3K4me3/H3K27ac identified 7 427 common Pa loci versus 1 247 common Pp loci. The high accessible-to-poised ratio supports a constitutively active shared program.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro GM-CSF macrophages; common (not signal-specific) loci.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.

## Open questions
Whether the same ratio holds for M-CSF-differentiated macrophages.
