## Statement

Removing adrenal-derived glucocorticoid signalling exacerbates stress-induced AD-like dermatitis, indicating the HPA axis is a brake on, not a driver of, stress-aggravated skin inflammation. The sympathetic nervous system is the proximal driver.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], fig. S6A–C.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only; surgical adrenalectomy ablates all adrenal output (corticosterone + adrenal medullary adrenaline).

## Counter-evidence

Contradicts the traditional cortisol-driven stress-AD model (Nicolaides 2015; Lin 2017).
