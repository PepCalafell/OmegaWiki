## Statement

Human NK cells are resistant to STING-induced cytotoxicity due to low intrinsic STING expression and blunted IRF3 response.

## Evidence summary

Knelson 2022 Cancer Immunol Res: human NK cells express low intrinsic STING and show blunted IRF3 response, making them resistant to STING-agonist-induced cell death. Confirmed in patient-derived organotypic spheroids treated with STING agonism.

## Conditions and scope

Human NK cells; partial conservation in mouse. Underlies the structural asymmetry between NK and T cells in STING-targeted therapy.
