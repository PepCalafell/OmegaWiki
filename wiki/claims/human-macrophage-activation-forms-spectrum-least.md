## Statement
Human macrophage activation is best described by a spectrum of at least nine distinct transcriptional programs, extending the classical M1-versus-M2 polarization model.

## Evidence summary
- Network analyses identified central regulators "associated with all macrophage activation complemented by regulators related to stimulus-specific programs" (p.274, SUMMARY).
- "Network modeling of this data set led us to extend the current M1 versus M2 polarization model to a 'spectrum model' with at least nine distinct macrophage activation programs" (p.275).

## Conditions and scope
Derived from in-vitro monocyte-derived macrophages; the bipolar M1/M2 axis is recovered as a special case when only IFN-γ / IL-4 are used.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper; single-cell in-vivo taxonomies later refine (not refute) the spectrum framing.
