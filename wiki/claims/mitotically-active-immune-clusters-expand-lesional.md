## Statement

Cycling (MKI67+/TOP2A+) immune cell clusters were markedly expanded in rash-affected skin, indicating active in-situ proliferation of Trm, Treg, ILC/NK, and CD8+ T cell populations — i.e. the Treg/Trm expansion is at least partly proliferation-driven.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results and Discussion of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]. Supports [[concepts/treg-trm-expansion-cd8-exhaustion-chronic]].

## Conditions and scope

Inferred from transcriptional cell-cycle markers, not direct proliferation assays.

## Counter-evidence

None reported; mechanism inferred from cycling-gene expression.
