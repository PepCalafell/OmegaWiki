## Statement

Under normoxia, HIF-1α controls a much larger fraction of the hepatocellular carcinoma proteome than of the cervical-carcinoma proteome: approximately 26% of Huh7 proteins are differentially expressed on HIF1A knockout at 21% O₂, versus about 10% in HeLa — a striking normoxic dependency given HIF-1α is not detectable in asynchronous normoxic Huh7 cultures.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3145–3146) — quantitative proteomics comparison.
  - Quote: "the expression of approximately 26% of the total proteins identified in Huh7 cells and 10% of the total proteins identified in HeLa cells was affected by HIF1A knockout under normoxia."
  - Huh7 normoxia: 217 proteins up + 592 down by HIF-1α; HeLa normoxia: 428 up + 215 down.

## Conditions and scope

Two biological replicates (third confirmatory); protein-level (not mRNA); differential-expression thresholds P<0.05 and >1.5 fold. Percentages are of identified, not total, proteomes.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. Magnitude depends on proteome depth/identification (3112 vs 6043 proteins across the two lines).
