## Statement

Tumour cells under cycling hypoxia exhibit increased multipolar mitoses, lagging chromosomes and DNA bridges with consequential aneuploidy, in a HIF1α-dependent manner, in breast, colorectal and oropharyngeal SCC models.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Cycling hypoxia. HIF1α-dependent. Multiple cancer cell lines.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
