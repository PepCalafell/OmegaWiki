## Statement

Continuous inhaled systemic hypoxia (8% O2) significantly reduces solid tumor growth across subcutaneous Panc02 PDAC, orthotopic E0771 breast cancer, and a KPC genetic PDAC model in immunocompetent mice.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.1): "we showed that systemic hypoxia, in contrast to localized tumor hypoxia, decreases tumor growth in vivo across multiple cancer types and preclinical models" — Panc02 subcutaneous (Fig.1b), E0771 orthotopic mammary (Fig.1c), and KPC pancreas-weight (Fig.1f) all show reduced growth under 8% O2 vs 21% O2; moderate hypoxia (11%) intermediate. (type: methodological, strength: strong)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
