## Statement

A single-cell cSCC atlas built from matched healthy and cancer biopsies of 5 patients (11 paired samples) comprises 45,909 QC-passing cells, resolving 5 major cell classes and a hierarchical immune taxonomy down to 19 immune cell (sub)types.

## Evidence summary

scRNA-seq with three-level annotation; KCs dominate (70.6% of cells). Cell labels validated by transfer to CosMx spatial data and consistency with skin-layer anatomy. (p.4)

## Conditions and scope

Within-patient comparison of disease states; cSCC only (melanoma reference is separate, by snRNA-seq).

## Counter-evidence

None internal; cohort is small (5 patients) limiting generalisability.

## Open questions

Whether the immune subtype taxonomy generalises to larger cSCC cohorts.
