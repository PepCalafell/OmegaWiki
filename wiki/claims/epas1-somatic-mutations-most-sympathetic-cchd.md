## Statement

In tumors arising under chronic systemic hypoxia (CCHD), somatic EPAS1 mutations are present in the large majority of sympathetic PPGLs (24/27, 88.8%), occurring exclusively in tumor DNA and not in germline.

## Evidence summary

Targeted Sanger sequencing of a 27-patient sympathetic CCHD-PPGL cohort; WES excluded other PPGL susceptibility genes, marking EPAS1 as the genetic driver.

## Conditions and scope

Specific to sympathetic (catecholamine-secreting) PPGL under systemic hypoxia; does not apply to parasympathetic tumors.

## Counter-evidence

None within the cohort; 3/27 sympathetic tumors were EPAS1-wild-type.
