## Statement

In vitro co-administration of Dcn with Tgf-β significantly dampens Col1a1 (P<0.01) and Pdgfrb (P<0.04) activation-marker induction in cultured hepatic stellate cells, while quiescent marker Reln is unaffected.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — qPCR after in vitro HSC culture stimulated with Tgf-β ± Dcn; significant reduction in Col1a1 (P<0.01) and Pdgfrb (P<0.04); Lox/Acta2 trend reduction not significant (Fig. 6h; Suppl. Fig. 10f).

## Conditions and scope

Cultured mouse HSCs; in vitro; pharmacological Dcn supplementation; n unspecified in excerpt.
