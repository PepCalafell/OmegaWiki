## Statement
In Kras-mutant lung cancer models, senescent macrophages suppress cytotoxic T cell responses; their clearance ameliorates lung cancer development.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
