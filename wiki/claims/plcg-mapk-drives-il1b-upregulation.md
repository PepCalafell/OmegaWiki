## Statement

PLCγ acts downstream of PS receptors to sustain MAPK and prime pro-Il1b; PLC inhibition abolishes IL-1β without blocking caspase-8/GSDMD cleavage.

## Evidence summary

Separates the lytic-death arm from the IL-1β arm. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.8).

> "Treatment with a PLC inhibitor (U-73122) inhibited and delayed MAPK activation ... it abrogated the production of both pro- and mature IL-1β"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
