## Statement

In all five examined skin diseases, the PERIVASC I neighborhood not only expands spatially but acquires disease-specific increases in interleukins (IL-6, IL-1β, IL-17F, IL-24...), chemokines (CXCL13, CCL17, CCL13, IL-8), and ECM-remodeling factors (MMP1, MMP3, MMP11, POSTN, TNC, COL1A1).
