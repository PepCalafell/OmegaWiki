## Statement

CD206hi IM depletion reduced B220+ B cell and CD3+ T cell aggregates ~80% (B16F10) and caused complete loss of TLS in KPAR1.3 and AgerCreERT2KP adenocarcinoma models, concomitant with increased tumor burden.

## Evidence summary

Immunohistochemistry quantification of B/T cell aggregates and TLS (Fig. 2e-g). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; melanoma forms peribronchial aggregates, adenocarcinomas form organized TLS.
