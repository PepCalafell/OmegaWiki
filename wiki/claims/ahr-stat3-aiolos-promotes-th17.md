## Statement

Under Th17-polarising cytokines (IL-6/IL-21 + TGFβ), STAT3 drives AHR expression; AHR cooperates with STAT3 to induce Aiolos (suppressing IL-2), inhibits STAT1/STAT5, and recruits RORγt to the *Il22* promoter, supporting Th17 / Th22 cell differentiation and IL-22 production.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Veldhoen 2008; Quintana et al.; Apetoh et al.).
