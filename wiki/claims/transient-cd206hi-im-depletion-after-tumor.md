## Statement

A single DT dose on day 4 reproduced the increased-tumor phenotype of two doses, indicating transient depletion of CD206hi IMs after tumor seeding is sufficient to promote growth.

## Evidence summary

B16F10 melanoma single-dose vs two-dose comparison (Supplementary Fig. 2c). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].
