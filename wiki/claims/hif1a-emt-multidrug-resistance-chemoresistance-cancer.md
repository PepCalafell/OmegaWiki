## Statement

HIF-1α is a central transcriptional driver of treatment failure in solid tumors through two convergent programs: (i) Epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) via NF-κB cross-talk, miR-421 (targeting E-cadherin/caspase-3), miR-675-5p, and Twist activation — enabling invasion and metastasis; (ii) Multidrug resistance via transcriptional induction of P-gp/MDR1, BCRP, miR-27a, and autophagy-supported survival. The HIF-1α-EMT-MDR axis underlies cisplatin resistance in gastric/pancreatic cancers, sorafenib resistance in HCC, and ATP-driven chemoresistance in breast cancer.

## Evidence summary

Multiple primary studies across gastric, colon, breast, pancreatic chapters of Luo et al. 2022.

## Conditions and scope

- Solid tumors with hypoxic regions
- Cancer stem cell compartments
- Often co-engaged with HIF-2α (sorafenib resistance via TGFα/EGFR axis in HCC)

## Counter-evidence

Some HIF-1α-driven autophagy is protective (limits chemotherapy toxicity to bystander cells).
