## Statement

In the in vivo Perturb-seq screen, CRISPR KOs of the multi-state TF genes `Hic1`, `Stat3`, `Prdm1` and `Ikzf3` (encoding AIOLOS) each reduce TEXterm cell frequency by approximately 90% versus scramble controls.
