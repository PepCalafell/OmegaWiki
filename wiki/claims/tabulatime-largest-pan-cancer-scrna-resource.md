## Statement

TabulaTIME is the largest pan-cancer scRNA-seq tumor microenvironment integration reported to date, combining 4,483,367 cells across 103 studies, 36 cancer types, and 746 donors.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "we collected 4,483,367 cells across 36 cancer types and constructed a pan-cancer resource named TabulaTIME" (Han 2025, p.1).
