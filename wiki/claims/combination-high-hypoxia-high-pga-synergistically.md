## Statement

In prostate cancer and sarcoma, high intratumoural hypoxia combined with high percent genome alteration produces the worst biochemical relapse-free / metastasis-free survival, with a synergistic adverse effect beyond either factor alone.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Localized prostate cancer; soft-tissue sarcoma. Clinical cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
