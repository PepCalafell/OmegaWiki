## Statement

Hypoxic cancer cells acidify the extracellular TME to pH 5.8–6.5 via HIF1α-induced CA9/CA12 and MCT1/4 lactate export. The acidic milieu inhibits CTL activation, proliferation, cytokine production and induces T-cell apoptosis — a key immune-evasion mechanism beyond direct PD-L1 upregulation.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxic tumor cores; effect amplifies with tumor size and lactate accumulation.

## Counter-evidence

Some tumor regions remain at neutral pH due to local perfusion; CTL function recovery occurs upon CAIX or MCT4 inhibition.
