## Statement

Human proteins (and their network neighbourhoods) targeted by commensal effectors are
genetically enriched for microbiome-associated immune, metabolic and other complex
disease traits.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.448–450, Fig.4b): enrichment for
immunoglobulin isotype switching, cancers, psoriasis, asthma, allergies, SLE. Targeting
convergence proteins (REL, TCF4) may extend effects to skin/lung. 26% of HuMMI effectors
detectable in skin microbiome samples.

## Conditions and scope

Associative (GWAS causal-gene mapping); none of the immune diseases predominantly affect
the gut.

## Counter-evidence

Trait associations are statistical, not mechanistic.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether effector targeting contributes causally to disease aetiology.
