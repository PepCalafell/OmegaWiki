## Statement

The two stress-activated sympathetic subtypes serve different effector functions: Npy+ neurons mediate cutaneous vasoconstriction; Pdyn+ neurons mediate eosinophil recruitment and inflammation. Vasomotor and immunomodulatory sympathetic outputs are anatomically and functionally separable.
