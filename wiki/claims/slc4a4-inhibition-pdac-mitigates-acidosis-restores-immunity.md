## Statement

Inhibition of the bicarbonate transporter SLC4A4 in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma reduces TME acidification and improves the anticancer immune response, supporting acidosis as a druggable axis of metabolic immunosuppression.

## Evidence summary

- Preclinical PDAC evidence cited in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- PDAC models; clinical translation pending.

## Counter-evidence

- Off-target effects on systemic pH need careful management.

## Linked ideas

- Pairing SLC4A4 blockade with ICB in PDAC.

## Open questions

- Drug candidates with appropriate pharmacokinetic profile for chronic dosing.
