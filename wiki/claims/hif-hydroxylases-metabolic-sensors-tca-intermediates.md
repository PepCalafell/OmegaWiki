## Statement

Because the HIF hydroxylases use 2-oxoglutarate as co-substrate, they are sensitive to the abundance of TCA-cycle-derived metabolites (succinate, 2-hydroxyglutarate, 2-oxoglutarate) and to ROS — so HIF integrates metabolic status, not just oxygen tension.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Links to foundations [[foundations/2-hydroxyglutarate]], [[foundations/sucnr1-succinate-receptor]] and concept [[concepts/warburg-effect-hif1a-glycolytic-reprogramming]].

## Conditions and scope

Immunometabolites such as succinate (innate immunity, via IL-1β/HIF1α) and S-2-hydroxyglutarate (CD8⁺ T cell fate) are the immunologically salient examples.

## Counter-evidence

The precise mechanism(s) by which ROS regulate HIF expression/activity are explicitly noted as unclear.
