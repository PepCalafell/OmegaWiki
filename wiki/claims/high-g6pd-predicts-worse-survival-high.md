## Statement

In lung adenocarcinoma, high G6PD expression is associated with worse overall survival while high ABCG2 (itaconate exporter) expression is associated with improved survival, with the ABCG2 benefit strongest in stage I disease.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1471–1472) — correlational/clinical evidence.
  - Quote: "high G6PD expression is correlated with significantly worse overall survival, whereas high ABCG2 expression—the itaconate exporter—was associated with improved outcomes."

## Conditions and scope

Bulk-expression survival association (Kaplan-Meier/HR); no causal inference, confounders possible.

## Counter-evidence

Bulk survival proxies; ABCG2 prognosis is context-dependent across cancers ([[claims/abcg2-itaconate-opposite-prognostic-value-lung]]). Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
