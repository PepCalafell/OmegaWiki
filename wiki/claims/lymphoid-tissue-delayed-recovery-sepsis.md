## Statement

After sublethal LPS endotoxemia, nonlymphoid tissues (heart, lung, kidney, brain, liver, etc.) return to transcriptional steady state within 5 d, whereas lymphoid tissues (spleen, thymus, LNs, bone marrow) remain perturbed, providing a transcriptional substrate for long-term sepsis-associated immunosuppression.

## Conditions and scope

Endotoxemia only at the 5-day window; longer-term dynamics and CLP recovery dynamics untested in this study.
