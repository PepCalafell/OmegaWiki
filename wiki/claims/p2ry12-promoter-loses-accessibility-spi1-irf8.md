## Statement

Under hypoxic glioma co-culture, the chromatin accessibility peak at the P2ry12 promoter is nearly lost, and this peak contains binding motifs for SPI1/PU.1 and IRF8 — master regulators of microglial identity — mechanistically linking hypoxic chromatin closure to loss of the homeostatic microglial marker.

## Evidence summary

IGV tracks with FIMO motif annotation (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.12).

> "the ATAC-seq peak at the P2ry12 (a Mg-GAM marker) promoter was nearly lost in BV2 cells co-cultured with glioma under hypoxia. This peak contained motifs for Spi-1 proto-oncogene (SPI1, encoding PU.1) and interferon regulatory factor 8 (IRF8), key regulators of microglial identity and P2ry12 expression"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; hypoxic glioma co-culture; averaged from two biological repeats.

## Counter-evidence

Motif presence is correlative; direct TF binding/occupancy at the peak not assayed.
