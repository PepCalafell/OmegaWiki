## Statement

NF-κB binds the HIF1A and HIF2A promoters and induces their mRNA transcription. Canonical inputs upstream of NF-κB — Toll-like receptor ligation (LPS→TLR4), IL-1β/IL-1R, TNF — all upregulate HIF-α at the transcriptional level. HIF-1α and HIF-2α conversely also feed back into NF-κB activation, creating a self-reinforcing inflammation-hypoxia loop.

## Evidence summary

Promoter-mapping studies of HIF1A and HIF2A identified κB elements; ChIP confirms p65 binding under inflammatory stimulation. Functional rescue with NF-κB inhibitors (BAY11-7082) reduces HIF-α induction.

## Conditions and scope

- TLR4: LPS → MyD88/TRIF → NF-κB → HIF-1α
- IL-1β/IL-1R → NF-κB → HIF-2α in chondrocytes
- TNFα → NF-κB → HIF-1α in macrophages

## Counter-evidence

Cell-type-dependent magnitude; in some contexts (B cells), HIF-α regulation is more glucose/PI3K than NF-κB.
