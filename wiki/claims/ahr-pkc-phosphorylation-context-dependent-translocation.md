## Statement

The same AHR phosphorylation site (S36) can produce opposite phenotypes depending on the cell-type kinase repertoire: classical PKC in COS-7/HeLa inhibits AHR nuclear translocation, whereas PKCθ in T cells enhances it. This is a paradigmatic example of how identical PTMs can deliver opposite functional outputs across cell types, supporting the layered context-specificity model.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews the canonical references for S12/S36 (refs 144, 145) and S68 (ref 146).

## Conditions and scope

Cell-line evidence; in-vivo PTM mapping across tissues remains incomplete (most high-throughput sites in Fig. 3 of the paper lack assigned kinases).

## Counter-evidence

— PKC effects on AHR are themselves cell-line-dependent: in HepG2/HeLa, PKC modulation does not alter AHR nuclear translocation or XRE binding, despite using similar PKC-modulating compounds (conflicting reports reviewed in the paper).
