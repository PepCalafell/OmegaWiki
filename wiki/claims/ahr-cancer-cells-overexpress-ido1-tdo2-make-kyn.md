## Statement

Multiple cancers (glioblastoma, breast, oral, gastric) over-express IDO1 and TDO2, generating tryptophan-derived AHR agonists (kynurenine and downstream metabolites) that chronically activate AHR in tumour and tumour-microenvironment cells, suppressing antitumour T cell responses and promoting tumour progression.

## Evidence summary

Opitz et al. 2011; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
