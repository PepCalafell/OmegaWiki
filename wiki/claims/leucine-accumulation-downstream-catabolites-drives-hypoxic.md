## Statement

Leucine itself (not its downstream catabolites) is the active species: α-KIC rescues leucine-depleted cells only when BCAT can convert it back to leucine; BCAT inhibition abolishes the rescue.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 4A–E). α-KIC/BCAT-IN-2 epistasis exploiting BCAT reversibility.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxia; relies on the reversible leucine ⇌ α-KIC BCAT reaction.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
