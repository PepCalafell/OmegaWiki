## Statement

Clinical JAK inhibitors Filgotinib and Tofacitinib strongly inhibit STAT1 phosphorylation and interferon-stimulated gene expression in macrophages even though kinobeads assays do not detect their binding to any JAK-family kinase — a clear binding-versus-function discordance.

## Evidence summary

Despite absent kinobeads JAK binding, Filgotinib was the strongest IFN-gene impairer and Tofacitinib inhibited STAT1/IFN genes, illustrating [[concepts/discordance-between-vitro-kinase-inhibitor-selectivity]]. Links [[foundations/filgotinib]], [[foundations/stat1-tf]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; partial kinome coverage of kinobeads may underlie the apparent non-binding.

## Counter-evidence

The "no binding" result may reflect kinobeads detection limits rather than true absence of JAK engagement.
