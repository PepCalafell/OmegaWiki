## Statement
Self-Organizing-Map (SOM) clustering and hierarchically clustered correlation coefficient matrices (CCM) independently validate the spectrum model, showing condition-specific structure rather than a bipolar M1/M2 axis.

## Evidence summary
- "Performing SOM clustering ... revealed that every stimulus was characterized by a specific cluster structure" (p.275, Figure 1H).
- "we did not identify a bipolar structure within the CCM, but rather a condition-specific spectrum of correlation coefficients in 10 major clusters" (p.275, Figure 1I).

## Conditions and scope
Bioinformatic validation on the same in-vitro data set.
