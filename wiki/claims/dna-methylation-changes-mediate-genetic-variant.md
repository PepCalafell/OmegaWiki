## Statement

BCG-induced DNA methylation changes act as in silico causal mediators relaying genetic-variant effects onto trained-immunity cytokine changes, predominantly for IFN-γ.

## Evidence summary

More mediation linkages arise with methylation as the mediator than as the outcome, supporting a genotype→methylation→cytokine ordering.

## Conditions and scope

Observational mediation; cannot establish true causality.

## Counter-evidence

Bidirectional cases exist; nominal thresholds; no experimental confirmation.

## Open questions
