## Statement

Drug-target attention is highly uneven: ADRA1A is targeted by 99 distinct approved drugs — 5% of all approved drugs — despite being the subject of only about 130 publications.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Five per cent of all approved drugs currently approved (99 distinct molecules) target the protein ADRA1A, which is involved in cell growth and proliferation." (p.3); "The gene ADRA1A is targeted by 99 different drugs … It is the subject of just 130 publications." (p.2)

## Conditions and scope

Approved-drug target database; human.

## Counter-evidence

High targeting may reflect a genuinely tractable receptor family, not arbitrariness.
