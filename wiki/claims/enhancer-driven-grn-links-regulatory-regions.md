## Statement

Integrating scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq, CIMA constructs an enhancer-driven gene regulatory network of eRegulons connecting regulatory regions to target genes and recovering known lineage transcription factors.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (methodological): 404 eRegulons (237 high-quality) linking 84,625 regulatory regions to 13,645 target genes; recovered PAX5/BACH2 (B), SPI1/CEBPB (mono), RUNX3/STAT4 (T) (p.4-6).

## Conditions and scope

73 L4 immune cell types; region-to-gene links are correlational.
