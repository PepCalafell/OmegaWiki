## Statement

A tissue-infiltrating monocyte subset expressing SPP1, CCL2, FN1, IL4I1 and a pro-inflammatory program (NLRP3, IL1B, CCL3, CCL4, CXCL8) — distinct from peripheral CD14 classical / CD16 non-classical monocytes — is significantly enriched in tumor lesions of HCC PD-1 responders.

## Evidence summary

- Distinct from circulating CD14/CD16 monocyte states.
- Pro-inflammatory cytokine/chemokine profile.
- Enriched in responder tumors.

## Conditions and scope

- HCC-specific; responder-association mostly correlational.

## Counter-evidence

- Could reflect downstream consequence of T-cell-induced inflammation rather than causal contributor.
