## Statement

DUSP4 — not the related DUSP1 — is the specific ERK1/2 phosphatase whose efferocytosis-induced repression is required for sustained p-ERK and downstream Ptgs2/COX2/Tgfb1 induction. siDusp4 epistatically rescues DNMT3A or MAT2A loss.

## Evidence summary

siDusp4 (not siDusp1) rescues Ptgs2/COX2/Tgfb1 in DNMT3A- or MAT2A-deficient macrophages; cannot rescue siMapk1/3-treated, consistent with operating on ERK. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only.
