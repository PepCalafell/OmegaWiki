## Statement

MIBI quantification of 35 protein markers in FFPE tissue defines novel breast cancer patient subgroups with differential clinical outcomes.

## Evidence summary

Multiplexed ion beam imaging (MIBI) simultaneously quantified 35 protein markers in FFPE tissue samples and detected novel subgroups of patients with breast cancer with differential clinical outcomes (Polak et al. 2024, p.530; Keren et al. 2018).

## Conditions and scope

Breast cancer FFPE samples; MIBI 35-plex.
