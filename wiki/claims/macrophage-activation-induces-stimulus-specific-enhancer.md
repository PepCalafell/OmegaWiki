## Statement
Human macrophage activation reshapes the enhancer landscape in a stimulus-specific manner, with many more activation-specific enhancers than activation-specific promoters.

## Evidence summary
Activation-specific strong, weak and poised enhancers vastly outnumber activation-specific promoters; enhancer-associated genes are enriched for immune effector functions, while promoter remodeling shapes the common transcriptome.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages; defined by elevated H3K4me1/H3K4me3 tag densities versus other states.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Open questions
