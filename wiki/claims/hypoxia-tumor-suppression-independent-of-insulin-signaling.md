## Statement

Pten-knockout Panc02 tumors with constitutive insulin/PI3K signaling still show significant growth suppression under systemic hypoxia, so reduced insulin signaling does not account for the anti-tumor effect.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.6): "hypoxia treatment still significantly decreased the growth of Pten-deficient tumors, suggesting that hypoxia's suppressive effect was independent of insulin signaling" — Single-clone Pten KO Panc02 (Fig.S2c); Fig.3f, S2d show hypoxia suppresses Pten-deficient tumors. (type: mechanistic, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
