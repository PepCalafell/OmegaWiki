## Statement

Octyl itaconate reprograms IL-4-polarized anti-inflammatory (pro-tumor) macrophages toward a stress-activated, partially pro-inflammatory and tumor-suppressive state by suppressing PPP intermediates and G6PD activity.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1469–1471) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "Octyl Ita reprogrammed anti-inflammatory macrophages toward a tumor-suppressive state, as CM from treated cells reduced A549 proliferation more effectively than direct Octyl Ita exposure."
  - "Octyl Ita reprograms anti-inflammatory macrophages toward a stress-activated, partially pro-inflammatory state that is associated with redox signaling and metabolic remodeling."

## Conditions and scope

Human PBMC-derived macrophages; convergence toward the pro-inflammatory state is partial (IFN signatures not fully restored). Marker changes fit the pro-/anti-tumor macrophage dichotomy.
