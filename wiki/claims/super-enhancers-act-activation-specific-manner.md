## Statement
Super-enhancers in human macrophages are largely activation-specific, with a smaller common core shared across all activation states.

## Evidence summary
Up to 820 SEs per condition, 200 common to all four; the common CCR1/XCR1-region SE could be linked to CCR1 expression.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages; SEs defined from H3K27ac peaks.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Open questions
