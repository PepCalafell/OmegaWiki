## Statement

In mouse and human GBM single-cell datasets, the GAM hypoxia score correlates positively with Lgals3 and negatively with Tmem119 and P2ry12 in TAMs (most pronounced in Mg-TAMs), confirming the in vitro marker dysregulation in vivo.

## Evidence summary

Correlation analysis across species and disease stage (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.5).

> "Gene expression showed a statistically significant positive correlation between the hypoxia score and Lgals3 and a negative correlation with Tmem119 and P2ry12 in TAMs, especially in Mg-TAMs ... confirmed in humans in newly diagnosed and recurrent GBM"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse GL261 TAM and human GBM TAM scRNA-seq (Antunes/Wang, GSE163120).

## Counter-evidence

Correlative single-cell evidence; cannot separate per-cell reprogramming from compositional effects.
