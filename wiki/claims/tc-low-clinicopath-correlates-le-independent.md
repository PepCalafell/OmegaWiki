## Statement
Low TC enrichment is significantly associated with multiple adverse pathological features, while LE enrichment is an independent axis that does not co-vary with standard clinical covariates.

## Evidence summary
Fig. 5e–f bar plots; Supplementary Fig. 5d shows weak negative TC-LE correlation (r=-0.17).

## Conditions and scope
TCGA HPV-negative OSCC bulk samples; standard clinicopathology variables.

## Counter-evidence
Weak negative TC-LE correlation could partially explain the asymmetry; multivariate analysis would clarify independence.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Whether LE provides additive prognostic value over the AJCC staging system.
