## Statement

Kisspeptin-10 directly and selectively suppresses IFN-γ production capacity in human PBMCs, validating an immunomodulatory role.

## Evidence summary

Dose-range functional assay shows significant IFN-γ inhibition without affecting other T-cell cytokines (IL-17, IL-22), supporting kisspeptin as a modulator linking KISS1 methylation to IFN-γ.

## Conditions and scope

Single in vitro assay; healthy-donor PBMCs.

## Counter-evidence

Downstream signaling and in vivo relevance not established; one experimental system.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether endogenous KISS1 methylation reproduces this effect in vivo.
