## Statement

Pooled multi-fragment 24-48 h tumour explant cytokine/chemokine/T cell activation marker readouts predict ICB response across melanoma, NSCLC, breast, ovarian and RCC.

## Evidence summary

Short-term ICB treatment (24-48 h) could predict ICB efficacy in 37 tumour explants from patients with melanoma, NSCLC, breast cancer, ovarian cancer and RCC, with pooled cytokine/chemokine/T cell activation marker analysis predicting response in a validation cohort (Polak et al. 2024, p.531).

## Conditions and scope

Patient-derived tumour fragment explants pooled per patient; validation cohort.
