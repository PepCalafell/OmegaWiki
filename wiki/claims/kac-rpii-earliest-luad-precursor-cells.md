## Statement

KAC/RPII alveolar intermediate cells precede morphologically recognizable lung precursor lesions (AAH, AIS) and seed clones shared with paired LUAD, positioning them as the earliest discernible precancerous population in lung adenocarcinoma evolution.

## Evidence summary

Spatial clonal architecture in 25 patients shows that earliest clones consistently map to RPII regions. In patients with evolution pattern 1a, most RPII clones are shared with both precursor and invasive paired lesions; in patterns 1b and 2, RPII clones can also be precursor- or invasive-specific. KAC score (derived previously by the same group) is elevated across shared and stage-specific clones, and CytoTRACE positions KACs at the least-differentiated end of the alveolar lineage. snRNA-seq and Xenium confirm KAC abundance/identity at single-cell resolution.

## Conditions and scope

Findings derived from synchronously resected human precursor + LUAD specimens (sampling bias acknowledged) and from carcinogen-driven Gprc5a−/− mouse model.

## Open questions

- Are KACs reversible by inflammation resolution before mutational acquisition?
- Do KACs in non-cancerous COPD/IPF tissue carry the same trajectory risk?
