## Statement

Sustained hypoxia induces DNA damage (γH2AX, comet assay) while diminishing PARP1, compromising DNA-repair capacity in PPGL-derived cells.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence, Western blot, and comet assay (see [[foundations/gamma-h2ax-dna-damage-marker]], [[foundations/comet-assay-single-cell-gel-electrophoresis]]).

## Conditions and scope

In-vitro PC12 model; effect strongest after prolonged (36-day) hypoxia.

## Counter-evidence

Short-term hypoxia produced smaller effects.

## Open questions

Whether reduced PARP1 creates a therapeutic vulnerability (PARP inhibition).
