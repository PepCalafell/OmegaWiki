## Statement

A subset of recurrently hypoxia-associated genes (143 of 1,060) is significantly correlated with sensitivity to at least three anticancer drugs in cancer cell lines, indicating extensive interaction between hypoxia-associated molecular features and drug response. YAP1 is a prominent example, linked to resistance to 49 drugs and sensitivity to five.

## Conditions and scope

- Cell-line correlations; in vitro associations require patient-level validation.

## Counter-evidence

- Correlation, not causation; cell lines lack the in vivo hypoxic microenvironment.
