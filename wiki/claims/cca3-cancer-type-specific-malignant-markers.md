## Statement

In 3CA v2, malignant cells carry cancer-type-specific marker portfolios — recovering known cases (PMEL/melanoma, KLK3/prostate, ESR1/breast, CDKN2A/HPV+ HNSCC) and surfacing novel context-dependent genes (APOA2/liver, ANKRD30A/breast).
