## Statement

Across the 3CA v2 cohort, malignant cells are the most proliferative cell type, with on average more than 15% of malignant cells observed cycling.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 5b, cycling fraction per cell type.

## Conditions and scope

Average masks substantial variation across cancer types.

## Counter-evidence

In ccRCC and similar slow-cycling tumours, malignant cycling is much lower (~5%).
