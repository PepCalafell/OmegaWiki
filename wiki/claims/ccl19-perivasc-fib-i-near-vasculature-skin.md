## Statement

The CCL19-high perivascular fibroblast subset (Perivasc Fib I), distinguished from CCL19-low Perivasc Fib II within an APOE+/CXCL12+/C3+ shared signature, localises specifically to the perivascular space adjacent to dermal vessels.

Healthy adult skin; spatial assignment is MERFISH-based.
