## Statement

Acetate is taken up by B cells and converted to acetyl-CoA, feeding the TCA cycle and increasing protein acetylation; this metabolic-acetylation coupling drives IL-10⁺ Breg differentiation and IgA/IgG output.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from primary microbiota-B-cell literature.

## Conditions and scope

- Murine mucosal B-cell systems; humans-in-vivo evidence limited.
