## Statement

At the protein level, hypoxia score-high tumours recurrently lose PTEN (de-repressing PI3K/Akt) and gain fibronectin (promoting EMT), with additional apoptosis-pathway shifts. These protein changes correlate with differential anticancer drug responses, linking the hypoxia proteome to therapeutic vulnerability.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — RPPA proteomic alterations and drug-response correlations (Fig. 4b).

## Conditions and scope

- RPPA covers a curated protein panel, not the full proteome.

## Counter-evidence

- Direction of some apoptosis-protein changes (BCL2 down, caspase-7 up) is context-dependent.
