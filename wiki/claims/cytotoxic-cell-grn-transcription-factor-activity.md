## Statement

In CD8 and unconventional T cells, the activity of cytotoxicity-associated transcription factors rises with donor age, defining age-associated GRNs.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (correlational): EOMES, RUNX3, and TBX21 activity positively correlated with age in CD8/unconventional T; age-related GRN e.g. NFATC3→PRF1/SLFN5 in CD8 CTL-GZMK (p.6).

## Conditions and scope

CD8 and unconventional T cells; cross-sectional cohort (ages 20-77).
