## Statement

10x Visium spatial transcriptomics on transcardially formaldehyde-perfused (intact-tissue) WT vs Stat1-KO mouse spleens confirms that STAT1-driven ISG expression (Oas3, Ifit3, Ifit1) is significantly reduced in Stat1-KO specifically in white-pulp Cluster 4, while housekeeping gene expression and overall spleen architecture (Cd8a T-cell areas, Cd33 macrophage areas) are unchanged. This rules out ex vivo cell-handling as the origin of the bulk RNA-seq baseline JAK-STAT signal.
