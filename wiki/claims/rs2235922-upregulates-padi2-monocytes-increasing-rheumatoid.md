## Statement

The rs2235922 A allele increases PADI2 expression specifically in cMono-CD14 monocytes, refining a prior whole-blood eQTL to a monocyte-specific effect associated with rheumatoid arthritis risk.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (mechanistic): eQTL effect significant only in cMono-CD14 (PeQTL=1.7e-63); SMR with RA (PSMR=1.9e-6); expression positively correlated with RA risk (p.9).

## Conditions and scope

Classical monocytes; SMR association with literature support; not functionally tested here.
