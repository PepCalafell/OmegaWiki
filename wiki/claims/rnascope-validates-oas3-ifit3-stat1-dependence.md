## Statement

Single-molecule RNAscope RNA-FISH for Oas3 and Ifit3 in FFPE mouse spleen demonstrates that these classical interferon-stimulated genes are robustly expressed in WT and almost completely absent in Stat1-KO at single-cell / sub-cellular resolution, in both Cd3+ T-cell-rich white pulp and red pulp areas. This sub-cellular-resolution validation complements the Visium spatial data and definitively excludes ex vivo cell-handling as the cause of the STAT1-dependent baseline ISG signal.
