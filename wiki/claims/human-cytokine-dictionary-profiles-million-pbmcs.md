## Statement

The Human Cytokine Dictionary is a single-cell perturbation atlas of 9,697,974 PBMCs from 12 healthy donors stimulated in vitro for 24 h with each of 90 individual cytokines (or PBS), sequenced by Parse Biosciences split-pool barcoding; 16 major cell types were annotated (12 retained for response analysis).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: methodological.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
