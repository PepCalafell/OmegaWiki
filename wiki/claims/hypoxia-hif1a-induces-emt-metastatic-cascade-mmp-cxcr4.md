## Statement

HIF1α activates a coordinated metastatic-cascade transcriptional program in hypoxic cancer cells — inducing uPAR, MMP1, CXCR4, osteopontin (OPN/SPP1), LOX, IL-8 and VEGF — that drives invasion, ECM remodeling, chemokine-driven migration, angiogenesis, and microvascular density.

## Evidence summary

Documented per cancer type in Table 1 of [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023): lung adenocarcinoma (uPAR/MMP1), uveal melanoma (CXCR4), HCC (OPN), multiple myeloma (IL-8), CRC (LOX), HCC (VEGF), and many more.

## Conditions and scope

Pan-cancer; specific regulator-malignant-behavior pairs are tumor-context-specific.

## Counter-evidence

Some experiments failed to find correlation between hypoxia and p53; pathway redundancy in some cancers.
