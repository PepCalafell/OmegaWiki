## Statement

FOLR2 macrophages and TREM2 macrophages are both significantly enriched in HCC tumor lesions compared with adjacent tumor-free liver — even though their downstream functional/response associations diverge (TREM2 → responders; FOLR2 → non-responders).

## Evidence summary

- Mo-mac state frequencies in matched tumor vs adjacent samples.
- Both states preferentially populate tumor nodules.

## Conditions and scope

- HCC neoadjuvant cohorts; not tested in advanced/unresectable HCC.

## Counter-evidence

- None within this paper.
