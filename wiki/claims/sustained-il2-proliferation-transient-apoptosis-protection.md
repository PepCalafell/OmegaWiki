## Statement

Using ASNeo2 to terminate IL-2 signalling at defined times reveals that sustained signalling is required for primary human T-cell proliferation, whereas a brief transient pulse (~5 min) is sufficient to protect cells from apoptosis (≈2× survival at three days versus unstimulated control), despite suppression of most downstream IL-2 target proteins.

## Evidence summary

"Whereas sustained stimulation was required for proliferation, protection from apoptosis was evident after a short transient stimulation: cells treated with ASNeo2 and then with effector 5 min afterwards survived three days later at double the rate of an unstimulated control" (p.7). Survival difference P < 1×10⁻⁴; division difference not significant.

## Conditions and scope

Primary human T cells; CFSE division + survival at 3 days; n = 4.

## Counter-evidence

Apoptosis protection reflects reduced caspase-3 and increased BCL2 (a specific downstream branch), not global signalling restoration.
