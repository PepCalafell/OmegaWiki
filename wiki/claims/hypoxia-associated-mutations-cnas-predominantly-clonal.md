## Statement

Bulk-sequencing analyses indicate hypoxia is associated with clonal (truncal) rather than subclonal mutations, and in prostate cancer 99% of hypoxia-associated CNAs occur early during tumour evolution — implying hypoxia exerts selective pressure before subclonal diversification.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Bulk WGS analyses; needs spatial/single-cell validation.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
