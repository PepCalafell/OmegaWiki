## Statement

In C2C12 cells, IL6-induced atrophy + glucose hyperconsumption + one-carbon metabolite elevation is all reversed by FIDAS-5 — placing the methionine cycle pharmacologically downstream of IL6 in the cachexia phenotypic axis and making MAT inhibition a candidate intervention point downstream of IL6 receptor signalling.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "the induction of IL6 signalling was associated with an induction of one-carbon metabolites and myotube atrophy, which was rescued upon FIDAS-5 treatment. FIDAS-5 also significantly reduced glucose consumption in IL6-treated cells."

## Conditions and scope

C2C12 myotubes; in vitro only.

## Counter-evidence

In vivo recapitulation (FIDAS-5 in tumour-bearing mice) not tested — translatability open.
