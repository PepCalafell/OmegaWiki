## Statement

AHR signalling drives the conversion of pathogenic Th17 cells into IL-10-producing Tr1 cells in vivo, providing a built-in mechanism to limit Th17-driven immunopathology.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Gagliani et al. 2015 Nature; Mascanfroni et al.).
