## Statement

Human HCC primary tumours that seed polyclonal metastases show transcriptional activation of hypoxia signalling and elevated HIF1α staining without distinguishing genomic alterations; HIF1α-overexpressing HCC lines show increased metastatic potential in vivo.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Hepatocellular carcinoma; molecular pathology + in vivo cell-mixing.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
