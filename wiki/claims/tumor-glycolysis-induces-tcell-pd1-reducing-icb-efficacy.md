## Statement

Tumour-cell glycolysis (and the resulting metabolic stress of TILs) induces high PD-1 expression on infiltrating T cells, blunting immune-checkpoint blockade efficacy. Pharmacologically or genetically lowering tumour glycolysis restores ICB responsiveness.

## Evidence summary

- Cross-laboratory data summarised in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Glycolytic-high tumours; preclinical evidence dominant.

## Counter-evidence

- PD-1 is induced by many other signals (chronic antigen, IFN-γ), so glycolysis is one input among several.

## Linked ideas

- Combine metabolic inhibitors with ICB based on glycolytic signatures.

## Open questions

- Which glycolytic enzyme is the optimal target for ICB combination clinically?
