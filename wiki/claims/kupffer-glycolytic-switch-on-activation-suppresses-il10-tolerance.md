## Statement

Quiescent Kupffer cells maintain low glycolysis and contribute to hepatic immunological tolerance via IL-10. Upon immunogenic activation, glucose uptake and PDK-driven glycolysis are upregulated, with concomitant loss of tolerogenic IL-10 output.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]].

## Conditions and scope

- Acute activation only; chronic activation in MASH/steatohepatitis has different metabolic phenotype.
