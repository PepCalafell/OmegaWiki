## Statement

The overwhelming majority of clinically actionable cancer genes (110 of 121; 90.9%) are altered in association with hypoxia status in at least one molecular layer and cancer type. Because these genes are the targets of FDA-approved drugs spanning immunotherapy, chemotherapy, hormone therapy, and targeted therapy, hypoxia status is broadly relevant to predicting and combining cancer therapies.

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — five-layer survey of 121 actionable genes (Fig. 7a).

## Conditions and scope

- "Actionable" defined by a curated FDA drug-target list; association in ≥1 cancer type counts.

## Counter-evidence

- Bias is distributed across many cancer types; few genes are biased in all.
