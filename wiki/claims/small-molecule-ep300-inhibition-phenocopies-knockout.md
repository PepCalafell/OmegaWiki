## Statement
Pharmacological inhibition of EP300 — targeting either the bromodomain reader (SGC-CBP30) or the catalytic writer domain (A-485) — reproduces the ISG-upregulating effect of Ep300 genetic knockout, orthogonally validating EP300 as a repressor of the interferon response. Notably, Spp1 behaved differently between KO (decrease) and catalytic inhibition (increase), suggesting distinct mechanisms for ISG vs inflammatory gene regulation.

## Evidence summary
qPCR of five ISGs across KO and two inhibitors converged on upregulation ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figures 5H, S10G; uses [[foundations/ep300-histone-acetyltransferase]]).

## Conditions and scope
Macrophage cell line; targeted qPCR panel.

## Counter-evidence
Spp1 discordance between genetic KO and catalytic inhibition indicates the EP300 mechanism is gene-context dependent.
