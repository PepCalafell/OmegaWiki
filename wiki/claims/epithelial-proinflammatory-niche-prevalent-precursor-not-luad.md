## Statement

Epithelial-proinflammatory niches — defined by IL1R1-high KAC/AAH cells juxtaposed with IL1B-high macrophages (Xenium 5K Prime cluster C15; TMA cluster C4) — are stage-specific to precursor lesions and dissipate as lesions become invasive.

## Evidence summary

Across three independent platforms (Visium ST, Xenium 5K Prime, Xenium 298+100 gene TMA), IL1B⁺ macrophage neighborhoods of AAH, AIS, and inflamed AT2 cells are significantly enriched relative to advanced tumor cells. Other proinflammatory subsets (CCL2⁺, IL18⁺, NFKB1⁺, CSF1⁺) follow the same precursor-enriched pattern. Inflammatory CAFs (iCAFs) are also most abundant in AAH/AIS.

## Conditions and scope

Holds for both human resected lesion cohorts and mouse NNK-exposed Gprc5a−/− tumors at 3 vs 7 months post-NNK.

## Counter-evidence

None internal. Externally, late-stage NSCLC TME literature emphasizes immunosuppressive niches (SPP1⁺/TREM2⁺ TAMs) consistent with the depletion of pro-inflammatory niches at advanced stages.

## Open questions

- Stage-specific niche transitions: what triggers loss of IL1B-IL1R1 niches in MIA/LUAD?
- Are CT-detected indeterminate nodules with active IL1B-IL1R1 niches at higher risk of progression?
