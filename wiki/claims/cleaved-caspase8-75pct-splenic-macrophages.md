## Statement

TNF induces cleaved caspase-8 in ~75% of splenic CD68+ macrophages in vivo, reduced by TRIF deficiency.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence quantification, 4h post-TNF. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.3).

> "we observed cleaved caspase-8 (CC8) staining in ~75% of CD68+ cells in the spleens of WT mice 4 hours after injection with TNF, which was reduced in Trif-/- spleens"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

_None yet._

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
