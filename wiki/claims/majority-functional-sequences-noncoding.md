## Statement

Most functional sequence in the human genome is non-coding — promoters, enhancers, lncRNAs, and regulatory motifs — and variation there perturbs gene-regulatory networks rather than protein sequence.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "it is now appreciated that the majority of functional sequences in the human genome do not encode proteins." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

Reflects field consensus; "functional" definition remains debated (cf. ENCODE controversies).

## Counter-evidence

The exact fraction of the genome that is functional is contested.
