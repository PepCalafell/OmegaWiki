## Statement

While HIF-1α is required for baseline glycolytic capacity in BMDMs, it is not required for the rapid glycolytic flux induced by LPS — suggesting LPS upregulates glycolysis acutely via post-translational activation of rate-limiting enzymes (PFKFB3, PKM2), not via HIF-1α-driven transcription.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 5A,B; Suppl. Fig. 7A-C. Concordant signals from genetic KO and siRNA.

## Conditions and scope

Acute (minutes-to-hours) LPS response; in vitro BMDMs. Does not exclude HIF-1α dependence of late-phase (hours-to-days) glycolytic gene expression.

## Counter-evidence

Long-term glycolytic gene/protein expression is HIF-1α-dependent ([[claims/bmdm-hif1a-required-baseline-glycolysis]]) — separates kinetic windows.
