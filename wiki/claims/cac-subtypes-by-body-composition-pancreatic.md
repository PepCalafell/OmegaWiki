## Statement

In advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, at least two clinical CAC subtypes are reproducibly identifiable on serial CT — "adipose-tissue-loss only" and "adipose + skeletal-muscle loss" — distinct from a no-loss group and independent of tumour response, sarcopenia or obesity. Both subtypes confer worse survival.

## Evidence summary

Klassen et al. cohort identified the two phenotypes on serial CT-L3 imaging; sarcopenic obesity was specifically associated with the adipose-only phenotype.

## Conditions and scope

Established for advanced PDAC on combined chemotherapy. Other tumour types (NSCLC via TRACERx, liver cancer via Jin trajectories) show similar subtype structure but with different proportions.

## Counter-evidence

Cross-sectional studies pooling all CAC patients into one group implicitly contradict subtype existence; this reflects definitional collapse rather than evidence against.

## Linked ideas

- Trial stratification should incorporate body-composition subtypes.

## Open questions

- Do molecular profiles (e.g., one-carbon metabolism markers) discriminate the two subtypes?
