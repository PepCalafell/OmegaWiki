## Statement

Neutrophil-depletion timing is decisive: 1A8 24h before TNF protects, but 1A8 4h before TNF preloads macrophages with engulfed neutrophils and accelerates lethality (100% death in 5h).

## Evidence summary

Links macrophage efferocytic burden to susceptibility. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.12).

> "mice treated with 1A8 4 hours before injection were highly sensitive to TNF-induced lethality, with 100% of mice succumbing within 5 hours"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.
