## Statement

DHPS-mediated hypusination of eIF5A is a cell-intrinsic, tissue-agnostic requirement for monocytes to differentiate into mature tissue-resident macrophages (RTMs) and for mature RTMs to persist.

## Evidence summary

Central finding of [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Carrizo et al., *Nature* 2026), supported by constitutive and inducible deletion, multi-tissue phenotyping, scRNA-seq, proteomics and ribosome profiling. Mechanistically tied to [[foundations/deoxyhypusine-synthase-dhps]], [[foundations/eif5a-hypusine]] and [[concepts/polyamine-hypusine-axis-macrophage-residency]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse models; steady state (not damage). Human relevance inferred, not directly tested.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper.
