## Statement
Gene expression scales with enhancer state in human macrophages: strong > weak > poised enhancers, consistent with the general chromatin-activity model.

## Evidence summary
Average expression ordering Es > Ew > Ep held for every activation state; active promoters likewise outperformed poised promoters.

## Conditions and scope
Correlative link between chromatin state and steady-state mRNA.

## Counter-evidence
The activation TR network is an exception where expression does not track chromatin state (see [[claims/tr-network-gene-expression-stimulus-specific]]).

## Open questions
