## Statement

Engagement of TYRO3/AXL/MERTK by PS-bound bridging ligands (GAS6, PROS1) during efferocytosis biases macrophages toward anti-inflammatory, immunosuppressive output — a homeostatic clearance program co-opted by tumors.
