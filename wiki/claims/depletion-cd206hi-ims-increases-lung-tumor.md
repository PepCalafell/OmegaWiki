## Statement

DT-mediated CD206hi IM depletion increased tumor burden ~3.7-fold (B16F10 melanoma), 7.2-fold (KPAR1.3 adenocarcinoma) and 2.3-fold (spontaneous AgerCreERT2KP adenocarcinoma) relative to Cx3cr1DTR controls.

## Evidence summary

Tumor quantification across models (Fig. 2b-d); fold changes reported with P<0.0001-0.0008. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung cancer models; DT on days 3 and 7 (or weekly in autochthonous model).

None recorded at ingest.
