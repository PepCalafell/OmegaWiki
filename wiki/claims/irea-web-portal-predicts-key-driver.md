## Statement

Through the IREA web portal a researcher can enter a list of genes involved in an immune process and receive, within minutes, enrichment-based predictions of the key cytokines driving that process — without local installation or coding.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/immune-dictionary-immune-response-enrichment-analysis]] (Lai, …, Cui; AAI 2025 abstract). This is the on-demand, browser-based realization of the IREA inference method [[foundations/irea-immune-response-enrichment-analysis-software]].

## Conditions and scope

Gene-list input; cytokine-driver enrichment against the mouse lymph-node Immune Dictionary signatures.
