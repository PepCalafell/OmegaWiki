## Statement

In melanoma spatial communities, regulatory T cells and fibroblasts have a high probability of co-occurring with melanocytes, defining a melanocyte–fibroblast–Treg colocalized microenvironment.

## Evidence summary

Colocalization probabilities across three Xenium melanoma samples, corroborated by CODEX/Xenium cross-modality community analysis. (p.9)

## Conditions and scope

Melanoma only; few samples.

## Counter-evidence

Colocalization is statistical co-occurrence, not proof of functional interaction.

## Open questions

Whether the Treg-fibroblast-melanocyte niche is immunosuppressive and outcome-related.
