## Statement

Among TAM functional states, only the ISG (interferon-stimulated-gene) signature — not angiogenic or MHC-II — consistently correlates with the effector CD8+ T-cell signature (GZMB, GZMA, PRF1, IFNG, TBX21) across multiple cancer cohorts.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2071) — correlational evidence. Linear regression of patient-level signature averages across NSCLC, HNSCC, non-Hodgkin lymphoma scRNA-seq datasets.
  - Quote: "Neither the angiogenic nor the MHC-II+ phenotypes correlated with the effector CD8+ T-cell signature. In contrast, the ISG signature in TAMs consistently exhibited a high correlation with the effector"

## Conditions and scope

Cross-cohort correlational analysis; association not causation.
