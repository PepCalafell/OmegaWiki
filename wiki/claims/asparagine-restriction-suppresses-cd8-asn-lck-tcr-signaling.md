## Statement

Asparagine directly enhances CD8 T-cell activation by binding the proximal TCR tyrosine kinase LCK and promoting its phosphorylation. Asn restriction (dietary or via L-asparaginase) impairs CD8 effector function; prolonged restriction induces NRF2-mediated adaptive restoration of activation.

## Evidence summary

- Recent CD8 T-cell amino-acid contribution studies reviewed in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- CD8 activation models in vitro and in vivo; informs ASNase off-target effects.

## Counter-evidence

- Adaptive NRF2 response partially mitigates Asn restriction over time.

## Linked ideas

- Trade-off between ASNase tumour benefit and T-cell impairment.

## Open questions

- Whether NRF2 adaptation is sufficient to preserve antitumour immunity in patients on ASNase.
