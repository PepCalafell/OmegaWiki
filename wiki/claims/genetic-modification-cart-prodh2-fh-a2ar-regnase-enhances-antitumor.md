## Statement

Knockout of Regnase-1 and overexpression of PRODH2/PGC1α promote mitochondrial OxPhos and memory formation in CAR-T cells; overexpressing fumarate hydratase (FH) and knocking down the adenosine A2A receptor (A2AR) alleviates the detrimental effects of tumour-derived fumarate and adenosine on CAR-T cells; modulation of arginine-pathway genes (ASS, OTC, ARG1/2) sustains arginine availability for cytotoxic CAR-T cells.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple genetic-screen and hypothesis-driven CRISPR studies summarised in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Preclinical CAR-T gene-edited models; subset of edits at clinical-trial stage.

## Counter-evidence

- Genetic CAR-T engineering is slower and costlier to validate clinically than pharmacological conditioning.

## Linked ideas

- Combinatorial editing with metabolic conditioning to overcome solid-tumour barriers.

## Open questions

- Which edit combinations deliver durable solid-tumour CAR-T benefit in patients?
