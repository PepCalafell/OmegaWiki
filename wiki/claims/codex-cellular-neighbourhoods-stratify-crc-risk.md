## Statement

CODEX-defined cellular neighbourhoods in CRC FFPE samples stratify patients for tumour progression risk and predict immunotherapy response.

## Evidence summary

CODEX imaging plus computational analysis defined cellular neighbourhoods stratifying CRC patients for tumour-progression risk; cellular topography uncovered by CODEX predicts immune therapy response (Polak et al. 2024, p.530; Schürch et al. 2020).

## Conditions and scope
