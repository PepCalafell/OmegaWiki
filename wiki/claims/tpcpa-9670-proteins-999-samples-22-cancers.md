## Statement
The Pan-Cancer Proteome Atlas (TPCPA) is a DIA-MS-based dataset of 9,670 quantified proteins across 999 primary cancer samples spanning 22 cancer types (18 solid + 4 liquid), generated by single-shot LC-MS without sample multiplexing.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Cancer Cell — primary descriptor paper for the dataset.
- 11,250 protein groups identified, 9,670 quantified post-filtering.
- Sample composition includes both fresh-frozen and FFPE inputs across two acquisition laboratories.

## Conditions and scope
- Bulk tissue, primary cancers only; metastatic and adjacent-normal samples filtered out.
- Single-shot DIA-MS (no TMT multiplexing).

## Counter-evidence
None within scope; this is a dataset descriptor claim.
