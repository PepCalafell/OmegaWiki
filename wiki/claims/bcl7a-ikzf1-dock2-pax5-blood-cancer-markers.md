## Statement
Pan-cancer DE identifies BCL7A, IKZF1, SEPTIN6, DOCK2, PTPRC, PAX5 and PASK among the top blood-cancer-enriched proteins; most are established cancer genes, validating the discovery pipeline and surfacing PASK as a metabolism/differentiation candidate.

- Compared against all solid cancers, not stratified by hematologic subtype.
