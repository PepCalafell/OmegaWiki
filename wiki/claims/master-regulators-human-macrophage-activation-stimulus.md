## Statement
Each macrophage activation condition is governed by a distinct set of predicted master-regulator TFs that combine enriched expression with predicted binding to accessible network elements.

## Evidence summary
STAT1/IRF1 (MIFNγ), IRF4 (MIL4), STAT4/ETS2 (MTPP) emerged as stimulus-specific master regulators, consistent with known signaling pathways.

## Conditions and scope
Computational prediction; not validated by perturbation in this study.
