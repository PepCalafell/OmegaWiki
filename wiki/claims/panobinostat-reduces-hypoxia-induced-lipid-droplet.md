## Statement

The pan-HDAC inhibitor panobinostat reduces hypoxia-induced lipid droplet accumulation in microglia and macrophages, demonstrating that the hypoxic lipid-laden phenotype is epigenetically (HDAC/H3K27ac) controlled and reversible.

## Evidence summary

BODIPY lipid-droplet quantitation with panobinostat (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.14).

> "Consistent with gene expression data, panobinostat impaired LD formation in hypoxia ... These findings underscore the link between hypoxia-driven epigenomic regulation and GAM functions"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 and BMDM; in vitro hypoxia + panobinostat.

## Counter-evidence

Pan-HDAC inhibition is non-specific; in vivo relevance not tested.

## Open questions

Whether reducing lipid droplets via HDAC inhibition reduces tumor-supportive GAM function in vivo.
