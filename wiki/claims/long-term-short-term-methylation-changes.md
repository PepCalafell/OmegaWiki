## Statement

The trained-immunity-related methylation signal is carried by long-term (T90–T0) changes rather than the transient vaccine-phase or recovery-phase changes.

## Evidence summary

Only the long-term DNAm-C sites that associate with cytokines also change upon BCG, supporting long-term epigenetic changes as the substrate of immune memory.

## Conditions and scope

Overlap analysis at nominal significance.

## Counter-evidence

Small overlap counts; suggestive thresholds.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether the long-term sites are necessary for the trained phenotype.
