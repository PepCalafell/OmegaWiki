## Statement

Whereas KO of TEXterm single-state TFs (Hic1, Zscan20, Zfp324, Jdp2) preferentially expands the PD1+CX3CR1+ TEXeff/effector-like population, KO of `Prdm1` or `Stat3` instead expands the PD1+CX3CR1−SLAMF6+ TEXprog progenitor population and upregulates TEXprog-signature genes — distinguishing routes by which different multi-state vs single-state TF KOs reroute the exhaustion trajectory.
