## Statement

Replicate concordance for spatial-DMT in mouse E11 embryo at 50 μm pixel size is Pearson r=0.9836 (DNAm) and r=0.9752 (RNA) between matched body parts of two technical replicates.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Extended Data Fig. 2a–f.

## Conditions and scope

Same chip-design, same lab, same staging (E11), 50 μm pixel size. Inter-laboratory reproducibility not yet reported.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.

## Open questions
