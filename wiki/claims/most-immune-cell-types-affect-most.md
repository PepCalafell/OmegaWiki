## Statement

The cell-cell interactome inferred from the Immune Dictionary shows that most immune cell types can affect almost every other cell type through at least one cytokine, with exceptions only for unstimulated B and T cells in this dataset.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lymph node, baseline + cytokine-perturbed conditions.
