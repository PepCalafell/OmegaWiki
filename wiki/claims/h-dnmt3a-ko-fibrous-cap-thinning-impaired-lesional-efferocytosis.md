## Statement

DNMT3A is required for two functional features of plaque stability in atherosclerosis — fibrous cap thickness and lesional efferocytosis — providing a candidate mechanism linking DNMT3A CHIP mutations to coronary artery disease risk.

## Evidence summary

Histomorphometry on aortic root sections. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

Ldlr-/- WTD atherosclerosis; aortic root only.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
