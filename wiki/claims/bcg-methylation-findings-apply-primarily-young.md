## Statement

The BCG-induced methylation signatures are most representative of young adults (<30); the effect attenuates in older participants.

## Evidence summary

Subgroup analysis shows reduced significance in older participants, bounding the generalizability of the findings by age.

## Conditions and scope

Age-stratified sensitivity analysis; cohort skewed young.

## Counter-evidence

Small older subgroup limits power to detect age-specific effects.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Age-dependent methylation responses to BCG in older populations.
