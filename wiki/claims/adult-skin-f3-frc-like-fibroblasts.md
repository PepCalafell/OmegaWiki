## Statement

Adult skin F3 FRC-like fibroblasts may derive from a prenatal lymphoid-tissue-organizer (LTo) program: prenatal skin CCL19+ fibroblasts resemble adult F3 and co-cluster with intestinal mesenchymal LTo cells, and the silent LTo program can be re-activated in disease (notably hidradenitis suppurativa).

## Evidence summary

Adult-prenatal-intestine integration and LTo-marker expression (Fig. 8); disease-specific LTo reactivation.

## Conditions and scope

Correlative/integrative; developmental hypothesis.

## Counter-evidence

No direct lineage tracing of prenatal-to-adult transition.
