## Statement

The growth-suppressive consequence of G6PD/PPP inhibition by Octyl itaconate is driven primarily by reduced ribose-5-phosphate availability for nucleotide biosynthesis rather than by NADPH/redox depletion.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1468) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "Overexpression of TKT to increase non-oxidative PPP flux partially restored proliferation, which indicated that reduced ribose production rather than NADPH depletion is the primary driver of growth suppression."

## Conditions and scope

A549; only partial rescue by TKT, so redox stress ([[claims/octyl-itaconate-lowers-glutathione-gsh-gssg]]) still contributes a parallel arm.

## Counter-evidence

Partial (not full) TKT rescue leaves room for a NADPH contribution; confidence moderate. Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
