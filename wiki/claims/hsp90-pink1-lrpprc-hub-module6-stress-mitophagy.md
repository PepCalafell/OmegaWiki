## Statement
TPCPA module 6, prominent in colon cancers but more widely expressed, is enriched for stress and chaperone biology, with HSP90AA1, HSP90AB1, PMPCB, LRPPRC and PINK1 as hub proteins, connecting cytosolic stress-response chaperones to mitochondrial protein import and mitophagy. All five are nominated as potential drug targets.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figure 3 and discussion of module 6.
- HSP90 inhibition is an established anticancer approach (refs 49–50).
- PINK1 is regulated by mitochondrial import machinery including HSP90α and PMPCB (refs 54–58).

## Conditions and scope
- Hub identity is correlation-based on bulk DIA-MS; functional dependency is not tested in this paper.

## Counter-evidence
- Mitophagy can be pro- or anti-tumorigenic depending on context; module-level evidence does not specify direction.
