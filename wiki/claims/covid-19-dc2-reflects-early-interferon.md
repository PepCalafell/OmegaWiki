## Statement

The time-since-onset axis (DC2) in COVID-19 is dominated by interferon-β-stimulated genes, reflecting an interferon response that is rapidly induced at disease onset and subsequently dampens over time.

## Evidence summary

Figure 2h of [[reconstructing-developmental-disease-progression-sample-level]], from trajectory-based NB-GLM DE on CD14 monocytes ordered along DC2.

## Conditions and scope

Primarily characterized in CD14 monocytes; independent of severity.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
