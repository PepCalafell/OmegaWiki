## Statement

RNA-inferred CNV analysis showed G09 uniquely correlated with chromosome 1q copy-number gains; the peak region contained 10 genes including ADAM15 and EFNA1 (ECM remodeling/EMT). Tumors with higher 1q CNV scores harbored more LRRC15+ myCAFs across multiple cancer types, independently validated in TCGA, suggesting cancer-cell genetics may shape the G09 TME.

## Evidence summary

RNA-inferred CNV correlation with TCGA validation. (p.9) Quote: "G09 exhibited significant correlations with CNVs on chromosome 1q".

## Conditions and scope

Correlational link between inferred CNV and CAF abundance; causality not established.
