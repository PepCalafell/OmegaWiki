## Statement

Terminal F7 myofibroblasts are predicted to form via two routes: a direct path from the F2 universal lineage and an indirect path from F1 superficial fibroblasts through an intermediate F6 inflammatory-myofibroblast state.

## Evidence summary

PAGA, RNA velocity, CellRank2 (Fig. 5a–c); consistent with in vivo mouse lineage tracing.

## Conditions and scope

Inferred from snapshots; trajectory methods limited for convergent fates.

## Counter-evidence

Authors note trajectory inference cannot resolve multiple states converging to one phenotype; lineage plasticity likely.

## Open questions

Direct human lineage tracing of the F1→F6→F7 route.
