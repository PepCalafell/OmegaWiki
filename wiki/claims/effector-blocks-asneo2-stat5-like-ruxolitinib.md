## Statement

ASNeo2 activates STAT5 phosphorylation in human NK cells (YT-1), and adding the effector immediately halts pSTAT5 accumulation and gradually reduces it to a low level — blocking ASNeo2 activity nearly as effectively as the JAK inhibitor ruxolitinib, but acting at the receptor (ligand dissociation) rather than on JAK kinase activity.

## Evidence summary

"After the effector is added, STAT5 phosphorylation immediately stops accumulating and gradually decreases to a low level. The effector blocks ASNeo2 activity nearly as effectively as does ruxolitinib, a JAK1 inhibitor" (p.6).

## Conditions and scope

YT-1 NK cell line; pSTAT5 dose–response and time courses.

## Counter-evidence

Ruxolitinib acts pathway-wide; the effector is ligand-specific — equivalence is in pSTAT5 shutdown magnitude, not mechanism.
