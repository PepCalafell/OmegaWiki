## Statement

In nine independent LUAD bulk-transcriptomic cohorts the response-to-hypoxia gene signature correlates strongly with the tumour-associated neutrophil (TAN) signature but shows little correlation with the normal adjacent neutrophil (NAN) signature.

## Open questions

- Does the same selectivity hold for LUSC or for non-lung adenocarcinomas with established hypoxic niches (prostate, breast)?
