## Statement

SARS-CoV-2 hijacks the host hypoxia response: viral ORF3a triggers mitochondrial ROS generation that stabilizes HIF-1α (via PHD inhibition), and stabilized HIF-1α reciprocally promotes viral replication and induces pro-inflammatory cytokine expression, contributing to the cytokine storm phase of severe COVID-19. HIF-1α similarly facilitates HSV-1 and VSV infections, suggesting a generalizable hypoxia-virus replication co-option.

## Evidence summary

Luo Lab's own work (cited as ref 147) demonstrating ORF3a-HIF-1α coupling. Codo et al. independently confirmed mitochondrial ROS-HIF-1α-replication axis in monocytes.

## Conditions and scope

- Severe COVID-19 with monocyte/macrophage hyperactivation
- Counter-finding: Prieto-Fernández et al. showed hypoxia reduces SARS-CoV-2 binding by lowering ACE2/NRP1/HS — entry vs replication may diverge

## Counter-evidence

Hypoxia may reduce viral entry via ACE2 downregulation even while HIF-1α facilitates intracellular replication post-entry — entry vs replication dissociation.
