## Statement

Upon detection of fungal-PAMP cargo, LPMs physically recruit mitochondria to phagosomes; glutaminolysis drives ETC complex II to fuel mtROS and the respiratory burst. The pathway couples cellular metabolism directly to antimicrobial output.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] (Geric 2019).

## Conditions and scope

- Yeast/zymosan stimulation; effect across pathogen panels not fully tested.
