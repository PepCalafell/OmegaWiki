## Statement

The chemoattractant in the sympathetic-eosinophil axis is neuronal CCL11 acting on eosinophil CCR3. The Pdyn+ sympathetic neuron is the chemokine source — not the canonical epithelial / Th2-derived CCL11 source.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], Fig. 5A–D, fig. S15.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only; in vitro chemotaxis. In vivo CCL11 source localisation by IHC on PdynNet-TD skin.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
