## Statement

Genes most associated with the inferred differentiation rate correspond to known lineage-defining regulators, validating the biological meaningfulness of the estimated differentiation parameters.

## Evidence summary

Recovery of textbook markers (Pf4/Gata1, Klf1/Gata1, Cebpa/Cebpe) provides face validity for the drift-association test.

## Conditions and scope

Associations, not proven regulatory causality; single dataset.

## Counter-evidence

Authors acknowledge findings are associations that may lack regulatory causality.
