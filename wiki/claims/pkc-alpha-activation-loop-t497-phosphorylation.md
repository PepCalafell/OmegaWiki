## Statement
The activation-loop residue T497 of PKCα (PRKCA) shows higher phosphorylation in both M2a and M2c macrophages compared with M1, consistent with increased PKCα catalytic competence in immunosuppressive states.

## Evidence summary
- "the activation residue of the PKCα kinase (aka PRKCA), T497, had higher phosphorylation levels in both M2 states when compared to M1. The PKCα kinase is known to play a role in anti-inflammatory processes and ... negatively regulate the NF-κB induced genes" (p.11).
- Activation-loop phosphorylation is "often sufficient for the kinase to switch to its active state" (p.11).

## Conditions and scope
Direct phosphosite evidence (stronger than footprint inference) localized to a known regulatory residue; in vitro macrophages.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
