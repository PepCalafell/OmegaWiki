## Statement

Oleic acid uptake fuels FAO and OXPHOS in human Treg, raising Foxp3 expression and STAT5 phosphorylation in a positive-feedback loop that stabilizes Treg suppressive function.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] reviewing primary human Treg-metabolism studies.
