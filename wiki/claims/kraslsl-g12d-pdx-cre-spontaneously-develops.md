## Statement
KrasLSL-G12D/+;PDX-Cre or KrasLSL-G12D/+;p48-Cre mice spontaneously develop early PanINs, a low frequency of which progress to PDAC.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
