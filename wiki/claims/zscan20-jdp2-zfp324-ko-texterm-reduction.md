## Statement

CRISPR KO of three TEXterm single-state TF genes — `Zfp324`, `Zscan20`, and `Jdp2` — reduces TEXterm cell representation by 78%, 54%, and 43% respectively in chronic LCMV-Clone 13 Perturb-seq.
