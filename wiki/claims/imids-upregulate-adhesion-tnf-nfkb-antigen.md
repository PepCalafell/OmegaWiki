## Statement

Immune-mediated inflammatory diseases (IMIDs) showed the characteristic upregulation of adhesion-molecule signatures, TNF-via-NFκB signaling, and antigen cross-presentation/antigen-presenting signatures in circulating immune cells.

## Evidence summary

Heatmap of corrected signature activity across diseases/cell types (Fig. 2a; p.634), recapitulating known IMID inflammatory biology.

## Conditions and scope

IMID group; signature-level activity; magnitude varies by disease and cell type.

## Counter-evidence

IFN type 1/2 signatures were instead downregulated in most IMIDs.
