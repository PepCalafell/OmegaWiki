## Statement

Microfluidic MOS reaggregation of bulk colorectal tumours preserves infiltrating immune populations and correlates in vitro and clinical responses to anti-PD1 and bispecifics.

## Evidence summary

Microfluidic reaggregation of dissociated bulk colorectal tumours into MOSs, preserving infiltrating immune populations, exhibited a correlation between in vitro and clinical responses (Polak et al. 2024, p.527/531; Wang et al. 2022).

## Conditions and scope

Single-cell-suspension-derived MOS from colorectal cancer; anti-PD1 and bispecific antibody testing.
