## Statement

The designed steric clash in the strained ternary complex is resolved by strain distributed across multiple structural locations: the binder fusion bends, the directly clashing portion of the target becomes disordered, and the target twists at its interface, disrupting interfacial hydrophobic packing and shearing the interfacial strand pairing.

## Evidence summary

"the binder fusion bends, the portion of the target that directly clashes with the switch becomes disordered and the target twists at its interface, disrupting the interfacial hydrophobic packing ... and shearing the interfacial strand pairing" (p.4). DEER spectroscopy and MD simulations indicate the ternary complex is dynamic with varying strain at each location.

## Conditions and scope

AS1 ternary complex; crystallography + DEER + MD.

## Counter-evidence

Strain distribution is system-specific; magnitude/direction vary across designed variants.
