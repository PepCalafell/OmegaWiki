## Statement

ELISA of lung tissue showed ~2.7-fold (CXCL9), 2.6-fold (CXCL10) and 1.5-fold (CXCL13) reductions in CD206hi IM-depleted Pf4CreCx3cr1DTR mice vs controls.

## Evidence summary

ELISA at day 16 post-tumor inoculation in KPAR1.3 model (Fig. 2h); P<0.0001, P<0.0001, P=0.0398. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse KPAR1.3 lung adenocarcinoma.
