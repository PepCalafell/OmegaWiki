## Statement

Among circulating immune subsets, CD14 monocytes undergo the most extensive transcriptional remodeling in response to interferons, for both IFN-I and IFN-II.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.3, p.5-6): "CD14 monocytes exhibiting the most extensive transcriptional remodeling"; monocytes segregate distantly on PCA and show the largest IFN-γ DEG count. See [[core-versus-subset-specific-isg-programs]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating blood monocytes; in-vitro stimulation.

## Counter-evidence

T cells and plasma cells showed only modest responses, i.e., the effect is lineage-specific.

## Open questions
