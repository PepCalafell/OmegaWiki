## Statement
In brain-metastasis tumors, the fraction of proinflammatory (M1-like) macrophages correlates strongly with the fraction of B cells among immune cells (Spearman ρ = 0.91).

## Evidence summary
- "in the set of BrM samples, we found a strong correlation between the fraction of proinflammatory macrophages and B cells (Spearman ρ = 0.91, P < 2.1 × 10⁻⁶)" (p.14).
- Melanoma-origin BrM (often less immunogenic) showed a higher fraction of proinflammatory macrophages (>33%) than breast/colorectal/lung tumors (p.14).

## Conditions and scope
Small cohort (15 BrM samples); correlational; macrophage proinflammatory status assigned via the proteomic signature.

## Counter-evidence
Authors note the sample size was too small to statistically evaluate the tumor-of-origin observation; B-cell prognostic value is context-dependent (can be favorable or a risk factor).

## Open questions
Is the macrophage–B-cell association mechanistic (e.g. shared inflammatory niche) or incidental, and does it predict immunotherapy response?
