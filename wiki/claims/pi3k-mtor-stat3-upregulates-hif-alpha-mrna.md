## Statement

PI3K → AKT → mTOR (mTORC1) signaling acts upstream of HIF-α at the mRNA level. mTORC1-mediated phosphorylation of STAT3 in hypoxia induces HIF-1α transcription. In cancer, this axis is constitutively elevated and drives angiogenic gene expression. In activated T cells, mTOR-HIF signaling supports glycolytic reprogramming and survival.

## Evidence summary

Multiple primary papers integrated by Luo et al. 2022. The PI3K-mTOR-HIF axis is one of the most consistent cross-talks observed across cancer cell lines, immune cells, and pathological hypoxia models.

## Conditions and scope

- Requires intact PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling
- Often co-active with NF-κB and ERK arms (parallel inputs to HIF-α)
- Modulated by negative regulators PTEN, TSC1/2, REDD1

## Counter-evidence

Cell-type-dependent: rapamycin inhibition does not always fully suppress hypoxia-induced HIF-1α accumulation, indicating mTOR is one of several inputs rather than strictly necessary.
