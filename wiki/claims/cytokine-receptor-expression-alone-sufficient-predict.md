## Statement

Some cytokines (IL-1α, IL-1β) induce responses in T cells, NK cells and DCs without high receptor transcript detection; conversely, some cells with detectable receptors show no response after anti-PD-1 — receptor presence is therefore necessary but insufficient.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse in vivo; verified across multiple cytokine × cell-type combinations.
