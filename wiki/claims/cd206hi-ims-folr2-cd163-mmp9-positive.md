## Statement

CD206hi IMs coexpress Folr2, Cd163, Mmp9 (and Pf4, with a Lyve1+ fraction), while CD206lo IMs express Tmem119, Mmp12, Mmp13 and Ccr2; recMacs are Ly6c2+Vcan+Thbs1+Fn1+Ccr2-high.

## Evidence summary

scRNA-seq feature plots and DEGs (Fig. 1c-e) define the marker profiles distinguishing the subsets. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; markers from murine scRNA-seq.
