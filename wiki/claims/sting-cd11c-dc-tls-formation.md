## Statement

Host STING activation, particularly in CD11c+ DCs, is required for tumor-associated tertiary lymphoid structure formation.

## Evidence summary

Chelvanambi 2021 (melanoma), Zhao 2024 Sci Immunol (lung cancer with HSC cGAS overexpression), Sawada 2025 (pancreatic cancer with STING + LTβR co-activation). TLS formation entirely dependent on host STING within CD11c+ DCs.

## Conditions and scope

Murine models; human validation pending. TLS presence correlates with survival in human cohorts.
