## Statement

PDOTS-based screens identify CDK4, CDK6 and TBK1 inhibitors as enhancers of the ICB response in melanoma and MSI-high CRC.

## Evidence summary

Utilizing PDOTS identified inhibitors of CDK4, CDK6 and TBK1 that enhanced the ICB response (Polak et al. 2024, p.527; refs 148-150).

## Conditions and scope

PDOTS small-molecule + ICB combination screens; effects observed in melanoma and MSI-high colorectal cancer.
