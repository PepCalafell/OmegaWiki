## Statement

MiTE-144 ex vivo on RCC PDTFs upregulates a coordinated cytotoxic/proliferation program (PRF1, GZMA/B/H, MCM3/4/6, CCND2) and downregulates exhaustion-associated genes (CXCR4, DUSP1, TNFAIP3, TOX) in both T and NK cells, with CITE-seq protein-level confirmation of CD25/CD69/NKG2D activation markers.
