## Statement

TREM2 macs engaged in direct spatial contact with PD-1hi CD8 T cells upregulate MHC class II antigen-presentation molecules (HLA-DQA1, -DQB1, -DRB1, -DRA), the IFN-γ receptor IFNGR1, and the T-cell costimulatory molecule CD86 — consistent with enhanced antigen-presenting and stimulatory function in contact-engaged TREM2 macs. Distal TREM2 macs instead drift toward alternative-mac markers (MARCO, FCN1).

## Evidence summary

- Proximity-conditional DE on TREM2 macs stratified by contact with PD-1hi CD8.
- Coordinated upregulation of MHC-II + IFNGR1 + CD86.

## Conditions and scope

- Correlational.

## Counter-evidence

- Could reflect ongoing T-cell-derived IFN-γ inducing MHC-II in already-proximal TREM2 macs (downstream consequence rather than cause).
