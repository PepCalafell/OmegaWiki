## Statement

MDSC-expressed ARG1 catabolises L-arginine, locally depleting the TME and suppressing T cells. Inhibition of ARG restores arginine levels and improves T-cell function in preclinical models. However, systemic arginine depletion via PEGylated arginine deiminase (ADI-PEG20) has not improved overall survival in hepatocellular carcinoma, partly because it also strips arginine from tumour-infiltrating T cells.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple preclinical ARG inhibition studies (CB-1158/INCB001158, OATD-02).
- ADI-PEG20 hepatocellular carcinoma trial — null result.

## Conditions and scope

- MDSC-rich solid tumours.

## Counter-evidence

- Some arginine-auxotrophic tumours benefit from systemic depletion.

## Linked ideas

- Tumour-selective vs immune-cell-sparing arginine modulation.

## Open questions

- Local delivery of ARG inhibitors to TME without systemic arginine perturbation.
