## Statement

Type 2B ccRCC VHL missense mutants (except R167Q) gain enhanced mitochondrial enrichment, stronger VHL–MCCC2 association, greater leucine-driven glutaminolysis, and faster tumor growth; TCGA missense-VHL tumors progress faster than truncating ones — a HIF-independent metabolic gain-of-function.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 7H–N, S10D–K). Mutant panels, xenografts, TCGA-KIRC correlations.

## Conditions and scope

Clinical evidence is correlative (TCGA, n≈109/group) plus xenografts; no dedicated patient trial. R167Q is an exception.

## Counter-evidence

R167Q (type 2B) does not show the enhanced mitochondrial gain.
