## Statement

Three subsets of TAMs — TREM2-expressing, SPP1-expressing, and CXCL9/10-expressing — define the most commonly recurring states across tumors of different tissue types.

## Evidence summary

Supported by convergent pan-cancer single-cell evidence reviewed by Sun et al. 2026, building on prior atlases (Park et al. 2022, Cheng et al. 2021).

## Conditions and scope

Pan-solid-tumor observation. Authors caution that molecularly similar macrophages can have different roles depending on cancer type, stage, and therapy — three-state binning is a useful but reductive description of an underlying spectrum.
