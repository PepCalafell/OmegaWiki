## Statement

EPAS1 gain-of-function mutation functionally lowers cellular oxygen consumption via COX4I2 induction, supporting survival under hypoxia by matching ETC activity to oxygen supply.

## Evidence summary

Seahorse extracellular-flux assay (see [[foundations/seahorse-extracellular-flux-analyzer]]); mechanism in [[concepts/cox4i2-isoform-switch-balances-oxygen-consumption]].

## Conditions and scope

Engineered HEK293 model with double ODD mutant; effect statistically strong.

## Counter-evidence

In-vivo tumor contribution inferred, not directly measured.
