## Statement

Lactic acid and PGE2 antagonize GM-CSF: of 969 GM-CSF-induced genes, lactate downregulates 581 and PGE2 downregulates 474, including MHC-II genes Cd74 and Ciita, while each further upregulates only ~48-52.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2065) — quantitative evidence. RNA-seq combinatorial DEG analysis in BMDMs; asymmetry (581/474 down vs 52/48 up) quantifies the antagonism.
  - Quote: "among GM-CSF-induced genes (n = 969), lactic acid and PGE2 downregulated the majority (581 and 474, respectively), including MHC-II-associated genes (e.g., Cd74 and Ciita)"

## Conditions and scope
