## Statement

Efferoptotic death and IL-1β release proceed independently of NLRP3 and caspase-1.

## Evidence summary

Genetic KO controls for canonical inflammasome. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "Nlrp3-/- and Casp1-/- macrophages were equally susceptible to cell death as WT macrophages"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.
