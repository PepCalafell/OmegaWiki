## Statement

Knocking out Ldha and Cox2 (the enzymes producing lactic acid and PGE2) in tumor cells increases MHC-II+ TAMs and decreases angiogenic (CX3CR1-MHC-II-) TAMs in both 4T1 and MC38 tumors in vivo.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2067) — mechanistic evidence. Ldha/Cox2 double-KO 4T1 and MC38; metabolite-confirmed loss; FACS TAM subset quantification (n=6).
  - Quote: "in both the 4T1 and MC38 models, dKO tumors showed increased MHC-II+ TAMs and decreased angiogenic (CX3CR1MHC-II) TAMs compared with controls, demonstrating that tumor-intrinsic LP production critically dictates TAM subsets"

## Conditions and scope

Two syngeneic models; tumor-intrinsic double knockout.
