## Statement

The prognostic direction of ABCG2 (itaconate exporter) flips by cancer type — protective (HR<1) in lung and breast, adverse (HR>1) in colorectal and gastric — mirroring the tissue-dependent anti- vs pro-tumor role of itaconate.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1472) — correlational evidence.
  - Quote: "ABCG2 showed favorable prognostic associations (hazard ratio [HR] < 1) in lung and breast cancers ... In contrast, ABCG2 was correlated with poor prognosis (HR > 1) in colorectal and gastric cancers."

## Conditions and scope

Expression-survival associations across cancer types; ABCG2 is a surrogate for itaconate-export capacity, not itaconate itself.

## Counter-evidence

Correlative, uses a transporter proxy; interpretation depends on the broader context-dependence model. Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
