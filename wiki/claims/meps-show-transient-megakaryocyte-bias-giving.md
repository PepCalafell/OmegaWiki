## Statement

Density-transport analysis of megakaryocyte-erythroid progenitors reveals a transient megakaryocyte output bias (Day 12–27) that resolves into balanced erythroid–megakaryocyte output from Day 49, mirroring the system-wide MK-to-balanced shift.

## Evidence summary

CDT plus CellRank velocity-kernel fate probabilities and early/late MEP signature differences provide convergent support.

## Conditions and scope

Model-inferred on a retrained Meg–Ery subset; single dataset.

## Counter-evidence

Early dynamics may be partly tamoxifen-influenced.
