## Statement

Il1rl1/ST2 is among the ribosome-depleted transcripts; reduced ST2 protein blunts IL-33-induced alternative activation, plausibly contributing to the peritoneal monocyte-to-RTM defect.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 4b,c); links [[foundations/st2-il1rl1-il33-receptor]] to [[foundations/eif5a-hypusine]].

## Conditions and scope

ST2's contribution appears peritoneum-specific; authors note it may not drive the transition in all tissues.

## Counter-evidence

Multiple transcripts, not ST2 alone, are implicated; tissue-specificity limits generality.
