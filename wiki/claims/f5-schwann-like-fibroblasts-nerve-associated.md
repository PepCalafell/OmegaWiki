## Statement

F5 Schwann-like fibroblasts (SCN7A, FMO2, FGFBP2, OLFML2A) are nerve-associated: the NGFR+ subcluster is perineural and colocalizes with Schwann cells, while the RAMP1+ subcluster is enriched near innervated eccrine glands.

## Evidence summary

Spatial colocalization and CGRP-receptor gene expression (Fig. 2b,c; Extended Data Figs).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy skin.
