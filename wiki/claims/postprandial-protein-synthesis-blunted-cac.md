## Statement

Muscle protein synthesis is blunted in response to feeding (anabolic resistance) in patients with colorectal- and pancreatic-cancer-associated cachexia, in contrast to healthy controls in whom postprandial protein synthesis is normally stimulated. This anabolic resistance rationalizes the consistent failure of nutritional-support-only interventions to reverse muscle loss in CAC.

## Evidence summary

Stable-isotope amino acid tracer studies in CRC and PDAC CAC patients vs healthy controls.

## Conditions and scope

CRC, PDAC; mechanistically plausible across other CAC.

## Counter-evidence

None directly contradicting; some early-CAC cohorts may retain partial anabolic response.

## Linked ideas

- Motivates combining nutrition with exercise and/or anabolic drugs.

## Open questions

- Mechanism of anabolic resistance (mTOR signaling? insulin resistance? amino acid uptake?).
