## Statement

DECODE builds its labelled training set by drawing cell-type proportion vectors from a uniform distribution, sampling cells from single-cell reference data accordingly at a preset total count, and aggregating their profiles into pseudotissue samples with known composition.

## Evidence summary

"A random cell-type proportion vector for all target cell types is drawn from a uniform distribution... cells are randomly sampled... and their molecular profiles are aggregated to form one pseudotissue sample. Repeating this procedure multiple times yields the DECODE training set" (p.597–598).
