## Statement

In human GBM (IvyGAP), LGALS3 expression is highest in hypoxic perinecrotic and pseudopalisading regions — mirroring hypoxia markers SLC2A1/VEGFA — while TMEM119 and P2RY12 are lowest there and highest in the infiltrating tumor and leading edge.

## Evidence summary

Spatially resolved regional expression in human GBM (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.5).

> "LGALS3 expression was highest in the perinecrotic and pseudopalisading regions (CTpnz and CTpcan), similar to classic hypoxia markers SLC2A1 or VEGFA ... TMEM119 and P2RY12 expression was lowest in these hypoxic areas and highest in the IT and LE"

## Conditions and scope

Human GBM; bulk microdissected anatomic regions (IvyGAP).

## Counter-evidence

Bulk regional signal conflates cell-type composition with per-cell expression; authors acknowledge this.
