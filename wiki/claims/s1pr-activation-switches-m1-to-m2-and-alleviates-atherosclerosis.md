## Statement

S1P engagement of S1PR family — with a specific S1PR2 requirement in peritoneal macrophages — reduces TNF-α/IL-6 output, switches macrophages from M1 to M2 phenotype, and attenuates atherosclerotic-lesion progression in LDLR⁻/⁻ mice.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from atherosclerosis-macrophage S1P literature.

## Conditions and scope

- Murine LDLR⁻/⁻ atherosclerosis; pharmacological SEW2871 agonism.

## Counter-evidence

- Other compartments (T cells, neutrophils) experience S1P primarily through S1PR1 — receptor-specific effects are tissue-dependent.

## Open questions

- Whether selective S1PR2 modulation can repolarize tumour-associated macrophages.
