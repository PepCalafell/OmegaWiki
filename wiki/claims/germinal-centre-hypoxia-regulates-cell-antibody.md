## Statement

Physiological oxygen gradients in germinal centres — deepening as B cells clonally expand — regulate B cell proliferation, phenotype and antibody quality, including effects on immunoglobulin class switching (direction of which remains debated).

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Cho et al. *Nature* 2016 and Abbott et al. 2016).

## Conditions and scope

GC light-zone hypoxia derives from oxygen consumption during DC–B cell interactions.

## Counter-evidence

Reports disagree on whether GC hypoxia decreases or potentiates class-switch recombination.
