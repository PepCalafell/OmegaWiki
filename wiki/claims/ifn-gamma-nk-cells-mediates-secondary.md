## Statement

Monocyte responses to IL-2/IL-12/IL-15 — despite low primary receptor expression — are explained by a secondary cascade: NK CD56hi cells produce IFN-γ in response to the primary cytokine, and the monocyte profile matches its direct IFN-γ response (Pearson r>0.5).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: mechanistic.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
