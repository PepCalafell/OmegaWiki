## Statement

Acute chemogenetic activation of eosinophils alone is sufficient to trigger increased scratching behaviour in AD-like mice, demonstrating eosinophil-intrinsic capacity to drive itch.

## Conditions and scope

Sufficiency for itch only; long-term dermatitis severity not tested in this experiment.
