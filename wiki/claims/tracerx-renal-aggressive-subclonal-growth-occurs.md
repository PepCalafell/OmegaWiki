## Statement

Spatial mapping of genomic alterations onto whole-tumour images in ccRCC (TRACERx Renal Consortium) shows aggressive subclonal growth localized to peri-necrotic (hypoxic) tumour centres, with elevated somatic CNAs, higher proliferation and higher tumour grade in those regions.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Clear-cell renal cell carcinoma; TRACERx Renal whole-tumour spatial sampling.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
