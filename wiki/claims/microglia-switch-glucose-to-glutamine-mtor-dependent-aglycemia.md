## Statement

Under glucose deprivation, microglia preserve OXPHOS by switching to glutamine as the primary fuel, in an mTOR-dependent manner. The flexibility supports CNS homeostasis under fluctuating energy supply.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Bernier 2020.

## Conditions and scope

- In-vivo mouse data with NADPH-imaging readouts.
- Aging-driven loss of this plasticity links to neuroinflammation.
