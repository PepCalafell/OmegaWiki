## Statement

TREM2-expressing tumour-associated macrophages enriched in lipid-rich and hypoxic niches suppress NK cell activity and expand exhausted CD8⁺ T cell populations, contributing to immunologically cold tumour states.

## Evidence summary

Pan-cancer scRNA-seq and spatial transcriptomic studies converge on TREM2⁺ TAMs as a hypoxia/lipid-niche immunosuppressive state.

## Conditions and scope

Lipid-rich, hypoxic niches; pan-tumour with NSCLC support.

## Counter-evidence

TREM2 also has homeostatic, tissue-repair roles outside the tumour context.
