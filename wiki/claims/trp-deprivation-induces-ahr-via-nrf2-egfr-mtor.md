## Statement

Cellular Trp deprivation induces AHR at the mRNA and protein level and enhances AHR activity. The induction pathway is cell-type-specific: NRF2 signalling in HEK293; EGFR-RAS-mTORC1 + p38/MAPK in glioblastoma. AHR therefore behaves not only as a Trp-catabolite-binding TF but also as an amino-acid-scarcity sensor whose expression itself responds to Trp availability.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews the relevant studies (refs 64, 72 of the paper).

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type-specific upstream signal; experimental Trp deprivation is non-physiological in magnitude. In tumour microenvironments where IDO1/TDO2/IL4I1 deplete extracellular Trp, this axis would amplify AHR expression in stromal/tumour cells.

— none reviewed.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

- Does Trp deprivation upregulate AHR in T cells, where AHR drives Treg/Th17 polarisation?
- Is mTORC1 hyperactivation in cancer sufficient to induce AHR even at normal Trp levels?
