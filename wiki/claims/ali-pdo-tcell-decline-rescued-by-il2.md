## Statement

The T cell compartment of ALI PDOs declines over time but is partially preserved for >70 days by IL-2 and other cytokine supplementation.

## Evidence summary

T cell compartment in ALI PDOs declines over time but can be maintained for >70 days in culture and is partially preserved by IL-2 and other cytokine supplementation (Polak et al. 2024, p.526; Neal et al. 2018).

## Conditions and scope

Long-term ALI PDO culture from melanoma, RCC and NSCLC biopsies.
