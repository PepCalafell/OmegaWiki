## Statement

Leucine specifically (not isoleucine/valine) allosterically activates glutamate dehydrogenase across the physiological range, via a regulatory site (human R204) confirmed by a structural homolog co-crystal.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 4G–H). Dose-response GDH activity and R204M mutant.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro enzyme assay; structural support from bovine GDH (PDB 8ar7).

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[concepts/leucine-allosteric-gdh-glutaminolysis-activation]].

## Open questions

- How leucine allostery integrates with other GDH regulators (ADP, GTP) in vivo.
