## Statement

Across a broad panel of preclinical models (colitis, ileitis, ischaemia–reperfusion, transplant, infection, fibrosis), pharmacologically activating HIF by inhibiting its hydroxylases is net anti-inflammatory and tissue-protective.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (Table 1). Links to [[foundations/dimethyloxalylglycine-dmog]], [[foundations/fg-4592-roxadustat]]. Concept: [[concepts/hydroxylase-inhibitor-anti-inflammatory-therapy]].

## Conditions and scope

Largely barrier/mucosal and ischaemic indications; efficacy in human inflammatory disease is not yet established.

## Counter-evidence

The benefit does not generalize to live bacterial sepsis (see [[claims/hydroxylase-inhibitors-exacerbate-lethal-bacterial-sepsis]]).
