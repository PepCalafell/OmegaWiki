## Statement

Biomedical research attention is highly concentrated: by 2017, 22% of gene-related publications referenced just 1% of genes, while about 3% of genes had no publications at all.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "We find that, by 2017, 22% of gene-related publications referenced just 1% of genes." (p.1); "3% of genes were not discussed by any publication." (p.2)

## Conditions and scope

Human genes; literature-derived attention metric.

## Counter-evidence

Some concentration is justified by genuine biological importance (e.g. TP53).
