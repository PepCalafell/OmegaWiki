## Statement

Pharmacologic AHR antagonists (HP163 in vivo, CH-223191 in vitro) suppress Zika virus replication and ameliorate ZIKV-induced microcephaly and intrauterine growth retardation in mouse models; CH-223191 also suppresses SARS-CoV-2 and HCoV-229E replication, supporting AHR antagonism as a broad-spectrum antiviral strategy.

## Evidence summary

Giovannoni et al. 2020 (ZIKV); Giovannoni et al. 2021 (SARS-CoV-2); reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
