## Statement

Within a single tumour entity (notably DLBCL), distinct subsets rely predominantly on aerobic glycolysis ("glycolytic tumours") versus mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation ("OxPhos tumours"), with the latter fuelled by glycolysis, glutamine, and fatty acids. Metabolic heterogeneity is now documented across most human cancers and is a determinant of therapeutic response.

## Evidence summary

- DLBCL initial split into BCR vs OxPhos clusters (Caro et al. 2012, Cancer Cell).
- Subsequent extension to multiple solid and haematological tumour types reviewed here.

## Conditions and scope

- Applies to bulk tumour metabolic phenotyping; intra-tumour metabolic heterogeneity adds further granularity not always captured.

## Counter-evidence

- Pure "glycolytic" vs "OxPhos" dichotomy is a simplification; many tumours sit on a continuum.
