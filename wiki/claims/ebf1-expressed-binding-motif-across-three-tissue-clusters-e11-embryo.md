## Statement

EBF1 is the only TF in the motif-enrichment analysis whose expression and motif-enrichment hit across three otherwise distinct E11 spatial clusters (face W0, brain W2, heart W6), consistent with EBF1's prior-reported partnership with TET2 as a sequence-specific recruiter of active demethylation.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 2e. Cross-references Guilhamon et al. *Nat. Commun.* 2013 (EBF1–TET2 partnership in cancer).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse embryo, single stage; correlative motif enrichment without direct EBF1 ChIP.

## Counter-evidence

EBF1 motif overlaps with other E-box-adjacent TF motifs; cross-tissue enrichment could partially reflect motif promiscuity.

## Open questions
