## Statement
Tissue-resident macrophages (modeled in mouse) regulate their TR network through integrated epigenetic and transcriptional control, resembling tissues rather than the activation network.

## Evidence summary
The 321-TR murine tissue-macrophage network showed dichotomous accessibility tracking expression (e.g. Gata6, Sall1), indicating identity-type regulation.

## Conditions and scope
Murine reanalysis (Amit et al.); human tissue-macrophage epigenome data not yet available.

## Counter-evidence
Contrasts with the human activation network finding.

## Open questions
Whether human tissue macrophages confirm the murine pattern.
