## Statement
Applying the in-vitro macrophage activation modules to human alveolar macrophages reveals that COPD patients exhibit an unexpected loss of inflammatory signatures — including depletion of the IFN-γ-linked module — contradicting prior literature reporting IL-4/IL-13 enrichment in COPD.

## Evidence summary
- "in smokers a glucocorticoide (GC)-associated gene module (41) was most significantly enriched ... In contrast to previous literature, we did not see an enrichment of IL-4-IL-13-associated signatures in COPD patients" (p.281).
- "the most significantly depleted ... WGCNA module in COPD patients was module 8 (linked to IFN-γ stimulation)" (p.281, Figure 5D).

## Conditions and scope
Data-driven GSEA on two combined public alveolar-macrophage cohorts; observational.

## Counter-evidence
Prior literature (Shaykhiev et al. 2009) reported IL-4/IL-13 signatures in COPD — this paper contradicts that with a module-based approach.

## Open questions
Mechanism of inflammatory-signature loss in COPD alveolar macrophages and its therapeutic implications.
