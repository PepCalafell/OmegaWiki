## Statement

Genetic effects on expression and accessibility are partially shared across immune cell types, with high directional concordance but only moderate overlap of significant associations.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (quantitative): Mean π1 0.69 (eQTL) / 0.62 (caQTL); mean rb 0.82 (eQTL) / 0.79 (caQTL); ~62% of shared eGenes/caPeaks regulated by independent lead variants (p.7-8).

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type pairs with sufficient discovery; common variants.
