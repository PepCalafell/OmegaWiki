## Statement

STAT5BN642H (Stat5-hyp) T cells, despite expressing a stabilized hyperactive STAT5, regulate gene sets that show very weak correlation with the WT IL-2-driven STAT5 response (Spearman r=0.071 at 1.5h, r=0.248 at 20h). The mutant TF redirects regulatory activity toward AURORA kinase, cell-cycle, E2F4 and FOXM1 target signatures. This quantitative dissociation supports the neomorphic-TF interpretation of STAT5BN642H rather than a simple "constitutively active STAT5" model.
