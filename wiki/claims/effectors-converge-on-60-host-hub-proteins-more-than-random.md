## Statement

T3SS effectors from many strains converge on a small set (~60) of human hub proteins
far more than chance, marking those proteins as functionally important interaction nodes.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.446, Fig.2f–i): significant convergence
on few host interactors (P<0.0001); 60 convergence proteins; targeting by effectors from
4 strains highly significant (P=0.004). Five convergence proteins belong to the NF-κB
module.

## Conditions and scope

Inferred from one interactome with ~32% sampling sensitivity.

## Counter-evidence

None within the study.
