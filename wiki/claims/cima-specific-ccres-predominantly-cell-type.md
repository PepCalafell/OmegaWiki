## Statement

Regulatory elements unique to CIMA (not overlapping bulk/single-cell references) are mostly accessible in only one immune cell type, indicating the atlas expands the cell type–specific cCRE catalog.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (correlational): cCREs shared with bulk/single-cell datasets were accessible in ≥10 cell types (>45%), whereas CIMA-specific elements were restricted to a single cell type (>50%); >70% overlap with single-cell vs 42% with ENCODE bulk (p.6).

## Conditions and scope

Comparison against ENCODE rDHS and single-cell chromatin references.
