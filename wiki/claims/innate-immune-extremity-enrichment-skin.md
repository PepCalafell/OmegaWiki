## Statement

Monocytes, macrophages and dendritic-cell subsets — together with their vascular associates (VECs, HECs, pericytes, Perivasc Fib I/II) — are preferentially enriched in extremity skin relative to trunk skin.

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult skin only; no functional validation of immune-cell role at the enriched sites.
