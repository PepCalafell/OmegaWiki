## Statement

The EBV-encoded nuclear antigen EBNA-3 binds AHR and amplifies its TCDD-driven transcriptional activity, while neutralising the cell-growth-inhibitory effect that TCDD normally exerts on EBV-infected B cells. This is one of the few well-characterised examples of a viral protein directly engaging AHR as a host factor.
