## Statement

Combining anti-TNF antibody with genetic deletion of IL-18, IFN-γ or IL-1β counteracts a large fraction of CLP-induced tissue gene expression changes (31.9%–63.3% of 7,106 DEGs), demonstrating functional necessity of the four-cytokine module beyond sufficiency.
