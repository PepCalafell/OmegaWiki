## Statement

Stable-isotope [1,2-¹³C₂]-glucose tracing directly confirms that Octyl itaconate decreases oxidative pentose phosphate pathway flux (reduced M+1 pentose phosphates) while glycolysis is not increased.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1464) — methodological/quantitative (flux tracing) evidence.
  - Quote: "[1,2-¹³C₂]-glucose tracing showed reduced M + 1 labeling of pentose phosphates (R5P, Ru5P, and xylose-5-phosphate [X5P]), which confirmed a decreased oxidative PPP flux ... increased lactate M + 2/M + 1 ratio suggested reduced glucose shunting into the oxidative PPP rather than enhanced glycolysis."

## Conditions and scope

A549 cells; corroborates pool-size metabolomics in [[claims/octyl-itaconate-depletes-pentose-phosphate-pathway]].
