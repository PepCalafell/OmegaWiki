## Statement

Long-term BCG-induced methylation changes are associated with changes in ex vivo production of all four measured cytokines, most extensively IFN-γ and IL-1β.

## Evidence summary

FDR-significant DNAm-C–cytokine associations for T90–T0 (not for T14–T0/T90–T14), linking durable methylation to both monocyte- and T-cell-derived cytokine responses.

## Conditions and scope

Heterologous S. aureus cytokine readouts; correlational.

## Counter-evidence

Association, not causation; cell-composition contributions possible.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether these methylation–cytokine links are causal or co-regulated.
