## Statement
LE spots have a different non-cancer neighbourhood from TC spots: ecm-myCAFs, intermediate fibroblasts, macrophages and CD8+ T cells are all enriched as LE neighbours, while macrophages dominate the TC neighbourhood through adhesive desmosomal signalling.

## Evidence summary
Fig. 3i comparative boxplot; supplementary signalling figures.

## Conditions and scope
HPV-negative OSCC, ST data; non-cancer identity assigned by largest non-cancer CARD deconvolution proportion.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.

## Open questions
Whether CD8+ T cell enrichment at LE is functional (anti-tumour) or dysfunctional (exhausted infiltrate).
