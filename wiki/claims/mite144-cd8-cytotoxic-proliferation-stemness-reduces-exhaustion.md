## Statement

MiTE-144 expands CD8⁺ T cells in TME and tdLNs and reprograms them toward cytotoxic, proliferative, and stem-like states (Gzma↑, Mki67↑, Tcf7↑) while downregulating the exhaustion transcription factor Tox. Combination with αCTLA-4 amplifies the effect.
