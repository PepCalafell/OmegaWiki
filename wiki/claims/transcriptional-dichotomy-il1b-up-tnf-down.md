## Statement

Efferocytosis produces a transcriptional dichotomy: pro-Il1b mRNA is upregulated while Tnf mRNA is suppressed.

## Evidence summary

TNFR1/TRIF-independent for the Il1b arm. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.6).

> "treatment of macrophages with neutrophils and TNF not only promoted IL-1β maturation but also enhanced the up-regulation of pro-Il1b mRNA ... coculture of macrophages with neutrophils resulted in a strong decrease in Tnf mRNA levels"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
