## Statement

Although the wound bed is hypoxic, the macrophage M1→M2 transition during skeletal muscle regeneration proceeds normally in mice with LysM-Cre HIF1α/HIF2α deletion. HIFs are needed for proinflammatory macrophage *infiltration* and the day-1 glycolytic burst, but not for the resolutive transition itself.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Lodge 2022 and related conditional-KO studies.

## Conditions and scope

- Muscle injury models; cardiac/liver fibrosis may differ.
- Hypoxia may still be permissive (not instructive) via lactate, succinate, or pH effects on macrophages.

## Counter-evidence

- One mild-trauma model showed slight regeneration impairment with macrophage HIF deletion, suggesting context-dependence.
