## Statement

Pulsed MPS1 inhibition induces micronuclei and STING activation, synergizing with decitabine in KRAS-LKB1 lung cancer.

## Evidence summary

Kitajima 2022 Cancer Cell: pulsed MPS1 inhibition + decitabine restores STING-IFN signaling and immunogenicity in KRAS-LKB1 mutant lung cancer. Synergy reported in HCC and lung cancer cell lines.

## Conditions and scope

KRAS-LKB1 (KL) lung cancer; pulsatile dosing required to avoid toxicity; combination with epigenetic priming.
