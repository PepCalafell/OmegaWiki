## Statement

Host-directed protein secretion capability is near-universal among gut Pseudomonadota:
92% of genomes encode at least one of T3SS, T4SS or T6SS.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.444): "527 of the 568 Pseudomonatoda
genomes (92%) have at least one host-directed secretion system," with T4SS (315) and
T6SS (474) also common, both of which may inject effectors into host cells.

## Conditions and scope

Reference genomes; T4SS/T6SS can also serve non-host-directed functions.

## Counter-evidence

T4SS/T6SS are not exclusively host-directed, so functional injection capacity is an
upper bound.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[commensal-t3ss-host-directed-secretion]].

## Open questions

Which of these systems are functionally host-directed in vivo.
