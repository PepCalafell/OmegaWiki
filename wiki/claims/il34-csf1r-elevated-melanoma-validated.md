## Statement

The keratinocyte-produced cytokine IL34 and its macrophage receptor CSF1R form an interaction that is elevated in melanoma relative to keratinocyte cancers, validated by RNAScope/STRISH colocalization in dermal immune-rich regions and associated with antigen-presentation and lipid-metabolism pathways.

## Evidence summary

Cross-modality spatial prediction plus experimental RNAScope validation; pathway enrichment in IL34-CSF1R+ melanoma spots. (p.12)

## Conditions and scope

Strongest in melanoma but also detected in BCC/cSCC dermis.

## Counter-evidence

RNA-level validation; protein-protein interaction not directly shown for this pair.

## Open questions

Whether IL34-CSF1R blockade modulates melanoma TAM activity therapeutically.
