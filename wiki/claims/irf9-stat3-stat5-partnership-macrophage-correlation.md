## Statement

IRF9 KO macrophage transcriptional changes correlate more strongly with STAT3 KO and STAT5 KO macrophage transcriptional changes than with STAT1 KO or STAT2 KO changes. This is correlative evidence that under homeostasis IRF9 may form non-ISGF3 complexes with STAT3 or STAT5 in macrophages. Biochemical evidence for the proposed IRF9-STAT3 and IRF9-STAT5 complexes is not yet provided.

## Counter-evidence

- Correlation is computed across all DE genes; alternative explanations include shared downstream targets via different upstream complexes, or shared dependence on tissue context-induced cytokines.
- No co-IP / proximity-labeling data in the paper supports a direct IRF9-STAT3 or IRF9-STAT5 interaction.
