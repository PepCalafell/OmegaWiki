## Statement

Loss of IRG1 polarizes tumor-associated macrophages toward a pro-tumor (CD80-low, CD206-high) phenotype, whereas IRG1+ TAMs carry a pro-inflammatory, anti-tumor transcriptional program.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1461) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "TAMs from Irg1−/− tumors showed a pro-tumor phenotype, with reduced CD80 and increased CD206."
  - "IRG1+ TAMs exhibited a pro-inflammatory, anti-tumor transcriptional program."

## Conditions and scope

Marker-based (CD80/CD206) polarization within the classic pro-/anti-tumor macrophage dichotomy; consistent across genetic and transplant models.
