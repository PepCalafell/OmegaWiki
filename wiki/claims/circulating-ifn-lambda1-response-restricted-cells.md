## Statement

In circulating immune cells, IFN-λ1 (IFN-III) responses are largely confined to B cells (especially plasma cells) and CD8 memory/γδ T cells, tracking IFNLR1 expression; there are no genes uniquely induced by IFN-λ1 — all overlap the IFN-α/β repertoire, with lower fold changes.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.5). Contrasts with the textbook view that IFN-III acts mainly on epithelial cells. See [[type-interferon-ifna-ifnb]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating immune subsets only.

## Counter-evidence

None internal; differs from epithelial-centric IFN-III literature.
