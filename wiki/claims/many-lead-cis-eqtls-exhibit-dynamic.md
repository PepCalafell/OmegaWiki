## Statement

Using a single-cell Poisson mixed-effects model, a large share of lead cis-eQTLs in B cells and monocytes change effect size along the differentiation trajectory.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (quantitative): 32% of B-cell and 46.9% of monocyte lead cis-eQTLs were dynamic; e.g. BLK β shifted −0.54→−0.78 across pseudotime in B cells (p.8).

## Conditions and scope

B cells and monocytes; pseudotime inferred from scRNA-seq.
