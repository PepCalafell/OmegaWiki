## Statement

Single-nucleotide in silico mutagenesis with CIMA-CLM predicts how noncoding variants alter chromatin accessibility, with predictions concordant with SMR and GWAS evidence.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (methodological): Predicted reduced accessibility for rs3783996 (VASH1) in Bn-TCL1A (consistent with SMR); predicted increased accessibility for the RA-risk variant rs2069235 (SYNGR1) (p.12).

## Conditions and scope

Open chromatin 501-bp regions; computational predictions, partially cross-checked against SMR/GWAS.
