## Statement

In a mouse KRAS-mutant LUAD model, STAT3 knockout reduces tumour burden in females via oestrogen-dependent antitumour immunity (reversed by tamoxifen) and accelerates tumour growth in males via NF-κB activation, CXCL1-driven neutrophilia and IL-6 induction (mitigated by IL-6 blockade or neutrophil depletion).

## Evidence summary

Mouse interventional study with pharmacological rescue experiments.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse KRAS-mutant LUAD; sex effects may differ in human disease.

## Counter-evidence

Translation to patients with KRAS-mutant NSCLC is unproven.
