## Statement

Across the CKI panel, signal-induced H3K27ac is perturbed by at least one inhibitor at the large majority of stimulus-regulated cis-regulatory elements (~85% LPS, ~89% IL-4), with a predominance of downregulatory over upregulatory effects.

## Evidence summary

From ~16,500 LPS- and ~5,000 IL-4-regulated CREs, perturbation-likelihood analysis showed near-pervasive coverage; affected and unaffected CREs differed minimally in stimulus-induced H3K27ac amplitude. See [[foundations/h3k27ac-histone-acetylation-mark]] and [[concepts/epigenome-based-functional-profiling-kinase-inhibitors]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS and IL-4; perturbation likelihood > 1.

## Counter-evidence

A few inhibitors preferentially upregulated H3K27ac, and a subset nearly selectively impaired only one stimulus's response.
