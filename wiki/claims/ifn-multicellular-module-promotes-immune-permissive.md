## Statement

A cellular co-occurrence network identified an IFN-I multicellular module (M-IFN1) of cross-lineage IFIT1+ populations. M-IFN1-high tumors showed higher proportions of CXCL9+ TAMs, germinal-center-related clusters, and overall T cells, and ligand-receptor analysis indicated M-IFN1 cells recruit immune cells via chemokines and support B-cell differentiation (TNFSF13B axis), supporting an immune-permissive role.

## Evidence summary

Co-occurrence + ligand-receptor inference. (p.6) Quote: "M-IFN1 showed potential to facilitate the formation of an immune-hot TME".

## Conditions and scope

Inferred from frequency correlation and ligand-receptor predictions; not perturbational.
