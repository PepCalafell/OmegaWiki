## Statement

Most lines lose fitness under hypoxia, but SH4 (melanoma), 786O and Caki1 (clear-cell renal cell carcinoma) show increased relative growth; Caki1 is VHL-wild-type, implying VHL-independent metabolic resistance.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.5): "a subset, including SH4 (melanoma), 786O, and Caki1 (renal cell carcinomas), showed increased relative growth" — Relative fitness scores from GENEVA (Fig.2e); ccRCC lines evade hypoxia-induced downregulation of de novo purine synthesis genes (Fig.5b). (type: correlational, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
