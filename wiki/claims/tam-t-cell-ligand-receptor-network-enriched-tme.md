## Statement

In integrated pan-cancer scRNA-seq (breast / lung / colon), TAM-T cell ligand-receptor circuitry is the densest immune-cell-pair network and is significantly enriched in TME versus adjacent-healthy tissue, spanning chemokine, co-stimulatory, inhibitory, and immunosuppressive axes.

## Evidence summary

MultiNicheNetR comparative analysis; Figure 1M-N chord diagrams; multiple L-R pairs reaching significance in tumor enrichment.
