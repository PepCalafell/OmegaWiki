## Statement

TME-group membership only modestly tracked tumor stage and showed minimal age/sex association; apart from G03 and G05, groups contained mixtures of cancer types. More than half of CRC, ESCA, NPC, and LUAD tumors were T-centric, whereas 61% PAAD, 50% PRAD, and 43% OV were S-centric (G08-G10); mIHC on new samples confirmed LRRC15+ myCAF enrichment and CD146+ PvC depletion in OV vs PRAD.

## Evidence summary

Cross-cancer composition analysis with mIHC validation. (p.7-8) Quote: "apart from G03 and G05, other TME groups exhibited a mixture of various cancer types".

## Conditions and scope

Proportions depend on the cohorts sampled per cancer type.
