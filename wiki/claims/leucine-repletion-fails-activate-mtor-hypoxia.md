## Statement

Although leucine canonically activates mTOR, in hypoxia (which suppresses mTOR) leucine repletion does not activate mTOR — so the GDH/glutaminolysis axis, not mTOR, is the operative leucine output here.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 4F). Immunoblot of mTOR pathway activity under hypoxia with leucine.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxia-specific; rules out the canonical leucine→mTOR route in this context.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope; consistent with prior reports that hypoxia represses mTOR.

Supports [[concepts/leucine-allosteric-gdh-glutaminolysis-activation]].
