## Statement

BCG vaccination significantly alters whole-blood DNA methylation at 11 CpG sites (FDR < 0.05) over 90 days, with five gaining and six losing methylation.

## Evidence summary

Genome-wide longitudinal mixed-effects model on 751,564 EPIC probes; 11 FDR-significant CpGs, eight of which show opposite T14–T0 vs T90–T0 directions.

## Conditions and scope

Healthy young adults; whole-blood methylome; effect sizes modest and partly cell-composition correlated.

## Counter-evidence

No non-vaccinated control group; only partial replication in an independent cohort.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether the 11 sites are cell-intrinsic methylation changes or reflect cell-composition shifts.
