## Statement

T cell-intrinsic STING signaling is cytotoxic and impairs T cell proliferation, memory, and effector function.

## Evidence summary

Gulen 2017, Larkin 2017, Cerboni 2017, Wu 2020 Immunity. SAVI patients have impaired memory T cells; murine SAVI shows decreased CD4/CD8 populations. Robust STING activation is cytotoxic across species and conserved across tested human agonists (Knelson 2022; Sivick 2018).

## Conditions and scope

Direct or paracrine STING activation in CD4/CD8 T cells; signal-strength dependent; involves apoptosis, ER stress, calcium homeostasis, NF-κB, IRF3/STAT1.
