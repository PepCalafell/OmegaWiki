## Statement

Standard myeloma induction therapy — particularly bortezomib–lenalidomide–dexamethasone (VRd) — selectively attenuates systemic IFN-I activity (most prominently normalizing monocyte IFN-α) while leaving IFN-II (IFN-γ) activity unchanged.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.8). Quantified with the [[ifn-ifn-ii-activity-deconvolution-scoring]] framework on longitudinal samples.

## Conditions and scope

NDMM induction setting; two cycles; small DVRd cohort.

## Counter-evidence

Two patients with low baseline IFN-α showed post-treatment increases; DVRd IFN-α reductions did not reach significance outside monocytes.

## Open questions

Whether persistent IFN-γ after therapy contributes to treatment resistance.
