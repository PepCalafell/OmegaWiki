## Statement

Tissue-resident macrophage depletion (CD169-DTR + DT) in early KP NSCLC increases the intratumoural percentage of CD3⁺ T cells, IFNγ⁺TNF⁺CD8⁺ polyfunctional effector T cells, and the CD8⁺/Treg ratio — restoring an effector-skewed immune profile that is otherwise suppressed by the TRM niche.

## Evidence summary

- Imaging-quantified intratumoural CD3⁺ T cell percentage ↑ (P=0.041)
- Polyfunctional IFNγ⁺TNF⁺CD8⁺ T cells ↑ (P=0.016)
- CD8⁺/Treg ratio ↑ (P=0.022)
- Total CD3⁺ T cell number not significantly different (P=0.17) — implies redistribution / activation rather than recruitment

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC; pre-tumour TRM depletion
- Day 30 endpoint
- n=9 mice per group

## Counter-evidence

- Total CD3⁺ T cell number unchanged (only percentage ↑) suggests the effect is on T cell distribution / polyfunctionality, not absolute infiltration
- Cross-species relevance to human NSCLC not directly tested
