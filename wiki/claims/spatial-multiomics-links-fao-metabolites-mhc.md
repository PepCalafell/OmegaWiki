## Statement

Paired spatial transcriptomics and metabolomics in human NSCLC show MHC-II TAM niches are enriched for fatty-acid-oxidation intermediates (ketone bodies, acylcarnitines) while angiogenic TAM niches are enriched for lactic acid and prostaglandins (PGA1, PGE2).

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2068-2071) — correlational evidence. Visium CytAssist + AFADESI-MSI on 2 NSCLC patients; MultiVI + CellCharter niche integration; spatial cross-correlation of metabolites vs TAM signatures.
  - Quote: "the angiogenic TAM niches also showed increased levels of glycolysis products like lactic acid and high concentrations of prostaglandins (PGA1 and PGE2)"

## Conditions and scope

Only two patient samples; ~100 µm spatial resolution; GM-CSF undetectable spatially.
