## Statement

EGFR-mutant lung adenocarcinoma — particularly in smokers — is characterised by a low TMB, reduced immune infiltration, and fibroblast-enriched stromal niches that confer resistance to both ICIs and EGFR TKIs.

## Evidence summary

Multi-cohort transcriptomic and spatial analyses cited.

## Conditions and scope

EGFR-mutant LUAD; subgroup effects strongest in smokers.

## Counter-evidence

Some EGFR-mutant patients respond to ICI + chemotherapy combinations.

## Open questions
