## Statement

HIF-1α is a metabolic gatekeeper of CD4+ T helper lineage commitment and CD8+ effector function. HIF-1α-driven glycolysis biases CD4 differentiation toward Th17 and away from Treg (HIF-1α loss reciprocally enhances Tregs). In CD8+ T cells, HIF-1α is required for productive anti-cancer effector responses. HIF-1α also shapes B-cell differentiation and IL-10 production via glycolysis.

## Evidence summary

Shi et al. 2011 (Th17/Treg); Palazon et al. 2017 (CD8+ anti-tumor). Both integrated by Luo et al. 2022.

## Conditions and scope

- CD4+ T cells in inflammatory contexts (autoimmunity, infection)
- CD8+ TILs in tumor microenvironment
- B cells: HIF-1α-glycolysis drives IL-10 in regulatory B cells

## Counter-evidence

Chronic tumor hypoxia paradoxically exhausts CD8+ T cells via HIF-1α-driven inhibitory receptor expression — same axis can become detrimental at long timescales.
