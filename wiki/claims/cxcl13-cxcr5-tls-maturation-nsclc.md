## Statement

In NSCLC patients receiving nivolumab + ipilimumab + chemotherapy, CXCL13–CXCR5 chemokine signalling is activated, supporting lymphocyte recruitment and TLS maturation.

## Evidence summary

Bulk and spatial analyses of patient biopsies identify CXCL13–CXCR5 axis activation paralleling TLS organisation.

## Conditions and scope

Specific to combination ICI + chemotherapy contexts; biopsy timing matters.

## Counter-evidence

Causal role of the axis requires interventional studies.
