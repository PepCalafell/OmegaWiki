## Statement

Spatial mapping showed AMs (Car4/Chil3/Ear1) largely excluded from tumor regions, while ~60-71% Vcan+Cx3cr1+Mafb+ recMacs and ~19-25% Mmp12+C5ar1+Mafb+ CD206lo IMs of total lung macrophages populated tumor cores and margins.

## Evidence summary

Graph-based clustering of Xenium data with macrophage proportion quantification (Fig. 3d,e). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; proportions of total lung macrophages.
