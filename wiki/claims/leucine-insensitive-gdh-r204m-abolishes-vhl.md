## Statement

A GDH point mutant (R204M) that retains basal activity but cannot sense leucine severs VHL-promoted reductive glutaminolysis and hypoxic proliferation, demonstrating that leucine→GDH allostery is required for the phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 4I–Q). Separation-of-function reconstitution + isotope tracing + rescue by citrate/nucleosides.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxia-specific for the reductive route; normoxic oxidative glutaminolysis is leucine-independent.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[concepts/leucine-allosteric-gdh-glutaminolysis-activation]].

## Open questions

- Druggability of the leucine→GDH axis for hypoxic disease.
