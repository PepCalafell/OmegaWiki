## Statement

Efferocytosis broadly increases macrophage genomic 5-methylcytosine content; both MAT2A activity and DNMT3A protein are required for the full increase.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative %5mC assay shows AC-induced increase, partially blocked by MAT2A inhibition and partially blocked by DNMT3A absence. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only; bulk DNA, not site-resolved.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
