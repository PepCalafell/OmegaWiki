## Statement

AHR SUMOylation at K63/K510 paradoxically stabilises the receptor (by blocking lysine ubiquitination) while repressing its transcriptional output. Ligand binding inhibits AHR SUMOylation, releasing the receptor for transcription but committing it to ubiquitin-mediated degradation. AHRR is also SUMOylated (K542/K583/K660), enhancing its co-repressor binding (ANKRA2, HDAC4, HDAC5). ARNT SUMOylation at K245 has only a marginal effect on AHR transcription.
