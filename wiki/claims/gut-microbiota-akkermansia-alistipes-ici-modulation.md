## Statement

In NSCLC, enrichment of Akkermansia, Ruminococcus and Bifidobacterium correlates with enhanced ICI response; dysbiosis dominated by Alistipes promotes immunosuppression (via IL-6 and related cytokines) and ICI resistance.

## Evidence summary

Multiple stool-microbiome cohort studies and preclinical models.

## Conditions and scope

ICI-treated NSCLC patients; effects context-dependent.

## Counter-evidence

Specific taxa-to-outcome associations are not always replicated across cohorts.
