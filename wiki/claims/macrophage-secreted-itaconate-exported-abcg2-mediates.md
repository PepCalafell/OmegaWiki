## Statement

Itaconate exported from macrophages via the transporter ABCG2 acts non-cell-autonomously to suppress adjacent tumor-cell proliferation; blocking export (ABCG2 knockdown) reduces the anti-proliferative activity of macrophage conditioned media.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1471) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "ABCG2 knockdown attenuated the anti-proliferative activity of CM from pro-inflammatory macrophages, which indicated that secreted itaconate contributes to macrophage-mediated tumor suppression."

## Conditions and scope

In vitro conditioned-media assay; supports the paracrine arm of the model together with direct cancer-cell effects ([[claims/irg1-itaconate-directly-inhibits-cancer-cell]]).

## Counter-evidence

Extracellular itaconate is generally low; local niche concentrations inferred. Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
