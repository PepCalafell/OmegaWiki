## Statement

Whereas local tumor hypoxia (poorly perfused niches) promotes aggression via angiogenesis, nutrient uptake and therapy resistance, whole-body hypoxia suppresses growth by tumor-wide nucleotide depletion, challenging the paradigm of hypoxia as a uniformly negative prognostic factor.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.1): "These findings challenge the long-held paradigm of hypoxia as a negative prognostic factor in cancer progression" — Authors contrast disrupted intratumoral 'division of labor' under systemic hypoxia with local hypoxia's pro-tumor effects (Discussion). (type: mechanistic, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
