## Statement
Among the most differentially phosphorylated proteins between macrophage states, the tumor-suppressor PML carries 12 phosphoresidues with higher phosphorylation in the proinflammatory M1 state than in both M2a and M2c.

## Evidence summary
- "the tumor suppressor promyelocytic leukemia protein (PML), which had 12 phosphoresidues with higher phosphorylation levels in the M1 state compared to both M2a and M2c macrophages. PML has multiple roles in the formation of PML-nuclear bodies and ... is linked to the IFN-γ signaling pathway" (p.10).
- The PML-nuclear-body partner SP100 had eight M1-high phosphosites (p.10).

## Conditions and scope
Direct phosphosite evidence; in vitro macrophages; functional consequence of PML hyperphosphorylation not established here.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
