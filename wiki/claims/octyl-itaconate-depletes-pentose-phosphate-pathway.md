## Statement

Octyl itaconate rapidly depletes pentose phosphate pathway intermediates and reduces the R5P/Ru5P ratio, indicating impaired oxidative PPP activity, without global changes in glycolysis or mitochondrial respiration.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1464) — mechanistic/quantitative evidence.
  - Quote: "LC-MS ... revealed the rapid depletion of pentose phosphate pathway (PPP) intermediates within 6 h of treatment, which intensified by 24 h ... A reduced ribose-5-phosphate (R5P)/ribulose-5-phosphate (Ru5P) ratio indicated impaired oxidative PPP activity."

## Conditions and scope

A549 cells; OCR and ECAR remained unchanged, arguing for a targeted PPP effect rather than broad metabolic collapse.
