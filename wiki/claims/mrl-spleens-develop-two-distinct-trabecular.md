## Statement

In lupus-affected mouse spleens, CellCharter resolves two trabecular spatial clusters with opposing CD31 / Ly6G profiles and distinct positional preferences: CD31+/Ly6G− (near PALS) and CD31−/Ly6G+ (within red pulp).

## Evidence summary

Cluster proximity heatmaps and immunofluorescence staining for CD31, ERTR7, and Ly6G in Fig. 3j; Extended Data Fig. 4b for marker expression.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse model only; whether equivalent niches occur in human spleen pathology remains open.

## Open questions

- Are the trabecular Ly6G+ clusters functionally analogous to TAN-like neutrophil niches seen in lung tumours?
