## Statement

A single dose of the CCR5 antagonist maraviroc given 3 h before intranasal antigen reduced antigen-bearing Ly6C+ moDCs in draining lymph nodes ~76% without affecting CD26+ DC migration; the effect was transient (only at 3 h, not 24/48 h pre-antigen), consistent with maraviroc's ~16 h half-life.

Flow cytometry of antigen-bearing moDCs vs DCs (Fig. 5e; Extended Data Fig. 8). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].
