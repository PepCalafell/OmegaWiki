## Statement

A substantial fraction of immune cis-eGenes and cis-caPeaks are specific to a single cell type, and pairwise enrichment analysis confirms genuine (not power-driven) cell type specificity.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (correlational): 2769 (28.84%) eGenes and 28,898 (55.19%) caPeaks were specific to a single cell type; enrichment of pairwise-specific effects in ~46% of cell-type pairs after accounting for power (p.7-8).

## Conditions and scope

PBMC immune cell types, common variants (MAF>0.1), East Asian (Chinese) cohort.
