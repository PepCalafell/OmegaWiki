## Statement

Under hypoxia, HIF-inducible genes gain chromatin accessibility predominantly at genic (gene-body/non-promoter) peaks rather than promoter-only regions, and HIF-1A/HIF-2A motifs are enriched specifically in these genic peaks.

## Evidence summary

HIF-metagene-partitioned ATAC analysis (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.10).

> "ATAC-seq peaks located solely at promoters of HIF-inducible genes were not significantly increased by hypoxia ... including genic peaks revealed significant upregulation ... HIF-1A and HIF-2A enrichment only in genic but absent from promoter-only regions"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; HIF metagene set (Lombardi et al. 2022).
