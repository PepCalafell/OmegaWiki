## Statement

A harmonized human skin fibroblast resource was built by re-processing 2.1 million scRNA-seq cells (32 datasets, 251 donors) with scVI, yielding 357,276 high-quality fibroblasts.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative figures stated in Results and Fig. 1a; QC and marker-based fibroblast selection described.

## Conditions and scope

Adult human skin; published datasets integrated.
