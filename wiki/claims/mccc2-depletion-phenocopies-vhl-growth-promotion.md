## Statement

Depleting MCCC2 reproduces the growth-promoting effect of VHL WT specifically under hypoxia (not normoxia), establishing MCCC2 inhibition as sufficient for the VHL phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 3B, S4C–D). Epistasis/phenocopy via shMCCC2.

## Conditions and scope

Causal-sufficiency evidence; ties the phenotype to MCCC inhibition rather than other VHL functions.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[concepts/vhl-mccc2-leucine-catabolism-inhibition]].

## Open questions
