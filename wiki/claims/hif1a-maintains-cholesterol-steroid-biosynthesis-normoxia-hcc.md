## Statement

In hepatocellular carcinoma (Huh7) cells, HIF-1α is required under normoxia to sustain expression of steroid/cholesterol biosynthetic enzymes and to maintain total cellular cholesterol content; this dependency is absent in HeLa cells, adding a lipid/cholesterol arm to the normoxic HIF-1α metabolic program.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3154–3156) — proteomics cluster + biochemical cholesterol assay.
  - Quote: "knocking out HIF-1α in Huh7 cells resulted in the downregulation of many crucial steroid biosynthetic enzymes irrespective of oxygen levels, indicating that HIF-1α is involved in the production of cholesterol/steroids under normoxia in HCC cells."
  - Quote: "In Huh7 cells, cholesterol levels were elevated under hypoxia and both normoxic and hypoxic cholesterol contents were strongly reduced in the absence of HIF-1α ... the absence of HIF-1α did not significantly affect cholesterol concentration under either normoxia or hypoxia [in HeLa]."

## Conditions and scope

Total cholesterol (free + esters) normalized to protein; n=8. Enzyme-level via LFQ proteomics. Liver-cell-specific lipid metabolism likely amplifies this dependency.
