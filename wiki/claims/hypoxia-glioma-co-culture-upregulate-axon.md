## Statement

Genes more strongly induced by combined hypoxia and glioma co-culture in microglia are enriched for axon development, axon guidance, and tissue-morphogenesis pathways — an unexpected, interaction-specific program.

## Evidence summary

GO over-representation of interaction-specific upregulated genes (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.5).

> "hypoxia also upregulated genes from axon development, axon guidance, or tissue morphogenesis pathways in BV2 cells ... these GO terms were more enriched in glioma co-cultured BV2 than in hypoxia alone"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; transcriptomic GO enrichment.

## Counter-evidence

Enrichment is correlative; functional consequence in microglia not tested.

## Open questions

Functional meaning of axon-guidance gene induction in tumor microglia (e.g. semaphorin–plexin signaling).
