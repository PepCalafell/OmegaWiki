## Statement

Hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF) is the principal transcription factor mediating adaptation to low oxygen: an oxygen-labile HIFα subunit dimerizes with the constitutive HIF1β subunit to activate an adaptive transcriptional programme.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Links to foundations [[foundations/hif1a]], [[foundations/hif2a]], [[foundations/arnt-hif1b]].

## Conditions and scope

Not all hypoxic transcriptional responses are HIF-dependent; the review focuses on HIF in immune cells but notes other oxygen-sensitive factors (e.g. NF-κB).
