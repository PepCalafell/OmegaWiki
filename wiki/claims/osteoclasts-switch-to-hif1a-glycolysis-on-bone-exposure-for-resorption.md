## Statement

Whereas resting/differentiating osteoclasts use OXPHOS fuelled by glutaminolysis, **bone-exposed osteoclasts switch to glycolysis with HIF-1α stabilization and lactate production**, which is required for collagen-degradation and resorption activity. Glucose withdrawal lowers resorption; complex I inhibition augments it — illustrating a bidirectional OXPHOS↔glycolysis switch.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Arnett 2003, Indo 2013, Lemma 2017, and Morten 2013.

## Conditions and scope

- In-vitro bone substrate assays; in-vivo confirmation via hypoxic-bone osteoclast studies.
