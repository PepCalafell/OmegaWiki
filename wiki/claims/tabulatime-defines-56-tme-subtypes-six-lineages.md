## Statement

TabulaTIME defines 56 cell subtypes across 6 major TME lineages (cytotoxic lymphocytes; conventional/regulatory T cells; B lymphocytes; myeloid cells; fibroblasts; endothelial cells), with epithelial/malignant as a 7th compartment treated separately.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "We have defined 6 major cell lineages and 56 cell subtypes within the TME using an integrated approach" (Han 2025, abstract/p.1).
