## Statement

Under normoxia, PHD enzymes hydroxylate HIFα prolines (enabling pVHL-mediated ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation) and FIH hydroxylates an asparagine (blocking CBP/p300 co-activator recruitment); hypoxia inhibits both hydroxylases, stabilizing and derepressing HIF.

## Conditions and scope

This is the canonical oxygen-dependent axis; a lysosomal, oxygen-independent route to HIF degradation also exists (e.g. suppressed by CO₂/hypercapnia).
