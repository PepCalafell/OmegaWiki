## Statement

PRMT5 (not PRMT1/PRMT7) symmetrically dimethylates MCCC2 at R292 under normoxia; this methyl-arginine blocks VHL from binding MCCC2. Hypoxia represses the modification, de-blocking the interaction.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 6G–M, S8A). PRMT isoform specificity, MS site mapping, in vitro methylation, competitive binding.

## Conditions and scope

R292K (charge-preserving, methylation-dead) still cannot disrupt VHL binding unless paired with phosphomimetic VHL Y185E — revealing a dual-PTM requirement.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
