## Statement

Hypoxia, via HIF1α (with ROS as a co-signal), induces the ATP-binding-cassette efflux transporters P-glycoprotein (PGP/MDR1), breast-cancer-resistance protein (BCRP) and multidrug-resistance-associated protein 1 (MRP1), pumping chemotherapeutic agents out of cancer cells and lowering intracellular drug concentrations. CAF-secreted IL-6 amplifies the axis via miR-27a → PGP.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023) — colon cancer and broader review evidence.

## Conditions and scope

Multiple cancers; ABC-transporter family redundancy means knockout of one is usually compensated.

## Counter-evidence

Drug efflux is one of many mechanisms; intracellular drug-inactivation (CDD), drug-target alteration, and BNIP3-pathway suppression also contribute.
