## Statement

When the liver macrophage niche is acutely depleted, DHPS-deficient mice fail to restore homeostasis — exhibiting congestion, abnormal sinusoids and necrosis — demonstrating that myeloid DHPS is needed for tissue repair/maintenance.

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 5h-j, Supplementary Fig. 19); relates to [[foundations/kupffer-cells]].
