## Statement

In patients with melanoma or NSCLC undergoing adoptive T-cell therapy, expression of glycolytic genes in the tumour inversely correlates with immune-cell infiltration, implicating tumour metabolic state as a determinant of ACT efficacy.

## Evidence summary

- Cohort-level correlation cited in Ricci 2025 review.

## Conditions and scope

- ACT-treated melanoma and NSCLC cohorts; needs replication in non-ACT settings.

## Counter-evidence

- Correlation does not establish causality; confounding by tumour mutational burden possible.

## Linked ideas

- Use glycolytic signature as patient-stratification marker for combination metabolic-immune therapies.

## Open questions

- Whether targeted glycolysis inhibition can rescue ACT efficacy in glycolytic-high tumours.
