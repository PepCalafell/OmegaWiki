## Statement
In stage-2 colorectal cancer (AMC cohort), proteome-derived immune consensus cluster classification predicts recurrence-free survival more significantly than CMS subtype, with CC1 (immune-active, CMS2/3-enriched) showing better RFS than CC2 (CMS1/4-enriched). The signal is corroborated at RNA level on independent material from the same tumours.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figure 8D–E and Figure S8E.

## Conditions and scope
- AMC stage-2 cohort; replicated in EMC stage-1/2 (CMS-direction inverted but immune-CC direction preserved).
- Univariate survival analysis only; multivariate adjustment for stage/age/MSI not reported.

## Counter-evidence
- CMS4 has the worst RFS independently; immune CC may partly track CMS4 burden.
- No external validation cohort.

## Open questions
- Does immune CC retain prognostic value after multivariable adjustment for stage, MSI, KRAS, BRAF?
