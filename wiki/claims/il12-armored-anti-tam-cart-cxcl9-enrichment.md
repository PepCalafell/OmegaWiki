## Statement

CAR T cells armored with IL-12 and directed at TREM2 or FOLR2 deplete immunosuppressive TAMs and reprogram the repopulating compartment toward CXCL9⁺ immunogenic macrophages, enabling cancer-reactive CD8 T-cell recruitment and durable tumor control in preclinical ovarian/lung/CRC models.
