## Statement

A second prospective neoadjuvant HCC cohort (cemiplimab + SBRT, NCT03916627 Cohort B2) independently reproduces the discovery-cohort observation that TREM2 macs are specifically enriched in PD-1 responders.

## Evidence summary

- 21 enrolled, 16 scRNAseq-profiled, 6 responders.
- TREM2-mac enrichment in responder tumor samples.
- KC tumor-exclusion replicated.

## Conditions and scope

- Different treatment regimen (cemiplimab+SBRT vs cemiplimab alone).
- Modest cohort size; not adequately powered for sub-stratification.

## Counter-evidence

- None.
