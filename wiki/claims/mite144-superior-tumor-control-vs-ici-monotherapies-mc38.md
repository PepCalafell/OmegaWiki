## Statement

MiTE-144 as monotherapy nearly abrogates MC38 tumor growth and outperforms anti-TREM2, anti-PD-1, anti-CTLA-4 monotherapies as well as anti-TREM2 combined with anti-PD-1 or anti-CTLA-4, with concordant superiority in the MCA205 syngeneic model.
