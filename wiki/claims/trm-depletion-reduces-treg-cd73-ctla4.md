## Statement

Tissue-resident macrophage depletion (CD169-DTR + DT) in early KP NSCLC reduces the number of tumour Treg cells and reduces CTLA-4 and CD73 expression on the remaining Tregs. The effect is restricted to the tumour: no changes in Treg cells in tumour-draining lymph nodes or spleen, indicating a local tumour-niche function rather than systemic Treg dysregulation.

## Evidence summary

- Tumour Treg numbers reduced in CD169-DTR + DT
- CTLA-4 and CD73 expression on tumour Tregs reduced
- No changes in tumour-draining lymph node or spleen Tregs
- Consistent with TRM-selective Treg-licensing axis

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC; pre-tumour TRM depletion
- Flow-cytometric Treg analysis

## Counter-evidence

- Whether the reduced CD73/CTLA-4 reflects a different Treg subpopulation (recruitment-skewing) vs altered programming on the same cells is not directly resolved
- Effect size and statistical confidence quoted in extended figures only
