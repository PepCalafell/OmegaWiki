## Statement

Across all cytokines and cell types, receptor transcript expression correlates with response magnitude at r≈0.4, and responses generally require a minimum baseline receptor expression of ~8 cpm.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: quantitative.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
