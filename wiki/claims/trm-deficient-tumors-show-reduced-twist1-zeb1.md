## Statement

In vivo confirmation of the TRM-induced EMT programme: KP-GFP tumour cells in TRM-depleted (CD169-DTR + DT) mouse lungs at day 5 post-injection show significantly reduced expression of EMT transcription factors TWIST1 (P=0.05) and ZEB1 (P=0.03) compared to TRM-sufficient (WT + DT) mice.

## Evidence summary

- Confocal imaging at day 5 post-KP-GFP injection
- TWIST1⁺ KP cells: reduced in CD169-DTR + DT (P=0.05)
- ZEB1⁺ KP cells: reduced in CD169-DTR + DT (P=0.03)
- Two independent experiments

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC; CD169-DTR depletion model
- Single early time point (day 5)
- Imaging-based quantification (not transcriptomic)

## Counter-evidence

- P-values borderline (0.05 / 0.03) suggesting modest effect size
- Single time point; whether the EMT-reduction persists across tumour progression untested
- TWIST1/ZEB1 are not the only EMT TFs; SNAI1, SNAI2, FOXC2 not measured
