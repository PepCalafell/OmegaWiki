## Statement

In vitro and in vivo hypoxic cancer models display a 2- to 5-fold increase in gene mutation frequency relative to normoxic controls, driven by hypoxia-induced DNA double- and single-strand breaks, gene amplification, impaired HR repair, and MMR deregulation.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023), citing the canonical preclinical literature.

## Conditions and scope

Cell-line and model-dependent. The fold-increase depends on hypoxia duration, pO₂, and oncogenic background.

## Counter-evidence

Some studies show mutation rates are unchanged under acute hypoxia; the elevated rate requires chronic exposure plus reoxygenation cycles.
