## Statement

eFibro_CTHRC1 cells are predominantly tumor-derived and co-express the canonical CAF markers FAP, LRRC15, and POSTN across nearly all 21+ cancer types analyzed, while being absent or rare in healthy samples — establishing CTHRC1 as the pan-cancer canonical marker of ECM-remodeling CAFs.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "eFibro_CTHRC1 cells also expressed canonical CAF markers, including FAP, LRRC15 and POSTN, which are prevalent in nearly all cancer types but not in healthy samples" (Han 2025, p.8).

## Linked ideas

- Use CTHRC1 + FAP + LRRC15 + POSTN as minimal panel for ECM-CAF identification in any solid tumor scRNA-seq.
