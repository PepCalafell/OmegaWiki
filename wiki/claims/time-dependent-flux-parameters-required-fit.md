## Statement

Over a 9-month in vivo haematopoiesis time course, models with fixed dynamic parameters fail to fit the data; time-dependent growth/differentiation/diffusion rates are necessary.

## Evidence summary

Model-comparison argument (time-dependent vs static); large early-timepoint parameter variation motivates time-dependence.

## Conditions and scope

One long in vivo dataset; early variation may partly reflect tamoxifen perturbation.

## Counter-evidence

None; though some early variation may be artefactual rather than true rate change.
