## Statement

PADIT-seq activities for EGR1 quantitatively recapitulate MITOMI-derived Kd measurements at Pearson r=0.94, including at low-affinity sites.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]] (Fig.1b).

## Conditions and scope

EGR1 specifically; agreement spans the affinity range covered by MITOMI.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
