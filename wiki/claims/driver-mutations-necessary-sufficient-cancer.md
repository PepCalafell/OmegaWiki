## Statement
Driver mutations are usually necessary but not sufficient to drive early cancer progression; most driver-mutant cells in normal tissue do not become cancer.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
