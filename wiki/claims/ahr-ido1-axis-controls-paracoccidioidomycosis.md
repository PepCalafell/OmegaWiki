## Statement

In murine P. brasiliensis infection, Ahr-KO and Ido-KO animals display overlapping susceptibility phenotypes (increased fungal load, lung pathology, mortality). Ido-KO mice show reduced Ahr expression after infection, supporting an obligate IDO1→KYN→AHR axis controlling antifungal immunity in lung paracoccidioidomycosis.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 104–105.

## Conditions and scope

Murine pulmonary model only; human paracoccidioidomycosis is endemic to South America.
