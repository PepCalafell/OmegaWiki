## Statement

Cell-free conditioned medium from tumour-associated TRMs (tTRM-CM) is sufficient to promote KP NSCLC tumour-cell migration in transwell assays — significantly more than control CM or tumour-MDM CM. This indicates that the TRM-induced invasiveness signal is partly mediated by soluble secreted factors.

## Evidence summary

- Transwell assay; 18 h migration window
- KP-GFP cells migrate ~3× more toward tTRM-CM than control CM (P<0.0001)
- tMDM-CM also produces some migration but less than tTRM-CM (P=0.0003)
- Two independent experiments

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse-derived TRMs and tMDMs; KP-GFP target cells
- Conditioned medium harvested from sorted macrophage cultures (timing not specified in main text)

## Counter-evidence

- Transwell migration is not strictly EMT-specific; could reflect chemotaxis without full EMT programme
- The specific soluble factors in tTRM-CM are not identified
