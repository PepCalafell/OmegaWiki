## Statement

A single intravenous DT injection in Pf4CreCx3cr1DTR mice preferentially depletes Pf4+Cx3cr1+ CD206hi (FOLR2+) IMs (maximal by day 7, recovered by day 15) without affecting microvessel density, tissue area or lung morphology.

## Evidence summary

Flow cytometry time course (Fig. 2a) and H&E/PECAM-1/SMA staining (Supplementary Fig. 1) confirm selective depletion and preserved vasculature. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; depletion is preferential to CD206hi compartment, not single-subset-specific.
