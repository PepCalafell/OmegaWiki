## Statement

In the DEX-induced thymocyte apoptosis model, haematopoietic DNMT3A deletion in mice reduces all readouts of the DNMT3A-COX2-TGFβ1 pathway in thymic macrophages and elevates DUSP4 — recapitulating the in vitro mechanism in vivo.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence + ELISA in thymus sections of BMT chimeras at 18 h post DEX. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

C57BL/6J recipient BMT with Vav1Cre-conditional DNMT3A KO donor; thymus only; one time point (18 h).

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
