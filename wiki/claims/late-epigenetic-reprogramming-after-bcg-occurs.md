## Statement

The majority of long-term methylation increases after BCG occur after day 14, defining a delayed 'late' epigenetic effect distinct from the immediate transcriptional response.

## Evidence summary

Trajectory analysis (T0→T14→T90) shows a dip-then-rise pattern for most increased T90 sites; T90 vs T14 enriched in inflammatory/antiviral and nervous-system pathways.

## Conditions and scope

Pattern-level evidence across suggestive CpGs.

## Counter-evidence

No mechanistic driver identified; observational time course.

## Open questions

What signals trigger the delayed methylation changes between weeks 2 and 12.
