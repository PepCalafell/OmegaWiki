## Statement

CYP1A1 acts both as an AHR target gene and as the metabolic enzyme that degrades endogenous AHR agonists (FICZ, indoles), forming a self-limiting negative feedback loop; constitutive intestinal CYP1A1 expression depletes AHR ligands and breaks barrier immunity, while CYP1A1 inhibition raises AHR ligand levels and enhances IL-22.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]; primary data Schiering et al. 2017 (Nature).
