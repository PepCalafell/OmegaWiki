## Statement

Stimulation-agnostic expression shows cytokine production is strongly cell-type specific: IL-10 by CD14 monocytes, CD40L by CD4 T/MAIT cells, IL-32 by T cells (esp. Tregs), and chemokines dominated by monocytes/cDCs.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: correlational.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope.
