## Statement

CIMA is a population-scale multi-omics resource profiling 10,247,216 PBMCs from 428 Chinese adults by scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq, annotated into 73 hierarchical immune cell types.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (methodological): 6,484,974 scRNA-seq + 3,762,242 scATAC-seq cells; 428 adults (189 M/239 F, ages 20-77); 73 L4 cell types cross-validated with Azimuth and CellTypist (p.1-2).

## Conditions and scope

Chinese adults without active disease; PBMCs only.
