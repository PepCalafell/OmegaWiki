## Statement

In females, IFN-γ trained-immunity-associated CpG sites correlate with cortisol (and 11-deoxycortisol), whereas male sites show no sex-hormone correlation.

## Evidence summary

Hormone-correlation analysis ties female epigenetic-immune associations to glucocorticoids, supporting a hormonal axis in female trained immunity.

## Conditions and scope

Correlational; female subgroup; specific hormones.

## Counter-evidence

Small sets; correlation only.

## Open questions

Whether cortisol causally shapes female IFN-γ responses via methylation.
