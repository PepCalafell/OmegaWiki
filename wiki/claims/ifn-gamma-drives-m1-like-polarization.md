## Statement

IFN-γ induces a strong M1-like polarization program in CD14 monocytes — upregulating proinflammatory/anti-tumor markers (CXCL9, CD40, FCGR1A, SLAMF7) while suppressing M2-like and tumor-promoting markers (THBS1, CD163, CD36, FN1).

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.6). Consistent with the canonical M1 activator role of [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]]; involves [[cxcl9-chemokine]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating monocytes in vitro; "M1-like" used by analogy to macrophage polarization.

## Counter-evidence

None internal.
