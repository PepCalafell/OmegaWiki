## Statement

Hypoxia causes epigenetic silencing at promoter regions of key MMR genes (MSH2, MSH6, MLH1), driving frameshift mutations and microsatellite instability in a HIF1α-dependent manner; MLH1 protein decreases up to 24h and is sustained to 72h under chronic hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Chronic hypoxia, cell-line dependent. HIF1α-dependent. Involves MYC promoter-occupancy reduction and hypoxia-inducible repressor binding.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
