## Statement

Transient IL-2 stimulation (ASNeo2 followed by effector-induced dissociation) upregulates genes for apoptosis suppression (BCL2, NIBAN2, RNF157), cell-cycle regulation (CDKN2B, CDK6, CCND2, PIM1), cytokine-signalling suppression (SOCS2, CISH) and immune activation, but — unlike sustained stimulation — does not activate MYC- and E2F-driven cell-cycle-progression genes or mTORC-driven metabolic programs, indicating IL-2 signalling must be sustained to pass the G1/S checkpoint.

## Evidence summary

"Transient stimulation ... upregulated genes that are involved in suppressing apoptosis (BCL2, NIBAN2 and RNF157), cell-cycle regulation (CDKN2B, CDK6, CCND2 and PIM1), suppressing cytokine signalling (SOCS2 and CISH) and immune activation" (p.7). "Sustained stimulation activated genes associated with mTORC-driven metabolic changes and MYC- and E2F-driven cell-cycle progression ... but transient stimulation did not ... suggesting that IL-2 signalling must be sustained to pass the G1/S checkpoint."

## Conditions and scope

Primary human T cells; bulk RNA-seq at 6 h; n = 3.

## Counter-evidence

Single 6 h timepoint; gene-set interpretations rest on hallmark enrichment.
