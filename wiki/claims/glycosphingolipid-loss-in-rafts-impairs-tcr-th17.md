## Statement

Lipid-raft composition (glycosphingolipid content and α2,6-sialylation status) gates productive TCR signalling. Depleting either disrupts raft TCR translocation, reduces NF-κB activation, suppresses Th17 differentiation, and ameliorates ulcerative colitis in murine models.

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]].

## Conditions and scope

- CD4 T cells; UC disease models.

## Counter-evidence

- Glycosphingolipid depletion has off-target effects on other receptors.
