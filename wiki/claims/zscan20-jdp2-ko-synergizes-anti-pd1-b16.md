## Statement

In the B16-GP33 melanoma ACT model, combining `Zscan20` or `Jdp2` KO Cas9+ P14 cells with anti-PD1 mAb produces significantly smaller tumour volumes and weights and improved survival compared with isotype IgG2a control or either intervention alone, indicating ICB synergy from TEXterm single-state TF disruption.
