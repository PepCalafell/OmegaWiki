## Statement

Keratinocyte cancers have distinct interaction signatures: cSCC is enriched for osteopontin (SPP1)-integrin/CD44 interactions and angiogenesis pairs, while BCC is enriched for WNT signaling (WNT5A-FZD) and angiogenesis-related interactions — indicating angiogenesis is upregulated in both but via different regulatory routes.

## Evidence summary

Per-cancer enriched LR pairs from MMCCI integration with mechanistic annotation (SPP1-integrin→PI3K/Akt/MAPK; WNT coordinating with Hedgehog in BCC). (p.10-11)

## Conditions and scope

cSCC and BCC; small cohort; interaction inference.

## Counter-evidence

Requires experimental validation; some pairs supported only computationally.

## Open questions

Whether WNT5A-FZD targeting affects BCC proliferation alongside Hedgehog inhibition.
