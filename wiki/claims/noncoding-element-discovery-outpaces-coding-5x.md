## Statement

After the HGP draft, discovery of non-protein-coding genomic elements grew explosively, outpacing protein-coding gene discovery by roughly fivefold and continuing to grow.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "So far, that growth has outstripped the discovery of protein-coding genes by a factor of five, and shows no signs of slowing." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

Includes lncRNAs, promoters, enhancers, regulatory motifs, SNPs, pseudogenes.
