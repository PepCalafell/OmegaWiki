## Statement

Cytokine pairs regulate a substantial fraction of genes synergistically or antagonistically relative to their single-cytokine baselines, with proportions varying by tissue (highest in liver, lowest in bone marrow).

## Conditions and scope

Specific to the three TNF-anchored pairs at fixed sublethal doses; quantitative fractions are dose-dependent.
