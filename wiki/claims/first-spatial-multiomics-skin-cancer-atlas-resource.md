## Statement

This work constitutes the first comprehensive spatial multiomics reference for the three major skin cancers (cSCC, BCC, melanoma), integrating 12 orthogonal single-cell and spatial technologies across 24 donors and made publicly available as an interactive resource (skincanceratlas.com / skInteractive).

## Evidence summary

12 enumerated technologies; matched healthy/cancer cSCC and a benign→invasive melanocytic continuum; public browser-based atlas, gene explorer, and interactome. (p.1, p.14, p.18)

## Conditions and scope

European-ancestry-relevant cancers comprising ~70% of all cancers; 24 patients total.

## Counter-evidence

Small cohort limits population generalisability (mitigated by GWAS integration).

## Open questions
