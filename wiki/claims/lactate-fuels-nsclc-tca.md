## Statement

In vivo heavy-isotope tracing in NSCLC patients demonstrates that lactate, traditionally considered a waste product, is utilized by the tumour to fuel the TCA cycle. This refines the Warburg/Cori paradigm by adding a direct lactate-oxidation route in addition to the classical Cori shunt.

## Evidence summary

Faubert / Hensley NSCLC in-vivo tracing; multiple subsequent confirmations.

## Conditions and scope

NSCLC tumours; tumour subtype dependence (squamous vs adenocarcinoma) not fully resolved.

## Counter-evidence

Some tumour types may rely more on glucose oxidation directly; metabolism is heterogeneous.

## Linked ideas

- Affects rationale for lactate-shuttle inhibitor (MCT1/MCT4) therapeutics.

## Open questions

- Does lactate-TCA utilization correlate with cachexia severity?
