## Statement

DHPS-deficient macrophages are larger, rounder and more spherical with reduced overlap onto stromal/tissue structures, linking the adhesion/signalling defect to altered cell shape and tissue positioning.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 4j-n, Supplementary Fig. 18).

## Conditions and scope

Lung, kidney and microglia imaging; in vitro and in situ.

None.
