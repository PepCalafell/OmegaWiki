## Statement

Native immune organoid approaches (ALI, PDOTS) are inherently low-throughput and difficult to genetically engineer due to multicellularity and architecture preservation.

## Evidence summary

Lentiviral and CRISPR manipulations are more easily performed in conventional organoids; organoid cultures involving tissue fragments or slices are less amenable to such manipulations and are complicated by obstacles to cell type-specific gene expression (Polak et al. 2024, p.530).

## Conditions and scope

ALI and PDOTS platforms with preserved multicellular architecture.
