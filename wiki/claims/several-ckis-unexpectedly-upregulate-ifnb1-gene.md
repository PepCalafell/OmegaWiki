## Statement

A set of mechanistically diverse CKIs unexpectedly increased Ifnb1 gene expression in LPS-stimulated macrophages; for BGT-226, Lapatinib and Tofacitinib this was associated with H3K27ac gain at IRF3-bound CREs, suggesting TBK1/IRF3 hyperactivation.

## Evidence summary

Seven inhibitors upregulated Ifnb1 without forming a coherent network cluster; three showed IRF3-bound CRE hyperacetylation and pervasive interferon-gene super-induction. Links [[foundations/irf3-interferon-regulatory-factor-3]], [[foundations/type-interferon-ifna-ifnb]], [[concepts/cki-induced-retroelement-activation-drives-interferon]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS stimulation.

## Counter-evidence

The Ifnb1-upregulating CKIs were otherwise epigenomically divergent, so a single shared mechanism is not established.
