## Statement
Genomic analysis and lineage tracing in TP53-mutant human gastric organoids cultured over 2 years revealed clonal evolution, selection and accumulation of CNVs during preneoplasia.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
