## Statement

Macrophage efferoptosis requires the TLR adaptor TRIF; Trif-/- macrophages are largely protected from TNF+neutrophil-induced death.

## Evidence summary

TRIF requirement is macrophage-intrinsic (adoptive coculture). (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "there was a substantial reduction of cell death in Trif-/- and Tnfr1-/- macrophages cultured under the same conditions"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
