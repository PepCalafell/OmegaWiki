## Statement

By interaction score (sender strength × receiver sensitivity), IL-32 emerges as a dominant signaling molecule linking all T-cell subtypes to all other cell types; NK CD56hi signals to nearly all cell types whereas NK CD56low generates no connections.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: mechanistic.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
