## Statement

In a cross-disease application of the HIRISA scoring framework, autoimmune cohorts (SLE > RA) exhibit the strongest IFN-α responses — exceeding myeloma, infection, and aging — with B cells showing particularly high IFN-I activity.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.8-9). Demonstrates the [[ifn-ifn-ii-activity-deconvolution-scoring]] framework as a standardized cross-disease comparator.

## Conditions and scope

Public datasets uniformly re-annotated; relative-to-control scoring.

## Counter-evidence

Cross-cohort batch effects partly mitigated but not eliminated.

## Open questions
