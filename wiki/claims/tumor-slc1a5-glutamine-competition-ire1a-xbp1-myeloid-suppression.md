## Statement

Tumour cells upregulate the glutamine transporter SLC1A5 to outcompete myeloid cells for glutamine, depleting glutamine in the TME and triggering IRE1α/XBP1 endoplasmic-reticulum-stress signalling in myeloid cells, with increased GPR109A expression and immunosuppressive polarisation.

## Evidence summary

- Yang et al. liver cancer study reviewed in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Strongest evidence in liver cancer; mechanism likely broader.

## Counter-evidence

- Tumour glutamine demand varies; SLC1A5-low tumours show different myeloid programming.

## Linked ideas

- Tumour-selective SLC1A5 inhibition to relieve myeloid suppression.

## Open questions

- Which myeloid subsets (TAMs, MDSCs, monocytes) are most sensitive to glutamine deprivation in vivo?
