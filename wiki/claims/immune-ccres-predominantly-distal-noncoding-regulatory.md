## Statement

The CIMA cCRE catalog is dominated by intronic, intergenic, and transposable-element regions, with only a small minority in promoters.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (quantitative): 338,036 cCREs (501-bp); 6.9% promoter, 31.2% intronic, 20.7% intergenic, 32.9% overlapping transposable elements (p.6).

## Conditions and scope

scATAC-seq–defined open chromatin in immune cell types.
