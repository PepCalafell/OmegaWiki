## Statement

In vivo, MHC-II+ TAMs have ~27,578 chromatin sites more accessible than angiogenic TAMs, versus only ~3,979 more accessible in angiogenic TAMs, indicating MHC-II+ TAMs occupy a more permissive, plastic chromatin state.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2066) — quantitative evidence. ATAC-seq of CX3CR1/MHC-II-sorted TAMs from 4T1 tumors; MHC-II loci closed in angiogenic TAMs.
  - Quote: "a large number of chromatin sites (n = 27,578) were more accessible in MHC-II+ TAMs compared with angiogenic TAMs, whereas only a limited set (n = 3,979) of chromatin sites showed greater accessibility in angiogenic TAMs"

## Conditions and scope

4T1 (and MC38) models; sorted-population ATAC-seq.
