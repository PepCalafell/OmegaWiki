## Statement

Xenium spatial transcriptomics showed CD206hi IMs (Folr2/Cd163/Mmp9) localize adjacent to bronchial airways and visceral pleura, whereas CD206lo IMs (Mmp12) and Fn1+Vcan+ recMacs predominate in tumor-dense regions.

## Evidence summary

Spatial mapping of marker genes (Fig. 3a); high-resolution imaging (Fig. 3c). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; targeted Xenium panel, n=2 melanoma + n=2 adenocarcinoma.
