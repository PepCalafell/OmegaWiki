## Statement

Hypoxia upregulates lipid storage-related genes — including Plin2, Plin3, Hilpda, Soat1, and Lgals3 — in both microglia (BV2) and macrophages (BMDM), alongside triglyceride-synthesis genes, indicating enhanced lipid production and storage capacity in hypoxic GAMs.

## Evidence summary

RNA-seq analysis of lipid metabolism pathways (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.12).

> "Lipid storage-related genes were upregulated in both cell types (Lgals3, perilipin 2 [Plin2], Plin3, hypoxia inducible lipid droplet associated (Hilpda), and sterol O-acyltransferase 1 [Soat1])"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 and BMDM; in vitro hypoxia; cholesterol-biosynthesis axis was cell-type-divergent.

## Counter-evidence

Fatty-acid uptake genes (Fabp3/4/5/7) were decreased, indicating a storage-favoring rather than uptake-favoring shift.
