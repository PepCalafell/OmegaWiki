## Statement

ARNT2, the ~80%-identical ARNT paralog primarily expressed in kidney, central nervous system, and retinal epithelium, dimerises with AHR but produces only minimal canonical CYP1A1 induction and reduces TCDD-driven CYP1A1 protein induction by ~30%. ARNT2 likely behaves as an inhibitor rather than facilitator of canonical AHR activation in tissues where it is dominantly expressed.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] (Opitz et al. 2023) reviews the relevant murine Hepa-1 study.

## Conditions and scope

Murine cell-line evidence; tissue-specific consequences (CNS, kidney) not directly tested.

## Counter-evidence

3-methylcholanthrene and BaP-driven AHR activation predominantly forms AHR-ARNT (not AHR-ARNT2) complexes — ligand-specific competition is possible.

## Open questions
