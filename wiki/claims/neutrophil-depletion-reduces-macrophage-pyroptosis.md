## Statement

Depleting neutrophils (1A8, 24h) reduces splenic macrophage caspase-8 and GSDMD activation by ~50%, implicating neutrophils as drivers of macrophage pyroptosis.

## Evidence summary

Also eliminates GSDMD+ macrophage aggregates. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.3).

> "neutrophil depletion decreased the percentage of CD68+ cells with CC8 and cleaved GSDMD by ~50%"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
