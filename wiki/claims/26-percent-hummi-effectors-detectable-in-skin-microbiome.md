## Statement

Commensal T3SS effectors are not gut-exclusive: roughly a quarter of HuMMI effectors are
also detectable in skin microbiome samples, indicating cross-niche sharing.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450): "26% of HuMMI effectors are
detectable in skin microbiome samples, suggesting that commensal effectors are shared
across ecological niches." Offered as one explanation for enrichment of skin/lung disease
associations.

## Conditions and scope

Sequence detection in skin metagenomes; functional secretion in skin not tested.

## Counter-evidence

Detection ≠ active injection in the skin niche.

## Linked ideas

Relates to [[effector-targets-enriched-gwas-immune-metabolic-disease-variation]].

## Open questions

Whether skin-resident strains actively inject these effectors locally.
