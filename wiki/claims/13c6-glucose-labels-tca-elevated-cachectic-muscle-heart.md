## Statement

In cachectic C26 mice, glucose-derived 13C label accumulates in TCA intermediates of skeletal and cardiac muscle to a much greater extent than in controls — including in higher isotopologues (M+3, M+4) that require anaplerotic or multi-turn flux — demonstrating a glucose-availability-dependent muscle hypermetabolism phenotype that is invisible to bulk metabolite measurements.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "we observed an unexpected increase in the labelling of TCA metabolites in GC muscle, soleus muscle and heart upon glucose injection... the labelling of higher isotopologues (M + 3 and more) of TCA cycle metabolites suggested a rewiring of glucose flux and hypermetabolism."

## Conditions and scope

In vivo intraperitoneal tracer; short (1 h) tracer window; limited to fast-turnover pools.

## Counter-evidence

Pre-cax mice show only trends — early hypermetabolism is statistically softer than full Cax.
