## Statement

Cancer-associated fibroblast STING activation produces CXCR3 chemokines (CXCL9/10/11) that recruit cytotoxic T cells.

## Evidence summary

Arwert 2020 PDAC: CAF STING activation drives type I IFN and T cell migration. Knelson 2022: in human pleural mesothelioma explants, CXCR3 ligand expression was particularly pronounced in CAFs relative to other TME cells after STING agonism. CAF compartment under-explored.

## Conditions and scope

Pancreatic, head/neck, and pleural mesothelioma tumor contexts; murine studies dominate.
