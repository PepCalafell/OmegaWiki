## Statement

IDO1 and TDO degrade tryptophan to kynurenine in the TME, promoting Treg activation, MDSC expansion and effector-T-cell suppression. Despite strong preclinical support, IDO1 inhibition (epacadostat) failed to improve PFS/OS when combined with pembrolizumab in metastatic melanoma (ECHO-301). The strategy is being revisited with biomarker-driven patient selection.

## Evidence summary

- Mechanistic studies of IDO1/TDO in TME.
- ECHO-301 phase 3 melanoma trial failure (Long et al. 2019).

## Conditions and scope

- Solid tumours, especially melanoma; brain tumours via TDO.

## Counter-evidence

- New biomarker-selected trials may demonstrate context-specific benefit.

## Linked ideas

- Pathway-redundancy with IDO2 and TDO obscures monotherapy effects.
- AhR signalling downstream of kynurenine independently drives immunosuppression.

## Open questions

- Will biomarker-stratified IDO1 trials succeed where pan-melanoma trials failed?
