## Statement

Tumour lactate efflux via MCT1/MCT4 acidifies the TME; the resulting acidosis suppresses cytotoxic T-cell and NK-cell activation while leaving FOXP3+ Tregs functionally intact, enriching the immunosuppressive compartment.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple primary studies reviewed in Ricci 2025.
- Treg resistance to lactate antiproliferative effects.
- Bicarbonate-transporter (SLC4A4) inhibition in PDAC mitigates acidosis and restores immunity.

## Conditions and scope

- Most evident in glycolytic, lactate-rich tumours.

## Counter-evidence

- Lactate also serves as a beneficial carbon source for some immune-cell subsets; biology is bidirectional.

## Linked ideas

- pH normalisation as adjuvant to ICB.

## Open questions

- Optimal pharmacological strategy to neutralise TME acidosis without disturbing systemic pH.
