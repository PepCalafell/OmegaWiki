## Statement

In triple-negative breast cancer, hypoxia alters expression of cell-cycle and SAC genes CDC20, BUB1, PLK1 and KIF4A independent of gene copy number or mutation, implicating hypoxia alone as a driver of mitotic dysfunction.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

TNBC bioinformatic analysis; mechanism independent of CN/mutation.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
