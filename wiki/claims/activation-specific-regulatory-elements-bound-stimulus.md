## Statement
The regulatory elements gained in each activation state are predicted to be bound by stimulus-specific transcription factors (IRF1/MIFNγ, STAT6/MIL4, FOSL2/MTPP).

## Evidence summary
Top positional weight matrices at activation-specific accessible enhancers matched signal-appropriate TFs, linking specific stimuli to specific TF programs.

## Conditions and scope
Bioinformatic prediction, not ChIP validation of these TFs.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Open questions
