## Statement

The HIF hydroxylases provide hypoxic sensitivity to NF-κB by oxygen-dependent hydroxylation of NF-κB pathway components (IKKβ, p105/NFKB1, IκBα), while cytokine-/bacterially-driven NF-κB transcriptionally induces HIF1α — making the two master inflammatory/hypoxic pathways mechanistically interdependent.

## Conditions and scope

A second, transcription-level mechanism (distinct from cooperative chromatin binding) by which hypoxia regulates inflammatory gene expression.
