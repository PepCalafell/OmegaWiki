## Statement

Within LUAD tumour sections, tumour-cell response-to-hypoxia signature score is highest near tumour-associated neutrophils and decreases monotonically with spatial distance from them.

## Evidence summary

Extended Data Fig. 7a shows the distance-dependent anticorrelation; method-agnostic — recapitulated by STAGATE.

## Open questions

- Is hypoxia driving neutrophil recruitment, or are neutrophils driving local tumour hypoxia (oxygen consumption / vascular disruption)?
