## Statement

More than 300,000 regulatory network interactions — protein–non-coding and protein–protein bindings — have been charted, providing a network/systems-level complement to the single-gene Mendelian view.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Today, more than 300,000 regulatory network interactions have been charted — proteins binding with non-coding regions or with other proteins." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

Charted interactions to ~2017; interactome remains incomplete.

## Counter-evidence

Charted interactions are a small, biased sample of the true interactome.
