## Statement

In lymphoid malignancies, the ratio of ARNT isoform 1 (long) to ARNT isoform 3 (short) — controlled by the splicing factor RBFOX2 — sets AHR transcriptional output. ARNT iso1 promotes tumour growth/survival; loss of iso1 biases AHR response toward inflammation, while loss of iso3 biases toward immunosuppression. CKII phosphorylation of ARNT iso1 (S77) is required for optimal AHR activity.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews studies in Karpas 299 and Peer cell lines plus B/T cell baselines.

## Conditions and scope

Lymphoid malignancy context. Whether the iso1/iso3 axis operates in solid tumours is open.
