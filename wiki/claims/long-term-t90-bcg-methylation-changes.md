## Statement

DNA methylation changes persisting to day 90 after BCG vaccination, enriched in infection/immune pathways, represent durable epigenetic memory.

## Evidence summary

Long-term CpGs map to infection-response pathways and overlap trained-immunity-associated sites, unlike the reversible short-term changes.

## Conditions and scope

Memory inferred at 90 days; longer durability untested; whole-blood signal.

## Counter-evidence

Association-level; no control for natural temporal/seasonal drift beyond sensitivity analysis.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

How long beyond 90 days the memory persists and in which cells.
