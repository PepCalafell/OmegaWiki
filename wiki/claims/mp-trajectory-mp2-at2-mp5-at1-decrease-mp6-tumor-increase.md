## Statement

Lung adenocarcinoma progression is mirrored by a smooth, statistically significant transcriptional gradient — loss of alveolar (AT2, AT1) MPs and gain of tumor-associated MP6 — providing a quantitative trajectory for staging at the molecular level.

## Linked ideas

- MP-based scoring could complement histology for lesion staging where morphology is ambiguous (e.g., AIS vs MIA).
