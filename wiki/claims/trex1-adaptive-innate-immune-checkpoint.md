## Statement

TREX1 functions as an adaptive negative-feedback innate immune checkpoint restraining STING-IFN in cancer cells.

## Evidence summary

Tani 2024 Cancer Discov: TREX1 induced downstream of STING-IFN; inhibition restores STING-IFN, recruits T/NK cells, sensitizes to anti-PD-1. Marinkovic 2025 and Xing 2025 Cancer Res: systemic TREX1 inactivation produces selective TME inflammation while sparing T cells.

## Conditions and scope

Tumor cells with intact cGAS/STING; preclinical models; first-in-class TREX1 inhibitors in development.
