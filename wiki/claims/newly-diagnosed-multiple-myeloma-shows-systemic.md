## Statement

Newly diagnosed multiple myeloma (NDMM) is characterized by systemic and bone-marrow elevation of both IFN-I and IFN-II responses across major immune subsets, accompanied by raised plasma IFN-α2A/IFN-β and modestly elevated IFN-γ.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.7, p.9), in both PBMC and BMMC compartments, validating HIRISA-predicted subset-specific ISGs in vivo. Uses the [[ifn-ifn-ii-activity-deconvolution-scoring]] framework.

## Conditions and scope

NDMM cohorts; substantial interpatient heterogeneity.

## Counter-evidence

IFN responses varied widely among individuals due to disease heterogeneity.

## Open questions

Whether elevated marrow IFN-γ contributes to immune dysregulation in the myeloma niche.
