## Statement

COX2-derived PGE2 is the obligate intermediate transducing efferocytosis to TGF-β1 induction in macrophages; blocking COX2 or PGE synthase abolishes AC-induced Tgfb1, while blocking Tgfb1 does not feed back on Ptgs2.

## Evidence summary

siPtgs2 in BMDMs, NS-398 in HMDMs, and siPtges all block AC-induced Tgfb1. siTgfb1 does not block AC-induced Ptgs2 (ED Fig. 1l-m). Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM/HMDM in vitro.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
