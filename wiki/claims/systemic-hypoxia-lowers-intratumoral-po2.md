## Statement

Mice housed in systemic hypoxia have lower directly-measured intratumoral pO2 than normoxic controls, confirming systemic hypoxia translates into local tumor hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.3): "tumors from mice housed in hypoxic conditions exhibited lower pO2 than those from normoxic counterparts" — Clark-type electrode measurement in exposed subcutaneous tumors of intubated mice (Fig.1d); normoxia n=12, hypoxia n=7. (type: quantitative, strength: strong)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
