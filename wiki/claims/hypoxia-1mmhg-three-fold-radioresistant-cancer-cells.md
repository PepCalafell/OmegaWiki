## Statement

At pO₂ ≈ 1 mmHg, cancer cells are approximately 3× more resistant to ionizing radiation than well-oxygenated cells (oxygen enhancement ratio, OER ≈ 3). Lethal DNA DSB formation under hypoxia is reduced 2–3-fold. HIF1α-induced DNA-PK and SPINK1 paracrine signaling (EGFR/NRF2 antioxidant) further enhance repair and neighbour protection.

## Evidence summary

Cited from [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023), consistent with the canonical Gray 1953 result.

## Conditions and scope

Acute / chronic hypoxia at clinically meaningful pO₂. OER varies with dose fractionation; hyperfractionation partially mitigates.

## Counter-evidence

Reoxygenation between fractions reduces effective OER; some cell lines show OER closer to 2.
