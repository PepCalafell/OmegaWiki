## Statement

Most EPAS1-mutant sympathetic CCHD-PPGL tumors display microsatellite instability (loss of mismatch-repair proteins), unlike parasympathetic EPAS1-WT tumors.

## Evidence summary

IHC for MMR proteins (see [[foundations/microsatellite-instability-msi]]); links systemic hypoxia to DNA-repair impairment.

## Conditions and scope

Small sample (n=9 sympathetic); IHC-based MSI assessment.

## Counter-evidence

2/9 sympathetic tumors did not show clear MMR loss.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Mechanistic link from hypoxia/HIF2α to acquired MMR loss.
