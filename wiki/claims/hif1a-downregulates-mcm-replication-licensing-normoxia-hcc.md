## Statement

Under normoxia, HIF-1α suppresses the expression of DNA-replication-licensing helicase components (represented by MCM3) specifically in hepatocellular carcinoma (Huh7) cells and not in HeLa cells, linking normoxic HIF-1α activity to cell-cycle/replication control; Huh7 WT and HIF1A-knockout cells also differ in their G1 fraction.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3155–3156) — proteomics + MCM3 immunoblot + FACS.
  - Quote: "the expression of several proteins involved in the licensing of DNA replication was significantly downregulated by HIF-1α in Huh7 cells ... but not in HeLa cells. The proteomic data were verified in the case of MCM3 ... by immunoblotting."
  - Cell-cycle FACS: WT Huh7 70% G1 vs HIF1A−/− 60.1% G1; HeLa WT vs KO similar (51.8% vs 53.5%).

## Conditions and scope

Single verified marker (MCM3) of a broader licensing cluster; correlational link between HIF-1α and cell-cycle distribution, not a direct target-gene demonstration.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. Modest cell-cycle-fraction differences; direction (HIF-1α represses licensing) is proteome-derived.
