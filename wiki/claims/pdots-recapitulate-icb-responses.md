## Statement

PDOTS and MDOTS retain autologous lymphocytes and myeloid cells and recapitulate in vivo ICB responses and resistance.

## Evidence summary

MDOTS and PDOTS retain autologous lymphocytes (B and T cells) and myeloid cells (monocytes, DCs, MDSCs and TAMs) and successfully assessed immunotherapy responses, including recapitulating in vivo responses and resistance to ICB (Polak et al. 2024, p.527; Jenkins et al. 2018).

## Conditions and scope

Microfluidic culture of 40-100 μm tumour fragments in central collagen channel.
