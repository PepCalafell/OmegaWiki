## Statement

High-altitude human EPAS1 adaptation is loss-of-function (reducing HIF2α/EPO to avoid polycythemia), the functional inverse of the gain-of-function EPAS1 mutations selected in hypoxia-developed tumors — convergence on the gene with opposite molecular consequences (functional plasticity).

## Evidence summary

Literature synthesis plus the paper's tumor findings; confirmed in EPAS1-edited mice for the reproductive-fitness phenotype.

## Conditions and scope

Defines the "functional plasticity" facet of the convergence thesis; see [[concepts/convergent-epas1-adaptation-high-altitude-populations]].

## Counter-evidence

Some Andean variants (H194R) are also inactivating but outside the ODD, showing variant heterogeneity.
