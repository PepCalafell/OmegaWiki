## Statement

IFNγ induces a Mac-b polarization state overexpressing M1-associated pro-inflammatory genes (Cxcl9, Cxcl10), while IL-4 and IL-13 induce a distinct Mac-e state marked by Chchd10, Glrx, Retnla — recapitulating and refining the classical M1/M2 paradigm at scRNA-seq resolution.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Marco+ macrophages in mouse lymph node, in vivo.
