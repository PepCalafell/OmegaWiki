## Statement

PCAWG analysis of 1,188 tumours across 27 cancer types showed that tumours with high hypoxia scores have elevated SBS6 (HR/MMR-deficient), ID6 (HRD), SBS21 and ID2 (MMR-deficient) signatures in the absence of germline DDR mutations, matching the cell-line model of hypoxia-induced HR and MMR suppression.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Population-scale WGS analysis (PCAWG). Hypoxia scoring via mRNA-based hypoxia signatures.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
