## Statement

Broadly expressed IFNAR1/2 receptor abundance does not predict the magnitude of the IFN-I response, whereas IFN-γ and IFN-λ1 response magnitudes do correlate with cognate receptor (IFNGR2, IFNLR1) expression; IFN-γ induces negative-feedback downregulation of its own receptor chains.
