## Statement

Type III secretion systems are common, not rare, among commensal gut Pseudomonadota:
roughly 79% of reference genomes carry a complete T3SS.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]]: EffectiveDB detected complete T3SS in
449/568 (79%) gut Pseudomonadota reference genomes (p.444). The abstract states "80% of
Pseudomonadota from healthy gut microbiomes also have intact type III secretion systems."

## Conditions and scope

Reference-genome based; culturing bias acknowledged. In 16,179 MAGs only 770 (5%) showed
complete T3SS, likely an underestimate from assembly fragmentation.

## Counter-evidence

Detection tool sensitivity may both miss divergent systems and (less likely) overcall.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[commensal-t3ss-host-directed-secretion]].

## Open questions

True prevalence given undetectable divergent systems.
