## Statement

The ~90% EPAS1 variant prevalence in hypoxia-developed PPGL quantitatively mirrors the ~90% prevalence of selected EPAS1 variants in Tibetans and Sherpas — convergent selection across populations and tumors.

## Evidence summary

Cross-comparison of allele/mutation frequencies; see [[concepts/convergent-epas1-adaptation-high-altitude-populations]].

## Conditions and scope

Convergence is at the gene level; variant types and functional effects differ (LOF in populations, GOF in tumors).

## Counter-evidence

Andean highlanders (more recent, no introgression) show much lower EPAS1 frequencies, indicating convergence is not universal across all high-altitude populations.

## Open questions
