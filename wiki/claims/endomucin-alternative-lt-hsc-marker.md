## Statement

Endomucin may serve as a more specific LT-HSC marker than CD90 or CD49f, at least in human BM scp-MS data.

## Evidence summary

Direct protein abundance comparison at the HSC cluster (Fig. S2B). Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]].

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+ scp-MS dataset (n=6 donors). Not functionally validated (no xenograft or LTC-IC on Endomucin-sorted cells).

## Counter-evidence

CD90 and CD49f are extensively validated as LT-HSC markers in xenotransplant studies; Endomucin is not yet validated at this functional level in humans.

## Open questions

- Does Endomucin-enriched sorting prospectively increase LTC-IC / xenograft frequency vs CD90/CD49f sorting?
