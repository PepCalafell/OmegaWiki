## Statement

Applying the Trm1 AD/PV signature to other inflammatory dermatoses, the bullous pemphigoid sample segregated with atopic dermatitis (consistent with BP's type-2 inflammation and dupilumab responsiveness) and the lichen planus samples segregated with psoriasis (consistent with LP's TH1/TH17 activity and response to IL17 antagonists).

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results and Discussion of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]. Supports [[concepts/trm1-th2-th17-molecular-classification-inflammatory]].

## Conditions and scope

Very small n (1 BP, 2 LP); illustrative extension of the framework.

## Counter-evidence

Single/duplicate samples cannot establish disease-level generalization.
