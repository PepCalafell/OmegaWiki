## Statement

Across two independent Xenium cohorts (5K Prime + 5K, and 298-gene + 100-gene custom add-on TMA), the macrophage subclusters with highest IL1B expression are significantly more abundant in epithelial neighborhoods of KACs, AAH, and inflamed AT2 cells than around normal alveolar or advanced tumor cells.

## Conditions and scope

Holds at single-cell spatial resolution; iStar super-pixel ST corroborates IL1B-rich macrophage colonies surrounding RPII and AAH.
