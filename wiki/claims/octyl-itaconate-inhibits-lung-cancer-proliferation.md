## Statement

Octyl itaconate potently inhibits proliferation across lung cancer cell lines irrespective of KEAP1 mutational status, demonstrating that its anti-proliferative action is independent of the canonical KEAP1/NRF2 axis.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1464) — pharmacological evidence.
  - Quote: "Octyl Ita potently inhibited proliferation across lung cancer cell lines regardless of Kelch-like ECH-associated protein 1 (KEAP1) status, which indicated KEAP1/NRF2-independent activity."

## Conditions and scope

Cancer-cell arm only; contrasts with NRF2-dependent effects in macrophages (see [[claims/itaconate-ppp-effects-nrf2-independent-cancer]]).
