## Statement

TCDD-driven AHR activation increases DNA methylation at the promoters of canonical tumour suppressors p16(INK4a)/CDKN2A and p53/TP53, reducing their transcription. AHR-dependent hypermethylation also silences BRCA1 in MCF-7 breast cancer cells. AHR can therefore act as a tumour promoter via epigenetic repression of tumour-suppressor genes.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews refs 237-238.

## Conditions and scope

Strongest evidence in MCF-7 + TCDD; tissue-/ligand-specific generalisation not established.

## Counter-evidence

— in other contexts, AHR has documented tumour-suppressive functions (lung-cancer metastasis suppression; AHRR-tumour-suppressor role) — emphasising that the epigenetic-tumour-promoter and the tumour-suppressor activities co-exist.
