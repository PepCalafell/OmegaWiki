## Statement

The inferred dynamics distinguish lineages: megakaryocyte and erythroid lineages have coupled growth and differentiation rates, while the neutrophil lineage shows a decoupled growth/differentiation relationship.

## Evidence summary

Pattern derived from the model's per-lineage rate estimates, supported by cell-cycle correlation for Mk/Ery.

## Conditions and scope

Model-derived, single dataset; no orthogonal experimental confirmation of the coupling.

## Counter-evidence

Neutrophil growth–cell-cycle correlation is lost at later (homeostatic) stages.
