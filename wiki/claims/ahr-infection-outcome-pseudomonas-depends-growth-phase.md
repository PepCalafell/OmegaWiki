## Statement

Infection outcome in P. aeruginosa pneumonia is determined by the ratio of AHR-activating to AHR-inhibiting quorum-sensing molecules expressed by the inoculum. Late-growth-phase bacteria (high phenazine agonists) are cleared better in WT than in Ahr-KO mice; early-growth-phase bacteria (high HSL/HHQ antagonists) accumulate more in WT than in Ahr-KO mice. The AHR thus acts as a density-aware regulator that times immune response intensity to virulence-factor expression.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] (Fig. 4, refs 9–10).

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in murine acute pneumonia models; whether the same applies to chronic CF airway infection (where stationary-phase biofilm bacteria dominate) is untested.

## Counter-evidence

None within the review's scope.
