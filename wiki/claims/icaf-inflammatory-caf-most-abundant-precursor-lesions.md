## Statement

Inflammatory CAFs are not a feature of established LUAD but of *precursor* lesions, paralleling the precursor-restricted distribution of IL1B-IL1R1 niches and reinforcing a stage-specific stromal-immune-epithelial inflammatory axis.

## Linked ideas

- iCAFs may be a non-immune mediator of the precancer-stage proinflammatory niche; their decline in LUAD is concordant with proinflammatory niche dissipation.
