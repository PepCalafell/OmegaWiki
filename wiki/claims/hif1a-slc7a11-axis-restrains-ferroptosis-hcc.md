## Statement

In hepatocellular carcinoma cells, HIF-1α upregulates SLC7A11 (the cystine/glutamate antiporter xCT), elevating intracellular cysteine for glutathione synthesis, sustaining GPX4 activity, and thereby restraining lipid-peroxidation-dependent ferroptotic death. In hepatic stellate cells, sorafenib targeting this axis enables ferroptosis to reverse fibrosis. The HIF-1α/SLC7A11 module is therefore a context-dependent ferroptosis brake.

## Evidence summary

Fan et al. and related primary studies cited in Luo 2022. HIF-1α ChIP at SLC7A11 promoter confirmed in HCC lines; SLC7A11 knockdown reverses HIF-1α-mediated ferroptosis resistance.

## Conditions and scope

- HCC: HIF-1α/SLC7A11 confers ferroptosis resistance
- Hepatic stellate cells: same axis mobilizable for fibrosis reversal
- Cardiomyocytes: SENP1-deSUMOylated HIF-1α also restrains ferroptosis

## Counter-evidence

DEHP/MEHP-induced HIF-1α/HO-1 axis can promote (rather than restrain) ferroptosis — outcome depends on which downstream HIF target dominates.
