## Statement

Effective MiTE-144 therapy upregulates both inflammatory monocyte-like and hypoxia-associated TAM modules simultaneously, with concurrent loss of IFN-response. This co-occurrence challenges simple M1/M2 or "inflammatory vs hypoxic" TAM dichotomies and suggests a hypoxia-marked but functionally-permissive TAM state under cytokine pressure.
