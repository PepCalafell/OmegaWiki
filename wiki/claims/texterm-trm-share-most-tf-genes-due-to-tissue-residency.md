## Statement

Among the nine CD8+ T cell states profiled, TEXterm and TRM cells share the largest overlap of TF genes (examples include `Egr2`, `Crem`, `Prdm1`), consistent with their shared tissue residency niche and complicating distinction by transcriptional readouts alone.
