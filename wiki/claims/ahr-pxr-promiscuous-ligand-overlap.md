## Statement

A substantial fraction of compounds annotated as AHR agonists also activate the pregnane X receptor (PXR). Because PXR shares regulatory targets (xenobiotic metabolism, CYP3A4/CYP2B6) and signalling partners (RXR, co-activators), parallel PXR activation may explain ligand-specific differences attributed to AHR alone.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] cites Denison et al.'s biochemical surveys showing high AHR/PXR agonist overlap.

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type-dependent (PXR is highly expressed in liver and intestine, less so elsewhere). Effects of AHR agonists in PXR-low contexts should be cleaner.

## Counter-evidence

— none surveyed in this paper.
