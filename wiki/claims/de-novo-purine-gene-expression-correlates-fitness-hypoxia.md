## Statement

Hypoxia-induced changes in de novo purine synthesis genes (PPAT, MTHFD1, PAICS, ATIC) positively correlate with lineage-specific fitness, whereas purine-salvage gene changes do not; hypoxia-tolerant lines maintain biosynthetic gene expression.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.9): "Changes in expression of several key de novo purine synthesis genes, i.e., PPAT, MTHFD1, PAICS, and ATIC, were significantly and positively associated with relative fitness in hypoxia" — GENEVA scRNA-seq correlations (Fig.5b-d, S4); iPAGE enrichment of de novo purine genes among fitness-positive genes. (type: correlational, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
