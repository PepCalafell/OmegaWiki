## Statement

Activation of Pdyn+ skin-innervating sympathetic neurons is *sufficient* to mimic psychological stress in driving dermatitis exacerbation — confirming necessity (PdynNet-Abl/hM4Di) plus sufficiency.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], Fig. 4A–G, fig. S14.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only. Effect specific to MC903-pre-conditioned (inflamed) skin; not tested on naive skin in this experiment.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
