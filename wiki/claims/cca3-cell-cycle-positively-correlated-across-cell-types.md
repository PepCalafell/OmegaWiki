## Statement

In 3CA v2 tumours, proliferation rates across different cell types (malignant, T cell, macrophage, fibroblast, endothelial, etc.) are positively correlated, suggesting that TME-wide factors stimulate cycling in multiple cell types simultaneously.

## Evidence summary

Spearman correlation matrix (Fig. 5c) with FDR-corrected significant pairs.

## Conditions and scope

Correlational — does not establish causal TME cues.
