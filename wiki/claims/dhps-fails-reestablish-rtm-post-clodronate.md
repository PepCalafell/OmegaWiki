## Statement

Acute macrophage depletion shows DHPS-deficient monocytes are recruited and become CD64+CD11c+ macrophages but cannot complete differentiation into Siglec-F+ alveolar (or peritoneal) RTMs.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] using [[foundations/clodronate-liposomes]] (Fig. 2e-g, Extended Data Fig. 5f,g).

## Conditions and scope

Both lung (intratracheal) and peritoneal (i.p.) niches; control mice replenish RTMs normally.

## Counter-evidence

None.

## Open questions

- Whether providing exogenous DHPS targets rescues replenishment.
