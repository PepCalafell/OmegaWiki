## Statement

Belzutifan (Merck trade name Welireg; development codes MK-6482 / PT2977) is a small-molecule allosteric inhibitor of HIF-2α (EPAS1) that binds the PAS-B internal pocket and disrupts HIF-2α / ARNT (HIF-1β) heterodimerization. On 13 August 2021, the FDA approved Belzutifan for adult patients with Von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) disease who require therapy for VHL-disease-associated renal cell carcinoma (RCC), CNS hemangioblastoma, or pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (pNET) and do not require immediate surgery — making it the first FDA-approved HIF inhibitor of any kind. Belzutifan is selective for HIF-2α over HIF-1α despite high PAS-B sequence identity. Multiple combination trials with pembrolizumab, Lenvatinib, and cabozantinib are extending the indication to sporadic ccRCC and pheochromocytoma / paraganglioma.

- [[papers/hypoxia-driven-crosstalk-between-tumor-tumor]] — Bai 2022 *Molecular Cancer* review (p.13-14) details Belzutifan's mechanism, FDA approval, and combination strategies.
- Pivotal: Jonasch et al. 2021 *NEJM* 384:2036-2046 "Belzutifan for Renal Cell Carcinoma in von Hippel-Lindau Disease."
- Companion: Else et al. 2022 *NEJM* (Belzutifan in Pacak-Zhuang syndrome — somatic-mosaic activating EPAS1 mutation).
- Mechanism crystallography: Wallace et al. 2016 / Wehn et al. 2018 — PAS-B pocket binding mode.

## Conditions and scope

- FDA-approved indication is VHL-disease-associated tumors (RCC, hemangioblastoma, pNET).
- Sporadic ccRCC efficacy in monotherapy is variable; combinations are being tested.
- Belzutifan is HIF-2α-selective: tumors driven by HIF-1α are not addressed by Belzutifan alone.
- Anemia is the dominant adverse event (≥75% with hemoglobin <10 g/dL); on-target via HIF-2α-EPO axis.
- Hypoxia, fatigue, and dizziness are common.
- Pediatric / lifelong VHL therapy: long-term safety data accumulating.

## Counter-evidence

- Real-world durability beyond 2-3 years in VHL patients (lifelong therapy) is being established.
- Resistance: HIF-1α bypass, NRF2 upregulation, and other resistance mechanisms emerging.
- Pulmonary hypertension and metabolic effects in HIF-2α-loss-of-function animal models — relevance to human safety debated.
- Sporadic ccRCC trials show variable response; predictive biomarker beyond VHL status is still missing.

## Open questions

- Predictive biomarker for response in sporadic ccRCC and other tumors beyond VHL status alone.
- Optimal combination partner (pembrolizumab vs Lenvatinib vs cabozantinib) — head-to-head trials needed.
- Long-term safety in young VHL patients on lifelong therapy.
- Resistance mechanisms (HIF-1α bypass, NRF2 etc.) and second-line strategies.
- Whether HIF-2α-specific blockade affects TAM-side biology (Spint1 secretion in HIF-2α-high TAMs is HIF-2α-dependent — Belzutifan may release pro-HGF cleavage and partially offset its tumor-restraining effect).
