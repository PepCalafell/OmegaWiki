## Statement

IL1R1 is genetically required for full Kras G12D-driven LUAD development and for the abundance of LAMP3⁺/KRT8⁺ alveolar transformed cells, providing receptor-level causal evidence that completes the IL-1β-IL1R1-KAC-LUAD axis.

## Linked ideas

- Together with anti-IL-1β pharmacology, this knockout result establishes IL1R1 as a target rather than merely a marker.
