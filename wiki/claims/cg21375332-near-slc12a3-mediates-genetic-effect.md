## Statement

The SLC12A3-proximal CpG cg21375332 mediates about a third of a genetic variant's (rs604639) effect on BCG-induced IFN-γ change.

## Evidence summary

Quantified proportion mediated (32.7%); biologically plausible via SLC12A3's role in IL-18-induced IFN-γ.

## Conditions and scope

Single mediation triplet; in silico.

## Counter-evidence

Not experimentally validated; observational.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether SLC12A3 methylation causally tunes IL-18/IFN-γ signaling.
