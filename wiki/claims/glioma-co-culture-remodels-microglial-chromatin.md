## Statement

For chromatin accessibility (unlike the transcriptome), glioma co-culture has a stronger effect on BV2 microglia than hypoxia alone, with combined glioma+hypoxia exposure producing the most extensive accessibility dysregulation.

## Evidence summary

ATAC-seq PCA and peak counts (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6–8).

> "CC with glioma cells had a stronger effect on BV2 chromatin accessibility (PC1 axis) than hypoxia (PC2 axis) ... CC in hypoxia further dysregulated chromatin accessibility compared to normoxic CC or hypoxia alone (4,418 up and 3,885 down peaks in hypoxic coculture)"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; fixed-cell ATAC-seq.

## Counter-evidence

Contrasts with the transcriptome, where hypoxia dominates — chromatin and expression layers are differently weighted.
