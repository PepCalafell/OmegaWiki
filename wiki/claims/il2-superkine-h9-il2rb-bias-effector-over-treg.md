## Statement

The H9 IL-2 superkine variant, engineered for high IL-2Rβ affinity, is used in MiTEs to bias expansion toward effector T and NK cells over Tregs. The bias is partial in vivo — MiTE-144 still expands Tregs slightly in TME and markedly in tdLNs, motivating αCTLA-4 combination.
