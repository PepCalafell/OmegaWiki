## Statement

The HuMMI network offers candidate mechanisms linking CD-prevalent effectors to IBD: they
physically interact with Crohn's susceptibility proteins (COG6, TNIP1) that bridge to
UC-associated genes.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450, Fig.5g): CD-prevalent strains'
effectors bind COG6 (→ UC gene RTP5); Efe_13 binds TNIP1 (NF-κB regulator) connected to
two UC genes. Authors frame these as hypotheses inviting a homeostatic-shift model
(↑CD risk, ↓UC risk).

## Conditions and scope

Interaction-network inference; mechanistic relevance explicitly stated as requiring
future study.

## Counter-evidence

No functional/causal validation in IBD provided.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[effector-prevalence-crohns-vs-uc-divergence]].

## Open questions

Whether these interactions causally shift IBD homeostasis.
