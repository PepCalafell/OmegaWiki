## Statement

Functional validation by CRISPR/Cas9 KO confirms SOD1, TALDO1, and H1F0 as bona fide regulators of long-term human hematopoietic potential — elevating their roles from correlative protein abundance to causal HSC dependencies.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative LTC-IC limiting dilution; magnitudes vary across the three genes (SOD1 strongest, TALDO1 mildest). Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 5D-F).

## Conditions and scope

Primary human CD34+ BM cells; AAVS1 as control locus; 5-week LTC-IC.

## Counter-evidence

Mouse Sod1 / Sod2 knockouts do not phenocopy this — species difference acknowledged by authors.

## Open questions
