## Statement

Efferocytosis reduces TNF-driven upregulation of the prosurvival factor cFLIP (Cflar), removing the caspase-8 brake.

## Evidence summary

PLC inhibition does not rescue Cflar, separating it from the IL-1β arm. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.8).

> "coculture of macrophages with neutrophils decreased the TNF-mediated up-regulation of Cflar"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.
