## Statement

The two STAT1 splicing isoforms (STAT1α full-length and STAT1β truncated) have distinct and partially opposing transcriptional roles in homeostatic immune cells. STAT1α effects correlate strongly with the full Stat1-KO phenotype, while STAT1β effects correlate weakly with full Stat1-KO and even diverge in direction for many genes. STAT1β is therefore not a simple dominant-negative truncation but an independently functional isoform.
