## Statement

At the network-neighbourhood level, commensal effector targets are genetically enriched
for Crohn's disease specifically, not ulcerative colitis, plus metabolic (cholesterol,
diabetes) traits.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450, Fig.4c,d): IBD enriched (P=0.0008),
Crohn's P=8.5×10⁻⁵, UC not significant; neighbourhoods also harbour skin/lung disease and
HDL/LDL (P=0.006/0.008) and diabetes susceptibility.

## Conditions and scope

Associative network-genetics analysis.

## Counter-evidence

Statistical association does not establish causal effector contribution.
