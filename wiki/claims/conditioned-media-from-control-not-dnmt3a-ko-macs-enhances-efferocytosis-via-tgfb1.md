## Statement

The pro-efferocytosis output of efferocytosing macrophages is mediated by secreted TGF-β1 and is DNMT3A-dependent; conditioned media from DNMT3A-KO macrophages lacks this paracrine effect.

## Evidence summary

Conditioned-media transfer with neutralizing anti-TGF-β1 antibody. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM in vitro.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
