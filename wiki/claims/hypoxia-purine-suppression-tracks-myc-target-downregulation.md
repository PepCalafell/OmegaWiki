## Statement

Across GENEVA lines, hypoxia-induced changes in de novo purine synthesis genes correlate with changes in Myc target gene expression, and Myc-target suppression correlates with reduced fitness, implicating decreased Myc activity in purine suppression.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.11): "changes in the expression of the de novo purine synthesis genes ... in hypoxia were correlated with changes in the expression of most Myc targets" — GENEVA correlations (Fig.S5a-b); Myc is a known transcriptional driver of purine synthesis genes; mechanism not yet causally tested. (type: correlational, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
