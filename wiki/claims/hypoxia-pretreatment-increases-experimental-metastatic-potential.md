## Statement

Pre-exposure of tumour cells to hypoxia before implantation increases their ability to colonize lung and other organs in xenograft models, consistent with hypoxia priming a pro-metastatic phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Experimental xenograft metastasis models.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
