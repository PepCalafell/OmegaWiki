## Statement

GSDMD is the executioner pore of efferoptosis; Gsdmd-/- macrophages show attenuated death.

## Evidence summary

Consistent with Gsdmd-/- mice being protected from TNF lethality. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "macrophages with noncleavable caspase-8 (D387A) or GSDMD deficiency exhibited attenuated cell death"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
