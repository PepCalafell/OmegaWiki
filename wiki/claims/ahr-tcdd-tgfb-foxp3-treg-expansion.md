## Statement

AHR activation by TCDD or related agonists, in the presence of TGFβ, induces SMAD1 and the Ikaros-family TF Aiolos to stabilise Foxp3 expression and expand Foxp3⁺ Tregs (including the TIGIT⁺ regulatory subset).
