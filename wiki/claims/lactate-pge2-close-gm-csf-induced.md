## Statement

At the chromatin level, GM-CSF opens 1,376 sites (enriched for bZIP/AP-1 and ETS motifs), and lactic acid + PGE2 suppress a subset of these — selectively repressing ETS-family-associated accessibility that GM-CSF activates.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2065-2066) — mechanistic evidence. ATAC-seq of BMDMs across all LGP combinations; HOMER motif enrichment; LP also suppressed 2,011 GM-CSF-accessible sites.
  - Quote: "LP treatment strongly suppressed chromatin sites associated with ETS family, suggesting that LP may selectively repress a subset of ETS-regulated genes that are activated by GM-CSF"

## Conditions and scope

In vitro BMDM ATAC-seq; motif-family-level inference.
