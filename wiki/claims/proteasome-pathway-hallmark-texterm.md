## Statement

TF community analysis predicts and flow cytometry confirms that TEXterm cells have the highest proteasome activity among CD8+ T cell states in chronic LCMV and across mouse tumour TILs, and proteasome-high antigen-specific CD8+ T cells exhibit reduced tumour control in an adoptive transfer model.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 2g–l: pathway prediction from TEXterm community-1 (catabolism, proteolysis, autophagy, ubiquitin-proteasome), flow-cytometry quantification of proteasome MFI, and sorted-transfer functional test.
