## Statement

In vivo αTIM3 antibody attenuates hypothermia, improves survival, lowers serum IL-1β and LDH (not CXCL-1/IL-6), and reduces splenic caspase-8 cleavage in TNF-induced SIRS.

## Evidence summary

Establishes efferocytosis as a therapeutic target. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.8).

> "We observed attenuated TNF-induced hypothermia and improved survival in αTIM3-treated mice ... reduced serum IL-1β and LDH levels"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
