## Statement

Oral HIF prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitors (roxadustat/FG-4592, daprodustat, vadadustat) raise endogenous EPO and haematocrit and are well tolerated in chronic kidney disease anaemia — clinical proof that HIF-activating drugs are usable in humans and a basis for repurposing toward immune-related disease.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Provenzano 2016; Brigandi 2016; Pergola 2016). Links to [[foundations/fg-4592-roxadustat]], [[foundations/erythropoietin-epo]]. Concept: [[concepts/hydroxylase-inhibitor-anti-inflammatory-therapy]].

## Conditions and scope

Validated endpoint is anaemia, not inflammation; systemic EPO induction is the main on-target side effect to manage.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope for the anaemia indication.
