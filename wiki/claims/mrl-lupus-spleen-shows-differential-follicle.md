## Statement

Differential cluster neighbourhood enrichment between MRL-lupus and BALBc mouse spleens shows a rewired interaction pattern: the B-follicle cluster shifts its preferential neighbour from the marginal zone to the B-PALS boundary, accompanied by decreasing B-follicle prevalence and increasing B-PALS prevalence with disease stage.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 3c (cluster proportions per stage); Fig. 3e–f (representative tissues at early/intermediate/late); Fig. 3g (differential NE heatmap).

## Conditions and scope

CODEX panel and cell-type definitions from Goltsev et al. 2018; n=3 BALBc + 6 MRL samples spanning early/intermediate/late stages.

## Open questions

- Whether this rewiring reflects T-cell infiltration from B-PALS into the germinal centre (germinal-center-reaction-like dynamics) is mechanistically inferred but not proven.
