## Statement

In the BATTLE trial cohort, advanced NSCLC patients whose tumours had high hypoxia scores had worse overall survival after sorafenib treatment than low-hypoxia-score patients. This provides preliminary clinical-trial-level evidence that tumour hypoxia status influences targeted-therapy outcome.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — retrospective stratification of BATTLE NSCLC patients by hypoxia score (Supplementary Fig. 10d).

## Conditions and scope

- A single retrospective trial analysis; modest sample size; hypoxia inferred from mRNA.

## Counter-evidence

- Authors note most clinical trials lack tumour hypoxia status, limiting validation.
