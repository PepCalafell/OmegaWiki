## Statement

The prospective TRACERx-NSCLC body-composition arm operationally defines CAC as meeting any one of: >10% SKM loss, >20% SAT loss, >20% VAT loss, or grade-4 BMI-adjusted weight loss between diagnosis and first relapse. Patients with predominantly isolated SKM loss show distinct primary-tumour gene-expression profiles compared with patients losing all compartments, supporting molecular subtypes.

## Evidence summary

Prospective TRACERx CT-L3 segmentation tied to tumour multi-region transcriptomics.

## Conditions and scope

Operable NSCLC; thresholds derived from this cohort; whether the same thresholds apply to advanced disease is unconfirmed.

## Counter-evidence

Cohort-specific cutoffs may not transfer across populations; reference values are White-non-Hispanic-biased.

## Linked ideas

- Operational thresholds for trial stratification.

## Open questions

- Which specific tumour pathways drive isolated SKM loss vs combined loss?
