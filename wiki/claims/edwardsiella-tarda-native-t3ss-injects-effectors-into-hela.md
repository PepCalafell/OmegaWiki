## Statement

At least one gut commensal (E. tarda) has a functional native T3SS that injects
effectors into human cells, demonstrating endogenous secretion capability beyond the
heterologous Salmonella system.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.443–446): E. tarda injected 3/4 tested
effectors reproducibly and significantly; C. pasteurii and P. massiliensis showed
occasional signals (sporadic activation); E. coli strains gave no signal. Only Eta_3 was
also positive in the Salmonella system.

## Conditions and scope

Many commensals require specific host/environmental cues for secretion; 6/11 strains
untestable.

## Counter-evidence

E. coli strains yielded no signal despite encoding T3SS.

Supports [[commensal-t3ss-host-directed-secretion]]. Relates to [[edwardsiella-tarda]].

## Open questions
