## Statement
Inflammatory macrophage activation, unlike lineage determination, is governed primarily at the transcriptional level via a pre-defined TR network whose loci are already in an open-chromatin state.

## Evidence summary
The combination of uniform open chromatin across the activation network, stimulus-specific TR expression, and contrasting integrated regulation in tissue networks supports transcriptional (not epigenetic) gating of activation.

## Conditions and scope
Top-level conclusion of the study; in-vitro human GM-CSF macrophages.

## Counter-evidence
Generalization to other differentiation routes (M-CSF) and in-vivo macrophages remains to be shown.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Whether other functionally plastic cell types use the same pre-defined-open-chromatin strategy.
