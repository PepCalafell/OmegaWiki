## Statement

Transient IL-2 stimulation downregulates oxidative-phosphorylation genes (the early step of the activation-associated OXPHOS→glycolysis shift, before glycolysis genes are upregulated) and activates genes for mitotic-spindle formation (e.g. DOCK2, KIF1B), suggesting that preparations for mitosis begin immediately after T-cell activation, before cell-cycle checkpoints are passed.

## Evidence summary

"transient stimulation downregulates oxidative-phosphorylation genes ... (glycolysis genes are not yet upregulated)" and "Transient stimulation did activate genes associated with formation of the mitotic spindle (for example, DOCK2 and KIF1B) ... suggesting that preparations for mitosis are made immediately after T cell activation, before cell-cycle checkpoints" (p.7).

## Conditions and scope

Primary human T cells; bulk RNA-seq at 6 h; hallmark gene-set enrichment; n = 3.

## Counter-evidence

Transcriptional snapshot only; metabolic flux not directly measured.
