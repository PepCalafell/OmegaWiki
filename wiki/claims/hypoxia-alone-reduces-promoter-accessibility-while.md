## Statement

Hypoxia alone reduces chromatin accessibility at gene promoters in microglia, whereas the combination of glioma contact and hypoxia increases promoter openness — most CC+hypoxia up-promoter peaks (1,515 of 1,680) require both stimuli, not hypoxia alone.

## Evidence summary

Promoter-peak overlap analysis (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.10).

> "hypoxia alone reduces promoter accessibility, whereas the combined glioma presence and hypoxia increase promoter openness in hypoxic BV2 cells ... 1,515 required both hypoxia and glioma"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; fixed-cell ATAC-seq.
