## Statement

HIF and inflammation are bidirectionally coupled: hypoxia activates HIF to shape immunity, while the products of immune activity (cytokines, ROS, NO, bacterial ligands, immunometabolites) feed back to increase HIF signalling — a feedforward loop tying immune cell metabolism to inflammation.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (Boxes 2 and 3). Mechanistically overlaps with [[claims/shared-oxygen-dependent-hydroxylases-couple-hif]].

## Conditions and scope

The loop is most prominent in pathological niches where inflammatory mediators are abundant.
