## Statement

HIF2α (not HIF1α) drives sorafenib resistance in hypoxic hepatocellular carcinoma by transcriptionally activating the TGF-α/EGFR and COX-2 pathways. HIF2α-induced dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) expression in macrophages also confers 5-FU chemoresistance.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023). Provides direct rationale for combining HIF2α inhibitors (belzutifan, PT2385) with sorafenib in hypoxic HCC.

## Conditions and scope

HCC under sorafenib; macrophage-driven 5-FU resistance in colorectal contexts.

## Counter-evidence

Sorafenib resistance is multi-factorial; FGF/PDGFR signaling, autophagy, and immune evasion also contribute.
