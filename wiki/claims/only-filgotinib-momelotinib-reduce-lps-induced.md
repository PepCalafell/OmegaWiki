## Statement

Among the six JAK inhibitors used at their kinobeads-derived EC50, only Filgotinib and Momelotinib produced a significant reduction in LPS-induced STAT1 (Tyr701) phosphorylation; all six inhibited at four-fold higher concentration.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative flow cytometry of p-STAT1 established a concentration-dependent ranking, with Filgotinib and Momelotinib most potent. Links [[foundations/filgotinib]], [[foundations/momelotinib]], [[foundations/stat1-tf]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS 2 h; concentrations defined by kinobeads EC50 (an indirect measure that may differ from cell-based EC50).

## Counter-evidence

Genome-wide H3K27ac, more sensitive than p-STAT1 FACS, showed all JAKi impaired a common IRF/STAT-bound CRE set — so the FACS-defined potency ranking understates shared on-target activity.
