## Statement

The known-pathogenic SNP rs606231230 (preaxial polydactyly) sits in a limb-specific HOXD13-bound enhancer and the risk allele creates multiple overlapping HOXD13 binding sites — a concrete clinical example of the overlapping-binding-sites mechanism for disease-associated noncoding variants.
