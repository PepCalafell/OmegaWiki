## Statement

The Ahrd allele encodes an AHR variant with reduced TCDD affinity. Mice carrying Ahrd, when infected with T. cruzi, achieve a balanced pro/anti-inflammatory response, restrict parasite replication, develop less liver disease and survive longer than both WT (Ahrb, high affinity) and Ahr-KO mice. The data establish AHR signal-magnitude — not the presence/absence of AHR — as the relevant therapeutic variable, providing genetic rationale for partial-agonist SAHRMs.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 114–116. Ahrb (high-affinity) WT mice show TCDD/3HK+ITE-driven Treg-mediated immunosuppression and increased parasitaemia; Ahr-KO mice show reduced parasitaemia but exaggerated inflammation. Ahrd lands in between.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in mouse Chagas disease model.
