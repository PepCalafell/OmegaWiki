## Statement

In Dhps-ΔM peritoneum, mature Timd4+ RTM clusters are diminished while immature monocyte-derived clusters (Ccr2+ cluster 1; Adgre1-high Timd4- cluster 2) expand, consistent with a block in RTM maturation.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 3a-c) using [[foundations/scrna-seq-10x-chromium]].

## Conditions and scope

Single experiment, n=3/condition; lung scRNA-seq mirrored the finding (loss of Siglec-F+ cluster 0).

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
