## Statement

13C-adenine tracing shows increased 13C labeling of AMP under hypoxia, indicating tumors compensate for suppressed de novo synthesis by increasing purine salvage flux.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.9): "increased the fraction of AMP carrying a 13C label, indicating a suppression of de novo purine synthesis and an increased reliance on salvage" — In vitro (Fig.5f) and in vivo (Fig.5g) normalized 13C-AMP higher in hypoxia; consistent with bioenergetic-stress salvage dependence. (type: mechanistic, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
