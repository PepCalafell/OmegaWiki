## Statement

In the C26 cancer-cachexia mouse model, multi-tissue polar metabolomics (PLSDA and 3D PCA) reveals that Cax mice are cleanly separated from controls in plasma, liver, eWAT and iWAT, while skeletal muscle and heart metabolite profiles show higher within-group variability and no clear clustering.

## Evidence summary

Quoted from Fig. 1 e-l: "plasma, liver and adipose tissue metabolomes of C26 cachectic animals clearly clustered apart from all other groups... Skeletal muscle and heart metabolite profiles showed a higher variability, especially in the cachectic group, and thereby no clear clustering was observed for these tissues."

## Conditions and scope

C26 model; male BALB/c; 6-h fast; LC-MS LIMeX platform; n = 4 per group.
