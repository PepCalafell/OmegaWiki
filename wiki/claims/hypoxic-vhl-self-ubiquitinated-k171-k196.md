## Statement

Under hypoxia VHL is self-ubiquitinated (K171/K196) and degraded; HIF1A normally shields the VHL complex from self-targeting, so loss of hydroxylated HIF1A engagement in hypoxia exposes VHL to self-ubiquitination.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 1D–G, S2E–N). CHX/MG132 stability assays, K171R/K196R rescue, depletion experiments (HIF1A vs EPAS1), and VHL–HIF1A fusion preferential-ubiquitination test.

## Conditions and scope

Cell-line evidence; shielding inferred from depletion/fusion experiments. K171/K196 overlap with TOM22-binding motifs, linking degradation and import as competing fates.

## Counter-evidence

WSB1 was previously reported as a VHL E3 ligase, but acts predominantly under normoxia.
