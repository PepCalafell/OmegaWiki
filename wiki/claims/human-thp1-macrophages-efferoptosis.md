## Statement

Human THP-1-derived macrophages recapitulate efferoptosis: TNF+neutrophils increase death and mature IL-1β release, supporting human relevance.

## Evidence summary

Cross-species (human macrophage + mouse neutrophil) confirmation. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.6).
