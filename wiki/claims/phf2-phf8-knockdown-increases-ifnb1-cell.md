## Statement

Transient siRNA knockdown of PHF2 together with PHF8 increases IFNB1 expression and cell death, and the IFNB1 induction depends on cGAS (lost in cGAS-knockout fibroblasts), validating PHF demethylases as suppressors of cGAS-driven innate activation.

## Evidence summary

In HCT116 and wild-type BJ cells, dual PHF2/PHF8 knockdown raised IFNB1 and LDH-measured cell death; the response was abolished in cGAS-KO BJ cells, establishing cGAS dependence.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro functional validation in two cell lines with a cGAS-KO genetic control; the strongest mechanistic evidence in the paper.

## Counter-evidence

No in vivo tumor data; effect is transient-knockdown-based; PHF2's reported context-dependent oncogenic/suppressive roles complicate translation.
