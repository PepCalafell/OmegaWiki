## Statement

Although designed to bias away from Treg expansion, MiTE-144 still increases Treg frequencies modestly in the TME and more strongly in tumor-draining lymph nodes. Adding anti-CTLA-4 (which depletes Tregs) blocks this expansion, contributing to the observed synergy.
