## Statement

The neomorphic STAT5BN642H mutant in homeostatic CD8+ T cells abrogates the repressive chromatin role of WT STAT5 and instead increases accessibility at T-cell effector loci — granzymes Gzmk and Gzmb, killer cell lectin-like receptors Klrc1 and Klre1, and the cell-cycle marker Mki67. This chromatin rewiring provides a mechanistic basis for the hyperproliferative T-cell phenotype and the leukemogenic potential of STAT5BN642H.
