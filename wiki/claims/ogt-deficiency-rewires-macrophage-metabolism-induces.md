## Statement

Ogt deficiency alters large-cavity-macrophage metabolism — increasing dependence on glucose over mitochondrial respiration and altering lipid metabolism — and increases reactive oxygen species and DNA damage, producing a senescence-like phenotype (enlarged cells, senescence markers, spontaneous inflammatory cytokine secretion) with accumulation in cycling (G2/M) populations that fail to divide productively.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/glcnacylation-shapes-macrophage-tissue-residency-alternative]] (commentary on Heieis et al.). Frames [[foundations/glcnacylation]]/[[foundations/ogt-glcnac-transferase]] as a metabolic regulator of the macrophage cell cycle whose loss diverts cells toward senescence rather than self-renewal.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse [[foundations/large-cavity-macrophage-lcm-peritoneal]] populations. Bundles several linked observations (metabolism, ROS/DNA damage, senescence, cell-cycle) reported together; commentary-level confidence.
