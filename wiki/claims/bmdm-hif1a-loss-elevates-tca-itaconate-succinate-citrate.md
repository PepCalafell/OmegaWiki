## Statement

LPS-stimulated Hif1a⁻/⁻ BMDMs accumulate the canonical immunoregulatory TCA-cycle metabolites — itaconate, succinate, citrate — at higher levels than controls, even as lactate falls. This metabolite reshaping is consistent with the enhanced mitochondrial OXPHOS phenotype and provides a candidate mechanism (citrate → acetyl-CoA / fatty acid synthesis / epigenetic modifications; itaconate-mediated anti-inflammatory signalling) for the reduced cytokine output.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 5F; Suppl. Fig. S11B.

## Conditions and scope

In-vitro mouse BMDMs; metabolite levels measured at 6 and 24 h LPS. Does not distinguish synthesis vs efflux; does not directly link metabolite elevation to cytokine suppression mechanistically.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
