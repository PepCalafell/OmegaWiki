## Statement

Alveolar macrophages depend on a GM-CSF (CSF2) and TGFβ-induced PPARγ program for lipid catabolism, cholesterol handling, and surfactant clearance. PPARγ loss collapses AM numbers, accumulates intracellular lipids, and produces alveolar proteinosis; PPARγ-, LXR-, or statin-based interventions reduce proteinosis preclinically.

## Evidence summary

Canonical in pulmonary immunology (Schneider 2014, Yu 2017). Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] alongside BACH2, C/EBPβ, LXR, and VHL/HIF regulators that converge on the AM lipid program.

## Conditions and scope

- Most evidence mouse; human relevance via CSF2/CSF2R/PPARG-linked alveolar proteinosis.

## Counter-evidence

- Inducible-deletion studies suggest PPARγ is more important for development than for adult AM maintenance.
