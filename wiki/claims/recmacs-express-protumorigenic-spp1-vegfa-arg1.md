## Statement

Fn1+Vcan+ recMacs are enriched for canonical tumor-promoting transcripts Spp1, Vegfa, Arg1 and Cd274 (PD-L1), and express Ccl2; CD206lo IMs also express Ccl2.

## Evidence summary

scRNA-seq feature plots (Fig. 1f, Extended Data Fig. 1h,j) show enrichment of these transcripts in recMacs vs CD206hi IMs. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung B16F10 melanoma model.
