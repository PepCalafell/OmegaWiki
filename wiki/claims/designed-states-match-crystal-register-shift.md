## Statement

X-ray crystal structures confirm that the designed host states and host–effector complexes match their design models (≤1.3 Å Cα RMSD), and that effector binding induces the designed register-shift conformational change from the open state X to the effector-bound state Y.

## Evidence summary

"the crystal structures of the hosts alone and of the host–effector complexes closely match the design models (maximum 1.3 Å Cα root mean square deviation)" (p.4). Unbound structures show an open hydrophobic cleft in state X; effector-bound structures show the two switch domains register-shifted into state Y.

## Conditions and scope

AS1 and AS5 designed systems; static crystal structures.

## Counter-evidence

Some strain/variation observed in target–AS1 structures; ternary complex is dynamic (DEER/MD) rather than a single rigid state.
