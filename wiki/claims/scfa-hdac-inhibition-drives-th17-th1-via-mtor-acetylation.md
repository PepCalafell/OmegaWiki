## Statement

In T cells, SCFA effects on Th17/Th1 generation route through HDAC inhibition (not GPR41/43), raising p70-S6K acetylation and rS6 phosphorylation to engage mTOR.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] reviewing primary HDAC-T-cell studies.

## Conditions and scope

- T-cell differentiation contexts; effect may be context- and dose-dependent.

## Counter-evidence

- Some studies attribute Treg-promoting SCFA effects to GPR43 rather than HDAC.

## Open questions
