## Statement

Mutant p53 binds TBK1 directly and prevents STING-TBK1-IRF3 trimerization, suppressing innate immune signaling.

## Evidence summary

Ghosh 2021 Cancer Cell: mutant p53 promotes tumor immune surveillance evasion by directly binding TBK1 and preventing the trimeric TBK1-STING-IRF3 complex required for IRF3 phosphorylation and nuclear translocation.

## Conditions and scope

Tumors carrying gain-of-function TP53 missense mutations; mechanism may extend to other TP53 hotspots.
