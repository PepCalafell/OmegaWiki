## Statement

Classical M1/M2 polarization signatures fail to cleanly segregate macrophage subtypes within the pan-cancer TME, indicating high plasticity and heterogeneity beyond the M1/M2 dichotomy.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "traditional M1/M2 signatures cannot clearly distinguish the macrophage subtypes within the TME, indicating a high level of plasticity and heterogeneity among macrophages" (Han 2025, Fig. 2e).

## Linked ideas

- Use functional MP (meta-program) annotation rather than M1/M2 for TME macrophages.
