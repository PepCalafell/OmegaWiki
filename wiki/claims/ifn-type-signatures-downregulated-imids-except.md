## Statement

In most IMIDs and cell types, IFN type 1 and type 2 signatures were significantly downregulated, with the notable exception of non-naive CD8 T cells, which showed upregulation — pointing to a cell-type-specific interferon mechanism. By contrast, the IFN-induced signature was strongly upregulated in almost all immune cell types.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 2a,c (p.634, p.637); the apparent paradox (downregulated IFN type 1/2 signatures yet upregulated IFN-induced signature) motivated the downstream GRN analysis of STAT1/SP1.

## Conditions and scope

IMID group; distinguishes the IFN type-1/2 stimulus signatures from the IFN-induced response signature.

## Counter-evidence

Direction differs in infections (IFN-induced increased in Flu/COVID, decreased in HIV/HBV).
