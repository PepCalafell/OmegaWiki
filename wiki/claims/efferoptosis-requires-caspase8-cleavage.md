## Statement

Efferoptosis depends on caspase-8 autoprocessing; noncleavable Casp8 D387A macrophages are protected.

## Evidence summary

Genetic point-mutant evidence. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "macrophages with noncleavable caspase-8 (D387A) or GSDMD deficiency exhibited attenuated cell death"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.
