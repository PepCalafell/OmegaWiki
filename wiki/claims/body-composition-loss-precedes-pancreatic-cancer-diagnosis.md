## Statement

Skeletal-muscle, visceral-adipose and subcutaneous-adipose loss can be detected on imaging BEFORE clinical pancreatic-cancer diagnosis, supporting the existence of a pre-CAC phase that may serve for early detection.

## Evidence summary

Retrospective analyses of pre-diagnostic CT imaging in patients who subsequently developed pancreatic cancer showed measurable body-composition loss in the months preceding diagnosis.

## Conditions and scope

Pancreatic cancer with available pre-diagnostic CT scans (usually incidental imaging or surveillance for other conditions); selection bias likely.

## Counter-evidence

Prospective surveillance has not been done; weight loss is a known non-specific pre-cancer feature and may not equal CAC-specific compartment loss.

## Linked ideas

- Pre-CAC could enable pancreatic-cancer early detection biomarkers.

## Open questions

- Can pre-CAC body-composition signatures be combined with circulating biomarkers (CA19-9, ctDNA) to improve early detection?
