## Statement

Over four rounds of serial in vivo re-implantation, Panc02 tumors grown in hypoxic mice consistently grow slower than normoxic tumors, with no overt resistance phenotype emerging.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.11): "Over repeated passages, tumors in hypoxic mice consistently grew slower than tumors in normoxic mice, suggesting that no overt resistance phenotype emerged" — Serial re-implantation passages 2-5 (Fig.6a-b). (type: methodological, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
