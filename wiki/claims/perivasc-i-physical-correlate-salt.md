## Statement

The authors propose that PERIVASC I — with its CCL19+ perivascular fibroblasts, HEC-like vessels, perivascular lymphocytes and dendritic cells — is the cellular and spatial realisation of the 1983 SALT concept proposed by Streilein.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Discussion, citing Streilein 1983.

## Conditions and scope

Conceptual / interpretive claim; rests on structural similarity to FRC/lymph-node biology, not on functional reconstitution.

## Counter-evidence

PERIVASC I lacks B-cell follicles and germinal centers, so it is not formally a tertiary lymphoid structure.
