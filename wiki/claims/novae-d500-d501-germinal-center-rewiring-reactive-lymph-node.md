## Statement

In a paired nondiseased vs reactive lymph-node comparison, the germinal-center domain D500 (CD79+CR2+ mature B cells) is connected to a single neighboring domain in the nondiseased slide but to five in the reactive slide, with an accompanying inversion of D500/D501 cell proportions.
