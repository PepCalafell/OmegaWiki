## Statement

Kupffer cells, the liver-resident macrophages, are significantly depleted from HCC tumor nodules relative to matched adjacent tumor-free liver — paralleling the alveolar mac depletion observed in NSCLC.

## Evidence summary

- Defined by canonical KC markers (MARCO, CD5L, TIMD4, LYVE1, VCAM1, CETP, IFI27, CFP).
- Quantitative reduction in tumor vs adjacent.
- Consistent with prior NSCLC alveolar mac depletion (Casanova-Acebes 2021).

## Conditions and scope

- HCC; not generalised to other liver cancers (cholangiocarcinoma, metastases).

## Counter-evidence

- Sampling bias possible if KCs sit at tumor-stromal interface and are lost in dissociation.
