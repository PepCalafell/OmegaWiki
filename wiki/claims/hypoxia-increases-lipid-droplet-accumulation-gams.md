## Statement

Hypoxia significantly increases lipid droplet accumulation (number, volume, total fluorescence) in microglia (BV2) and macrophages (BMDM), confirming at the organelle level the transcriptional induction of lipid-storage genes.

## Evidence summary

BODIPY lipid-droplet quantitation (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.14).

> "we next assessed lipid droplet (LD) levels in BV2 and BMDM monocultures using boron-dipyrromethene (BODIPY) staining and found them significantly increased under hypoxia"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 and BMDM monocultures; in vitro hypoxia; three replicates.
