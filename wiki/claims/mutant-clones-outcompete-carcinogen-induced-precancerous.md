## Statement
Carcinogen-induced precancerous lesions are outcompeted by mutant clones in the adjacent normal oesophagus, challenging the assumption that precancers always progress.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
