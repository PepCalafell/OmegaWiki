## Statement

While exhausted CD8+ T cells and NK cells increased in both diseases, their expansion was substantially greater in psoriasis vulgaris (CTLex +74.6%, NK +99.4% vs HC) than in atopic dermatitis (CTLex +23.0%, NK +20.4% vs HC).

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]. Supports [[concepts/treg-trm-expansion-cd8-exhaustion-chronic]].

Relative CD45+ proportions; figure S3/table S6.

## Counter-evidence

Small per-disease sample sizes (7 AD, 8 PV).
