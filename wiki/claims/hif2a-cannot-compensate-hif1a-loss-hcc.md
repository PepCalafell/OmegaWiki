## Statement

Although hepatocellular carcinoma (Huh7) cells express both HIF-1α and HIF-2α, endogenous HIF-2α does not compensate for the loss of HIF-1α: HIF1A-knockout Huh7 cells still fail to grow/adapt under both normoxia and hypoxia, demonstrating non-redundant, HIF-1α-specific functions in HCC.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3142, 3156) — isoform expression + knockout phenotype.
  - Quote: "despite the ability of Huh7 cells to express HIF-2α, they were unable to adapt (either under normoxia or hypoxia) to the knockout of HIF-1α, suggesting that, in HCC cells, HIF-2α cannot compensate for the loss of HIF-1α."
  - Consistent with clinical data correlating HIF-1α (not HIF-2α) with poor HCC prognosis.

## Conditions and scope

Based on endogenous HIF-2α levels in Huh7; no forced HIF-2α overexpression rescue was attempted. HeLa_S3 lacks endogenous HIF-2α (separate control).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. The non-compensation is inferred from failure to rescue, not from HIF-2α gain-of-function testing.
