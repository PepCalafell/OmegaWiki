## Statement

Knockout of any ISGF3 member (STAT1, STAT2, or IRF9) and knockout (or kinase-inactivation) of TYK2 each cause downregulation of the core interferon-stimulated gene set (Oas3, Ifit1, Ifit3, Mx1, Oasl1, Oas2) in unstimulated splenic CD8+ T cells and macrophages. This is direct evidence that tonic ISGF3-mediated transcription is active under homeostatic conditions, not only after acute IFN stimulation.
