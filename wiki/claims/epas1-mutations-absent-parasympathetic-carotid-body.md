## Statement

EPAS1 somatic mutations do not occur in parasympathetic carotid-body PPGLs, even under systemic hypoxia, contrasting sharply with sympathetic tumors.

## Evidence summary

Targeted sequencing and WES across CCHD and non-CCHD parasympathetic cohorts.

## Conditions and scope

Defines the tissue specificity of EPAS1 selection (sympathoadrenal lineage only).

## Counter-evidence

None.

## Open questions

Why parasympathetic lineage is refractory to EPAS1-driven adaptation.
