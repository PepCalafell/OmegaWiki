## Statement

ERK1/2 kinase activation is required for AC-induced Ptgs2/COX2 and Tgfb1 in macrophages; pharmacological and genetic blockade of ERK abolishes the response.

## Evidence summary

U0126 and siMapk1+siMapk3 block AC-induced Ptgs2/Tgfb1/TGFβ1 in BMDMs and HMDMs. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM/HMDM in vitro.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
