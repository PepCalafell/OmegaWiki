## Statement

All three regulatory T cell classes (eTreg1, eTreg2, central-memory cmTreg) expanded 2.19- to 3.13-fold in rash-affected versus healthy skin, generalizing across rash types a Treg-expansion trend previously reported only anecdotally.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]; Treg markers [[foundations/foxp3-tf]]. Supports [[concepts/treg-trm-expansion-cd8-exhaustion-chronic]].

## Conditions and scope

Aggregate rash-vs-HC; both AD and PV disease-level analyses also showed Treg expansion.

## Counter-evidence

None reported, though the authors note Treg expansion does not equate to successful regulation.
