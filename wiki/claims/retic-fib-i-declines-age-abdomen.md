## Statement

Retic Fib I, the dominant cell type of the STROMA neighborhood, decreases numerically with age in abdominal skin, while vascular/perivascular constituents (HEC, VEC, pericytes, SM) increase.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Fig. 4e, Discussion.

## Conditions and scope

The authors argue this may underlie the well-known age-related collagen decrease (Varani et al., as cited).

## Counter-evidence

Solé-Boldo et al. 2020 reported per-cell gene-expression decline in inguinal skin fibroblasts but did not assess numerical decline directly.
