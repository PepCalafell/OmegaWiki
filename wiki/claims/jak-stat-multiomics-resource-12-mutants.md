## Statement

The Fortelny et al. 2024 study assembled a unique pathway-scale resource of 469 RNA-seq and 496 ATAC-seq profiles spanning twelve JAK-STAT mouse genotypes, matched WT controls, and five immune cell types from spleen, with raw + processed data and interactive browser tracks at http://jakstat.bocklab.org and GEO GSE204736. This is the most comprehensive bulk-omics dissection of a single signaling pathway in unstimulated immune cells to date.
