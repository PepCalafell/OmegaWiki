## Statement

Antigen-engaged BCRs cluster into cholesterol- and sphingolipid-rich lipid rafts; Lyn activates downstream SYK/BLNK, recruiting BTK and PLC-γ2 to engage NF-κB and PI3K-AKT for B-cell proliferation, differentiation, and antibody production. Immature B cells fail to cluster stably and undergo apoptosis.

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]].
