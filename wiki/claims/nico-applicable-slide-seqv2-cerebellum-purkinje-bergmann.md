## Statement

NiCo applied to Slide-seqV2 mouse cerebellum (10 µm pixels) recovers the canonical Purkinje neuron–Bergmann glial cell interaction and detects covarying calcium-signaling secretory programs from Bergmann glia to Purkinje neurons, demonstrating generalizability to sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics when per-pixel cell-type assignments are available.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — NiCo interaction and covariation modules applied with author-supplied per-pixel cell-type annotations on Slide-seqV2 cerebellum data; recovers Purkinje–Bergmann interaction and calcium-signaling covariation (Suppl. Fig. 11).

## Conditions and scope

Slide-seqV2 pixels = 1–2 cells, so confounding possible; relies on author-supplied annotations.
