## Statement

PX-478 (S-2-amino-3-[4'-N,N,-bis(2-chloroethyl)amino]phenyl propionic acid N-oxide dihydrochloride) is an HIF-1α-selective small-molecule inhibitor. Mechanistically it acts at multiple levels:
1. Reduces HIF-1α protein levels (post-translational destabilization, likely via increased ubiquitination).
2. Reduces HIF-1α transactivating activity (independent of stabilization, suggesting direct inhibition of the HIF-1α-CBP/p300 interaction).
3. Inhibits HIF-1α deubiquitination (preventing rescue from degradation).
4. Blocks hypoxia-induced VEGF synthesis.

In pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) specifically, PX-478 drastically reduces GM-CSF expression and the incidence of perineural invasion (PNI). PNI is a clinically important pattern of PDAC dissemination along nerve fibers; the GM-CSF reduction may explain the PNI reduction (GM-CSF supports myeloid recruitment along nerves). PX-478 has been studied in phase I trials (NCT00522652) for advanced solid tumors.

## Conditions and scope

- HIF-1α-preferential but not strictly HIF-1α-only (some HIF-2α inhibition at higher doses).
- Most evidence is preclinical; clinical translation has been slower than HIF-2α inhibitors.

## Counter-evidence

- Multi-step mechanism makes target deconvolution difficult; the exact molecular target(s) of PX-478 are not fully resolved.
- Clinical development has lagged Belzutifan / PT2385; phase II/III data for PX-478 are sparse.

## Open questions

- What is the precise molecular target of PX-478 — is it HIF-1α directly, or upstream (e.g. CDK1)?
- Does PX-478 sensitize PDAC to immune checkpoint blockade by reducing GM-CSF-driven myeloid recruitment?
- Are newer-generation HIF-1α inhibitors (with better PK and on-target selectivity) likely to outperform PX-478 clinically?
