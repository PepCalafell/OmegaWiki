## Statement

Under hypoxia, mitochondrial VHL specifically reduces incorporation of leucine carbon into citrate and lipogenic acetyl-CoA, an effect dependent on VHL mitochondrial import (lost in VHL M2).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 3C–D, S4E–H). Stable-isotope tracing.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxia-specific for the VHL WT-vs-M2 difference; under normoxia both WT and M2 inhibit leucine degradation.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

Supports [[concepts/vhl-mccc2-leucine-catabolism-inhibition]] and [[concepts/leucine-allosteric-gdh-glutaminolysis-activation]].
