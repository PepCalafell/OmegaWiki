## Statement

DHPS-deficient macrophages internalize and clear apoptotic cells and stressed erythrocytes less efficiently, a loss of a core homeostatic RTM function.

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 5a,f,g). Connects to [[concepts/efferocytosis-anti-inflammatory-clearance]].
