## Statement

Outside sympathetic PPGL, EPAS1 somatic mutations are vanishingly rare and not under positive selection in any other cancer type.

## Evidence summary

cBioPortal and TCGA Pancancer Atlas queries.

## Conditions and scope

Establishes the exclusivity of EPAS1 selection to sympathoadrenal PPGL under hypoxia.

## Counter-evidence

None.

## Open questions
