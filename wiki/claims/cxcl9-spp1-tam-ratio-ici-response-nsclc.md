## Statement

A low CXCL9:SPP1 macrophage ratio in the tumour microenvironment is associated with resistance to immune checkpoint inhibitors and poor prognosis in NSCLC and other solid tumours.

## Evidence summary

Pan-cancer single-cell and spatial profiling support CXCL9⁺ TAM as antitumorigenic and SPP1⁺ TAM as immune-excluding; ratio collapses into a tractable biomarker.

## Conditions and scope

Best supported in colorectal, breast and NSCLC; sensitive to assay type and TAM annotation.

## Counter-evidence

Single-axis collapse ignores TREM2⁺, COL1A1⁺ and FOLR2⁺ TAM states.
