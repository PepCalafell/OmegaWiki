## Statement

Comparing macrophage subtypes by reported functional signatures, SPP1+ TAMs (c66) exhibited elevated expression of multiple M2-related pathways including response to hypoxia, ECM remodeling, and angiogenesis, consistent with their established pro-tumoral function and contrasting with the M1-like IFN-related TAMs.
