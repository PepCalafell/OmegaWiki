## Statement

Membrane-incorporated DHA reduces antigen-presenting cell MHC-II and costimulatory output, blunts IL-6/IL-12 cytokine release, and dampens CD4 T-cell activation; combined with EPA it favours Treg generation and suppresses Th1/Th2 in food-allergy models.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from primary allergy and PUFA-immunology studies.

## Conditions and scope

- Animal allergy and ex-vivo CD4 systems.

## Counter-evidence

- Mixed human-trial outcomes for n-3 supplementation in allergic disease.
