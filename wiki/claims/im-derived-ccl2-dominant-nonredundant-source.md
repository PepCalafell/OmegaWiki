## Statement

Tissue-resident IM-derived CCL2, rather than endothelial, recMac or DC-derived CCL2, was required for recMac recruitment and melanoma lung metastasis progression.

## Evidence summary

Compartment-resolved chimeras (busulfan vs irradiation, Ccl2-/- donors) isolate IM-CCL2 as the dominant source (Fig. 4e-g). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 melanoma lung metastasis.
