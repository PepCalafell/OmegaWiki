## Statement

The product side of one-carbon metabolism is broadly elevated across all eight cachexia target tissues in C26 cachectic mice, while substrates (glycine, serine) are depleted in plasma — a coherent flux signature consistent with activated one-carbon turnover.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 3 b-g; metabolites #1-#20 of the one-carbon panel. Quoted: "We observed a clear increase in the levels of the vast majority of these metabolites in all tissues of Cax animals as well as in the tumour... especially in products of this pathway (methylated products of the methionine cycle, such as sarcosine, MNAM, methyllysines, dimethylglycine; and products of the folate cycle, such as thymidine)."

## Conditions and scope

Eight tissues across n = 4 mice/group; pathway pre-defined as one-carbon-related (substrates + products).

## Counter-evidence

Magnitude varies across tissues; some metabolites (e.g., MNAM) dominate liver while others (sarcosine, methyllysines) dominate muscle/adipose — tissue-specific dominant acceptors (see [[claims/tissue-specific-methylated-product-signature-cachexia]]).
