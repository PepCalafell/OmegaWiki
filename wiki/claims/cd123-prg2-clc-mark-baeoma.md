## Statement

The BaEoMa progenitor cluster is identifiable by CD123 surface expression together with high PRG2 and CLC protein levels — both eosinophil-granule components.

## Evidence summary

Cluster-specific protein abundance signatures derived from differential expression on 11 scp-MS clusters. Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 2 E-F).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+ HSPCs.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
