## Statement

AMPK-coupled FAO supports central-memory CD8⁺ T-cell formation, while CPT1 blockade in macrophages paradoxically promotes CD36-driven LDL uptake and atherosclerotic lipid accumulation — a tissue-specific divergence in FAO-targeting outcomes.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]].

## Conditions and scope

- Memory T-cell biology; atherosclerosis macrophage models.

## Counter-evidence

- Etomoxir-based CPT1 inhibition has off-target effects on ANT; interpretation requires genetic CPT1A loss.

## Open questions

- Whether CPT1A-selective inhibitors can preserve T-cell memory while limiting macrophage lipid accumulation.
