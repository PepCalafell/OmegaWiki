## Statement

EPAS1 mutations in hypoxia-developed PPGL are gain-of-function, clustering in the oxygen-dependent degradation domain (around Pro531), impairing VHL-mediated degradation.

## Evidence summary

Sequencing of recurrent ODD missense alleles; mechanism supported by prior functional work from the group.

## Conditions and scope

ODD residues 529–542; see [[concepts/epas1-gain-function-oxygen-degradation-domain]].

## Counter-evidence

None; one in-frame deletion is the only non-missense lesion.
