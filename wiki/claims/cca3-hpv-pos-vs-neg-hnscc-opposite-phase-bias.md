## Statement

In 3CA v2, HPV+ and HPV− HNSCC show opposite cell-cycle phase biases (G1/S vs G2/M, respectively) despite both being among the most proliferative cancer types overall. The opposite bias is mechanistically explained by HPV E7 degradation of pRb in HPV+ tumours, whereas HPV− HNSCC retains functional pRb but loses TP53.

Fig. 5f and Discussion p.1095.

## Conditions and scope

Cohort-dependent; supports the broader RB1 → G1/S and TP53 → G2/M associations.
