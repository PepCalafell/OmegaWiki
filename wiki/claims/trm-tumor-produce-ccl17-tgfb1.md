## Statement

Tumour-associated alveolar TRMs in KP NSCLC upregulate the chemokine CCL17 and the cytokine TGFβ1 compared to healthy-lung TRMs. CCL17 is a CCR4 ligand involved in Treg recruitment to tumours; TGFβ1 is a master regulator of Treg differentiation. Together they provide a molecular basis for the spatial co-localisation of Tregs with TRMs in tumour lesions.

## Evidence summary

- Bulk RNA-seq DEGs: Ccl17 and Tgfb1 upregulated at day 15 KP NSCLC TRMs
- Inferred functional role from prior literature (Mucida 2007, Soroosh 2013, Mizukami 2008)

## Conditions and scope

- Transcript-level evidence; protein-level secretion not directly measured in this paper
- Mouse-only

## Counter-evidence

- Other Treg-recruiting chemokines (CCL22, CCL1, CXCL12) not measured here
- Functional role of TRM-derived CCL17/TGFβ1 in driving the Treg-licensing phenotype not directly tested by knockout/knockdown
