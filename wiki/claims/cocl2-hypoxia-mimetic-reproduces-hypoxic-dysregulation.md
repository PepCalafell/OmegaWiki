## Statement

The hypoxia-mimetic drug CoCl2, which stabilizes HIF-α by inhibiting prolyl hydroxylases, reproduces the hypoxic phenotype — increasing Lgals3 and decreasing P2ry12/Tmem119 — implicating HIF stabilization in the marker dysregulation.

## Evidence summary

CoCl2 and mild (1% O2) hypoxia both reproduced the changes (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.3).

> "Both the mild hypoxia and CoCl2 increased Lgals3 expression at both mRNA and protein levels ... both mild hypoxia and CoCl2 decreased their expression"

## Conditions and scope

In vitro myeloid cells; pharmacologic HIF stabilization.

## Counter-evidence

CoCl2 has HIF-independent off-target effects; pharmacologic mimicry is suggestive, not definitive, of HIF dependence.

Whether genetic HIF1α/HIF2α perturbation confirms HIF-dependence of each marker.
