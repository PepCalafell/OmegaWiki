## Statement

Methionine derived from apoptotic-cell proteins enters efferocytosing macrophages via phagolysosomal degradation and is metabolically converted to SAM, as demonstrated by 13C5,15N-isotope tracing.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative LC-MS/MS: 13C5,15N-SAM specifically detected in PKH26+ (AC+) macrophages, not AC- macrophages, and abolished by bafilomycin. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM with Jurkat AC; in methionine-free media to isolate AC-derived methionine.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
