## Statement

The authors propose that hypoxia in growing tumour islands triggers HIF1α-mediated secretion of neutrophil-recruiting chemokines (CXCL1/2/3/5/8, S100A8/9, IL-6, IL-8, SOD2); recruited TANs further reinforce inflammatory and EMT-promoting signalling, producing a positive feedback that stabilises a hypoxia-EMT cancer-cell state.

## Counter-evidence

- No direct genetic or pharmacological perturbation is performed to validate the feedback.
- Sequencing-based methods alone cannot disentangle cause from effect.
