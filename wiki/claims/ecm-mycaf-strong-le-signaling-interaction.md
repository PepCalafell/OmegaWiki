## Statement
Ecm-myCAFs are the dominant stromal partner of LE cancer cells, with both greater interaction count and strength than cancer-cancer signalling at the LE or TC.

## Evidence summary
Supplementary Fig. 3g; ecm-myCAF-LE interaction strength outstrips LE-LE and TC-TC signalling. Top ligand-receptor pairs are COL1A1-SDC1 and FN1-SDC1.

## Conditions and scope
HPV-negative OSCC ST, CellChat inference.

## Counter-evidence
Inferred interactions are not perturbationally validated.

## Open questions
Whether LRRC15-targeted ADCs (e.g. Samrotamab) reverse LE biology via ecm-myCAF depletion.
