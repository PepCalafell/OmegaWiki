## Statement

F3 FRC-like fibroblasts are spatially enriched in the superficial perivascular region adjacent to immune cells, defining a superficial perivascular immune niche.

## Evidence summary

Spatial transcriptomics (Visium/Xenium) and H&E annotation; niche confirmed in inflamed atopic dermatitis.

## Conditions and scope

Skin; superficial perivascular niche.

## Counter-evidence

None.

## Open questions

Whether localization is required for the predicted immune-organizing function.
