## Statement

Metabolomics of tumor homogenates and interstitial fluid shows nucleotide intermediates among the most depleted metabolites under hypoxia, with specific depletion of adenine, adenosine and AMP and accumulation of the breakdown product hypoxanthine.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.7): "Nucleotide intermediates and nucleotide derivatives were among the most significantly depleted metabolites in tumor homogenates from mice exposed to hypoxia" — Tumor homogenate (normoxia n=16, hypoxia n=8) and TIF metabolomics (Fig.4a-e); IMP enriched, di/trinucleotides depleted. (type: quantitative, strength: strong)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
