## Statement

Hypoxia globally increases the repressive histone marks H3K27me3 and H3K9me3 in both microglia (BV2) and glioma (GL261) cells, indicating a broad shift toward repressive chromatin under low oxygen.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence of histone methylation marks (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6).

> "Hypoxia globally increased histone 3 (H3) lysine 27 trimethylation (H3K27me3) or H3 lysine 9 trimethylation (H3K9me3) marks in both glioma and BV2 cells"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 and GL261; in vitro hypoxia; immunofluorescence.

## Counter-evidence

Global IF measures bulk levels, not locus-specific deposition; consistent with known hypoxic inhibition of oxygen-dependent demethylases.
