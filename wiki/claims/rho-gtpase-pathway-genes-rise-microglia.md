## Statement

Genes in the Rho GTPase pathway are progressively upregulated in microglia along the scSLIDE Alzheimer trajectory — an enrichment linked to aberrant microglial activation and proposed as a therapeutic target, but not previously identified in single-cell AD studies.

## Evidence summary

Figure 4f-h of [[reconstructing-developmental-disease-progression-sample-level]]; presented as a novel interpretable consequence of the increased power of trajectory-based DE, reproduced in Psych-AD.

## Conditions and scope

Microglia specifically; framed by the authors as a new finding enabled by continuous modeling.

## Counter-evidence

Novel and as yet not independently functionally validated; confidence held below the strongly-validated CPS claim.

## Linked ideas

Specific instance of [[trajectory-differential-expression-recovers-alzheimer-linked]].

## Open questions

Is Rho GTPase activation causal or a downstream marker of disease-associated microglia?
