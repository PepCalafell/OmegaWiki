## Statement

Reduced handgrip strength is consistently associated with poor survival in CAC across European-based and Chinese cohorts, and with slow walking speed in elderly Indian populations. HGS is the most commonly reported functional endpoint in CAC clinical trials.

## Evidence summary

Multi-population epidemiologic evidence; HGS used in 12 (36%) of trials reviewed.

## Conditions and scope

Population norms vary; requires age- and sex-stratified cutoffs.

## Counter-evidence

In some trials, HGS was non-significant while 6MWT/ECOG were positive — suggesting differential sensitivity.

## Linked ideas

- HGS as a baseline stratification covariate.

## Open questions

- Whether HGS predicts response to anti-CAC interventions vs only baseline prognosis.
