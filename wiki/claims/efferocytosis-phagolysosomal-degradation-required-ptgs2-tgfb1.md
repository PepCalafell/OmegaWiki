## Statement

Phagolysosomal degradation of engulfed apoptotic cells (not engulfment per se) is the obligate trigger for AC-induced Ptgs2 (COX2) and Tgfb1 upregulation in bone marrow-derived and human monocyte-derived macrophages.

## Evidence summary

Two orthogonal perturbations of phagolysosomal degradation (siRbcn against Rubicon, blocking LAP-driven lysosome-phagosome fusion; bafilomycin A1, blocking V-ATPase-driven acidification) abolish AC-induced Ptgs2 and Tgfb1 in BMDMs (Fig. 1a-d) and HMDMs (Fig. 1e-f) without affecting AC engulfment (ED Fig. 1f). Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

Limited to BMDM and HMDM with Jurkat AC source; not tested with other AC types or in tissue-resident macrophages.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
