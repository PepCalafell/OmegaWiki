## Statement

F6 inflammatory myofibroblasts, equivalent to skin F6, are significantly more abundant in inflamed than non-inflamed IBD intestinal tissue and their proportion tracks clinical inflammation severity, extending the inflammatory-myofibroblast concept across tissues.

## Evidence summary

IBD scRNA-seq with metadata; linear regression of F6 proportion vs inflammation score (Fig. 7e).

## Conditions and scope

Intestinal IBD; cross-tissue equivalence to skin F6.

## Counter-evidence

Correlative association.

## Open questions
