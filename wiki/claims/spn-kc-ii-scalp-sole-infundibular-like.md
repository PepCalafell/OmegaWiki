## Statement

Spinous keratinocyte cluster II is preferentially enriched in scalp and sole skin and shows an infundibular-like transcriptional program (S100A8, SOX9, GJB2, GJB6).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Extended Data Fig. 3.

## Conditions and scope

Donor-matched scalp/sole datasets are lacking; site-specific vs donor-specific variation not fully resolved.

## Counter-evidence

Neither scRNA-seq nor MERFISH resolved fully distinct scalp-specific or sole-specific IFE KC subsets — authors hypothesise Spn KC II is shared.

## Open questions
