## Statement

Tissue context determines how much of a cytokine pair's effect is shared with other TNF-anchored pairs: liver and kidney are dominated by a common TNF-anchored program, while heart, spleen and lymph nodes display pair-specific transcriptional signatures.

## Conditions and scope

Qualitative tissue partition; thresholds for "shared" vs "pair-specific" are operational, not absolute.
