## Statement

None of the eight tested TEXterm single-state TF gene KOs (`Zfp324`, `Irf8`, `Zfp410`, `Nfatc1`, `Zscan20`, `Jdp2`, `Arid3a`, `Etv5`) negatively affect TRM cell frequency or TRM-signature gene expression in acute LCMV–Armstrong infection — confirming the selectivity predicted by Taiji.
