## Statement

Atopic dermatitis-specific upregulated genes localized to skin-resident memory classes, ILC classes, and effector memory CTLs, and were enriched for TH2-associated transcripts including TWIST1, IL17RB, the AD susceptibility loci NBAS and CYSLTR1, the epigenetic regulator KMT2A (MLL1), the lncRNA NEAT1, and AHNAK.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]. Supports [[concepts/trm1-th2-th17-molecular-classification-inflammatory]]; reinforces models of ILC and CD8+ involvement in atopic diathesis.

## Conditions and scope

AD-vs-PV DEGs meeting the heterogeneity filter in at least one lymphocyte population.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
