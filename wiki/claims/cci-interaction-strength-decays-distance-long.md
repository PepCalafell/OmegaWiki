## Statement

GITIII's influence tensor recapitulates the general principle that cell–cell interaction strength decays with distance, and additionally enables identification of specific long-range interactions that would be overlooked by methods that rely on predefined spatial neighbourhoods.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/identifying-spatial-single-cell-level-interactions]] (p.146): "It successfully recapitulates the general principle that interaction strength decays with distance, and enables the identification of specific long-range interactions that would be overlooked by methods that determine CCIs only within predefined spatial neighbourhoods." Demonstrated across diverse spatial transcriptomics datasets, species, organs, and platforms per the commentary. See [[concepts/cci-influence-tensor]].
