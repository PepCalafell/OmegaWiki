## Statement

Stringently-defined melanoma cells (BRAF V600E subtype) upregulate immune-evasion checkpoints (CTLA4, CD274/PD-L1), cytokine/immunoregulatory genes, and melanin-synthesis genes relative to normal melanocytes, while downregulating the suppressor CST6.

## Evidence summary

68 DE genes spanning immune evasion, signaling, differentiation, and melanin pathways. (p.7, Fig 4b-c, Fig S5)

## Conditions and scope

BRAF V600E melanomas (~50% of cases); only 118 melanoma cells.

## Counter-evidence

Very small melanoma-cell number; some immune-gene signal may reflect contamination/admixture.

## Open questions

Whether the evasion signature is melanoma-cell-intrinsic vs niche-derived.
