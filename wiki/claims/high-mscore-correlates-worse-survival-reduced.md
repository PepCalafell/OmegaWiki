## Statement

In the IMvigor210 cohort, patients with high Mscore had significantly worse overall survival and a markedly lower ICB response rate (14%) than low-Mscore patients (44%, P<0.001).

## Evidence summary

Outcome association from [[papers/multiomics-analysis-cxcl9-macrophages-immunotherapy-response]]; downstream of [[claims/five-gene-mscore-predicts-immune-checkpoint]].

## Conditions and scope

Single-arm atezolizumab (anti-PD-L1) cohort; retrospective; dichotomised by survminer-derived cutoff.

## Counter-evidence

Cutoff optimisation can inflate effect sizes; no external prospective validation.
