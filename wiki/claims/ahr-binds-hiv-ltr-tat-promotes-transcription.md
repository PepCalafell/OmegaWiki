## Statement

AHR directly binds the HIV-1 5'LTR and the viral Tat protein, recruiting transcription factors to viral promoters and activating viral transcription. siRNA/shRNA knockdown of AHR impairs HIV infectivity in Magi/CCR5, HEK293T, and primary CD4+ T cells. Kynurenine and FICZ AHR agonists reactivate HIV from latency in PBMCs of cART-treated individuals.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 133–138, 141. CH-223191 reduces HIC1 (Tat-mediated transcription repressor) expression and increases HIV replication in CD4+ T cells from individuals on antiretroviral treatment.

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type specific: FICZ activation blocks HIV replication at early steps in macrophages (ref 140) but not in CD4+ T cells — illustrating divergent AHR-HIV outcomes by cell type.

## Counter-evidence

Macrophage FICZ-AHR blocks early HIV replication (ref 140) — opposite to T-cell AHR effects.
