## Statement

STING-agonist ADCs (XMT-2056 HER2; TAK-500 CCR2; EGFR-STING) reduce systemic cytokine release relative to free agonist.

## Evidence summary

XMT-2056 (diABZI + HER2 antibody): ~10× lower systemic cytokines vs free agonist, Phase 1 ongoing (NCT05514717). TAK-500: 100× lower effective dose preclinically — but Phase 1/2 + pembrolizumab (NCT05070247) terminated for futility.

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type-targeted delivery; reduces toxicity but does not on its own deliver clinical efficacy (TAK-500 cautionary).
