## Statement

AHR confers resistance to M. tuberculosis: Ahr-KO mice show higher early lung bacterial burden and earlier mortality than WT; in human macrophages, RNAi/CH-223191 inhibition of AHR reduces Mtb phagocytosis, while KYN or indirubin agonism reduces intracellular Mtb load. The Mtb naphthoquinone phthiocol is a documented AHR-activating bacterial ligand.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 9 and 80.

## Conditions and scope

Mechanism links AHR to expression of antimycobacterial cytokines/chemokines; restored by exogenous AHR-agonist provision.

## Counter-evidence

Variation across model systems is acknowledged; whether AHR-targeted host-directed therapy benefits TB clinically is not yet established.
