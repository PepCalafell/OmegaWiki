## Statement

Kupffer-cell abundance increases under NASH (1.61-fold, P=2.06×10⁻¹³) and western-diet-with-alcohol (WDA, 1.23-fold, P=1.38×10⁻²) conditions but remains stable under high-fat diet (HFD), consistent with stronger inflammatory recruitment in NASH/WDA.

## Evidence summary

DECODE applied across mouse liver multiomics cohorts (285 samples, transcriptomics+proteomics+metabolomics; chow/HFD/HFD-TLC/NASH/WDA) (Fig. 6f,g). Authors attribute the increase to inflammation-driven Kupffer-cell recruitment in NASH and Western-alcohol diets.

## Conditions and scope

Abundances are deconvolution-inferred, not directly counted; mouse cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

None reported; mechanism is interpretive.
