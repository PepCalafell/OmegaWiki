## Statement

In mouse liver MERSCOPE, stellate cell Fa2 significantly covaries with Kupffer cell Fa1 (log10P = -3.08, 7781 co-localized pairs out of 11,881 stellate and 38,932 Kupffer cells).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — Ridge regression on Kupffer cell Fa1 dependence of stellate Fa2 in full MERSCOPE liver slice; replicated on technical replicate and on 22.7%-subsampled region (Fig. 6a; Suppl. Fig. 8–9).
