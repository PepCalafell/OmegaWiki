## Statement

Hypoxia severely impairs NK-cell cytotoxic effector function by reducing intracellular perforin and granzyme B, downregulating the activating receptor NKG2D, inducing HIF1α-driven ADAM10 metalloproteinase that sheds MICA from cancer cell surfaces, and triggering HIF2α-ITPR1 driven autophagy that degrades granzyme B. Hyperoxia and engineered high-affinity NK (haNK) partially rescue.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxic TME; haNK approach maintains function under acute hypoxia.

## Counter-evidence

NK cell HIF1α deletion has paradoxical effects — inhibits some tumor growth but also reduces sVEGFR1, promoting non-productive angiogenesis and metastasis.
