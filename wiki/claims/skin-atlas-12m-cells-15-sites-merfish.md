## Statement

The study generates an organ-wide MERFISH atlas of ~1.2 million cells spanning 114 samples, 22 adult donors and 15 anatomic skin sites, with 45 cell types localised in situ.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] (Restrepo et al., *Nat Genet* 2026).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult skin only; demographic coverage is limited to the 22-donor MERFISH cohort.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
