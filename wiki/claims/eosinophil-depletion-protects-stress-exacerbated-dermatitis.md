## Statement

Eosinophils are required for stress-exacerbated AD-like dermatitis but not for baseline MC903-induced AD pathology. Stress-driven worsening of TEWL, scratching, dermal thickening and CD45+ infiltration is abolished in eosinophil-deficient mice.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], Fig. 2G–J, fig. S5.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only; constitutive (developmental) eosinophil ablation.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope. Clinically, IL-5/IL-5Rα depletion of eosinophils has shown limited benefit in AD trials — the discussion frames this as consistent with the present finding (eosinophils dispensable for baseline disease).
