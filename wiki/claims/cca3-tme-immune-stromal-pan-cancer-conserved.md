## Statement

TME immune (T, B, NK, macrophage, dendritic, mast, plasma) and stromal (fibroblast, endothelial, pericyte) cell types have only minimal cancer-type effect on average pseudobulk expression in 3CA v2, supporting pan-cancer pooling of these populations for TME analyses.

## Evidence summary

Pseudobulk comparison (Fig. 4f).

## Conditions and scope

Refers to average expression profiles, not within-tumor state distributions.

## Counter-evidence

State-level heterogeneity in TAMs and T cells differs across cancer types, even when average profiles are similar.
