## Statement

ERK and broader MAPK signaling raise HIF-1α at both the transcriptional level and the transactivation level — partly by phosphorylating the p300/CBP coactivator complex to enhance HIF-1α-dependent transcription, and partly by stress-induced HIF-1α mRNA upregulation. This axis is engaged by therapeutic stressors (hyperthermia, photodynamic therapy) that promote treatment resistance via HIF-1α-driven survival programs.

## Evidence summary

ROS-ERK and AKT-ERK arms both converge on HIF-1α. Pharmacological MEK/ERK inhibition reduces HIF-1α induction under multiple stress paradigms in tumor cells.

## Conditions and scope

- Active under therapy stress and constitutive MAPK activation (RAS/RAF mutant tumors)
- Synergizes with ROS-PHD inhibition axis

## Counter-evidence

ERK inhibition alone rarely fully abrogates HIF-1α accumulation under bona fide hypoxia.
