## Statement

The small-molecule HIF-1/2 transcriptional inhibitor 32-134D combined with anti-PD-1 increases hepatocellular carcinoma eradication from 25% (anti-PD-1 alone) to 67% (combination) in mice — direct evidence for synergy between hypoxia-axis inhibition and immune-checkpoint blockade.

## Evidence summary

Preclinical evidence quoted in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse HCC model only; awaiting clinical replication.

## Counter-evidence

Single inhibition of HIF1α in immune cells can paradoxically induce PD-L1 expression in cancer cells, impairing anticancer immunity — requires multichannel combination.
