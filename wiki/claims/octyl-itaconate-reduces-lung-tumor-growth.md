## Statement

The cell-permeable itaconate derivative 4-octyl itaconate (Octyl Ita) suppresses lung tumor growth and metastasis across in vivo mouse models, ex vivo human tumor precision-cut lung slices, and in vitro cell lines, while shifting TAMs toward an anti-tumor phenotype.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1463–1465) — pharmacological evidence.
  - Quote: "4-octyl itaconate (Octyl Ita) reduces tumor growth in vitro, in vivo, and in ex vivo human tumor precision-cut lung slices."
  - Reduced total tumor area/lesion size (KrasLA2), fewer/smaller nodules and lower proliferation (LLC1), enriched anti-tumor TAMs (CD80+) in TD-PCLS.

## Conditions and scope

Multiple models and delivery routes; apoptosis effects were modest/context-dependent.
