## Statement

Tumour explants and organotypic slice cultures preserve the TME for only hours to days, in contrast to native immune organoids that can be maintained for weeks to months.

## Evidence summary

Tumour explants are short-term (48-96 h) cultures, whereas native immune organoids can be maintained for weeks to months (Polak et al. 2024, p.532).

## Conditions and scope

Tumour fragment explants vs ALI/PDOTS organoid platforms.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded at ingest.
