## Statement

Latent longitudinal trajectory modelling on n=2,138 body-composition measurements in liver-cancer patients identifies two distinct trajectories for skeletal muscle area and for total adipose-tissue area — "stable" and "sharp-falling". Patients with sharp-falling trajectories are defined as CAC and show worse overall survival, progression-free survival, and treatment response.

## Evidence summary

Single-cohort latent-trajectory analysis (Jin et al.); first demonstration of trajectory-based CAC definition in liver cancer.

## Conditions and scope

Liver cancer; trajectory modelling requires longitudinal CT availability.

## Counter-evidence

None known; needs replication in other tumour types.

## Linked ideas

- Methodological precedent for prospective trajectory-based stratification.

## Open questions

- Do trajectory subtypes correspond to body-composition subtypes from cross-sectional analyses?
