## Statement
UMAP and hierarchical clustering of TPCPA proteomes group samples by cancer type and largely separate solid from non-solid (blood) cancers, with cross-laboratory and FF/FFPE-mixed samples (blood, liver, prostate, DLBCL) co-clustering by tumor type — indicating biological signal dominates over batch effects.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 — Figure 1C–D and Figure S1F.

## Conditions and scope
- Bulk DIA-MS proteomes only; multi-laboratory acquisition; minimum 5 samples / cancer type.

## Counter-evidence
- Some sub-clustering within solid cancers is interspersed (heterogeneous types) — clustering captures dominant signal, not perfect separation.
