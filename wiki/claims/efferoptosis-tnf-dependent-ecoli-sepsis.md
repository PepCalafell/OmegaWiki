## Statement

In E. coli-induced septic peritonitis, Tnfr1-/- mice retain efferocytosis but abrogate death of neutrophil-engulfing macrophages and survive better, confirming efferoptosis is TNF-dependent.

## Evidence summary

Despite similar serum TNF, CXCL-1/IL-6/IL-1β drop in Tnfr1-/-. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.12).

> "in the absence of TNF signaling, macrophages are capable of undergoing homeostatic efferocytosis without switching to lytic efferoptosis"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.
