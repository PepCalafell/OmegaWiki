## Statement

STING agonists show a major translational gap between preclinical mouse efficacy and human clinical efficacy.

## Evidence summary

After nearly a decade of clinical testing, no STING agonist has progressed beyond Phase 2. Multiple Phase 1 and 2 trials (ADU-S100, MK-1454, MK-2118, SNX281, TAK-500) were terminated for lack of efficacy or business reasons.

## Conditions and scope

Direct cGAMP-class and non-CDN agonists; intratumoral delivery; mostly monotherapy or PD-1 combination.
