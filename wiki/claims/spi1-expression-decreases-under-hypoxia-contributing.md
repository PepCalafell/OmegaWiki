## Statement

Spi1 (PU.1) expression itself decreases under hypoxia in microglia, which may compound the loss of P2ry12 (a PU.1/IRF8 target) beyond chromatin closure at its promoter.

## Evidence summary

Observed in RNA-seq, proposed as a contributing mechanism (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.12).

> "we found that Spi1 expression decreased in hypoxia in our RNA-seq data, potentially contributing to the downregulation of P2ry12"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; RNA-seq association.

## Counter-evidence

Authors frame this as a possibility ("potentially"); causal contribution not tested.

_None yet._

## Open questions

Whether restoring Spi1 rescues P2ry12 under hypoxia.
