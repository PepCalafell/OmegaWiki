## Statement
TPCPA hallmark analysis adds MTORC1 pathway activation as a new proteome-level feature of colorectal cancer consensus molecular subtype CMS1, complementing the established immune-rich / MSI-high characterisation of this subtype.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figure 7A.

## Conditions and scope
- 195 CRC samples; protein hallmark scoring; cohort-stratified subanalysis is consistent.

## Counter-evidence
- mTORC1 enrichment has been described transcriptionally in MSI-H CRC; the novelty here is at the proteome level.
