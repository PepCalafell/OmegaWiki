## Statement

TP53 is the prototypical superstar gene: discovered in 1979, mentioned in 9,232 publications (1976–2017), crucial to cell growth and death, and altered in more than 50% of tumour sequences.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "it is mentioned in 9,232 publications between 1976 and 2017"; "Variations in this gene are found in more than 50% of tumour sequences." (p.1, p.2–3)

## Conditions and scope

Human cancers; bibliometric counts to 2017.

## Counter-evidence

Intense study here is biologically justified, unlike purely momentum-driven cases.
