## Statement

Tumors with high microsatellite instability (MSI-H) express significantly less PHF2 than MSI-low tumors, in line with PHF2 supporting genomic stability.

## Evidence summary

Direct expression comparison between MSI groupings showed lower PHF2 in MSI-H tumors.

## Conditions and scope

Cross-sectional TCGA comparison; supports the genome-stability framing of PHF2 in vivo.

## Counter-evidence

Correlational only; PHF8 not reported to show the same MSI association.

## Open questions

Does PHF2 loss cause MSI, or do MSI processes downregulate PHF2?
