## Statement
TPCPA hallmark analysis adds ROS pathway, p53 pathway, UV response, and hypoxia signaling as new proteome-level features of CRC CMS4, refining the previously RNA-defined mesenchymal/EMT/angiogenesis signature of this poor-prognosis subtype.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figure 7A.

## Conditions and scope
- 195 CRC samples; consensus molecular subtype assignment from RNA.

## Counter-evidence
- Some of these hallmarks were known transcriptionally for CMS4-related tumours; the novelty is the proteome confirmation.
