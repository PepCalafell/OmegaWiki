## Statement

CD8 effector responses, especially IL-12-induced IFN-γ output under nutrient restriction, require ACLY-derived acetyl-CoA. FASN sits at a therapeutic trade-off: its loss raises tumour MHC-I and enhances CD8 killing of cancer cells and impairs Treg function (suppressing tumour growth), but it is required for memory CD8 survival — creating a narrow therapeutic window for FASN inhibitors.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]].

## Conditions and scope

- Murine tumour models; in vitro CD8 / Treg systems.

## Counter-evidence

- Some tumour types appear to depend less on FASN; dose-window not yet defined clinically.

## Open questions

- Whether intermittent or low-dose FASN inhibition can capture the antitumor / Treg-blocking benefit without ablating memory CD8 survival.
