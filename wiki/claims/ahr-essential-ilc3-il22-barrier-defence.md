## Statement

AHR is required for ILC3 survival and proliferation (IL-7R, Bcl-2, Ki67), for NKp46⁺ IL-22-producing ILC3 maintenance via Notch, and for clearance of barrier pathogens including segmented filamentous bacteria and *Citrobacter rodentium*.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Kiss et al. 2011; Lee et al.; Qiu et al.).
