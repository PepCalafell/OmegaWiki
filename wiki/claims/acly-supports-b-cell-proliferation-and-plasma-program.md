## Statement

ACLY-generated cytoplasmic acetyl-CoA is required for B-cell proliferation, endomembrane expansion supporting Ig secretion, and the CD138/Blimp-1 plasma-cell differentiation program; SCD-mediated synthesis of oleic acid (MUFA) is independently required for germinal-centre formation during immunization and infection.
