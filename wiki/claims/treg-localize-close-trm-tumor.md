## Statement

In KP NSCLC tumour lesions, FOXP3⁺ regulatory T cells (Tregs) localize in close spatial proximity to MRC1⁺ tissue-resident macrophages (TRMs), with mean inter-cell distance of ~15 μm. Treg accumulation increases with tumour progression and correlates with tumour growth.

## Evidence summary

- Confocal imaging at days 5, 10, 15, 25, 30 post KP injection
- Mean Treg-TRM distance ~15 μm at day 15
- Treg cell numbers and frequency increase with tumour progression
- Proliferative Ki67⁺ Tregs increase at day 15 and day 30 (P=0.0011, P<0.0001)

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC
- Single longitudinal experiment (n=3-8 mice per time point)
- 2D imaging-based quantification

## Counter-evidence

- Spatial proximity does not prove causal contact; mediated also by chemokine gradients
- Single experiment; replication in independent cohorts would strengthen
- Human Treg-TRM spatial relationship not directly tested in this paper
