## Statement

Multivariate Cox-regression analyses across 7 independent LUAD cohorts identify the response-to-hypoxia signature and the TAN signature — but not the NAN signature — as significantly associated with worse overall survival.

## Open questions

- Are the two signatures statistically independent prognostic features in a pooled meta-Cox, or does one dominate the other?
- Is the prognostic value retained after immunotherapy (ICI) era treatment cohorts?
