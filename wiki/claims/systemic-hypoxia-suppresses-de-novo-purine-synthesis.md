## Statement

Dual gamma-15N-glutamine/8-13C-adenine tracing shows hypoxia lowers 15N labeling of IMP and AMP both in Panc02 cells and in tumors in vivo, demonstrating suppressed de novo purine synthesis.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.9): "both the in vitro and in vivo labeling experiments indicate that hypoxia suppressed de novo purine synthesis" — In vitro 0.5% O2 (Fig.5f) and in vivo 5h infusion (Fig.5g, S4d-i); normalized 15N-IMP/AMP lower in hypoxia. (type: mechanistic, strength: strong)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
