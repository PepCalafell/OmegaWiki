## Statement

ISG+ TAMs induced by Adar knockout express T-cell-recruiting chemokines (Cxcl9/10) and Cd40, enhancing CXCL10-CXCR3 and CD40-CD40L myeloid-T interactions, yielding fewer exhausted (LAG3+PD-1+) and more cytotoxic (GZMB+) CD8+ T cells.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2071-2072) — mechanistic evidence. CellPhoneDB myeloid-T interaction analysis; FACS of CD8 exhaustion/effector markers in Adar-mKO tumors.
  - Quote: "scRNA-seq and flow cytometry revealed fewer exhausted (LAG3+ PD-1+) and more cytotoxic (GZMB+) CD8+ T cells in Adar mKO tumors compared with controls"
