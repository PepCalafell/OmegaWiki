## Statement
Within the open-chromatin activation TR network, mRNA expression of central TRs is stimulus-specific even though the promoter/enhancer chromatin landscape is uniform — implying a transcriptional control layer beyond epigenetic state.

## Evidence summary
Differential-expression patterns of network TRs differed sharply by stimulus while chromatin marks were uniform across stimuli, decoupling expression from accessibility.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages; headline observation supporting the open-chromatin-predefined network concept.

## Counter-evidence
Tissue networks instead show expression tracking chromatin (see [[claims/human-tissue-tr-networks-follow-integrated]]).

## Open questions
