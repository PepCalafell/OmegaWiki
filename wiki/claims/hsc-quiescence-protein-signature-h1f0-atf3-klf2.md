## Statement

A protein-level signature of HSC quiescence in human CD34+ HSPCs centers on H1F0 (linker histone), ATF3, KLF2, and chromatin compaction; exit from quiescence is marked by CDK6, SOX4, and APEX1.

## Evidence summary

Correlation-based ranking of mRNA and protein abundance against pseudotime in the HSC subset. Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 4 D-G).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+; HSC subset analysis on the joint-latent-space pseudotime.

## Counter-evidence

mRNA layer alone often missed these signals (low effect sizes in low-transcription HSCs).
