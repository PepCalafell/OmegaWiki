## Statement
Reverse network engineering identifies JUNB (AP-1), NFKB1 (NF-κB), and CREB1 among the most highly expressed hub transcription factors, representing activation-independent common denominators of human macrophage activation (alongside the novel hub HIVEP1).

## Evidence summary
- "we ... generated a network of the top five TFs (JUNB, NFKB1, HIVEP1, CREB1, and HBP1)" (p.283, Figure 6D).
- "Roles in macrophage activation have been established for all of these TFs, except for the zinc finger protein HIVEP1" (p.283).
- Gene prioritization added STAT3, HMGA1, NFE2, ZNF148, etc. (p.283, Figure 6E).

## Conditions and scope
Hub ranking by expression; associative network evidence.
