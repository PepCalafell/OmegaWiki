## Statement
The major macrophage hub gene loci are enriched for transcription-factor binding (26 of 27 hub TFs with significant predicted binding) and for PU.1 occupancy plus permissive H3K4me3 histone marks, indicating active transcription during macrophage activation.

## Evidence summary
- "Twenty-six out of 27 of these TFs showed significantly enriched binding prediction (p value < 0.05)" (p.283).
- "a strong enrichment of PU.1 binding and permissive histone marks H3K4me3 at the loci of the 869 major hub genes indicate that these genes are likely to be highly transcribed" (p.283, Figure S5).

## Conditions and scope
PWM predictions plus ChIP-seq evidence.
