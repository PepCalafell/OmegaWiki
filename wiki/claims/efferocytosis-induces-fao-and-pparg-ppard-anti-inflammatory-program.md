## Statement

After engulfment, macrophages catabolize apoptotic-cell-derived lipids to fuel FAO and OXPHOS; this lipid load induces PPARδ (and PPARγ in Ly6C⁻ subset), driving opsonin upregulation and an anti-inflammatory transcriptional program required for further apoptotic-cell clearance.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Mukundan 2009 and Bouhlel 2007.

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse tissue regeneration models.

## Counter-evidence

- PPARγ-deficient macrophages can still acquire anti-inflammatory phenotype in vivo — implying redundancy/compensation by PPARδ.
