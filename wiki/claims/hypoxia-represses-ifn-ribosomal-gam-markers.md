## Statement

Hypoxia broadly remodels GAM subtype marker programs: it predominantly downregulates IFN-, transitory-, and ribosomal-GAM markers while upregulating phagocytic and chemotactic GAM markers, suggesting cytoskeletal remodeling and debris-clearance functions.

## Evidence summary

Assessed against curated GAM-cluster marker sets (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6).

> "Markers of IFN, transitory and ribo-GAMs were predominantly downregulated, while phagocytic and chemotactic GAM genes were upregulated by hypoxia"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia, confirmed in primary BMDM.

## Counter-evidence

Some lipid-cluster genes (Ftl1, Fth1, Fabp5) were downregulated only in co-culture, indicating gene-specific complexity.

## Open questions

Whether the functional shift (more phagocytic/chemotactic) translates to altered debris clearance in vivo.
