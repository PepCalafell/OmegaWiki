## Statement

Cholesterol crystals are phagocytosed and stored in macrophage lipid droplets; their accumulation drives dose-dependent IL-1β secretion through caspase-1 / NLRP3 inflammasome activation, with intracellular C5aR1 → mitochondrial ROS providing a required second signal.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] integrating cholesterol-crystal and complement biology.

## Conditions and scope

- Sterile inflammation, atherosclerosis.

## Counter-evidence

- C5aR1 role challenged in some C5-deficient models — mechanism may be redundant in vivo.

## Open questions

- Therapeutic potential of intracellular C5aR1 blockade alongside IL-1β neutralisation (canakinumab).
