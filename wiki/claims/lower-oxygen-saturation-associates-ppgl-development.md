## Statement

Greater hypoxia severity (lower oxygen saturation) is associated with higher likelihood of PPGL development among CCHD patients.

## Evidence summary

SatO2 comparison overall and in the unoperated (natural-history) subgroup.

## Conditions and scope

Observational clinical data; small absolute SatO2 differences.

## Counter-evidence

Difference in means is modest (~2–4%).

## Open questions

Dose–response threshold of hypoxia for tumor risk.
