## Statement

A conserved core IFN-I antiviral ISG program (e.g., IFI44L, ISG15, LY6E, TAP1, MX1, IFI6) is strongly and broadly induced across all human immune cell types, with higher fold changes than subset-specific genes.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.4-5). The core program converges on antiviral defense, consistent with [[isgf3-complex]] biology and [[type-interferon-ifna-ifnb]]. See [[core-versus-subset-specific-isg-programs]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating immune subsets; IFN-I stimulation.

## Counter-evidence

None; subset-specific programs coexist but at lower magnitude.

## Open questions
