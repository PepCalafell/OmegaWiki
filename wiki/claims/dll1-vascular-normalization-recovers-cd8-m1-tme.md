## Statement

Elevated DLL1 (Notch ligand) in breast and lung cancer induces long-term tumor vascular normalization, alleviating intra-tumoral hypoxia, promoting IFN-γ⁺ CD8 T-cell accumulation, and shifting macrophages from M2 toward the M1 phenotype — a mechanism that avoids the narrow window/rebound problem of anti-VEGF normalization.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Breast and lung models; cancer-type-specific.

## Counter-evidence

DLL1 in other contexts can be pro-tumorigenic via Notch signaling on tumor cells directly.
