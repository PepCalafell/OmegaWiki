## Statement

Clinical-association analysis across the atlas identified clusters negatively correlated with tumor progression and positively associated with overall survival and ICB objective response rates, enriched for adaptive immune response and leukocyte adhesion genes. Conversely, six clusters linked to poor prognosis were enriched for ECM organization, vascular development, and wound healing.

## Evidence summary

Associative, cross-cohort analysis; not causal. (p.3) Quote: "six clusters linked to poor prognosis were involved in extracellular matrix (ECM) organization, vascular development, and wound healing".

## Conditions and scope

Correlational associations across heterogeneous cohorts; confounders (cancer type, stage) not fully controlled.
