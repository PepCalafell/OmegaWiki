## Statement

TREM2⁺ TAMs are associated with worse OS in skin and lung cancers and sarcomas, where they dampen anti-tumor immunity.

## Counter-evidence

In HCC, TREM2⁺ macrophages are linked to better PD-1 response and pro-immune function — see [[claims/trem2-tam-hcc-better-pd1-response]].
