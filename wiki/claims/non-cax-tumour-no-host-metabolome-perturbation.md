## Statement

Non-cachexia-inducing NC26 mouse colon carcinoma cells produce tumours but do not perturb host-tissue metabolomes, demonstrating that metabolic alterations in the C26 model are tied to the cachexia phenotype rather than to tumour presence per se.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 1 e-l and Ext Fig. 2: "Non-cachexia-inducing NC26 tumours did not cause any major alterations in the metabolite profiles of host tissues compared with Ctrl... highlighting that most metabolic alterations are associated with the presence of a cachexia-inducing tumour."

## Conditions and scope

NC26 vs C26 syngeneic injections in BALB/c; matched tumour size between Non-cax and C26-Cax groups (tumour weight controls for tumour-mass-effects).

## Counter-evidence

Tumour metabolomes themselves differ between NC26 and C26, but host tissues are largely spared in Non-cax.
