## Statement

Macrophage function in vivo is best understood by integrating spatial/anatomical niche with transcriptional state rather than relying on surface markers alone; CD206 (Mrc1) is neither macrophage-restricted nor predictive of tumor-promoting function.

## Evidence summary

Synthesis of depletion, chimera and spatial-transcriptomic results across the study (Discussion). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].
