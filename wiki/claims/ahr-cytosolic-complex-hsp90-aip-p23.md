## Statement

In its inactive state, AHR is part of a cytoplasmic protein complex including an HSP90 dimer, the co-chaperone p23, AIP (XAP2), and c-Src; this complex preserves AHR's high-affinity ligand-binding conformation and prevents its nuclear translocation and ubiquitination.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] with cryo-EM structures of indirubin-bound complexes underpinning the model.
