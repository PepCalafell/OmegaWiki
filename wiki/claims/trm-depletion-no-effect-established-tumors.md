## Statement

Tissue-resident macrophage depletion in already-established KP NSCLC lesions (CD169-DTR + DT administered at days 12-15) does NOT affect tumour burden, the Treg compartment, the CD8⁺/Treg ratio, or the number of CD3⁺ tumour-infiltrating T cells — in marked contrast to the strong phenotypic effect observed when TRM depletion precedes tumour engraftment. This temporal restriction defines TRM niche function as an early-stage phenomenon.

## Evidence summary

- Late TRM depletion (days 12-15): tumour burden NS; Tregs NS; CD8/Treg ratio NS; CD3⁺ TIL NS
- Establishes the temporal window of TRM-niche dependence
- Mirrors the early-depletion phenotype as a control

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC
- Late depletion timing: day 12 onwards
- Same readouts as early-depletion experiment

## Counter-evidence

- The cellular and molecular reason for temporal restriction is not directly identified
- TRM repopulation kinetics post-DT not characterised; partial repopulation might mask any latent phenotype
