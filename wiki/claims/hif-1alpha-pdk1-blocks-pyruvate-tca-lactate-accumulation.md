## Statement

Under hypoxia, HIF1α transcriptionally activates pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1 (PDK1), which phosphorylates and inactivates pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH); pyruvate is then diverted away from the TCA cycle and reduced to lactate, raising intracellular ATP and reducing mitochondrial ROS — rescuing hypoxic cancer cells from apoptosis.

## Evidence summary

Direct mechanism quoted in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023). Coupled to MCT1/4 lactate export, NHE1 proton extrusion, and CA9 surface bicarbonate trapping, driving the acidic TME.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxic cancer cells. The shunt is essential for survival under chronic hypoxia.

## Counter-evidence

In some tumors with intact mitochondria, partial TCA flux persists via glutaminolysis (HIF1α inhibits α-KGDH but does not eliminate TCA entirely).
