## Statement

MiTE-144 (alone or with anti-PD-1/CTLA-4) disrupts multiple TAM–T cell ligand-receptor axes conserved between mouse and human tumor atlases, including Cd86-Ctla4, Pdcd1-Cd48, Ccr8-Ccl3, Lgals1-Cd69, Lgals3bp-Itgb1, and Tgfbr1-Tgfb1.
