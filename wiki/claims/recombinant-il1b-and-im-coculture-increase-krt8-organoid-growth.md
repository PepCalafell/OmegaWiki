## Statement

Direct exposure to IL-1β — whether as recombinant protein or via co-culture with primary lung interstitial macrophages — is sufficient to promote KAC-lineage organoid growth from parental AT2 cells, providing causal evidence that the IL-1β-IL1R1 niche drives the KAC transition functionally rather than merely correlating with it.
