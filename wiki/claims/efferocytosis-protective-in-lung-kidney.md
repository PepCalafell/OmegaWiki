## Statement

Blocking efferocytosis (TIM3 inhibition) exacerbates TUNEL+ cell death in lung and kidney, showing efferocytosis is tissue-protective there even as it is lethal in the spleen.

## Evidence summary

Reveals tissue-context-dependent costs/benefits. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.8).

> "TIM3 inhibition did not prevent and even exacerbated TNF-induced cell death as measured by TUNEL staining in the lung and kidney"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
