## Statement

In contrast to prior literature reporting HIF-1α induction in TR-AMs under Poly(I:C) or influenza infection (Zhu and colleagues), the present authors find that HIF-1α is stabilised in TR-AMs only by hypoxia or pharmacological PHD inhibition, not by LPS or Poly(I:C). They attribute the discrepancy to GM-CSF supplementation in prior studies, which induces TR-AM proliferation and may reflect proliferation-coupled rather than inflammation-coupled HIF-1α biology.

## Conditions and scope

In-vitro TR-AMs without GM-CSF; mouse only. The challenge of prior literature is conditional on culture conditions.

## Counter-evidence

Zhu and colleagues (refs 48, 49 in paper) report Poly(I:C)/influenza-induced HIF-1α in TR-AMs cultured with GM-CSF. Paper interprets this as a culture-conditions artifact / proliferation-linked effect.
