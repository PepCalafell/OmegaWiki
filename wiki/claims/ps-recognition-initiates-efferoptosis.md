## Statement

PS recognition is the initiating event of efferoptosis: annexin-V masking, CD14 deficiency, or TIM3/TAM inhibition blocks it, and PS-coated beads suffice to trigger TNF-dependent death.

## Evidence summary

Multiple orthogonal perturbations of PS sensing. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.7).

> "we coated dead neutrophils with annexin V to block exposed PS and observed a substantial decrease in macrophage cell death ... the stimulation of macrophages with PS-conjugated beads was sufficient to induce cell death in TNF-activated macrophages"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
