## Statement

In human RCC patient-derived tumor fragments cultured ex vivo, MiTE-144 (alone and combined with anti-PD-1) selectively expands CD8 cycling/memory T cells and intermediate-stage NK cells without expanding Tregs or hypoxic TAMs.
