## Statement

Beyond the conserved 48-gene core, TREM2 mac DEGs split into 209 liver-specific (HCC) and 89 lung-specific (NSCLC) genes. Liver-specific genes include MT1G, MT1H (oxidative-stress/ferroptosis protection) and CALR (pro-phagocytic, anti-CD47). Lung-specific genes include CCL20, S100A10, SPP1 (matricellular/tissue-remodeling).

## Evidence summary

- Tissue-specific DEGs computed after subtraction of resident-mac background (KCs in liver, AMs in lung).
- 209 vs 89 gene counts reported.

## Conditions and scope

- Numbers depend on DEG thresholds and background-correction strategy.

- None reported.
