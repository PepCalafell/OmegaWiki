## Statement

Commensal effectors preferentially target immune-signalling functions — notably MDP
response (the NOD2/Crohn's axis), NF-κB and SAPK/JNK — plus metabolic enzymes.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.448, Fig.4a): most enriched functions
include 'response to muramyl dipeptide', NF-κB and SAPK/JNK; 5 convergence proteins in
the NF-κB module; metabolic-enzyme enrichment (P=0.0001) via Recon3D. NF-κB signalling
is common to commensal and pathogen targets; MDP response and collagen biosynthesis are
commensal-specific.

## Conditions and scope

Enrichment analysis of HuMMI targets.

## Counter-evidence

Beyond glycerophospholipid metabolism, no metabolic subsystem stood out.
