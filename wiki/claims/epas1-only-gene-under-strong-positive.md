## Statement

Selection inference identifies EPAS1 as the sole gene under statistically significant strong positive selection in CCHD-PPGL.

## Evidence summary

dN/dS analysis (see [[foundations/dn-ds-positive-selection-inference]]); extreme ratios with q = 0.

## Conditions and scope

Cohort-specific; extreme dN/dS values reflect a low-mutation tumor type with focal selection.

## Counter-evidence

In normoxic TCGA PPGL, HRAS and NF1 show stronger selection than EPAS1.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Robustness of extreme dN/dS estimates in a small cohort.
