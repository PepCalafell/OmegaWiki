## Statement

Applied to external datasets, huCIRA recovers known and novel disease cytokine activity: SLE flare shows type I IFN + IL-15 (and IL-32-β in NK/non-classical monocytes) enrichment; MS shows cytotoxic NK CIPs (IFN-γ, IL-15); NSCLC tumor monocytes show GM-CSF/IL-3 activity driving TAM reprogramming.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: correlational.

## Conditions and scope

External published datasets analyzed via huCIRA: SLE PBMC scRNA-seq (162 cases/99 controls), MS PBMC scRNA-seq (5/5), NSCLC spatial transcriptomics (1 patient).
