## Statement

Commensal T3SS effectors functionally modulate NF-κB signalling in both directions —
some activate it at baseline, others suppress it under inflammatory (TNF) stimulation.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450, Fig.5a,b): 5/26 effectors activated
baseline NF-κB; 3 reduced NF-κB under TNF stimulation. NF-κB emerged repeatedly across
the study as a central targeted pathway.

## Conditions and scope

Overexpression in HEK293 reporter cells; not native injection.

## Counter-evidence

Effects are heterogeneous and context (stimulation) dependent.

## Linked ideas

Defines part of [[slim-pdz-effector-host-interface]] functional consequences; relates to
[[nf-kb-p65-rela]], [[tnf-tumor-necrosis-factor]].

## Open questions

Mechanism and in vivo relevance of bidirectional NF-κB modulation.
