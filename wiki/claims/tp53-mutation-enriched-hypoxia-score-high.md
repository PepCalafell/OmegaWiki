## Statement

TP53 is the most consistently hypoxia-associated mutated gene: its mutation frequency is markedly higher in hypoxia score-high tumours (e.g. 62.3% vs 8.0% in BRCA; 73.5% vs 34.6% in LUAD). This links the hypoxic state to p53 loss-of-function and to differential drug response (resistance to AZ-628, sensitivity to the prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitor DMOG).

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — TP53 mutation frequency and drug-response associations (Fig. 6a–c).
- Mechanistically consistent with prior reports that p53 mutation diminishes oxygen consumption.

## Conditions and scope

- Strongest in BRCA and LUAD; direction consistent across the seven cancer types analysed for mutation.

## Counter-evidence

- Association does not establish whether hypoxia selects for TP53 mutation or TP53 loss promotes a hypoxic phenotype.
