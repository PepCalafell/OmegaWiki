## Statement

Metabolites commonly altered in ≥2 cachexia target tissues map by KEGG pathway analysis to a coherent up-down pattern: one-carbon / pyrimidine / arginine pathways are upregulated; TCA / glycolysis / Ala-Asp-Glu / nitrogen / pyruvate metabolism are downregulated — a flux signature consistent with diversion of substrates into one-carbon metabolism at the expense of canonical energy generation.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 2 d-e: pathway maps and impact scores; Fig. 2 f-g: heatmaps of cross-tissue commonly altered metabolites. Quoted: "products of one-carbon metabolism (for example, sarcosine and dimethylglycine) and related pathways, such as pyrimidine synthesis... and arginine biosynthesis/metabolism."

## Conditions and scope

KEGG pathway analysis; significance threshold one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD; consistent across Cax/Ctrl and Cax/Non-cax comparisons.

## Counter-evidence

Tumour metabolomes show a separate pattern (treated separately in the paper).
