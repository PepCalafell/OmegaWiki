## Statement

G6PD is the functionally required target for Octyl itaconate's anti-proliferative effect: the drug adds no further growth inhibition when G6PD is already knocked down, and downstream PPP metabolites (not antioxidants) rescue proliferation.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1468) — mechanistic (epistasis) evidence.
  - Quote: "Octyl Ita failed to further reduce proliferation in G6PD-knockdown A549 cells ... Supplementation with downstream PPP metabolites (6-phosphogluconate, D-ribose, and ribose-5-phosphate) dose-dependently rescued proliferation, whereas antioxidant treatment with N-acetylcysteine (NAC) had no effect."

## Conditions and scope

A549 knockdown model; establishes G6PD as the non-redundant node linking Octyl Ita to growth suppression.
