## Statement

Two orthogonal IL6 perturbations (neutralising antibody and tumour-cell IL6 KO) both rescue cachexia weight loss without affecting tumour size and both suppress Nnmt mRNA induction in cachectic liver — a clean double dissociation showing that tumour-derived IL6 is required for the cachexia metabolic phenotype but not for tumour growth.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "IL6 inhibition did not reduce tumour size but improved cachexia-associated weight loss... Nnmt induction in liver by C26 tumours was repressed by 77% with IL6-neutralising antibody, and by 89% upon IL6 KO in C26 cells."

## Conditions and scope

Antibody dosing schedule and IL6-KO clone validation in Ext Fig. 5 k-m. Tumour size matched at endpoint despite slower early growth of IL6-KO tumours.

## Counter-evidence

C26-IL6-KO tumours grow more slowly initially — confounder for very early time points, but not at the cachexia-endpoint comparison.
