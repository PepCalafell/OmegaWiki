## Statement

MAT2A-catalysed conversion of methionine to S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) is required for AC-induced Ptgs2 and Tgfb1 induction; exogenous SAM bypasses the MAT2A requirement.

## Evidence summary

Two orthogonal MAT2A perturbations (PF-9366 chemical inhibition; siMat2a knockdown) block AC-induced Ptgs2/Tgfb1/TGFβ1 in BMDMs and HMDMs. Exogenous SAM rescues. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM/HMDM in vitro.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
