## Statement

Despite preclinical evidence that TREM2 blockade sensitizes to PD-1 in mouse models, clinical trials of anti-TREM2 (PY314) failed to deliver clinically significant antitumor activity, suggesting antagonism alone is insufficient to reprogram TREM2⁺ TAMs in human tumors.
