## Statement

Cycling hypoxia increases TMB and neoantigens in vitro, which would predict ICB sensitivity, yet hypoxic tumours are clinically refractory to checkpoint blockade — implying hypoxia-mediated immune-evasion mechanisms override the neoantigen signal.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Clinical observation; multiple tumour types.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
