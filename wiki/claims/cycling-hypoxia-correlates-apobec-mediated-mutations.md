## Statement

Cycling hypoxia and replication stress induce accumulation of APOBEC-mediated mutations in cell lines, and APOBEC mutation frequency correlates with elevated hypoxia signature scores in human tumours.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Cycling hypoxia. Cell-line + translational patient evidence.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
