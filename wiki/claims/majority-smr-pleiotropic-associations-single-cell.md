## Statement

Of 1196 SMR pleiotropic associations linking variants to molecular features and traits, the large majority act in a single immune cell type.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (quantitative): 73.2% of 1196 SMR associations (833 variants, 138 eGenes, 280 caPeaks, 68 traits across 68 cell types) were significant in only one cell type (p.8-9).

## Conditions and scope

154 traits including lipids, metabolites, 91 inflammation proteins, immune diseases; SMR over cis-xQTL.
