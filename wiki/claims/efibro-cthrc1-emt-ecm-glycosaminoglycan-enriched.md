## Statement

eFibro_CTHRC1 cells are top-enriched for EMT, ECM receptor interaction, and focal adhesion pathways and exhibit notably upregulated glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis (chondroitin sulfate / dermatan sulfate), defining their ECM-remodeling and matrix-deposition phenotype.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "eFibro_CTHRC1 cells were enriched for EMT and ECM receptor interaction pathways... the glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis–chondroitin sulfate/dermatan sulfate pathway... was notably upregulated in eFibro_CTHRC1 cells" (Han 2025, Fig. 3f,g).

## Linked ideas

- Glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis as candidate metabolic target for anti-CAF therapy.
