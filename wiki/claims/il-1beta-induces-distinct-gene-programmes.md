## Statement

IL-1α and IL-1β trigger highly cell-type-specific gene programmes — neutrophils upregulate Cd14 (GP27), MigDCs/LCs upregulate Ccr7 (GP12), Tregs induce Hif1a/Ctla4 (GP22) — composing a coordinated multicellular response.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

In vivo mouse lymph node, 4-h post-injection.
