## Statement

Associations between baseline methylation and trained-immunity cytokine responses are far more numerous in males than females, with no overlap and frequent direction differences.

## Evidence summary

Markedly larger male CpG sets across all four cytokines; nearly half of associations differ in direction between sexes.

## Conditions and scope

Sex-stratified baseline EWAS; young-adult cohort.

## Counter-evidence

Imbalanced power; biological vs statistical drivers unresolved.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Why baseline methylation is more predictive of trained immunity in males.
