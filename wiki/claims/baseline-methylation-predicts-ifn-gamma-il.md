## Statement

Baseline-methylation prediction of trained immunity is cytokine-specific, largely confined to IFN-γ.

## Evidence summary

Strong baseline signal for IFN-γ, essentially none for IL-1β/TNF-α, single CpG for IL-6, indicating selective epigenetic encoding of the T-cell-derived IFN-γ response.

## Conditions and scope

Baseline timepoint; later timepoints (T14/T90) show different cytokine patterns.

## Counter-evidence

Absence of signal could reflect power for monocyte cytokines.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Why IFN-γ (T-cell-derived) is preferentially predicted from baseline methylation.
