## Statement

F6 inflammatory myofibroblasts peak in diseases with scarring risk and are relatively absent in established fibrosis, supporting their role as an intermediate, risk-associated population rather than a terminal fibrotic state.

## Evidence summary

Compositional prevalence by category (Fig. 4b); consistent with trajectory and wound time-course data.

## Conditions and scope

Correlative compositional inference.

## Counter-evidence

Snapshot data cannot prove transition directly.
