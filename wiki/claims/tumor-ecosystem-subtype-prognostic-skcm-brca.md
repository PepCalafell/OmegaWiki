## Statement

The five-class tumor ecosystem subtype classification significantly stratifies overall survival in TCGA SKCM (P=2.54×10⁻⁵) and BRCA (P=0.0229), with profibrotic-dominated DHP/NIHS subtypes having the worst outcomes.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "Statistical significance was assessed via the log-rank test, with P values of 2.54 × 10−5 for SKCM and 0.0229 for BRCA" (Han 2025, Fig. 7i).

## Linked ideas

- Profibrotic-ecotype dominance as a poor-prognosis stratifier driving DHP/NIHS classification.
