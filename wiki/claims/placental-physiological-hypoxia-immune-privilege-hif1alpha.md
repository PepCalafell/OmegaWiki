## Statement

Physiological hypoxia activates HIF1α in the growing placenta, which drives HLA-G (shielding semi-allogeneic trophoblasts from NK cells) and PD-L1 (inactivating infiltrating T cells) — providing fetal immune privilege.

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Links to foundations [[foundations/hif1a]], [[foundations/pd-l1-cd274]].

## Conditions and scope

Mechanism couples a developmental physiological hypoxia to checkpoint/MHC regulation; parallels tumour immune evasion strategies.
