## Statement

MiTE-144 administered at 200 μg i.v. every 3 days induces no measurable systemic inflammation, hepatotoxicity, splenomegaly, or histopathological inflammation, contrasting sharply with the lethal toxicity of unmasked αTREM2-IL-2SK at lower doses.
