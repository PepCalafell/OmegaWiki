## Statement
In TPCPA WGCNA module 5 (colon cancer–enriched, cell-adhesion biology), GFPT1/GFAT1 and GFPT2/GFAT2 (glutamine–fructose-6-phosphate transaminases) emerge as hub proteins alongside CTNNB1/β-catenin, suggesting that the hexosamine biosynthetic pathway / O-GlcNAcylation axis is mechanistically coupled to β-catenin activity in colon cancer biology — a relationship previously documented in pancreatic cancer.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 module 5 hub protein list; cross-citation to GFAT1 / hexosamine / β-catenin pathway literature (ref 44–46).
- GFPT1 and GFPT2 are independently established as druggable in pancreatic and lung cancer models.

## Conditions and scope
- Hub assignment is correlation-based, not mechanistic; downstream wet-lab validation in colon models is not provided in this study.

## Counter-evidence
- The cross-citation context derives from pancreatic / lung cancers; direct demonstration in colon cancer is indirect here.
