## Statement

Although the six JAK-family inhibitors share a common core of IRF/STAT-bound downregulated CREs, each induces a distinct overall spectrum of epigenomic and transcriptional effects, with off-target activity accounting for the majority of the differences.

## Evidence summary

The 6 JAKi failed to cluster and had largely unrelated nearest neighbors; up-regulatory effects were JAKi-specific and TF-resolved. Links [[foundations/tyk2-kinase]], [[foundations/jak2-kinase]], [[foundations/stat1-tf]], [[concepts/polypharmacology-clinical-kinase-inhibitors]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; concentrations at kinobeads EC50 for the intended target.

## Counter-evidence

A common IRF/STAT-bound downregulated CRE set was shared by all JAKi (genuine on-target convergence).
