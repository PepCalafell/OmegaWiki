## Statement

Hypoxia directly upregulates Lgals3 (galectin-3) at both mRNA and protein levels across microglial and macrophage cells, alongside canonical hypoxia genes (Vegfa, Glut1).

## Evidence summary

Tested in three independent mouse myeloid cell types (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.3).

> "In all three cell types, hypoxic conditions (<0.1% O2) induced upregulation of Lgals3 at the mRNA and protein levels, along with known hypoxia-induced genes vascular endothelial growth factor A (Vegfa) and glucose transporter protein type 1 (Glut1)"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia, RAW264.7 macrophages, primary BMDM; in vitro hypoxia.

None identified at ingest.
