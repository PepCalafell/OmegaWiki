## Statement

Chronic (not cycling) hypoxia downregulates BRCA1, BRCA2 and RAD51 at mRNA and protein levels, producing transient HR deficiency that phenocopies BRCAness and renders cells PARPi- and cisplatin-sensitive.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Chronic hypoxia (pO2 ≤ 1% O2 for ≥24h). Cell-line and xenograft systems; in situ validation in human tumours.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
