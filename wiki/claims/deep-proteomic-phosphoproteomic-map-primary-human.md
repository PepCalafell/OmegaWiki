## Statement
Label-free MS-based (phospho)proteomic profiling of primary human M1, M2a, and M2c macrophages quantified 5342 proteins and 5905 phosphopeptides, mapping to 2313 phosphoproteins.

## Evidence summary
- "The label-free proteome and phosphoproteome quantification resulted in the identification of a total of 5342 proteins and 5905 phosphopeptides, which mapped to 2313 phosphoproteins" (p.9).
- Macrophages from four donors; all polarization states of the same donor compared in a matched design to limit inter-donor variability.

## Conditions and scope
In vitro monocyte-derived macrophages; depth typical of primary-cell phosphoproteomics, with substantial missingness requiring imputation.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
