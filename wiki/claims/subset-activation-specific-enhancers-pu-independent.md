## Statement
Whereas common regulatory elements are PU.1-enriched, a subset of activation-specific enhancers form independently of PU.1, proposed to be the human counterpart of murine latent enhancers.

## Evidence summary
PU.1 motif enrichment dropped at activation-specific sites; authors interpret these as PU.1-independent latent enhancers established by signal-dependent TFs.

## Conditions and scope
Interpretive inference; no direct PU.1 knockout/ChIP in this human system.

## Counter-evidence
PU.1 dominates common elements (see [[claims/pu-motif-enriched-common-active-promoters]]).

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Whether these human elements show latent-enhancer memory after signal withdrawal.
