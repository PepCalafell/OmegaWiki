## Statement

CD36 is the dominant cell-surface receptor driving AC-induced ERK1/2 activation and downstream COX2/TGFβ1 in macrophages; MerTK contributes modestly.

## Evidence summary

siCd36 robustly reduces p-ERK, COX2, and LAP-TGFβ1 MFI in PKH26+ macrophages by flow. MerTK-KO shows only modest reduction. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM; flow cytometry; single-receptor perturbations.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope; combinatorial CD36/MerTK KO not tested.

## Open questions
