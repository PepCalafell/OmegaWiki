## Statement

CD14 monocytes are the dominant IFN-γ responder among circulating immune cells, with 792 differentially expressed genes (600 unique to monocytes).

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.6). Shared IFN-γ program enriched in antiviral defense (GBP1, GBP2, GBP5, [[stat1-tf]], IRF1). See [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating monocytes; in-vitro IFN-γ.

## Counter-evidence

None.
