## Statement

Hypoxia upregulates expression of low-fidelity translesion-synthesis polymerases via HIF1, enabling continued DNA replication past lesions but with increased mutation rate — a core contributor to the hypoxic mutator phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

HIF1-dependent; applies to proliferating hypoxic cells.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
