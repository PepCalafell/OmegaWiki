## Statement

Despite being used at the EC50 for different (paralogous) kinases — Midostaurin for TBK1 and Momelotinib for IKBKE — the two inhibitors produced extremely similar epigenomic and transcriptional effects, demonstrating the approach's sensitivity to relationships that thermal-stability assays only partially detect.

## Evidence summary

The pair were nearest neighbors with near-identical H3K27ac/RNA-seq profiles, shared IRF3-bound downregulated CREs and IFN-gene suppression; CETSA confirmed differential TBK1 stabilization consistent with kinobeads. Links [[foundations/midostaurin]], [[foundations/momelotinib]], [[foundations/ikbke-ikk-epsilon-kinase]], [[foundations/tbk1-tank-binding-kinase-1]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS.

## Counter-evidence

The shared effect could partly reflect each compound's broader off-target portfolio rather than solely the TBK1/IKBKE axis.
