## Statement

AMPKα1 activity rises in macrophages shortly after tissue injury and is required for transition to the anti-inflammatory state and appropriate muscle regeneration. The finding mechanistically couples AMPK-driven OXPHOS to resolution.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Mounier 2013.
