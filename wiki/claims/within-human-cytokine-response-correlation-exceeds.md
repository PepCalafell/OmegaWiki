## Statement

Even versus a time-matched human IFN-β dataset (Kang et al., 6 h), within-human response correlations (mean r=0.61) exceed cross-species ones (0.34-0.45), indicating both genuine species divergence and experimental-design differences contribute to the human-mouse gap.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: quantitative.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
