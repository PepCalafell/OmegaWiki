## Statement

HIF-1α and HIF-2α exert opposing influences on the MYC transcriptional network: HIF-1α restrains MYC by upregulating MXI1 (a competitive MAX-binding repressor of MYC), shifting cells toward a glycolytic-quiescent state; HIF-2α stabilizes the MYC/MAX heterodimer and enhances SP1 cooperativity with MYC, amplifying proliferation. This divergence underlies tissue/tumor-specific functional differences between HIF-1α-dominant and HIF-2α-dominant cancers.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in Luo et al. 2022; supported by ChIP, co-IP studies of MYC/MAX/HIF-2α complexes (e.g., Gordan/Simon studies in ccRCC, neuroblastoma).

## Conditions and scope

- VHL-null ccRCC: HIF-2α/MYC amplification drives proliferation
- Neuroblastoma: HIF-2α stabilizes MYCN/MAX
- HIF-1α-dominant tumors: MXI1-mediated MYC repression slows proliferation but enhances glycolysis

## Counter-evidence

In some endothelial cell contexts HIF-2α may not robustly amplify MYC; pathway is cell-type-conditional.
