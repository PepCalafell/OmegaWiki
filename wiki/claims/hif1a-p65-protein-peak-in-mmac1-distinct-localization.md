## Statement

Both HIF1α and p65 protein levels are maximal in mMAC1 (hypoxic LPS-activated). HIF1α increases in both cytoplasm and nucleus under hypoxia. p65 accumulates cytoplasmically under hypoxia but only translocates to the nucleus upon LPS activation. The inverse subcellular effect of LPS on the two TFs (HIF1α more cytoplasmic, p65 more nuclear after LPS) suggests distinct regulation.

## Evidence summary

- Western blot time course of HIF1α and p65 with α-tubulin loading control (Calafell 2024 Fig. 3A).
- IF quantification of HIF1α and p65 in cytoplasm vs nucleus 2h after LPS (Fig. 3B-C).
- HIF1α maximum at 2h after LPS in hypoxia (Fig. 3A).

## Conditions and scope

- M-CSF MACs; 2h post-LPS time point primarily; n=2 IF replicates.

## Counter-evidence

- No genetic perturbation to validate localization signal specificity.

## Linked ideas

- Coregulation framework: HIF1α primed first, p65 recruited upon LPS — see claim hif1a-binds-earlier-than-p65-cobound-peaks.
