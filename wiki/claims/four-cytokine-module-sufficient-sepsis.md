## Statement

The four-cytokine module {TNF, IL-18, IFN-γ, IL-1β} delivered as three TNF-anchored pairs is sufficient to reproduce sepsis at the levels of (1) tissue DEG programs, (2) 195-cell-type abundance shifts, (3) plasma organ-injury markers, and (4) hypothermia.

## Conditions and scope

Sufficiency demonstrated only with recombinant cytokine injection in young mice; necessity per cytokine shown by loss-of-function in companion experiments.
