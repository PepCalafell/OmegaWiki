## Statement

Hepatic HIF-2α elevation enhances glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity by upregulating IRS-2 signaling, decreasing hepatic gluconeogenesis, and improving dyslipidemia. This effect is specific to HIF-2α (HIF-1α does not phenocopy) and represents a tissue-restricted therapeutic axis for type 2 diabetes — distinct from the deleterious effects of chronic hepatic HIF-2α in NAFLD/steatohepatitis.

## Evidence summary

Two independent primary studies (Taniguchi et al., Wei et al.) cited in Luo 2022. Mechanistically, HIF-2α-driven IRS-2 transcription restores insulin signaling.

## Conditions and scope

- Liver-targeted HIF-2α gain-of-function in T2D models
- Distinct from chronic HIF-2α activation in NAFLD which promotes steatohepatitis

## Counter-evidence

Constitutive HIF-2α activation in liver (Rankin et al.) impairs fatty acid β-oxidation and causes severe fatty liver — chronic stabilization is detrimental despite acute metabolic benefits.
