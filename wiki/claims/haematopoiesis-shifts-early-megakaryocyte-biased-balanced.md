## Statement

Over the months-long time course, the haematopoietic system transitions from a fast, megakaryocyte-biased output to a slower, balanced homeostatic output, with rates stabilizing after roughly Day 76.

## Evidence summary

Described by the authors as a previously unrecognised shift, visible only with time-dependent flux parameters. Model-inferred temporal trend corroborated by vWF+ HSC enrichment dynamics and MEP density-transport analysis.

## Conditions and scope

Single dataset; early-phase signal partly confounded by tamoxifen induction.

## Counter-evidence

Authors flag tamoxifen perturbation as a possible contributor to early dynamics.
