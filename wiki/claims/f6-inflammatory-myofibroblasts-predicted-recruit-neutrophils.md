## Statement

Receptor-ligand analysis predicts that F6 inflammatory myofibroblasts recruit and maintain neutrophils (CXCL5/6/8-CXCR2), monocytes/macrophages (CCL5/26-CCR1, CSF3-CSF3R) and B cells (CXCL13-CXCR5) in skin and other tissues.

Cell-cell communication analysis (Fig. 7c,d) with cross-tissue ligand expression.
