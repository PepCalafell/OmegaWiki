## Statement

Functional MRI of the hypothalamus distinguishes lung-cancer patients with vs without anorexia following oral nutritional supplementation, providing direct CNS evidence for the involvement of central appetite circuits in cancer-associated anorexia. Complementary work in CRC-CAC patients (Simoes et al., n=22) documented broader structural and functional CNS changes consistent with neuroinflammation.

## Evidence summary

Two small fMRI cohorts in lung cancer and colorectal cancer.

## Conditions and scope

Small samples; further replication needed.

## Counter-evidence

None directly; broader generalization untested.

## Linked ideas

- Hypothalamic fMRI as a candidate pharmacodynamic biomarker for central CAC therapeutics.

## Open questions

- Does fMRI signature respond to anti-GDF-15 or ghrelin agonism?
