## Statement

Across two neoadjuvant cohorts, HCC patients classified as pathological responders (complete or partial) exhibited lower post-resection recurrence rates than non-responders.

## Evidence summary

- Combined discovery + validation cohort follow-up.
- Recurrence definition: LIRADS-5 intrahepatic lesion or new extrahepatic metastatic foci.

## Conditions and scope

- Modest cohort sizes; survival comparisons not adjusted for tumor stage / underlying liver disease.

## Counter-evidence

- Confounding by selection bias (responders may have favourable baseline biology).
