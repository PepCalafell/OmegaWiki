## Statement

Combination anti-IL-1β + anti-PD-1 administered in precancerous windows produces the most pronounced anti-tumor effects across volumetric, cellular (KAC reduction, CD8 expansion), and cytokine (CCL3/IL33/IL17A/IL23/IL21/CXCL1 reduction) endpoints, while also offsetting PD-1-induced macrophage enrichment.

## Linked ideas

- Suggests a precancer-stage interception trial: high-risk lung-cancer-screening cohorts could be candidates for IL-1β±PD-1 combination prophylaxis, contrasting with the failed CANOPY combinations in established NSCLC.
