## Statement

Although HIF-1α is dispensable for TR-AM steady-state metabolism, it is essential for the glycolytic adaptation that protects TR-AMs from ETC-inhibitor-induced cytotoxicity following pharmacological PHD inhibition. Without HIF-1α, FG-4592 cannot rescue TR-AMs from mitochondrial-inhibitor death.

## Conditions and scope

Pharmacological HIF stabilization (FG-4592) as a hypoxia surrogate; ETC inhibition by rotenone or antimycin A. Mouse TR-AMs in vitro.
