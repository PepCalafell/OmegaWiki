## Statement

MiTE-144 induces a TAM transcriptional shift away from suppressive TREM2/MMP14 and IFN-response programs toward an inflammatory, monocyte-like phenotype with simultaneous upregulation of hypoxia-associated modules (Arg1, Nos2, Hmox1). The co-occurrence of "inflammatory" and "hypoxia" signatures during effective therapy is biologically notable and not the canonical "M1-like" pattern.

## Counter-evidence

The upregulation of hypoxia-associated TAM modules during a therapeutic response runs counter to the simple "hypoxic TAMs are bad" narrative seen in many other studies (cf. [[hypoxia-pd-l1-tam-immune-evasion]]). Open question whether these are functionally distinct hypoxia-mark TAMs.
