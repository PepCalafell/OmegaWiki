## Statement

RNR inhibition under hypoxia accumulates ROS and activates the ATM–MRE11 axis; MRE11 degrades stalled replication forks in an HR-dependent manner. When coupled with hypoxia-induced HR deficiency, this forces use of error-prone polymerases and elevates mutation rate.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Moderate-severe hypoxia with concurrent HR deficit; proliferating cells.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
