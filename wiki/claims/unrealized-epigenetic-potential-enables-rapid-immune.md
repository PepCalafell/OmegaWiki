## Statement
Many immune genes carry "epigenetic potential" — promoter chromatin accessibility in excess of their current transcription — which is rapidly realized as transcriptional upregulation upon immune stimulation. Genes lacking pre-established epigenetic potential are upregulated more slowly.

## Evidence summary
Gene-centric integration of RNA-seq and ATAC-seq quantified divergence between expression and promoter accessibility; LPS broadly established epigenetic potential while Listeria additionally triggered its realization ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figure 3; see [[concepts/epigenetic-potential-macrophage]]).

## Conditions and scope
Murine BMDMs in vitro. Concept is quantitatively defined per-gene relative to promoter accessibility.

## Counter-evidence
None recorded.

## Open questions
Which regulators set vs realize epigenetic potential, and does the concept transfer to other cell types?
