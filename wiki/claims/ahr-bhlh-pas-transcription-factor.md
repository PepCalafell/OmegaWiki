## Statement

The aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AHR) is a ligand-activated transcription factor of the bHLH–PAS family, comprising an N-terminal basic helix-loop-helix domain, two PAS domains (PAS-A: ARNT stabilisation; PAS-B: ligand-binding pocket), and a C-terminal transactivation domain.
