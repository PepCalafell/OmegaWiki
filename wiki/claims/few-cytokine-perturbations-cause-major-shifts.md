## Statement

Across 90 cytokine perturbations, abundant PBMC cell types show no significant compositional change; only rarer subsets shift (e.g. >2-fold decrease in NKT cells upon interferon, CD16 monocytes upon IL-4), confirmed by scCODA compositional modeling.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: correlational.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
