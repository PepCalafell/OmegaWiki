## Statement
The IFN-γ macrophage activation network is centered on STAT1 as a hub TF and the IL-4 network on STAT6, with additional TFs (IRF4, FOXQ1, FOXD2) newly associated with the IL-4 program.

## Evidence summary
- "This analysis revealed STAT1 as a central hub in the IFN-γ-condition and STAT6 as a hub in the IL-4-condition" (p.278, Figure 3D).
- "IRF4 and the forkhead box proteins FOXQ1 and FOXD2, which were not previously associated with the IL-4 activation network" (p.278).

## Conditions and scope
TF correlation networks from module genes; predictions, partly validated against known biology.
