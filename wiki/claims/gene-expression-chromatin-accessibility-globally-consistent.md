## Statement
Across the macrophage immune time course, gene expression and promoter chromatin accessibility move in globally consistent directions, but the two modalities frequently decouple at the individual-gene level: many genes change in only one modality, and divergent genes (epigenetic potential vs relative transcriptional abundance) are systematically identifiable.

## Evidence summary
Modality integration and divergent-gene quantification ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figures 1, 3B; uses [[foundations/atac-seq]]). This grounds the [[concepts/epigenetic-potential-macrophage]] / [[concepts/relative-transcriptional-abundance]] framework.

## Conditions and scope
Murine BMDMs in vitro; promoter-focused accessibility metric.
