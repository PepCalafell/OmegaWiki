## Statement

Across the 15 sampled body sites, both cellular diversity (Shannon) and density (cells per 100 µm²) increase centrifugally from central trunk sites toward peripheral / distal sites.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Fig. 2a-b.

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult skin; not all body coordinates sampled.

## Counter-evidence

Hair-density alone does not explain the gradient (sole has high diversity despite being hairless).
