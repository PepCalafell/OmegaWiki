## Statement

RIPK1 contributes to efferoptosis through its scaffolding function rather than kinase activity.

## Evidence summary

Kinase-dead die normally; RIPK1-null fetal-liver macrophages show delayed death. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "kinase inactive (Ripk1D138N/D138N) macrophages were as susceptible as WT ... RIPK1 [deficient] exhibited delayed kinetics of cell death"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.
