## Statement

Co-culture of patient-derived MMR-deficient CRC organoids with autologous peripheral blood T cells did not predict clinical response to combination ICB.

## Evidence summary

Co-culture of patient-derived mismatch repair-deficient CRC organoids with autologous peripheral blood T cells could not predict clinical response to combination ICB (Polak et al. 2024, p.531).

## Conditions and scope

Reconstituted PDO + PBMC co-culture in MMR-deficient CRC; small cohort; combination CTLA4+PD1 ICB.
