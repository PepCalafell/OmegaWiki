## Statement

Although IFIT1+ TAMs and the M-IFN1 module associate with immune-permissive features, IFIT1-high tumors lacking CXCL9 (quadrant II) instead featured immunosuppressive SPP1+ TAMs, LRRC15+ CAFs, and EMT/myogenesis-high cancer cells. Thus type I interferon signaling alone does not guarantee an immune-hot microenvironment, reflecting the known pro- and anti-tumor duality of IFN-I.

## Evidence summary

Four-quadrant stratification on TAM abundance. (p.5,9) Quote: "IFIT1high status alone does not equal to the immune "hot" phenotype".
