## Statement

Baseline serum sTREM2 levels measured by ELISA are significantly higher in HCC patients who pathologically respond to PD-1 blockade than in non-responders, in both the discovery cohort and an independent validation cohort.

## Evidence summary

- ELISA quantification of sTREM2 in pre-treatment serum.
- Replicated in two cohorts (discovery: cemiplimab; validation: cemiplimab+SBRT).
- Additional baseline-serum samples from NCT04123379 arms C/D/E (n=17).

## Conditions and scope

- Single-time-point baseline measurement.
- Cohort sizes are modest; prospective validation required.

## Counter-evidence

- sTREM2 sources may include non-tumor mac populations (Kupffer, microglia, bone marrow); systemic readout limits anatomical specificity.
