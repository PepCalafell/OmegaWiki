## Statement

A random forest model separating ICB-responsive TME groups (G01/G04/G06) from others yielded a 36-gene panel, all elevated in responder groups. Across 11 public bulk RNA-seq ICB cohorts the panel was consistently higher in responders and achieved superior AUCs versus published signatures and major cell-type proportions.

## Evidence summary

Random-forest feature selection validated on 11 external cohorts. (p.10) Quote: "our panel achieved superior AUCs, demonstrating robust predictive power".

## Conditions and scope

Retrospective bulk-cohort validation; prospective clinical validation pending.
