## Statement

Ex-vivo metabolic conditioning during CAR-T manufacturing — including MPC inhibition (UK-5099), MCT1 inhibition, LDH inhibition, transient glucose restriction, sodium-bicarbonate treatment, and metformin (mild complex I inhibitor / AMPK activator) — biases T cells toward a memory phenotype with enhanced mitochondrial OxPhos and improves antitumour persistence in preclinical models, including human-leukaemia xenografts and HER2-CAR-T in lung adenocarcinoma.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple preclinical studies referenced in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Pre-infusion ex vivo conditioning of CAR-T cells; murine xenograft and immunocompetent models.

## Counter-evidence

- GMP-compatible translation of these conditioning steps untested at scale.

## Linked ideas

- Combine pharmacological conditioning with genetic edits for synergy.

## Open questions

- Long-term safety and clinical persistence in patients.
