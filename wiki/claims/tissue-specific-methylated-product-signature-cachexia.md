## Statement

While the broad one-carbon-product elevation is shared across tissues, each tissue has a distinct dominant methylated product: MNAM in liver (NNMT-driven), sarcosine and methyllysines in adipose and skeletal muscle (GNMT / KMT2A/B-driven). This tissue-typed methyl-acceptor distribution reflects tissue-specific methyltransferase expression overlaid on the shared upstream methionine-cycle activation.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "we observed tissue specificity in terms of methylated products, with MNAM being the main methyl acceptor in liver, sarcosine and methyllysine in adipose tissue and muscles."

## Conditions and scope

Cross-tissue MS quantitation, n = 4/group; replicated in PDAC and humanised SW480 models.

## Counter-evidence

Tissue-specific patterns are not absolute — overlap exists (e.g., sarcosine also elevated in liver, MNAM also detectable elsewhere) — but dominant products are clearly tissue-distinguishing.
