## Statement

Successive engulfment of apoptotic cells by macrophages requires (i) UCP2-mediated lowering of mitochondrial membrane potential after each engulfment event and (ii) DRP1-mediated mitochondrial fission. Loss of either stalls continuous efferocytosis and disrupts inflammation resolution.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Park 2011 (UCP2) and Wang 2017 (DRP1).

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse thymic and tissue-injury models.
