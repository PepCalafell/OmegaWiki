## Statement

HIF1 transcriptionally upregulates glycolytic enzymes and represses oxidative phosphorylation, shifting cells toward aerobic glycolysis — a metabolic reprogramming that underlies HIF's control of immune effector function.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Semenza et al. 1994 on glycolytic gene control). Links to [[foundations/hif1a]] and concept [[concepts/warburg-effect-hif1a-glycolytic-reprogramming]].

## Conditions and scope

Increased glycolytic flux is associated with the activation state of multiple immune cell types; in tumours this is the Warburg effect.
