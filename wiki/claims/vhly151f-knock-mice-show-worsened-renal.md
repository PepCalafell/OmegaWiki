## Statement

A knock-in mouse carrying the phospho-dead Vhl Y151F (equivalent to human VHL Y185F) suffers worse renal ischemia-reperfusion injury; the injury is relieved by BCAT inhibition or dietary leucine and reversed by GDH inhibition (R162), confirming the leucine→GDH axis in vivo.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 7A–G, S9–S10). In vivo genetics + pharmacology with KIM-1/BUN/CREA endpoints.

## Conditions and scope

In vivo demonstration in mouse renal ischemia; mutant kidneys (or eCF506-co-treated WT) behave equivalently, linking SRC-pVHL to the phenotype.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
