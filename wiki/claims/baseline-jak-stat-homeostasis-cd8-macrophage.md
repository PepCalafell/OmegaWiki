## Statement

Under homeostatic, unperturbed in vivo conditions, JAK-STAT signaling components — particularly the ISGF3 trio (STAT1/STAT2/IRF9) plus TYK2, with cell-type-specific cooperation from STAT3/STAT5 — actively maintain low-level expression of interferon-stimulated genes and broader regulatory programmes in splenic CD8+ T cells and macrophages. This baseline activity is mechanistically distinct from acute cytokine-driven JAK-STAT signaling: it depends on the in vivo tissue context and is partially rescued by IFN-β.
