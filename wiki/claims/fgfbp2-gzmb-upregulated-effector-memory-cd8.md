## Statement

Decomposing the upregulated CD8 IFN signature to gene level revealed that FGFBP2 and GZMB increase specifically within effector-memory CD8 T-cell subtypes (EM CX3CR1 high, EM CX3CR1 int, Eff HOBIT, Activated), most strongly in ulcerative colitis — suggesting activation of circulating cytotoxic effectors potentially before tissue infiltration.

## Evidence summary

Gene-level dot plot of FGFBP2/GZMB across non-naive CD8 subpopulations (Fig. 2d; p.637). The authors note these markers were recently described in CD8 T cells at sites of epithelial damage.

## Conditions and scope

Specific effector-memory CD8 subsets; circulating compartment.

## Counter-evidence

Tissue-infiltration precedence is a hypothesis, not directly demonstrated.
