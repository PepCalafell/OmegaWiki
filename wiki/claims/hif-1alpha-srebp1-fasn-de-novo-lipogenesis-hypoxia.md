## Statement

HIF1α transcriptionally upregulates SREBP-1, which in turn induces FASN — driving de novo fatty acid synthesis essential for membrane biogenesis, energy storage, and survival under hypoxic stress. HIF-1/2α also induce SCD1 in clear cell renal cell carcinoma.

## Evidence summary

Mechanism summarized in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023). Connects hypoxia-induced glucose-to-lipid metabolic re-routing via SREBP1.

## Conditions and scope

Cancer cells under chronic hypoxia; ccRCC particularly. PHD3-loss reduces ACC2 hydroxylation, promoting FAO (alternate axis).

## Counter-evidence

In some tumors HIF1/2α actually *inhibit* CPT1A (FAO-rate-limiting), shifting toward lipid droplet storage rather than oxidation — opposite direction.
