## Statement

HIF-1α loss in BMDMs broadly suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokine production after LPS, whereas TR-AM cytokine output is HIF-1α-independent at baseline and only becomes HIF-1α-dependent after pharmacological glycolytic priming. Together, the ontogeny-divergent inflammatory phenotype mirrors the metabolic phenotype.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 5E; Suppl. Fig. S11A.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse in vitro; LPS as the inflammatory stimulus; not tested for non-LPS TLR agonists in BMDMs (Poly(I:C) failed to stabilise HIF-1α in TR-AMs).

## Counter-evidence

None within paper. Consistent with prior literature on HIF-1α requirement for IL-1β / NF-κB cross-talk in BMDMs.
