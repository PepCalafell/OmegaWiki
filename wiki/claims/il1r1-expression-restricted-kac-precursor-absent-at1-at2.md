## Statement

IL1R1 expression is restricted to KAC and precursor alveolar cells, marking them as the unique alveolar epithelial subset that can receive IL-1β signaling — distinguishing them from normal AT1/AT2 cells which lack IL1R1.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "IL1R1 was significantly and highly expressed in KACs and precursor cells, and most evidently in precursor lesions, and absent in normal AT1 and AT2 subsets" (p.329). Mouse Il1r1 expression in scRNA-seq peaks at 3 months post-NNK (precancerous window).

## Linked ideas

- IL1R1 IHC/IF as an alveolar-progenitor biomarker on lung-cancer screening biopsies.
