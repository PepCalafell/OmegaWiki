## Statement
TPP-activated macrophages carry a distinctive surface phenotype: 51 markers are selectively elevated versus M1/M2/Mb, with CD14, CD23, CD25, CXCR7, and CD197 confirmed by flow cytometry.

## Evidence summary
- "A total of 51 cell surface markers were elevated in MTPP but not M1, M2, or Mb" (p.279).
- "we confirmed significantly elevated expression for CD14, CD23, CD25, CXCR7, and CD197 on MTPP (p value < 0.05), while CD86 was elevated on both M1 and MTPP" (p.279, Figure 4A).

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro; mean fluorescence intensities from ≥3 experiments.
