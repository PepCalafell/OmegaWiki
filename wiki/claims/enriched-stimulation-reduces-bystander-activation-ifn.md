## Statement

Stimulating enriched, fractionated immune populations independently yields more consistent and physiologically interpretable IFN-α signatures than stimulating whole PBMCs, by minimizing bystander (paracrine) activation such as broad CCL8 induction.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.4). Methodological rationale for the HIRISA design ([[hirisa-human-interferon-response-immune-subsets]]).

## Conditions and scope

In-vitro PBMC stimulation; specific to paracrine-amplified genes.

## Counter-evidence

CD14 monocytes responded comparably in enriched and PBMC cultures; the effect is lineage-dependent.

## Open questions

How much bystander activation contributes to in-vivo disease IFN signatures.
