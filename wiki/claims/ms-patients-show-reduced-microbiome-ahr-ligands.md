## Statement

Patients with multiple sclerosis (MS) have reduced circulating levels of microbiome-derived AHR agonists (indole derivatives), consistent with a model in which the AHR-ligand supply from the gut microbiome contributes to immune tolerance and is depleted in MS.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Rothhammer et al. clinical correlations).
