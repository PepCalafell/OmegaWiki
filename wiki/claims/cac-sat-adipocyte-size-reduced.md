## Statement

In patients with gastrointestinal cancer-associated cachexia, abdominal subcutaneous-adipose-tissue adipocytes are smaller in size compared with weight-stable cancer patients, while the number of adipocytes is unchanged. This pattern supports lipolysis (not apoptosis or impaired adipogenesis) as the dominant mechanism of SAT wasting.

## Evidence summary

Histomorphometric studies of SAT in GI-CAC patients vs weight-stable controls.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in GI-CAC abdominal SAT; VAT may differ.

## Counter-evidence

In tumour-proximal adipose tissue, reduced lipogenesis has also been observed (Dahlman et al.) — suggesting locally distinct mechanisms.
