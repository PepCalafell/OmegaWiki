## Statement

Hypoxia-associated miRNA dysregulation reshapes a regulatory network whose target genes are enriched in core cancer signalling pathways (PI3K/Akt, Hippo, Ras, p53, EGFR, HIF-1). miRNA changes are accompanied by anticorrelated changes in their target mRNAs, linking the miRNA layer to metabolic reprogramming, invasion/metastasis, and stemness programs.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — 87 recurrent miRNAs, 3,793 anticorrelated targets, pathway enrichment (Fig. 4a).

## Conditions and scope

- Target predictions are computational; direct miRNA–mRNA binding not experimentally verified here.

## Counter-evidence

- Anticorrelation does not prove direct miRNA repression.
