## Statement

Across breast, lung, colorectal and ovarian human tumors, TAMs are the immune cell type most consistently in close spatial proximity to T cells within the TME, ahead of DCs, Tregs and NK cells.

## Evidence summary

MERFISH spatial atlas of ~1.86 M cells from public datasets, processed with ResolVI for transcript-assignment correction. Empirical cumulative-distribution-functions of minimum distances (Figures 1C, 1F, 1I, 1L) put TAM-T cell pairs at the lowest distance percentiles versus other pairs (DC-T, TAM-NK, TAM-Treg).

## Conditions and scope

Solid tumors of four cancer types; DCs reach comparable proximity in ovarian cancer. Generalisation to HCC, melanoma, RCC, GBM remains untested in this study.
