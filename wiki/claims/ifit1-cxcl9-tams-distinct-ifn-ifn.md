## Statement

Beyond the pro-tumoral SPP1+ TAM (c66), the atlas resolved two IFN-related TAM subsets: CXCL9+ TAMs (c68) with high IFN-gamma activity and IFIT1+ TAMs (c69) with type I interferon response programs. CytoSig analysis showed c69 preferentially regulated by IFN-I and c68 by IFN-gamma, and the two subsets carried antagonistic TFs (IRF1 vs IRF2).

## Evidence summary

Atlas plus CytoSig, SCENIC, and in vitro confirmation. (p.4) Quote: "IFIT1+ and CXCL9+ TAMs represented two distinct cellular states in tumors, likely induced by IFN-I and IFN-γ, respectively".

## Conditions and scope

Inferred induction from in vitro stimulation and signaling-activity inference; direct in vivo lineage tracing not performed.
