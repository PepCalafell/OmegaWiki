## Statement

HuMMI interactions are of biophysical quality on par with well-documented literature
interactions and occur within the human cellular environment.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.446–447, Fig.2c–e): yN2H validation rate
of 172 random HuMMI interactions indistinguishable from positive controls; co-IP in
HEK293 confirmed 13/18 evaluable candidate pairs; no negative control scored positive,
demonstrating assay reliability.

## Conditions and scope

10/32 co-IP pairs unevaluable (unspecific binding or poor expression).

## Counter-evidence

Prokaryotic proteins were harder to test in yN2H, requiring tailored reference sets.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[microbiome-host-meta-interactome-hummi]].

## Open questions

Directionality and functional consequence of each interaction.
