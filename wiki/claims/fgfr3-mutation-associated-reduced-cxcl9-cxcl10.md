## Statement

In bladder cancer, somatic FGFR3 mutation is associated with lower expression of the immune chemokines CXCL9 and CXCL10 and the checkpoint genes PDCD1 (PD-1) and CD274 (PD-L1), consistent with an immune-cold phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Correlational mutation-expression finding from [[papers/multiomics-analysis-cxcl9-macrophages-immunotherapy-response]], linking [[foundations/cxcl9-chemokine]], [[foundations/cxcl10-chemokine]], [[foundations/pd-1-receptor-pdcd1]], and [[foundations/pd-l1-cd274]].

## Conditions and scope

Cross-sectional association in retrospective cohorts; no causal/mechanistic test.

## Counter-evidence

Association does not establish that FGFR3 mutation causes immune exclusion.
