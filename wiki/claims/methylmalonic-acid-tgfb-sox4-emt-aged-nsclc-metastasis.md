## Statement

Elevated methylmalonic acid promotes EMT (↑fibronectin, ↑vimentin, ↓E-cadherin), activates TGFβ signalling and SOX4 expression, and enhances metastatic potential in mouse NSCLC models.

## Evidence summary

Mouse model with MMA supplementation and pathway profiling.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse NSCLC; correlation with patient-level metastasis still emerging.

## Counter-evidence

MMA pleiotropy in mitochondrial physiology complicates intervention.
