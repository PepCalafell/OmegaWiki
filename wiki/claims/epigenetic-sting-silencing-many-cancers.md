## Statement

Tumor cells most commonly silence cGAS/STING epigenetically (DNMT/EZH2/KDM5) rather than by mutation.

## Evidence summary

Documented across glioblastoma, colorectal, melanoma, ovarian, TNBC, KRAS-LKB1 NSCLC, and SCLC neuroendocrine subtypes. Mechanisms: DNMT-mediated CpG promoter methylation, EZH2-deposited H3K27me3, KDM5 H3K4me3 removal.

## Conditions and scope

Solid tumors with intact cGAS/STING genes; reversible with epigenetic inhibitors.
