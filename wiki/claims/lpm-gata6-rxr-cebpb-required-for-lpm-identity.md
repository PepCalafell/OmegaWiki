## Statement

The transcription factors GATA6, RXRα/β, and C/EBPβ are jointly required for LPM differentiation, survival, and lipid-handling identity. GATA6 loss raises OCR and lowers aspartoacylase; C/EBPβ loss de-represses LXRα/PPARγ; RXR loss leads to lipid accumulation. The triad anchors LPM lipid-niche metabolism.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] (Rosas 2014; Schneider 2014; Daniel 2018).

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse data; SPM phenotype is distinct and not covered.
