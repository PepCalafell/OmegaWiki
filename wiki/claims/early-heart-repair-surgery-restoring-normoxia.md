## Statement

Early complete surgical correction of CCHD (restoring normoxia) is associated with markedly lower PPGL incidence, suggesting normoxia restoration may be protective.

## Evidence summary

Surgical-status comparison across the CCHD cohort.

## Conditions and scope

Association only; confounded by disease severity and indication for surgery.

## Counter-evidence

Sicker patients may both avoid surgery and independently be at higher tumor risk (confounding).

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether early normoxia restoration is causally protective.
