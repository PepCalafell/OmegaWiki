## Statement

Endogenous itaconate localizes predominantly to non-tumor lung tissue and is markedly depleted within lung tumor regions, indicating suppressed itaconate metabolism inside tumors.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1461) — spatial metabolomics evidence.
  - Quote: "Itaconate was markedly reduced in tumor regions compared with adjacent non-tumor tissue, which indicated suppressed itaconate metabolism in lung tumors."
  - Itaconate was undetectable in KrasLA2/Irg1−/− lungs, confirming IRG1 dependence and signal specificity.

## Conditions and scope

MALDI-MSI (semi-quantitative ion intensity) in the KrasLA2 autochthonous model; spatial depletion was not seen in prior bulk analyses.
