## Statement

Daily HypoxyStat dosing decreases oxygen offloading from hemoglobin and suppresses Panc02 tumor growth to a degree comparable to inhaled systemic hypoxia, offering a clinically translatable hypoxia-mimetic.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.13): "HypoxyStat dosing was sufficient to decrease tumor growth relative to the vehicle control and to a comparable degree as inhaled hypoxia" — 400-600 mg/kg HypoxyStat daily (Fig.6e-f); small-molecule alternative to confined hypoxic housing. (type: pharmacological, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
