## Statement

Direct comparison of four AHR agonists (TCDD, PCB126, ITE, FICZ) in mouse FluV infection identifies TCDD as the only one that increases viral titres and body-weight loss. TCDD-driven mortality is associated with neutrophilia, iNOS-dependent IFNγ in lung endothelium and impaired DC migration. The result establishes that AHR-ligand identity — not AHR activation status alone — dictates infection outcome.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 70, 118–122. Most recent work shows AHR activation in mouse endothelium during FluV reduces vascular leakage and protects lung tissue (ref 126).

## Conditions and scope

Murine influenza infection; doses, routes and timing standardised within the cited comparison.

## Counter-evidence

Endothelial AHR shows protective effects in some FluV studies — illustrating tissue-cell-type-specific effects on top of ligand specificity.
