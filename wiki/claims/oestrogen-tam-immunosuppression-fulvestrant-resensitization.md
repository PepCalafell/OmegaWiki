## Statement

In a mouse melanoma model, oestrogen signalling polarizes TAMs to an immunosuppressive phenotype that drives CD8⁺ T-cell exhaustion and ICI resistance; combining ICI with the selective oestrogen receptor degrader fulvestrant reverses this phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Mouse interventional model with pharmacologic rescue.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse melanoma; testing in NSCLC pending.

## Counter-evidence

Translation to oestrogen-receptor-low tumours is unclear.
