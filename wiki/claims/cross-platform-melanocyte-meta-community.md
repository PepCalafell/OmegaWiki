## Statement

Cross-platform integration of spatial transcriptomics defines a reproducible melanocyte-enriched meta-community (containing fibroblasts, basal KC, T cells, DCs) whose member communities are significantly more abundant in melanoma than in keratinocyte cancers.

## Evidence summary

Meta-community matching across Visium/CosMx/Xenium with consistent composition and melanoma-enriched abundance. (p.9)

## Conditions and scope

Requires comparable annotations across platforms; small cohort.

## Counter-evidence

Panel differences across platforms; abundance comparison from few samples.

## Open questions
