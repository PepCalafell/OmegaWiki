## Statement

Genes near the positively IFN-γ-associated baseline CpGs are most strongly enriched for the kisspeptin receptor system pathway.

## Evidence summary

Enrichment via CPDB; kisspeptin pathway top-ranked, motivating the downstream functional kisspeptin experiment.

## Conditions and scope

Enrichment of annotated genes; subject to pathway-annotation bias.

## Counter-evidence

Enrichment alone does not prove functional relevance (addressed by the validation experiment).
