## Statement

In NSCLC, COL11A1⁺GREM1⁺ CAFs colocalize with SPP1⁺ TAMs adjacent to cancer cells; this stromal axis correlates with higher tumour burden and worse chemo–ICI outcomes, and is hypothesised to physically obstruct CTL infiltration.

## Evidence summary

Post-treatment spatial profiling cited by the review establishes correlation; physical-obstruction mechanism remains hypothesis-level.

## Conditions and scope

NSCLC post-chemo–ICI; platform- and annotation-dependent.

## Counter-evidence

The exclusion mechanism is hypothesised rather than directly tested.

## Open questions
