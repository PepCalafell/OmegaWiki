## Statement

Methylation at the BCG-associated CpG sites is strongly correlated with neutrophil (and neutrophil-subtype) abundance, most prominently at day 90.

## Evidence summary

Cell-proportion correlations and flow-cytometry subtype counts converge on neutrophils; aligns with prior reports of BCG-induced neutrophil reprogramming.

## Conditions and scope

Whole-blood deconvolution-based estimates plus flow validation.

## Counter-evidence

Implies some BCG methylation signal may be composition-driven rather than cell-intrinsic.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

How much of the BCG methylation signature is intrinsic vs neutrophil-fraction shift.
