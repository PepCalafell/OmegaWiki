## Statement

Combining systemic hypoxia with gemcitabine further slows Panc02 tumor growth beyond either monotherapy.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.11): "Gemcitabine and hypoxia treatment alone decreased tumor growth, and the combination of the two treatments further slowed tumor growth" — 100 mg/kg gemcitabine IP weekly then escalated (Fig.6c). (type: pharmacological, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
