## Statement

Iron metabolism genes in red pulp (RPMs) and Kupffer (KCs) macrophages are co-induced by SPI-C and NRF2, with BACH1 as a heme-sensing repressor: heme binding releases BACH1, allowing SPI-C/NRF2 to drive HMOX1, ferroportin, and ferritin expression — the transcriptional core of macrophage iron recycling.

## Evidence summary

Established by Kohyama 2009 (SPI-C), Haldar 2014 (heme→SPI-C in RPMs), Bonnardel 2019 / Sakai 2019 (Notch→SPI-C in KCs), and synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]]. Complementary to [[concepts/kupffer-cell-iron-recycling]].

## Conditions and scope

- SPI-C loss collapses RPMs/EIMs but spares KCs — implying SPI-C-independent inputs in liver.
- HMOX1 loss collapses spleen/liver/marrow macrophages broadly.
