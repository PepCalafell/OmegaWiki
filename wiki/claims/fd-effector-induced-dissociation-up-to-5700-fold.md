## Statement

Incorporating designed strained excited (ternary) states into coupled protein systems enables effector-induced increases in dissociation rate as high as 5,700-fold.

## Evidence summary

Abstract: "incorporating excited states enables the design of effector-induced increases in dissociation rates as high as 5,700-fold." The 5,700-fold maximum is reported for an optimized, degradation-safeguarded ASNeo2 variant (Extended Data Fig. 8); ASNeo2 itself shows 1,500-fold.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro and cell-surface kinetic measurements of designed switch–binder fusions; fold change is effector-saturated off-rate vs base off-rate.

## Counter-evidence

Fold acceleration varies widely across designs (12-fold to 5,700-fold) depending on strain tuning; not all designs achieve large acceleration.
