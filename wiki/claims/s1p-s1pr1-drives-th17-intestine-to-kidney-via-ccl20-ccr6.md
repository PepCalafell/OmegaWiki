## Statement

Th17 cells primed in the gut leave the intestine in an S1PR1-dependent fashion and home to kidney via CCL20/CCR6, providing an inter-organ S1P-chemokine axis that links gut immunity to renal autoimmunity.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from gut-kidney axis primary literature.

## Conditions and scope

- Murine nephritis models with Th17 involvement.

## Counter-evidence

- Other trafficking axes (S1PR2, integrins) likely co-contribute.
