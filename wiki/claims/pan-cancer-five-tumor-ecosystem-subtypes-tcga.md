## Statement

Hierarchical clustering of TabulaTIME-derived cell-type signature scores on 8,743 TCGA tumors yields five tumor ecosystem subtypes — DLP (desert low-purity), NIHS (naive immune + high stromal), DHP (desert high-purity), AILS (activated immune + low stromal), AIHS (activated immune + high stromal) — with distinct CD8+ T infiltration and tumor purity profiles.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "8,743 individuals from TCGA classified into four distinct TME subtypes based on clustering of the signature of all cell types" (Han 2025, Fig. 7d).

## Linked ideas

- Patient stratification for anti-stromal trials by ecosystem subtype.
