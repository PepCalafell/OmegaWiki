## Statement

Hypoxic cancer cells secrete CCL28, recruiting Tregs into the TME. HIF1α in Tregs drives their polarization and immunosuppressive function, contributing to CRC progression. HIF1α inhibition in Tregs suppresses tumor growth.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

CRC primarily; opposing effect (HIF1α-mediated Foxp3 degradation reducing Tregs) reported in other contexts — context-dependent.

## Counter-evidence

HIF1α-dependent transcriptional program also supports Th17 development via RORγt cooperation; the Treg-vs-Th17 balance is tissue-specific.
