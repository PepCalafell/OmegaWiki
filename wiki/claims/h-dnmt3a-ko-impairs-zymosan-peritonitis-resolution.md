## Statement

Haematopoietic DNMT3A deletion impairs the DNMT3A-COX2-TGFβ1 resolution axis in vivo in a sterile peritonitis model, leading to delayed PMN clearance and impaired resolution.

## Evidence summary

Flow cytometry on peritoneal exudate macrophages plus ELISA on lavage. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

Zymosan A1-induced peritonitis; 12-48 h time course.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.

## Open questions

- Whether the impaired resolution can be rescued by exogenous PGE2 (TGF-β1 rescue is demonstrated separately).
