## Statement

The melanocyte-enriched melanoma community is characterised by collagen-CD44 ligand-receptor signaling and, by multimodal (RNA+protein+metabolite) pathway analysis, by enriched tyrosine metabolism (melanin precursor) and pyrimidine metabolism (linked to UV-associated melanoma and treatment resistance).

## Evidence summary

Collagen-CD44 enrichment in CosMx_6; three lines of evidence for tyrosine metabolism and pyrimidine upregulation via MetaboAnalyst joint pathway analysis. (p.9)

## Conditions and scope

Melanoma community; metabolite annotation from MALDI-MSI glycomics is coarse.

## Counter-evidence

Glycomics cell typing is less defined; metabolite-pathway inference indirect.

## Open questions

Whether targeting tyrosine/pyrimidine metabolism alters melanoma progression.
