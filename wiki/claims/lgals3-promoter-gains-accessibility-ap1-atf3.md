## Statement

The hypoxia-induced accessible chromatin region at the Lgals3 promoter is enriched for AP1 and ATF3 binding motifs, both stress/hypoxia-active factors linked to tumor-supportive macrophage functions including lipid metabolism — providing an epigenomic basis for hypoxic Lgals3 induction.

## Evidence summary

IGV tracks with FIMO motif annotation (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.12).

> "At the Lgals3 promoter, the upregulated accessible chromatin region was enriched in binding motifs for AP1 and ATF3 motifs, both linked to tumor-supportive macrophage functions, including lipid metabolism"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; hypoxic glioma co-culture; two biological repeats.

## Counter-evidence

Motif enrichment is correlative; TF occupancy not directly tested.

Whether AP1/ATF3 are required for hypoxic Lgals3 induction in microglia.
