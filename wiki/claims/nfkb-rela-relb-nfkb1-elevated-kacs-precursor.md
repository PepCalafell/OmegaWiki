## Statement

KACs and precursor lesion cells display elevated transcription of NF-κB subunits (RELA, RELB, NFKB1) and elevated NF-κB and interferon signature scores compared with normal AT1/AT2 alveolar subsets or invasive LUAD cells, with KRAS-mutant precursors showing the strongest signal.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse data corroborate: KACs show highest Il1r1, Rela, Relb, Nfkb1 vs AT1, AT2, tumor. STAT3 (another LUAD pro-tumor TF) does *not* show this preferential precursor enrichment.

## Linked ideas

- NF-κB axis as the proximal effector of IL-1β-IL1R1 niche signaling, anchoring the mechanistic rationale for [[concepts/il1b-precancer-interception-luad]].
