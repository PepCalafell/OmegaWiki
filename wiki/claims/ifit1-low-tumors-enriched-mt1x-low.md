## Statement

In IFIT1-low tumors (quadrants III/IV), cancer cells showed elevated metal-response and peroxisome signaling, the TME was enriched for MT1X+ populations, and low-activation T cells (CD8+LEF1+, IL7R+) were overrepresented, consistent with an immune-inactivation state. M-IFN1-low tumors similarly enriched low-activation lymphocytes and MT1X+ populations.

## Evidence summary

Quadrant and module-based associative analysis. (p.5,6) Quote: "low-activation T cells, such as CD8+LEF1+ and IL7R+ T cells, were overrepresented in these tumors".
