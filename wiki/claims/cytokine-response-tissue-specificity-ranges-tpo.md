## Statement

A tissue-specificity index (TSI) on response magnitude shows TPO is the most cell-type-specific cytokine (TSI 0.93, HSPC-only) while IL-15 is the least specific (TSI 0.07, affects all cell types).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: quantitative.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
