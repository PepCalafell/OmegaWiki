## Statement

Genome-wide association studies now generate more than 30,000 SNP–trait papers per year, and a large fraction of the associations fall in non-coding, regulatory regions rather than protein-coding sequence.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "There are now more than 30,000 papers per year linking SNPs and traits. A large fraction of these associations are in the once-dismissed non-coding regions." (p.3) GWAS enabled by HapMap (completed 2010) and 1000 Genomes (completed 2015).

## Conditions and scope

Human common-variant association studies.

## Counter-evidence

Associations are statistical; causal interpretation in non-coding regions is hard.
