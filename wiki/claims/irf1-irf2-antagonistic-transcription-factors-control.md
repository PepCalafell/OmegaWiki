## Statement

SCENIC regulon analysis across macrophage subsets showed CXCL9+ (c68) and IFIT1+ (c69) TAMs shared many TFs but were distinguished by the antagonistic pair IRF1 (c68) and IRF2 (c69), suggesting distinct transcriptional programs underlie the two interferon-related states.

## Evidence summary

Computational TF-activity inference (SCENIC), not perturbational. (p.4) Quote: "two functionally antagonistic TFs, IRF1 and IRF230, were preferentially harbored by c68 and c69, respectively".

## Conditions and scope

Based on regulon-activity inference; no IRF1/IRF2 knockdown/overexpression validation in this work.
