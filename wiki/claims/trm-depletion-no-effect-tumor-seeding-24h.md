## Statement

The reduced tumour burden observed in CD169-DTR + DT TRM-depleted mice is not due to impaired tumour-cell seeding: at 24 hours post intravenous KP injection, similar numbers of KP cells are recovered from the lungs of TRM-sufficient (WT + DT) and TRM-deficient (CD169-DTR + DT) mice.

## Evidence summary

- KP cell recovery at 24 h post intravenous injection: equal between WT + DT and CD169-DTR + DT
- Validates that downstream tumour-burden effects reflect post-seeding niche function, not seeding efficiency

## Conditions and scope

- 24-hour time point only
- Mouse KP intravenous tail-vein orthotopic model

## Counter-evidence

- Subtle differences in seeding location, intra-pulmonary distribution, or initial proliferation kinetics may not be captured by this readout
- Other macrophage populations (e.g., MDM precursor monocytes) may compensate for TRM loss in shaping seeding
