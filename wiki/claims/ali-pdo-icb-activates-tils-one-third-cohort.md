## Statement

Approximately one-third of ALI PDOs from melanoma, RCC and NSCLC show patient-specific T cell activation and tumour killing upon anti-PD1/PDL1 ICB.

## Evidence summary

In approximately one-third of ALI PDOs, TILs showed patient-specific activation and tumour-killing potential upon anti-PD1 and anti-PDL1 ICB (Polak et al. 2024, p.531; Neal et al. 2018).

## Conditions and scope

ALI PDO cohort from human melanoma, renal cell cancer and NSCLC biopsies.
