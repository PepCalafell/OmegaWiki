## Statement

High-resolution Xenium imaging showed CD206lo IMs (Mmp12) and recMacs (Vcan) within the TME coexpress Ccl2, which was distributed throughout tumor regions overlapping C1qb+ macrophages.

## Evidence summary

Spatial overlays of Vcan/Mmp12/Ccl2 and C1qb/Cd163/Ccl2 (Fig. 4a-d). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 and KPAR1.3 lung models.
