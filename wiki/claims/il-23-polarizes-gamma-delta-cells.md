## Statement

γδ T cells in vivo exposed to IL-23 polarize into a distinct Tgd-f state overexpressing Il22, Gzmb, Serpinb1a, Ffar2, consistent with a Th17/Tc17-like effector phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Lymph-node γδ T cells, no TCR stimulation.
