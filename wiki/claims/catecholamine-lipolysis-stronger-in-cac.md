## Statement

In ex-vivo adipose tissue from CAC patients, hormone-sensitive lipase (HSL) is more responsive to stimulation by catecholamines (epinephrine, norepinephrine) and by natriuretic peptides than in adipose tissue from weight-stable patients. This sensitization provides a mechanistic explanation for the enhanced lipolytic drive in CAC.

## Evidence summary

Ex-vivo adipose-tissue functional assays in CAC vs control patients.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in human adipose tissue ex vivo; whether sympathetic outflow is also elevated in vivo is unclear.

## Counter-evidence

Mouse-model evidence emphasizes ATGL primacy independent of HSL phosphorylation (cytokine-mediated lipolysis).
