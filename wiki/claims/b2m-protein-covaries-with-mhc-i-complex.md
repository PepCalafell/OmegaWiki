## Statement

B2M is regulated at the protein level by stabilization in the MHC class I complex with HLA-A/B — explaining its mRNA-protein discordance via protein-protein interaction rather than translation rate.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative protein-protein correlation matrix; B2M protein tracks HLA-A and HLA-B protein profiles even when its mRNA does not. Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 6 F-G).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+ HSPCs.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
