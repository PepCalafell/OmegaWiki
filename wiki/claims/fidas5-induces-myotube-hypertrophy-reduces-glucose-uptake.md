## Statement

Pharmacological blockade of MAT (FIDAS-5) drives the C2C12 myotube phenotype opposite to L-methionine — hypertrophy with hypometabolism — confirming that the methionine cycle is causal (not just correlated) for the atrophy/hypermetabolism phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "FIDAS-5 significantly reduced one-carbon metabolite levels and led to a phenotype opposite to L-methionine-treated cells in a dose-dependent manner, characterised by myotube hypertrophy associated with reduced glucose consumption."

## Conditions and scope

C2C12 cells; pharmacological validation only — no genetic MAT KO comparison in the same experiment.

## Counter-evidence

Possible off-targets of FIDAS-5 at 5 μM; orthogonal validation with PF-9366 or genetic Mat2a KD would strengthen.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

- Whether genetic Mat1a or Mat2a KO in C2C12 phenocopies FIDAS-5.
- Whether the hypertrophy is sustained beyond 48 h or transient.
