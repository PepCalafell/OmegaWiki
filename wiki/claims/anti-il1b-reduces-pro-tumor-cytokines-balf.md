## Statement

IL-1β blockade has a measurable pharmacodynamic effect on the pulmonary cytokine milieu — reducing CCL3, IL33, IL17A and (in combination) IL23, IL21, CXCL1 — supporting that the niche-driven inflammatory program is reversible by upstream IL-1β neutralization.

## Linked ideas

- BALF cytokine profiling could serve as a non-invasive pharmacodynamic biomarker in human precancer interception trials.
