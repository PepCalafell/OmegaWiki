## Statement

Exogenous IFN-β stimulation of context-deprived ex vivo WT splenic CD8+ T cells and macrophages largely restores the JAK-STAT and IFN-driven transcriptional signatures lost upon deprivation, with one notable exception: STAT1β-dependent genes (those that change in Stat1b-only mice) are not rescued. This suggests STAT1β-dependent expression under homeostasis depends on cues distinct from type-I IFN — possibly tissue-context-specific cytokines or cell–cell contacts.
