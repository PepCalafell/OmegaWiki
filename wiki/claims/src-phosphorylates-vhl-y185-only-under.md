## Statement

SRC directly phosphorylates VHL at Y185 specifically under hypoxia; this phospho-mark is required for VHL to bind MCCC2 and execute the hypoxic metabolic rewiring, without affecting VHL's HIF functions or mitochondrial import.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 5A–L, S6). In vitro kinase assay, Phos-tag, Y185F mutant, SRC depletion, eCF506 inhibitor.

## Conditions and scope

Phospho-Y185 proposed to H-bond MCCC2 R292 (docking). SRC also targets PRMT5 (parallel arm).

## Counter-evidence

Among hypoxia-induced kinases (SRC/MET/INSR), only SRC phosphorylated VHL.
