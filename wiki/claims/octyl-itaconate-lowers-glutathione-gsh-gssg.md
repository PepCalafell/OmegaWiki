## Statement

Octyl itaconate reduces glutathione (GSH) levels and the GSH/GSSG ratio, indicating redox stress consistent with loss of PPP-derived NADPH used to regenerate reduced glutathione.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1466) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "Octyl Ita lowered glutathione (GSH) levels and reduced the GSH/GSSG ratio, which was consistent with impaired PPP-derived NADPH production."
  - HMOX1 strongly induced at transcript and protein levels, consistent with oxidative stress from PPP inhibition and glutathione depletion.

## Conditions and scope

A549; part of a G6PD–ROS–HMOX1 stress-response axis downstream of PPP inhibition.

## Counter-evidence

Later data indicate ribose limitation (not NADPH depletion) is the primary growth-suppression driver ([[claims/reduced-ribose-production-rather-than-nadph]]); redox stress is a parallel consequence. Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
