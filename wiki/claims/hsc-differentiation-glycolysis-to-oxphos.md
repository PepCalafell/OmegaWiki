## Statement

Proteomic evidence supports the long-standing model that HSC quiescence is glycolysis-dominated and that differentiation drives a transition to oxidative phosphorylation, marked by ATP synthase subunit upregulation.

## Evidence summary

GSEA on cluster-specific protein signatures plus pseudotime-correlated protein abundance trajectories. Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 4F, fig. S13-S17).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+; HSC and early differentiation states.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope; consistent with prior literature on HSC metabolic state (ref 51 in paper).

## Open questions

- Whether the switch is driver or consequence of differentiation, and whether modulating it perturbs hematopoietic potential.
