## Statement
The RIPK2 activation-segment autophosphorylation site S176 — essential for catalytic activity — is phosphorylated at higher levels in the proinflammatory M1 macrophage state than in both M2 states.

## Evidence summary
- "the S176 phosphoresidue within the RIPK2 kinase had a significantly higher phosphorylation level in the M1 state when compared to both M2 states. The phosphosite maps within the RIPK2 activation segment, and the S176 residue itself is annotated as an auto-phosphorylation site essential for the RIPK2 catalytic activity" (p.11).
- "RIPK2 plays an important role in the activation of proinflammatory pathways, including NOD signaling and NF-κB" (p.11).

## Conditions and scope
Direct activation-loop phosphosite evidence; identifies RIPK2 as an M1 proinflammatory kinase upstream of NF-κB.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
