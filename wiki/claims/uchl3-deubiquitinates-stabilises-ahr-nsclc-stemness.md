## Statement

UCHL3 interacts with AHR, removes ubiquitin chains, and thereby stabilises the receptor. UCHL3-driven AHR stabilisation underpins stem-like properties (self-renewal markers, tumour-initiating capacity) in non-small-cell lung cancer cells.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews Ouyang et al. 2020.

## Conditions and scope

NSCLC stem-like populations. Direction of effect in non-lung tumours and in non-stem cancer cells not established.

## Counter-evidence

— none reviewed.
