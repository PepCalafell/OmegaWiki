## Statement
The study generated a standardised resource data set of 299 human macrophage transcriptomes spanning 28 distinct activation stimuli (29 conditions including baseline) on a single microarray platform.

## Evidence summary
- "We stimulated human macrophages with diverse activation signals, acquiring a data set of 299 macrophage transcriptomes" (p.274).
- 9,498 unique genes present in at least one condition; data deposited under super series GSE47189 (p.286).

## Conditions and scope
Monocyte-derived macrophages (GM-CSF or M-CSF, 72 hr) further activated with 28 stimuli; microarray (Human HT-12V3 / WG-6V3 Illumina).

## Counter-evidence
None.

## Open questions
Extension of the resource to single-cell or tissue-resident contexts.
