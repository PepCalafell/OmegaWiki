## Statement

Two TREM2-expressing TAM clusters (3_ICIMac1: SPP1/RNASE1/NUPR1; 4_ICIMac2: APOE/APOC1) recapitulate a melanoma immunotherapy-resistance signature reported in prior literature, yet themselves associate with response (not resistance) in CPI1000+ pan-cancer bulk RNAseq. The discrepancy points to a counterintuitive context-dependence of TREM2-positive TAMs in ICI biology.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/using-pan-cancer-atlas-investigate-tumour]] (Coulton et al., *Nat Commun* 2024, Fig. 2c, 4a). Tension with mouse TREM2-inhibition data (Molgora et al. 2020 *Cell*, ref 71) and APOC1-inhibition HCC data (ref 74).

## Conditions and scope

Pan-cancer bulk RNAseq context; mouse TREM2-KO data uses syngeneic models that may not generalize to human pan-cancer.

## Counter-evidence

- Mouse TREM2 deletion enhances anti-PD-1 efficacy and reduces tumour growth (Molgora 2020) — opposite direction.
- APOC1 inhibition reverses M2 polarization and enhances anti-PD-1 in HCC (ref 74) — supports TREM2/APOC1+ TAMs as resistance-associated.

## Open questions
