## Statement

The healthy intestinal epithelium sits in "physiological hypoxia" (luminal pO₂ falling from ~30 mmHg in the duodenum to ~3 mmHg in the sigmoid colon), which stabilizes epithelial HIF1 and induces a barrier-protective gene programme supporting gut homeostasis.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Karhausen et al. 2004; Albenberg et al. 2014; He et al. 1999). Links to [[foundations/hif1a]], [[foundations/inflammatory-bowel-disease]].

## Conditions and scope

Quantitative pO₂ values from murine measurements; HIF1 here is protective, contrasting with HIF2's reported pro-inflammatory role in the gut.

## Counter-evidence

HIF2 has been associated with promotion of intestinal inflammation, an isoform-divergent caveat.
