## Statement

DNMT3A is the obligate de novo DNA methyltransferase required for AC-induced Ptgs2 and Tgfb1 expression in macrophages; the SAM substrate alone is insufficient without DNMT3A.

## Evidence summary

H-DNMT3A-KO BMDMs and siDNMT3A HMDMs fail to induce Ptgs2/COX2/Tgfb1 after AC engulfment; AC uptake is unaffected; exogenous SAM fails to rescue in DNMT3A-null cells. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM, HMDM in vitro; multiple AC sources.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
