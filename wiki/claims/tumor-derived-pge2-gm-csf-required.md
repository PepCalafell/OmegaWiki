## Statement

Conditioned media from Cox2-knockout (PGE2-deficient) or Csf2-knockout (GM-CSF-deficient) 4T1 tumor cells fail to induce ARG1 in macrophages, showing tumor-secreted PGE2 and GM-CSF are required for the angiogenic TEM phenotype.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2064) — mechanistic evidence. Cox2-KO and Csf2-KO 4T1 lines; ELISA-confirmed loss of factor secretion; TCM-transfer to BMDMs.
  - Quote: "conditioned media from these KO cells failed to induce ARG1 expression in TEMs, demonstrating that tumor-secreted PGE2 and GM-CSF are essential for establishing the angiogenic TEM phenotype"

## Conditions and scope

4T1 model; single-gene tumor-intrinsic knockouts.
