## Statement

The HGP's enduring significance lies in the systems/network era of genomics it enabled — mapping interactions between cellular components — rather than in the protein parts-list catalogue itself. A survey of components is necessary but not sufficient to understand a complex system.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "we think that the HGP is more notable for the new era of genomics it ushered in, than for the protein catalogue itself … Complexity arises from the diversity of the interactions between components." (p.4)

## Conditions and scope

Interpretive thesis (a Comment), not an empirical measurement.

## Counter-evidence

Counterfactual is untestable — "there is no world without an HGP for comparison"; other factors (computing, sequencing) also drove these developments.
