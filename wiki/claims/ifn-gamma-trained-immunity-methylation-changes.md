## Statement

Methylation changes tied to IFN-γ trained immunity enrich for mTOR and VEGFA-VEGFR2 signaling, pathways with known roles in T-cell activation and trained immunity.

## Evidence summary

Pathway enrichment connects the methylation–cytokine associations to canonical trained-immunity/T-cell-activation signaling (mTOR), plus immune-related IL-2/IL-18 pathways.

## Conditions and scope

Annotation/enrichment-based; suggestive CpGs.

## Counter-evidence

Indirect; no functional perturbation of these pathways here.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether mTOR-pathway methylation is mechanistically required for IFN-γ training.
