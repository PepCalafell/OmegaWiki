## Statement

The neomorphic STAT5BN642H mutant TF drives a transcriptional program enriched for AURORA kinase signaling (consistent with Pham 2018), cell-cycle progression, and target genes of E2F4 and FOXM1. This target-set switch — distinct from WT STAT5 — provides a mechanistic basis for STAT5BN642H's role in driving T-cell proliferation and lymphoma/leukemia development.
