## Statement

Evofosfamide (TH-302), a prototype hypoxia-activated prodrug, demonstrates robust hypoxia-selective cytotoxicity preclinically (patient-derived xenografts in vitro and in vivo) but failed two phase III trials — NCT01746979 in advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma (MAESTRO) and NCT01440088 in soft-tissue sarcoma — neither of which used hypoxia biomarker stratification for patient selection.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023). Table 2 lists clinical trials.

## Conditions and scope

Phase III data is OS-endpoint negative; preclinical efficacy intact.

## Counter-evidence

Preclinical efficacy in xenografts suggests the mechanism is sound — the failure is widely interpreted as biomarker-stratification failure rather than mechanism failure.
