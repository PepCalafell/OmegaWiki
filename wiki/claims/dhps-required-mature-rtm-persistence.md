## Statement

Deleting DHPS in already-mature RTMs (via inducible CX3CR1-ERT2cre) leads to their progressive loss with increased caspase-3, demonstrating an ongoing maintenance requirement independent of the differentiation step.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] using [[foundations/cx3cr1-creer-fate-mapping]] (Fig. 2j-o).

## Conditions and scope

Kidney, heart RTMs and microglia; tamoxifen-inducible adult deletion.
