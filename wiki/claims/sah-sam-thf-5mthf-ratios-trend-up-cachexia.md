## Statement

The SAH/SAM ratio trends upward in cachectic plasma, adipose tissue and tumour, and THF/5-methylTHF trends upward in liver — supporting active methionine-cycle and folate-cycle flux but not at the same statistical confidence as the abundance-level metabolite claims.

## Evidence summary

Ext Fig. 4 p-q. Quoted: "trends towards elevated SAH/SAM ratios in Cax plasma, adipose tissue and tumour and THF/5-methylTHF in liver (the only tissue in which such metabolites were detectable)."

## Conditions and scope

Limited by MS detection of low-abundance metabolites (THF, 5-methylTHF). Most ratios reported as trends rather than p < 0.05.

## Counter-evidence

Trends rather than significant changes in most tissues — confidence lower than for the abundance-level product elevations.
