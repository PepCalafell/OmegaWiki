## Statement

COX4I2 is a reproducible EPAS1-HIF2α transcriptional target upregulated in EPAS1-mutant and hypoxic PPGL contexts.

## Evidence summary

Differential expression converging on 14 mitochondrial genes, with COX4I2 highlighted across all three cohorts.

## Conditions and scope

Bulk RNA-seq; consistent direction across human tumors and cell line.

## Counter-evidence

None reported; HMGCL co-upregulated but less mechanistically explored.

## Open questions
