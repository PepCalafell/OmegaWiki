## Statement

Bilirubin and biliverdin — products of haem catabolism — directly modulate AHR activity and act as competitive AHR ligands. This provides a Trp-independent endogenous ligand source that links haem-pigment biology to AHR signalling in infection (e.g., malaria).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] (Table 1, refs 43–44). Acute Plasmodium infection raises plasma bilirubin/biliverdin levels (p.188), correlating with increased AHR activation.

## Conditions and scope

Competitive displacement of radiolabelled AHR ligand by bilirubin/biliverdin; direct binding not yet measured.

## Counter-evidence

None within the review's scope.
