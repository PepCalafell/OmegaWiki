## Statement

Myeloid-specific Adar knockout expands ISG+ TAMs, significantly slows tumor growth in LLC and B16F10 models, and enhances the efficacy of anti-PD-1 treatment.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2070-2071) — pharmacological evidence. LSL-Cas9;Lyz2-Cre HSC transplant sgAdar mKO; tumor-growth curves; scRNA-seq shows enrichment of two ISG+ TAM subsets.
  - Quote: "Adar mKO mice exhibited significantly slower tumor growth compared with controls ... Adar mKO also slowed tumor growth in the B16F10 model and enhanced the efficacy of anti-PD-1 treatment"

## Conditions and scope

LLC and B16F10 models; myeloid-restricted knockout via HSC transfer.
