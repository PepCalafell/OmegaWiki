## Statement

IREA applied to anti-PD-1-treated vs untreated mouse tumours identified TGFβ1 as showing the most negative cytokine response score, consistent with TGFβ1's documented role in attenuating immune-enhancing effects of PD-1/PD-L1 blockade.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Anti-PD-1 mouse tumour scRNA-seq.
