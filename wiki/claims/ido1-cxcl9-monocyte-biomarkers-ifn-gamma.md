## Statement

IDO1 and CXCL9 are robust, monocyte-specific transcriptomic biomarkers of IFN-γ (IFN-II) activity; at the protein level CXCL9 and CD64 are IFN-γ-specific, whereas CXCL10, CD38, and CD274 are induced by both IFN-I and IFN-γ.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.6-7), with flow-cytometry validation. See [[cxcl9-chemokine]], [[cxcl10-chemokine]], [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating monocytes; biomarkers are subset-specific (monocyte context).

## Counter-evidence

CXCL10/CD38/CD274 are shared (not IFN-γ-specific), so cannot discriminate IFN type alone.
