## Statement

Moderate to severe hypoxia impairs RNR (RRM1/RRM2) activity due to the O2-dependent diferric-tyrosyl radical in RRM2, reducing dNTP availability and slowing/stalling replication forks; ATR-CHK1 are activated as a consequence.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Moderate–severe hypoxia, S-phase cells.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
