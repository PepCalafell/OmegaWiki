## Statement

Culturing bone-marrow-derived macrophages in tumor-conditioned media (tumor-educated macrophages) upregulates hypoxia/angiogenesis genes (Vegfa, Slc2a1, Arg1) and downregulates MHC-II (Cd74, Ciita) and interferon-response genes.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2063-2064) — mechanistic evidence. Bulk RNA-seq of BMDMs cultured with 4T1 or Panc02 TCM vs untreated; angiogenic TEMs were immunosuppressive to CD8 T cells ex vivo.
  - Quote: "Bulk RNA-seq of these TEMs revealed significant upregulation of genes associated with hypoxia and angiogenesis ... genes involved in MHC-II ... and interferon responses ... were substantially downregulated"

## Conditions and scope

Ex vivo tumor-educated-macrophage model; two tumor cell lines.
