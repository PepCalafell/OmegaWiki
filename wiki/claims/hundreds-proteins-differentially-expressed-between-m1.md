## Statement
At the protein level, 675 and 806 proteins are significantly differentially expressed between M1 and M2a, and between M1 and M2c macrophages, respectively, recovering canonical polarization markers.

## Evidence summary
- "we found that 675 and 806 proteins had significantly different expression levels between M1 and M2a, and between M1 and M2c macrophages, respectively" (p.9).
- M1-high: CD86, GBP1, IDO1, ISG15, MX1; M2a-high: CD209, CD206/MRC1; M2c-high: CD209, CD163 (p.10).

## Conditions and scope
In vitro polarized macrophages; flow cytometry validated CD86 (M1), CD206 (M2a), CD163 (M2c) marker enrichment.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
