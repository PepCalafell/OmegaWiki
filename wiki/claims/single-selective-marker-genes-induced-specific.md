## Statement
Some macrophage stimuli induce a single selective marker gene (e.g. IFN-β→ZNF77, IFN-γ→FEM1C), but distinguishing closely related conditions generally requires combinations of markers.

## Evidence summary
- "IFN-β selectively induced ZNF77, while IFN-γ selectively induced FEM1C" (p.276, Figure 2B).
- "SERINC2 was induced by PGE2 but also by PGE2+P3C, suggesting that gene combinations are necessary to distinguish complex input signals" (p.276).

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro stimulation; future studies need panels of markers.
