## Statement

Baseline DNA methylation is the strongest single omics predictor of IFN-γ trained-immunity magnitude, exceeding genetics (PRS), proteins, and metabolites.

## Evidence summary

Variance-partition with feature selection and collinearity pruning; methylation > metabolites > others; combined model ~56.1% variance; significance confirmed by randomization.

## Conditions and scope

Cross-sectional variance partition; selected-feature model; young-adult cohort.

## Counter-evidence

No proteins reached significance for selection; variance estimates sensitive to feature choice.

## Open questions

Whether methylation's predictive edge holds out-of-sample and across ages.
