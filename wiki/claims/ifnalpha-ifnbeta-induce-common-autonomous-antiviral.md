## Statement

IFNα1 and IFNβ induce common antiviral gene programmes (GP27, GP33, GP34) across almost all cell types, contrasting with the cell-type-specific IL-1 family responses; this represents an autonomous viral defence programme.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lymph node in vivo, 4-h post-injection.
