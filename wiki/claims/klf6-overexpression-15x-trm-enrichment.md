## Statement

Retroviral KLF6 overexpression in P14 CD8+ T cells produces approximately 15-fold enrichment in the small intestine and ~42-fold more CD69+CD103+ TRM-like cells over empty-vector controls in acute LCMV–Armstrong infection, with no increase in terminal exhaustion under chronic LCMV-Cl13 challenge.
