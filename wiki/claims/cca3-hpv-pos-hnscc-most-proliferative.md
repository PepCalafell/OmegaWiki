## Statement

HPV+ head and neck squamous cell carcinoma exhibits the highest fraction of cycling malignant cells (>45%) of any cancer type in 3CA v2, consistent with HPV E7 inactivation of pRb and HPV E6 inactivation of p53.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 5d, with mechanistic interpretation in the Discussion.

## Conditions and scope

Cohort-dependent; effect may shift with additional HPV+ samples.
