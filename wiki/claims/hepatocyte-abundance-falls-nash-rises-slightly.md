## Statement

Hepatocyte abundance is significantly reduced in NASH (1.12-fold, P=1.87×10⁻³) and slightly increased in HFD (1.03-fold, P=1.48×10⁻²), which authors link to hepatocyte apoptosis in NASH versus oxidative-stress-driven proliferation under HFD.

## Evidence summary

DECODE liver cohort analysis (Fig. 6f,g); HFD-TLC and WDA hepatocyte decreases were not statistically significant. Authors interpret via NASH hepatocyte apoptosis and HFD oxidative-stress proliferation.

## Conditions and scope

Deconvolution-inferred; NASH and WDA groups have very small n (6 and 4).

## Counter-evidence

The small sample sizes and observational design limit strength; mechanism is interpretive.

## Open questions
