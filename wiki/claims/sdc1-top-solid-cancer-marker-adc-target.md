## Statement
SDC1 emerges as a top solid-cancer-enriched protein in TPCPA. As a cell-surface and plasma-detectable target of the antibody-drug conjugate indatuximab ravtansine, SDC1 is an immediately translatable biomarker / therapeutic candidate beyond multiple myeloma, where it is most extensively profiled.
