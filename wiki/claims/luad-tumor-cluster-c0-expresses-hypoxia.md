## Statement

The tumour-enriched CosMx cluster C0 in patient LUAD-9 (Nanostring 960-gene panel) marks tumour cells expressing a hypoxia-responsive, EMT-permissive, neutrophil-chemoattractant transcriptional state.

## Evidence summary

Differential expression and gene-set-enrichment evidence (Fig. 5c) plus signature-score maps in Fig. 5d / Extended Data Fig. 6.

## Conditions and scope

Validated on LUAD-9 R1; reproduced on MERFISH and IMC LUAD datasets via cluster-level analysis (see related claims).

## Counter-evidence

The same signatures could be a generic stress / hypoxia response — not lung-cancer-specific.

## Open questions

- Are these tumour cells genetically distinct (clonal selection) or transcriptionally plastic (microenvironmental induction)?
