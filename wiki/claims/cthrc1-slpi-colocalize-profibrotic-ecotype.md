## Statement

eFibro_CTHRC1 ECM-remodeling CAFs and Macro_SLPI profibrotic TAMs colocalize spatially in tumor regions across multiple cancer types — defining a unified profibrotic spatial ecotype at the malignant–normal interface.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "eFibro_CTHRC1 and Macro_SLPI cells showed a high correlation at the spot level (R > 0.5)... mIHC staining of CTHRC1, SLPI, CD68 and SPP1 in oral cancer and NSCLC samples further verified the colocalization" (Han 2025, p.11).

## Linked ideas

- Profibrotic ecotype as a unified anti-stromal therapeutic target.
- Functional dependence between CAFs and TAMs in this niche requires perturbation studies.
