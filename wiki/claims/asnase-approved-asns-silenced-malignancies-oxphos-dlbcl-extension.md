## Statement

L-asparaginase exploits asparagine auxotrophy in tumours with epigenetic silencing of asparagine synthetase (ASNS) — clinically approved for acute lymphoblastic leukaemia (T-ALL and B-ALL) and NK/T-cell lymphoma. Recent evidence extends benefit to OxPhos-dependent DLBCL with variable ASNS expression.

## Evidence summary

- Established ASNase approvals (Elspar, Oncaspar, Erwinaze).
- Chiche/Ricci 2024 DLBCL data extending ASNase utility based on metabolic phenotype.

## Conditions and scope

- ASNS-silenced or OxPhos-dependent tumours; importance of metabolic stratification.

## Counter-evidence

- Hypersensitivity, pancreatitis, coagulopathy limit broader use.

## Linked ideas

- Extension to other OxPhos-leaning solid tumours pending validation.

## Open questions

- Companion-diagnostic strategy to identify ASNase-responsive non-haematological cancers.
