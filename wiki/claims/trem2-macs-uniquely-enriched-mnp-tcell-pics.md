## Statement

By PIC-seq (physically-interacting cell sequencing), TREM2 macs — among all mo-mac states identified in HCC — are uniquely enriched in MNP/T-cell heterotypic doublets versus singlets, indicating preferential direct physical contact with tumor-infiltrating T cells.

## Evidence summary

- PIC-seq applied to HCC tumor and adjacent normal tissue.
- Greater MNP/T-cell PICs in tumor vs normal.
- TREM2 macs preferentially deconvolved among PIC-derived MNPs vs singlets.

## Conditions and scope

- T-cell deconvolution within PICs is limited; only MNPs are confidently annotated.
- Snapshot of stable interactions; transient contacts may be missed.

## Counter-evidence

- None reported in this paper.
