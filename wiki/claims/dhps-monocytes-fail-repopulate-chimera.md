## Statement

When DHPS-deficient and WT bone-marrow precursors compete in irradiated hosts, only WT precursors efficiently reconstitute RTM pools — a cell-intrinsic defect in the monocyte-to-RTM transition.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 2d, Extended Data Fig. 5d,e).

## Conditions and scope

Competitive setting controls for host environment; isolates intrinsic requirement.
