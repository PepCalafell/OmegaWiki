## Statement

The exhausted cytotoxic CD8+ T cell class (CTLex; PDCD1+ LAG3+) was proportionally increased by 80.4% (4.5%→8.2% of CD45+ cells) in rash-affected versus healthy control skin, with exhaustion evident in virtually every rash sample.
