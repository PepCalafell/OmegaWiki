## Statement

The study delivers HuMMI, the first systematic commensal-effector–human protein
interactome: 1,255 verified interactions, 286 effectors, 426 human proteins.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.444–446, Fig.2a): HuMMIMAIN 1,067
interactions; HuMMIRPT 39 (sampling sensitivity ~32%); HuMMIHOM 394 (181 non-redundant);
total 1,255 unique interactions between 286 effectors and 426 human proteins.

## Conditions and scope

Y2H assay sensitivity ~13–17.5%; ~32% sampling sensitivity for main screen.

## Counter-evidence

Incomplete sampling means the true network is larger.

## Linked ideas

Defines [[microbiome-host-meta-interactome-hummi]]. Uses [[yeast-two-hybrid-y2h]].

## Open questions

How many interactions remain unsampled at higher depth.
