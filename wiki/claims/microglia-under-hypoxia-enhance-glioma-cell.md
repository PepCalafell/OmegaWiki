## Statement

Hypoxia-exposed microglia enhance glioma cell migration and invasion: the most efficient GL261 transmigration occurs when glioma cells experience both hypoxia and microglia, and hypoxic co-culture induces a pro-migration transcriptional program in glioma cells.

## Evidence summary

Matrigel invasion assay plus glioma RNA-seq (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6).

> "The most efficient transmigration occurred when glioma cells were exposed to both hypoxia and microglia ... microglia under hypoxia enhance glioma migration and invasion"

## Conditions and scope

GL261 glioma ± BV2 microglia; in vitro invasion; one-tailed Wilcoxon, n=5.

## Counter-evidence

In vitro invasion assay; mediators of the pro-invasive crosstalk not identified.
