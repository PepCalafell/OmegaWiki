## Statement

After nuclear translocation, the AHR–ARNT heterodimer binds the consensus xenobiotic/dioxin response element (XRE/DRE) 5'-TNGCGTG-3', with AHR contacting 5'-TNGC and ARNT contacting GTG-3', recruiting coactivators (e.g., p300) to drive target gene transcription.

## Evidence summary

Established for decades via ChIP, footprinting, and reporter assays; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
