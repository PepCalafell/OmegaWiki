## Statement

Recruited activated neutrophils consume large amounts of local oxygen (NADPH oxidase burst), generating "inflammatory hypoxia" that stabilizes epithelial HIF and induces barrier-protective target genes (e.g. GLUT1) — paradoxically promoting resolution in the gut, such that neutrophil depletion worsens colitis.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Campbell et al. *Immunity* 2014). Concept: [[concepts/inflammatory-hypoxia-neutrophil-oxygen-depletion]].

## Conditions and scope

Pro-resolution in intestinal mucosa; potentially injurious in lung (bystander epithelial damage). Patients lacking functional NADPH oxidase (chronic granulomatous disease) present with IBD-like syndrome.

## Counter-evidence

Tissue-context-dependent: the same oxygen-depleting activity is damaging in pulmonary settings.
