## Statement

Tumour-cell IL6 KO eliminates the cachexia-associated elevation of one-carbon-metabolism products at the metabolite level (MNAM and thymidine in liver; di- and tri-methyllysine in GC muscle), demonstrating that IL6 is necessary not only for the transcriptional but also for the metabolite-level phenotype of the cachexia one-carbon axis.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Metabolomics confirmed the significant increase of multiple products of the one-carbon metabolism in C26 cachectic liver (for example, #10 MNAM and #18 thymidine) and GC muscle (for example, #11 di- and #12 tri-methyllysine) and the near-complete absence of their enrichment upon IL6 KO."

## Conditions and scope

Independent cohort from the antibody experiment; n = 3/group (lower-powered).

## Counter-evidence

Some residual elevation may persist below detection threshold; "near-complete absence" rather than "complete absence."
