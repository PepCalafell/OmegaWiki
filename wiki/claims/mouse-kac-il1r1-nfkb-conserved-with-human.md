## Statement

The IL1R1-NF-κB transcriptional program that uniquely marks human KACs is conserved in mouse Gprc5a−/− NNK-induced KACs, establishing the model as mechanistically faithful for testing IL-1β interception strategies.

## Linked ideas

- Cross-species conservation justifies translational extrapolation from anti-IL-1β experiments in Gprc5a−/− mice to human precancer interception trials.
