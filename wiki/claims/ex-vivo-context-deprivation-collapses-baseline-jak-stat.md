## Statement

Short-term (20h) ex vivo culture of WT mouse spleen CD8+ T cells and F4/80+ macrophages — removing them from the tissue context — collapses baseline JAK-STAT and IFN transcriptional signatures in both cell types. The deprivation transcriptome strongly mirrors the patterns seen in Stat1/Stat2/Irf9/Tyk2 knockouts under homeostasis, demonstrating that baseline JAK-STAT activity is driven by cell-extrinsic tissue-context signals rather than by cell-intrinsic mechanisms.
