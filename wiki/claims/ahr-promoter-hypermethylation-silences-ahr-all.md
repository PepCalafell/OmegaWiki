## Statement

In a substantial fraction of acute lymphoblastic leukemia patients (~33%) and across multiple cancer cell lines (osteosarcoma U2OS/143B, DLBCL U-2932 R2/OCI-LY19, CML K562, ALL Jurkat/REH), the AHR promoter is hypermethylated, blocking SP1 binding and silencing AHR transcription. HDAC inhibitors (TSA, butyrate) restore AHR expression in several of these cell lines, while MCF-7 breast cancer cells are refractory — underscoring cell-type-specific epigenetic architecture.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews refs 193-201.

## Conditions and scope

Lymphoid/hematologic and selected solid-tumour cell lines. Other AHR-driven cancers may instead show hypomethylation (e.g. HK2-driven; cf. [[claims/hk2-ahr-target-promotes-ahr-promoter-demethylation]]).

## Counter-evidence

In several cancers AHR is over-expressed and active — implying AHR methylation status is bidirectional across tumour types.
