## Statement

LXRα and LXRβ are required for development and immune function of SIGNR1⁺ marginal zone and CD169⁺ metallophilic splenic macrophages. Loss-of-function increases susceptibility to bacterial and parasitic infection. The mechanism is independent of LXR's classic role in reverse cholesterol transport and is linked instead to clearance of phagocytosed cargo.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from A-Gonzalez 2013 and follow-up macrophage-LXR studies.

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse data; human MZM equivalence to mouse MZM is contested.
