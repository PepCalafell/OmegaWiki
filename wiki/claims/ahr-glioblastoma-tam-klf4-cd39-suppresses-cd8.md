## Statement

In glioblastoma, cancer-cell-derived kynurenine activates AHR in tumour-associated macrophages, where AHR drives KLF4 expression to suppress NF-κB and induce CD39 — together promoting an immunosuppressive TAM phenotype and CD8⁺ T-cell dysfunction.

## Evidence summary

Takenaka et al. 2019 Nat Neurosci; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
