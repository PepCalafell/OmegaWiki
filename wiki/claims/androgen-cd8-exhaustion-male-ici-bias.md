## Statement

Androgen receptor signalling drives accumulation of progenitor exhausted CD8⁺ T cells in mouse urothelial cancer and in human NSCLC TILs, correlating with reduced ICI efficacy in males relative to females.

## Evidence summary

Mouse interventional study plus matched human TIL phenotyping.

## Conditions and scope

Sex-dimorphic effects; androgen-rich male host.

## Counter-evidence

Some male NSCLC subsets (e.g., heavy smokers with high TMB) respond well to ICIs.
