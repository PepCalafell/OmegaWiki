## Statement

The pairwise effects of TNF plus IL-18, IFN-γ, or IL-1β collectively recapitulate most of the organism-wide transcriptional response to sepsis across 9 organs in mouse models.

## Evidence summary

DEG overlap percentages, biomarker gene coverage (45.7%/43.8%/32.6% of 258 sepsis biomarkers respectively), and replication against CLP and vaccinia (WR) viral sepsis models. The other 12 cytokine pairs averaged 382 ± 298 DEGs versus 7,083 / 4,071 / 2,452 for the three key pairs.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only; bacterial sepsis (LPS, CLP) and one viral sepsis model (vaccinia WR). Human translation untested.

## Open questions

- Do the same three pairs hold in human sepsis or in other cytokine storm syndromes?
- What fraction of human-specific cytokines (e.g., IL-32) might shift the apex pair?
