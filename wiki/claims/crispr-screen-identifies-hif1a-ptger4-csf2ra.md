## Statement

An ex vivo CRISPR screen of ~10,200 sgRNAs (2,245-2,425 genes) in primary tumor-educated macrophages identifies multilevel regulators of the angiogenic (ARG1+) phenotype, including Hif1a, Ptger4, Csf2ra, Adar, Trim28, Spi1, Rreb1, Rbpj, Lamtor1/Flcn, and SWI/SNF components.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2064) — methodological evidence. MAGeCK analysis of ARG1-negative sorted TEMs vs input; individual-KO validation confirmed reproducibility.
  - Quote: "The screen revealed enriched regulators, including transcription factors (Spi1, Rreb1, and Rbpj), hypoxia components (Hif1a), metabolic sensors (Lamtor1 and Flcn), chromatin remodelers (SWI/SNF complex), and immune modulators (Adar, Trim28, Ptger4, and Csf2ra)"

## Conditions and scope

Ex vivo primary-macrophage screen; ARG1-negative sort defines the phenotype.
