## Statement

In human cancer-associated cachexia, expression of ubiquitin-proteasome system markers MURF1 and Atrogin-1, ubiquitinated-protein abundance, and 26S proteasome proteolytic activity have been reported as elevated in some cohorts and unchanged in others. The discrepancy is largely attributable to definition heterogeneity (Stephens used >5% weight loss; Khal used 1-11% moderate / >11% severe), cancer-type differences, and disease-stage variation.

## Evidence summary

Multiple small-cohort studies with divergent results synthesized in the 2025 *Cancer Discovery* review.

## Conditions and scope

Mechanistically real but quantitatively variable across cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

Mouse-CAC clearly shows UPS upregulation; the conflict is specific to human data.
