## Statement

Hypoxia-driven gene expression and chromatin accessibility changes are only partially concordant in glioma-co-cultured microglia (Pearson/Spearman ≈ 0.24): ~25% of upregulated and ~18% of downregulated genes show matching accessibility shifts.

## Evidence summary

Paired RNA/ATAC correlation (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.10).

> "The Spearman and Pearson correlation coefficients (0.24) for ATAC-seq peaks and gene expression indicated a partial but significant correlation ... 645 had a concordant increase in chromatin accessibility (24.76%) ... 345 had matched reductions in accessibility (17.76%)"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; co-culture under hypoxia.

## Counter-evidence

Partial concordance implies much expression change is chromatin-independent (fast TF-driven) or chromatin change is expression-neutral.
