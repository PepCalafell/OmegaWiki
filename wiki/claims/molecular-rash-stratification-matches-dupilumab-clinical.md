## Statement

Among clinically indeterminate rashes molecularly stratified with Trm1 DEGs, the two cases that segregated with atopic dermatitis (CIR-A, CIR-B) responded to dupilumab while the one that segregated with psoriasis (CIR-E) failed dupilumab — suggesting molecular class can predict therapeutic response where histopathology cannot.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]; drug [[foundations/dupilumab-anti-il4ra]]. Supports [[concepts/molecular-stratification-indeterminate-rash-predicts-dupilumab]].

## Conditions and scope

Anecdotal-scale (3 treated patients); explicitly framed by authors as motivating larger trials.

## Counter-evidence

Sample size precludes statistical confidence in the response prediction; confounders untested.
