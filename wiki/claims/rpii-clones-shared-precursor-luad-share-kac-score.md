## Statement

Reactive type II pneumocyte (RPII) regions are populated by clones shared across normal-adjacent tissue, precursor lesions, and invasive LUAD; these clones display elevated KAC transcriptional scores, evidencing that the KAC-RPII state precedes morphological precursor lesions in space and time.
