## Statement
Integrating multi-omics data into a single interaction network identifies the transcription regulators FOS, NCOR2, and PPARγ as central nodes (transcriptional hubs) of the immunosuppressive M2a macrophage state.

## Evidence summary
- "central network elements in the M2a state included transcription regulators FOS, NCOR2, and PPARγ. FOS was reported to be able to suppress inflammation and the PPAR pathway is one of the hallmarks of M2a macrophages" (p.13).
- Contrasts with M1 central regulators STAT1, STAT3, RELA, JUN, NFKB2, NCOR1 (p.13).

## Conditions and scope
Network-derived hubs (high centrality) are correlative, not validated drivers; in vitro polarized macrophages.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper; the authors note a literature bias toward well-studied proteins in interaction databases.

## Open questions
