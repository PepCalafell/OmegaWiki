## Statement

Sequencing ribosome-engaged transcripts pinpoints 13 mRNAs whose ribosome engagement (not total abundance) drops in DHPS-deficient macrophages, most encoding cell-adhesion, signalling and apoptosis proteins — candidate hypusine-eIF5A-dependent targets.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] using [[foundations/ribotag-ribosome-profiling]] (Fig. 4a). Defines [[concepts/eif5a-selective-translation-cell-adhesion-mrnas]].

## Conditions and scope

One experiment; filtered to transcripts stable in input across genotypes to isolate translational effect.

## Counter-evidence

Multiple genes rather than any single one are implicated.
