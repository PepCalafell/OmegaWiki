## Statement

Mice with macrophage-specific genetic deletion of Ogt (Lyz2ΔOgt) have decreased O-GlcNAcylation, reduced canonical IL-4-driven alternatively-activated-macrophage (AAM) marker expression, and defective helminth control in vivo — establishing O-GlcNAcylation as required for IL-4-driven alternative activation.

## Evidence summary

Reported in the *Nature Reviews Immunology* Journal Club commentary [[papers/glcnacylation-shapes-macrophage-tissue-residency-alternative]] summarizing Heieis et al. (bioRxiv 2026). Links O-GlcNAcylation ([[foundations/glcnacylation]] via [[foundations/ogt-glcnac-transferase]]) to the [[foundations/il4-cytokine]]-driven AAM programme.

## Conditions and scope

In vivo mouse model; myeloid/macrophage-restricted deletion via [[foundations/lysm-cre]]. Confidence is commentary-level (secondary source on a preprint).
