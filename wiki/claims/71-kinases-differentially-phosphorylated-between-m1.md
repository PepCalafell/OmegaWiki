## Statement
Seventy-one protein kinases show significantly different phosphorylation levels between M1 and M2 macrophages, with the MAPK signaling pathway significantly over-represented among them.

## Evidence summary
- "In total, 71 protein kinases had significantly different quantitative levels of one or more phosphosites in the comparison between M1 and M2 phosphoproteomes" (p.10).
- "21 of the 71 significant kinases belonged to the MAPK signaling pathway (this presented an enrichment ... FDR < 0.05), 14 kinases belonged to the Chemokine signaling pathway and 13 to the mTOR signaling pathway" (p.10).

## Conditions and scope
In vitro polarized macrophages; pathway membership by KEGG annotation.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
