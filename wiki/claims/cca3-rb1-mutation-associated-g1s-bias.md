## Statement

RB1 mutations are the genomic alteration most consistently associated with G1/S phase bias of cycling malignant cells in TCGA across cancer types, consistent with pRb's role as the G1/S gate.

## Evidence summary

TCGA driver-mutation analysis (Fig. 5g, Extended Data Fig. 10b–c).

## Conditions and scope

Bulk RNA-seq level; HPV+ tumours show G1/S bias without RB1 mutation due to E7-mediated pRb degradation.

## Counter-evidence

None.
