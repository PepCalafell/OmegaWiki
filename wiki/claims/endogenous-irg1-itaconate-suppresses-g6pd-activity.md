## Statement

Endogenous IRG1-derived itaconate restrains macrophage G6PD activity: removing IRG1 (knockdown or knockout) elevates G6PD activity without changing its protein level, confirming conserved metabolic regulation.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1471) — mechanistic (loss-of-function) evidence.
  - Quote: "IRG1 knockdown in pro-inflammatory macrophages ... led to G6PD activity enhancement without altering protein abundance ... Irg1−/− mouse BMDMs displayed elevated G6PD activity following LPS stimulation."

## Conditions and scope

Human and mouse macrophages; IRG1-deficient macrophages also adopt a pro-tumor phenotype (reduced TNF/CD80, increased CSF1R/CD206).
