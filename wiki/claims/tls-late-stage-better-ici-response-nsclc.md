## Statement

In NSCLC chemo–ICI cohorts, complete responders frequently display late-stage TLS marked by exhaustion genes (IFNG, HAVCR2), whereas non-responders display activated TLS with strong germinal-centre signatures concomitant with hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

Spatial profiling cited by the review distinguishes lymphoid-aggregate / activated / declining / late TLS states with different prognostic correlates.

## Conditions and scope

Post-treatment biopsies; chemo–ICI combinations.

## Counter-evidence

Hypoxia's role in arresting TLS maturation is correlative.

## Open questions
