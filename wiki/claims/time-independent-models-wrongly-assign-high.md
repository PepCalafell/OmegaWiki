## Statement

Static (time-independent) flux models incorrectly infer high HSC growth rates, contradicting established HSC quiescence, whereas the time-dependent model correctly predicts rare HSC division.

## Evidence summary

Model-comparison result aligned with known HSC biology and low scRNA-seq cell-cycle scores; supports the need for time-dependent rates.

## Conditions and scope

Single dataset; demonstrates a failure mode of static-rate modelling.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
