## Statement

In MRL-model systemic-lupus mouse spleens, B-PALS boundary, B-follicle, and marginal-zone spatial clusters acquire significantly higher curl and lower linearity than in healthy BALBc spleens, indicating loss of normal anatomical architecture.

## Evidence summary

Two-tailed Wilcoxon tests with P values reported above. Cluster shape descriptors (curl, linearity) used per `[[concepts/cluster-shape-analysis-spatial]]`.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse spleen, MRL vs BALBc comparison. Sample number is modest (3 vs 6) but cluster-component counts are larger.

## Open questions

- Does the same morphological signature appear in human lupus nephritis or in other germinal-centre-driven autoimmune diseases?
