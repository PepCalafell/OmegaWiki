## Statement

Across single-cell datasets of mouse and human lung tumors, macrophages (and monocytes) are the dominant IRG1-expressing cell population.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1461) — methodological (scRNA-seq) evidence.
  - Quote: "Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) identified macrophages as the major Irg1-expressing population in the mouse lung TME ... IRG1 expression was enriched in myeloid cells, particularly in macrophages and monocytes."

## Conditions and scope

Limited numbers of IRG1-expressing cells detected (possible sequencing-depth/spatiotemporal effects); IRG1 higher in normal lung than tumor.

## Counter-evidence

Absence of IRG1 in TCGA bulk lung cancer data — attributed by authors to insufficient depth or spatiotemporal regulation. Single-source claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
