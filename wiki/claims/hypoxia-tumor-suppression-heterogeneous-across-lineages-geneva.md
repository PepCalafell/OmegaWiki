## Statement

In the GENEVA multiplexed mosaic xenograft of 20 human cancer lines, systemic hypoxia reduces overall tumor growth, but per-lineage relative fitness varies widely, revealing cell-intrinsic heterogeneity in hypoxia sensitivity.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.5): "systemic hypoxia led to a marked and significant decrease in mosaic tumor growth in the multiplexed xenograft setting" — SNP-based deconvolution + scRNA-seq (10x 3' HT v3.1) of mosaic tumors in NSG mice, 21% vs 8% O2, 8 days (Fig.2b-e). (type: methodological, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
