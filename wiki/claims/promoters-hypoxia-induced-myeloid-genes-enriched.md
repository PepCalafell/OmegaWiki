## Statement

Promoters of hypoxia-upregulated myeloid marker genes are enriched for HIF-1/2α, MAX, RUNX, NF-κB, and AP1 motifs, while hypoxia-downregulated genes are enriched for ISRE, BCL6, and ETV1 — implicating diverse TFs beyond HIF in hypoxic myeloid reprogramming.

## Evidence summary

Promoter motif enrichment analysis (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6).

> "Besides HIF-1/2α motifs, hypoxia-upregulated genes also showed enrichment for MYC associated factor X (MAX), runt-related transcription factor (RUNX), nuclear factor κB (NF-κB), activator protein-1 (AP1) ... motifs for interferon-stimulated response element (ISRE), B-cell lymphoma 6 protein (BCL6), and ETS variant transcription factor 1 (ETV1) were enriched in promoters of hypoxia-downregulated genes"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; computational motif enrichment.

## Counter-evidence

Motif enrichment is correlative; TF binding/causality not directly tested.
