## Statement

The myeloid glycoprotein CD300e is upregulated by IFN-α (IFN-I) but downregulated by IFN-γ (IFN-II) at both RNA and protein levels, indicating antagonistic regulation by the two pathways.

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.7). Contrasts [[type-interferon-ifna-ifnb]] vs [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]] effects in monocytes.
