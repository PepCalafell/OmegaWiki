## Statement

CRISPR KO of the multi-state TF genes `Hic1` and `Gfi1` reduces both TEXterm cell frequency in chronic LCMV and TRM cell frequency/TRM-signature gene expression in acute LCMV, mirroring the known multi-state TF `Prdm1` and validating Taiji's TF state-selectivity assignments.
