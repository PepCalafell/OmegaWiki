## Statement

Pretreatment with anti-TNF or anti-IL-1β neutralizing antibodies completely prevents lethality and hypothermia from a lethal LPS dose in mice, whereas anti-IL-18 or anti-IFN-γ yield only partial protection.

## Conditions and scope

Prophylactic, not therapeutic; mouse only. Clinical anti-TNF trials in established human sepsis have failed, reflecting timing rather than mechanism.
