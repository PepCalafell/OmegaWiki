## Statement

In the mouse primary motor cortex, clustering astrocytes and layer 2/3 intratelencephalic-projecting neurons based on GITIII's influence tensor revealed cell subgroups with sharper spatial boundaries than those derived from gene expression alone.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/identifying-spatial-single-cell-level-interactions]] (p.146): "In the mouse primary motor cortex, clustering astrocytes and layer 2/3 intratelencephalic-projecting neurons based on GITIII's influence tensor revealed cell subgroups with sharper spatial boundaries than those derived from gene expression alone." This is the commentary's headline example of CCI-informed clustering. See [[concepts/cci-influence-tensor]].

## Conditions and scope

Single tissue (mouse primary motor cortex), two cell types. Demonstrative, not a systematic benchmark.
