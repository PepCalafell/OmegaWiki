## Statement

Different AHR ligands occupy the PAS-B pocket in distinct orientations, inducing different AHR conformations, partner-protein recruitment, and tissue-specific transcriptional programmes — leading to qualitatively different immune outcomes (Treg vs Th17, tolerogenic vs pro-inflammatory DC) that motivate the design of selective AHR modulators (SAHRMs).

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Wright et al. 2024 cryo-EM; multiple ligand-bias studies).
