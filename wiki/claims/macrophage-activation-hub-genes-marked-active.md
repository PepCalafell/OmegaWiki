## Statement
The most highly connected hub genes of the human macrophage multidimensional model are characterized by permissive promoter marks far more than by enhancer marks.

## Evidence summary
Among 869 hub genes, 94% had a Pa and 27% an Es/Ew, supporting that permissive promoters dominate the common activation-associated gene class.

## Conditions and scope
Hub genes defined by prior network model; cross-referenced to ChIP-seq chromatin states.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Whether hub-gene promoter accessibility is causal for their network centrality.
