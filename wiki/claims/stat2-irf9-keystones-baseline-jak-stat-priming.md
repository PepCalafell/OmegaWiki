## Statement

Across the 12 JAK-STAT mutant models tested under ex vivo IFN-β stimulation, knockouts of STAT2 and IRF9 most strongly compromise the IFN-β-induced restoration of WT gene expression — including the core ISG response and a broader set of stimulation-rescued genes. This identifies STAT2 and IRF9 as the keystones of baseline JAK-STAT priming under homeostasis: they are required to maintain the cells in a state poised for rapid IFN response.
