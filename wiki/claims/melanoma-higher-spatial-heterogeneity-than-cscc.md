## Statement

Spatial cell-type heterogeneity, quantified by Rao's quadratic entropy on single-cell CosMx data, is significantly higher in melanoma than in cSCC, possibly because melanoma cells adhere less and thus have more diverse neighbours.

## Evidence summary

FOV-level entropy across three cancer types; melanoma > cSCC significant; immune-rich FOVs most heterogeneous. (p.8)

## Conditions and scope

Few patients (2–4 per type); authors explicitly flag the need for larger cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

Small sample; heterogeneity may be confounded by FOV selection/immune content.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether the heterogeneity difference holds in larger cohorts and relates to outcome.
