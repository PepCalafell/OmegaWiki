## Statement

CD206hi IMs predominantly express the antitumorigenic chemokines Cxcl13, Cxcl9 and Cxcl10, associated with lymphocyte positioning.

## Evidence summary

scRNA-seq feature plots (Fig. 1f) show Cxcl13/Cxcl9/Cxcl10 enriched in CD206hi IMs; ELISA confirms corresponding lung protein. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; flow-sorted extravascular CD64+CD11b+ phagocytes.
