## Statement

Males and females show distinct, non-overlapping, often oppositely-directed DNA methylation changes after BCG vaccination.

## Evidence summary

Sex-stratified short- and long-term EWAS yield disjoint CpG sets with divergent directions, indicating strong sex-specificity of the BCG epigenetic response.

## Conditions and scope

Suggestive-threshold sites; modest subgroup sizes.

## Counter-evidence

No FDR-significant sex-stratified CpGs; male results unvalidated externally.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Mechanistic basis (hormonal) of the divergent trajectories.
