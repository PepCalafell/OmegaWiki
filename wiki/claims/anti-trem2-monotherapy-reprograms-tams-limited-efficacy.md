## Statement

Antagonistic anti-TREM2 antibody monotherapy reprograms TAMs molecularly (loss of Mmp12/Spp1, preserved Siglec1/Trem1/Ifit2) but achieves only minor anti-tumor efficacy in syngeneic models, motivating combination strategies.
