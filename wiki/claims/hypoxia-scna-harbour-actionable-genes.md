## Statement

Hypoxia status is associated with recurrent somatic copy number alterations that contain clinically actionable genes — including EGFR amplification (7p11.2) linked to erlotinib sensitivity and PDCD1 deletion (2q37.3) relevant to immune checkpoint therapy. The hypoxic microenvironment may therefore shape drug and immunotherapy response partly by altering copy number.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — chromosome-level SCNA mapping and drug-response links (Fig. 6d,e).

## Conditions and scope

- Actionable-gene examples drawn primarily from LGG; SCNA significance is cancer-type specific.

## Counter-evidence

- Some actionable SCNAs (e.g. 7q31.2 MET amplification) were enriched in hypoxia score-low samples.
