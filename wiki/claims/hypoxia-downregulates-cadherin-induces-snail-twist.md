## Statement

Hypoxia coordinately downregulates the epithelial marker E-cadherin and upregulates mesenchymal/EMT-TF markers N-cadherin, SNAIL, vimentin, TWIST and JAGGED2, modulating lineage plasticity towards invasive/metastatic phenotypes.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Cell-line and xenograft EMT readouts.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
