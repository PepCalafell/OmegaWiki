## Statement

The meta-program (MP) profile of KACs — low MP2 (AT2-alveolar) and high MP6 (tumor/KAC) plus MP7 (inflammatory/stress) — is closer to precursor lesion cells than to normal alveolar AT2 cells, supporting their identity as a transcriptionally pre-malignant alveolar intermediate.

## Evidence summary

Direct quote: "the MP landscape of KACs resembled that of lesions, including reduced MP2 (alveolar) and more prevalent tumor-associated MP6" (p.327). RPII spots in ST recapitulate this pattern at super-pixel resolution via iStar.

## Linked ideas

- KACs as preferred targets for transcriptional intercept biomarkers based on MP6 detection in CT-guided needle biopsies.
