## Statement

A meaningful fraction of lead immune xQTLs are common in the Chinese cohort but rare in other ancestries, and some overlap loci under latitude-based selection, indicating ancestry-specific regulatory architecture.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (correlational): 10.4% of lead xQTLs had MAF<0.01 in EUR/AFR/ALFA; 7 lead xQTLs overlapped 98 latitude-selected loci across 20 cell types; eGene overlap 43.79% with OneK1K (EUR) vs 93.28% with ImmuNexUT (East Asian) (p.8).

## Conditions and scope

East Asian cohort; common-variant focus (MAF>0.1).
