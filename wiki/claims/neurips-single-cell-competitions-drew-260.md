## Statement

The Open Problems team set up multimodal single-cell data-integration competitions at NeurIPS 2021 and 2022 that attracted over 260 and 1,600 participants, respectively.

## Evidence summary

"we previously set up popular competitions for multimodal data integration at NeurIPS 2021 and 2022, with over 260 and 1,600 participants, respectively" (p.1038). The competitions reuse the platform's batch-integration and matching-modality tasks as challenge definitions.

## Conditions and scope

Two competition editions; counts are author-reported participant numbers.

## Counter-evidence

None material; sourced participation figures.

## Linked ideas

Demonstrates the reach of the platform as a method-development driver via [[foundations/openproblems-benchmark]].

## Open questions
