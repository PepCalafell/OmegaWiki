## Statement

The disease-severity axis (DC3) in COVID-19 is enriched for genes involved in neutrophil degranulation, a pathway previously linked specifically to myeloid responses in severe disease, and is largely non-overlapping with the interferon program of DC2.

## Evidence summary

Figure 2i,j of [[reconstructing-developmental-disease-progression-sample-level]]; the programs of DC2 and DC3 were generally non-overlapping but would be indistinguishable in case-control analysis.

## Conditions and scope

Severity continuum within infected donors.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.
