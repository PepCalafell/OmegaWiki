## Statement

Methyl groups from apoptotic-cell-derived methionine are routed via SAM into macrophage genomic DNA as 13C-5-methylcytosine, providing direct metabolic evidence linking AC cargo to macrophage DNA methylation.

## Evidence summary

LC-MS/MS of macrophage DNA hydrolysate detects 13C-mC after exposure to 13C-methionine-labelled ACs; bafilomycin and MAT2A inhibition combinatorially abolish the signal. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only; tracing is bulk DNA, not site-specific.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
