## Statement

HIF and the unfolded protein response form a reciprocal regulatory loop. Hypoxia/HIF-1α activate UPR effectors (PERK, ATF6) directly and through VEGF, while UPR transcription factor XBP1 is hypoxia-inducible and drives tumor growth. Conversely, sustained ER stress can downregulate HIF expression. The interaction shapes tumor cell adaptation, chemoresistance, and apoptosis decisions.

## Evidence summary

Multiple primary papers integrated by Luo et al. 2022 demonstrate HIF-1α induction of UPR markers in alveolar epithelium and tumor models; XBP1 hypoxia-inducibility is mapped to HRE-adjacent elements.

## Conditions and scope

- Tumor microenvironment combining hypoxia and proteostatic stress
- Alveolar epithelium in acute lung injury
- Chemoresistance contexts where UPR-HIF coupling buffers cytotoxic stress

## Counter-evidence

Some chronic ER stress models suppress HIF-1α translation via PERK-eIF2α phosphorylation — context-dependent direction.
