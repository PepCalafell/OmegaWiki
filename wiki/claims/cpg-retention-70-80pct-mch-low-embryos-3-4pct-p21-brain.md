## Statement

In spatial-DMT, CpG sites retain 70–80% of their methylation signal (canonical methylation read-out) across samples; non-CpG (mCH, mostly mCA) methylation is < 1% in embryos and rises to ~3–4% in P21 brain, reproducing the known postnatal accumulation of non-CpG methylation in mammalian neurons.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 1d and Extended Data Fig. 3d.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse tissue only; EM-seq conversion via TET2+APOBEC; whole-genome bisulfite-free.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
