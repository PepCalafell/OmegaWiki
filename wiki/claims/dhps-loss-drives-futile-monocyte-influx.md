## Statement

Because DHPS-deficient macrophages cannot mature into self-maintaining RTMs, tissues continually recruit monocytes that nonetheless fail to re-establish the resident pool — a futile cycle of influx and turnover.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]]; corroborated by parabiosis and clodronate experiments. Reflects the model in [[concepts/macrophage-ontogeny-resident-vs-monocyte-derived]].

## Conditions and scope

Observed across peritoneum and lung; proposed to be driven by sensing of a dearth of mature RTMs.

None.
