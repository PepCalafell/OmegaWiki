## Statement

Comparing 81 shared cytokines, human PBMC and mouse lymph-node responses correlate weakly (median gene-wise r≈0.19); only ~11.3% of strongly regulated genes are concordant and ~6.9% are strongly discordant.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: quantitative.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.

None within the paper's scope.
