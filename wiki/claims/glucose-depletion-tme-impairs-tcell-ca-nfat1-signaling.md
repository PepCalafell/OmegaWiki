## Statement

Cancer-associated stromal/myeloid cells outcompete T cells for glucose in the TME. Reduced glucose availability and GLUT1 downregulation impair the Ca2+-NFAT1 signaling axis, suppressing T-cell function and shaping immune evasion.

## Evidence summary

- Murine TME models cited in Ricci 2025.
- Glycolytic gene signature inversely correlates with immune infiltration in melanoma and NSCLC patients on ACT.

## Conditions and scope

- Demonstrated in murine adoptive-transfer models; biomarker correlation in patients.

## Counter-evidence

- Recent data suggest cancer-associated fibroblasts and myeloid cells (not tumour cells) are the primary glucose consumers.

## Linked ideas

- Tumour glucose competition as a target for nutrient-restoration combinations with ICB.

## Open questions

- Whether localised glucose supplementation can restore TIL function clinically.
