## Statement

Colorectal tumors with the highest innate immune activation carry greater tumor mutation burden, a distinct driver-mutation profile (less P53/KRAS/APC, more MUC16), more immune infiltration, and higher T-cell exhaustion marker expression.

## Evidence summary

Innate-group clustering of COAD tumors linked the high-activation group to mutation burden, MUC16 mutation, and exhaustion markers, with the exhaustion link replicated in an independent cohort.

## Conditions and scope

Within-cancer clustering analysis; correlational; supports innate activation as an index of immunogenicity coupled to exhaustion.

## Counter-evidence

Driver-gene mutation burden was not associated with outcomes here; no causal test of innate activation driving immunogenicity.
