## Statement
Single-cell genomic sequencing shows TP53-mutant clones occupy 5-10% of normal oesophagus by middle age and 15-30% by age 70.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
