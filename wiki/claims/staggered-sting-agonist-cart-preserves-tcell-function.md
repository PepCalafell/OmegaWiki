## Statement

Staggered intratumoral STING agonist before CAR-T infusion preserves T cell function while activating TME innate immunity.

## Evidence summary

Smith 2022 (CAR T + DMXAA breast cancer; ref 189): intratumoral STING agonist before CAR T can improve antitumor control because poor PK of STING agonists clears the agonist before CAR T arrives. Difficult to translate clinically.

## Conditions and scope

CAR-T-based combination therapy; depends on the poor PK of CDN-class STING agonists; difficult to operationalize in clinic.
