## Statement

Loss of HIF-1α in BMDMs is not metabolically neutral — it produces a compensatory or derepressed elevation of mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation: higher basal OCR, higher TCA cycle metabolite pools, and higher ETC complex protein and gene expression. This positions HIF-1α as a negative regulator of mitochondrial output in BMDMs at steady state.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 3C-E; Fig. 4C,D.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro BMDMs; tamoxifen-inducible deletion. TR-AMs show no comparable mitochondrial elevation under the same paradigm — phenotype is BMDM-specific.
