## Statement

The broad-spectrum inhibitor Midostaurin suppresses LPS-induced Ifnb1 and interferon-stimulated gene expression through inhibition of TBK1, with direct cellular target engagement demonstrated by TBK1 thermal stabilization in CETSA at its EC50.

## Evidence summary

Midostaurin reduced Ifnb1/ISGs and p-STAT1, downregulated IRF3-bound CREs, and stabilized TBK1 in CETSA. Links [[foundations/midostaurin]], [[foundations/tbk1-tank-binding-kinase-1]], [[foundations/cetsa-cellular-thermal-shift-assay]], [[foundations/irf3-interferon-regulatory-factor-3]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS; concentration at kinobeads EC50 for TBK1.

## Counter-evidence

Midostaurin is highly polypharmacological, so additional targets may contribute to its effects.
