## Statement

LPS sepsis induces neutrophil accumulation across the whole organism (17 tissues + vasculature), and the cytokine pairs TNF+IL-18 and TNF+IFN-γ are sufficient to drive even stronger lung neutrophil recruitment than LPS itself.
