## Statement

You cannot reliably infer an effector's host-interaction profile from sequence
similarity: similarity merely bounds, but does not determine, interaction similarity.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.447): within homology clusters
(≥90% identity) interaction-profile similarity spans identical to complementary;
conversely, dissimilar effectors can share targets (Extended Data Fig.3).

## Conditions and scope

Based on systematically profiled homolog clusters in HuMMIHOM.

## Counter-evidence

Sequence similarity does set an upper limit, so it is not entirely uninformative.

## Linked ideas

Defines [[effector-interaction-sequence-independence]].

## Open questions

What structural/motif features determine interaction profiles.
