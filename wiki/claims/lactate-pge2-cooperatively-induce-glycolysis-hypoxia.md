## Statement

Lactic acid and PGE2 act cooperatively: among lactate-induced genes, adding PGE2 further induces angiogenesis/glycolysis/hypoxia genes such as Arg1, Vegfa, and Slc2a1.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2065) — mechanistic evidence. RNA-seq combinatorial analysis: of 256 lactate-induced genes, L+P further induces 99 (incl. Arg1, Vegfa) and represses 62.
  - Quote: "PGE2 and lactic acid cooperatively induced genes related to glycolysis and hypoxia pathways (e.g., Arg1, Vegfa, and Slc2a1)"

## Conditions and scope

In vitro BMDM; DEG threshold LFC>1, FDR<0.05.
