## Statement
Most CMS-subtype-enriched proteins from TPCPA reproduce at the RNA level on external CRC cohorts, supporting cross-omics consistency of the proteome-derived CMS markers. A small minority (44 blood-related proteins) are not RNA-differentially abundant — consistent with translational regulation or extracellular contribution.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 — text after CRC CMS DE, plus TPCPA portal "CRC CMS prot/mRNA" module.

## Conditions and scope
- Bulk-tissue validation; per-protein discrepancies are aggregated.

## Counter-evidence
- The 79 CMS-enriched proteins that differ at RNA highlight the value of measuring protein directly for subset-defining biology.
