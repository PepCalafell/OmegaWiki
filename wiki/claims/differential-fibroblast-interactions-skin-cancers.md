## Statement

Fibroblasts are the dominant interacting cell across all three skin cancers, but the partner differs by subtype: fibroblast-T-cell interactions are stronger in keratinocyte cancers (cSCC/BCC), whereas fibroblast-melanocyte interactions are stronger in melanoma — a candidate correlate of differing biology and metastatic potential.

## Evidence summary

Cell-type-level differential interaction analysis; cSCC/BCC cluster together vs melanoma. (p.11)

## Conditions and scope

Small cohort; interaction inference.

## Counter-evidence

Inference-based; causal link to metastatic potential not tested.

## Open questions
