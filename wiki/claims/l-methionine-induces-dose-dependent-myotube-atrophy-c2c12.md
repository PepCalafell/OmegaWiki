## Statement

Excess L-methionine alone is sufficient to drive dose-dependent myotube atrophy in C2C12 cells without any cytokine or tumour-conditioned stimulus — establishing the methionine cycle as a *causal* upstream of an in vitro atrophy phenotype consistent with cachexia.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "L-methionine induced myotube atrophy, a classical cachexia feature in this cell culture setting, in a dose-dependent manner."

## Conditions and scope

Methionine-poor dialysed-serum medium to control exogenous methionine. Diameter quantified by ImageJ across 40-60 myotubes/well.

## Counter-evidence

Morphological readout only; molecular atrophy markers (atrogenes, autophagy flux) not measured.

## Open questions

- Whether the dose range (20-100 μM) is physiologically relevant to intramuscular methionine concentrations during cachexia.
- Whether SAM supplementation alone reproduces the atrophy phenotype (would localise the effect downstream of MAT).
