## Statement

Only the combination of lactic acid, GM-CSF, and PGE2 (LGP) — not any single factor or pair — induces ARG1 to levels comparable with tumor-conditioned-media-educated macrophages, establishing the three factors as necessary and sufficient for angiogenic polarization.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2064-2065) — mechanistic evidence. FACS ARG1 quantification under single/paired/triple treatment (25 mmol/L lactate, 2 ng/mL GM-CSF, 100 nmol/L PGE2); RNA-seq confirms LGP best mimics TEM transcriptome.
  - Quote: "when BMDMs were co-treated with the combination of lactic acid, GM-CSF, and PGE2, but not with any two factors alone, ARG1 expression reached levels comparable with those in TEMs"

## Conditions and scope

In vitro BMDM reconstitution; ARG1 as the angiogenic readout.
