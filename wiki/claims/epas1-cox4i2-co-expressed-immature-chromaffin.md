## Statement

EPAS1 and COX4I2 are co-regulated in developmentally immature chromaffin cells before birth, silenced upon maturation, and reactivated as a fetal-like program in EPAS1-mutant PPGL.

## Evidence summary

Single-cell RNA-seq trajectory analysis of the neural-crest-to-chromaffin lineage; consistent with mid-gestational lethality of EPAS1-knockout mice.

## Conditions and scope

Developmental window inference; see [[concepts/developmental-time-window-systemic-hypoxia-tumorigenesis]].

## Counter-evidence

Correlative single-cell evidence.
