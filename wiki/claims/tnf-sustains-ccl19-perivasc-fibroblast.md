## Statement

TNF signalling from PERIVASC I immune cells acts on perivascular fibroblasts (Perivasc Fib I) to sustain CCL19 expression, establishing a homeostatic stromal-immune feedback loop reminiscent of lymph-node FRC programs.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Fig. 5d and Discussion.

## Conditions and scope

Inference based on CellChat L-R analysis on integrated scRNA-seq; no anti-TNF perturbation in human skin demonstrated here.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper, but causality remains unproven.
