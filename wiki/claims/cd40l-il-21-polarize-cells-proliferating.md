## Statement

B cells exposed to CD40L or IL-21 in vivo polarize into a proliferating B-e state marked by Ncl, Eif4a1, Nme1, Eif5a, Ptma (translation/biosynthesis markers).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Resting lymph-node B cells, no antigen/BCR stimulation.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope.
