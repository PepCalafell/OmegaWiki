## Statement
The lineage-determining factor PU.1 (SPI1) motif is highly enriched at common active promoters and common strong enhancers shared across macrophage activation states.

## Evidence summary
HOMER motif analysis: PU.1 motif at 96% of common strong enhancers, P = 1E−832 at common active promoters; other ETS-family motifs (ETS1, GABPA, ELK4, FLI1) co-enriched.

## Conditions and scope
Bioinformatic motif prediction, not direct PU.1 ChIP in this dataset.

## Counter-evidence
A subset of activation-specific enhancers do not require PU.1 (see [[claims/subset-activation-specific-enhancers-pu-independent]]).

## Open questions
