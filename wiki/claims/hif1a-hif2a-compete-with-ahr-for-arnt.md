## Statement

The AHR transcriptional output is suppressed when HIF1α or HIF2α (EPAS1) is stabilised, because all three bHLH-PAS factors share ARNT/HIF1β as obligate dimerisation partner and HIFα has higher ARNT affinity under hypoxic conditions. Oxygen tension is therefore a direct modulator of AHR activity.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews the ARNT-competition mechanism and cross-references prior structural and reporter studies.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxic or pseudohypoxic contexts (CoCl₂, DMOG, VHL-deficient tumours). In normoxia, ARNT competition is negligible.

## Counter-evidence

— ARNT levels themselves are hypoxia-induced in a cell-line-specific manner, partially compensating the competition — the net AHR vs HIF outcome depends on the magnitude balance.

## Linked ideas

- Directly relevant to my thesis hypoxia work: predicts that AHR-driven phenotypes in the hypoxic tumour core would be muted relative to the normoxic periphery — testable with spatial transcriptomics.

## Open questions
