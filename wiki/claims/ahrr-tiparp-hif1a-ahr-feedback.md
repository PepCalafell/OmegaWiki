## Statement

Canonical AHR signalling is constrained by several negative-feedback mechanisms: the AHRR (competitor for ARNT), HIF-1α (also competes for ARNT under hypoxia), TIPARP (ADP-ribosylates AHR and co-factors), 26S-proteasome-mediated AHR degradation, and post-transcriptional repression via miRNAs and lncRNAs.
