## Statement
Highly sensitive bioluminescent enzyme-substrate pairs Akaluc/AkaLumine and NanoLuc/CFz detect as low as 10-1000 cells in deep organs like lung and brain.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
