## Statement

Stereoisomeric LCA derivatives provide a microbial-chemistry handle on Treg/Th17 balance: 3-oxoLCA binds and antagonizes RORγt to block Th17, while isoalloLCA induces mitochondrial ROS and NR4A1 binding at the Foxp3 locus to drive Treg generation, independently of vitamin-D-receptor and FXR CNS3 enhancers.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from Hang/Huh lab and related primary studies.

## Conditions and scope

- Gnotobiotic and inflammatory gut models; gut-microbial bile-acid pools.

## Counter-evidence

- Human reproducibility at physiological gut concentrations uncertain.
