## Statement

In the 3CA v2 cohort, snRNA-seq samples contribute disproportionately to a distinct cell-cycle MP and a second cilia MP containing HYDIN, RFX3, CFAP44 and DNAH7 — programs not detected in the scRNA-seq-only analysis.

## Evidence summary

Direct quantification in Fig. 3c and visualisation in Fig. 3d–e.

## Conditions and scope

Effect may reflect nuclear-vs-cytosolic transcript distribution, the cancer-type composition of snRNA-seq cohorts, or both.

## Counter-evidence

None.
