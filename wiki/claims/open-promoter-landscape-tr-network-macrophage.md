## Statement
The constitutively accessible promoter landmark within the TR network is a hallmark of monocyte-derived macrophages, not a generic genomic property.

## Evidence summary
Non-macrophage control gene sets (CNS-related, random) had markedly fewer accessible promoters than the macrophage activation TR network.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages with control gene-set comparison.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Open questions
Whether other plastic cell types possess analogous lineage-specific open TR networks.
