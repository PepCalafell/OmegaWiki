## Statement

Nivolumab response in clear-cell RCC ALI PDOs depends on CD8+ T cell infiltration rather than PDL1 expression level.

## Evidence summary

Nivolumab response was dependent on CD8+ T cell infiltration rather than on PDL1 expression levels in clear-cell RCC ALI PDOs (Polak et al. 2024, p.531; Esser et al. 2020).

## Conditions and scope

Clear-cell renal cell carcinoma ALI PDOs; biopsy-matched tumour material.
