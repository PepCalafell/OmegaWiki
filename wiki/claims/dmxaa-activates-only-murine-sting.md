## Statement

DMXAA (vadimezan, ASA404) fully activates only murine STING and was the prototypical translational failure.

## Evidence summary

DMXAA produced potent antitumor responses in mouse models but was found to bind and signal only mouse STING (Conlon 2013) and partially activate human STING (Temizoz 2024). Failed Phase 3 in advanced NSCLC.

## Conditions and scope

Specific to DMXAA chemotype; informs species-selectivity testing requirements for all subsequent agonists.
