## Statement

In mature primary mouse macrophages with tamoxifen-induced myeloid-specific HIF-1α deletion, baseline transcriptomic dependency on HIF-1α is highly asymmetric: BMDMs lose 305 differentially expressed genes (with prominent suppression of HIF-1α target genes); TR-AMs lose only 10. This demonstrates that HIF-1α is constitutively load-bearing in BMDM gene regulation but functionally silent in TR-AMs at steady state.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 1B,E; Suppl. Fig. S3A,B. Confirmed by HIF-1α-target heatmaps (Fig. 1C,F).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse only; LysM-CreERT2 model; in vitro after isolation. Does not assess in-vivo lung-tissue conditions or other tissue-resident macrophage populations.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper. Consistent with prior literature on postnatal HIF-1α-target silencing during TR-AM maturation.
