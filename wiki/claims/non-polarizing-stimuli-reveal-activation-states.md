## Statement
While canonical M1/M2 stimuli reproduce a bipolar axis, stimuli not linked to M1 or M2 polarization (free fatty acids, HDL, chronic-inflammation cocktails) reveal macrophage activation signatures beyond that axis.

## Evidence summary
- "when adding stimuli not linked to either M1 or M2 polarization, such as free fatty acids, high-density lipoprotein (HDL), or combinations of stimuli associated with chronic inflammation ... a spectrum of macrophage-activation signatures beyond the initial bipolar axis became apparent" (p.275, Figures 1E–1F).
- Earlier time points show the spectrum as a dense network of individual signatures (Figure 1G).

## Conditions and scope
Bulk in-vitro CRA; bipolar structure maintained until non-canonical stimuli added.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
