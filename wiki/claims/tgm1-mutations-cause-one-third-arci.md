## Statement

Approximately one-third of autosomal recessive congenital ichthyosis (ARCI) cases arise from mutations in *TGM1*, which encodes transglutaminase 1 (TG1) — an enzyme essential for cornified-envelope formation in the stratum corneum. The single most common *TGM1* mutation is c.877-2A>G, which disrupts a canonical splice-acceptor site and produces a truncated, non-functional TG1, making it the prototypical target for genome-correction therapy.
