## Statement

Of the six JAK inhibitors tested, only Momelotinib lowered Ifnb1 gene expression in LPS-stimulated macrophages, an effect attributable to its binding/inhibition of TBK1 (the IRF3-activating kinase) on top of JAK inhibition.

## Evidence summary

RNA-seq singled out Momelotinib as the lone Ifnb1-reducing JAKi; kinobeads detect Momelotinib–TBK1 binding, and its STAT1 effects likely combine direct JAK inhibition with reduced Ifnb1. Links [[foundations/momelotinib]], [[foundations/tbk1-tank-binding-kinase-1]], [[foundations/irf3-interferon-regulatory-factor-3]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse BMDM; LPS; concentration at kinobeads EC50 for IKBKE (TBK1 binding occurs at ~2.5× higher).

## Counter-evidence

TBK1 binding occurs at higher concentration than used, so the magnitude of the direct-TBK1 contribution at the screening dose is uncertain.
