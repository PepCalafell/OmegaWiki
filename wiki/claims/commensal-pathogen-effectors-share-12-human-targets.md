## Statement

Commensal and pathogen effectors exhibit both overlap and lifestyle-dependent
specificity in host targeting: a numerically small but statistically significant set of
human proteins is hit by both.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.446): 12 shared human targets
(P=0.014, OR=2.26); 3 subject to commensal convergence (P=0.067, OR=3.37). Limited by
sample size and the non-systematic nature of pathogen data.

## Conditions and scope

Pathogen interactions drawn from IntAct, not a matched systematic screen.

## Counter-evidence

The 3-protein convergence overlap is only marginally significant (P=0.067).

## Linked ideas

Relates to [[effector-convergence-host-hub-proteins]] and
[[commensal-pathogen-effector-divergence]].

## Open questions

Whether shared targets mark a conserved interkingdom interface.
