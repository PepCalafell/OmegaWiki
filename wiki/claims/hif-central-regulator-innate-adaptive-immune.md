## Statement

Across neutrophils, macrophages, dendritic cells, T cells, B cells and intestinal epithelial cells, HIF exerts distinct, cell-type-specific control over survival, differentiation and effector function, establishing it as a central transcriptional regulator of both innate and adaptive immunity.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Relates to concept [[concepts/m1-m2-polarization-paradigm]] and [[concepts/hif-dependent-glycolysis-immune-cell-differentiation]].

## Conditions and scope

Cell-type specificity means the directional effect of HIF (activating vs suppressive) differs by lineage and context.

## Counter-evidence

Other HIF-pathway components (PHD hydroxylases, pVHL) can regulate immune cells through HIF-independent mechanisms.
