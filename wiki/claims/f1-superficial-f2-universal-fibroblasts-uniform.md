## Statement

F1 superficial (papillary) and F2 universal (reticular) fibroblasts are present throughout the skin at different tissue depths — F1 adjacent to epithelium in the papillary dermis, F2 deeper in the reticular dermis.

## Evidence summary

Visium/Xenium spatial localization plus depth-specific marker expression (Fig. 1c, Fig. 2b,c).

Healthy skin.
