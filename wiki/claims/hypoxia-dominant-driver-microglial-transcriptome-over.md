## Statement

In BV2 microglia, hypoxia imposes a larger transcriptomic effect than glioma contact: hypoxia drives separation along PC1 and thousands of DEGs, whereas glioma contact alone (normoxia) changes far fewer genes.

## Evidence summary

Quantified by PCA and DEG counts (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.5).

> "Hypoxia imposed stronger sample separation (along PC1) compared to the CC effect (along PC2) ... BV2 CC with glioma cells induced 412 genes and decreased 44 genes ... Hypoxia in CC triggered broader transcriptional changes in BV2, with 2605 upregulated and 1942 downregulated genes"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia in vitro; bulk RNA-seq.

## Counter-evidence

Magnitude of contact effect may be underestimated in a simplified two-cell co-culture versus in vivo.
