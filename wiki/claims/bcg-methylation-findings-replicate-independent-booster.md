## Statement

A subset of the discovery-cohort methylation findings replicate with consistent direction in an independent BCG booster cohort, albeit with limited power.

## Evidence summary

Directional consistency for many CpGs and a few nominally significant replications across BCG-effect, baseline-TI, and female-specific analyses.

## Conditions and scope

n=17 replication; nominal significance only; few males.

## Counter-evidence

Limited statistical power; most sites not formally significant on replication.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Replication in larger, age- and ancestry-diverse cohorts.
