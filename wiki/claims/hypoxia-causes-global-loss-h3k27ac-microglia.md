## Statement

Hypoxia causes a global loss of histone acetylation, specifically H3K27ac, in microglia (BV2), as shown by both western blot over time and CUT&RUN around transcription start sites — consistent with increased HDAC activity in hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

Western blot plus CUT&RUN profiling (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.12–14).

> "Another important chromatin change observed in hypoxic GAMs was a global loss of histone acetylation ... H3 lysine 27 acetylation (H3K27ac) in BV2 cells and found it to be globally decreased in hypoxia"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 microglia; in vitro hypoxia; three biological replicates.

## Counter-evidence

None identified; consistent with prior reports of hypoxic HDAC activation.
