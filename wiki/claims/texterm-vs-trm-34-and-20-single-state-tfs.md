## Statement

Despite the strong transcriptomic overlap of TEXterm and TRM cells, Taiji PageRank predicts 34 TEXterm single-state TFs (e.g. ZSCAN20, JDP2, ZFP324, IRF8), 20 TRM single-state TFs (e.g. FOSB, KLF6, JUNB, ATF4), and 30 multi-state TFs active in both states (e.g. HIC1, GFI1, PRDM1, BHLHE40, NR4A2).

Fig. 1f–h bubble plots; Extended Data Fig. 2a (blue boxes); Supplementary Table 3.
