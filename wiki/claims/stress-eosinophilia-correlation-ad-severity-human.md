## Statement

In a retrospective cohort of 51 AD patients, the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) score correlates strongly and positively with both clinical disease severity (SCORAD r=0.665; NRS r=0.637; both P<0.001) and with blood (r=0.439, P=0.004) and skin (MBP-IHC) eosinophil counts, whereas neutrophils, basophils, monocytes, T/B cells and skin mast cells are unaffected. The correlation is specific to eosinophils.
