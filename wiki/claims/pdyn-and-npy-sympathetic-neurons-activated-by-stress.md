## Statement

Stress activates two molecularly distinct subsets of paravertebral noradrenergic sympathetic neurons defined by Pdyn (Prodynorphin) and Npy (Neuropeptide Y) expression. Together they account for >50% of stress-activated sympathetic neurons in stellate and T2 ganglia. Pdyn is also expressed in human stellate sympathetic ganglia.
