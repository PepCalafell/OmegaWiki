## Statement

The temporal pattern of hypoxia, independent of mean oxygen level, shapes immunity: intermittent (cycling) hypoxia tends to drive stronger inflammatory responses — including NF-κB activation — than sustained hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Ryan et al. *Circulation* 2005; Michiels et al. on cycling hypoxia). Concept: [[concepts/intermittent-versus-sustained-hypoxia-inflammation]].

## Conditions and scope

Pattern (frequency/amplitude of reoxygenation) is the salient variable; pathological niches skew toward chaotic intermittent hypoxia.

None within scope.
