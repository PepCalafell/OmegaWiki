## Statement

A subset of TYK2-mediated transcriptional effects in homeostatic immune cells persists in the catalytically-inactive Tyk2-K923E (kinase-dead) mutant while requiring full TYK2 protein for restoration. The kinase-dependent and kinase-independent gene sets diverge most clearly on IL-12-regulated genes (Phb, Eftud2, Pold2, Bnip1, Mcm7, Sec23b). This is direct evidence that TYK2 carries scaffolding / non-catalytic regulatory functions beyond its phosphotransferase activity, with implications for the predicted effects of catalytic vs allosteric TYK2 inhibitors.
