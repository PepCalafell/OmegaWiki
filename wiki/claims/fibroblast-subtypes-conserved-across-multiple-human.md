## Statement

Several skin fibroblast subtypes — F2 universal, F3 FRC-like, F6 inflammatory myofibroblast, F7 myofibroblast — are conserved across human tissues; F2/3 perivascular and F5 NGFR+ (nerve-associated) are proposed as additional cross-tissue populations.

## Evidence summary

Cross-tissue integration of ~5.8M cells (~1M fibroblasts) and marker-based assessment (Fig. 6).

## Conditions and scope

Semi-supervised integration; differing gene panels across datasets.

## Counter-evidence

Authors note integration may underestimate tissue-specific differences.

## Open questions
