## Statement

In large-scale COVID-19 drug-repurposing screens, only about 1% of promising candidates targeted a viral protein; the majority were drugs that modulated human proteins not directly involved in SARS-CoV-2 activity — a concrete instance of network-drug logic.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "only 1% of promising candidates targeted a viral protein — the majority were drugs that modulated human proteins not directly involved in SARS-CoV-2 activity." (p.3–4) Cites Gysi et al., arXiv:2004.07229 (2020).

## Conditions and scope

Computational/large-scale repurposing screen results.

## Counter-evidence

Direct antiviral targeting remains a valid and sometimes superior strategy.
