## Statement

During E11 oligodendrogenesis, demethylation events at neighbouring VMRs couple bidirectionally to gene expression — Nrg3 (oligodendrocyte marker) is activated upon methylation loss, while Pdgfra (progenitor marker) is silenced upon methylation loss — illustrating regulatory diversity beyond the canonical "methylation = silencing" rule.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 3b.

## Conditions and scope

E11 mouse embryo; specific to two marker genes; mechanism not resolved.

## Counter-evidence

Mechanism (whether the same demethylation drives both events or different VMRs) not disentangled.

## Open questions
