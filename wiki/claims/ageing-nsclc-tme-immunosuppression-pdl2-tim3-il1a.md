## Statement

In LUAD, biopsies from patients >70 show elevated PD-L2 and TIM3, increased immunosuppressive TAMs, reduced TANs, naïve/memory-skewed CD8 states, and IL-1α-rich tumour-induced myelopoiesis. This correlates with reduced overall survival in early-stage disease but not advanced disease.

## Evidence summary

Age-stratified bulk and single-cell profiling.

## Conditions and scope

Patients >70 with early-stage LUAD.

## Counter-evidence

NSCLC incidence paradoxically decreases above age 80, suggesting non-monotonic effects.

## Open questions
