## Statement

In a HNSCC CosMx protein-assay cohort, the Novae spatial domain D497 — enriched in FOXP3+ regulatory T cells and stromal/endothelial cells — is significantly less abundant in MGC-positive patients (two-tailed t-test P = 0.0028), suggesting reduced immunosuppressive activity in MGC-positive tumours.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 5b top panel; cell-type composition in Fig. 5c.

## Conditions and scope

n=8 patient cohort; correlational with MGC stratification.

## Counter-evidence

Small cohort; biological mechanism (causal vs reactive) not established.

— none yet.

## Open questions

- Whether D497 reduction is a cause or consequence of the favourable MGC-associated prognosis.
- Validation in larger HNSCC cohorts (TCGA-HNSC).
