## Statement

Irradiated Ccl2-/- BM chimeras (CCL2 absent in all myeloid cells including resident IMs) had ~7.0-fold lower metastatic burden than busulfan-conditioned Ccl2-/- chimeras (which retain CCL2-producing host IMs), which grew tumors comparably to WT.

## Evidence summary

Congenic CD45.1/CD45.2 tracking confirms IMs host-derived (CCL2+) after busulfan vs donor-derived after irradiation (Fig. 4f,g). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 melanoma; busulfan spares long-lived resident IMs.
