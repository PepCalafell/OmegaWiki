## Statement

O-GlcNAcylation is dynamically regulated along the cavity-macrophage maturation trajectory — from bone-marrow-derived monocytes through small (SCM) and converting (CCM) cavity macrophages to mature large cavity macrophages (LCMs).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/glcnacylation-shapes-macrophage-tissue-residency-alternative]] (commentary on Heieis et al.). Positions [[foundations/glcnacylation]] as a stage-dependent modification across the [[foundations/large-cavity-macrophage-lcm-peritoneal]] maturation hierarchy.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse peritoneal cavity macrophage differentiation. Commentary-level, qualitative (no quantitative dynamics reported in the secondary source).
