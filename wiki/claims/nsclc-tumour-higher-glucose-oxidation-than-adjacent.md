## Statement

Direct in vivo [13C]-glucose isotope tracing in patients with NSCLC shows that tumour tissue exhibits higher glucose oxidation (greater labelling of TCA intermediates) than adjacent normal lung tissue, revising the simple "Warburg-only" view of tumour metabolism.

## Evidence summary

In-vivo tracer experiments in surgical NSCLC patients.

## Conditions and scope

NSCLC; not universal across tumour types.

## Counter-evidence

Some NSCLC tumours retain a glycolysis-dominant phenotype; intra-tumour heterogeneity.
