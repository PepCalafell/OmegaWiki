## Statement

F3 FRC-like skin fibroblasts share the transcriptional program of lymphoid-organ fibroblastic reticular cells, including immune-cell chemoattraction, survival support, and MHC-II antigen presentation.

## Evidence summary

Marker-gene expression (Fig. 1c) and comparison to FRC signatures (Extended Data Fig. 1h).

## Conditions and scope

Transcriptomic resemblance; functional FRC activity inferred, not proven.

## Counter-evidence

None; functional validation pending.

## Open questions
