## Statement

CD44 is a dominant, melanoma-enriched receptor whose interactions with MMP9, FN1 and FGF2 are validated at nano-scale by Proximity Ligation Assay; CD44-FGF2 is proposed as a candidate therapeutic target, and the implicated LR pairs carry prognostic value in TCGA.

## Evidence summary

Computational nomination → PLA protein-proximity validation (three pairs) → TCGA prognostic association. (p.12)

## Conditions and scope

Melanoma; "candidate" target — no functional/therapeutic perturbation performed.

## Counter-evidence

PLA proximity ≠ proof of functional/druggable interaction; therapeutic value untested in vivo.

## Open questions

Whether CD44-FGF2 disruption blocks melanoma invasion in functional models.
