## Statement

An early-enriched, declining population of vWF-expressing megakaryocyte-biased HSCs underlies the early megakaryocyte skew, scoring higher in an alternative-megakaryocyte / vWF+ P-HSC transcriptional signature during the early phase.

## Evidence summary

Compositional and signature-score evidence (max p=0.00735), consistent with the known platelet-primed HSC pathway.

## Conditions and scope

Signature-based and computational; not clonal proof of a fast pathway in this dataset.

## Counter-evidence

vWF marks graded bias, not committed fate; early enrichment may be partly induction-driven.

## Open questions
