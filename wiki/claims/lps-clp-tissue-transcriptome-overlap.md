## Statement

Sublethal LPS and CLP sepsis induce highly overlapping organ-level gene expression changes, with overlap rising with CLP severity, validating LPS as a tractable proxy for the early organ-wide response to bacterial sepsis.

## Conditions and scope

Acute phase (0.25–1 d) only; late-phase dynamics may diverge.
