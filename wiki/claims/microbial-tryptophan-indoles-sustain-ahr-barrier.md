## Statement

Commensal microbial metabolism of dietary tryptophan (via bacterial tryptophanase and related enzymes) produces indole derivatives (IAA, I3A, tryptamine, I3S) that activate host AHR, sustaining IL-22 / ILC3 / Treg-driven barrier homeostasis; loss of microbiota-derived AHR agonists (CARD9 mutations, antibiotic dysbiosis) impairs barrier function.

## Evidence summary

Zelante et al. 2013 Cell; Lamas et al. 2016 Nat Med; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
