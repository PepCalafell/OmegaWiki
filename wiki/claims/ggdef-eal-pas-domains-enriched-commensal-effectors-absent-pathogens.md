## Statement

Commensal effectors are enriched for cyclic-di-GMP-related (GGDEF, EAL) and PAS sensor
domains absent from pathogen effectors, implicating c-di-GMP signalling in interkingdom
communication.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.443): GGDEF (58), EAL (50), PAS-fold
(32) among the most common domains; GGDEF/EAL "none of which was found in pathogen
effectors." Two domains acting on the same second messenger occur at high frequency.

## Conditions and scope

Domain annotation only; functional activity not directly assayed here.

## Counter-evidence

860 effectors had no identifiable domain.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[commensal-pathogen-effector-divergence]]. Relates to [[cyclic-di-gmp]].

## Open questions

Whether c-di-GMP outputs act on the host or on bacterial behaviour during cohabitation.
