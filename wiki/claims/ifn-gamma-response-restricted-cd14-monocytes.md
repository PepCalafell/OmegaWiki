## Statement

IFN-γ (IFN-II) transcriptomic responses among circulating immune cells are largely restricted to CD14 monocytes and B cells, with NK cells and most T-cell subsets unresponsive, tracking low IFNGR2 expression.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.6). See [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]], [[core-versus-subset-specific-isg-programs]].

## Conditions and scope

Circulating immune subsets; resting cells (not pre-activated T cells).

## Counter-evidence

Naïve/memory B cells responded strongly (353/349 DEGs); plasma cells did not (low IFNGR1/2).

## Open questions

Whether activated/effector T cells upregulate IFNGR2 and become IFN-γ responsive.
