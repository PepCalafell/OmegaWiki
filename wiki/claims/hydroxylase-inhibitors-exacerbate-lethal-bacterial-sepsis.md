## Statement

The anti-inflammatory benefit of HIF hydroxylase inhibition is context-dependent: the same drugs protect against sterile LPS-induced sepsis yet worsen survival in live bacterial (caecal ligation and puncture) sepsis.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (cites Hams et al. 2011). Bounds the therapeutic claim [[claims/pharmacological-hydroxylase-inhibition-broadly-anti-inflammatory]].

## Conditions and scope

Distinguishes sterile inflammation (benefit) from active bacterial infection (harm), where HIF-boosted host responses may be maladaptive.

## Counter-evidence

This is itself the counter-context to the broad anti-inflammatory claim.
