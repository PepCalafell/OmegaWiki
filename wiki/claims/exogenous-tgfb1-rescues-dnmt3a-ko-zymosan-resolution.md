## Statement

The defective resolution in H-DNMT3A-KO mice in zymosan peritonitis is downstream of TGF-β1 — exogenous TGF-β1 rescues the phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Functional rescue with i.p. TGF-β1 normalizes PMN counts and increases efferocytosis index in DNMT3A-KO mice. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

Zymosan peritonitis only; functional rescue.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
