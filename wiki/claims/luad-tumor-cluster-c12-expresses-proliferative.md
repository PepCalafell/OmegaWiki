## Statement

A second tumour-enriched cluster (C12) within the same LUAD-9 tumour expresses proliferation-associated genes (MKI67, FGFR1, FGFR2, EZH2) and is spatially segregated from the hypoxia/EMT/chemokine cluster C0.

## Evidence summary

Differential expression (Fig. 5c) and signature-score spatial maps (Fig. 5d, Extended Data Fig. 6a,e). Anticorrelation gradient observed across multiple LUAD samples.

## Open questions

- Are C0 and C12 distinct subclones or two transcriptional states of the same clonal population?
