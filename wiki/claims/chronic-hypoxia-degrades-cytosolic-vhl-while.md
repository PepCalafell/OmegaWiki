## Statement

Chronic hypoxia (1% O₂) drives degradation of the bulk cytosolic VHL pool while a residual fraction translocates into mitochondria, establishing a non-canonical mitochondrial VHL pool.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Li et al. 2026, Fig. 1B–C, S1E–F). Mitochondrial/cytosolic fractionation (VDAC1/GAPDH markers) and confocal colocalization with MitoTracker.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in transformed and non-transformed human cell lines under prolonged hypoxia; basal mitochondrial VHL exists at much lower levels in normoxia (prior reports).

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope; two prior studies reported low-level mitochondrial VHL in non-hypoxic conditions (cited).

Supports [[concepts/mitochondrial-vhl-noncanonical-hypoxia-function]].
