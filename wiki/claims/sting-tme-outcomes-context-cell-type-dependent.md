## Statement

Activation of STING in the tumor microenvironment produces qualitatively different — sometimes opposite — functional outcomes depending on which cell type receives the signal, the duration of activation, and whether engagement is intrinsic (cell-autonomous dsDNA) or extrinsic (paracrine cGAMP).

## Evidence summary

Tumor cells: silencing or chronic NF-κB-driven pro-tumor inflammation. Myeloid/DCs: M1 polarization, antigen cross-presentation, TLS formation. T cells: apoptosis and impaired proliferation. NK cells: cytotoxicity enhancement. ECs: vascular normalization, T/NK trafficking. CAFs: CXCR3 chemokine production.

## Conditions and scope

The "net" therapeutic effect of a STING agonist depends entirely on which TME compartment dominates the response, and is therefore deeply tumor-type-, dose-, and delivery-method-dependent.
