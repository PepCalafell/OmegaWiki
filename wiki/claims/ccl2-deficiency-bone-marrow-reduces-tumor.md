## Statement

BM chimeric Ccl2-/- mice showed ~10.8-fold reduction in lung tumor burden and ~2.7-fold decrease in Ly6C+CD11b+ recMac accumulation versus WT BM chimeras.

## Evidence summary

Surface metastasis and recMac quantification at day 16 (Fig. 4e; P<0.0001 metastases, P=0.0097 recMacs). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 melanoma; lethal irradiation + Ccl2-/- BM (endothelial CCL2 preserved).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded at ingest.
