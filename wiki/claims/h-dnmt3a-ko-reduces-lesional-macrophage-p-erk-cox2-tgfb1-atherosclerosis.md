## Statement

The DNMT3A-COX2-TGF-β1 pathway operates in atherosclerotic plaque macrophages in vivo; haematopoietic DNMT3A loss specifically reduces the pathway without affecting global atherogenesis parameters.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence of aortic root sections + systemic metabolic readouts. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

Ldlr-/- WTD atherosclerosis model; aortic root only.
