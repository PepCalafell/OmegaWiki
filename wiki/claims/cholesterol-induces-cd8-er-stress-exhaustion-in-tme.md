## Statement

Tumour-microenvironment-derived cholesterol uptake by CD8⁺ T cells induces ER stress, which couples to exhaustion programs and impairs antitumor function; elevated plasma cholesterol exacerbates the effect systemically.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from primary studies (Ma et al. and related works).

## Conditions and scope

- Solid tumours with lipid-rich TMEs; hypercholesterolemic patients.

## Counter-evidence

- Some tumours show CD8-cholesterol uptake without exhaustion if XBP1 axis is blocked.

## Open questions
