## Statement

The per-metacell mcDiv divergence score increases as a metacell becomes more heterogeneous, exhibiting a strong negative correlation with ground-truth metacell purity (Spearman ρ = −0.948 for MetaCell on semi-synthetic data).

## Evidence summary

Validated on a scDesign3-simulated semi-synthetic dataset (five major cell types, true γ* = 50) where each cell's ground-truth biological state, and hence metacell purity, is known. The strong negative correlation held across MetaCell, SEACells, SuperCell, and MetaQ partitions.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated on semi-synthetic data; relies on the validity of the biological/technical variation decomposition used to simulate the data.

## Counter-evidence

None reported in the paper.

## Open questions

Whether the correlation strength is comparable on real datasets, where ground-truth purity must be approximated from fine-grained annotations.
