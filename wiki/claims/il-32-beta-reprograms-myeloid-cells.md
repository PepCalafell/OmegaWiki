## Statement

IL-32-β reprograms myeloid cells (monocytes, cDCs) from a Th1/antiviral chemokine profile (CXCL9/10/11, IL-18 down, median log2FC ~-3.8) to a neutrophil-recruiting profile (CXCL1/2/3/5/8, IL-1α/β up, median log2FC ~+5) — the signature human-specific finding.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: mechanistic.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.

None within the paper's scope.
