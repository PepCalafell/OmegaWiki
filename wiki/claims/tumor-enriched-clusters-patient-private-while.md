## Statement

In spatial transcriptomics of NSCLC, tumour-enriched clusters are largely patient-private (reflecting patient-level cancer-cell identity) while immune/stromal TME-enriched clusters are largely shared across patients.

## Open questions

- Does the pattern persist in larger cohorts and across other tumour types?
- Are patient-private tumour clusters explained by genetic vs epigenetic clonal differences?
