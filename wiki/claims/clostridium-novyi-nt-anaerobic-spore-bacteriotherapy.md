## Statement

Clostridium novyi-NT, an attenuated obligate-anaerobic bacterium, selectively germinates in hypoxic/necrotic tumor cores when delivered as spores. In mouse models of CRC, biliary, melanoma and squamous cell carcinoma, it significantly enhances radiotherapy efficacy. A phase 1 trial (NCT01924689) in solid tumor malignancies has been completed.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxic/necrotic-core-dependent; manufactured for clinical use.

## Counter-evidence

Bacterial therapy can recruit pro-tumor neutrophils (Salmonella); off-target inflammation risks.
