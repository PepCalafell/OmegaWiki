## Statement

In regulatory T cells and B cells, AHR activated by Kyn binds the TET2 promoter and induces TET2. TET2 then demethylates the NT5E (CD73) promoter, increasing extracellular adenosine production (AMP → adenosine) and reinforcing immune tolerance. In systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), reduced AHR drives NT5E promoter hypermethylation, lower CD73, and diminished anti-inflammatory adenosine.

Treg / B cell context. The Kyn-AHR-TET2 module connects tryptophan metabolism to adenosine immunosuppression — relevant to tumour immune evasion as well.
