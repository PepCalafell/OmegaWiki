## Statement

Severe hypoxia activates PERK via BIP release, leading to PERK autophosphorylation, eIF2α phosphorylation and global translation arrest; selective genes (CA9, ATF4, CHOP) are preferentially translated, supporting stress adaptation and increasing tolerance to extreme hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Severe hypoxia (<0.2% O2).

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
