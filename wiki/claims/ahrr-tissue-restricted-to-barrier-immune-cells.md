## Statement

AHRR's tissue distribution is restricted to immune cells of barrier organs (skin, intestine), where it likely restrains AHR overactivation. Ahrr knockout phenocopies Ahr deletion in colitis (aggravation) but diverges in LPS sepsis (Ahrr loss is protective, whereas Ahr loss is sensitising), pointing to cell-type-specific AHR/AHRR balance as the determinant of inflammatory tone.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews Brandstätter et al. (ref 93).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse genetics; human tissue distribution of AHRR is broadly consistent but exact cell-type resolution differs.

## Counter-evidence

AHRR is hypomethylated in lung tissue exposed to tobacco smoke — implying inducible AHRR expression beyond barrier immune cells; see [[claims/ahrr-hypomethylation-smoking-lung-cancer-risk]].
