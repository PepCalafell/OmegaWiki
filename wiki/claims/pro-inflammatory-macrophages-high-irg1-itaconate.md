## Statement

Macrophage IRG1/itaconate and G6PD activity are inversely coupled across polarization states: pro-inflammatory macrophages are IRG1/itaconate-high and G6PD-low with tumor-suppressive secretome, whereas anti-inflammatory macrophages are IRG1/itaconate-low and G6PD-high with tumor-supportive secretome.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1469) — mechanistic/correlational evidence.
  - Quote: "LPS/IFNγ-stimulated pro-inflammatory macrophages exhibited high IRG1 expression and itaconate production, reduced G6PD activity, and CM that suppressed tumor cell proliferation ... IL-4-stimulated anti-inflammatory macrophages displayed lower IRG1/itaconate levels, elevated G6PD activity, and a tumor-supportive CM."

## Conditions and scope

Human PBMC-derived macrophages; links polarization state to a shared IRG1–G6PD metabolic axis.
