## Statement
Kinase-footprint inference nominates IRAK1 and IRAK4 (with GAK) as upregulated kinases of the M2a state and CAMKK2 and GAK as upregulated kinases of the M2c state — candidate novel regulators of immunosuppressive macrophages.

## Evidence summary
- "Analysis of phosphoresidues upregulated in the M2a state suggested a high activity of interleukin 1 receptor-associated kinase (IRAK) 1 and IRAK4 kinases ... a higher activity of the LRRK2 and cyclin G associated kinase (GAK) kinases in M2a macrophages" (p.12).
- "In M2c macrophages, the Kinase Library analysis suggested upregulation of the GAK and calcium/calmodulin dependent protein kinase kinase 2 (CAMKK2) kinases ... High expression levels of CAMKK2 were previously reported in TAMs and ... support tumor growth" (p.12).

## Conditions and scope
Inferred activities (not perturbation-validated). The authors note IRAK1/4 could act via the negative regulator IRAK-M, complicating interpretation.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper, but inference-level evidence with acknowledged ambiguity in the IRAK axis.

## Open questions
Do IRAK1/4, CAMKK2, or GAK causally sustain immunosuppressive macrophage states, and via which adaptors?
