## Statement

Itaconate, produced by IRG1/ACOD1 in activated macrophages, non-competitively inhibits SDH (complex II) to limit succinate-driven inflammation, electrophilically modifies KEAP1 to activate NRF2, and exerts antifibrotic effects in lung: AMs lacking itaconate are more profibrotic, IPF patient AMs have reduced IRG1/itaconate, and exogenous itaconate or 4-OI is therapeutic preclinically.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] integrating Lampropoulou 2016, Mills 2018, and Ogger 2020 (IPF AMs).

## Conditions and scope

- Effects are dose-dependent; high itaconate may suppress beneficial inflammation.
- Itaconate has off-target electrophilic effects (TET2 inhibition; see [[foundations/4-octyl-itaconate-tet2-inhibitor]]) — therapeutic specificity not fully mapped.

## Counter-evidence

- Some studies report itaconate-independent effects of IRG1.
