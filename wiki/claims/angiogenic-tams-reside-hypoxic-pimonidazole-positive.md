## Statement

Only angiogenic (CX3CR1-MHC-II-) TAMs, not MHC-II+ TAMs, stain positive for the hypoxia probe pimonidazole, showing angiogenic TAMs reside in hypoxic niches and MHC-II+ TAMs in normoxic areas.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2068-2070) — mechanistic evidence. Pimonidazole intracellular staining across 4T1, LLC, MC38 (n=6); immunofluorescence (F4/80, CD74, hypoxia probe) confirms spatial segregation.
  - Quote: "only the angiogenic (CX3CR1MHC-II) TAMs, not the MHC-II+ TAMs, stained positively with the hypoxia probe, indicating that angiogenic TAMs reside in hypoxic niches, whereas MHC-II+ TAMs are found in normoxic areas"

## Conditions and scope

Three mouse tumor models; probe-based hypoxia readout.
