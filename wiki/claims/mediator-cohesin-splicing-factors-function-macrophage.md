## Statement
Chromatin- and RNA-associated machineries beyond classical transcription factors — the mediator complex subunits MED8 and MED14, the cohesin subunit SMC1A, and the splicing factors SF3B1 and SFPQ — are functional regulators of the macrophage immune response, identified through their knockout transcriptional signatures.

## Evidence summary
Cross-prediction grouping plus shared differential gene sets implicated these regulators ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figures 5E-I, 6; Smc1a-Myd88 similarity consistent with a prior cohesin-TLR report).

## Conditions and scope
Macrophage cell line; functional roles inferred from KO transcriptional effects.

## Counter-evidence
Roles are inferred from transcriptional signatures, not direct biochemical assays here.
