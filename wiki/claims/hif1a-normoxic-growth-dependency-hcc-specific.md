## Statement

The requirement for HIF-1α under normoxia is cell-type-specific: hepatocellular carcinoma (Huh7) cells depend on HIF-1α for normoxic growth and survival, whereas cervical carcinoma (HeLa) cells do not — both, however, require HIF-1α to adapt to hypoxia.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3142) — side-by-side knockout comparison.
  - Quote: "Lack of HIF-1α expression increased the rate of cell death and inhibited cell proliferation in both cell lines under mild hypoxic conditions ... However, under normoxia, only the hepatoma-derived Huh7 cells exhibited significant sensitivity to the absence of HIF-1α ... indicating that there is a cell-specific HIF-1α role even in the presence of abundant oxygenation."

## Conditions and scope

Two cell lines only (Huh7, HeLa); HepG2 supports the HCC-specific normoxic metabolic role but growth phenotype was assayed primarily in Huh7 vs HeLa.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. Cell-type specificity is based on two lineages; broader panel untested.
