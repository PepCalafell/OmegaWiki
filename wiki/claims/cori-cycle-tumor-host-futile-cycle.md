## Statement

Cancer rewires host metabolism to favour energetically expensive futile cycles, prominently the Cori cycle in which lactate produced by the tumour is recycled to glucose in the liver at the expense of host ATP. This contributes to host hypermetabolism and CAC.

## Evidence summary

Review synthesis based on tracer studies and indirect calorimetry; quantitative human Cori-cycle flux remains rare.

## Conditions and scope

Mechanistically supported; quantitative contribution to REE elevation per patient is uncertain.

## Counter-evidence

Some tumours use lactate directly to fuel TCA (Faubert), so a fraction of tumour-released lactate may not re-enter the Cori cycle.
