## Statement

VHL enters mitochondria through the TOM complex — particularly TOM22 — which recognizes positively charged residues in the VHL α-domain helices; mutating these residues (VHL M2) abolishes import and the hypoxic growth-promoting function.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 2B–I, S3). Domain mapping, charge mutants, co-IP with TOM20/22/70, in vitro TOM22-N binding, TOM22 gain/loss.

## Conditions and scope

Import competes with cytosolic VBC complex assembly; K171R/K196R (degradation-resistant) increases mitochondrial VHL — ubiquitination sites overlap TOM22-binding motifs.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
