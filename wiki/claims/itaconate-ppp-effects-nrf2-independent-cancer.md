## Statement

The itaconate/Octyl itaconate effect on PPP metabolism and the oxidative-stress response is cell-type-specific: NRF2-independent (but G6PD-dependent) in cancer cells, and NRF2-dependent in macrophages, likely reflecting differing baseline inflammatory/oxidative states.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1473) — mechanistic evidence.
  - Quote: "itaconate/Octyl Ita modulates PPP metabolism and oxidative stress response in cancer cells in an NRF2-independent manner, while the effects of itaconate/Octyl Ita in macrophages are NRF2-dependent."

## Conditions and scope

A549 vs human macrophages; authors attribute the divergence to inflammatory/oxidative context (per Swain et al.).
