## Statement

Restoring H3K27ac with the pan-HDAC inhibitor panobinostat rescues the hypoxia-repressed microglial marker Tmem119 and blocks the hypoxia-induced upregulation of Lgals3 (mRNA and protein), causally implicating HDAC-mediated deacetylation in the hypoxic marker dysregulation.

## Evidence summary

Pharmacologic rescue with RNA-seq confirmation (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.14).

> "a pan-HDAC inhibitor, panobinostat, rescued expression of the hypoxia-repressed microglial marker Tmem119. Conversely, panobinostat impaired hypoxia-induced upregulation of Lgals3 mRNA and protein ... Panobinostat clearly reversed hypoxia-induced expression of several GAM-associated genes, including lipid storage regulators like Lgals3, Plin3, and F10"

## Conditions and scope

BV2 and BMDM; in vitro hypoxia + 10 nM panobinostat.

## Counter-evidence

Pan-HDAC inhibition is non-specific; the responsible isoform and direct targets are not identified.

## Open questions

Whether isoform-selective HDAC inhibition reproduces the marker rescue.
