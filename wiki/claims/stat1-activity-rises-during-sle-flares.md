## Statement

STAT1 and SP1 activities track SLE disease dynamics: STAT1 activity is elevated during flares (especially in CD8 T cells), whereas SP1 activity is more prominent in myeloid populations in the absence of flares — linking the two regulators to disease exacerbation state.

## Evidence summary

Flare/non-flare comparison on the Perez et al. dataset (Fig. 2f; p.637), two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test, FDR-adjusted P<0.05.

## Conditions and scope

SLE flare dynamics; single external cohort; correlative.

## Counter-evidence

Single cohort; inferred TF activity; needs prospective validation.
