## Statement

EZH2 and DNMT inhibition reinvigorate STING and restore MHC-I antigen presentation in melanoma, TNBC, and SCLC.

## Evidence summary

Falahat 2021/2023 (melanoma, decitabine); Kitajima 2019 (KRAS-LKB1 NSCLC, EZH2); Mahadevan 2021 Cancer Discov (SCLC neuroendocrine, EZH2). Reversal of cGAS/STING promoter hypermethylation restores antigen presentation and ICB responsiveness.

## Conditions and scope

Tumors with epigenetic STING silencing; combination with STING agonism, DNA-damaging therapy, or ADCs proposed.
