## Statement

Depletion of tissue-resident macrophages (CD169-DTR + intranasal DT, days 0 and 3) before KP NSCLC tumour engraftment reduces tumour burden — both average tumour area per tumour (P=0.0028) and tumour area per lung area (P=0.0345) — without changing the number of tumours per lobe (P=0.46). TRM-depleted tumours show increased apoptotic (CC3⁺) and slow-cycling (p27⁺) cells.

## Evidence summary

- n=9 WT + DT, n=10 CD169-DTR + DT
- Tumour area metrics significantly reduced
- Number of tumours unchanged → seeding not the mechanism (consistent with the 24-h seeding control)
- CC3⁺ apoptosis ↑, p27⁺ slow-cycling ↑ in TRM-depleted tumours
- Day 30 endpoint

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC, day 30 endpoint
- Pre-tumour TRM depletion (DT days 0 and 3, KP injection day 0)
- Independent of tumour seeding (verified by 24-h control)

## Counter-evidence

- Late-stage tumour depletion (day 12-15 onwards) has NO effect (separate claim)
- The mechanism integrates EMT reduction, Treg loss, and CD8 effector recovery — apportioning relative contributions remains an open question
- Effect size moderate; complete tumour ablation not achieved

## Open questions

- Whether combining TRM depletion with checkpoint blockade further reduces burden
