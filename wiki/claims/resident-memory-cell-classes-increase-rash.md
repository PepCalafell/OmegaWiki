## Statement

Skin-resident memory T cell classes increased proportionally in rash-affected versus healthy skin: Trm1 by 71.4% (6.3%→10.7%) and Trm2 by 108.7% (4.3%→9.0%) of CD45+ cells.

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]; Trm markers [[foundations/itgae-cd103-integrin-trm-marker]]. Supports [[concepts/treg-trm-expansion-cd8-exhaustion-chronic]]; relates to [[concepts/tissue-resident-memory-cd8-t-cell-trm]].
