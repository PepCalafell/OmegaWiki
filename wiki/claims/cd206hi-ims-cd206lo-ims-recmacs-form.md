## Statement

In lung cancer, tissue-resident CD206hi and CD206lo interstitial macrophage (IM) subsets and Ly6c2+Fn1+Vcan+ recruited macrophages (recMacs) constitute a functional division of labor governing the balance between tumor-suppressive and tumor-promoting macrophage programs.

## Evidence summary

scRNA-seq + spatial transcriptomics + genetic depletion across three lung cancer models reveal opposing functions of CD206hi IMs (antitumor) vs CD206lo IMs and recMacs (protumor). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung cancer (B16F10 melanoma metastasis, KPAR1.3 and AgerCreERT2KP adenocarcinoma).
