## Statement

High cGAS pathway activation co-occurs with broadly reduced transcription of protein-coding genes across cancers, including RNA-processing and DNA-repair genes, and increased type-2 immunity genes.

## Evidence summary

834 genes negatively vs 6 positively associated with cGAS activation pan-cancer; pathway enrichment implicated RNA processing and DNA repair among the downregulated set.

## Conditions and scope

Correlational pan-cancer association; interpreted via prior literature on DNA-damage-induced transcriptional stress.

## Counter-evidence

No causal test; the asymmetry could reflect confounding by tumor genomic state.

## Open questions

Is the transcriptional suppression a cause or consequence of cGAS-activating DNA damage?
