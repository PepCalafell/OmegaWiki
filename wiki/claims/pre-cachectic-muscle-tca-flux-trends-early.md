## Statement

Pre-cachectic mice already show flux-modelling trends in the same direction as Cax mice for muscle TCA flux acceleration — suggesting that glucose hypermetabolism in muscle precedes overt body-weight loss and could potentially serve as a pre-cachexia biomarker.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "The Pre-cax state showed trends towards increased flux in all above-mentioned reactions despite unchanged body weight and composition, indicating that glucose hypermetabolism in muscle may be an early event contributing to cachexia."

## Conditions and scope

Pre-cax statistical underpowering reduces confidence; trends rather than significant differences.

## Counter-evidence

Trends could be inflated by interpretation; full Cax flux changes are the statistically anchored finding.
