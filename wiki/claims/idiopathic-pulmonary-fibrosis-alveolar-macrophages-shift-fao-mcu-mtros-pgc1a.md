## Statement

In IPF and bleomycin lung-fibrosis models, alveolar macrophages first upregulate glycolysis and then commit to FAO via MCU- and mtROS-driven PGC-1α expression. Blocking this axis (e.g. MCU inhibition) reverses AMs to glycolysis and protects from fibrosis — making macrophage metabolic state a candidate therapeutic node.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Gu 2019 and Cui 2019.

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse models and IPF patient AM observation; clinical trials targeting macrophage metabolism in IPF still negative (NAC, metformin).

## Counter-evidence

- Clinical metformin and N-acetylcysteine trials in IPF showed no benefit despite preclinical signal, indicating either translational gap or off-target macrophage effects.
