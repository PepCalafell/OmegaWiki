## Statement

In the inflamed-eosinophil context, Adrb2 agonism is proinflammatory — promoting degranulation rather than the canonical Gs-cAMP anti-inflammatory effect described in cytokine-primed eosinophils. Context-dependent Adrb2 signalling explains the long-standing clinical paradox around β2-agonists and eosinophilic inflammation.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], Fig. 5H–I.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse blood eosinophils ex vivo; human eosinophil response not tested.

## Counter-evidence

Pedersen 1993: salmeterol *reduced* serum ECP/Epx in asthma patients (cytokine-primed eosinophil context). The paper frames this as a context-dependent (Gs-cAMP vs β-arrestin-2) branching.
