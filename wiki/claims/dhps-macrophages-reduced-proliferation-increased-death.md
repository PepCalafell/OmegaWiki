## Statement

DHPS-deficient macrophages have a reduced capacity for proliferation coupled with increased apoptosis, explaining poor tissue persistence and continual turnover.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 2h,i; Extended Data Fig. 5h-k).

## Conditions and scope

Steady state and IL-4c-stimulated self-renewal contexts.

## Counter-evidence

CSF1R signalling (a survival factor) was unchanged, so the survival defect is downstream/independent of CSF1R.
