## Statement

Arnt (HIF-1β) knockout Panc02 tumors are suppressed by systemic hypoxia to the same extent as wild-type, and HIF-target activation does not correlate with fitness in GENEVA, so tumor-intrinsic HIF stabilization is not required for the anti-tumor effect.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.7): "indicating that HIF-dependent transcription in cancer cells is not necessary for tumor suppression in hypoxia" — Single-clone Arnt KO (Fig.S2e-f); Fig.3i no WT-vs-KO difference; Fig.3j no HIF-target/fitness association. (type: mechanistic, strength: strong)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
