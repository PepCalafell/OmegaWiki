## Statement

The conserved transcriptional signatures distinguishing atopic dermatitis from psoriasis were found specifically within CD69+CD103+ skin-resident memory T (Trm) cells, identifying this population as the locus of disease-discriminating molecular dysregulation that bulk profiling had obscured.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Discussion of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]; markers [[foundations/itgae-cd103-integrin-trm-marker]]. Supports [[concepts/trm1-th2-th17-molecular-classification-inflammatory]] and [[concepts/tissue-resident-memory-cd8-t-cell-trm]].

## Conditions and scope

Author interpretation explaining why prior bulk studies showed poor reproducibility (cell-type conflation).

## Counter-evidence

Restricted to the populations adequately sampled; APCs were underpowered.
