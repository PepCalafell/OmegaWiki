## Statement

The clinical landscape now contains both AHR agonists (tapinarof, FDA-approved for adult plaque psoriasis) and AHR antagonists (IK-175, BAY-2416964 in solid-tumour trials). This validates AHR as a tractable drug target and motivates extending AHR-directed approaches to infectious disease (e.g., AHR antagonism for SARS-CoV-2, ZIKV; AHR agonism for TB host-directed therapy).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]]. Companion 2025 NRDD review ([[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]) details PSOARING/ADORING trial outcomes and antagonist pipelines.

## Conditions and scope

Tapinarof is a topical formulation (limits to skin disease); systemic AHR therapeutic dosing remains preclinical.
