## Statement

In a precancerous treatment window in NNK-induced Gprc5a−/− mouse LUAD, IL-1β neutralization outperforms PD-1 blockade as monotherapy for suppressing lung adenoma and adenocarcinoma volumes.

## Linked ideas

- This claim — combined with the syngeneic-LUAD null result (no effect of anti-IL-1β on established tumors) — anchors the stage-specific therapeutic concept [[concepts/il1b-precancer-interception-luad]].
