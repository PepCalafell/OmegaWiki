## Statement

Higher eFibro_CTHRC1 signature scores are significantly associated with worse overall survival in TCGA kidney renal clear cell carcinoma (P=0.00523) and bladder urothelial carcinoma (P=0.00568), consistent with prior reports for FAP+ and LRRC15+ CAFs.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "Higher expression of eFibro_CTHRC1 signature genes was correlated with worse clinical outcomes... kidney renal clear cell carcinoma (log-rank test, P = 0.00523) and bladder urothelial carcinoma (log-rank test, P = 0.00568)" (Han 2025, Fig. 3h).

## Linked ideas

- CTHRC1 signature as pan-cancer prognostic biomarker complementary to LRRC15/FAP.
