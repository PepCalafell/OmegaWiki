## Statement

An average of 51 DEGs per cytokine × cell-type combination (range 0–1,510) were identified, with the largest response seen in NK cells stimulated with IL-18 (>1,000 genes) and very small or null responses for many cytokine × cell-type combinations.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

FDR-adjusted P<0.05, |log2FC|>0.25; mouse lymph node, 4 h.
