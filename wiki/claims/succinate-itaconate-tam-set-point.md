## Statement

Within TAMs, succinate and itaconate exert opposing immunometabolic effects (pro-inflammatory HIF-1α/IL-1β axis vs anti-inflammatory KEAP1/NRF2 axis), and their balance constitutes a tunable metabolic set point for therapeutic reprogramming.
