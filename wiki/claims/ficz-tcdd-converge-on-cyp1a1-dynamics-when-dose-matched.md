## Statement

When the dose and timing of FICZ are titrated to deliver TCDD-equivalent CYP1A1 induction, FICZ and TCDD elicit the same AHR transcriptional dynamics and downstream responses, suggesting that apparent ligand-specific divergence between FICZ and TCDD on CD4+ T cell differentiation reflects differences in occupancy/duration rather than intrinsic ligand bias.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews a follow-up to the FICZ-vs-TCDD CD4+ T cell discrepancy showing dose/timing equivalence collapses the opposition.

## Conditions and scope

Relevant when high-affinity AHR ligands fully occupy the primary PAS-B pocket. Does not apply to partial or secondary-pocket-occupying ligands (e.g. [[foundations/vemurafenib]]) where ligand-specific transcriptomes are mechanistically distinct.

## Counter-evidence

PTM-driven studies in monocyte-derived macrophages show that BaP induces ~5-fold more ubiquitination/phosphorylation than FICZ at comparable CYP1B1/AHRR mRNA induction — implying that even when transcriptional output matches, non-transcriptional outputs can diverge in a ligand-specific manner.
