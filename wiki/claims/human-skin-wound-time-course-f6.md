## Statement

A time-resolved human skin wound dataset shows a temporal progression — baseline (no myofibroblasts) → day 1 (rare F6) → day 7 (F6 predominant) → day 30 (F7 predominant) — providing real-time support for F6 as an intermediate state toward terminal F7 myofibroblasts.

## Evidence summary

Wound dataset of 58,823 cells (Fig. 5d), proportions by time point.

## Conditions and scope

External human wound dataset; donor-level proportions.

## Counter-evidence

Indirect support for trajectory; not lineage tracing.
