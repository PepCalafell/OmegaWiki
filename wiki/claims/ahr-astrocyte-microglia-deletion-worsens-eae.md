## Statement

Cell-specific AHR deletion in astrocytes (GFAP-Cre) or microglia (Cx3cr1-Cre) exacerbates EAE through unleashed NF-κB-driven pro-inflammatory responses; AHR activation in these CNS-resident cells suppresses neuroinflammation.

## Evidence summary

Rothhammer 2016 (astrocyte); Rothhammer 2018 (microglia); reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
