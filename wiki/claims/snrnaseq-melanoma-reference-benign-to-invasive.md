## Statement

A single-nuclei melanoma reference from three pathologist-graded lesions (benign naevus, severely dysplastic naevus, invasive melanoma; 10,747 nuclei) provides a continuum from benign to invasive disease and yields 118 high-confidence melanoma cells, addressing the prior lack of representative cutaneous melanoma single-cell data spanning lesion stages.

## Evidence summary

snRNA-seq of FFPE tissue; 11 immune types + 5 KC types + endothelial/fibroblast/pericyte/Schwann/sweat-gland clusters; melanoma cells separated from melanocytes by multi-evidence calling. (p.5)

## Conditions and scope

Three patients only; archival thin melanomas (Stage I, Breslow <1mm for some assays).

## Counter-evidence

Very small n; single severely-dysplastic and single benign sample.

## Open questions

Whether the benign→invasive continuum holds across more lesions and patients.
