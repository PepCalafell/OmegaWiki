## Statement

Physiological hypoxia in the bone marrow promotes haematopoietic stem cell quiescence and self-renewal, at least partly through HIF-dependent control of metabolism — but the precise role of HIF (and which isoform) is contested, with one study finding HIF2α dispensable for HSC maintenance.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Relevant existing wiki paper: [[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]].

## Conditions and scope

HIF1β (ARNT) removal reduces HSC viability; HIF2α reported non-essential in one study — hence the controversy.

## Counter-evidence

A cited study found no role for HIF2α in HSC maintenance, in tension with HIF-dependent quiescence models.
