## Statement

Psoriasis-specific upregulated genes were concentrated in the Trm1 and Trm3 resident-memory classes and were enriched for TH17-linked transcripts, including the known PV markers IL17F and CXCL13 plus GNLY, CTLA4, KLRB1, MGAT4, and PIK3R1.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Results of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]; markers [[foundations/il-17a-il17f-cytokines]], [[foundations/cxcl13-chemokine]]. Supports [[concepts/trm1-th2-th17-molecular-classification-inflammatory]].

## Conditions and scope

PV-vs-AD DEGs meeting the heterogeneity filter in at least one lymphocyte population.

## Counter-evidence

None reported; consistent with prior PV TH17 literature.
