## Statement

Hypoxia — the condition where cellular oxygen demand exceeds supply — is a shared feature of focal sites of immune activity across both health (physiological immunological niches) and disease (pathological immunological niches), making oxygen a central microenvironmental regulator of immunity.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]] (Taylor & Colgan, *Nat Rev Immunol* 2017). The framework is the organizing thesis of the review; see concept [[concepts/physiological-versus-pathological-hypoxia-immunological-niche]].

## Conditions and scope

Physiological hypoxia is sustained, moderate and spatially ordered; pathological hypoxia is severe, chaotic and often intermittent. The immunological consequence depends on the niche, not on hypoxia per se.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope; this is a definitional synthesis.
