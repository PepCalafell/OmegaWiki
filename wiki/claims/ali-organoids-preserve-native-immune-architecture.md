## Statement

Air-liquid interface (ALI) tumour organoids preserve native tumour-stroma-immune architecture including CAFs, CTLs, T helpers, TAMs, NK, B and NKT cells.

## Evidence summary

ALI culture of minced tumour fragments grows tumour cells en bloc with endogenous stromal and immune populations, preserving heterogeneity, mutational burden and architecture without reconstitution (Polak et al. 2024, p.526; Neal et al. 2018, Cell).

## Conditions and scope

ALI culture of fresh tumour biopsies with collagen embedding and transwell air-liquid geometry.
