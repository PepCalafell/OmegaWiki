## Statement

Pre-vaccination methylation at 41 CpG sites is significantly associated with the magnitude of BCG-induced IFN-γ trained immunity.

## Evidence summary

FDR-significant baseline EWAS for TI(IFN-γ); positive sites enriched in phospholipase/kisspeptin/LPA pathways, negative sites in apoptosis/FAS pathways; five sites replicated nominally.

## Conditions and scope

Heterologous (S. aureus) IFN-γ readout; whole blood; young adults.

## Counter-evidence

39% of sites remain suggestive after 12-cell-type adjustment, implying some cell-type driving.

## Open questions

Which CpGs are causal for IFN-γ readiness vs markers.
