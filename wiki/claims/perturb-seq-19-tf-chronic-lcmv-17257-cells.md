## Statement

A retroviral dual-gRNA Perturb-seq library (40 vectors, 76 TF gRNAs + 4 gScramble controls) targeting 19 TF genes (12 TEXterm single-state + 7 multi-state) is transduced into Cas9+ P14 CD8+ T cells, transferred into LCMV–Clone 13 infected recipients, and read out by scRNA-seq across 17,257 cells.
