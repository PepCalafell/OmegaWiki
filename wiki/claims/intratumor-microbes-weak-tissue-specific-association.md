## Statement

Tumor-resident microbial abundance correlates with innate immune activation in a weak and tissue-specific manner, implying microbes are only one of several PRR triggers (with self-antigens/neoantigens likely contributing more).

## Evidence summary

Colon adenocarcinoma innate scores tracked gut-resident taxa while lung squamous carcinoma tracked a distinct microbiome; associations were stronger for more abundant taxa but modest overall (low R values).

## Conditions and scope

Pan-cancer/mucosal-cancer microbiome association from TCGA-derived abundance; correlational and weak.

## Counter-evidence

Low correlation strength; microbiome estimates from unaligned reads carry contamination caveats; authors note other (non-microbial) PRR triggers.
