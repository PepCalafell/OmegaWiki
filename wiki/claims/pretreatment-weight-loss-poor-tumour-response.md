## Statement

Pretreatment weight loss in patients with breast and gastrointestinal cancers is associated with poor tumour response and with severe dose-limiting toxicity, often leading to dose reductions or treatment cessation and contributing to worse outcomes.

## Evidence summary

Multiple cohorts cited in the 2025 Cancer Discovery review.

## Conditions and scope

Cytotoxic chemotherapy regimens; less clear for targeted therapy / immunotherapy.

## Counter-evidence

None directly contradicting; some cohorts show similar effects for immunotherapy.

## Linked ideas

- LBM-based dose adjustment (not currently standard).

## Open questions

- Whether prophylactic CAC intervention prior to therapy improves outcomes.
