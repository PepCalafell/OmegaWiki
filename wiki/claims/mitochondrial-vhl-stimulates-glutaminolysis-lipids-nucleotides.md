## Statement

Mitochondrial VHL redirects metabolism from low-yield leucine oxidation toward higher-yield glutaminolysis, generating reductive-lipogenesis acetyl-CoA and glutamine-derived nucleotides that support hypoxic proliferation.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 3D–E, S5A–D). Glutamine isotope tracing into palmitate/stearate and nucleotides.

## Conditions and scope

Hypoxia-specific; the substitution favors glutamine carbon/nitrogen over leucine-derived acetyl-CoA.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

Supports [[concepts/leucine-allosteric-gdh-glutaminolysis-activation]].

## Open questions
