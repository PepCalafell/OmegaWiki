## Statement

The Gs-coupled prostaglandin E2 receptors EP2 and EP4 collectively mediate PGE2-driven Tgfb1 induction in efferocytosing macrophages.

## Evidence summary

Combined siPtger2 + siPtger4 silencing blocks AC-induced and exogenous PGE2-induced Tgfb1 in BMDMs (Fig. 1j-k). Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only; individual receptor contributions not separately dissected.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
