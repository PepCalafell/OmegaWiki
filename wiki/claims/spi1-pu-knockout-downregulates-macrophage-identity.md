## Statement
Knockout of Spi1 (encoding PU.1) consistently reshapes the macrophage transcriptome throughout the Listeria time course, broadly downregulating macrophage-identity markers (CD14, CD115/CSF1R, CD64) and immune signaling pathways (TLR, TNF-α, JAK-STAT), consistent with its role as a master/lineage-determining transcription factor.

## Evidence summary
Spi1 KO cells co-clustered across all time points in UMAP and showed broad pathway downregulation ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figures 4B, 4D; uses [[foundations/spi1-pu1-master-tf]]).

## Conditions and scope
Macrophage cell line; cell-intrinsic; survival unaffected (constitutive regulatory role).

## Counter-evidence
None for downregulation; see companion claim on unexpected ISG increase.
