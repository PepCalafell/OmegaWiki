## Statement

In a three-dimensional human ARCI skin model, the eTD-CBE base editor produced a 12% mean on-target *TGM1* gene-correction rate and restored approximately 30% of wild-type transglutaminase 1 (TG1) enzyme activity — a level the authors regard as likely sufficient to alleviate the most severe skin symptoms in patients.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/editing-skin-place-vivo-genome-correction]]. Relates to [[foundations/transglutaminase-1]], [[foundations/autosomal-recessive-congenital-ichthyosis]], and [[concepts/in-situ-genome-editing-skin]].

## Conditions and scope

3D skin model (not in vivo human skin, not primary patient cells); functional readout is TG1 enzyme activity. The clinical-benefit threshold is inferred, not established.

## Counter-evidence

None within the source; durability assessed only to 48 h.
