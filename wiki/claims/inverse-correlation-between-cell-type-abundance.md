## Statement

There is a statistically significant inverse correlation between immune-cell-type lymph node abundance and the number of cytokine genes expressed (Pearson r = -0.71, P = 0.0065), suggesting rare cell types are crucial cytokine producers in immune cell–cell communication.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Non-B, non-T immune cell types in lymph nodes.

None within the paper's scope.
