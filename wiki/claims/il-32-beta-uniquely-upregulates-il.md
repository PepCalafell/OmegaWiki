## Statement

IL-32-β is the only cytokine that strongly upregulates the IL-10 family (IL-10, IL-19, IL-24) in myeloid cells, giving its proinflammatory neutrophil-recruiting program a self-regulating character; this IL-10-family induction is weaker in interferon-group donors.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: mechanistic.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
