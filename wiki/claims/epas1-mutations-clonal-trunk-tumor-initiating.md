## Statement

EPAS1 mutations are present in nearly all tumor cells (CCF ≈ 0.99–1.0), marking them as truncal, tumor-initiating events.

## Evidence summary

Cancer cell fraction analysis (see [[foundations/cancer-cell-fraction-ccf]]); 8/9 clonal in CCHD.

## Conditions and scope

Inference from purity/copy-number-corrected CCF.

## Counter-evidence

1/9 CCHD tumors showed a subclonal EPAS1 mutation.
