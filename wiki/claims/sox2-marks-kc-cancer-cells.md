## Statement

The transcription factor SOX2 is specifically expressed in keratinocyte cancer cells and absent from normal epithelial cells, marking malignant KCs in both UMAP and spatial tissue space.

## Evidence summary

Visual and quantitative specificity across single-cell and spatial data; consistent with prior literature that SOX2 is essential for cSCC cancer-initiating cells. (p.6-7)

## Conditions and scope

cSCC; SOX2 is one of the six cross-platform-validated KC cancer markers.

## Counter-evidence

Specificity shown by expression mapping, not functional knockout in this study.

## Open questions

Whether SOX2 is required for maintenance vs initiation in these tumours.
