## Statement

The AHR–IL-22 axis in RORγt+ ILC3s alone is sufficient for resistance to Citrobacter rodentium: mice with AHR-deficient ILC3s phenocopy whole-body Ahr-KO susceptibility (colitis, high faecal burden, dissemination, early death), while restoring constitutively active AHR only in RORγt+ ILCs protects from infection.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 82–85.

## Conditions and scope

Specifically tested in C. rodentium colitis; ILC3 IL-22 also relevant to S. enterica and segmented filamentous bacteria.

## Counter-evidence

None within the review's scope.
