## Statement

In DLBCL, GAPDH protein expression serves as a clinically practical marker of metabolic status: low-GAPDH tumours are OxPhos-leaning and exhibit complete response to L-asparaginase-based regimens.

## Evidence summary

- Chiche/Reverso-Meinietti/Ricci 2024 referenced here as the basis for GAPDH-based metabolic stratification of DLBCL.

## Conditions and scope

- Restricted to DLBCL with current evidence.

## Counter-evidence

- mRNA-level GAPDH does not necessarily reflect protein expression or enzyme activity.

## Linked ideas

- Extension to non-DLBCL OxPhos tumours where ASNase may benefit patients.

## Open questions

- Does GAPDH IHC generalise as a metabolic biomarker beyond DLBCL?
