## Statement

Cell-type abundance shifts along scSLIDE's Alzheimer trajectory — decreasing SST+ inhibitory interneurons and increasing microglia in severe disease — match established AD pathophysiology (neuronal loss with reactive gliosis), and reach significance where binary case-control abundance tests do not.

## Evidence summary

Figure 3h,i and Supplementary Table 3 of [[reconstructing-developmental-disease-progression-sample-level]]; reproduced in Psych-AD.

## Conditions and scope

Trajectory-based abundance testing; consistent across both AD cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

Case-control comparisons showed the same trends but did not survive multiple-testing correction — a power difference, not a contradiction.

## Linked ideas

Abundance-level support for [[continuous-disease-progression-modeling]].

## Open questions

Which microglial subtypes drive the increase along the trajectory?
