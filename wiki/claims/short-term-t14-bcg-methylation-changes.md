## Statement

The acute day-14 BCG methylation changes largely revert between day 14 and day 90, indicating a transient short-term effect followed by a recovery phase.

## Evidence summary

All 39 short-term suggestive CpGs reversed direction in T90–T14; demethylated T14 sites enriched in active-transcription states (eFORGE), consistent with an acute, reversible transcriptional response.

## Conditions and scope

Suggestive-threshold sites; pattern-level rather than per-site FDR evidence.

## Counter-evidence

Few sites reach genome-wide significance; no untreated control.

## Open questions

What resets the acute methylation marks during recovery.
