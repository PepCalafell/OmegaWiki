## Statement

The three TNF-anchored cytokine pairs account for ~52% of the cell-type abundance changes induced by sublethal LPS at day 0.5, implying that the remaining half of cellular effects depends on additional cytokine or non-cytokine factors (complement, coagulation, DAMPs, etc.).

## Conditions and scope

The quantitative fraction is specific to the day-0.5 readout and the selected dose. It is a lower bound on how much "cytokine-encoded" sepsis is, since higher-order combinations were not tested.
