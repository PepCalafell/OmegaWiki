## Statement

Systemic hypoxia plus anti-CTLA4 antibody nearly completely abolishes orthotopic E0771 tumor progression, exceeding either treatment alone.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.11): "systemic hypoxia plus ICI further decreased tumor growth, nearly completely abolishing cancer progression" — 100 ug anti-mouse Ctla4 vs IgG2b on days 3,6,9,12 (Fig.6d). (type: pharmacological, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
