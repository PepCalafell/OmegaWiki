## Statement

Hypoxia engages multiple pyroptosis arms: (i) nuclear translocation of PD-L1 drives GSDMC transcription, executing a non-classical pyroptotic program in tumor cells; (ii) hypoxia-induced ROS activate NLRP3 inflammasome assembly with caspase-1 cleavage of GSDMD in an NF-κB/HIF-1α-dependent manner; (iii) hypoxia/reoxygenation triggers caspase-11/GSDMD pyroptosis in cardiomyocytes with IL-18 release.

## Evidence summary

GSDMC arm characterized by Hou et al. (Nature Cell Biol 2020); NLRP3-HIF-1α coupling supported in macrophage models. All integrated by Luo et al. 2022.

## Conditions and scope

- Tumor cells: PD-L1 nuclear localization + hypoxic stress
- Macrophages: NLRP3 + ROS + HIF-1α
- Cardiomyocytes: ischemia/reperfusion

## Counter-evidence

In some tumor models hypoxia suppresses pyroptosis to maintain survival — direction is cell-type and severity dependent.
