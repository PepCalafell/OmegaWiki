## Statement

Knockout of STAT5 or STAT6 in homeostatic mouse macrophages increases chromatin accessibility at many genomic regions, indicating that under steady-state conditions both factors act as epigenomic **repressors** in macrophages — not just inducible activators. This is consistent with prior STAT6-mediated repression of M1-polarization enhancers and extends the repressive epigenomic role to STAT5.
