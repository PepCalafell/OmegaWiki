## Statement

Loss of HIF-1α (CRISPR/Cas9 knockout) impairs the survival and proliferation of hepatocellular carcinoma (Huh7) cells not only under hypoxia but also under normoxia, indicating that HIF-1α is required for HCC cell fitness irrespective of oxygen availability.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3142–3144) — genetic knockout + rescue.
  - Quote: "under normoxia, only HIF1A−/− Huh7 cells showed increased death and reduced proliferation rates compared to wild-type Huh7 cells, implying that HIF-1α is essential for hepatocellular carcinoma cell survival irrespective of oxygen levels."
  - GFP-HIF-1α re-expression restored survival, excluding CRISPR off-target effects.

## Conditions and scope

Huh7 hepatoma line; normoxia (21% O₂) and hypoxia (1% O₂). Contrasts with HeLa, which tolerates HIF1A knockout under normoxia.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded at ingest. In vitro single-line phenotype (rescued genetically); generalization to primary HCC untested.
