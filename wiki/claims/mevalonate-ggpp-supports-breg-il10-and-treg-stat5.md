## Statement

GGPP — an intermediate of the mevalonate pathway downstream of HMG-CoA reductase — supports two distinct regulatory arms: Breg IL-10 output through PI3Kδ-AKT-GSK-3-Blimp-1, and Treg suppressive function through STAT5 phosphorylation in response to IL-2. Statins, by blocking HMG-CoA reductase, suppress both arms while paradoxically enhancing Th1/cytolytic CD8 antitumor responses.
