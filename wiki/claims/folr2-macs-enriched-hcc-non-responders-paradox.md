## Statement

FOLR2 macrophages accumulate within HCC tumor nodules and are enriched in patients who fail to respond to PD-1 blockade — paradoxically, the same FOLR2-mac signature in breast cancer is associated with enhanced T-cell infiltration and improved survival. This reinforces the broader tissue-specificity principle for TAM functional output.

## Evidence summary

- FOLR2-mac frequencies higher in non-responder tumors.
- Consistent with onco-fetal niche literature (Li et al.).

## Conditions and scope

- HCC-specific.
- Correlational.

## Counter-evidence

- Breast-cancer FOLR2 literature (Ramos et al.) shows favourable association.
