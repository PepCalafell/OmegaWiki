## Statement

Cells from different skin-cancer lineages differ far more transcriptomically (thousands of DE genes between cSCC and melanoma) than cancer cells differ from their non-cancer counterparts within a lineage (hundreds of DE genes), indicating lineage identity dominates over malignant transformation in the transcriptome.

## Evidence summary

DE counts: 3257 (cSCC>melanoma), 2713 (melanocytic>cSCC) vs 176 (cancer-KC vs healthy-KC) and 68 (melanoma vs melanocyte), with distinct pathway enrichments. (p.6, Fig S5)

## Conditions and scope

Bulk-of-DE comparison; depends on cancer-cell definitions and sample sizes per group.

## Counter-evidence

Small malignant-cell numbers (745 KC cancer, 118 melanoma) may under-power within-lineage DE.
