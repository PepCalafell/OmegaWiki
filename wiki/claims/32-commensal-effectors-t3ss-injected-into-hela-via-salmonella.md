## Statement

A reliable injection assay confirms that commensal candidate effectors are bona fide
T3SS substrates: 32 of 97 tested were specifically injected into human cells.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.443, Fig.1e): NanoLuc HiBiT split-luciferase
assay with ΔsctV negative control; 32/97 effectors from 11 strains specifically and
significantly injected. Lower success vs controls attributed to phylogenetic diversity
and missing chaperones/cofactors.

## Conditions and scope

Heterologous Salmonella host may lack cognate chaperones (false negatives).

## Counter-evidence

Some false effector identifications cannot be fully excluded.
