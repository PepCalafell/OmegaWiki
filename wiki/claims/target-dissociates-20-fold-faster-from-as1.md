## Statement

Even in the absence of effector, the target dissociates ~20-fold faster from AS1 than from an unhindered binder fusion, attributable to minor residual strain in the target–AS1 complex — an effect that elevates the base off-rate and thereby limits the net fold acceleration.

## Evidence summary

"the target dissociates 20-fold faster from AS1 than from an unhindered binder fusion ... probably because of minor strain in the target–AS1 complex" (p.4). Reducing this base off-rate was one of two strategies to maximize the dissociation-rate enhancement.

## Conditions and scope

AS1 designed host; SPR off-rate comparison.

## Counter-evidence

Some redesigned variants showed reduced base off-rates relative to AS1.
