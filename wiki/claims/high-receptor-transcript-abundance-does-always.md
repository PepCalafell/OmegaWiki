## Statement

High receptor transcript levels do not guarantee a strong response (e.g. Decorin, Resistin, LT-α2β1, TRAIL, FasL, TGF-β1), because protein abundance, receptor stability and post-translational modifications modulate signaling — receptor mRNA is an unreliable predictor of cytokine response.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: methodological.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
