## Statement

RANKL+M-CSF-driven osteoclast differentiation requires mitochondrial biogenesis and OXPHOS complex I (regulated by iron, PGC-1β, alternative NF-κB) and is fuelled by glutaminolysis through SLC1A5 and glutaminase-1 under c-Myc control. Glutamine withdrawal or inhibition of SLC1A5/c-Myc blocks osteoclastogenesis.

## Evidence summary

Synthesized in [[papers/metabolism-tissue-macrophages-homeostasis-pathology]] from Indo 2013, Ishii 2009, and Go 2020.

## Conditions and scope

- In-vitro osteoclastogenesis.

## Counter-evidence

- 2-deoxy-d-glucose does not block osteoclastogenesis, indicating glycolysis is not required for differentiation per se.
