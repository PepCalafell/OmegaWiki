## Statement

In hepatocellular carcinoma cells, HIF-1α is required to maintain expression of glycolytic/gluconeogenic enzymes (including HK2 and GAPDH) under normoxia, not only under hypoxia — a normoxic HIF-1α-dependent glycolytic program validated across two HCC lines.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3146–3154) — proteomics, KEGG enrichment, western blot, second cell line.
  - Quote: "in normoxic Huh7 cells, the most prominent HIF-1α-upregulated proteins belong to pathways involved in carbon metabolism, glycolysis/gluconeogenesis, and steroid biosynthesis."
  - Quote: "Silencing of HIF-1α expression in HepG2 cells by shRNA resulted in the downregulation of two representative glycolytic enzymes (hexokinase 2 and GAPDH) under both normoxia and hypoxia, confirming the normoxic role of HIF-1α in HCC cells."

## Conditions and scope

Cluster 1 glycolytic enzymes are HIF-1α-induced irrespective of O₂ in Huh7; in HeLa, the equivalent cluster is HIF-1α-dependent mainly under hypoxia (oxygen-sensitive).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. In vitro; enzyme activity/flux not measured (protein levels only).
