## Statement
Weighted gene coexpression network analysis (WGCNA) partitions the macrophage activation data into 49 coexpression modules whose eigengenes map onto specific stimulation conditions.

## Evidence summary
- "We identified 49 distinct coexpression modules containing 27 to 884 genes per module" (p.278).
- "the classical M1 and M2 stimuli showed prominent ME patterns (modules 8 and 15, respectively)" (p.278, Figure 3B).

## Conditions and scope
WGCNA on n=160 macrophage samples (72 hr); modules reused as gene sets for downstream GSEA.
