## Statement

IRF9 has transcriptional regulatory activities independent of the canonical STAT1-STAT2-IRF9 ISGF3 complex. Specific IRF9-only targets (Rdh14 in macrophages and T cells; Tprkb; Usb1 in T cells) are downregulated in IRF9 KO without being affected by STAT1 or STAT2 KO. This is direct evidence that IRF9 forms (or contributes to) alternative regulatory complexes under homeostasis, and substantially broadens IRF9's functional repertoire beyond the canonical ISG-axis textbook role.
