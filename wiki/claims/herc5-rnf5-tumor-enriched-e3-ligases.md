## Statement
Differential analysis of E3 ubiquitin ligases across TPCPA identifies tumor-type-enriched E3s — HERC5 in esophageal cancer and RNF5 in liver cancer — as candidate handles for PROTAC selectivity, expanding the small set of E3 ligases (e.g., CRBN, VHL) currently in use for PROTAC design.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figure 2C and surrounding text.

## Conditions and scope
- Expression-level enrichment only; tumor-selective recruitment of these E3s by bifunctional degraders has not been demonstrated in this paper.

## Counter-evidence
- High expression does not guarantee functional capacity for PROTAC-mediated substrate degradation in those tissues.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
- Can HERC5- or RNF5-recruiting PROTACs degrade canonical oncoproteins selectively in their tumor-of-enrichment context?
