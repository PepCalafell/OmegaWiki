## Statement

GRN analysis reveals sex differences in transcription factor activity within immune cell types, with several TFs more active in females than males.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (correlational): In CD4 T cells, HIF1A, NFKB1, and STAT5B showed higher activity in females and BHLHE40 in males; STAT1 elevated in females in ncMono-FCGR3A (p.6).

## Conditions and scope

Specific immune subsets; observational sex comparison.
