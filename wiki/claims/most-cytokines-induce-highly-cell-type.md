## Statement

Across 86 cytokines and 17+ immune cell types in mouse lymph nodes, most upregulated DEGs are specific to a single cell type rather than shared, demonstrating cell-type-specific cytokine pleiotropy as a general principle.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse skin-draining lymph nodes, 4-h post-injection, in vivo. Supraphysiological cytokine doses.

None within the paper's scope.
