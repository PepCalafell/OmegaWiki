## Statement
The constitutively open chromatin landscape is a property of the macrophage activation network TRs specifically, not of regulators generally — TRs never expressed lack accessible promoters.

## Evidence summary
Only 12–15% of never-expressed TRs had accessible promoters and 12–17% strong enhancers, versus >92% for expressed network TRs, showing accessibility marks the macrophage-relevant regulator set.

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro human macrophages across 29 conditions.

## Counter-evidence
None within paper.

## Open questions
