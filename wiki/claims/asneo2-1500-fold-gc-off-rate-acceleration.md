## Statement

Fusing the de novo IL-2 mimic Neo2 to the designed hinge switch (ASNeo2) such that the effector-bound state clashes with the common gamma chain creates a switchable cytokine: adding the effector induces a 1,500-fold increase in the γc off-rate from the active IL-2Rβγc complex, and an optimized variant reaches 5,700-fold.

## Evidence summary

"For one of these, ASNeo2, binding the effector induces a 1,500-fold increase in the γc off-rate" (p.6). A variant with topological safeguards against degradation reached "5,700-fold, the highest fold change of all our designed systems."

## Conditions and scope

In vitro SPR on the designed IL-2Rβγc system.

## Counter-evidence

Highest fold-change (5,700) is from a specific safeguarded variant, not base ASNeo2.
