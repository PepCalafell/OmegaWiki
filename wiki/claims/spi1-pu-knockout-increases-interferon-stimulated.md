## Statement
Beyond its activating/lineage role, PU.1 (SPI1) may directly repress a subset of interferon-stimulated genes: Spi1 knockout macrophages show elevated ISG expression (Stat1, Usp18, Gbp2, Ifit3, Ifitm3, Nos2).

## Evidence summary
Correlative single-cell observation in KO cells, interpreted in light of a prior report of PU.1 repressor activity ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], p.8; uses [[foundations/spi1-pu1-master-tf]]).

## Conditions and scope
Macrophage cell line; correlative, not mechanistically dissected here.

## Counter-evidence
Effect is correlative; direct repression not demonstrated mechanistically in this study.

## Open questions
Does PU.1 directly bind and repress these ISG loci, or is the increase indirect?
