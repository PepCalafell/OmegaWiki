## Statement

To validate the two markers, human BMDMs were stimulated with IFN-gamma or type I interferon; CXCL9 was specifically high upon IFN-gamma and IFIT1 specifically high upon IFN-I, confirming the cytokine-marker correspondence inferred from the atlas.

## Evidence summary

Controlled in vitro BMDM assay. (p.4) Quote: "in vitro assays confirmed the specific high expression of CXCL9 and IFIT1 in human bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) upon the stimulation of IFN-γ and IFN-I, respectively".

## Conditions and scope

In vitro monocyte-derived macrophages; may not capture full tissue-resident TAM context.
