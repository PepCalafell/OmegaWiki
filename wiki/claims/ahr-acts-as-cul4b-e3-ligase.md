## Statement

Ligand-activated AHR can function as the substrate-recognition subunit of a CUL4B-containing E3 ubiquitin ligase, targeting proteins for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation; this activity competes with AHR's transcription-factor activity for ARNT availability.

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]; primary discovery Ohtake et al. 2007.
