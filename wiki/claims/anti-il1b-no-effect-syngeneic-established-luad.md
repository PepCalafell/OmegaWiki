## Statement

Once LUAD is established as an invasive tumor, IL-1β neutralization no longer suppresses growth — the same intervention that works in the precancerous niche is mechanistically inactive in advanced disease.

## Linked ideas

- Provides the internal experimental analog to the clinical CANTOS (worked, prevention) vs CANOPY (failed, late-stage NSCLC) divergence — supporting [[concepts/il1b-precancer-interception-luad]] as the unifying explanation.
