## Statement

The hypoxic-tumour + neutrophil niche identified at the transcriptomic level (CosMx, MERFISH) is recovered at the protein level in a large IMC tissue microarray of LUAD samples (416 cores), where tumour cluster C23 expresses MPO and HIF1A and is invariably surrounded by neutrophil-enriched cluster C7.

## Open questions

- Whether protein-level MPO in tumour cells reflects neutrophil-shed MPO uptake vs intrinsic tumour-cell expression is mechanistically unresolved.
