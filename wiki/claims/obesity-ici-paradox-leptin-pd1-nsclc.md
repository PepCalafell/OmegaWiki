## Statement

In NSCLC, higher BMI correlates with improved PFS and OS under PD-1 blockade because leptin- and IL-6-driven T-cell exhaustion upregulates PD-1, creating dependency on the targeted axis.

## Evidence summary

Retrospective patient cohorts plus mechanistic mouse studies.

## Conditions and scope

PD-1-axis ICI; not yet validated for anti-TIGIT/LAG3 combinations.

## Counter-evidence

Obesity also reduces tolerance of combination chemo–ICI in some studies.

## Open questions

- Stratify ICI trials by BMI and add metformin in obese arms.
