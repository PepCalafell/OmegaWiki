## Statement

A prospective intervention trial using conventional CRC PDOs to test mTOR, PI3K, EGFR, MEK and VEGFR inhibitors did not yield objective clinical responses to organoid-recommended treatment.

## Evidence summary

A prospective intervention trial using conventional CRC PDOs did not demonstrate objective clinical responses to the organoid-predicted recommended treatment for mTOR, PI3K, EGFR, MEK or VEGFR agents (Polak et al. 2024, p.531).

## Conditions and scope

Conventional epithelial-only CRC PDOs in a prospective umbrella trial.
