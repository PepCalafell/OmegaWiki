## Statement

In a HNSCC CosMx protein-assay dataset stratified by multinucleated giant cell (MGC) presence, Novae identifies a TLS-overlapping spatial domain (D492) characterised by colocalisation of TCM CD4+ T cells, plasma B cells, and macrophages — consistent with the immune-cell composition of TLSs in HNSCC.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 5a–c (heatmap of cell-type distributions across Novae domains) and Supplementary Fig. 20 (H&E-TLS overlap).

## Conditions and scope

n=8 patient cohort; correlational observation, no functional validation.

## Counter-evidence

Cohort size limits statistical power; TLS overlap is qualitative against H&E.
