## Statement
Unlike the macrophage activation network, human tissue-related TR networks show strict integration of chromatin state and expression: expressed TRs carry accessible promoters, non-expressed TRs do not.

## Evidence summary
A dichotomous accessible-vs-inaccessible distribution tracked tissue-specific TR expression across five tissues, exemplified by CDX2 (intestine) and NKX2-1 (lung).

## Conditions and scope
Consortium tissue data; contrast case to the activation network.
