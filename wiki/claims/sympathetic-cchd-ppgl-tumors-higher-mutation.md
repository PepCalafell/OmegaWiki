## Statement

Systemic-hypoxia-developed sympathetic PPGL tumors carry greater genomic instability and mutation burden than their normoxic counterparts.

## Evidence summary

WES-based MSI score, CN events, and TMB comparison (P values 0.0287, 0.0028, 0.0047).

## Conditions and scope

Small CCHD cohort (n=9) vs larger non-CCHD comparators.

## Counter-evidence

Modest absolute difference (15 vs 9 mutations); PPGL is a low-TMB tumor type overall.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Causality between hypoxia-induced repair loss and the mutation pool that yields EPAS1 variants.
