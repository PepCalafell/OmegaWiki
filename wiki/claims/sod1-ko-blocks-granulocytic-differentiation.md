## Statement

SOD1 loss produces a near-complete colony-formation defect with proliferative arrest and a specific block in granulocytic maturation — the strongest of the three KO phenotypes in the paper.

## Evidence summary

Multiple readouts converge: small CFU-G only, reduced cell counts in stromal co-culture, severe LTC-IC drop (1/153). Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 5 G-H, fig. S18-S19).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+; ex vivo culture systems.

## Counter-evidence

SOD2 KO replicates much of the phenotype but lacks the proliferative-arrest component — the SOD1-specific component is the proliferation block.
