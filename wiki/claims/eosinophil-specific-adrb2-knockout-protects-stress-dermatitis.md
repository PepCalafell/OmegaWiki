## Statement

The eosinophil-intrinsic Adrb2 receptor is the critical noradrenergic-signalling node converting sympathetic noradrenaline into eosinophil-driven AD exacerbation. Cell-type-specific genetic proof of the effector arm of the sympathetic-eosinophil axis.
