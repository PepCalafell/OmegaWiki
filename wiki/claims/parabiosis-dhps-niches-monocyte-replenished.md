## Statement

In WT:Dhps-ΔM parabiotic pairs, the WT partner contributes cells to the Dhps-ΔM RTM niches, demonstrating that DHPS-deficient niches no longer self-maintain and instead depend on circulating precursors.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] using [[foundations/parabiosis]] (Fig. 2c, Extended Data Fig. 5).

## Conditions and scope

WT:WT controls show no parabiont contribution, isolating the effect to DHPS loss.

None.
