## Statement

The web application provides interactive, exploratory visualization of each major immune cell type's transcriptomic response to cytokine stimulation, and is optimized specifically for browsing perturbational single-cell RNA-sequencing data.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/immune-dictionary-immune-response-enrichment-analysis]] (Lai, …, Cui; AAI 2025 abstract). Exposes the perturbation atlas underlying [[foundations/immune-dictionary-dataset]] / [[foundations/cytokine-perturbation-scrna-seq-vivo-lymph]] for browser-based inspection.
