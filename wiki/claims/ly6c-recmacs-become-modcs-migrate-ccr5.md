## Statement

Ly6C+CD11b+ recMacs differentiate into moDCs that migrate to draining lymph nodes through CCR5-CCL5 signaling (not CCR7) and act as immunosuppressive antigen-presenting cells inducing antigen-specific regulatory T cells.

## Evidence summary

Ccr5 expression in Ly6c2+ recMacs, Ccl5 in migratory DCs/LN T cell zone (Fig. 5a,b); builds on Rawat et al. 2023 JEM. See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung tumor-draining lymph nodes.
