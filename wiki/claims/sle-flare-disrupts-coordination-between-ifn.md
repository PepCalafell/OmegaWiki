## Statement

SLE flare is marked by disrupted coordination between IFN-I and IFN-II programs: IFN-α and IFN-γ scores become significantly negatively correlated in B cells, monocytes, and T cells (strongest in memory T and B cells, r = −0.69 to −0.76) — an imbalance absent in managed disease.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.9). Enabled by per-cell-type IFN-I/IFN-II separation ([[ifn-ifn-ii-activity-deconvolution-scoring]]).

## Conditions and scope

SLE; correlation analysis across subsets; small cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

Correlation is associative, not mechanistic.

## Open questions

Whether restoring IFN-I/IFN-II balance is therapeutically tractable in lupus.
