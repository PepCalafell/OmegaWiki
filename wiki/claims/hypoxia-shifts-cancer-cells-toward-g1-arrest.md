## Statement

Across most GENEVA lines, hypoxia induces a shift toward G1 arrest and reduced S-phase, consistent with general proliferation suppression; the magnitude correlates with differential tumor growth.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.5): "hypoxia induced a shift toward G1 arrest and reduced S-phase occupancy" — Cell-cycle scoring from hallmark gene signatures in scRNA-seq (Fig.2f, S1c). (type: correlational, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
