## Statement
By overlaying human macrophage transcriptomes onto ImmGen-defined murine core signatures, the study refines an activation-independent core macrophage signature and a surface-marker panel (CD14, CD32/FCGR2A, MERTK, CD64/FCGR1A, CD13/ANPEP) that distinguishes macrophages from dendritic cells and blood monocytes across species.

## Evidence summary
- Three gene groups identified by conservation of macrophage-vs-DC differential expression across activation states (p.284, Figure 7B).
- "this approach identified cell surface markers (CD14, FCGR2A [CD32], MERTK, FCGR1A [CD64], CD13 [ANPEP]) that distinguish human macrophages from both DCs and CD14+ blood monocytes" (p.284, Figure 7D).

## Conditions and scope
Monocyte-derived macrophages and blood comparators; not tissue-resident ontogenies.
