## Statement

The relative composition of fibroblast subtypes differs systematically across clinical scarring-risk categories: low-risk diseases are F1/F3-rich without myofibroblasts, scarring-risk diseases are F6-enriched, and established fibrosis is F7/F8-enriched.

## Evidence summary

Compositional analysis by disease category (Fig. 4a,b), cross-validated by spatial transcriptomics.

## Conditions and scope

Correlative; scarring categories clinically assigned; neurofibroma excluded.
