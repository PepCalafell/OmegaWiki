## Statement

The curative effect of ionizing radiation requires an intact STING/cGAS pathway.

## Evidence summary

Deng 2014 Immunity, Carozza 2020 Nat Cancer. DNA-damaging chemotherapies (cytarabine, cisplatin, etoposide) also activate STING but have minimal effect in cGAS/STING-silenced tumors.

## Conditions and scope

Conventional and high-dose radiotherapy; effect amplified by TREX1 or ENPP1 inhibition.
