## Statement

M2-like macrophages depend heavily on glutaminolysis: a high α-KG-to-succinate ratio promotes polarisation toward an immunosuppressive state. Conversely, inhibition or genetic ablation of glutamine synthetase (GLUL) in macrophages fosters M1-like polarisation through enhanced glycolysis and HIF-1α activation.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple primary studies on macrophage glutamine metabolism reviewed in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- M1 vs M2 polarisation in tumour-associated and inflammatory contexts.

## Counter-evidence

- M1/M2 dichotomy is an oversimplification; many TAM states are continuous.
