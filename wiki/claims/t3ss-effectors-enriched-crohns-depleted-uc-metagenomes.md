## Statement

Commensal effector content diverges between IBD subtypes: 64 effectors are enriched in
Crohn's disease metagenomes yet depleted in ulcerative colitis.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450, Fig.5e,f): in >800 IBD individuals
vs 334 healthy controls, 64 effectors enriched in CD and depleted in UC (FDR<0.01). This
opposing pattern was unexpected because Pseudomonadota abundance reportedly rises in both
IBDs, and mirrors the differential anti-TNF therapy response (effective in CD, not UC).

## Conditions and scope

Observational metagenomics; restricted to HuMMI-interacting effectors.

## Counter-evidence

Abundance vs prevalence distinction; confounders possible.

## Open questions

Causal role in CD risk and possible UC protection.
