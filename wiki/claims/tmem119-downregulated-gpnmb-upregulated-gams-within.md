## Statement

In human GBM tissue (CODEX multiplex imaging of an 80-sample TMA), GAMs within hypoxic (GLUT-1+) regions show predominant TMEM119 downregulation (39/43 hypoxic cores) and specific GPNMB upregulation (32/43), while LGALS3 is upregulated (29/43) but not restricted to myeloid cells — confirming in vivo, at the protein level, the hypoxia-driven marker dysregulation.

## Evidence summary

CODEX multiplex immunofluorescence on a human GBM TMA (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.14).

> "predominant TMEM119 downregulation in GAMs within hypoxic areas (39 of 43 hypoxic cores) ... GPNMB ... specifically upregulated in GAMs present in hypoxic areas (32 of 43 hypoxic cores) ... While LGALS3 was upregulated in hypoxia (29 of 43 hypoxic cores), this was not limited to myeloid cells"

## Conditions and scope

Human GBM TMA (70 GBM + 10 normal cerebrum); 43 cores with hypoxic regions analyzed.

## Counter-evidence

LGALS3 upregulation is not myeloid-specific, supporting its status as a general hypoxia-inducible gene rather than a clean GAM-subtype marker.
