## Statement

TNF plus neutrophils enhance assembly of a RIPK1-ZBP1-caspase-8 death complex (TRIFosome), captured by RIPK1 immunoprecipitation.

## Evidence summary

p43/p18 caspase-8 fragments co-immunoprecipitate with RIPK1. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.5).

> "ZBP1 was constitutively bound to RIPK1 in resting macrophages, and this binding was enhanced by treatment with neutrophils and TNF ... binding of the p43 and p18 active fragments of caspase-8"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

_None yet._

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
