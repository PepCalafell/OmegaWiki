## Statement

Open Problems in Single-Cell Analysis is a living, community-guided benchmarking platform hosting 12 single-cell tasks (9 base tasks, extending to 12 with subtasks).

## Evidence summary

Direct claim of the paper: "we present Open Problems, a living, extensive, community-guided platform including 12 current single-cell tasks" (p.1035), and "We started by defining 9 Open Problems tasks (Fig. 2a), which extends to 12 with the inclusion of subtasks" (p.1037). Tasks span label projection, dimensionality reduction, batch integration, spatial decomposition, denoising, multimodal matching, cell–cell communication, perturbation prediction and spatially variable genes.

## Conditions and scope

Snapshot of the v1 platform; the task list is designed to be extended by the community, so the count is a lower bound that grows over time.

## Counter-evidence

None — definitional claim about the authors' own platform.

## Open questions

How many tasks the community will sustain long-term, and whether task quality stays uniform as the platform grows.
