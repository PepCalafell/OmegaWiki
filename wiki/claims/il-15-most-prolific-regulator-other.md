## Statement

IL-15 is the most prolific regulator of other cytokines, significantly activating or repressing other cytokines 146 times across cell types — a network hub role consistent with its low tissue-specificity (TSI 0.07).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: quantitative.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
