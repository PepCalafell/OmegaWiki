## Statement

Patients with quantitatively similar TME cell-type composition can differ qualitatively in how those cell types organise spatially: some show cell-type admixing within a single cluster, others show clean segregation into separate clusters.

## Open questions

- Whether admixing vs segregation has independent prognostic or therapeutic value beyond composition.
