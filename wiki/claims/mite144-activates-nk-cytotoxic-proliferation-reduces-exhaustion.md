## Statement

MiTE-144 shifts NK cells from early activation states toward proliferative cytotoxic phenotypes, upregulating perforin, granzymes and proliferation markers while reducing early-exhaustion-associated transcripts; increases NK abundance in both TME and tumor-draining lymph nodes.
