## Statement

Unlike pharmacological TAK1-inhibition death, efferoptosis does not require GSDME; Gsdme-/- macrophages die normally even though caspase-3 is activated.

## Evidence summary

Distinguishes efferoptosis from mixed apoptotic/pyroptotic TAK1i death. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.8).

> "efferoptosis occurred independently of GSDME despite the activation of caspase-3"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
