## Statement

A pretreatment stem-immunity hub microniche enriched in TCF7⁺PD1⁺CD8⁺ T cells, CCR7⁺LAMP3⁺ DCs, CCL19⁺ CAFs and CXCL10⁺ TAMs is associated with subsequent response to PD-(L)1 blockade in NSCLC.

## Evidence summary

Originally identified by chemokine-driven spatial organization analyses; replication across cohorts pending.

## Conditions and scope

Pretreatment biopsies; spatial-platform dependent.

## Counter-evidence

Cohort sizes are modest; multi-centre replication pending.
