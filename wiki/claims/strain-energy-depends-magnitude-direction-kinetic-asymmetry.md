## Statement

The global strain energy of the ternary complex depends on both the magnitude and the direction of the deformation required to resolve the designed clash, and strain can distribute non-uniformly throughout the structure, producing kinetic asymmetry between the forward and reverse facilitated-dissociation pathways.

## Evidence summary

"the global strain energy of the ternary complex depends on both the magnitude and the direction of the deformation ... and that strain can distribute non-uniformly throughout the structure, leading to kinetic asymmetry" (p.5). Representative designs show differing forward vs reverse fold accelerations (e.g. 2,400-fold forward / 510-fold reverse; 150/30; 120/120).

## Conditions and scope

Designed AS1-derived variants; forward/reverse SPR titrations; spring-model interpretation.

## Counter-evidence

Quantitative strain energies are inferred from AF2 predictions and a simple spring model, not directly measured.
