## Statement

VHL/MCCC2-driven hypoxic growth depends on glutamine; removing glutamine abolishes the advantage, while supplying the downstream products (citrate + nucleosides) rescues VHL-deficient cells — placing glutaminolysis products as the functional output.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 3F, S5G–I). Deprivation and metabolite-rescue epistasis.

## Conditions and scope

Both carbon and nitrogen moieties of glutamine implicated; rescue uses citrate plus a nucleoside cocktail.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
