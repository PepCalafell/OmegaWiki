## Statement

F3 FRC-like fibroblasts are enriched in human skin but rare or absent in mouse skin, corresponding to mouse Ccl19+ fibroblasts that reside predominantly in lymphoid organs — a human-skin-specific stromal feature.

## Evidence summary

Comparative analysis with mouse cross-tissue atlas (Buechler et al.) (Fig. 8f,g).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy skin cross-species comparison.

## Counter-evidence
