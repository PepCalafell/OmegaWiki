## Statement

Although Pdyn+ neurons can in principle release dynorphin (acting on Oprk1), this peptidergic arm is dispensable for stress-induced AD exacerbation in mouse. The functionally critical neuron-eosinophil signal is noradrenaline → Adrb2, not dynorphin → Oprk1.
