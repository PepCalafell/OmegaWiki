## Statement

Bulk RNA-seq across five cachexia target tissues in the C26 model shows that cachexia (not the presence of a tumour) is the dominant explanatory variable for transcriptome variance (89-100%); ~340 genes are similarly altered across multiple tissues — a coordinated transcriptional response.

## Evidence summary

Ext Fig. 5 a-g; quoted: "we observed a huge remodelling of tissue transcriptomes in Cax compared with Ctrl mice, with cachexia explaining 89% to near 100% of gene expression changes (depending on tissues) versus tumour explaining up to 11% changes... 340 genes were significantly altered in a similar manner between metabolic tissues of cachectic animals."

## Conditions and scope

n = 4/group; bulk RNA-seq; 5 tissues. Tumour explanation capped at 11%.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope; results validated in PDAC models and SW480 humanised model.
