## Statement

GSDMD p30 puncta appear in ~30% of splenic CD68+ macrophages after TNF and are reduced ~60% by TRIF deficiency.

## Evidence summary

Marks active pyroptosis in vivo. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.3).

> "punctate signal in ~30% of CD68+ cells in the spleens of WT mice, which was reduced by ~60% in TRIF-deficient mice"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
