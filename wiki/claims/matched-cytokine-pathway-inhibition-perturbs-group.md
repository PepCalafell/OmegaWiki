## Statement

CytoSig predicted dominant cytokines per ICB-resistant group (MCSF/GCSF for G05/G07, TGFB1 for G08-G10, VEGFA for G08). In public treatment datasets, CSF1R-inhibitor-treated tumor-bearing mice showed robust decreases in G05/G07 signatures with other groups largely unaffected; VEGFR inhibition lowered G08 and TGFB inhibition lowered G08-G10 signatures, supporting group-matched intervention.

## Evidence summary

Signature-score response in public perturbation datasets. (p.10) Quote: "CSF1R inhibitor-treated tumor-bearing mice showed robust decreases in G05/G07 signatures relative to controls".

## Conditions and scope

Signature-level, mostly mouse/public data; no prospective patient-matched trial in this work.
