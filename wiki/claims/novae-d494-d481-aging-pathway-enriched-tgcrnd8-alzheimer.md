## Statement

In a longitudinal mouse-brain study comparing TgCRND8 (Alzheimer-like) and control mice across three time points each, Novae spatial domains D494 (Neurod6-high) and D481 show the highest brain-aging-pathway enrichment in the oldest TgCRND8 (17.9 months) brain.

## Evidence summary

Fig. 6f (aging-pathway enrichment heatmap), Fig. 6g (SVG expression patterns).

## Conditions and scope

n=6 mouse slides; one mouse per condition; descriptive enrichment, not a formal cohort statistical test.

## Counter-evidence

Single-mouse design; aging-pathway score is a derived signature, not a direct biological readout.

— none yet.

## Open questions

- Whether D494/D481 enrichment reproduces in larger Alzheimer-mouse cohorts.
- Whether Neurod6 expression dynamics drive or follow aging-pathway activation.
