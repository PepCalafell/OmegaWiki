## Statement

In glutamine-poor muscle injuries, macrophages serve as glutamine sources for nearby satellite cells, accelerating muscle regeneration. Macrophage-targeted inhibition of glutamine dehydrogenase-1 (i.e. blocking glutamine oxidation while preserving secretion) further improves regeneration.
