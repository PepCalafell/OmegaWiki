## Statement

Application of scTaiji to a curated human pan-cancer CD8+ T cell multi-omics atlas confirms cross-species conservation: 19 of 34 mouse TEXterm single-state TF genes and 22 of 30 multi-state TF genes display conserved activity patterns in human TEXterm cells, while TRM-selective TFs (NR4A1, KLF6, FOSB) show enriched activity in human TRM clusters.
