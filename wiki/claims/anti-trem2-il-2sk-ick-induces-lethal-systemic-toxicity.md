## Statement

Systemic administration of an unmasked αTREM2-IL-2 superkine immunocytokine in mice is lethal, driven by cytokine-storm-level systemic IFN-γ, IL-2, IL-6 and acute hepatotoxicity (elevated ALT/AST).
