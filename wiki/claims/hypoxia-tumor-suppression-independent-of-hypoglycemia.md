## Statement

Although systemic hypoxia lowers blood glucose, supplementing mice with 30% glucose does not rescue tumor growth under hypoxia, so hypoglycemia does not explain the anti-tumor effect.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.5): "Glucose supplementation slightly increased tumor growth in normoxic mice but had no discernible effect in hypoxic mice" — 30% glucose drinking water rescue experiment (Fig.3d, S2b); hypoxia still suppresses despite restored glucose. (type: mechanistic, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
