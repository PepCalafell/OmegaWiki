## Statement

In KP NSCLC, tumour-associated alveolar TRMs upregulate MHC class II expression — including H2-M2, H2-AA, H2-AB1, and H2-Q7 — while maintaining open chromatin at MHC-II loci across conditions. The upregulation suggests that tumour TRMs gain enhanced antigen-presentation capacity to CD4⁺ T cells.

## Evidence summary

- Bulk RNA-seq DEGs at day 15 and day 30 vs healthy lung
- ATAC-seq: MHC-II loci accessible across conditions
- H2-M2, H2-AA, H2-AB1, H2-Q7 specifically upregulated

## Conditions and scope

- Mouse KP orthotopic NSCLC
- Functional consequence (actual antigen presentation to CD4⁺ T cells) is inferred, not directly tested

## Counter-evidence

- Whether MHC-II upregulation translates to increased peptide presentation requires direct functional assays (e.g., MHC-II tetramer staining, OT-II proliferation)
- MHC-II expression on macrophages can be uncoupled from co-stimulation; its functional output depends on B7 / CD40 levels
