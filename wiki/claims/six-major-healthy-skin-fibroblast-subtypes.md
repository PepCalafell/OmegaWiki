## Statement

Healthy adult human skin fibroblasts resolve into six major subtypes: F1 superficial (papillary), F2 universal (reticular), F2/3 perivascular, F3 FRC-like, F4 hair-follicle-associated (three subclusters), and F5 Schwann-like (two subclusters).

## Evidence summary

Defined from 165,649 healthy/nonlesional fibroblasts by differential expression and pathway analysis; each subtype validated spatially (Visium/Xenium) in distinct niches.

## Conditions and scope

Healthy/nonlesional adult skin; subtypes observed across covariates.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper; nomenclature differs from prior studies, which this work harmonizes.

## Open questions
