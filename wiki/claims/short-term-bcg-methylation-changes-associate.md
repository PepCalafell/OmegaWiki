## Statement

Day-14 BCG methylation changes are linked to the decrease in circulating inflammatory proteins (notably CD6 and OPG), suggesting short-term methylation contributes to reduced systemic inflammation.

## Evidence summary

CpG–protein correlations show inverse coupling of T14 hypermethylation with protein decreases; long-term (T90) CpGs uncoupled from protein levels.

## Conditions and scope

Relies on previously published protein decreases; correlational.

## Counter-evidence

Only four FDR-significant CpG–protein pairs; no causal test.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether methylation change precedes or follows the protein decrease.
