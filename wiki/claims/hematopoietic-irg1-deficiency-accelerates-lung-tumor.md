## Statement

IRG1 deficiency restricted to the hematopoietic (bone-marrow-derived) compartment is sufficient to accelerate lung tumor progression, implicating IRG1 in bone-marrow-derived cells (macrophages) as anti-tumor.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1461) — mechanistic (bone-marrow-transplant) evidence.
  - Quote: "Bone marrow transplantation demonstrated that hematopoietic IRG1 deficiency was sufficient to accelerate LLC1 tumor progression, thus increasing proliferation, reducing apoptosis, and decreasing CD80 expression in TAMs."

## Conditions and scope

LLC1 orthotopic tumors in WT recipients reconstituted with Irg1-/- marrow; macrophages were the only consistently altered immune population across models.
