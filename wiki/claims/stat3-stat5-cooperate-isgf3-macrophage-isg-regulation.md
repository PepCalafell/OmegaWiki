## Statement

In homeostatic splenic macrophages, STAT3 and STAT5 knockouts produce downregulation of a subset of interferon-stimulated genes — an effect not observed (or much weaker) in T cells. This indicates cooperative ISG regulation between STAT3/STAT5 and the canonical ISGF3 complex under homeostatic conditions, and provides a mechanistic basis for the IRF9-STAT3/STAT5 partnership hypothesis.
