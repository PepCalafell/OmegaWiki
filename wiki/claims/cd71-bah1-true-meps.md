## Statement

Adding CD71 and BAH-1 (CD110) to FACS gating cleanly separates true MEPs (S100A4 high, RAP1B high) from GMP-like cells that masquerade as MEPs under conventional gating (ELANE, MPO high).

## Evidence summary

scp-MS protein markers within the FACS-MEP gate dichotomize cleanly by BAH-1. Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Fig. 1 H-J, fig. S3).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human BM CD34+; additional FACS markers CD71, BAH-1.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
