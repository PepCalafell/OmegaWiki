## Statement

Haematopoietic DNMT3A loss functionally impairs macrophage-mediated continual efferocytosis of apoptotic thymocytes in vivo, leading to AC accumulation, tissue necrosis, and impaired thymus resolution.

## Evidence summary

Histology + flow cytometry quantification of efferocytosis and necrosis at 18 h post DEX. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

DEX-thymus model; thymic macrophage population.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
