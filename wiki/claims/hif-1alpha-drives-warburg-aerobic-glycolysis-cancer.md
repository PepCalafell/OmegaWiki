## Statement

HIF1α is the master transcription factor amplifying aerobic glycolysis (the Warburg effect) in cancer cells, upregulating PGAM1, PKM, PGK1, LDHA, LDHC, and LDH-5 to shift metabolism away from oxidative phosphorylation toward lactate production. HIF2α and MYC cooperate.

## Evidence summary

Documented in [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023) with mechanism, target enzyme list, and MYC cooperation.

## Conditions and scope

Cancer cells under hypoxia or pseudohypoxia; HIF2α has overlapping targets (constitutive LDHA); MYC is required for full Warburg phenotype.

## Counter-evidence

Some tumor cells (e.g., MCT1+) consume lactate as a respiratory fuel rather than producing it — lactate symbiosis model.
