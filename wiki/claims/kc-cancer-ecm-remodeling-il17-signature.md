## Statement

Keratinocyte cancer cells show a transcriptional program of extracellular-matrix remodeling (multiple MMPs and SERPINs) and inflammation, including IL-17 pathway components, largely distinct from melanoma.

## Evidence summary

176 DE genes (169 cSCC-specific) spanning matrix degradation, differentiation, and IL-17/inflammation signatures. (p.6, Fig 4c, Fig S5)

## Conditions and scope

cSCC; single-cell DE of stringently-defined cancer KCs.

## Counter-evidence

Single-cohort DE; functional roles inferred from pathway membership.

## Open questions

Which MMP/SERPIN activities drive invasion vs are bystanders.
