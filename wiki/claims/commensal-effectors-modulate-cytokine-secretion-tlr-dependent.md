## Statement

Commensal effectors exert complex, stimulus-specific effects on intracellular immune
signalling, including suppression (Cpa_12) and TLR1/2-specific enhancement of IL-6/IL-8.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.450, Fig.5c,d): Cpa_12 reduced cytokine
secretion; others enhanced IL-6/IL-8 specifically after Pam3CSK4 (mimicking Gram-positive
Bacteroidetes lipopeptides), not after TNF or flagellin — suggesting effectors may
modulate responses to Gram-positive Bacteroidetes and influence interphyla competition.

## Conditions and scope

Caco-2 overexpression; stimulus-dependent.

## Counter-evidence

Effects vary by effector and stimulus.

## Linked ideas

Relates to [[pam3csk4-tlr12-agonist]], [[il-6-cytokine]], [[cxcl8-il8]].

## Open questions

Whether interphyla competition via TLR modulation occurs in vivo.
