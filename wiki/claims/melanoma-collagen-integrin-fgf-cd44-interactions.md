## Statement

After multi-sample MMCCI integration, melanoma shows 37 enriched ligand-receptor pairs (vs 16 in BCC, 17 in cSCC), dominated by collagen-integrin interactions (15/37) and FGF signaling (6/37, including FGF-CD44 and FGF-FGFR1).

## Evidence summary

Integrated interaction counts and the collagen-integrin/FGF composition of the melanoma-specific set. (p.10-11)

## Conditions and scope

Two integrated platforms (CosMx+Visium); small cohort.

## Counter-evidence

Interaction inference requires experimental validation (partly provided for CD44 pairs).

## Open questions

Which collagen-integrin pairs causally drive melanoma invasion.
