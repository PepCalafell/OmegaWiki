## Statement

Tumor cell STING is a cell-intrinsic suppressor of aerobic glycolysis via HK2.

## Evidence summary

Zhang 2023 Nat Cell Biol: STING acts as a metabolic checkpoint restricting aerobic glycolysis by targeting HK2. cGAMP treatment of T cells impairs basal glycolysis and glycolytic capacity, blocking proliferation (Kuhl 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Tumor cells and T cells; key contribution to STING-induced T-cell anti-proliferative effect.
