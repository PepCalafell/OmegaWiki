## Statement

Combining topical mRNA-lipid nanoparticles with laser microablation — generating transient micropores extending into the viable epidermis, with pore depth controlled via non-invasive reflectance confocal microscopy — transfected 39.1% of basal keratinocytes (the skin stem-cell compartment) with base-editing cargo in a three-dimensional ARCI skin model, implying a durable and potentially curative delivery route.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/editing-skin-place-vivo-genome-correction]]. Relates to [[concepts/laser-microablation-transdermal-lnp-delivery]] and [[foundations/mrna-lipid-nanoparticle]].

## Conditions and scope

3D skin model; reaching the basal stem-cell compartment is the key claim, since editing differentiated cells alone would be transient. Depends on controlled pore depth that preserves tissue integrity.

## Counter-evidence

None within the source; scaling to large surface areas and in vivo human skin untested.
