## Statement

Tapinarof is a direct AHR agonist that binds AHR–ARNT heterodimers, induces CYP1A1, and suppresses expression of IL-17A and IL-17F in T cells; AHR-deficient mice lose tapinarof's anti-inflammatory efficacy in the IMQ psoriasis model.

## Evidence summary

Smith et al. 2017 mechanism paper; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
