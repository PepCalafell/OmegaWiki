## Statement
The LE compartment is significantly enriched for cancer-hallmark programs (cell cycle, EMT, angiogenesis) relative to the TC, with the EMT score distributed across a broad continuum consistent with partial EMT.

## Evidence summary
SCPA-based pathway scoring across hallmark gene sets; Supplementary Fig. 2n.

## Conditions and scope
HPV-negative OSCC ST, malignant spots only.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
