## Statement

Interrogating fine-grained TME cellular compositions, the authors first found three major-compartment groups dominated by T, myeloid, or stromal cells, then refined this into 10 TME subgroups (G01-G10) chosen by complementary statistical metrics. The groups were robust under feature- and sample-based perturbations and revealed fine-grained compositional diversity beyond the trichotomy.

## Evidence summary

Unsupervised classification with hyperparameter selection for 10 groups. (p.6) Quote: "we further stratify all tumors into 10 stable groups".

## Conditions and scope

Group number (10) is a chosen resolution; alternative resolutions are possible.
