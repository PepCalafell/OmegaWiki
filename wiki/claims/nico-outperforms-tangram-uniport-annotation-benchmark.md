## Statement

NiCo outperforms Tangram and uniPort and matches or exceeds TACCO and cell2location in cell-type annotation accuracy across three benchmark imaging-based spatial datasets (intestine MERFISH, primary motor cortex MERFISH, mouse embryo seqFISH).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — Adjusted Rand Index and Jaccard similarity against author-annotated ground truths: NiCo outperforms Tangram/uniPort on all three datasets; matches or exceeds TACCO except on intestine ARI; cell2location higher on intestine and comparable on PMC (Fig. 2a).

## Conditions and scope

Authors' annotations treated as ground truths; three datasets; performance varies by tissue/dataset.
