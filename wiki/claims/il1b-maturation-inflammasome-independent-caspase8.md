## Statement

During efferoptosis, pro-IL-1β is matured by direct caspase-8 cleavage independently of caspase-1 and the canonical inflammasome.

## Evidence summary

Casp1-/- macrophages still cleave IL-1β; Casp8 D387A abolishes it. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.6).

> "IL-1β maturation was similarly dependent on caspase-8, but independent of caspase-1, suggesting direct cleavage of pro-IL-1β by caspase-8"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
