## Statement

F6 inflammatory myofibroblasts, F7 myofibroblasts, and F8 fascia-like myofibroblasts are disease-specific states absent from healthy skin, unified by a shared myofibroblast gene signature.

## Evidence summary

scPoli-derived disease-specific clusters with myofibroblast signature (Fig. 3c,g); distinguished by ECM/TGFβ and PIEZO2 (F7/F8) and fascia genes (F8).

## Conditions and scope

Diseased skin only.

## Counter-evidence

None.

## Open questions

Whether equivalent states exist subclinically in healthy aged skin.
