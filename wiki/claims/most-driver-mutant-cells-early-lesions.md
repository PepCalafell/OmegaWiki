## Statement
Lineage tracing and single-cell omics across pancreatic and oesophageal cancer models show that most mutant cells in early lesions do not give rise to cancer.

## Evidence summary
Reviewed in [[emerging-strategies-investigate-biology-early-cancer]] (Zhou, Tang & Wang 2024, Nat Rev Cancer).
