## Statement

The signalling integrator between sympathetic noradrenaline and eosinophil effector function is Adrb2 (β2-adrenergic receptor), not Oprk1 — establishing that Pdyn+ neurons act via co-released noradrenaline rather than via dynorphin opioid peptide.
