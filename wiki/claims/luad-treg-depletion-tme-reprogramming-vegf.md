## Statement

Genetic depletion of Tregs in a mouse LUAD model alters the transcriptional states of fibroblasts, endothelial cells and TAMs, enhances VEGF signalling, and produces increased vascularization with broad immunomodulation.

## Evidence summary

Mouse model evidence with paired transcriptomics.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse LUAD; not directly demonstrated in patients.

## Counter-evidence

Translation to human Treg-targeted therapies has been mixed.
