## Statement

The F6 inflammatory myofibroblast (IL11+MMP1+CXCL8+IL7R+) combines a myofibroblast core with an immune-related interleukin/chemokine/MMP program and elevated JAK-STAT and hypoxic pathway activity.

## Evidence summary

Marker dotplots (Fig. 3c) and PROGENy pathway scores (Fig. 3h).

## Conditions and scope

Disease-associated skin fibroblasts; maps to inflammatory CAF (iCAF) in cancer.

None.
