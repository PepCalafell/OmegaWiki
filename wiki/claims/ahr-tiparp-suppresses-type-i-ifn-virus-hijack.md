## Statement

AHR signalling activates TIPARP/PARP7, which ADP-ribosylates TBK1 and suppresses type I interferon production; multiple viruses (VSV, influenza, HSV-1, ZIKV, dengue, coronaviruses) exploit this AHR axis to dampen antiviral innate immunity.

## Evidence summary

Yamada et al. 2016; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
