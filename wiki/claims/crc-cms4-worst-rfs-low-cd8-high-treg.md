## Statement
CMS4 colorectal tumours have the poorest recurrence-free survival in the AMC stage-2 cohort and a distinctive proteome-level immune phenotype: low CD8+ T-cell and Th infiltration, high Treg, macrophage, mast-cell and eosinophil infiltration. This refines the proteomic profile of the CMS4 "inflamed phenotype".

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figures 8D, S8C; Table S8D.
- Concordant with prior RNA-level CMS4 immune characterisation (refs 86–88).

## Conditions and scope
- One discovery cohort (AMC stage-2 RFS); proteome signatures rather than IHC.

## Counter-evidence
None within scope.
