## Statement

Although phagocytic Macro_C1QC, anti-inflammatory Macro_THBS1, and profibrotic Macro_SLPI all originate from monocytes, Macro_SLPI follows a distinct developmental branch — pseudotime inferred.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "although they both originate from monocytes, the profibrotic Macro_SLPI signature follows a distinct developmental branch compared to the phagocytic Macro_C1QC or the anti-inflammatory Macro_THBS1 signature" (Han 2025, Fig. 2j).

## Linked ideas

- Lineage-tracing in mouse models would solidify branching origin of Macro_SLPI.
