## Statement

In TNF-activated phagocytes, efferocytosis of dying neutrophils is converted from an immunologically silent process into a lytic, caspase-8-dependent pyroptosis the authors term efferoptosis.

## Evidence summary

Defined across in vitro coculture and two in vivo models (TNF-induced SIRS, E. coli peritonitis). (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.1).

> "when activated with TNF, phagocytes efferocytosing neutrophils initiated a caspase-8-dependent, but NLRP3 inflammasome-independent, form of pyroptosis, which we termed 'efferoptosis'"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.
