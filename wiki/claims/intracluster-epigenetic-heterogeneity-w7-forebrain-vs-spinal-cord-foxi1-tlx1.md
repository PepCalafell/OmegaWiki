## Statement

Same WNN-cluster pixels at distant anatomical locations carry distinct methylation signatures reflecting region-specific TF binding: forebrain W7 pixels enrich FOXI1 (auditory development) at hypomethylated regions, while spinal-cord W7 pixels enrich TLX1 (spinal-cord maturation) — demonstrating intra-cluster epigenetic heterogeneity invisible to multi-modal cluster labels.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/spatial-joint-profiling-dna-methylome-transcriptome]], Fig. 5b.

## Conditions and scope

E11 mouse embryo; one specific WNN cluster (W7); two spatial regions compared.

## Counter-evidence

The analysis is illustrative on one cluster; generalisation to other clusters not exhaustively shown.
