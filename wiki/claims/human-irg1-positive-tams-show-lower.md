## Statement

In human lung tumors, IRG1-expressing macrophages have lower G6PD activity than IRG1-negative macrophages, validating the endogenous IRG1/itaconate suppression of G6PD in the human tumor microenvironment.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1471) — correlational (human) evidence.
  - Quote: "CD68+/IRG1+ macrophages exhibited lower G6PD activity than CD68+/IRG1− macrophages, which confirmed that endogenous IRG1/itaconate suppresses G6PD within the TME."

## Conditions and scope

Human patient tissue, single-cell-resolved in situ activity; associative (not perturbational) in humans.
