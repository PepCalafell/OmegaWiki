## Statement

The first scp-MS dataset spanning an in vivo human differentiation hierarchy quantifies >2900 proteins across 2500+ CD34+ HSPCs with ~68% missing values per cell.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mapping-early-human-blood-cell-differentiation]] (Furtwängler et al. 2025 *Science*, Fig. 1C).

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult human bone marrow; CD34+ selection by MicroBeads; FACS into single-cell wells; TMTpro 16-plex; RETICLE acquisition; SCeptre normalization.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
