## Statement

NOTCH3-dependent paracrine signalling among CAFs, pericytes and endothelial cells drives NSCLC invasion, collagen deposition, and induction of a TGFβ-related transcriptional signature linked to poor prognosis.

Mechanistic studies in stromal models and bulk patient transcriptomics cited by the review.
