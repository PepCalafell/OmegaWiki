## Statement

A six-gene keratinocyte-cancer signature — SOX2, LAMP3 (CD208), CXCL10, CXCL9, CCL5, UBE2C — is consistently upregulated across scRNA-seq and three spatial transcriptomics platforms (Visium, CosMx, Xenium), with CXCL9/CXCL10 the most significant.

## Evidence summary

Orthogonal cross-platform validation against a healthy-donor baseline; biological plausibility (SOX2 cancer-initiating TF, chemokines in T-cell infiltration, LAMP3 mature-DC marker, UBE2C cell division). (p.6)

## Conditions and scope

Keratinocyte cancers (cSCC); the markers are candidates for perturbation studies.

## Counter-evidence

Chemokines may reflect immune infiltrate rather than KC-intrinsic expression.

## Open questions

Functional necessity of each marker in KC carcinogenesis.
