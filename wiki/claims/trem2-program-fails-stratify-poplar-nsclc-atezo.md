## Statement

Applying the IMbrave150-validated top-10 TREM2 mac program score to POPLAR phase II NSCLC patients (atezolizumab vs docetaxel) fails to stratify response or overall survival — demonstrating that the predictive value of the TREM2 program is tissue-dependent and does not generalise from HCC to NSCLC.

## Evidence summary

- Same 10-gene score and quartile-cut method as IMbrave150.
- No significant difference in OS or response rate between TREM2hi and TREM2lo POPLAR patients.

## Conditions and scope

- POPLAR is a phase II atezo-monotherapy NSCLC trial; smaller than IMbrave150.
- Negative result; absence-of-evidence vs evidence-of-absence caveat.

## Counter-evidence

- Could reflect a different mac-state landscape or different baseline TREM2 expression in NSCLC; alternative scoring or signatures might still work.
