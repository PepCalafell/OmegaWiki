## Statement

ALI native immune organoids preserve TCR clonotype diversity and tumour-infiltrating T cell repertoires of cognate fresh biopsies.

## Evidence summary

Single-cell RNA sequencing and V(D)J sequencing demonstrated that native immune organoid models accurately preserve immune diversity and tumour-infiltrating T cell clonotypes of cognate fresh tumour biopsies (Polak et al. 2024, p.530; Neal et al. 2018).

## Conditions and scope

ALI PDOs from melanoma, RCC, NSCLC; biopsy-matched scRNA + V(D)J seq.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded at ingest.
