## Statement

Elevated HIF-1α protein expression — measured by immunohistochemistry, mRNA signatures, or downstream transcriptional surrogates — is consistently associated with worse disease-free survival, overall survival, and clinicopathologic markers of aggressive disease across major solid tumor types: colorectal, non-small cell lung cancer, gastric cancer, breast cancer, pancreatic cancer, and prostate cancer. The association is mechanistically grounded in HIF-1α-driven glycolysis, EMT, chemoresistance, multidrug efflux (P-gp), and immune evasion. HIF-2α prognostic value can diverge from HIF-1α (favorable in pancreatic cancer, unfavorable in breast cancer).

## Evidence summary

Multiple cohort studies cited across Luo 2022 disease chapters. Convergent meta-analytic evidence supports HIF-1α as a robust pan-cancer adverse prognostic biomarker.

## Conditions and scope

- IHC nuclear HIF-1α staining is most commonly assayed
- mRNA hypoxia signatures (Buffa, Winter, Sørensen) are validated surrogates
- HIF-2α prognostic direction depends on tumor type — pancreatic cancer shows favorable HIF-2α

## Counter-evidence

In subsets of breast cancers, diffuse normoxic HIF-1α expression carries favorable prognosis (vs perinecrotic hypoxic HIF-1α). Pancreatic cancer HIF-2α is favorable.
