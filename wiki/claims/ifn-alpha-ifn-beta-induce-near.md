## Statement

IFN-α and IFN-β elicit highly concordant transcriptomic programs across all 13 immune subsets; fold changes of shared genes are nearly identical despite IFN-β being used at one-tenth the IFN-α concentration.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.5). Both signal through IFNAR1/IFNAR2 ([[type-interferon-ifna-ifnb]]).

## Conditions and scope

In-vitro stimulation; specific concentrations used.

## Counter-evidence

A small number of genes uniquely regulated by either IFN, but at lower fold changes.

## Open questions

Whether concentration-matched IFN-α/β would diverge further.
