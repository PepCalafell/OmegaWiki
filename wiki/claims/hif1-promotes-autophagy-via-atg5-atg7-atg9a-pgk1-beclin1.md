## Statement

Hypoxia-induced HIF-1 activation engages autophagy through two mechanisms: (i) transcriptional upregulation of core autophagy genes ATG5, ATG7, ATG9A; (ii) post-translational activation through PGK1, which acquires moonlighting protein kinase activity under glutamate/oxygen deprivation to phosphorylate Beclin1 at Ser30 and bind the ATG14/VPS34/Beclin1 complex. The result is enhanced autophagic flux supporting tumor cell survival under hypoxic stress.

## Evidence summary

Transcriptional induction is demonstrated by ChIP-qPCR at HREs in ATG promoters. PGK1 kinase function is characterized by Lu and colleagues (referenced in Luo 2022). Pharmacological autophagy inhibition sensitizes hypoxic tumors.

## Conditions and scope

- Tumor cells under chronic hypoxia + nutrient limitation
- Engaged by mTORC1 inhibition and PHD-PP2A axis
- Bulk and selective autophagy (mitophagy) both modulated

## Counter-evidence

In some myocyte and immune-cell contexts, hypoxia rapidly inhibits autophagy via HK2-mediated TORC1 effects.
