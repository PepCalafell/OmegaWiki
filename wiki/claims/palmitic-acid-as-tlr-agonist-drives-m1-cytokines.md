## Statement

Palmitic acid engages TLR signaling on macrophages and drives the pro-inflammatory cytokine cascade (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6), contributing to high-fat-diet-driven metabolic inflammation.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/lipid-metabolism-homeostasis-disease]] from multiple primary studies of SFA-macrophage TLR engagement.

## Conditions and scope

- High-SFA / palmitate-rich settings; physiological vs supraphysiological dosing debated.

## Counter-evidence

- Some studies argue indirect activation via cholesterol / co-stimuli rather than direct TLR4 binding.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

- Whether direct palmitate binding to TLR4 or sterol-co-activation is the operative mechanism.
