## Statement

In mouse small-intestine MERFISH, stem/TA cell Fa1 (Olfm4/Lgr5/Hopx) significantly covaries with Paneth Fa1 (progenitor markers; log10P = -1.43, 217 co-localized pairs) and goblet Fa2 (log10P = -4.00).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — Ridge regression: stem/TA Fa1 ↔ Paneth Fa1 (log10P=-1.43, 217/1107/184 pairs); stem/TA Fa1 ↔ goblet Fa2 (log10P=-4.00); supported by top-correlating genes recovering stem markers and Paneth-progenitor markers (Fig. 4f–g).

## Conditions and scope

Single MERFISH intestinal dataset (Petukhov 2022).

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
