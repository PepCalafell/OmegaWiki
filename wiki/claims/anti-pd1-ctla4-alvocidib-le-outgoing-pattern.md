## Statement
In-silico perturbation predicts that immune-checkpoint blockade (anti-PD-1, anti-CTLA-4) and the CDK inhibitor Alvocidib should be effective in OSCC by reversing LE-state biology.

## Evidence summary
Supplementary Fig. 6b–f; cross-reference to AML Alvocidib trials and OSCC CDK-inhibitor preclinical work.

## Conditions and scope
In-silico only; no experimental validation in OSCC PDX or patients.

## Counter-evidence
Anti-PD-1/anti-CTLA-4 clinical activity in OSCC remains modest in unselected populations; the prediction may not generalise.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Whether OSCC patients with high LE score are differentially responsive to ICB.
