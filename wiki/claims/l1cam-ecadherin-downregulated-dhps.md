## Statement

Specific adhesion molecules L1CAM and E-cadherin are reduced both transcriptionally and at the protein level in DHPS-deficient macrophages, providing concrete molecular readouts of the adhesion defect.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 4h, Supplementary Table 6); E-cadherin expression is known to be polyamine-dependent in alternatively activated macrophages.

## Conditions and scope

Validated across tissues and at protein level by flow cytometry.

## Counter-evidence

None.
