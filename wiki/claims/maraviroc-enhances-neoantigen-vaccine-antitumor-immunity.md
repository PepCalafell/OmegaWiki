## Statement

Maraviroc given during neoantigen-peptide+poly(I:C) vaccination reduced metastatic burden 7.5-fold versus unvaccinated and 3.1-fold versus vaccine without maraviroc, indicating transient CCR5 blockade during priming enhances DC-based neoantigen vaccine efficacy.

## Evidence summary

Surface metastasis quantification (Fig. 5f; P=0.0035 vaccine vs vaccine+maraviroc). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse B16F10 melanoma; windowed CCR5 blockade during priming.
