## Statement

The rs34415530 T allele reduces IKZF4 expression specifically in CD4 Treg-FOXP3 cells, proposed to impair Treg suppression of dendritic cells, thereby raising circulating IL-12B and asthma susceptibility.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]] (mechanistic): IKZF4 was a CD4 Treg-FOXP3–specific eGene (PeQTL=3.1e-8); SMR linked the locus to IL-12B (PSMR=6.7e-5) and to asthma (p.9). Mechanism is a proposed model.

## Conditions and scope

CD4 Treg-FOXP3; associative SMR plus literature-based mechanistic hypothesis (not experimentally validated here).
