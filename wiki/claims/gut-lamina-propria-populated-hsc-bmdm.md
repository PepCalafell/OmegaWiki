## Statement

Unlike most adult tissues where the macrophage compartment is dominated by yolk-sac-EMP-derived self-renewing TRMs, the gut lamina propria is continuously replenished by HSC-derived monocytes and BMDMs after weaning. This makes the gut lamina propria a notable, well-documented exception to the EMP-TRM dominance model.

## Evidence summary

The review [[papers/physiology-diseases-tissue-resident-macrophages]] consolidates fate-mapping and chimerism evidence (CCR2, MS4A3 monocyte tracing, Ms4a3-Cre lineage labelling) showing the lamina-propria compartment turns over from blood after weaning, while neighbouring submucosal and myenteric-plexus macrophages remain embryo-derived. BMDM persistence in this niche may itself involve some local self-maintenance.

## Conditions and scope

- Adult mouse gut, post-weaning. Pre-weaning gut macrophages have a different ontogeny that is partially yolk-sac-derived.
- Compartment-specific: lamina propria vs submucosa vs myenteric plexus vs Peyer's patches differ.
- Inflammation (e.g. IBD models) accelerates monocyte recruitment further.

## Counter-evidence

- A residual EMP-derived contribution to lamina-propria macrophages persists into adulthood in some studies.
- Whether BMDM-derived gut macrophages can functionally substitute for TRMs is unclear; their tissue-instructive transcriptome is distinct from canonical EMP-derived TRMs.
