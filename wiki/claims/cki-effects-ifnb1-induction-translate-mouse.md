## Statement

The mechanism-discriminating effects observed in mouse macrophages reproduce in human THP-1 macrophage-like cells: TBK1-targeting Midostaurin reduces IFNB1 induction (LPS/dsDNA) whereas JAK-specific Filgotinib does not affect IFNB1 but reduces IFNβ-driven RSAD2.

## Evidence summary

THP-1 experiments separated TBK1–IRF3 effects (IFNB1 after LPS/dsDNA) from JAK–STAT effects (RSAD2 after IFNβ), confirming the mouse findings cross species. Links [[foundations/midostaurin]], [[foundations/momelotinib]], [[foundations/filgotinib]], [[foundations/tbk1-tank-binding-kinase-1]].

## Conditions and scope

Human THP-1 differentiated with PMA; specific stimuli (LPS, IFNβ, dsDNA).

## Counter-evidence

THP-1 is a transformed monocytic cell line, an imperfect proxy for primary human macrophages.
