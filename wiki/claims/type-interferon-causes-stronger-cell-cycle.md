## Statement
The temporary reduction of basic cell functions (cell cycle, metabolism) in macrophages is specific to the type-I interferon response (triggered by IFN-β, Listeria, and LPS) and is much less pronounced for the type II interferon IFN-γ.

## Evidence summary
Cluster-level enrichment analysis showed cell-cycle/metabolism clusters downregulated for IFN-β, Listeria, LPS but not IFN-γ ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figure 2E-H).

## Conditions and scope
Murine in vitro macrophages; the authors explicitly flag a possible species-specific difference relative to a prior human PBMC-derived macrophage study.

## Counter-evidence
A prior study reported stronger IFN-γ effects in human PBMC-derived M-CSF macrophages — the contrast may reflect species differences.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions
Is the IFN-I vs IFN-γ asymmetry in cell-cycle repression species-specific or context-specific?
