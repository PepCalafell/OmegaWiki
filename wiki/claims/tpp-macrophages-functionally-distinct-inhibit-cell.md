## Statement
TPP-activated macrophages are functionally distinct from M1/M2: they secrete CXCL5 and uniquely IL-1α, carry a distinct miRNA profile, and strongly inhibit T cell proliferation.

## Evidence summary
- "CXCL5 secretion was significantly induced by MTPP ... and IL-1α was only secreted by MTPP" (p.279, Figure 4C).
- "MTPP showed a strong inhibitory effect, clearly demonstrating that macrophage activation by TPP induced an effector program distinct from M1 and M2" (p.279, Figure 4D).
- MTPP had elevated hsa-miR-125a-5p and lacked M1/M2-associated miRNAs (p.281, Figure 4E).

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro functional assays.
