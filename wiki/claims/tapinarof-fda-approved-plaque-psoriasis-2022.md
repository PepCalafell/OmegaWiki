## Statement

Tapinarof cream 1% (Dermavant Sciences, VTAMA) is the first AHR-agonist therapeutic to be approved by the US FDA (2022) — for topical treatment of adult plaque psoriasis — demonstrating clinical proof-of-concept for AHR agonism with strong efficacy, durable remission and minimal systemic absorption.

## Evidence summary

Phase III PSOARING-1/2 and the PSOARING-3 long-term extension; reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
