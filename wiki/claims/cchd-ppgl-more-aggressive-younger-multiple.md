## Statement

Systemic hypoxia modulates PPGL phenotype toward earlier onset, multifocality, paraganglioma predominance, and greater metastatic risk.

## Evidence summary

Univariate regression odds ratios on a 989-patient cohort.

## Conditions and scope

Genetic status assumed (not always confirmed) for the extended cohort.

## Counter-evidence

Univariate analysis; residual confounding possible.

## Open questions

Whether EPAS1-GOF specifically (vs hypoxia generally) drives the metastatic propensity.
