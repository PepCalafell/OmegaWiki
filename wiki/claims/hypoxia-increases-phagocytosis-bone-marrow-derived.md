## Statement

Hypoxia (with LPS stimulation) functionally increases phagocytosis in primary BMDM, providing functional validation for the hypoxia-driven upregulation of phagocytic-GAM marker genes.

## Evidence summary

Functional assay confirming transcriptomic predictions (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.6).

> "We confirmed increased phagocytosis in BMDMs upon hypoxia and lipopolysaccharide (LPS) stimulation, supporting the functional relevance of the observed transcriptomic changes"

## Conditions and scope

Primary mouse BMDM; in vitro hypoxia with LPS.

## Counter-evidence

Tested with LPS co-stimulation; hypoxia-only effect on phagocytosis less directly isolated.

_None yet._

## Open questions

Whether enhanced phagocytosis serves necrotic-debris clearance in hypoxic tumor cores in vivo.
