## Statement
Stimulation with TNF, PGE2, and the TLR2 ligand P3C (TPP) induces coexpression modules (30, 32, 33) that are absent in IFN-γ (M1) or IL-4 (M2) macrophages, supporting a distinct chronic-inflammation program.

## Evidence summary
- "TNF, PGE2, and P3C (TPP, MTPP) induced a strong signal in modules 30, 32, and 33, which were not present in IFN-γ or IL-4 stimulated cells" (p.278).
- TPP signaling induced GO terms including "chronic inflammatory response" (p.279).

## Conditions and scope
In-vitro WGCNA; TPP linked to tuberculosis / granulomatous inflammation contexts.
