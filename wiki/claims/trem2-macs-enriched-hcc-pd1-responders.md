## Statement

In two independent neoadjuvant cohorts of resectable HCC (cemiplimab; cemiplimab+SBRT), the intratumoral abundance of TREM2-expressing mo-mac is significantly elevated in patients who exhibited pathological response to PD-1 blockade compared with non-responders.

## Evidence summary

- Discovery cohort: 20 patients (NCT03916627 Cohort B) treated with 2 cycles of cemiplimab pre-resection; pathological response 30%.
- Validation cohort: 21 patients (NCT03916627 Cohort B2) treated with cemiplimab + SBRT; scRNA-seq on 16; 6 responders.
- TREM2 mac frequency among mo-macs significantly enriched in responder tumor samples in both cohorts.

## Conditions and scope

- Restricted to resectable HCC, neoadjuvant PD-1 blockade with cemiplimab (± SBRT).
- Frequency, not absolute count.
- Pathologic-response definition is the surgical-pathology necrosis threshold, not RECIST.

## Counter-evidence

- TREM2 macs in NSCLC are pro-tumorigenic and limit NK infiltration ([[papers/tissue-resident-macrophages-provide-pro-tumorigenic]]); tissue-context dependence resolves the apparent conflict.
