## Statement

Cell-specific deletion of Ahr in TEK-expressing endothelial cells fully phenocopies whole-body Ahr-KO susceptibility to Plasmodium chabaudi (high parasitaemia, severe anaemia, early mortality). AHR signalling in the endothelium — rather than in haematopoietic / parenchymal cells — is therefore the critical compartment for malaria resistance.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing ref 110. Mechanism overlaps with cerebral-malaria observations in P. berghei ANKA, where Ahr-KO mice show increased leukocyte rolling/adherence and vascular permeability in the brain microvasculature.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in mouse P. chabaudi blood-stage; extension to cerebral malaria (P. berghei) suggested but not formally tested by endothelial-specific KO.

## Counter-evidence

None within the review's scope.
