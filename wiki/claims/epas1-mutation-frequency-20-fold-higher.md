## Statement

Systemic hypoxia is associated with a ~20-fold increase in EPAS1 mutation frequency in sympathetic PPGL (88.8% vs 4.5%).

## Evidence summary

Comparison of the CCHD cohort against TCGA normoxic PPGL, P < 0.0001.

## Conditions and scope

Sympathetic PPGL lineage; relies on TCGA as the normoxic comparator.

## Counter-evidence

None reported.

## Open questions
