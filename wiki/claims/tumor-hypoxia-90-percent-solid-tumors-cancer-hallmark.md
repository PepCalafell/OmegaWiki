## Statement

Hypoxia (pO₂ < 10 mmHg) is present in roughly 90% of solid tumors and is considered a hallmark of cancer, with documented prevalence in pancreatic, head-and-neck, breast, cervical, melanoma, prostate, and HNSCC patients.

## Evidence summary

Quoted directly from [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al., *Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy* 2023). Aggregates over 60 years of polarographic and clinical evidence.

## Conditions and scope

Applies to solid tumors. Liquid tumors (leukemia/lymphoma) excluded. Variability in measurement methods.

## Counter-evidence

Hypoxia detection varies with measurement: PET-based hypoxia (FMISO) often disagrees with mRNA signatures; pseudohypoxia (VHL/IDH-mutant) gives elevated HIF activation without low pO₂.
