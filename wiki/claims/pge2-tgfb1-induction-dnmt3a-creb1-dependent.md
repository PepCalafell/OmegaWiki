## Statement

The PGE2 → EP2/EP4 → p-CREB1 → Tgfb1 axis requires DNMT3A through an unresolved mechanism — DNMT3A loss abolishes PGE2-induced p-CREB1 phosphorylation and Tgfb1 induction, even when COX2/PGE2 are bypassed exogenously.

## Evidence summary

PGE2 rescue fails in DNMT3A-KO; p-CREB1 induction by PGE2 is absent in DNMT3A-KO; siCreb1 phenocopies the block. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM only; mechanism of DNMT3A dependence on p-CREB1 is unresolved.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
