## Statement

The immature, DHPS-deficient transitional macrophage state observed in the peritoneum has a conserved signature detectable in the lung, indicating a tissue-agnostic block in the monocyte-to-RTM transition.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Extended Data Fig. 8); supports [[concepts/polyamine-hypusine-axis-macrophage-residency]] as a tissue-agnostic node.

## Conditions and scope

Signature transfer between two tissues; interpretive.

## Counter-evidence

Individual downstream effectors may still be tissue-specific.
