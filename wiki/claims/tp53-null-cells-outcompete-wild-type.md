## Statement

Isogenic competition assays with cells differing only in TP53 status (WT vs null) or BCL2 status (WT vs overexpressing) show that mutant genotypes outcompete WT under hypoxic conditions, with selection attributed to decreased hypoxia-induced apoptosis.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Isogenic in vitro competition. TP53 or BCL2 status modulated.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
