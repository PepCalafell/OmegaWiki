## Statement

The cholesterol-derived oxysterol 7α,25-dihydroxycholesterol forms a chemotactic gradient sensed by activated CD4⁺ T cells through EBI2/GPR183, positioning them at the B-cell follicle / T-zone boundary, where they differentiate into Tfh and license germinal-centre formation.
