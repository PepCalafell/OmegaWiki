## Statement

Pharmacological inhibition of glutaminase (GLS1) with CB-839 reduces tumour growth in breast cancer and melanoma preclinical models by decreasing MDSC recruitment and promoting M1-like macrophage conversion, while sensitising tumours to anti-PD-1 / anti-PD-L1.

## Evidence summary

- Multiple preclinical studies referenced in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Demonstrated in melanoma, breast cancer, AML, RCC, NSCLC preclinical contexts.

## Counter-evidence

- Inhibition of glutamine pathway is incomplete because tumours rewire via glucose anaplerosis.

## Linked ideas

- Pairing GLS1 inhibition with ICB.

## Open questions

- Whether CB-839 clinical trials demonstrate translation; results from NCT02771626 awaited.
