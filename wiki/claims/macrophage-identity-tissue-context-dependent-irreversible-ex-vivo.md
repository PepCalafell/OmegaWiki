## Statement

Unlike T cells, ex vivo splenic macrophages lose both JAK-STAT/IFN signaling signatures AND macrophage-specific identity gene programs upon 20h tissue-context deprivation. Neither M-CSF (a canonical macrophage growth factor) nor IFN-β stimulation rescues the broader identity loss, while IFN-β does rescue much of the JAK-STAT signature. Therefore macrophages depend on tissue context to maintain cellular identity itself through multiple parallel cues, not merely to maintain baseline JAK-STAT activity.
