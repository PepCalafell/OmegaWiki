## Statement

MnO₂-based nanoparticles exploit the acidic, H₂O₂-rich tumor microenvironment to generate O₂ at the lesion site, alleviating intra-tumoral hypoxia, enhancing photodynamic / chemotherapy / immunotherapy efficacy, and inhibiting tumor growth and metastasis in mouse models (e.g., iPS-MnO₂@Ce6).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]] (Chen et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse model evidence; clinical translation limited.

## Counter-evidence

Mn toxicity and biodistribution concerns; off-target O₂ generation in healthy tissue.
