## Statement

Long COVID exhibits IFN-γ-dominant but IFN-α-suppressed signaling: elevated IFN-γ scores in B cells (p=0.04) and CD14 monocytes (p=0.02) versus acute COVID, alongside markedly reduced T-cell IFN-α activity relative to acute and mild COVID.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.9), consistent with reported increased circulating IFN-γ in long COVID. Uses the [[ifn-ifn-ii-activity-deconvolution-scoring]] framework.

## Conditions and scope

Long COVID; small cohorts; cross-dataset.

## Counter-evidence

T-cell IFN-γ elevation only a trend (p=0.06).

## Open questions

Whether the IFN-γ/IFN-α imbalance is a biomarker or driver of persistent symptoms.
