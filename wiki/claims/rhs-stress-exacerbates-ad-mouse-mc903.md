## Statement

RHS is a sympathetic-dominant psychological stressor that exacerbates pre-established MC903-induced AD-like dermatitis in mice; restraint (PPR) and cage-change paradigms, despite raising corticosterone, do not. The effect requires already-established inflammation (pre-stress or co-applied RHS has no effect on naive skin).

Murine MC903 dermatitis only. Generalisation to HDM/SEB AD model confirmed in fig. S13.
