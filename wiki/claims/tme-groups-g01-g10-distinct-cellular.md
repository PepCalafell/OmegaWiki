## Statement

G01-G04 (T-centric, immune-hot) included CXCL13+ T-cell-rich G01 and TLS-like B-cell-rich G04; G05-G07 (M-centric) spanned microglia-rich G05 (glioma/MB) and SPP1+/VCAN+ macrophage groups; G08-G10 (S-centric, immune-cold) ranged from endothelial/perivascular G08 to LRRC15+ myCAF-rich G09 and PI16+ normal-fibroblast-rich, earlier-stage G10.

## Evidence summary

Cluster-composition characterization per group. (p.6-7) Quote: "G08-G10 exhibited low immune-cell infiltration but high proportions of stromal cells ... indicative of immune "cold" or stroma (S)-centric tumors".

## Conditions and scope

Descriptive cellular characterization; group boundaries are continuous in practice.
