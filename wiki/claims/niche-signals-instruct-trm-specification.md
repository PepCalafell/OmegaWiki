## Statement

Tissue-resident macrophage subset specification is partly driven by niche-derived instructive signals that induce or maintain lineage-determining factor (LDF) expression: TGFβ instructs PPARγ in alveolar macs and SALL1 in microglia and ID3 in Kupffer cells; IL-34 instructs microglia and Langerhans cell identity; retinoic acid instructs GATA6 in peritoneal macrophages; desmosterol via LXRα instructs Kupffer cell identity; CSF2 (with TGFβ) instructs alveolar PPARγ; haem instructs SPI-C in red pulp macrophages.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/physiology-diseases-tissue-resident-macrophages]] crystallizes the niche-LDF pairing table from a decade of macrophage transcriptomics and conditional KO studies. Lazarov & Geissmann 2023 favour a *stochastic-selection* over *purely instructive* model: PreMacs already express patches of LDFs at E10.25 before tissue arrival, suggesting niche signals select pre-existing expression rather than instructing it de novo. However the niche-LDF pairing is well-supported as a *maintenance* mechanism in adults.

## Conditions and scope

- Most evidence is murine.
- Niche signals are necessary but probably not sufficient — broad expression of TGFβ and RA argues against single-cytokine instruction.
- The stochastic-selection vs purely-instructive distinction is unresolved; this claim is agnostic between the two models.

## Counter-evidence

- TGFβ, retinoic acid, IL-34 are not strictly tissue-restricted — they are broadly expressed but locally elevated. This complicates a strictly instructive interpretation.
- PreMacs already show LDF patches at E10.25 (Mass 2016), supporting at least partial cell-intrinsic stochastic specification.
- Forced cytokine exposure in vitro does not readily install full TRM identity — cocktails are needed.

## Open questions

- Whether niche signals are upstream initiators of LDF expression or maintenance signals downstream of stochastic LDF expression.
- Whether unique cytokine cocktails can functionally substitute for niche signals.
- The role of mechanical / metabolic niche signals (oxygen tension, ECM stiffness) in TRM specification — underexplored relative to cytokine-centric work.
