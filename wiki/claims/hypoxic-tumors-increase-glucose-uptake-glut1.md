## Statement

Tumors in hypoxic mice take up roughly twice as much 18F-FDG as normoxic tumors, and GENEVA tumors upregulate SLC2A1/GLUT1 under hypoxia, indicating compensatory glucose uptake rather than glucose starvation.

## Evidence summary

- **systemic-hypoxia-suppresses-solid-tumor-growth** (p.5): "tumors in hypoxic mice took up twice as much 18F-FDG as those in normoxic mice" — 18F-FDG PET-tracer uptake (%ID/g) (Fig.3b); SLC2A1 upregulation across lines (Fig.3c); no SLC2A1-fitness relationship (Fig.S2a). (type: quantitative, strength: moderate)

## Conditions and scope

Preclinical mouse models (syngeneic, GEMM, and human xenograft) under continuous inhaled hypoxia (typically 8% O2) or pharmacological hypoxia; single preprint source (not yet peer-reviewed).

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in the wiki yet. Some cell lines (notably ccRCC) deviate from the dominant response — see [[claims/ccrcc-and-select-lines-resist-hypoxia-tumor-suppression]].
