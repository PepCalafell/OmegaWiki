## Statement
The splicing factor SFPQ and the histone acetyltransferase EP300 act as negative regulators (constrainers) of the interferon response in macrophages, and their knockouts produce unexpectedly similar transcriptional signatures — interpreted as coordinated regulation of overlapping gene sets at the post-transcriptional (SFPQ) and transcriptional (EP300) layers.

## Evidence summary
Cross-prediction similarity and shared ISG-upregulation enrichment for Sfpq/Ep300 KOs ([[papers/integrated-time-series-analysis-high-content]], Figures 5G, 6D, 6G; uses [[concepts/perturbation-cross-prediction-functional-similarity-graph]]).

## Conditions and scope
Macrophage cell line; similarity is computational; mechanistic link between layers is hypothesized.

## Counter-evidence
The transcriptional-similarity-implies-shared-regulation inference is correlational.
