## Statement

Wet-lab dose–response experiments in two lung cancer cell lines confirm the study's computational predictions: under hypoxia, cells become more resistant to camptothecin and bexarotene but more sensitive to Akt inhibitor VIII and PHA-665752. This experimental validation supports the reliability of the hypoxia–drug-response associations derived from omics data.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — A549/H1299 CellTiter assays under 1% vs 21% O2 (Fig. 7b).

## Conditions and scope

- Limited to two NSCLC lines and four drugs; 3-day in vitro exposure.

## Counter-evidence

- Cell-line monoculture lacks the in vivo hypoxic microenvironment and stromal interactions.
