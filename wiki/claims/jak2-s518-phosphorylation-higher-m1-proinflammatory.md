## Statement
The kinase JAK2 carries the phosphosite S518 at higher levels in the proinflammatory M1 macrophage state, consistent with JAK2's central role in the JAK–STAT proinflammatory axis.

## Evidence summary
- "Among the kinases with significantly higher phosphosite levels (FDR < 0.05 and Log2FC > 1.5) in the M1 state compared to either of the M2 states was JAK2 (S518), which is known to be related to the main axis of the JAK-STAT proinflammatory pathways" (p.10).
- JAK2 also appeared as a high-centrality kinase node of the M1 network (p.13).

## Conditions and scope
In vitro polarized macrophages; S518 is a regulatory (non-canonical) serine site rather than the canonical activation-loop tyrosine.

## Counter-evidence
None within the paper.
