## Statement

Tumour-cell overexpression of the methionine transporter SLC43A2 (LAT4) outcompetes T cells for methionine, lowering intracellular SAM, H3K79me2 dimethylation, STAT5 expression and IL-2 signaling, and producing T-cell dysfunction. Tumour-specific invalidation of SLC43A2 or methionine supplementation restores T-cell function and synergises with ICB.

## Evidence summary

- Bian et al. 2020 (Nature) and subsequent confirmatory studies, summarised in Ricci 2025.

## Conditions and scope

- Demonstrated in murine models; biomarker translation pending.

## Counter-evidence

- Methionine restriction can have antitumour effects on certain auxotrophic tumours.

## Linked ideas

- Connects amino-acid competition to T-cell epigenome (DOT1L/H3K79me2 axis).

## Open questions

- Tumour-selective SLC43A2 inhibition without disturbing T-cell methionine uptake.
