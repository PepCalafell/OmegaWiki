## Statement

In CIN-high tumors, chronic cGAS-STING engages non-canonical NF-κB and IL-6/STAT3 to drive metastasis.

## Evidence summary

Bakhoum 2018 Nature: STING-dependent, TBK1-independent NF-κB drives tumor cell-autonomous metastasis. Hong 2022 Nature: cGAS-STING-IL-6-STAT3 supports CIN cancer survival; tocilizumab + STING activation impairs TNBC. Li 2023 Nature: chronic CIN-STING dampens IFN, promotes ER stress.

## Conditions and scope

Chromosomally unstable / CIN-high tumors; non-canonical NF-κB branch dominates over canonical IRF3-IFN.
