## Statement

The Immune Dictionary — a compendium of single-cell transcriptomic profiles of over 17 immune cell types responding to each of 86 cytokines in mouse lymph nodes in vivo — and its companion software IREA are deployed as a single freely-available web application for the biomedical research community.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/immune-dictionary-immune-response-enrichment-analysis]] (Lai, …, Cui; AAI 2025 abstract), the web-portal companion to the original atlas paper [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Web application deployment of the mouse lymph-node Immune Dictionary plus IREA; portal at www.immune-dictionary.org.

None within the abstract's scope.
