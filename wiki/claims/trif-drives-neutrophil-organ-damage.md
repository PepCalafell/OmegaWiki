## Statement

TRIF mediates TNF-induced neutrophil recruitment, endothelial interaction, vascular permeability, Nos2/NO, and TUNEL+ death in lung and kidney.

## Evidence summary

Neutrophil depletion phenocopies TRIF deficiency. (tnf-switches-homeostatic-efferocytosis-lytic-caspase, p.2).

> "extensive infiltration of Ly6G+ neutrophils in the lungs of wild-type mice 4 hours after injection with TNF that was abrogated in Trif-/- mice"

## Conditions and scope

Mouse macrophages (TNF-induced SIRS / E. coli peritonitis); confirmed in human THP-1 where noted.

## Counter-evidence

None identified at ingest.

## Open questions

Whether this holds under chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF and high-dose bacterial challenge.
