## Statement

In the peritoneal cavity, myeloid-specific Ogt deficiency disrupts tissue-resident macrophage homeostasis — reducing large cavity macrophages (LCMs) and increasing monocyte-derived small cavity and converting cavity macrophages (SCMs and CCMs); TIM4+ mature LCMs remain decreased even during the resolution of infection.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/glcnacylation-shapes-macrophage-tissue-residency-alternative]] (commentary on Heieis et al.). Concerns the [[foundations/large-cavity-macrophage-lcm-peritoneal]] compartment and its [[foundations/tim-4-timd4]]+ mature subset; implicates O-GlcNAcylation in maintaining residency rather than monocyte recruitment.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse peritoneal cavity, steady state and post-infection resolution. Commentary-level confidence.
