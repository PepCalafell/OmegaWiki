## Statement

Across all 86 cytokine × 17+ cell-type combinations, the majority (72%) of DEGs in response to cytokine treatment are upregulated rather than downregulated, indicating cytokines act primarily as activators of new gene programmes rather than repressors.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

DEGs computed via two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum, |log2FC|>0.25, FDR<0.05.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope.
