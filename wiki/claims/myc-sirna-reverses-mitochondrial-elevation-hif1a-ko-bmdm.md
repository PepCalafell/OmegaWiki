## Statement

The elevated mitochondrial respiration and ATP production in Hif1a⁻/⁻ BMDMs is causally Myc-dependent: silencing Myc returns OCR parameters to those of HIF-1α-competent control BMDMs. This establishes the c-Myc compensatory mitochondrial axis as causal, not merely correlative.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] Fig. 6E-G.

## Conditions and scope

Short-term siRNA rescue (48 h); causal claim limited to basal OCR/ATP. Inflammatory and ribosomal-biogenesis phenotypes were not formally Myc-rescued.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper.
