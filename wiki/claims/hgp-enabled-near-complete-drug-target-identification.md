## Statement

Before about the 1980s drugs were found largely by serendipity with unknown targets; until 2001 the probability of knowing all of a drug's protein targets was below 50% in any given year. After the HGP, the targets are known for almost all US-licensed drugs each year.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Until 2001, the probability of knowing all of a drug's protein targets was less than 50% in any given year. The HGP changed this. Now, the targets are known for almost all drugs licensed in the United States each year." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

US licensing; "target known" as recorded in drug databases.

## Counter-evidence

Off-target / polypharmacology effects mean "all targets known" is an idealization.
