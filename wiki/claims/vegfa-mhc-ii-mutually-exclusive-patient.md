## Statement

At the protein level, VEGFA (angiogenic) and MHC-II (HLA-DR/-DP/-DQ) expression are mutually exclusive in tumor-associated macrophages from fresh human tumors.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2063) — correlational evidence. Intracellular VEGFA vs surface HLA-DR/-DP/-DQ FACS on CD45+CD11b+ TAMs across breast, colon, lung cancers (n=23).
  - Quote: "flow cytometric validation in 23 patient-derived breast, colon, and lung cancer samples confirmed that VEGFA (angiogenic) and MHC-II were mutually exclusive in TAMs"

## Conditions and scope

Protein-level, three cancer types; a single antibody panel.
