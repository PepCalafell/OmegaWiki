## Statement
At the proteome level, CRC CMS2 shows enrichment of mitochondrial gene expression and translation programs and small-molecule metabolism, biology that prior transcriptomic CMS2 analyses did not identify. The authors suggest mitochondrial respiration could be supportive of CMS2 cell growth.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 — CMS subtype DE text and TPCPA data portal "CRC CMS prot/mRNA" module.

## Conditions and scope
- 195 CRC samples; protein-level comparison; cross-cohort consistency reported.

## Counter-evidence
- Transcriptomic studies have historically classified CMS2 by Wnt/MYC signalling rather than mitochondrial biology; the proteome signal may reflect post-transcriptional regulation.

## Open questions
