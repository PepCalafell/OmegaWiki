## Statement

The timing of systemic hypoxia onset determines PPGL tumor lineage: congenital hypoxia drives sympathetic tumors, later-onset hypoxia drives parasympathetic tumors.

## Evidence summary

Comparison of congenital vs Eisenmenger-syndrome hypoxia groups; see [[concepts/developmental-time-window-systemic-hypoxia-tumorigenesis]].

## Conditions and scope

Clinical correlation tied to developmental immaturity of adrenal medulla at birth.

## Counter-evidence

Modest sample in the childhood-onset group (n=11).

## Open questions

Whether the parasympathetic bias reflects maladaptation rather than true tumorigenesis.
