## Statement

MTAP deficiency confers STING-agonist resistance by destabilizing IRF3 and co-deletion of chromosome 9p type I IFN genes.

## Evidence summary

Recent reports (cited in this review as refs 156, 157, 2024-2025) show MTAP loss destabilizes IRF3 and is frequently co-deleted with CDKN2A and type I IFN genes on 9p — predictive biomarker for STING-agonist resistance.

## Conditions and scope

MTAP-deficient / 9p-deleted tumors; relevant fraction includes glioblastoma, mesothelioma, pancreatic, esophageal.
