## Statement

KRAS-mutant lung precursors uniformly co-host IL1B-IL1R1 niche signaling and elevated NF-κB/interferon signatures, while KRAS-WT precursors do not — coupling oncogenic KRAS to the precancer-stage proinflammatory niche.

## Conditions and scope

Inverse pattern in invasive LUAD: KRAS-mutant LUAD spots have *lower* NF-κB / IFN signatures than KRAS WT LUAD. The stage-specific switch suggests context-dependent inflammatory rewiring.

## Linked ideas

- Mechanistic link to KRAS-NF-κB-driven oncogenesis literature; potential biomarker stratification for precancer interception trials.
