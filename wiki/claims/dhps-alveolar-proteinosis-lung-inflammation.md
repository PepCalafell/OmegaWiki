## Statement

Without functional alveolar macrophages to clear surfactant, Dhps-ΔM mice develop progressive alveolar proteinosis accompanied by lung immune-cell infiltration, indicating disrupted tissue homeostasis.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 5b-e).

## Conditions and scope

Age-dependent worsening; consistent with proinflammatory shift in omics data.
