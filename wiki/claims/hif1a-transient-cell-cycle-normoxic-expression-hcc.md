## Statement

Under normoxia, HIF-1α is transiently expressed in a subpopulation of HCC cells (Huh7, HepG2) at the G2→M cell-cycle transition — coincident with the cyclin B1/CDK1 peak — and is undetectable in asynchronous cultures. This cell-cycle-restricted pulse is not seen in HeLa cells.

## Evidence summary

- **oxygen-independent-expression-hif-during-cell** (p.3154–3156) — synchronization/release immunoblotting.
  - Quote: "Only the synchronized Huh7 WT cells expressed significant levels of HIF-1α at time points that coincide with the transition of cells from G2 to M (5 h and 8 h post-release) and then, to G1 phase ... in HeLa WT cells, HIF-1α remained undetectable at all time points."
  - Quote: "the same time-dependent expression of HIF-1α was also demonstrated in an additional HCC-derived cell line (HepG2)."

## Conditions and scope

Requires cell-cycle synchronization (RO-3306) to detect; a small cell-cycle-restricted subpopulation. Builds on prior colon-carcinoma reports of G2/M normoxic HIF-1α.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded. Synchronization perturbs physiology; the "transient" nature is inferred from population immunoblots across timepoints.
