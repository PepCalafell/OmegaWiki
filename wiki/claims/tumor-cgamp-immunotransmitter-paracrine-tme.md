## Statement

Tumor-derived cGAMP acts as an extracellular immunotransmitter, imported by TME cells via SLC19A1/SLC46A2/LRRC8.

## Evidence summary

Carozza 2020 Nat Cancer; Cordova 2021 ACS Cent Sci (SLC46A2 macrophage); Luteijn 2019 Nature (SLC19A1); Zhou 2020 Immunity (LRRC8). Tumor cGAMP recruits DCs, polarizes macrophages, activates NK cells, and can also kill T cells.

## Conditions and scope

Solid tumors with intact cGAS; underpins ENPP1-inhibitor therapeutic rationale.
