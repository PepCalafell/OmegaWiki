## Statement

AHR activity in CD8+ effector T cells — sustained via IL-2 signalling through STAT5 and the Thp1-5–HTP pathway — is required for the quiescence transition that produces memory T cells. Ahr-KO mice rechallenged with L. monocytogenes-OVA lack antigen-specific memory T cells and carry higher bacterial burdens; an independent gut-specific Listeria model also confirms an AHR role in CD8+ memory.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/role-ahr-host-pathogen-interactions]] citing refs 90 and 91. Older study (ref 89) found no AHR role in memory; discrepancy attributed to model, dose and timing differences.

## Conditions and scope

Demonstrated in Listeria-OVA primary + rechallenge protocols.

## Counter-evidence

Ref 89 in the same review; methodology differences highlighted by the authors.
