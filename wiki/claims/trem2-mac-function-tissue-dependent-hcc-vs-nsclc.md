## Statement

The immunological function of TREM2 macs is tissue-dependent: hepatic TREM2 macs in HCC associate with PD-1 blockade response and survival benefit, while NSCLC, breast, and ovarian TREM2 macs have been characterised as immunosuppressive. Tissue-specific DEGs layered on top of a conserved core (48 genes) likely underlie this divergence.

## Evidence summary

- HCC: TREM2-mac enrichment in PD-1 responders; sTREM2 elevated; IMbrave150 stratification.
- NSCLC: prior literature + POPLAR failure to stratify.
- Tissue-specific gene programs (209 liver, 89 lung) layered on conserved 48-gene core.

## Conditions and scope

- Causal mechanism not demonstrated.
- Generalisation to other liver pathologies (cholangiocarcinoma, metastatic disease) not tested.

## Counter-evidence

- Some preclinical Trem2-/- HCC studies (refs 45, 46) show increased tumor burden in knockouts — consistent with protective role in HCC.
