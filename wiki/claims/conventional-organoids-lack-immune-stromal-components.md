## Statement

Conventional 2D cell lines and standard 3D organoids represent only tumour epithelium without immune or stromal cells.

## Evidence summary

Both cancer 2D cell lines and conventional 3D organoid models typically only represent the tumour epithelium without immune cells, confounding their use in modelling the human TME and immunotherapy responses (Polak et al. 2024, p.524).

## Conditions and scope

Submerged Matrigel/BME PDO cultures, by design lacking exogenously added immune or stromal cells.
