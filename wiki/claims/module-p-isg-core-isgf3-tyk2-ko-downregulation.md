## Statement

Among the 16 JAK-STAT gene-regulatory modules in homeostatic immune cells, module P (16 genes) is highly enriched for the previously defined ISG-core signature and is consistently downregulated across all ISGF3 member knockouts (STAT1, STAT2, IRF9) and TYK2 knockouts — including the kinase-dead TYK2-K923E. Module P thus represents the canonical tonic ISGF3-driven baseline JAK-STAT output.
