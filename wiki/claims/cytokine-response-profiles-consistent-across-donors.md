## Statement

Despite donor baseline heterogeneity, cytokine response magnitudes correlate well across donors (r=0.55±0.23) and outliers are rare (mean 1.2±0.8 per strong-impact cell type), so a single consensus log2FC per cell type is meaningful.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: correlational.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.
