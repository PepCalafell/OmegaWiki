## Statement

Tumour-cell upregulation of complement regulatory proteins CD55 and CD59 inactivates complement-mediated cytotoxicity, lowering CD8⁺ T-cell killing capacity; combining anti-CD55 or anti-CD59 with anti-PD-1 produces synergistic antitumour activity in a mouse lung cancer model.

## Evidence summary

Mouse interventional model with synergy demonstration.

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung cancer; balanced by counter-protumour effects of C5a.

## Counter-evidence

C5a and other complement components can also promote tumour progression via MDSC recruitment.
