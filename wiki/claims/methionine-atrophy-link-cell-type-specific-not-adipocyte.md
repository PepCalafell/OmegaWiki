## Statement

L-methionine treatment that drives atrophy + hypermetabolism in C2C12 myotubes has no equivalent effect in 3T3-L1 adipocytes — the methionine-driven phenotype is cell-type-specific to muscle cells, despite both cell types showing one-carbon enzyme upregulation in vivo cachexia.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Treatment of 3T3-L1 adipocytes with different doses of L-methionine... did not affect lipolysis or glucose consumption, suggesting that the connection is indeed cell type specific."

## Conditions and scope

3T3-L1 adipocytes; matched concentrations and timing to C2C12 experiment.

## Counter-evidence

Adipose-tissue in vivo phenotype in cachexia (lipolysis, browning) is well-documented — the cell-line experiment may not capture all adipocyte biology relevant to cachexia adipose loss.

## Open questions

- Whether primary mature adipocytes or brown adipocytes respond differently to methionine challenge than 3T3-L1.
- Whether methionine drives a different (non-lipolytic, non-glucose) phenotype in adipocytes — e.g., effects on adipokine secretion.
