## Statement

Peripheral noradrenergic sympathetic neurons — not the HPA axis (see related claim) — relay stress signals to inflamed skin. Their chemical depletion prevents stress-induced exacerbation of dermatitis and eosinophilia while leaving baseline AD pathology intact.
