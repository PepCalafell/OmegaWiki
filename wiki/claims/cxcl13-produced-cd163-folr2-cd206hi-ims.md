## Statement

Cxcl13 was enriched along bronchial airways coincident with Cd163+Folr2+ CD206hi IMs lining Epcam+ bronchial epithelium in both melanoma and adenocarcinoma models.

## Evidence summary

High-resolution Xenium imaging co-localizing Folr2/CD163/Cxcl13/Epcam (Fig. 3c, Extended Data Figs. 4-5). See [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]].

## Conditions and scope

Mouse lung; spatial colocalization.
