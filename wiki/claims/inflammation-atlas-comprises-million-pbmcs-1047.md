## Statement

The study assembled a single-cell Inflammation Atlas of more than 6.5 million (6,340,934 after filtering) peripheral blood mononuclear cells from 1,047 patients spanning 19 diseases in five groups (IMIDs, acute inflammation, chronic inflammation, infection, solid tumors), profiled alongside healthy controls.

## Evidence summary

Reported directly in the abstract and Results ("An inflammation landscape of circulating immune cells", p.634). Built from in-house and public datasets, split into a Main atlas and two validation sets (unseen patients, unseen studies).

## Conditions and scope

Human PBMCs only; multiple 10x Genomics chemistries; predominantly European ancestry.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper. Scale claim is a descriptive resource fact.
