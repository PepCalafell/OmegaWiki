## Statement

Multivariate logistic regression across 33 cancer types shows a positive correlation between hypoxia signature scores and CN17, the HRD-associated copy-number signature, and with aneuploidy signatures.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/tumour-hypoxia-driving-genomic-instability-tumour]] (Suvac, Ashton & Bristow, *Nature Reviews Cancer* 2025).

## Conditions and scope

Bulk tumour, pan-cancer (33 types). Correlative.

## Counter-evidence

None substantive in the reviewed literature, though see Limitations of source paper for cell-line/tissue dependence.
