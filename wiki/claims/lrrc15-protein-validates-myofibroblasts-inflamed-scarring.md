## Statement

Protein-level profiling of the myofibroblast marker LRRC15 confirms myofibroblast presence specifically in inflamed scarring-risk skin (hidradenitis suppurativa) and its absence in non-inflamed or low-scarring-risk inflamed skin.

## Evidence summary

Immunofluorescence of LRRC15/ADAM12 in representative samples (Fig. 4c).
