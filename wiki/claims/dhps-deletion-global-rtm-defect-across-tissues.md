## Statement

DHPS deletion in myeloid cells produces a defect in mature RTM populations across at least seven tissues, while total F4/80+ macrophage presence is largely retained.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] (Fig. 1, Extended Data Figs. 1-4), using tissue-appropriate RTM markers ([[foundations/tim-4-timd4]], [[foundations/siglec-f-eosinophil-marker]], CX3CR1).

## Conditions and scope

Steady-state young adult mice; defect defined by mature-RTM markers, not total macrophage number.

## Counter-evidence

Macrophage number per se was not uniformly reduced; the defect is specific to mature RTM identity.
