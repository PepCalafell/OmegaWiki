## Statement

A non-canonical AHR transcriptional output operates through an AHR-KLF6 heterodimer binding non-canonical XREs (NC-XREs, which lack ARNT engagement). The complex recruits carbamoyl phosphate synthase 1 (CPS1) to enhancer regions, driving homocitrullination (carbamylation) of histone H1 at K34 and inducing peptidyl-arginine-deiminase 2 (PAD2). PAD enzymes then citrullinate intracellular proteins, linking AHR signalling to carbamylation/citrullination programs implicated in rheumatoid arthritis.

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews Joshi et al. 2015 (ref 266) and O'Neil et al. 2020 (ref 267, RA carbamylation).

## Conditions and scope

Mechanistic discovery from a single study; cell-type generalisability and disease relevance (especially in cancer) remain open.
