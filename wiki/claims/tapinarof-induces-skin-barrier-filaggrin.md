## Statement

Tapinarof induces expression of skin-barrier structural proteins (filaggrin, loricrin, involucrin, hornerin) and ceramide lipid components in keratinocytes, and activates Nrf2-driven antioxidant enzyme expression — combining anti-inflammatory and barrier-repair / antioxidant effects.
