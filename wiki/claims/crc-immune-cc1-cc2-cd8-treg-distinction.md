## Statement
Two reproducible proteome-derived immune consensus clusters of CRC (CC1 and CC2) capture an adaptive-cytotoxic vs innate-immune skew. CC1 is dominated by activated CD8+ T cells, T helper cells and low Tregs; CC2 has higher Treg, macrophage, neutrophil and mast-cell signatures.

## Evidence summary
- Knol et al. 2025 Figures 8A–8C; Figure S8B; Table S8A.

## Conditions and scope
- 195 CRC bulk DIA-MS proteomes; CC3 cluster restricted to a single (NKI) cohort and excluded from cross-comparison.

## Counter-evidence
- ssGSEA-based immune subtype calls are inference-only; no flow / IHC orthogonal validation in this paper.
