## Statement

L-methionine alone — at concentrations that drive myotube atrophy — also drives a glucose-hyperconsumption / elevated TCA-flux phenotype in C2C12 myotubes, replicating in vitro the hypermetabolism signature observed in vivo in cachectic skeletal and cardiac muscle.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "L-methionine altered myotube metabolism towards higher glucose consumption... acceleration of glucose metabolism feeding into the TCA cycle."

## Conditions and scope

Cellular medium glucose quantitation by enzymatic assay; 13C-isotopologue distributions by LC-MS.

## Counter-evidence

Phenotype could partially reflect ATP demand from amino-acid catabolism in methionine-loaded cells rather than direct one-carbon-cycle effect on glycolysis/TCA.

## Open questions
