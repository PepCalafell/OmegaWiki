## Statement

Hepatic TREM2 macs in HCC uniquely upregulate metallothioneins MT1G and MT1H compared with their NSCLC counterparts; the authors propose this confers oxidative-stress and ferroptosis protection adapted to the liver microenvironment.

## Evidence summary

- DEG-level upregulation in HCC TREM2 macs.
- Mechanism inferred from canonical MT1 biology (chelation of redox-active metals, antioxidant role).

## Conditions and scope

- Inference only; no in vivo or in vitro functional test in this paper.

## Counter-evidence

- MT1G/MT1H are induced by hepatocyte-derived signals and may simply mark anatomical context, not function.
