## Statement

In systemic lupus erythematosus, STAT1 and SP1 exhibited opposing, cell-type-specific activities — STAT1 elevated in non-classical monocytes where SP1 was reduced, while SP1 activity rose across multiple cell types implicated in SLE pathogenesis — revealing distinct regulatory layers within the interferon response.

## Evidence summary

Level 2 TF-activity analysis (Extended Data Fig. 2b,c; p.637). Consistent with cited SLE literature on STAT1 expression and myeloid IFN response.

## Conditions and scope

SLE; correlative TF-activity inference.

## Counter-evidence

Inferred activities, not measured TF occupancy or phosphorylation.
