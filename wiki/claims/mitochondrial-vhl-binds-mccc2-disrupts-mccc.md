## Statement

Inside hypoxic mitochondria, VHL binds MCCC2 and reduces MCCC1–MCCC2 holoenzyme assembly, inhibiting the leucine-specific carboxylation step of BCAA catabolism.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. 3A, S4A–B). Co-IP proteomics and MCCC1–MCCC2 affinity assays comparing VHL WT vs M2.

## Conditions and scope

Mechanism requires mitochondrial import (lost in VHL M2) and the VHL 184–186 motif / Y185 for MCCC2 binding.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.
