## Statement

DNMT3A is the enzyme writing methyl marks on the CpG-rich Dusp4 promoter during efferocytosis in macrophages; this methylation is the inferred mechanism by which Dusp4 is repressed downstream of efferocytosis.

## Evidence summary

MeDIP-qPCR shows AC-induced 5mC enrichment at Dusp4 promoter in WT not in DNMT3A-KO macrophages. Reported in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]].

## Conditions and scope

BMDM; bulk MeDIP-qPCR, not single-site resolution.

## Counter-evidence

None within paper's scope.
