## Statement

Macro_SLPI is a TAM subtype with diminished phagocytic and inflammatory programs but the highest EMT and focal-adhesion meta-program scores, identifying it as the principal profibrotic / ECM-remodeling macrophage in the pan-cancer TME.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "the Macro_SLPI signature evinces a diminished phagocytic and inflammatory capacity, yet exhibits a markedly elevated ECM remodeling capability" (Han 2025, p.6).

## Linked ideas

- Macro_SLPI as ECM-remodeling sister state to Macro_SPP1 and ECM-Mac.
