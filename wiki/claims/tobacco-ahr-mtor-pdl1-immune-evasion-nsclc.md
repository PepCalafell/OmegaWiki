## Statement

Chronic tobacco smoking drives PD-L1 upregulation in NSCLC through aryl hydrocarbon receptor activation and downstream mTOR signalling, reinforcing immune evasion.

## Evidence summary

Mechanistic and correlational studies in smokers cited by the review.

## Conditions and scope

Heavy/persistent smokers; mechanism may differ in occasional smokers.

## Counter-evidence

Smokers also exhibit higher TMB and immune infiltration that partially counterbalance PD-L1 effects.

## Open questions
