## Statement

DHPS-deficient cells still develop into F4/80+ macrophages (CSF1R expression unchanged) but fail to complete the residency program, remaining as immature monocyte-derived macrophages.

## Evidence summary

From [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]]; supports a two-step model in [[concepts/polyamine-hypusine-axis-macrophage-residency]] where [[foundations/csf1r-receptor]] drives initial development and the hypusine axis drives residency.

## Conditions and scope

Inferred from preserved macrophage numbers, unchanged CSF1R, and persistence of immature populations.

## Counter-evidence

None.
