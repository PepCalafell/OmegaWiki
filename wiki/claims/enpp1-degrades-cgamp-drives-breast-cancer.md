## Statement

ENPP1 degrades extracellular cGAMP and promotes breast cancer growth/metastasis by dampening anti-tumor immunity.

## Evidence summary

Wang 2023 PNAS: ENPP1 drives breast cancer growth and metastasis via degradation of extracellular cGAMP-STING signaling; pharmacological ENPP1 inhibition slows primary tumor growth and metastasis. Multiple ENPP1 inhibitors (RBS3418, SR-8541A, ISM5939, TXN10128) in Phase 1/2.

## Conditions and scope

Breast cancer and other ENPP1-high cancers; effect amplified by combination with ionizing radiation.
