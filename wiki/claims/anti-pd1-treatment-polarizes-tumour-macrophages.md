## Statement

Applying IREA to a published scRNA-seq dataset of mouse tumours treated with anti-PD-1 inferred that monocytes/macrophages polarize toward the IFNγ-induced Mac-b (M1-like) state and away from IL-4-induced Mac-e, and that NK cells polarize toward the cytotoxic NK-e state induced by IL-2/IL-12/IL-15.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Mouse tumour scRNA-seq dataset (Gubin et al. 2018); anti-PD-1 vs control.
