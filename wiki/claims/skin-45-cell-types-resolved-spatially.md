## Statement

The MERFISH+scRNA-seq integration resolves 45 cell types in adult human skin, including a refined fibroblast taxonomy (papillary, reticular I/II, perivascular I/II, perineural, DP-like, dermal sheath).

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Fig. 1d-f and Extended Data Fig. 4.

## Conditions and scope

Healthy adult skin only.

## Counter-evidence

Steele et al. 2025 (Nat Immunol) report six rather than eight major fibroblast subsets — partially overlapping nomenclature.
