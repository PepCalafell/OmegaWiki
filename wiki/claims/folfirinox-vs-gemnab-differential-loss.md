## Statement

In advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), FOLFIRINOX (folinic acid, 5-fluorouracil, irinotecan, oxaliplatin) is associated with greater skeletal-muscle loss in males whereas GEM/NAB (gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel) is associated with greater adipose-tissue loss in both sexes. The pattern of body-compartment loss is therefore regimen- and sex-specific.

## Evidence summary

Single-cohort retrospective serial-CT analysis (Klassen et al.).

## Conditions and scope

Advanced PDAC on first/second-line systemic chemotherapy.

## Counter-evidence

None directly contradicting; mechanism unclear.

## Linked ideas

- Regimen choice may need to incorporate body-composition risk.

## Open questions

- Mechanism of regimen-specific differential effects on muscle vs adipose.
