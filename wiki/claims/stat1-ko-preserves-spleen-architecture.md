## Statement

Stat1-KO mice exhibit normal overall spleen tissue architecture — white pulp and red pulp morphology is preserved, and the spatial localization of CD8a+ T cells and Cd33+ macrophages is similar to WT — establishing that STAT1 loss does not cause gross structural defects that could explain the baseline ISG loss phenotype. The baseline JAK-STAT collapse in Stat1-KO is therefore not secondary to disrupted tissue context.
