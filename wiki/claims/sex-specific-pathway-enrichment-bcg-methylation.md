## Statement

The pathways enriched among BCG methylation changes differ by sex: sex-hormone/GnRH (female short-term), nervous system (male short-term), infection/TLR (male long-term), neuronal (female long-term).

## Evidence summary

Distinct pathway enrichments per sex and timepoint, consistent with hormone- and neuro-immune crosstalk shaping the BCG response differently in men and women.

## Conditions and scope

Enrichment-based; suggestive CpGs.

## Counter-evidence

Annotation bias; indirect.

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

Whether these pathway differences drive sex-dimorphic clinical BCG outcomes.
