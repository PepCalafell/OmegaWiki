## Statement

Higher Macro_SLPI signature scores are significantly associated with worse overall survival in TCGA esophageal carcinoma (P=0.014) and skin cutaneous melanoma (P=0.0001), with consistent effects in additional cancer types.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "Higher Macro_SLPI signature scores were strongly associated with an increased risk of death in various cancer types, such as esophageal carcinoma (log-rank test, P = 0.014) and skin cutaneous melanoma (log-rank test, P = 0.0001)" (Han 2025, Fig. 2k).

## Linked ideas

- Macro_SLPI signature as a prognostic biomarker complementary to CAF signatures.
