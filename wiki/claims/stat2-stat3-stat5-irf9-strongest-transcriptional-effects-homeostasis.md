## Statement

Among the 12 JAK-STAT mutant models tested in homeostatic splenic immune cells, knockouts of STAT2, STAT3, STAT5 and IRF9 produce the largest transcriptome changes (differentially expressed gene counts) in both CD8+ T cells and macrophages. STAT1 KO has noticeably milder effects despite STAT1's central role in the ISGF3 complex — indicating that STAT1's main contribution under homeostasis is via the heterotrimer rather than as a solo TF, and that STAT2/IRF9 do extra non-STAT1-mediated work.
