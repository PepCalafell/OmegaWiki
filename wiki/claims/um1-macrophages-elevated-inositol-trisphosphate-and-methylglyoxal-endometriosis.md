## Statement

In the eutopic endometrium of donors with endometriosis, uterine M1 (uM1) macrophages exhibit increased conversion of myo-inositol-bisphosphate to myo-inositol-trisphosphate (via PLC isoforms — an NF-κB-activating step) and increased methylglyoxal production (a reactive metabolite that drives tissue damage and proinflammatory responses), nominating two specific metabolic axes that may sustain the proinflammatory phenotype of uM1 in endometriosis.

## Evidence summary

scCellFie differential analysis (Cohen's D >0.75, FDR <0.01) identifies macrophages as the only immune cells with significant metabolic dysregulation in endometriosis vs control eutopic endometrium (Fig 6a). Subsetting to uM1 macrophages reveals the two metabolic shifts above (Fig 6c). The findings dovetail with Mareckova et al. 2024 (not yet ingested in this wiki) reporting an imbalance and proinflammatory tilt of uM1 in endometriosis.

## Conditions and scope

Endometriosis cohort defined by HECA / Marečková dataset; eutopic-endometrium analysis only (ectopic-lesion macrophage metabolism inferred separately via Visium + cell2location).

## Counter-evidence

No orthogonal measurement of inositol-phosphates or methylglyoxal in primary uM1 cells.
