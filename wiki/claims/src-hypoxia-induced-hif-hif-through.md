## Statement

Hypoxia increases SRC expression/activity in a HIF-1- and HIF-2-dependent manner (SRC promoter carries HREs), providing the upstream signal that links oxygen tension to VHL Y185 phosphorylation.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]] (Fig. S6I–K). Kinase screen and HIF-dependence.

## Conditions and scope

Confidence moderate: HRE/HIF-dependence partly leans on prior literature; demonstrated in cell lines.

## Counter-evidence

None within scope.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[concepts/src-vhl-y185-phosphorylation-mitochondrial-axis]].

## Open questions

- Direct ChIP confirmation of HIF occupancy at the SRC promoter in these models.
