## Statement

In multiple DC subsets, AHR activation by tolerogenic ligands (ITE, FICZ in some contexts, VAF347, TCDD) reduces MHC class II, CD80, CD86, IL-6, IL-12 and TNF; increases IDO1, IDO2, IL-27, TGFβ, retinoic acid; and expands Foxp3⁺ Tregs and IL-10⁺ Tr1 cells.
