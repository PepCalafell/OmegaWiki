## Statement

A small set of cytokines (IL-1, common γ-chain interleukins, IL-4, IL-10, interferons, IL-32-β) are broad responders affecting many cell types; most cytokines elicit limited, cell-type-restricted responses.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/single-cell-cytokine-dictionary-human-peripheral]] (Oesinghaus, Seelig, Theis et al., bioRxiv 2025). Type: correlational.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro human PBMC (12 healthy donors), 24 h cytokine stimulation, Parse Biosciences split-pool scRNA-seq.

None within the paper's scope.
