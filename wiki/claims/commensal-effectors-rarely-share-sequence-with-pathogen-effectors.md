## Statement

The commensal effector repertoire is largely novel at the sequence level relative to
known pathogen effectors.

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.443–444): high similarity for only 0.5%
of strain and 3% of meta-effectors; even sensitive jackhmmer searches against ~124M
UniRef90 sequences found significant similarity for only ~5% (strain) and 22.5%
(meta-effectors).

## Conditions and scope

Pathogen effector reference set is finite (1,195 effectors).

## Counter-evidence

Meta-effectors show higher (22.5%) pathogen similarity than strain effectors.

## Linked ideas

Supports [[commensal-pathogen-effector-divergence]].

## Open questions

Functions of the sequence-novel commensal effectors.
