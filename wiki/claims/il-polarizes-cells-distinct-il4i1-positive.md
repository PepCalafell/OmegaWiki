## Statement

B cells exposed to IL-4 in vivo polarize into a distinct B-f state marked by Il4i1 upregulation along with St7, Samsn1, Tmem123, Dock10.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Resting lymph-node B cells, no antigen/BCR stimulation.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope.
