## Statement

Hypoxia significantly downregulates the homeostatic microglial markers P2ry12 and Tmem119 in microglia and macrophages, regardless of myeloid cell origin — opposite in direction to Lgals3.

## Evidence summary

Measured by qPCR across cell types (hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated, p.3).

> "the expression of microglial markers Tmem119 and P2ry12 was significantly downregulated. While Tmem119 and P2ry12 are microglia markers, their expression was detected in RAW 264.7 and BMDM cells, and was suppressed by hypoxia"

## Conditions and scope

BV2, RAW264.7, BMDM; in vitro hypoxia (<0.1% O2) and mild hypoxia/CoCl2.
