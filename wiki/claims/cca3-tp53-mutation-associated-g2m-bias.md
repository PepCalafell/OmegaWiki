## Statement

TP53 mutations are the most consistently G2/M-phase-bias-associated driver alteration across cancer types in TCGA, mechanistically explained by p53's role as a gatekeeper of the G1/S transition.

## Evidence summary

TCGA driver-mutation analysis (Fig. 5g, Extended Data Fig. 10a–b).

## Conditions and scope

Correlational at the bulk RNA-seq level; perturbation studies would confirm causation.

## Counter-evidence

None.
