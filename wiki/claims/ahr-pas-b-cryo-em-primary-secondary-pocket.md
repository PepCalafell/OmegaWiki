## Statement

Cryo-EM structural studies on the AHR PAS-B ligand-binding pocket — solved in complex with chaperones (HSP90 + AIP for human; HSP90 + PTGES3 for mouse) — define two binding subsites: (i) a primary pocket selective for planar π-electron-rich hydrophobic ligands via polar-residue hydrogen-bond/π-π networks, and (ii) a less geometrically constrained secondary pocket compatible with larger ligands. Indirubin occupies only the primary pocket.
