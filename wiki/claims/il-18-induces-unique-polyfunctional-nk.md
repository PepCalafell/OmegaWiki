## Statement

IL-18 triggers an NK polarization state (NK-f) characterized by upregulation of >1,000 genes — an order of magnitude more than IL-2/IL-12/IL-15 — including Myc (proliferation), Xcl1 (DC recruitment), Csf2/GM-CSF (myeloid maturation), Gzmb (cytotoxicity), and Kit/Batf (differentiation).

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

In vivo mouse lymph node NK cells, 4 h post IL-18 injection.
