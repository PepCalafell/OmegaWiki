## Statement

TNF+IL-18 induces the largest organism-wide transcriptional response of any cytokine pair tested (7,083 DEGs), exceeding TNF+IFN-γ (4,071) and TNF+IL-1β (2,452) and dwarfing all non-TNF pairs.

## Evidence summary

Quantitative DEG counts (limma, FDR<0.01, |log2FC|>2) at fixed dose 12 h post-injection, replicated across nine organs.

## Conditions and scope

Single dose, single timepoint; effect magnitudes will depend on dose, timing and tissue context.
