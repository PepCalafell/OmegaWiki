## Statement

The angiogenic capillary endothelial subtype capEndo_RGCC is tumor-enriched across cancer types and is computationally predicted (NicheNet) to be regulated by VEGFA, ANGPT1, FGF2, TGFB1, TGFA, and TNF — a candidate set of tumor-vascular activators.

## Conditions and scope

Direct quote: "Genes encoding the top 20 ligands inferred to regulate RGCC+ endothelial cells according to NicheNet" (Han 2025, Fig. 6g).

## Linked ideas

- capEndo_RGCC as bridging target between anti-angiogenic and anti-stromal therapy.
