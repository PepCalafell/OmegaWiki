## Statement

Elevated IL-4 signalling in bone-marrow myeloid progenitors generates immunosuppressive myeloid populations contributing to poor NSCLC prognosis. A first-in-human trial (n=6) combining dupilumab (anti-IL-4Rα) with anti-PD-(L)1 reduced circulating immunosuppressive monocytes and increased CD8⁺ infiltration in paired biopsies, with preliminary efficacy signals.

## Evidence summary

Preclinical mechanism plus very small first-in-human clinical signal.

## Conditions and scope

Six-patient trial; hypothesis-generating only.

## Counter-evidence

n=6 cohort cannot rule out chance; uncontrolled.
