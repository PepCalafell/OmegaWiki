## Statement

Predicted Tgfb1 (Kupffer) — Tgfbr3 (stellate) ligand-receptor pair, accompanied by stellate-side upregulation of antifibrotic proteoglycans Dcn and Bgn, mediates the Kupffer–stellate covariation in mouse liver.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/nico-identifies-extrinsic-drivers-cell-state]] — Ligand-receptor correlation panel and top-correlating-gene analysis on stellate Fa2 / Kupffer Fa1 identify Tgfb1 on KC and Tgfbr3 on HSC, plus Dcn, Bgn enriched among top stellate-correlating genes (Fig. 6b–c).

## Conditions and scope
