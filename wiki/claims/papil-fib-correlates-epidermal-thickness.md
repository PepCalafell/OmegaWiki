## Statement

Papillary fibroblast abundance correlates with epidermal thickness across skin sites (Spearman ρ = 0.26, P = 0.012), consistent with the proposal that papillary fibroblasts contribute to skin thickening as seen in volar-fibroblast intradermal therapy.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]] Fig. 4 / Extended Data.

## Conditions and scope

Correlative; no perturbation.

## Counter-evidence

Modest effect size; whether Papil Fib or Retic Fib II drives sole epidermal thickening remains open per Discussion.
