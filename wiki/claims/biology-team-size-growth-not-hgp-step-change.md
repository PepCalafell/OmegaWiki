## Statement

There is a common perception that the HGP marked a step change in collaboration size, but biology team sizes have in fact grown consistently since the 1950s; the HGP did not produce a discontinuity.

## Evidence summary

Quoted (sidebar 'No jump for big teams'): "There is a common perception that the number of authors collaborating on papers about the Human Genome Project (HGP) marked a step change. In fact, team sizes in biology have grown consistently since the 1950s." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

Author-count trend across biology; large-collaboration tails compared with fields like particle physics.

## Counter-evidence

Specific mega-consortia (HGP, ENCODE, 1000 Genomes) sit in the large-team tail even if the overall trend is smooth.
