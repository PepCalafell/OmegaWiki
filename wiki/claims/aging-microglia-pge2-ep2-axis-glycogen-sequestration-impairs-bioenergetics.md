## Statement

In the aged mouse brain, microglial PGE2 acting through the EP2 receptor sequesters glucose into glycogen, suppressing glycolysis and OXPHOS, generating an energy-deficient state that fosters neuroinflammation. EP2 blockade rescues bioenergetics and memory.
