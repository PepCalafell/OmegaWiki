## Statement

Among gut commensals, complete T3SS were found essentially only in Gammaproteobacteria
(notably Escherichia), and not in Beta- or Epsilon-proteobacteria (except Helicobacter).

## Evidence summary

[[effector-host-interactome-map-links-type]] (p.444): "T3SS were only detected in
Gammaproteobacteria, but not in Beta- or Epsilon-proteobacteria (except in Helicobacter
strains), and were especially common among Escherichia."

## Conditions and scope

E. coli resides close to the intestinal epithelium and frequently encodes complete
systems.

## Counter-evidence

Divergent systems in other classes may be missed by current tools.

Supports [[commensal-t3ss-host-directed-secretion]].

## Open questions

Whether undetected divergent systems exist in beta/delta-Pseudomonadota.
