## Statement

Genetic loss of IRG1 accelerates lung tumor growth and metastasis, increasing tumor burden and proliferation without affecting apoptosis, across multiple mouse lung tumor models.

## Evidence summary

- **irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor** (p.1461) — mechanistic (loss-of-function) evidence.
  - Quote: "the Irg1 loss significantly increased tumor growth and metastasis, with an increased tumor burden and proliferation but no change in apoptosis."

## Conditions and scope

Whole-body Irg1 knockout and orthotopic LLC1; effect on growth attributable in part to macrophage compartment (see bone-marrow transplant claim).

## Counter-evidence

Contrasts with pro-tumor IRG1 roles reported in ovarian, melanoma, colorectal, and pancreatic cancers — a context-dependence captured in [[claims/abcg2-itaconate-opposite-prognostic-value-lung]]. Single-source (lung) claim from [[papers/irg1-itaconate-rewires-macrophage-lung-tumor]].
