## Statement

In tumor-associated macrophages, the angiogenic gene program and the MHC-II-high program are mutually exclusive, and this anticorrelation is conserved across human and murine cancers.

## Evidence summary

- **functional-genetic-screens-reveal-key-pathways** (p.2063) — mechanistic evidence. scRNA-seq of 35,282 macrophages across 108 samples / 15 cancer types shows negative pairwise Pearson correlation between angiogenic and MHC-II AUCell signatures; validated by FACS in 23 patient samples.
  - Quote: "our analysis suggests that the mutually exclusive nature of angiogenic and MHC-II programs is a widely conserved feature in TAMs."

## Conditions and scope

Signature-level (AUCell) analysis; 'undetermined' cells exist that fit neither pole.
