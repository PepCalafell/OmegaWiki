## Statement

Although CD8+ T cell exhaustion was universal across rash types, exhausted cells carried few transcriptional differences between disease classes, leading the authors to interpret exhaustion as a shared chronic-inflammation end state rather than a causative, disease-specific driver.

## Evidence summary

Reported in Discussion of [[papers/classification-human-chronic-inflammatory-skin-disease]]. Supports [[concepts/treg-trm-expansion-cd8-exhaustion-chronic]]; relates to [[concepts/cd8-t-cell-exhaustion-texterm]].

## Conditions and scope

Author interpretation (mechanistic hypothesis), not a direct functional test.

## Counter-evidence

Few discriminating DEGs could also reflect underpowering rather than true mechanistic convergence.
