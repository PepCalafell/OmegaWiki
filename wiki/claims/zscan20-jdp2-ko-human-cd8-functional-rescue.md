## Statement

In human PBMC-derived CD8+ T cells subjected to chronic anti-CD3/CD28 stimulation, Cas9 RNP-mediated knockout of `ZSCAN20` or `JDP2` increases CCR7+ stem/memory-like fraction, lowers expression of LAG3, PD1 and TIM3, and increases the fraction of polyfunctional IFNγ+TNF+ and IL-2+ cytokine producers versus scramble.
