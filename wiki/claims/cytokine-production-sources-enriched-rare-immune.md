## Statement

FRCs expressed the highest number of distinct cytokines among lymph-node cell types, with basophils and ILCs also expressing large cytokine repertoires, consistent with prior observations of FRC immune-cartographer functions.

## Evidence summary

Reported in [[papers/dictionary-immune-responses-cytokines-single-cell]] (Cui, Hacohen et al., *Nature* 2024).

## Conditions and scope

Cytokine production inferred from transcript expression in PBS + perturbation conditions; equal-cell-number sampling.

## Counter-evidence

None within the paper's scope.
