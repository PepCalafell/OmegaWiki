## Statement

EPAS1-mutant PPGL tumors have a distinct oncometabolite profile (high fumarate, low succinate), associated with altered SDHB/FH enzyme expression and reproduced by HIF2α overexpression.

## Evidence summary

Metabolomics of human tumors and MPC HIF2α-overexpression model (Wilcoxon tests).

## Conditions and scope

Contrasts with SDHx-mutant tumors (high succinate); oncometabolites can disrupt α-KG-dependent dioxygenases and DNA repair.

## Counter-evidence

Correlational with respect to DNA-repair impairment.

## Open questions

Direct contribution of fumarate accumulation to MMR loss in these tumors.
