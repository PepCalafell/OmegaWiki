## Statement

Type I interferon signalling sensitizes breast cancer organoids to γδ CAR-T cell killing.

## Evidence summary

Live confocal imaging (BEHAV3D) of breast cancer organoids co-cultured with γδ TCR-expressing CAR-T cells indicated type I interferon signalling sensitization of cancer organoids to CAR T cell therapy (Polak et al. 2024, p.529; Dekkers et al. 2023).

## Conditions and scope

Breast cancer PDO + γδ CAR-T co-culture; live 3D imaging.

None recorded at ingest.
