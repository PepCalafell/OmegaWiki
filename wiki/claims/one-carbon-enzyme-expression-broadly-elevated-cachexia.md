## Statement

Across the five RNA-seq'd cachexia target tissues, one-carbon-metabolism enzymes are coordinately upregulated, but with isoform-specific tissue dominance: Mat1a and Nnmt in liver, Mat2a and Kmt2a/Kmt2b in muscle, Sat1/Gpx3/Gsta4 across multiple tissues. The pattern matches the metabolite-level tissue-typed methyl-acceptor signature.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "Mat1a (the gene product responsible for the conversion of methionine to SAM) and Nnmt (the gene product responsible for the conversion of NAM into MNAM) were strongly induced in livers of cachectic animals, in line with a previous report24, regulation of Mat2a, Kmt2a and Kmt2b (encoding enzymes producing methyllysines) was more specific to cachectic muscle... several changes were common across tissues, such as... Sat1, Gpx3 and Gsta4."

## Conditions and scope

Bulk RNA-seq; reproducible in PDAC models and humanised SW480.

## Counter-evidence

Not all methyltransferases are uniformly induced — e.g., Prmt6, Dnmt3a/b show tissue-variable patterns.
