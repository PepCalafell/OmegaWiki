## Statement

Hypoxia-associated changes across mRNA, DNA methylation, and miRNA layers act coherently on individual drug-response genes — exemplified by EGFR in oesophageal carcinoma, where upregulation, promoter hypomethylation, and loss of targeting miRNAs jointly track with sensitivity/resistance to specific drugs. This demonstrates that the hypoxic microenvironment shapes drug response through coordinated multi-layer regulatory networks.

## Evidence summary

- [[papers/characterization-hypoxia-associated-molecular-features-aid]] — ESCA integrative network linking mRNA/methylation/miRNA to drug response (Fig. 5).

## Conditions and scope

- Detailed for ESCA; a mirror-image network observed for genes overexpressed in hypoxia score-low samples.

## Counter-evidence

- Network is correlational and inferred across patients, not validated by perturbation.
