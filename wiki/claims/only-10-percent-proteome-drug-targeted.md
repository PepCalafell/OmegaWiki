## Statement

Only about 10% of the ~20,000 human proteins (2,149) are targeted by approved drugs; including experimental drugs raises the count to 3,119, leaving roughly 90% of the proteome pharmacologically untouched.

## Evidence summary

Quoted: "only about 10% — 2,149 — have so far been targeted by approved drugs … That leaves 90% of the proteome untouched by pharmacology. Experimental drugs in our data set increase this number to 3,119." (p.3)

## Conditions and scope

Depends on the drug-target database used; modality definitions affect "druggable".

## Counter-evidence

Some proteins may be genuinely undruggable; new modalities keep expanding the targetable set.
