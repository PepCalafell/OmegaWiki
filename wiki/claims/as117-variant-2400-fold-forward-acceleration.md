## Statement

Sampling and rebuilding the switch–binder fusion geometry to vary the magnitude and direction of the strained deformation produced variants with tuned kinetics; the fastest variant, AS117, achieved a 2,400-fold effector-induced (forward) acceleration of target dissociation.

## Evidence summary

"for the fastest variant (AS117), adding effector accelerated target dissociation by 2,400-fold, reaching a rate exceeding that of the original AS1" (p.4). Variants were selected for substantial AF2-predicted deformations spanning a variety of directions.

## Conditions and scope

In vitro SPR; designed AS1-derived variants.

## Counter-evidence

Fold acceleration varies non-monotonically with deformation; not all variants improved on AS1.
