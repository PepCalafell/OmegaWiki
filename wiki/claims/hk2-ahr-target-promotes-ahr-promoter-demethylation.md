## Statement

HK2, the dominant glycolytic hexokinase in cancer, is induced as an AHR target and itself reinforces AHR expression by promoting AHR promoter demethylation. The resulting AHR↑ → HK2↑ → AHR demethylation positive feedback loop links AHR activity to Warburg metabolism, with corresponding pan-cancer epigenetic patterns associated with poor overall survival.

## Evidence summary

[[papers/complex-biology-aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-activation]] reviews ref 195.

## Conditions and scope

Cancers where AHR is active and HK2 expression is induced — likely solid tumours under metabolic stress / hypoxia. Distinct from the AHR-promoter-hypermethylation pattern in ALL (cf. [[claims/ahr-promoter-hypermethylation-silences-ahr-all]]).

## Counter-evidence

— none reviewed.

## Linked ideas

- Connects AHR signalling to cancer metabolism / Warburg effect via HK2.
- Suggests dual targeting of AHR + HK2 in tumours with the positive-feedback signature.

## Open questions

- What is the mechanism by which HK2 promotes AHR demethylation — metabolite-driven (e.g. α-KG via OGT?), or via recruitment of demethylases?
- Does the AHR-HK2 axis explain part of the AHR-driven shift to glycolysis in TAMs and tumour cells?
