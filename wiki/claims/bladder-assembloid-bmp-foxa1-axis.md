## Statement

In 3D bladder assembloids, stromal-secreted BMP induces tumoural FOXA1 to determine tumour phenotype.

## Evidence summary

Genetic manipulation of 3D bladder assembloids bearing tumour epithelium, CAFs, endothelium, immune cells and a muscle layer identified that stromal-secreted BMP induces tumoural FOXA1 to determine tumour phenotype (Polak et al. 2024, p.525; Kim et al. 2020).

## Conditions and scope
