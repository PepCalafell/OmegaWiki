## Statement

Statistical filtering of Taiji PageRank scores across the 9-state CD8+ atlas partitions TFs into 136 single-state TF genes (each state harbouring 12–19 unique TFs) and 173 multi-state TF genes, including known regulators Tcf7 and Tbx21.
