## Statement

A primary route by which oxygen availability — through HIF — shapes immune effector function is by setting the cell's metabolic strategy: HIF-driven changes in glycolysis and other pathways translate microenvironmental hypoxia into altered immune cell behaviour.

## Evidence summary

Reviewed in [[papers/regulation-immunity-inflammation-hypoxia-immunological-niches]]. Builds on [[claims/hif1-promotes-glycolysis-represses-oxphos]].

## Conditions and scope

A general organizing mechanism rather than the sole one; HIF also acts via direct target genes (barrier proteins, checkpoint molecules, antimicrobial factors).

## Counter-evidence

None within scope; presented as the leading ("appears to be") mechanism.
