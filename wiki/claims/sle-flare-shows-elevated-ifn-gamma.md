## Statement

During systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) flare, T cells (all subsets except Tregs) show significantly elevated IFN-γ (IFN-II) scores relative to managed disease (p=0.001), while IFN-α scores remain comparable; naïve B cells show elevated IFN-α.

## Evidence summary

Supported by [[dissecting-type-ii-interferon-impacts-human]] (p.9). Implicates IFN-II, not just the canonical IFN-I signature, in lupus flare. See [[ifn-gamma-cytokine]].

## Conditions and scope

SLE; flare vs managed; small cohorts.

## Counter-evidence

IFN-α scores did not distinguish flare from managed.

## Open questions

Whether T-cell IFN-γ elevation is a cause or consequence of flare.
