## Statement

The authors propose a two-stage model of RTM development: CSF1R-driven initial macrophage generation, followed by a polyamine–hypusine–eIF5A-dependent stage that confers tissue occupancy and tissue-specific identity.

## Evidence summary

Synthesis in [[papers/transition-monocyte-tissue-resident-macrophage-requires]] Discussion; consistent with unchanged CSF1R but failed residency in DHPS-deficient cells. Frames [[concepts/polyamine-hypusine-axis-macrophage-residency]].

## Conditions and scope

Interpretive model, not directly tested as a staged epistasis experiment.

## Counter-evidence

Precise molecular mechanism (which transcripts, whether non-eIF5A substrates contribute) remains unresolved.

## Open questions

- Whether the two stages are genetically separable.
- How tissue polyamine availability tunes stage two.
