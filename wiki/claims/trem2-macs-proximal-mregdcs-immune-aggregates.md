## Statement

In HCC immune aggregates, mregDCs are spatially proximal to TREM2 macs, extending the previously described CXCL13+ Th / TCF1+ PD-1hi CD8 / mregDC ICB-response triad into a TREM2-mac-inclusive quartet.

## Evidence summary

- MERFISH neighbour analysis in immune-aggregate regions.
- mregDCs spatially adjacent to TREM2 macs.
- Co-localised with CXCL13+ helper T cells and TCF1+ PD-1hi CD8.

## Conditions and scope

- mregDC definition by signature; flow / IHC validation in this dataset.
- Proximity, not direct contact.

## Counter-evidence

- mregDCs are also found near FOLR2 macs (separate analysis).
