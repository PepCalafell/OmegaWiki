## Background

Malte D. Luecken is a senior scientist in Fabian Theis's group at Helmholtz Zentrum München, Institute of Computational Biology. He is the first author of the scIB benchmark paper and a co-author on the canonical Theis-lab tutorial "Current best practices in single-cell RNA-seq analysis" (2019). His work focuses on benchmarking and best-practice methodology for atlas-scale single-cell data integration.

## Research areas

- Single-cell RNA-seq integration benchmarking
- Atlas construction (Human Cell Atlas contributions)
- Best-practice methodology for scRNA-seq workflows
