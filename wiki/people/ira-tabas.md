## Profile

Richard J. Stock Professor of Medicine, Cell Biology, and Physiology at Columbia University Irving Medical Center. PI of one of the most influential laboratories in macrophage biology of atherosclerosis, having defined many seminal mechanisms of efferocytosis, resolution-mediator synthesis (PGE2, TGFβ1, LXR cholesterol axis), AC-cargo-driven resolution programs (arginine→putrescine, fatty acid→IL-10, methionine→DNMT3A), advanced plaque biology, and necrotic-core formation in atherosclerosis. Senior / co-corresponding author of Ampomah 2022 *Nat Metab*.
