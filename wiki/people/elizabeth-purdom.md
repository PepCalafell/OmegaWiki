Elizabeth Purdom is a statistician at UC Berkeley working on statistical methods for genomics and single-cell data, co-senior author providing the statistical framework (weighted Gaussian compositional model, hyperdimensionality and normalized-cut analyses) for the 2022 Science Immunology skin-disease classification study.

## Research areas

- Statistical methods for single-cell and genomic data.
- Compositional and network analysis of transcriptomics.
