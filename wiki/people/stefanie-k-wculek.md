Stefanie K. Wculek is a postdoctoral researcher in David Sancho's lab at CNIC, Madrid. Her work focuses on dendritic-cell and macrophage immunometabolism in cancer and tissue homeostasis. She is the first author of the 2022 Cellular & Molecular Immunology review on tissue macrophage metabolism.
