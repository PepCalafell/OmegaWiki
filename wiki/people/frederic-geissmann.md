## Research areas

- Tissue-resident macrophage ontogeny — yolk-sac EMP origin, three-wave haematopoiesis model
- Tissue-specific macrophage specification (LDFs + niche signals)
- Macrophage roles in tissue homeostasis, repair, and disease
- Adipose tissue macrophages and energy metabolism (PDGF-CC axis with co-author Cox)
- *Drosophila* hemocyte biology as a developmental model
- Macrophage diseases — leukoencephalopathies, lysosomal storage diseases, cancer

## My notes

Geissmann is a senior leader in the macrophage ontogeny field — h-index 83, 183 papers (per S2). His group's body of work establishing the EMP yolk-sac origin of TRMs and the niche-driven specification model is the dominant framework in the field. The 2023 Nature review is his canonical synthesis to date and is *the* reference for ontogeny and TRM-disease genetics. Particularly relevant for my thesis chapter on macrophage diversity — the *ancillary cell* framing he proposes is conceptually clean and useful for organizing a literature chapter.
