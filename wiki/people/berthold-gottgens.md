## Background

Berthold Göttgens is Professor of Molecular Haematology at the University of Cambridge and a Principal Investigator at the Cambridge Stem Cell Institute. His group studies the transcriptional control of blood stem and progenitor cells, blood development, and leukaemia, combining single-cell genomics, gene regulatory network modelling, and computational/dynamical approaches to haematopoiesis.

## Research areas

- Haematopoietic stem and progenitor cell biology
- Single-cell genomics of blood development and leukaemia
- Gene regulatory networks and transcriptional control of blood
- Computational/dynamical modelling of population dynamics
