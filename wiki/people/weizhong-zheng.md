## Background

Weizhong Zheng is the first author of pseudodynamics+, developed in the Göttgens lab (Cambridge Stem Cell Institute) in collaboration with the Theis lab. The work develops a physics-informed neural network framework for population-aware single-cell flux modelling on branching developmental landscapes.

## Research areas

- Physics-informed neural networks for single-cell dynamics
- Population dynamics and cell-flux modelling
- Single-cell trajectory inference
