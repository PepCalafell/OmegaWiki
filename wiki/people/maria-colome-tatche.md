## Background

Maria Colomé-Tatché is a group leader at the Biomedical Center, Faculty of Medicine, Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and at Helmholtz Zentrum München. Her group works on computational epigenetics and single-cell integration. She is a corresponding author on the scIB integration benchmark.

## Research areas

- Computational epigenetics
- Single-cell data integration
- Cross-omics methods development
