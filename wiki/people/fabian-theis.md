## Background

Fabian J. Theis is director of the Institute of Computational Biology at Helmholtz Zentrum München and full professor at the Technical University of Munich. His group develops machine-learning methods for single-cell omics, including scVI / scANVI (via the scvi-tools / scverse ecosystem), scArches, scGen, trVAE, CPA, and many others. He is a senior member of the Human Cell Atlas consortium and co-founder of scverse. Senior author on the scIB benchmark.

## Research areas

- Deep generative modelling for single-cell data
- Single-cell integration and reference mapping
- Perturbation modelling (scGen, CPA, ChemCPA)
- Human Cell Atlas method development
- scverse open-source ecosystem
