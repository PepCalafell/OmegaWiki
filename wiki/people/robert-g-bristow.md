## Research areas

- Tumor hypoxia, DNA repair, and genetic instability in solid tumors
- Localized prostate cancer biology and clinical translation
- Radiation oncology — combining hypoxia targeting with radiotherapy
- CPC-GENE consortium (co-leadership) — whole-genome sequencing of localized PCa
- Hypoxia-targeting therapeutics (evofosfamide / TH-302 trials)

## My notes

Bristow is the senior clinician-scientist anchor of the Toronto/Manchester hypoxia + prostate cancer axis, with a clinical practice in radiation oncology informing the molecular work. His 2008 Nat Rev Cancer review is the canonical statement of "hypoxia → DNA repair deficit → genetic instability," which the 2019 Bhandari paper extends to a pancancer scale. For HypoxiaVERSE, Bristow's body of work is the clinical-translational backbone connecting hypoxia biology to actionable trials (evofosfamide, MAESTRO).
