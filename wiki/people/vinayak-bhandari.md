## Key papers in vault

- [[papers/molecular-landmarks-tumor-hypoxia-across-cancer]] — first author (Nat Genet 2019). Conducted bioinformatics and statistical analysis, performed in vitro validation of miR-133a-3p, performed data visualization, wrote the first draft of the manuscript.

## Recent work

- Bhandari et al. 2019 *Nature Genetics* — pancancer hypoxia molecular landmarks; first author. The paper consolidates tumor hypoxia as a genomic-shaping force across 19 tumor types and 8,006 tumors, introduces the "nimbosus" framework with collaborators, and validates miR-133a-3p as a hypoxia-modulated tumor suppressor in prostate cancer.
