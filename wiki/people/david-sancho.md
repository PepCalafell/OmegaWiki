David Sancho is the PI of the Immunobiology Laboratory at CNIC, Madrid. His group studies innate-immune receptor (DNGR-1/CLEC9A) biology, dendritic-cell and macrophage immunometabolism, and translational tumor immunology. S2 h-index ≈ 65. Senior author of the 2022 CMI review on tissue macrophage metabolism.
