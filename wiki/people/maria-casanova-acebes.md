## My notes

First and co-corresponding author of the landmark Casanova-Acebes 2021 Nature paper that established the pro-tumorigenic niche role of alveolar TRMs in early NSCLC. The paper is the canonical reference for ontogeny-distinct TRM/MDM function in cancer and provides the in vivo causal evidence (CD169-DTR depletion) that anchors the entire concept. Now leading her own group at CNIO Madrid focused on translating these findings to early-stage cancer prevention strategies. Important reference for thesis work needing to disentangle TRM (PPARG/MARCO/SIGLEC1) vs MDM (TREM2/SPP1/APOE) functional contributions to lung cancer.
