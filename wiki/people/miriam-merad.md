- Mononuclear phagocyte biology (macrophages, monocytes, dendritic cells)
- Tumour-immune microenvironment in lung cancer and other solid tumours
- Immune cell ontogeny, fate mapping, and tissue-specific specialisation
- Translational tumour immunology and immunotherapy biomarkers

## My notes

One of the most influential macrophage / mononuclear phagocyte immunologists of the modern era (S2 h-index 136, paper count 515). The Merad lab's prior work on TAM diversity (Lavin 2017) established the Treg-enrichment phenomenon in NSCLC that the Casanova-Acebes 2021 paper subsequently mechanistically dissected. Her lab is one of the world's leading sources of fate-mapping / scRNA-seq / CD169-DTR methodology applied to tumour immunology. Manual tier override warranted given her field-defining stature; expect multiple future ingest events.
