## Definition

Lipid rafts are cholesterol- and sphingolipid-enriched membrane microdomains that cluster BCR and TCR with their kinases and adaptors upon antigen engagement, licensing the proximal signaling cascades that drive B- and T-cell activation. In B cells: BCR → Lyn → ITAM-PO₄ → SYK → BLNK → BTK/PLC-γ2 → NF-κB / PI3K-AKT. In T cells: TCR → Lck → CD3/ζ ITAMs → ZAP-70 → LAT → Gads/SLP-76/PLC-γ1 → Ca²⁺/PKC/MAPK.

## Intuition

Rafts act as a "signaling platform" — by concentrating coreceptors and kinases in a small membrane patch, they raise local catalytic density and pass a threshold for productive activation. Immature B cells fail to cluster BCRs stably and undergo apoptosis, illustrating raft clustering as a quality-control checkpoint.

## Variants

- BCR-raft pathway (Lyn-centric)
- TCR-raft pathway (Lck-centric)
- Glycosphingolipid-dependent CD4 raft (Th17 differentiation)
- Raftlin-scaffolded rafts (Lyn / GM1 enrichment)
- α2,6-sialylated rafts (TCR translocation control; UC pathology)

## Comparison

Operates in parallel with the soluble [[concepts/s1p-s1pr-immune-trafficking]] axis (cell motion) and the [[concepts/scfa-hdac-gpr-immune-regulation]] axis (transcriptional rewiring). Rafts are the spatial layer of immune-cell control.

## When to use

For mechanistic accounts of B/T cell activation, autoimmune therapy targets (ulcerative colitis, Th17 diseases), and for understanding how cholesterol-lowering drugs perturb immune signaling.

## Known limitations

- Raft existence in vivo is methodology-dependent (detergent resistance overestimates raft fraction).
- Most therapy work has focused on soluble lipid mediators rather than raft components.

## Open problems

- Whether α2,6-sialylation control is a viable handle for psoriasis / atopic dermatitis where Th17 is central.
- Drug-discovery tractability of "raft modulators" (cholesterol-depleting agents lack specificity).
