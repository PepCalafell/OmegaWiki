## Definition

CAR T cell platform targeting TAM-restricted markers (TREM2 or FOLR2) and "armored" by autocrine/paracrine IL-12 expression. The armoring molecule both supports CAR-T persistence and reprograms the residual TAM compartment, leading to enrichment of CXCL9⁺ immunogenic macrophages after depletion of TREM2⁺/FOLR2⁺ suppressive TAMs.

## Intuition

Pure TAM depletion has historically failed because the tumor regrows an equally suppressed myeloid compartment. Combining selective depletion (CAR-T against TAM-restricted antigens) with IL-12-driven reprogramming achieves both removal of suppressive TAMs and *installation* of an immunogenic CXCL9⁺ population that recruits and primes CD8 T cells. Preclinically effective in ovarian, lung, and CRC models.
