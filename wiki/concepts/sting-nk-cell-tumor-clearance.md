## Definition

NK cells are critical effectors of STING-agonist antitumor activity through three convergent mechanisms: (1) CXCL10-driven recruitment via downstream STING-IFN chemokines; (2) cGAMP-paracrine type I IFN priming for enhanced cytotoxicity; (3) cell-intrinsic STING-IFN that maintains TCF-1+ NK precursor populations and immunosurveillance. Importantly, human NK cells express low intrinsic STING and are resistant to STING-induced cytotoxicity.

## Intuition

NK cells are the structurally favored effector cell for STING-targeted therapy: they get the benefits (recruitment, activation, cytotoxicity) without the cost (cell death) and they kill MHC-I-low tumors that escape T-cell recognition — exactly the kind of tumor STING-IFN tends to produce as a side effect of MHC-I upregulation.

## Variants

- Endogenous NK + CAR-NK cells synergize with STING agonism in murine pleural mesothelioma, glioblastoma, and breast cancer models
- Mesothelin-targeted CAR NK + STING agonism: enhanced migration and killing in patient-derived organotypic tumor spheroids (PDOTS)
- STING-knockout NK cells show impaired metastasis control and TCF-1+ population maintenance

## When to use

When designing adoptive cell therapy combinations with STING agonists — NK and CAR-NK platforms should be preferred over CAR-T to exploit the resistance asymmetry.

## Open problems

- CAR-NK persistence, memory, and manufacturing scale-up
- Whether STING-IFN-induced MHC-I upregulation on tumor cells partially inhibits NK activity, requiring NK engineering to override "missing self" reversion

## My understanding

NK-STING is the most therapeutically exploitable asymmetry in the cGAS-STING field — directly addresses the T-cell cytotoxicity barrier and aligns with the broader NK-cell-therapy wave (CAR-NK, NK engagers). Expect this to become a major axis of clinical development.
