# LPM lipid-handling identity

Yolk-sac-derived F4/80ʰⁱ CD11bʰⁱ MHCIIˡᵒ large peritoneal macrophages (LPMs) sustain antimicrobial defence and dead-cell phagocytosis. Their identity, survival, and lipid-handling phenotype depend on three lineage-determining transcription factors: **GATA6** (retinoic-acid-induced from omental milky spots), **RXRα/β** (lipid retention via lysosome-like vesicles when lost), and **C/EBPβ** (derepresses LXRα/PPARγ when lost). LPMs use peritoneal **glutamate as a tissue-niche fuel**, exhibit high basal OXPHOS and ETC complex II activity, and recruit mitochondria to phagosomes during the **antimicrobial respiratory burst** (driven by glutaminolysis). IL-4 further upregulates OXPHOS/TCA while downregulating lipid metabolism (opposite to BMDMs). oxLDL via CD36 redirects LPMs to a glycolytic, NF-κB-driven inflammatory program with mtROS-generating ETC repurposing — illustrating how microenvironmental ligands fine-tune the same cell.
