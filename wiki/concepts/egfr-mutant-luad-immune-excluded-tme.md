## Definition

EGFR-mutant lung adenocarcinoma — common in female never-smokers and Asian populations — typically presents with low TMB, reduced immune infiltration and fibroblast-enriched stromal niches, conferring resistance to both ICIs and EGFR TKIs (especially in smokers).

## Intuition

A genetically defined NSCLC subset whose oncogenic driver and host-context create an intrinsically immune-cold and stromally fortified niche that combination immunotherapy struggles to penetrate.

## When to use

For ICI eligibility decisions in EGFR-mutant patients and for designing combination regimens (e.g., EGFR TKI + antifibrotic + ICI) in this subset.
