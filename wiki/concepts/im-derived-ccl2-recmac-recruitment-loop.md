## Definition

A protumor circuit in which intratumoral Ccl2⁺ interstitial macrophages (both CD206lo and CD206hi IMs) act as the dominant, nonredundant source of CCL2 that drives continued recruitment of Ly6c2⁺Fn1⁺Vcan⁺ recruited macrophages (recMacs) into lung tumors, fueling tumor progression.

## Intuition

CCL2 (MCP-1) recruits CCR2⁺ monocytes/recMacs. Many sources can make CCL2 (tumor cells, endothelium, recMacs, DCs), but compartment-resolved chimeras show that host tissue-resident IM-derived CCL2 specifically is required: sparing IM-CCL2 (busulfan Ccl2⁻ᐟ⁻ chimera) preserves tumor growth, whereas removing CCL2 from all myeloid cells including IMs (irradiated Ccl2⁻ᐟ⁻ chimera) sharply limits recMac influx and tumor burden.

## Formal notation

Ccl2⁺ IM (intratumoral) → CCL2 → CCR2⁺ Ly6C⁺ monocytes → recMac accumulation (Spp1/Vegfa/Arg1/PD-L1) → tumor progression.

## Variants

- Resident-vs-recruited dissection via [[busulfan-conditioning-myeloid-sparing]] vs irradiation chimeras with Ccl2⁻ᐟ⁻ donors.
- Both CD206hi and CD206lo IMs produce Ccl2, likely contributing with distinct timing/context.

## Comparison

Refines prevailing models that emphasize stromal, endothelial or cancer-cell CCL2; reframes tissue-resident Ccl2⁺ IMs as key architects of the suppressive myeloid environment. Upstream of the [[ccr5-modc-immunosuppressive-lymph-node-axis]].

## When to use

When attributing the cellular source of CCL2-driven monocyte recruitment in solid tumors, or designing source-selective CCL2/CCR2 interventions.

## Known limitations

- Mouse melanoma/adenocarcinoma lung models.
- CD206hi vs CD206lo IM contributions to the CCL2 pool are not separated.

## Open problems

- Whether targeting IM-derived CCL2 selectively (vs systemic CCR2 blockade) avoids disrupting protective IM functions.
- Timing/context dependence of CD206hi vs CD206lo IM CCL2 output.

## My understanding

This is the suppressive arm's recruitment engine: the same resident IM lineage that protects (CD206hi, CXCL13/9/10) also harbors a CCL2⁺ state that imports protumor recMacs — function is set by chemokine program and position, not lineage alone.
