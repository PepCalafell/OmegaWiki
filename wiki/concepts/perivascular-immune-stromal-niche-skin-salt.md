## Definition

PERIVASC I is a multicellular spatial neighborhood in adult human skin, identified by MERFISH cell-cluster co-occurrence, that surrounds dermal post-capillary venules and is enriched in CCL19+ perivascular fibroblasts (Perivasc Fib I), pericytes, high-endothelial-like cells (HEC), VECs, macrophages, CD4+ T helper cells, CD1C+ DCs and naive T cells.

## Intuition

PERIVASC I is the spatial unit that the 1983 SALT concept lacked a stromal scaffold for: a CCL19/CXCL12 chemokine niche maintained by TNF-driven fibroblast programs, sustaining lymphocyte recruitment and residence outside lymph nodes.

## Variants

- PERIVASC II — a stromal-vascular companion neighborhood dominated by Perivasc Fib II / pericytes / smooth muscle / VEC, without the CCL19+ immune-recruiting program.

## Comparison

- vs canonical tertiary lymphoid structure (TLS): smaller, no B/T compartmentalization, no germinal center.
- vs lymph-node T-zone FRC niche: shares CCL19/CXCL12 chemistry but lives perivascularly in skin dermis.

## When to use

When discussing the homeostatic stromal-immune interface of skin, the spatial correlate of inflammatory CCL19+ fibroblasts, or skin disease-associated immune expansion sites.

## Known limitations

- MERFISH-based; sparse transcriptome per cell.
- Demarcation between PERIVASC I and TLS-like / TLS / HS-tunnel disease-enriched neighborhoods is graded, not binary.

## Open problems

- Is PERIVASC I sufficient to maintain skin-resident memory T cells longitudinally?
- Does PERIVASC I expansion precede or follow inflammatory infiltration in skin disease?

## My understanding

For a thesis touching skin biology, PERIVASC I is the most useful new conceptual entity from this atlas: it gives a concrete cellular-spatial anchor for "skin immune surveillance" and lets disease comparisons (psoriasis, AD, HS, BCC, SCC) be framed as PERIVASC-I remodeling rather than vague "immune infiltration".
