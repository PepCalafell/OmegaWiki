# Microglia metabolic plasticity

Yolk-sac-derived microglia run **active mitochondrial OXPHOS** at steady state to meet baseline energetic demand — they express higher OXPHOS-related gene levels than astrocytes or neurons. Proinflammatory (LPS+IFNγ) stimulation collapses OCR and lowers OCR/ECAR, signalling a **glycolytic switch** similar to BMDMs; IL-4+IL-13 stimulation leaves OXPHOS high. In-vivo and in-situ NADPH imaging shows microglia adapt to **hypoglycemia/aglycemia** by switching from glucose to **glutamine** as the OXPHOS substrate, a process that requires **mTOR**; they also use FAO, lactate, and ketone bodies as needed. Aging produces an **energy-deficient microglia state** in which prostaglandin E2/EP2 signalling sequesters glucose into glycogen, lowering glycolysis and OXPHOS and worsening neuroinflammation; blocking EP2 rescues bioenergetics and memory.

The concept stresses microglia as **highly metabolically flexible** cells whose energy state is itself a regulator of CNS homeostasis.
