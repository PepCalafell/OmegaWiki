## Definition

AHR ligands differ in: (i) binding affinity (TCDD highest), (ii) metabolic stability (CYP1A1-mediated clearance), (iii) residue-level interactions in the PAS-B pocket, and (iv) the conformations they induce in AHR–ARNT–coactivator complexes. These differences translate into qualitatively different downstream gene expression and immunological outcomes (Treg vs Th17 bias, tolerogenic vs pro-inflammatory DC phenotype). A selective AHR modulator (SAHRM) is a ligand engineered to selectively engage some AHR functions while avoiding others — analogous to SERMs for the oestrogen receptor.

## Intuition

A toxic dioxin and a probiotic indole both "activate AHR" but produce opposite biology because they (i) persist for different times, (ii) sit in the pocket differently, (iii) engage different co-activators, and (iv) are produced in different cellular contexts. The therapeutic project is to find ligands that activate the beneficial subset.

## Variants

- High-affinity persistent (TCDD, dioxin-like) — sustained transcription, toxic
- High-affinity transient (FICZ) — strong but CYP1A1-cleared, transient
- Endogenous-like (ITE, indirubin, indoles) — tolerogenic in many models
- Stilbene scaffold (tapinarof) — clinical, topical, transient
- Tryptophan-derived microbial (IAA, I3A, tryptamine)
- Dietary (DIM, ICZ, kaempferol, quercetin)
- Synthetic antagonists (CH-223191, StemRegenin-1, HP163)

## When to use

- When choosing a research-tool ligand: match kinetics to question (FICZ for transient, TCDD for sustained)
- When designing AHR-targeted drugs: optimise for tissue, duration, and selectivity over toxic pocket conformations

## Known limitations

- The "AHR pocket" is unusually large and tolerates diverse chemotypes — both a drug-discovery advantage (diverse leads) and a problem (off-target promiscuity, dioxin-like risks)
- Lack of full-length AHR structure limits rational design

## Open problems

- Reliable biomarkers for ligand-biased AHR signalling in patients
- Predicting tissue-specific outcomes from pocket-interaction profiles
