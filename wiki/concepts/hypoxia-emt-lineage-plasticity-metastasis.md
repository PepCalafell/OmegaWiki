## Definition

Hypoxia mediates phenotypic plasticity supporting invasion and metastasis through three coupled mechanisms: (1) HIF-driven EMT-TF regulation (SNAIL, TWIST, ZEB) with E-cadherin↓ and N-cadherin/vimentin/JAGGED2↑; (2) epigenetic reprogramming via H3K9 demethylases (KDM4B, JMJD1A, JMJD2C) and EHMT1/2; (3) neuroendocrine transdifferentiation via ONECUT2 and related TFs.

## Intuition

Hypoxic pre-treatment of tumour cells increases experimental metastatic potential, and clinical data link intratumoural hypoxia to locoregional and distant metastases across many solid tumour types. Hypoxic cells also condition normoxic neighbours via IL-8–p65 paracrine signalling to acquire metastatic capacity.

## Variants

- HIF-EMT-TF axis (canonical)
- Hypoxia-driven neuroendocrine plasticity (ONECUT2)
- Hypoxia epigenetic reprogramming (KDM4/JMJD/EHMT)
- Paracrine hypoxia→normoxia metastatic priming (IL-8–p65)
- MAFF–IL-11–STAT3 hypoxia-induced metastasis axis

## When to use

Explains why hypoxic primary tumours seed polyclonal metastases (e.g., HCC, RCC peri-necrotic regions, prostate).

## Known limitations

EMT marker dynamics are partial and reversible (EMP). The relative contribution of cell-intrinsic vs paracrine hypoxia effects on metastatic potential remains unclear.

## Open problems

- Whether hypoxic "creeping" cells in tumour niches are primed metastatic seeds
- Mechanism by which polyclonal metastases originate from peri-necrotic hypoxic regions (e.g., TRACERx Renal)
- Therapeutic targeting of hypoxia-driven lineage plasticity

## My understanding

Hypoxia → metastasis is the clinical-outcome amplifier of the hypoxia → CIN → immune-evasion chain. The TRACERx Renal observation of aggressive subclonal growth in peri-necrotic centres is a striking spatial confirmation of this concept.
