## Definition

An immune-based classification of bladder cancer into five consensus-clustering subtypes (Classes A–E) defined on the IMvigor210 cohort, in which one class is markedly enriched for CXCL9⁺ macrophages (Class E in IMvigor210; the corresponding high-Macro-CXCL9 group is Class B in TCGA BLCA) and associated with distinct mutation and druggable-gene profiles.

## Intuition

Instead of M1/M2 or histological grade, tumours are grouped by the composition of their immune/macrophage compartment; the Macro-CXCL9-high class indexes a more T-cell-engaged, potentially ICI-favourable ecosystem.

## Variants

- IMvigor210-defined (Class E high) vs TCGA BLCA-defined (Class B high) — class labels are cohort-specific.

## When to use

When linking bladder cancer molecular subtype to immune composition and candidate druggable mutations for subtype-tailored therapy.

## Known limitations

- Class labels do not transfer across cohorts; subtype count depends on clustering parameters; clinical actionability unproven.

## Open problems

- Prospective replication and whether the subtypes predict differential drug response.

## My understanding

A reasonable immune-composition taxonomy but, like many consensus-cluster schemes, its cross-cohort instability (E vs B) limits direct clinical use.
