## Definition

The pairwise cytokine code is the empirical finding that three TNF-anchored cytokine pairs — TNF+IL-18, TNF+IFN-γ, and TNF+IL-1β — collectively suffice to recapitulate most of the organism-wide molecular, cellular, physiological and fitness response to bacterial and viral sepsis in mouse models, decoding the apparently chaotic systemic cytokine signal into a small, low-dimensional message.

## Intuition

Sepsis blood elevates many cytokines simultaneously, but most pairs produce only modest organ-wide change. Three TNF-centered pairs are special: they generate the most DEGs (TNF+IL-18 = 7,083), cover the largest fraction of sepsis biomarker genes, and reproduce LPS-induced cell-type abundance shifts across 9 organs. The pair, not the single, is the relevant unit — explaining decades of mixed results from single-cytokine blockade trials.

## Variants

- Bacterial sepsis encoding (LPS, CLP — best mimicked by TNF+IL-18)
- Viral sepsis encoding (vaccinia Western Reserve — also captured by the same three pairs)
- Tissue-specific weighting (kidney/liver largely shared across pairs; heart/spleen/LN pair-specific)

## When to use

Cite when interpreting cytokine therapeutic targets in cytokine storms, evaluating single-cytokine blockade outcomes, or designing combination-cytokine-perturbation experiments organism-wide.

## Open problems

- Half (≈48%) of LPS cellular effects remain unexplained by the three pairs — what other (non-)cytokine factors fill the gap?
- Direct vs indirect cytokine pair effects on each cell type are not separated
- Human translation of the pairwise code is untested
- Higher-order (triplet, quartet) cytokine combinations are not yet sampled

## My understanding

The paper's central concept: sepsis collapses into a 3-pair, 4-cytokine module with TNF at the apex. This reframes anti-cytokine therapy in sepsis from single-target failure to combinatorial target design, and links structurally to the broader "TNF hierarchy" picture in chronic inflammatory disease.
