## Definition

CAR T cell therapy uses autologous T cells genetically engineered to express a chimaeric antigen receptor (CAR) that binds a tumour-specific surface antigen via an antibody-derived single-chain variable fragment fused to T cell activation domains (CD3ζ + costimulatory). Upon antigen engagement, CAR-T cells are activated independently of MHC presentation.

## Intuition

CAR-Ts have transformed haematological malignancies (CD19+ B-ALL, lymphomas, BCMA+ multiple myeloma) but struggle in solid tumours because of antigen heterogeneity, immunosuppressive TME and poor trafficking. Adjacent modalities — CAR-NK, CAR-macrophages, TCR-engineered T cells, BiTEs — extend the strategy.

## Variants

- **Hematological CAR-T** — CD19, CD20, BCMA.
- **Solid tumour CAR-T** — GD2 (neuroblastoma), EGFRvIII (glioma).
- **CAR-NK** — MHC-independent, allogeneic-compatible.
- **CAR macrophages (CAR-M)** — phagocytic effectors.
- **γδ CAR-T** — innate-like, MHC-unrestricted.

## When to use

- Liquid tumours with strong shared antigens.
- Increasingly tested in solid tumours combined with ICB or cytokine engineering.

## Known limitations

- Solid tumour TME excludes and exhausts CAR-T cells.
- Cytokine release syndrome and neurotoxicity.
- Antigen loss / lineage switch (CD19→CD22) drives resistance.

## Open problems

- Improving CAR-T persistence and trafficking in solid tumours.
- Combinations that sensitize tumour cells (e.g. type I IFN signalling in breast cancer organoids).
- Overcoming hypoxic/metabolic suppression of CAR-T function.

## My understanding

Organoid-based CAR-T testing (live imaging via BEHAV3D, single-cell transcriptomics of CAR-T functional states) is one of the fastest ways to identify combinatorial sensitizers and predict patient-specific response for solid tumours.
