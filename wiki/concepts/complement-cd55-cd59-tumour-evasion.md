## Definition

Upregulation of complement regulatory proteins CD55 and CD59 in the tumour microenvironment inactivates complement-mediated cytotoxicity, lowering CD8⁺ T-cell killing capacity. Combining anti-CD55 or anti-CD59 with anti-PD-1 produced synergistic antitumour activity in a mouse lung cancer model. Complement components such as C5a play a counterbalancing protumorigenic role via MDSC recruitment.

## Intuition

The complement system is a double-edged sword in the TME — tumours hijack its regulators to evade lysis while exploiting downstream components for immunosuppression.

## When to use

For TME-targeted combinatorial therapy design and for interpreting complement-targeted preclinical NSCLC studies.
