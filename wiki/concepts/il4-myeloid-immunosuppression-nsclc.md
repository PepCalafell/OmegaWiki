## Definition

Elevated IL-4 signalling in bone-marrow myeloid progenitors expands immunosuppressive myeloid populations that infiltrate NSCLC and worsen prognosis. A first-in-human trial (n=6) combining the anti-IL-4Rα antibody dupilumab with anti-PD-(L)1 reduced circulating immunosuppressive monocytes and increased CD8⁺ infiltration in paired biopsies.

## Intuition

Targeting an upstream systemic cytokine reshapes the myeloid input to the TME at its source rather than at the tumour bed.

## When to use

When considering systemic-myeloid-targeting strategies as ICI adjuvants in NSCLC; the dupilumab + anti-PD-(L)1 combination is hypothesis-generating, requiring larger trials.
