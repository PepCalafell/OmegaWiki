## Definition

Proposed integrative model for TAM characterization to guide rational therapy design across six dimensions: (1) **Causal drivers** (cues that turn on specific pro/antitumor programs); (2) **Spatial niches** (where TAMs live and which cells they contact); (3) **Temporal dynamics** (how states change with disease and therapy); (4) **Signaling state** (active pathways); (5) **Metabolism**; (6) **Ontogeny** (developmental origin — resident vs monocyte-derived). The framework is paired with a 4D therapeutic design pipeline (Target / Molecules / Mode of action / Manufacturing).

## Intuition

Marker-defined TAMs behave differently depending on origin, niche, time, and signaling — so a single "TREM2⁺" or "SPP1⁺" label is insufficient to nominate a target. The 6D framework forces a more complete description before therapeutic intervention, with the goal of identifying subset- and niche-specific targets (e.g., SPP1/TREM2⁺ hypoxia-core TAMs to relieve suppression in lung cancer).
