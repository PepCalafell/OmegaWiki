## Definition

TREM2-expressing tumour-associated macrophages enriched in lipid-rich and hypoxic niches that curb NK cell activity, expand exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, and reinforce immunologically cold tumour states across NSCLC and other solid tumours.

## Intuition

A specialized TAM state adapted to nutrient-poor, hypoxic, lipid-laden microenvironments — analogous to physiological lipid-associated macrophages in adipose tissue but co-opted to suppress antitumour immunity.

## When to use

In stratifying NSCLC biopsies by macrophage state and identifying ICI-resistant hypoxic niches; as candidate target for TREM2-blocking antibodies in combination with PD-1 blockade.
