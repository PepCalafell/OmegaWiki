## Definition
Single-cell profiling of mutant-Kras-driven lung cancer and PanIN models has identified senescent cell populations — particularly senescent macrophages and Cox2-expressing senescent epithelial cells — that promote early-stage tumorigenesis by suppressing cytotoxic T-cell responses and shaping a pro-tumorigenic microenvironment.

## Intuition
Although classically viewed as a barrier to cancer, senescent cells secrete a SASP cocktail that, in the precancer niche, drives immunosuppression. Selective clearance of senescent macrophages ameliorates lung cancer development, motivating senolytic cancer-interception strategies.

## When to use
When designing precancer-stage interventions that target the stromal or myeloid microenvironment rather than mutant epithelial cells.

## Open problems
- Specificity of senolytic agents to tumour-promoting senescent populations
- Whether epithelial senescence at the precancer stage is uniformly tumour-promoting
