## Definition

NOTCH3-dependent paracrine signalling among CAFs, pericytes and endothelial cells that promotes NSCLC invasion, collagen production, and expression of a TGFβ-related transcriptional signature linked to poor prognosis.

## Intuition

NOTCH3 integrates stromal-vascular crosstalk into a pro-fibrotic, pro-invasive programme that is independent of cancer-cell-intrinsic Notch activity.

## When to use

For interpreting stromal NOTCH activity in NSCLC and as a candidate target for NOTCH3-selective inhibitors in combination with antifibrotic or antiangiogenic therapy.
