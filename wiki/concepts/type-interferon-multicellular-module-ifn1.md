## Definition

A co-occurring multicellular module (M-IFN1) composed of cross-lineage IFIT1+ (type I interferon-stimulated) cell populations across the tumor microenvironment, identified from positive cell-frequency correlations and supported by spatial colocalization analysis.

## Intuition

Type I interferon imprints an ISG program on many cell types at once, so IFIT1+ states across lineages rise and fall together, forming a coordinated module that shapes the surrounding immune niche.

## Variants

- M-IFN1-high vs M-IFN1-low tumors, with opposite immune phenotypes.

## Comparison

A specific, interferon-defined instance of [[tme-cellular-co-occurrence-multicellular-modules]].

## When to use

When reasoning about coordinated, IFN-I-driven multicellular states rather than single IFIT1+ cell types.

## Known limitations

- Built on frequency correlation and ligand-receptor inference, not perturbation.

## Open problems

- Whether M-IFN1 causally establishes the immune-permissive niche and can be therapeutically induced.

## Key papers

- [[pan-cancer-tumor-classification-holistic-tumor]] — defines M-IFN1 and its links to immune-permissive features.

## My understanding

An interferon-coordinated multicellular unit linking IFIT1+ states to T- and B-cell recruitment, but with a duality that can also accompany suppression.
