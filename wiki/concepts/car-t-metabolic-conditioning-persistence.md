## Definition

Use of pharmacological inhibitors (MPC: [[foundations/uk5099-mpc-inhibitor|UK-5099]], MCT1, LDH, [[foundations/metformin|metformin]]) or environmental cues (glucose restriction, sodium bicarbonate) during ex-vivo CAR-T cell expansion to bias differentiation toward a memory phenotype with enhanced mitochondrial OxPhos, histone acetylation, stemness, and post-infusion persistence.

## Intuition

Manufacturing-stage metabolic conditioning is the cheapest, safest place to engineer CAR-T quality — patient toxicity is avoided. Pair this with genetic edits (Regnase-1 KO, PRODH2/PGC1α OE) for combinatorial benefit.

## Formal notation

Ex vivo perturbation × CAR construct × patient state → in-vivo persistence and antitumour activity.

## Variants

- Small-molecule conditioning (MPC, MCT1, LDH, metformin).
- Genetic edits (Regnase-1 KO, PRODH2 OE, PGC1α OE, A2AR KO, FH OE, ARG modulation).
- Hybrid strategies.

## Comparison

- Complements canonical [[concepts/car-t-cell-therapy|CAR-T cell therapy]] manufacturing.

## When to use

- Solid-tumour CAR-T programs where persistence is limiting.
- Refractory haematological malignancies after standard CAR-T relapse.

## Known limitations

- GMP-scale validation lagging behind preclinical proof.
- Long-term safety data sparse.

## Open problems

- Optimal conditioning cocktails.
- Patient-specific TME-matched conditioning.

## Key papers

- Ricci 2025 catalogues the modalities; Moraly et al. review provides genetic-engineering complement.

## My understanding

This is the most actionable lever in current CAR-T research — small molecules added during manufacturing can deliver clinical-grade benefit without altering the cell product genetically.
