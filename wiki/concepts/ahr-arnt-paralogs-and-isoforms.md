## Definition

A layer of AHR context specificity arising from heterogeneity in the obligate AHR dimerisation partner ARNT/HIF1β: (a) the ARNT paralog ARNT2 (~80% identical, expressed mainly in kidney/CNS/retinal epithelium) dimerises with AHR but blunts canonical CYP1A1 induction; (b) ARNT isoform 1 and isoform 3 (differing by 15 aa) are co-expressed at equal ratios in naïve B/T cells, but lymphoid malignancies upregulate iso1 via the splicing factor RBFOX2, with iso1 supporting growth/survival and iso3 mediating immunosuppression on AHR ligand stimulation.

## Intuition

The "AHR signal" is not transduced by a single ARNT; the partner pool itself is structurally and functionally heterogeneous. Whether a cell is "AHR-responsive" depends on which ARNT it has.

## Formal notation

- ARNT2 expression high in kidney, CNS, retinal epithelium → AHR-ARNT2 dimers form but CYP1A1 induction blunted by ~30%.
- ARNT iso1 / iso3 ratio determines AHR responsiveness in lymphoid cells; CKII phosphorylates ARNT iso1 at S77 to potentiate AHR activity.
- RBFOX2 splicing factor shifts iso1/iso3 ratio toward iso1 in lymphoid malignancies.

## Variants

- ARNT2-dominant tissues vs ARNT-dominant tissues.
- ARNT iso1 high vs iso3 high lymphoid cells.

## Comparison

- Complementary to [[concepts/ahr-hif-arnt-competition]]: that concept tracks the *availability* of ARNT (which HIFα consumes); this concept tracks the *identity* of ARNT (which paralog/isoform).

## When to use

- When predicting AHR phenotype in renal, CNS, or retinal contexts (ARNT2 likely dominant).
- When interpreting AHR-driven lymphoma biology (iso1/iso3 axis).
- When designing AHR therapeutics for lymphoid malignancies — RBFOX2-targeted splicing modulation is a possible strategy.

## Known limitations

- In-vivo evidence in ARNT2-dominant tissues is sparse.
- Functional differences between iso1 and iso3 beyond growth/survival vs immunosuppression are not yet mapped.

## Open problems

- Cryo-EM of AHR-ARNT2 heterodimer on XRE — does ARNT2 sterically block productive XRE engagement?
- Are there iso-specific small molecules or splicing modifiers?

## My understanding

A neglected layer with clear therapeutic potential in lymphoid malignancies. The ARNT2-dominant tissue logic is particularly attractive for explaining tissue-specific AHR phenotypes (e.g. why CNS tumours respond differently to AHR antagonism).
