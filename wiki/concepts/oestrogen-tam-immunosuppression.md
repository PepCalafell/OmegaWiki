## Definition

In a mouse melanoma model, oestrogen signalling polarizes TAMs toward an immunosuppressive phenotype that drives CD8⁺ T-cell dysfunction/exhaustion and ICI resistance, an effect reversed by the selective oestrogen receptor degrader fulvestrant.

## Intuition

Female sex bias toward TAM-mediated immunosuppression can be pharmacologically reversed through ER targeting, opening combination strategies for ER-modulated and ER-naive tumours alike.

## When to use

In sex-stratified ICI trials, particularly for premenopausal patients; as rationale for combining SERDs/SERMs with ICIs in selected NSCLC contexts.
