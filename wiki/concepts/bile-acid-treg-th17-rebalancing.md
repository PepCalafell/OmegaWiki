## Definition

Primary bile acids synthesized by the liver (CA, CDCA) are converted by gut microbiota into secondary bile acids LCA, DCA and their variants (iso-, 3-oxo-, allo-, 3-oxoallo-, isoalloLCA, isoDCA). These derivatives act as ligands of nuclear receptors (FXR, RORγt) and membrane receptors (TGR5, FPR1) to rebalance Treg vs Th17, polarize macrophages, and modulate neutrophil chemotaxis and mast-cell activation.

## Intuition

LCA's structural variants are "natural Treg/Th17 tuners": 3-oxoLCA blocks Th17 by antagonizing RORγt; isoalloLCA boosts Treg by inducing mitochondrial ROS + NR4A1 → Foxp3. Engineered microbial consortia can shift the pool toward isoDCA/isoalloLCA for therapeutic Treg expansion.

## Variants

- 3-oxoLCA — RORγt antagonist → Th17 block
- isoalloLCA — mitochondrial ROS, NR4A1-Foxp3 → Treg induction (VDR/FXR-CNS3 independent)
- isoDCA — DC-FXR suppression, peripheral Treg expansion
- LCA/DCA — TLR2-mediated macrophage M1, GM-progenitor expansion
- 3-oxoLCA/isoLCA — TGR5 agonists → M2 macrophage
- Hydrophilic UDCA — mast-cell inhibition
- Lipophilic CDCA — mast-cell histamine release

## Comparison

Mechanistically distinct from [[concepts/scfa-hdac-gpr-immune-regulation]] (HDAC/GPR) — bile-acid effects route through nuclear and membrane bile-acid receptors. Both axes share microbiota-as-source and converge on Treg/Th17 rebalancing.

## When to use

For inflammatory bowel disease pathophysiology, gut-Treg engineering, hepatic-gut immune axis, and engineered microbial therapeutics.

## Known limitations

- Most data from murine gnotobiotic / ex vivo systems; human translation thin.
- Engineered consortia (e.g., isoDCA producers) not yet clinical.

## Open problems

- Reproducibility of isoalloLCA Treg induction at physiological human gut concentrations.
- Pharmacological agonists of TGR5 / antagonists of RORγt selective for immune over metabolic compartments.

## My understanding

This is the cleanest example of metabolite-as-drug in modern immunology — small-molecule bile-acid variants directly bind transcription factors that define T-helper fate.
