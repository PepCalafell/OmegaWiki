## Definition

A spatially defined stromal axis in NSCLC consisting of COL11A1⁺GREM1⁺ CAFs paired with SPP1⁺ TAMs adjacent to cancer cells. Enrichment of this axis correlates with higher tumour burden and worse outcomes of chemo–ICI therapy, and is hypothesised to physically and immunologically obstruct CTL infiltration.

## Intuition

A "wall" pattern at the tumour–stroma interface: CAFs deposit ECM (collagens, GREM1-modulated TGFβ), and SPP1⁺ TAMs reinforce it, preventing effector T cells from reaching tumour cells.

## When to use

When interpreting post-chemo–ICI spatial transcriptomics in NSCLC; as a candidate target for combination with antifibrotic / anti-SPP1 strategies.

## Known limitations

- Mechanism is hypothesised, not directly proven.
- COL11A1⁺GREM1⁺ CAF definitions are platform- and cohort-specific.
