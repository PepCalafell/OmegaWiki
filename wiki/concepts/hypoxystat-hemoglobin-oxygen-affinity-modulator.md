## Definition

A small molecule that increases hemoglobin's oxygen-binding affinity, decreasing oxygen off-loading to tissues and thereby inducing a systemic hypoxia-like state pharmacologically, without requiring confinement to hypoxic rooms or nasal cannula gas mixtures.

## Intuition

By shifting the hemoglobin-O2 dissociation curve, HypoxyStat lowers tissue oxygen delivery, recapitulating the metabolic consequences of inhaled systemic hypoxia in a dosable, clinically translatable form.

## Formal notation

_Not applicable._

## Variants

Dosed 400-600 mg/kg daily in mice; conceptually one of a class of compounds increasing Hb-O2 affinity.

## Comparison

A pharmacological route to [[systemic-hypoxia-anti-tumor-therapy]] vs environmental inhaled hypoxia.

## When to use

When considering clinical translation of systemic hypoxia therapy.

## Known limitations

Therapeutic window needs optimization; systemic-hypoxia side effects (pulmonary hypertension, erythrocytosis, edema) still apply.

## Open problems

Optimal dosing, safety, and efficacy in humans.
