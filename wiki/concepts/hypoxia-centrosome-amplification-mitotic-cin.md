## Definition

Hypoxia alters expression of centrosome- and mitotic-control genes (PLK4, PLK1, AURKA, CEP192, CDC20, BUB1, KIF4A) producing centrosome over-duplication, multipolar spindles, lagging chromosomes, anaphase/ultrafine DNA bridges and aneuploidy — independent of gene copy-number or mutation status.

## Intuition

Two coupled routes: (1) direct hypoxia modulation of mitotic gene expression (HIF1-dependent and HIF1-independent); (2) collateral effect of hypoxia-induced BRCA1/2/RAD51 suppression, since these HR proteins also have moonlighting roles in centrosome duplication. miR-210 induction yields centrosome amplification + multipolar spindles + polyploidy. PHD1 modulation of CEP192 was proposed then rebutted, leaving the precise oxygen-sensing mechanism unresolved.

## Variants

- PLK4-driven centrosome over-duplication under hypoxia
- miR-210-driven multipolar spindle formation
- BRCA-mediated centrosome dysfunction (indirect via DDR pathway)
- CDC20/BUB1 SAC dysregulation under hypoxia

## When to use

Use to explain elevated PGA, aneuploidy and chromothripsis in hypoxic tumours, especially prostate, breast and head-and-neck.

## Known limitations

Data are controversial; live-cell-microscopy in isogenic models lacking; PHD1–CEP192 proposed mechanism rebutted.

## Open problems

- Causal vs correlational role in driving CNAs
- Cycling vs chronic hypoxia contributions to centrosome defects
- Spatial validation in situ via single-cell centrosome imaging or spatial transcriptomics

The numerical-CIN side of the hypoxia-instability story (vs the SNV/HR-deficient point-mutation side captured by [[concepts/hypoxia-induced-mutator-phenotype]]). PGA and aneuploidy correlations with hypoxia score are robust in patient cohorts even though the mechanistic dissection is messy.
