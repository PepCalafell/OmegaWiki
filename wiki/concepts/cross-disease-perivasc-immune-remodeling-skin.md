## Definition

The observation that across five distinct skin diseases — atopic dermatitis (AD), psoriasis (PP), hidradenitis suppurativa (HS), basal-cell carcinoma (BCC) and squamous-cell carcinoma (SCC) — Visium ST spots labelled as PERIVASC I expand and acquire disease-specific chemokine, cytokine and ECM transcriptional programs, with disease-enriched sub-states (TLS, TLS-like, KC stress, HS tunnel, BCC tumor) emerging adjacent to the homeostatic PERIVASC I program.

## Intuition

A single spatial-architectural axis — PERIVASC I expansion — is shared across otherwise dissimilar skin diseases (autoimmune, neoplastic, suppurative), positioning the perivascular niche as a convergent therapeutic substrate.

## Variants

- TLS-rich expansion in HS and SCC.
- KC-stress neighborhood-coupled PERIVASC I in psoriasis and AD.
- BCC-tumor-adjacent PERIVASC I — niche co-option by carcinoma.

## Comparison

- Resonates with TLS-promoting CAF/fibroblast literature in solid tumors ([[concepts/cxcl13-cxcr5-tls-recruitment]] but skin-specific).

## When to use

When framing biomarker or therapeutic strategies that target shared inflammatory architecture rather than disease-specific antigens.

## Known limitations

- Visium spot resolution coarser than MERFISH; PERIVASC I mapping in disease relies on reference-based neighborhood prediction.
- Causality (PERIVASC I expansion → pathology vs pathology → PERIVASC I expansion) unresolved.

## Open problems

- Does anti-TNF therapy revert PERIVASC I expansion specifically?
- Can PERIVASC I score be used as a transversal disease-activity biomarker?
