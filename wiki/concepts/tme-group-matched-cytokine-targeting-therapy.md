## Definition

A stratification-driven treatment strategy that selects a cytokine-pathway intervention to match the dominant cellular/cytokine program of a tumor's TME group: CSF-pathway (CSF1R) inhibition for myeloid-centric groups, VEGF inhibition for vascular-rich groups, and TGFB inhibition for stromal/CAF-rich groups, intended alongside immune-checkpoint blockade.

## Intuition

If a TME group is defined by a dominant cytokine driver, targeting that driver should specifically perturb that group's expression signature while leaving others largely unaffected — converting a descriptive classification into actionable, group-matched therapy.

## Variants

- CSF1R inhibition → G05/G07 (M-centric).
- VEGFR inhibition → G08 (vascular).
- TGFB inhibition → G08–G10 (stromal/CAF).

## Comparison

Operationalizes the [[holistic-tme-based-pan-cancer-tumor]] classification for ICB non-responders; complements [[immune-checkpoint-blockade]] rather than replacing it.

## When to use

When designing combination strategies for ICB-resistant tumors based on their dominant TME program.

## Known limitations

- Evidence is signature-level from public/mouse perturbation datasets, not prospective patient-matched trials.

## Open problems

- Whether group-matched cytokine therapy improves clinical outcomes when combined with ICB.
