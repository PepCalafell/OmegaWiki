## Definition

A hypothalamic circuit centred on the arcuate nucleus (ARC) that integrates peripheral hormonal and inflammatory signals to set appetite and energy expenditure. The system comprises anorexigenic POMC neurons (expressing α-MSH, acting on MC3R/MC4R) and orexigenic NPY/AgRP neurons (AgRP inhibits MC3R/MC4R). In cancer-associated cachexia (CAC), inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α), GDF-15, leptin, ghrelin and tumour-derived factors disturb this circuit, biasing it toward anorexia and elevated energy expenditure.

## Intuition

Anorexia in CAC is not "just psychological" — it is a measurable circuit-level dysregulation in the hypothalamus, detectable on functional MRI, that responds to peripheral signals from the tumour and inflammatory milieu. Targeting MC4R (e.g., MC4R antagonism) or its peripheral inputs (GDF-15 → GFRAL receptor; ghrelin → GHSR) is a therapeutic strategy actively under clinical development.

## Formal notation

- Receptors: MC1R–MC5R (G-protein-coupled); MC3R/MC4R mediate CNS effects.
- Cell populations: POMC (anorexigenic, ARC + nucleus tractus solitarius); NPY/AgRP (orexigenic, ARC).
- Peripheral modulators: leptin (adipose, activates POMC, inhibits AgRP); ghrelin (stomach, activates AgRP via GHSR); GDF-15 (tumour/inflammation, acts via GFRAL on brainstem); IL-6, TNF, IL-1 (inflammation, suppress feeding centrally).

## Variants

- MC4R-antagonist therapeutics (in development for CAC).
- Anamorelin: ghrelin-receptor agonist; approved in Japan for NSCLC cachexia.
- Anti-GDF-15 / anti-GFRAL antibodies (in trials).

## Comparison

vs simple starvation: in starvation, AgRP is upregulated and food intake compensates; in CAC, anorexia persists even when peripheral lipostatic signals would normally drive feeding — indicating central override.

vs anorexia nervosa: AN preserves body cell mass relative to weight loss; CAC depletes body cell mass.

## When to use

When mechanistic claims about appetite regulation in CAC are made, OR when designing trials of central-melanocortin-targeting therapeutics (MC4R antagonists, ghrelin agonists, anti-GDF-15).

## Known limitations

- Human evidence for circuit-level dysregulation is sparse (only two small fMRI cohorts to date).
- GDF-15 → GFRAL axis is the best-validated cytokine-to-melanocortin link but is one of many inputs.
- Translation of mouse MC4R-antagonism efficacy to humans is incomplete.

## Open problems

- Which peripheral signal(s) (GDF-15 vs IL-6 vs leptin) dominates the central anorexia phenotype across cancer types?
- Does CNS structural change (Simoes et al.) reflect neuroinflammation, neurodegeneration, or both?
- Can hypothalamic fMRI be used as a pharmacodynamic biomarker in early-phase trials of central-acting anti-CAC drugs?

## My understanding

The most therapeutically actionable mechanistic node in CAC: peripheral mediators (GDF-15, IL-6, ghrelin) converge on the hypothalamus and have FDA/PMDA-approved or in-trial pharmacology. The bottleneck is patient stratification — not every CAC patient is anorexia-driven.
