# Efferocytosis-driven metabolic rewiring

The clearance of apoptotic cells (**efferocytosis**) is not metabolically neutral: an initial phase of increased glucose uptake / aerobic glycolysis is followed by sustained **mitochondrial OXPHOS fuelled by fatty acid oxidation** of phagocytosed corpse-derived lipids. Continuous efferocytosis requires **UCP2-mediated lowering of ΔΨm** and **DRP1-driven mitochondrial fission**; loss of either stalls successive engulfment. ETC complex III (Rieske iron-sulfur protein, RISP) is required in macrophages for IL-10 production and ventricular recovery after MI; NAD⁺ precursor nicotinamide mononucleotide rescues this and is cardioprotective. Apoptotic-cell-derived lipid substrate induces **PPARδ** and PPARγ, switching macrophages to an anti-inflammatory, opsonin-rich, regenerative state — coupling efferocytosis directly to inflammation resolution.
