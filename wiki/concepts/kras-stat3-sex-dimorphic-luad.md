## Definition

In a KRAS-mutant LUAD mouse model, STAT3 knockout produces opposite outcomes by sex: females show reduced tumour burden via oestrogen-dependent antitumour immunity (reversed by tamoxifen), whereas males show enhanced tumour growth driven by NF-κB activation, CXCL1-mediated neutrophilia, and IL-6 induction (mitigated by IL-6 blockade or neutrophil depletion).

## Intuition

A single oncogene–pathway perturbation interacts with host hormonal biology to produce divergent tumour phenotypes, illustrating why sex must be treated as a biological variable in KRAS-mutant NSCLC.

## When to use

When designing sex-stratified KRAS-targeted or STAT3-related trials and interpreting preclinical KP/KRAS LUAD studies.
