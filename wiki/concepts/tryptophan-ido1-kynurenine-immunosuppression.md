## Definition

Tumour-cell and dendritic-cell expression of IDO1 (and the hepatic/glioma enzyme TDO) catabolises L-tryptophan to N-formyl-kynurenine and kynurenine. Both the substrate depletion and the kynurenine-rich milieu suppress effector T and NK cells, enhance Treg function, and expand MDSCs and immunosuppressive DCs.

## Intuition

A double-edged sword: deplete the substrate, build up an AhR-ligand. Despite strong preclinical support, phase 3 epacadostat + pembrolizumab failed in metastatic melanoma (ECHO-301), but the pathway remains active research with biomarker-driven trials.

## Formal notation

Trp + O2 → N-formyl-kynurenine → kynurenine → AhR signalling → Treg / MDSC expansion.

## Variants

- IDO1 ([[foundations/ido1-indoleamine-dioxygenase|enzyme]]) — broadly expressed, IFN-γ-inducible.
- TDO — hepatic, also glioma-expressed.
- IDO2 — immunological role debated.

## When to use

- Patient stratification by IDO1/TDO/IDO2 expression and kynurenine levels.

## Known limitations

- Phase 3 monotherapy/ICB-combination failure (ECHO-301).
- Pathway redundancy obscures monotherapy effect.

## Open problems

- Biomarker-driven IDO1 trials.
- Combination with AhR antagonists.

## Key papers

- Ricci 2025; ECHO-301 melanoma trial.

## My understanding

The IDO1 case is the textbook clinical-translation cautionary tale; future trials need quantitative biomarkers, not enthusiasm.
