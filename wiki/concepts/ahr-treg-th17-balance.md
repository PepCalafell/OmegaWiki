## Definition

AHR is the highest-expressed AHR-pathway component in Tr1, Th17, and Treg cells, and it controls the polarisation balance among these subsets. TCDD-like agonists plus TGFβ promote SMAD1- and Aiolos-driven Foxp3⁺ Treg differentiation; FICZ in the presence of IL-6/IL-21 drives RORγt-/Aiolos-/STAT3-dependent Th17 differentiation; IL-27 plus AHR drives c-Maf-dependent Tr1 cells producing IL-10, IL-21 and CD39. AHR also promotes Th17 → Tr1 conversion to limit immunopathology.

## Intuition

AHR doesn't have a fixed "pro-" or "anti-inflammatory" sign — it amplifies whichever T-cell programme is being initiated by the cytokine milieu. The therapeutic lever is the ligand: pick a ligand that biases AHR toward tolerogenic co-factors (SMAD1, c-Maf) rather than Th17 co-factors (RORγt, Aiolos in IL-6-rich context).

## Variants

- TCDD + TGFβ → Foxp3⁺ Treg (with TIGIT⁺ regulatory subset enriched)
- ITE + TGFβ → tolerogenic Treg expansion in EAE
- FICZ + IL-6/IL-21 → RORγt⁺ Th17 (EAE worsens)
- IL-27 → STAT3 → AHR → c-Maf → IL-10⁺ Tr1 cells
- AHR-driven Th17 → Tr1 conversion

## When to use

- When picking AHR ligands for autoimmunity (favour Treg/Tr1 bias)
- When interpreting microbiome-derived AHR ligand effects on intestinal Treg/Th17 balance

## Known limitations

- Same ligand can drive Treg in some contexts and Th17 in others — depends on cytokines, tissue, and dose
- Many studies are mouse-centric; human Treg/Tr1/Th17 biology may differ in AHR dependence

## Open problems

- A clinically actionable biomarker for AHR-driven Treg vs Th17 bias
- Targeted SAHRMs that engage only Treg- or Tr1-favouring AHR conformations
