## Definition

A discrete subpopulation of paravertebral noradrenergic sympathetic neurons (~30% of stellate and thoracic T2 ganglion neurons; sNuc-seq cluster 3) co-expressing Pdyn and the noradrenergic marker Slc18a2 (Net), characterised by large soma, TH⁺ VIP⁻ Npy⁻ phenotype, dense innervation of the dermis of hairy skin (but not glabrous skin, lung, kidney, spleen, gut), and projection patterns traced back via PRV-Bartha to the paraventricular nucleus and locus ceruleus of the brain.

## Intuition

Sympathetic neurons obey a "label-line" organisation rule: distinct subtypes innervate distinct target tissues and mediate distinct effector functions. Pdyn⁺ neurons occupy the hairy-skin / piloerector niche, mechanistically separable from Npy⁺ neurons (vasomotor) and Npy⁺ VIP⁺ neurons (sweat / vasodilation). Their selective ablation, silencing, or optogenetic activation produces dermatitis phenotypes that map cleanly onto stress paradigms — providing the first sympathetic subtype-specific manipulation in skin neuroimmunology.

## Variants

- **PdynNet-TD mice** — intersectional Cre/Flp drives tdTomato + DTR in Pdyn⁺ noradrenergic neurons.
- **PdynNet-Abl** — DTx-induced ablation of the subtype.
- **PdynNet-CatCh** — optogenetic activation.
- **PdynNet-hM4Di** — chemogenetic silencing.

## Comparison

Contrasts directly with Npy⁺ sympathetic neurons (NpyNet line), which surround cutaneous blood vessels and mediate the vasoconstriction reflex during RHS but do not drive eosinophil-mediated dermatitis exacerbation. Distinct from cholinergic VIP⁺ sympathetic neurons (sweat) and from Npy⁺ VIP⁺ vasodilatory subtypes.
