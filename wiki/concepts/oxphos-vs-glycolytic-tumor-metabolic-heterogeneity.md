## Definition

A within-entity classification of human tumours into "glycolytic" subsets that route most glucose to lactate (Warburg-like, minimal mitochondrial activity) and "OxPhos" subsets that drive proliferation through mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation fuelled by glycolysis, glutamine, and fatty acids. First demonstrated in DLBCL and since extended to many human cancers.

## Intuition

Two tumours with identical histology can run on fundamentally different power plants. Metabolic stratification (e.g., GAPDH protein in DLBCL) opens the door to phenotype-matched therapy: OxPhos tumours respond to L-asparaginase, glycolytic tumours to glycolysis-targeted strategies.

## Formal notation

Phenotype space ≈ continuum between glycolytic (high LDHA/HK, high lactate) and OxPhos (high ETC complex activity, glutamine/fatty-acid oxidation).

## Variants

- DLBCL: BCR vs OxPhos clusters (Caro 2012); GAPDH protein discriminator.
- Solid tumours: PDAC, RCC, melanoma, NSCLC, breast cancer increasingly stratified.

## Comparison

- Distinct from the [[concepts/warburg-effect-hif1a-glycolytic-reprogramming|Warburg effect]] concept: Warburg is a mechanism (HIF1α-driven aerobic glycolysis), this is a tumour-level phenotype dichotomy.

## When to use

- Choosing patients for ASNase, IDH inhibitors, complex-I inhibitors, or other metabolic therapies.
- Designing combination regimens with ICB.

## Known limitations

- Continuum rather than strict dichotomy; intra-tumoural heterogeneity not captured.
- Bulk omics under-represents the phenotype.

## Open problems

- Routine non-invasive metabolic phenotyping in clinic.
- Spatial mapping of metabolic phenotype within a single tumour.

## Key papers

- Ricci 2025 review and Chiche/Ricci 2024 GAPDH-DLBCL biomarker work.

## My understanding

Metabolic stratification is the only way to make metabolic inhibitors translate clinically — pan-population trials have failed. This concept is the umbrella for ASNase rescue in DLBCL OxPhos subsets and analogous future biomarker stories.
