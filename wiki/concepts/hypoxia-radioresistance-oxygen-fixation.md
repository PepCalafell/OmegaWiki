## Definition

Hypoxic tumor cells are 2–3× more resistant to ionizing radiation than well-oxygenated cells (oxygen enhancement ratio, OER ≈ 2.5–3). The mechanism is "oxygen fixation": ionizing radiation generates free radicals (·OH, ·H) that break DNA; molecular O₂ is required to "fix" the damage into permanent peroxide groups (R-OO·), preventing repair. Under hypoxia, radicals are chemically reversed (by intracellular thiols) before fixation, and the damage is repaired.

## Intuition

At pO₂ < 10 mmHg, radiosensitivity drops sharply; at 1 mmHg, cells are 3× resistant relative to normoxia. In addition to the chemical-fixation mechanism, hypoxia *enhances* DNA repair via HIF1α-induced DNA-PK upregulation, secretes SPINK1 paracrine factor (radioprotecting neighbour cells via EGFR/NRF2 antioxidants), and protects the vasculature via radiation-induced VEGF/FGF/PDGF release.

## Variants

- Pure oxygen-fixation effect (acute physical hypoxia, OER ≈ 2.5–3)
- HIF1α-induced DNA-PK upregulation (chronic, transcriptional)
- SPINK1-EGFR-NRF2 paracrine radioprotection (Nrf2 antioxidant response)
- Vascular protection (VEGF/FGF/PDGF secreted under irradiation)

## When to use

Cite when discussing radiotherapy resistance, dose-painting rationale, hypoxia-relief strategies for radiotherapy enhancement, or DNA-PK / EGFR / NRF2 antagonism.

## Known limitations

- OER varies by cell type and fractionation regimen.
- Reoxygenation between fractions partially mitigates resistance.
- Hyperfractionation and stereotactic ablative regimens partially circumvent the hypoxic effect.

## Open problems

- Whether nano-O₂-generating radiosensitizers (BFO/BWO, MnO₂, Au@Pt) translate to clinical benefit.
- Whether HIF / DNA-PK / NRF2 co-inhibition with radiation overcomes hypoxic radioresistance.

## My understanding

OER is the oldest hypoxia-cancer phenotype documented (Gray 1953), and remains the most quantitatively robust. For thesis work, radioresistance is the most clinically actionable hypoxia phenotype because radiation doses can be modulated voxel-by-voxel (dose painting) using PET tracer maps.
