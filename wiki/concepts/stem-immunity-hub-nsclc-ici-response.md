## Definition

A spatially organised pretreatment microniche in NSCLC biopsies enriched for stem-like TCF7⁺PD1⁺CD8⁺ T cells, activated CCR7⁺LAMP3⁺ DCs, CCL19⁺ CAFs and CXCL10⁺ TAMs preferentially engaging TCF7⁻CD8⁺ T cells; its presence predicts response to PD-(L)1 blockade.

## Intuition

A "launching pad" for ICI response: chemokine-organized DC–CAF–TAM scaffold supports stem-like CD8 T cells that can be re-expanded by checkpoint release.

## When to use

For pretreatment biopsy interpretation and as candidate predictive niche signature for ICI response.
