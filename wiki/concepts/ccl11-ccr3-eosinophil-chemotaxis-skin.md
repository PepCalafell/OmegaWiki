## Definition

Selective recruitment of CCR3⁺ eosinophils into peripheral tissue driven by tissue-derived CCL11 (eotaxin-1). In stress-aggravated atopic dermatitis, the cellular source of CCL11 is the Pdyn⁺ noradrenergic sympathetic neuron (not the epithelium, fibroblast, or Th2 cell typically considered).

## Intuition

CCL11 is the dominant ligand in CCR3-mediated chemotaxis; CCR3 is the dominant chemokine receptor on mature eosinophils. The axis was characterised classically in allergic asthma. The Tian 2026 contribution is to add a *neural source* of CCL11 — sympathetic neurons themselves — closing a loop between brain stress responses and peripheral eosinophil trafficking.

## Variants

- **Pharmacological block** — small-molecule CCR3 antagonist SB297006.
- **Antibody block** — anti-CCR3 monoclonal (Grimaldi 1999).
- **Genetic block** — CCR3 KO mice (literature, not used in this paper).

## Comparison

Parallels but is mechanistically distinct from the IL-5 / IL-5Rα axis that mobilises eosinophils from bone marrow systemically — clinical IL-5 / IL-5Rα blockade has shown limited benefit in AD precisely because it does not address sympathetic-driven local chemoattraction (Whetstone 2025; Kang 2020). Coexists in inflamed skin with CCL24 and CCL26 eotaxins, which also engage CCR3.
