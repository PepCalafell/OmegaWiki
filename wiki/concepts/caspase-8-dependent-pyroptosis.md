## Definition

A lytic, gasdermin-D-mediated pyroptosis driven directly by active caspase-8 cleaving GSDMD, occurring independently of NLRP3, caspase-1, and the canonical inflammasome. It is licensed when prosurvival signaling (TAK1/NF-κB/cFLIP) is lost.

## Intuition

Caspase-8 — the "apoptotic" caspase — can act as a pyroptotic enzyme when scaffolded appropriately and freed from the cFLIP brake, cleaving GSDMD just as caspase-1 would.

## Formal notation

N/A.

## Variants

Seen with TAK1 inhibition, *Yersinia* YopJ, and LPS/TNF stimuli; the efferoptosis instance ([[concepts/efferoptosis-tnf-driven-pyroptotic-switch]]) is initiated by efferocytosis and is GSDME-independent.

## Comparison

Versus canonical pyroptosis: bypasses NLRP3/caspase-1. Versus apoptosis: lytic and GSDMD-dependent rather than membrane-conserving.

## When to use

Invoke when GSDMD cleavage and IL-1β release persist despite inflammasome/caspase-1 deficiency.

## Known limitations

Requires loss of cFLIP / TAK1 activity; thresholds vary by cell type.

## Open problems

Structural basis of direct caspase-8→GSDMD cleavage efficiency relative to caspase-1.
