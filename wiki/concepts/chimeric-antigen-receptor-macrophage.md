## Definition

Macrophages genetically engineered to express a chimeric antigen receptor that redirects their phagocytic and antigen-presenting functions toward tumor antigens. In contrast to CAR-T cells, CAR-Ms aim to combine targeted ADCP (antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis), cross-presentation, and inflammatory remodeling of the TME.

## Intuition

CAR-Ms exploit the fact that macrophages, unlike T cells, can both kill via phagocytosis and prime T cells via MHC-II antigen presentation. Persistence and trafficking remain major hurdles. CT-0508, a HER2-targeting CAR-M, did not show clinically significant antitumor activity in early trials — illustrating that the platform still requires combined approaches (e.g., armoring with IL-12, combining with checkpoint blockade).

## Variants

- First-generation: macrophages transduced ex vivo with anti-HER2 CAR (CT-0508)
- IL-12-armored anti-TAM CAR T cells (related but T-cell-borne): see [[concepts/il12-armored-anti-tam-car-t]]
