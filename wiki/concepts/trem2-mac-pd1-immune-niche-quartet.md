## Definition

A spatial cellular niche in HCC immune aggregates comprising four co-localised members: TREM2 macrophages, CXCL13+ PD-1hi CD4 helper T cells, TCF1+ PD-1hi CD8 progenitor T cells, and mregDCs (mature regulatory dendritic cells). Refinement of the prior CXCL13/TCF1/mregDC ICB-response triad (Hamon 2024) into a TREM2-mac-inclusive quartet, associated with response to PD-1 blockade in HCC.

## Intuition

If T-cell-centric ICB-response biology localises to a CXCL13/TCF1/mregDC triad, the TREM2-mac inclusion identifies macrophages as the fourth pillar — physically engaging the PD-1hi T-cell progenitors and bringing antigen-presenting + co-stimulatory + cytokine-receptor capacity into the niche.

## Formal notation

- Members: TREM2 mac + CXCL13+ PD-1hi CD4 Th + TCF1+ PD-1hi CD8 + mregDC
- Localisation: B-cell-rich immune aggregates within the HCC TME
- Detection: MERFISH spatial proximity analysis (Hamon 2025)
- Functional state: contact-engaged TREM2 macs upregulate MHC-II/CD86/IFNGR1; contact-engaged PD-1hi CD8 upregulate KLRK1/EOMES/GZMs

## Variants

- Discovery-cohort cemiplimab HCC variant
- Validation-cohort cemiplimab+SBRT variant
- Possible NSCLC variant — not characterised here

## Comparison

vs Hamon 2024 triad (CXCL13/TCF1/mregDC): same T-cell + DC component, adds TREM2 mac.
vs canonical TLS (B-cell follicle / T-cell zone / FDC / HEV): TLS-like in containing B cells and mature DCs but cellularly distinct (TLS lacks TREM2-mac inclusion in this formulation).

## When to use

When characterising HCC immune-aggregate biology, when designing ICB-response biomarker panels, or when extending T-cell-centric ICB models with myeloid involvement.

## Known limitations

- Quartet is observational/spatial, not perturbative.
- Causal role of TREM2 macs in aggregate formation or stabilisation not demonstrated.
- Generalisation to non-HCC tumors not tested.

## Open problems

- Does perturbing any quartet member (e.g., anti-TREM2 antibody) destabilise the niche and reduce ICB response in HCC?
- Are the quartet contacts simultaneously formed or sequentially recruited?
