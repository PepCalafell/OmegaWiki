## Definition

Androgen receptor signalling drives accumulation of progenitor exhausted CD8⁺ T cells across mouse urothelial cancer and human NSCLC, contributing to reduced ICI efficacy in males. Androgen-depriving agents or AR antagonists enhance ICI activity in preclinical models.

## Intuition

Sex hormones reshape the T-cell exhaustion landscape — testosterone biases CD8 cells toward a progenitor-exhausted state with limited responsiveness to checkpoint release.

## When to use

In sex-stratified ICI trial interpretation and as rationale for combining AR antagonists with anti-PD-(L)1 in male NSCLC patients.
