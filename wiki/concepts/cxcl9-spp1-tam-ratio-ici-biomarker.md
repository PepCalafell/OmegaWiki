## Definition

The ratio of CXCL9-expressing antitumorigenic TAMs to SPP1-expressing protumorigenic TAMs as a tissue-level marker of immune state and ICI response across solid tumours, including NSCLC. A low CXCL9:SPP1 ratio marks an immune-excluded, fibrotic, ICI-resistant TME.

## Intuition

CXCL9⁺ TAMs (IFNγ-driven) recruit effector T cells via CXCR3; SPP1⁺ TAMs remodel ECM and promote immune exclusion. The ratio collapses the heterogeneity of TAM states into a single biomarker tied to ICI outcomes.

## Variants

- Pan-cancer formulations of the ratio across colorectal, breast, NSCLC.
- Spatially weighted variants (TAM phenotype as a function of distance to tumour nest).

## When to use

As a candidate biomarker for ICI response stratification in NSCLC; in spatial transcriptomic atlases comparing responders and non-responders.

## Known limitations

- Single-axis collapse of TAM diversity; ignores TREM2⁺, COL1A1⁺, FOLR2⁺ states.
- Threshold definitions vary by platform/cohort.
