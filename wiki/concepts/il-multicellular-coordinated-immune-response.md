## Definition

IL-1α and IL-1β, unlike type I interferons, trigger highly cell-type-specific gene programmes rather than a uniform pathway. The result is a coordinated multicellular immune response in which each cell type contributes a distinct functional output: neutrophils amplify inflammation (Cd14, GP27), MigDCs and Langerhans cells initiate migration (Ccr7, GP12), and Tregs induce immune-suppressive programmes (Hif1a, Ctla4, GP22).

## Intuition

The functional consequence of IL-1 at the tissue level is not "IL-1 signaling" as a monolithic event — it is a structured division of labour among co-resident immune cell types. Antagonizing IL-1 systemically (anakinra, canakinumab) ablates *all* these distinct cellular contributions simultaneously, which explains both efficacy and off-target phenotypes.

## Variants

- IL-1 + neutrophil → chemokine amplification
- IL-1 + DC → migration priming (Ccr7, Nr4a3)
- IL-1 + Treg → immune suppression (Hif1a, Ctla4)
- IL-1 + monocyte → Il1b feed-forward inflammatory loop

## When to use

Use whenever interpreting bulk- or tissue-level IL-1 effects; do not collapse them into a single pathway score.

## My understanding

A specific instance of the broader [[concepts/cytokine-cell-type-specific-response-pleiotropy]] principle. Worth a standalone concept because IL-1 is one of the most clinically actionable cytokine axes (CANTOS, autoinflammatory disease, cardiometabolic indications).
