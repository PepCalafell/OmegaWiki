## Definition

Classification scheme for macrophage-targeted cancer therapies into three non-exclusive strategies: (1) **Depletion / reduced accumulation** — lower the pro-tumoral TAM burden by blocking survival cues, recruitment, or directed killing; (2) **Effectorization** — rapidly engage macrophage effector functions (phagocytosis, cytotoxic factor release) for immunogenic cancer cell killing; (3) **Reprogramming** — convert TAMs and their progenitors into antitumor states by rewiring signaling/metabolism, sustained activation/polarization, or genetic engineering.

## Intuition

Pure depletion (e.g., CSF1R blockade) has shown limited clinical benefit because tumors retain an immunosuppressed state and repopulating macrophages re-adopt suppressive programs. Effectorization alone (e.g., anti-CD47) can trigger anti-inflammatory feedback. The most effective next-generation regimens combine pillars — for example, IL-12-armored anti-TREM2 CAR T cells both *deplete* immunosuppressive TAMs and *reprogram* the repopulating compartment toward CXCL9⁺ states.
