## Definition

The TNF-centered hierarchy is the claim that TNF acts as the apex node of a multi-cytokine module: every cytokine pair that suffices to mirror sepsis contains TNF, anti-TNF prophylaxis fully rescues lethal LPS challenge, and TNF dominance in cross-tissue effects parallels the broader therapeutic prominence of anti-TNF agents in chronic inflammatory diseases.

## Intuition

Pairwise screening across 15 cytokine combinations places TNF at the apex empirically — not by curation. Each non-TNF pair has limited effect; each TNF pair has either synergistic or antagonistic non-additive effects. TNF blockade alone matches or beats anti-IL-18 / anti-IFNγ / anti-IL-1β in lethal LPS survival.

## When to use

Use when comparing single-cytokine therapeutic targets, interpreting why anti-TNF works as prophylaxis but not late-stage rescue in sepsis trials, or framing drug development for cytokine storm syndromes.

## Open problems

- Why is TNF the apex? Receptor ubiquity, kinetics, or signalling crosstalk?
- Why does anti-TNF fail in human sepsis when delivered after onset, despite the apex role?
- Is the same TNF apex preserved in human cytokine storm syndromes other than sepsis?

## My understanding

The hierarchy is the structural reason single-cytokine blockade trials in sepsis disappointed — they targeted satellites of the module, not the apex; and they intervened too late, after TNF had already produced its downstream cascade.
