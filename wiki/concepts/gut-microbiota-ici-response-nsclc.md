## Definition

Gut and lung microbiota compositions modulate systemic immunity and NSCLC TME state, affecting ICI efficacy. Akkermansia, Ruminococcus and Bifidobacterium correlate with enhanced antitumour immunity; Alistipes-driven dysbiosis (via IL-6 and other cytokines) promotes immunosuppression and resistance.

## Intuition

The microbiome acts as a modifiable, non-invasive lever on the TME — both a biomarker (faecal profiling) and a candidate intervention (probiotics, prebiotics, FMT).

## When to use

In NSCLC ICI biomarker discovery and combination trial design (e.g., FMT-augmented anti-PD-(L)1).

## Known limitations

- Highly context-dependent; specific taxa effects vary across cohorts.
- Causal interventions (FMT) remain in early-phase trials.
