## Definition

LUAD biopsies from patients aged >70 years show increased PD-L2, TIM3, immunosuppressive TAMs and reduced TANs, with a CD8 compartment shifted toward naïve- and memory-like (rather than classically exhausted) states. Crosstalk among DCs, NK and T cells is impaired, and IL-1α-rich tumour-induced myelopoiesis is amplified. Associated with reduced OS in early-stage disease and reduced ICI benefit in older patients.

## Intuition

Ageing is not simply "less immunity" but a qualitative rewiring of the TME toward inhibitory checkpoints, myeloid bias, and de-differentiated T-cell states.

## When to use

When stratifying NSCLC ICI candidates by age and designing age-aware combination strategies (e.g., IL-1α blockade, myeloid-targeted agents).
