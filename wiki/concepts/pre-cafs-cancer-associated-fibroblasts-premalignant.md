## Definition
Pre-CAFs are stromal fibroblast subpopulations identified in precancerous lesions of pancreas (PanIN), gastric, oesophageal and oral cancers that share many transcriptomic features with cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) of established tumours. They promote malignant progression and suppress cytotoxic immune responses already before invasion.

## Intuition
Microenvironmental reprogramming does not wait for invasion. Long before histologically defined cancer, stromal cells in precancerous lesions adopt CAF-like programmes that prime the tissue niche for transformation by suppressing CD8+ T-cell and NK-cell activity.

## When to use
When dissecting non-cell-autonomous drivers of early carcinogenesis and designing interception strategies that target the stromal compartment of precancerous tissue.

## Open problems
- Cell of origin of pre-CAFs (tissue-resident fibroblast vs recruited)
- Whether pre-CAFs are reversible by anti-inflammatory or stromal-modifying therapy
