## Definition

A mechanism whereby tumour-cell overexpression of the methionine transporter SLC43A2 (LAT4) outcompetes infiltrating T cells for methionine, lowering intracellular SAM and dimethylation at histone H3 lysine 79 (H3K79me2). The resulting epigenetic alteration reduces STAT5 expression and IL-2 signaling, producing T-cell dysfunction.

## Intuition

Tumours hijack the methylation reservoir — strip T cells of SAM and they lose a TF-program they need (STAT5/IL-2). Restoration via methionine supplementation or tumour-specific SLC43A2 blockade synergises with ICB in mouse models.

## Formal notation

[Met]T cell ↓ → [SAM]T cell ↓ → DOT1L-mediated H3K79me2 ↓ → STAT5/IL-2 ↓.

## Variants

- Tumour-specific SLC43A2 invalidation.
- Systemic methionine supplementation.
- Methionine restriction (opposing strategy in auxotrophic tumours).

## Comparison

- Distinct from glutamine/asparagine competition because of the strong epigenome readout.

## When to use

- Combining methionine modulation with ICB in SLC43A2-high tumours.

## Known limitations

- No selective SLC43A2 small-molecule inhibitor.
- Methionine restriction risk: also hits T cells.

## Open problems

- Tumour-selective SLC43A2 inhibitors.
- Biomarkers of SLC43A2-driven methionine competition.

## Key papers

- Bian et al. 2020 Nature; Ricci 2025 review.

## My understanding

The cleanest example of how tumour amino-acid uptake reprograms the T-cell epigenome — a model for thinking about methylation-coupled immunometabolism.
