## Definition

Spatial-profiling-defined progression of TLS in NSCLC from nascent "lymphoid aggregates" to "activated", "declining" and "late" stages, with the late-stage exhaustion-marker-rich form (IFNG, HAVCR2) correlating with complete response to chemo–ICI; activated TLS coexisting with hypoxia mark non-responders.

## Intuition

TLS biology is not "present vs absent" but a maturation trajectory; the *terminal* exhausted-CD8-skewed state captures the most productive antitumour response.

## When to use

For TLS-based stratification in NSCLC clinical trials and to refine TLS as an ICI biomarker.

## Known limitations

- Stage definitions are evolving and platform-dependent.
- Hypoxia's role in blocking maturation is correlative.
