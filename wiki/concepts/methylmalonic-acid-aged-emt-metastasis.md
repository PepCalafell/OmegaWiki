## Definition

Methylmalonic acid (MMA), a metabolite elevated in older individuals, induces EMT (↑fibronectin, ↑vimentin, ↓E-cadherin), activates TGFβ signalling and SOX4 expression, and enhances metastatic potential in mouse NSCLC models.

## Intuition

A systemic metabolite from ageing bridges senescence-related metabolism to tumour-cell-intrinsic EMT programmes.

## When to use

When investigating age-associated metastatic biology of NSCLC and proposing geriatric-targeted antimetastatic strategies.
