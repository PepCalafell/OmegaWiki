## Definition

Independent biological systems exposed to chronic systemic hypoxia — human populations adapted to high altitude (Tibetans, Sherpas, Andeans) and tumors arising in patients with chronic systemic hypoxia (e.g. cyanotic congenital heart disease) — converge on the same gene, **EPAS1 (HIF2α)**, as the dominant target of positive selection. Both systems reach ~90% prevalence of functional EPAS1 variants, an extreme degree of selection rarely seen for any single gene.

## Intuition

When the selective pressure is the same (low oxygen) and the master regulator of systemic hypoxia response is shared (HIF2α), evolution at the population scale and somatic clonal evolution at the tumor scale land on the same locus. The paper frames tumor evolution as a microcosm of population genetic adaptation, suggesting that genes positively selected in populations adapted to other natural stressors (UV, dietary restriction) may likewise be co-opted in the corresponding cancers.

## Variants

- Population-level germline/introgressed adaptation (Denisovan-derived EPAS1 haplotype in Tibetans)
- Somatic clonal adaptation in tumors (de novo EPAS1 missense mutations under systemic hypoxia)

## Comparison

Convergence is on the **gene**, not the **variant**: high-altitude human variants are loss-of-function (dampening the HIF2α–EPO axis to avoid polycythemia), whereas tumor variants are gain-of-function in the oxygen-degradation domain — opposite molecular consequences from the same locus (functional plasticity). See [[concepts/epas1-gain-function-oxygen-degradation-domain]].

## When to use

Use to motivate cross-disciplinary mining of population-genetics selection scans for candidate cancer drivers under matched environmental stressors.

## Known limitations

Demonstrated for one gene (EPAS1) in one tumor type (PPGL); generality to other stressor–cancer pairs is hypothesized, not shown.

## Open problems

- Do UV-adaptation or diet-adaptation genes drive the corresponding cancers (melanoma, metabolic tumors)?
- Mechanistic basis for why convergence is restricted to sympathoadrenal lineage.

## My understanding

A genuinely novel framing connecting evolutionary genomics and oncology; the strength is the quantitative parallel (90% vs 90%) and the functional-plasticity twist (LOF in populations, GOF in tumors).
