## Definition

Tapinarof cream 1% is the first FDA-approved (2022) AHR-targeting therapeutic, used for adult plaque psoriasis. PSOARING-1/2 phase III trials demonstrated superiority over vehicle; PSOARING-3 extension showed 41% complete clearance with a remittive effect of median 4 months off-treatment. Mechanism: AHR-dependent suppression of Th17 cytokines (IL-17A/F), upregulation of skin-barrier genes (filaggrin, loricrin, involucrin, hornerin, ceramides), and Nrf2-driven antioxidant programmes. Effects are lost in AHR-deficient mice. ADORING-1/2 phase III met endpoints for atopic dermatitis; ZBA4 trials confirmed efficacy in Japanese patients.

## Intuition

Tapinarof works because it (i) gives transient AHR activation (no dioxin-like persistence), (ii) repairs the epidermal barrier as well as suppressing inflammation, and (iii) memorises an anti-Th17 state in tissue memory T cells (likely tied to AHR-driven CD69⁺CD103⁺ memory T-cell biology) — giving durable clearance.

## When to use

- As the proof-of-concept template for designing AHR agonists in other epithelial-barrier diseases
- For comparing topical AHR agonism with steroidal/JAK-inhibitor approaches

## Known limitations

- Topical only; systemic AHR agonism remains untested clinically
- Common adverse events: folliculitis, contact dermatitis at application site
- Long-term effects of years-long topical AHR activation under monitoring

## Open problems

- Whether tapinarof's "remittive" effect generalises to other AHR agonists
- Mechanism of memory T-cell modulation in clinical disease control
