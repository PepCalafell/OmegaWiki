## Definition

Cholesterol accumulation in CD8⁺ T cells within the tumour microenvironment triggers endoplasmic-reticulum stress, driving T-cell exhaustion and uncontrolled tumour growth; elevated plasma cholesterol also disrupts systemic T-cell homeostasis in patients with hypercholesterolemia, contributing to chronic inflammation.

## Intuition

Lipid-rich TMEs (especially with TREM2⁺ lipid-laden TAMs nearby) push CD8 T cells past their lipid-handling capacity; the unfolded-protein response then locks them into a dysfunctional exhaustion program.

## Variants

- Cholesterol-driven Tex (ER-stress / XBP1 mediated)
- Oxidized-cholesterol / oxysterol-driven Tex (distinct route via LXR / EBI2 axes)

## Comparison

A lipid-specific branch of the broader [[concepts/cd8-t-cell-exhaustion-texterm]] program, mechanistically distinct from chronic-antigen-driven exhaustion.

## When to use

When modelling exhaustion in lipid-laden tumours (NSCLC, HCC, melanoma with lipid-laden TAMs), or when proposing cholesterol-lowering combinations with ICB.

## Known limitations

- Direct measurement of intracellular T-cell cholesterol in human tumours remains technically challenging.
- ER-stress-blockers (e.g., XBP1 inhibitors) are nonspecific.

## Open problems

- Whether statin or ACAT inhibitor combinations with ICB rescue CD8 function clinically.
- Interaction with TREM2-LAM lipid handoff.
