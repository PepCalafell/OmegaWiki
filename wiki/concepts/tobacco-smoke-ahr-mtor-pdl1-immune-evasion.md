## Definition

Persistent tobacco smoking drives chronic inflammation in lung tissue and upregulates PD-L1 expression on cancer and stromal cells through aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AHR) activation that signals via mTOR, reinforcing immune evasion in smoking-related NSCLC.

## Intuition

A direct mechanistic bridge between environmental exposure (tobacco-derived AHR ligands), a cancer-relevant transcription factor (AHR), a metabolic-translational hub (mTOR), and a checkpoint ligand (PD-L1).

## When to use

In smoking-stratified NSCLC analyses; potential rationale for combining AHR antagonists or mTOR inhibitors with anti-PD-(L)1 in heavy smokers.
