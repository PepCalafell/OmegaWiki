## Definition

The CXCL13 chemokine acting through CXCR5 on B cells and follicular helper T cells coordinates lymphocyte recruitment and organisation of tertiary lymphoid structures. Activated in NSCLC by nivolumab + ipilimumab + chemotherapy combinations, facilitating TLS maturation and antitumour immunity.

## Intuition

Co-opts the physiological B-cell follicle organisation programme to build immune hubs within tumours.

## When to use

In interpretation of combination-ICI biology and TLS-targeted therapeutic strategies (e.g., chemokine-priming).
