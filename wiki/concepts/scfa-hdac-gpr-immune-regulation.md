## Definition

Microbiota-derived short-chain fatty acids (acetate, propionate, butyrate) regulate immune cells through two convergent mechanisms: (i) signalling through GPR41 (FFAR3), GPR43 (FFAR2), and GPR109A on epithelial, adipose, and immune cells; and (ii) direct inhibition of histone deacetylases (HDACs), raising acetylation of NF-κB p65, p70-S6K, and other regulators to reshape transcription. Together this axis controls macrophage M1/M2 balance, Treg function, Th1/Th17 generation, Breg differentiation, plasma-cell output, neutrophil migration/NETosis, DC antigen presentation, and mast-cell degranulation.

## Intuition

A "dietary-fibre dial" — high-fibre diets raise SCFA pools, which favour M2 macrophages, Tregs, and plasma B cells; low-fibre/Western diets blunt this signal. The same SCFA family can produce opposite effects depending on cell type and isomer (acetate vs propionate/butyrate), so contextual interpretation is required.

## Variants

- HDAC-inhibition branch (dominant in T cells, B cells, M1→M2 switching)
- GPR43 (FFAR2) branch (epithelial / Treg)
- GPR109A branch (butyrate-restricted, dendritic-cell tolerogenic phenotype)
- cGAS-STING-IFN-I activation via GPR43 (acetate/propionate antiviral arm)

## Comparison

Distinct from but cooperating with [[concepts/efferocytosis-metabolic-rewiring-fao]] (apoptotic-cell-driven FAO/PPAR rewiring) and [[concepts/m1-m2-polarization-paradigm]] (canonical cytokine-driven polarization). SCFA-HDAC-GPR adds an exogenous, microbiota-derived axis on top.

## When to use

Whenever explaining microbiota-immune crosstalk, gut-derived immunomodulation in IBD/colitis/autoimmunity, dietary-fibre effects on Treg/Breg, or HDAC inhibitor mechanisms beyond cancer epigenetics.

## Known limitations

- Whether SCFAs drive peripheral CD4 → Foxp3⁺ Treg differentiation (vs sustaining Treg function) is debated.
- Effects are dose- and isomer-dependent (acetate suppresses M2 in asthma; propionate/butyrate suppress M1) — binary mappings mislead.

## Open problems

- Disentangle GPR43 vs HDAC contributions cell-type by cell-type.
- Translate the SCFA-Breg/IgA axis into mucosal vaccine adjuvants.
