## Definition

Therapeutic antibody whose paratope is occluded by a polymer or protein moiety that is cleaved by proteases (or other factors) enriched in the tumor microenvironment, restricting target engagement to the tumor and raising the therapeutic index. Used to mitigate on-target/off-tumor toxicity of targets like CD47.

## Intuition

For broadly expressed targets, systemic engagement causes hematologic or organ toxicity. Conditional unmasking limits binding to sites where the protease activity (e.g., MMP, urokinase) is elevated. Clinical example: ADG153, a masked CD47 antibody currently in clinical trials.
