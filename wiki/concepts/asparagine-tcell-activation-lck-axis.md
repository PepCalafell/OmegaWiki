## Definition

Recent discovery that L-asparagine directly enhances CD8 T-cell activation by binding the proximal TCR-signaling tyrosine kinase LCK and promoting its phosphorylation. Asn restriction (via L-asparaginase or dietary depletion) reduces TCR-proximal signaling and impairs CD8 effector function; prolonged restriction induces NRF2-mediated adaptive restoration.

## Intuition

Amino acids do more than build proteins: asparagine acts as a direct allosteric partner for an early TCR kinase. This makes ASNase pharmacologically immune-disruptive even when the tumour is the intended target.

## Formal notation

Asn → LCK binding → LCK-Y394 phosphorylation → TCR-proximal signal → IL-2 production / proliferation.

## Variants

- ASNase-mediated systemic Asn depletion.
- Dietary Asn restriction.
- Tumour-cell Asn sequestration in the TME.

## Known limitations

- NRF2 adaptation partially restores function on prolonged restriction.
- Asn supplementation could also feed tumour cells.

## Open problems

- Therapeutic window between Asn-depleting tumour effect and TIL impairment.
