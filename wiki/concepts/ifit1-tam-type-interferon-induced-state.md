## Definition

A tumor-associated macrophage state marked by IFIT1 and other type I interferon-stimulated genes, induced by type I interferon (IFN-I). It is transcriptionally distinct from the IFN-γ-driven CXCL9+ TAM and from the pro-tumoral SPP1+ TAM, and is governed by an IRF2-leaning regulatory program (versus IRF1 in CXCL9+ TAMs).

## Intuition

Interferons split TAMs into two M1-like states by interferon type: IFN-γ yields CXCL9+ T-cell-recruiting TAMs, while IFN-I yields IFIT1+ TAMs. IFIT1+ status reflects IFN-I exposure but does not by itself guarantee an immune-hot tumor — it can co-occur with immunosuppressive SPP1+ TAMs and LRRC15+ CAFs.

## Variants

- Cross-lineage IFIT1+ populations beyond macrophages anchor an IFN-I multicellular module.

## Comparison

Adds a third axis to the [[cxcl9-spp1-tam-ratio-ici-biomarker]] polarity: IFN-I (IFIT1+) vs IFN-γ (CXCL9+) vs pro-tumoral (SPP1+).

## When to use

When distinguishing the interferon type driving a tumor's macrophage program and interpreting IFN-I signaling, which has dual pro- and anti-tumor effects.

## Known limitations

- Induction inferred from in vitro stimulation and signaling-activity inference, not in vivo lineage tracing.

## Open problems

- Whether IFIT1+ TAMs are terminal or interconvert with CXCL9+/SPP1+ states.

A marker-defined, interferon-type-specific TAM state whose context determines whether it tracks immune activation or suppression.
