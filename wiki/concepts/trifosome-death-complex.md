## Definition

A TRIF-dependent, pro-death cytosolic complex containing RIPK1, ZBP1, and caspase-8 that drives caspase-8-dependent pyroptosis in response to LPS/TNF-type stimuli. RIPK1 contributes scaffolding (not kinase) activity; ZBP1 is constitutively RIPK1-bound and recruitment is enhanced by activating stimuli.

## Intuition

A molecular platform that brings caspase-8 into proximity for activation; assembling it (not RIPK1 kinase signaling) is what matters for death.

## Formal notation

N/A.

## Variants

Engaged in *Yersinia* infection, LPS+TAK1 inhibition, and here in TNF-driven efferoptosis.

## Comparison

Versus the ripoptosome/complex IIb: overlapping components but TRIF-nucleated and ZBP1-dependent.

## When to use

When caspase-8 activation requires TRIF and ZBP1 but is independent of RIPK1 kinase activity.

## Known limitations

Stoichiometry and structural arrangement remain incompletely defined.

## Open problems

How efferocytic signals are relayed to TRIFosome assembly at the molecular level.
