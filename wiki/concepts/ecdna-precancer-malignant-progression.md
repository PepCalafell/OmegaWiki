## Definition
Deep genomic sequencing of Barrett's oesophagus biopsies has identified extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) elements present already at the precancerous stage. These ecDNAs amplify oncogenes (ERBB2, KRAS, MYC) and immunomodulatory genes (CIITA, SOCS1) and are strongly enriched in patients who go on to develop oesophageal adenocarcinoma.

## Intuition
Unlike linear chromosomes, ecDNAs lack centromeres and segregate asymmetrically, generating cell-to-cell heterogeneity and a substrate for rapid positive selection. Their early appearance and progression-associated enrichment make ecDNA a candidate causal driver of the precancer-to-cancer transition.

## When to use
When considering genomic biomarkers or therapeutic targets specifically tied to the transition from precancerous lesion to invasive cancer.

## Open problems
- Whether ecDNA also drives progression in non-EAC cancers with precursor lesions
- Mechanism by which immunomodulatory ecDNA cargo (CIITA, SOCS1) shapes the early immune microenvironment
