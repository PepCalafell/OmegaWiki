## Definition

In preclinical models and obese-patient cohorts, the biguanide glucose-lowering agent metformin reprograms the immune TME by reducing Treg activity and improving Teff function, synergising with ICIs.

## Intuition

A widely available, low-toxicity drug repurposed as an immune-metabolic adjuvant for checkpoint blockade in metabolically dysregulated patients.

## When to use

In design of combination ICI trials enriched for obese or diabetic NSCLC patients.
