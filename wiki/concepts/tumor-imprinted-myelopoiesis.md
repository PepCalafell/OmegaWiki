## Definition

Systemic process by which tumor-derived soluble factors (IL-1α/β, IL-6, IL-8, CCL2, CSF-1, CSF-2, CSF-3) reach the bone marrow and spleen, mobilizing immunosuppressive myeloid progenitors and imprinting them with epigenetic modifications that bias their progeny toward pro-tumor TAM states before they ever reach the tumor. Splenic niches expand the reservoir of pro-tumorigenic myeloid progenitors.

## Intuition

This reframes TAM biology from a purely local TME-driven process to one with a systemic upstream component. The implication for therapy is that progenitor-targeted interventions (e.g., engineered HSCs producing IFN-α, as in temferon) may be more durable than tumor-site-only approaches because they prevent re-imprinting of repopulating macrophages.
