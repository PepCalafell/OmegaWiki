## Definition

Maturation of pro-IL-1β to its bioactive p17 form by direct caspase-8 cleavage, bypassing caspase-1 and the NLRP3 inflammasome. In efferoptosis the priming (pro-Il1b transcription) is supplied by PLCγ/MAPK downstream of PS receptors rather than by canonical TAK1/NF-κB.

## Intuition

Two things are needed for mature IL-1β: enough pro-IL-1β (priming) and an enzyme to cut it (maturation). Here PLCγ/MAPK supplies the priming and caspase-8 supplies the cut — neither step needs the inflammasome.

## Variants

Caspase-8 IL-1β cleavage was previously described in fungal infection; here it is coupled to efferocytosis and PLCγ-driven priming.

## Comparison

Versus canonical IL-1β maturation: independent of caspase-1/NLRP3; uses a non-canonical priming route (PLCγ/MAPK) decoupled from NF-κB.

## When to use

When mature IL-1β release persists in Casp1-/-/Nlrp3-/- settings.

## Known limitations

Demonstrated in macrophage models; quantitative contribution relative to canonical routes in vivo unclear.

## Open problems

Whether PLCγ-driven priming generalizes to other IL-1 family members.
