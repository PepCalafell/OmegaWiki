## Definition

In a KP-driven mouse LUAD model, depletion of regulatory T cells re-programs fibroblast, endothelial and TAM transcriptional states and amplifies VEGF signalling, leading to increased vascularization and altered innate/adaptive immune cell composition.

## Intuition

Tregs act not only as suppressors of effector T cells but as gatekeepers of stromal/vascular state; removing them rewires the entire microenvironmental ecosystem rather than simply releasing cytotoxic T cells.

## When to use

When interpreting Treg-depletion or Treg-targeted therapy experiments in lung cancer models; cautionary signal for antiangiogenic combinations.
