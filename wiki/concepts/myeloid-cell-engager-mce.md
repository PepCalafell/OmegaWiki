## Definition

Bispecific antibody platform that simultaneously engages a tumor antigen and a macrophage activating receptor (e.g., Dectin-1, FcγR, TLR-coupled receptor), forcing a productive synapse between the macrophage and the cancer cell to drive immunogenic phagocytosis. SYK-coupled receptors (such as Dectin-1) are favored because they trigger pro-inflammatory output rather than tolerogenic clearance.

## Intuition

MCEs outperform conventional opsonizing antibodies by raising the probability of engulfment even in suppressive milieus, and by creating a conduit for antigen transfer to MHC-II for T cell priming. Clinical example: Dren Bio's DR-0201 (CD20 × Dectin-1), in phase 1 (NCT06392477) for B-cell malignancies.

## Variants

- CD20 × Dectin-1 (DR-0201 / SAR448501)
- TG-1801: bispecific CD47 × CD19 (effectorization + checkpoint blockade)
