## Definition

Class of cell-surface receptors on TAMs that sense and clear cellular debris under homeostasis but enforce immunosuppressive programs in tumors. Blockade aims to relieve TAM-mediated suppression of T- and NK-cell immunity. Members include complement receptors (C3/C5a/C5aR1/C1q), MARCO, LAIR-1, LILRB2/LILRB4, CLEVER-1 (STAB1), TAM-expressed PD-1 and PD-L1, and TREM2.

## Intuition

Unlike T-cell checkpoints (PD-1/CTLA-4), innate checkpoints operate on phagocytes and modulate antigen presentation, scavenging, and tonic suppression of T/NK responses. Clinical efficacy has been heterogeneous: LILRB2/LILRB4 and CLEVER-1 have shown safety and partial efficacy in solid tumors; TREM2 antagonism (PY314) failed to show clinically significant antitumor activity despite preclinical promise, suggesting that some innate checkpoints are better repurposed as anchors for MCEs / CAR-T / nanoparticles rather than as pure antagonist targets.
