# M1 macrophage TCA-cycle breaks

Upon TLR/LPS activation, M1 macrophages introduce **two breaks** in the TCA cycle: (1) downregulation of IDH1 stalls isocitrate→α-ketoglutarate, accumulating citrate that fuels acetyl-CoA, histone acetylation of inflammatory loci, and de-novo FAS; concurrently, IRG1/ACOD1 diverts cis-aconitate to **itaconate**. (2) Itaconate inhibits SDH (complex II), blocking succinate→fumarate and accumulating **succinate**, which stabilizes **HIF-1α** and drives a second, sustained wave of glycolytic/Warburg-like metabolic reprogramming. mtROS from oxidized succinate amplifies inflammatory cytokine output and bactericidal activity, but also damages DNA and forces a NAD⁺-salvage dependence.

Itaconate additionally electrophilically modifies KEAP1 (activating NRF2) and modulates cytokines independently of succinate. The pathway is the **molecular core** of in-vitro proinflammatory macrophage immunometabolism and the basis for therapeutic interest in itaconate derivatives for fibrosis and inflammation.
