## Definition

In NSCLC, higher BMI correlates with improved PFS and OS under PD-1 blockade. Mechanistically, leptin and IL-6 secreted by adipose tissue drive T-cell exhaustion and upregulate PD-1, creating a therapeutic dependency that ICIs can exploit.

## Intuition

A pathological state (exhaustion) becomes a pharmacological asset when its dominant inhibitory axis is precisely the one targeted by the drug.

## When to use

In BMI-stratified ICI trial design and combination considerations (e.g., adding metformin in obese patients).
