LXRα (NR1H3) is a nuclear receptor that senses oxysterols and desmosterol to drive cholesterol efflux (ABCA1/ABCG1), fatty-acid synthesis (SREBP1c), and macrophage-niche identity programs. In the liver it is the highest-expressed transcription factor in Kupffer cells and is induced by liver sinusoidal endothelial Notch ligands; in the spleen marginal zone, LXRα/β control development and immune-clearance function of MZM/MMM macrophages. LXR agonists are explored therapeutically for atherosclerosis and pulmonary alveolar proteinosis.
