## Definition

IL-12B (p40) is the protein subunit shared by the heterodimeric cytokines IL-12 and IL-23, both central to T cell–mediated immunity. It is mainly secreted by activated antigen-presenting cells such as dendritic cells.

## Intuition

Because IL-12 and IL-23 drive Th1 and Th17 responses respectively, IL-12B levels tune inflammatory tone. Circulating IL-12B is highly heritable and genetically linked to immune-mediated disease.
