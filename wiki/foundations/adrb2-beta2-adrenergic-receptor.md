## Definition

Adrb2 is a Gs-coupled β2 adrenergic GPCR activated by noradrenaline/adrenaline. Canonical Gs–cAMP signalling drives anti-inflammatory effects, but β-arrestin-2 recruitment supports a parallel proinflammatory branch. In eosinophils, Adrb2 is the most abundantly expressed adrenergic receptor and its activation by sympathetic noradrenaline release triggers degranulation of cationic granule proteins (Epx, MBP) in inflamed AD-like skin.

## Relevance to active research

Eosinophil-specific Adrb2 knockout (Epx^iCre-Adrb2^fl/fl) protects against stress-induced exacerbation of dermatitis without altering baseline AD pathology, establishing Adrb2 as the receptor-level integrator between sympathetic noradrenergic tone and eosinophil effector function in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]. Connects to debated clinical role of β2-agonists (salmeterol, tulobuterol) in asthma/AD.
