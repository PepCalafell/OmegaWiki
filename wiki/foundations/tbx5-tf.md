## Definition

TBX5 is a T-box transcription factor essential for cardiac septation, conduction-system formation, and forelimb development. Heterozygous loss causes Holt-Oram syndrome (upper-limb + cardiac defects). Co-binds GATA4/NKX2.5 at cardiac enhancers. Used in Khetan 2025 as a structurally distinct (T-box) benchmark with human cardiomyocyte ChIP-seq data.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]], TBX5 has 461 PADIT-seq active 10-mers and ChIP-seq peaks significantly enriched for 4 consecutive overlapping active 8-mers, extending the overlapping-binding-sites model beyond the homeodomain/zinc-finger families.
