## Definition

Branched-DNA-amplified single-molecule RNA fluorescence in situ hybridization (Wang et al. 2012; Advanced Cell Diagnostics). Uses 20 paired Z-shaped probes per target with sequential signal amplification, providing single-transcript spatial resolution in FFPE or fresh-frozen tissue. The Multiplex Fluorescent Detection Kit v2 supports up to four channels per slide.

## Relevance to active research

- Standard high-specificity, single-molecule RNA spatial-validation method for tissue-level findings from bulk RNA-seq / spatial transcriptomics.
- Used in [[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]] to validate STAT1-dependent ISG expression (Oas3, Ifit3) at sub-cellular resolution in WT vs Stat1-KO spleen across white pulp (Cd3e+ T cell areas) and red pulp.
- Complementary to lower-resolution 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics; used together to rule out cell-isolation artefacts.
