NRF2 (NFE2L2) is a bZIP transcription factor regulated by KEAP1-mediated proteasomal turnover. Upon oxidative or electrophilic stress (including itaconate-mediated KEAP1 modification), NRF2 stabilizes and drives antioxidant-response-element (ARE) gene programs including HMOX1, NQO1, glutathione synthesis enzymes, and macrophage iron-handling genes. In red pulp and Kupffer macrophages, NRF2 cooperates with SPI-C to induce HO-1, ferroportin, and ferritin for iron recycling.
