## Definition

Phosphatidylserine is an inner-leaflet phospholipid externalized on apoptotic/dying cells, serving as the canonical 'eat-me' signal recognized by efferocytic receptors (TIM, TAM, CD14).

## Intuition

The flag dying cells raise so phagocytes know to eat them.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

PS recognition initiates efferoptosis: annexin V masking blocks death, and PS-coated beads suffice to trigger TNF-dependent macrophage death.
