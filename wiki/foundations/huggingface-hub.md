## Definition

Hugging Face Hub is the dominant open platform for hosting, versioning, and distributing pretrained machine-learning models, datasets, and inference demos. Hosts model weights with Git-LFS-style backing, supports model cards, and exposes a Python `huggingface_hub` client.

## Relevance to active research

Distribution channel for the pretrained Novae weights ([[papers/novae-graph-based-foundation-model-spatial]]), enabling zero-shot inference and fine-tuning by downstream users without retraining the 30M-cell foundation model.
