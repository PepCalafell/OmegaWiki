## Definition

Aiolos (IKZF3) is an Ikaros-family zinc-finger transcription factor in lymphoid cells. AHR cooperates with STAT3 to induce Aiolos in Th17 cells, where it suppresses IL-2 and stabilises the Th17 programme; AHR also induces Aiolos to stabilise Foxp3 in TGFβ-induced Tregs. Aiolos is a clinically validated cereblon-binding immunomodulatory drug substrate (lenalidomide, iberdomide).
