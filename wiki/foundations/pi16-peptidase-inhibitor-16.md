## Definition

PI16 is a secreted peptidase-inhibitor protein that marks the "universal" PI16+ fibroblast — a CD34+/DPP4+/MFAP5+ adventitial fibroblast state found across many human and mouse tissues and proposed to be a precursor/progenitor fibroblast.

## Intuition

PI16+ universal fibroblasts represent a tissue-agnostic baseline fibroblast pool from which more specialized, activated, or myofibroblast states can derive.

## Formal notation

Marker panel: PI16, CD34, DPP4, MFAP5 (universal/adventitial fibroblast signature).

## Key variants

Universal PI16+ vs perivascular/specialized fibroblast states.

## Known limitations

"Precursor" status is largely inferred from cross-tissue conservation and trajectory analysis, not direct lineage tracing in humans.

## Open problems

Whether PI16+ fibroblasts are a true multipotent progenitor in human skin.

## Relevance to active research

Marker of the F2 universal (reticular) skin fibroblast; KLF5 transcription-factor activity is reported to drive this universal Pi16+ state.
