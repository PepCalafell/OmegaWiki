## Definition

B16 (typically B16F10) murine melanoma cell line engineered to express the LCMV-derived peptide GP33–41 as a model neoantigen, enabling tumour control studies with antigen-specific P14 TCR transgenic CD8+ T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- Pre-clinical readout for TF KO–enhanced T cell therapy in Chung et al. 2025.
- Used to test ICB synergy: ZSCAN20 KO and JDP2 KO P14 cells + anti-PD1 mAb produce additive tumour control and survival benefit.
