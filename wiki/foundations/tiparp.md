## Definition

TIPARP (PARP7) is a mono-ADP-ribosyltransferase induced as an AHR target gene that inhibits AHR signalling by ADP-ribosylating AHR and/or co-factors. TIPARP also ADP-ribosylates TBK1, suppressing type I interferon production — a mechanism hijacked by viruses (influenza, HSV-1, coronaviruses) to evade innate antiviral immunity.

## Relevance to active research

Central to the "AHR limits type I IFN" axis discussed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]. PARP7 inhibitors (e.g., RBN-2397) are in clinical development for oncology and viral indications.
