## Definition

Hidradenitis suppurativa is a chronic, recurrent inflammatory disease of apocrine-bearing skin (axillae, groin, perineum) characterised by deep nodules, abscesses, sinus tracts ("tunnels") and scarring, with prominent immune-mesenchymal involvement and tertiary lymphoid structure-like infiltrates.

## Intuition

HS is the prototype skin disease where perivascular and tunnel-associated stromal-immune interplay drives architecture: CCL19+ fibroblasts, B/T aggregates and IL-17/TNF signalling create self-sustaining inflammatory niches that conventional anti-cytokine therapies only partially resolve.

## Key variants

- Hurley stages I-III by severity and scar formation.

## Known limitations

- Mechanistic models are still incomplete; follicular occlusion is the proximal event but downstream dysbiosis, immune amplification and stromal remodeling all contribute.

## Relevance to active research

- One of the five skin diseases mapped against the normal skin neighborhood atlas ([[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]]); HS exhibits HS-tunnel-enriched and TLS-like neighborhoods.
