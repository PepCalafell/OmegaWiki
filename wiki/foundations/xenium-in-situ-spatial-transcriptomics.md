## Definition

Xenium is a 10x Genomics imaging-based, in situ single-cell spatial transcriptomics platform that uses padlock probes and rolling-circle amplification for fluorescence detection of up to ~5,000 transcripts at sub-cellular resolution on FFPE/fresh-frozen tissue.

## Intuition

Unlike sequencing-based [[foundations/10x-visium-spatial-transcriptomics]] (spot-level, deconvolution-dependent), Xenium gives true single-cell spatial readouts at the cost of being targeted (panel-restricted). Predesigned panels (Human 5K Prime, 298-gene lung panel) can be supplemented by custom add-ons (here: 100 genes).

## Key variants

- Pre-designed transcriptome panels (e.g., 5K Prime, 298-gene human lung)
- Custom add-on panels
- Post-Xenium H&E for histopathological registration

## Relevance to active research

Used by Peng et al. 2026 ([[papers/multimodal-spatial-omics-reveal-co-evolution]]) to validate IL1B-high macrophage / IL1R1-high KAC neighborhoods at single-cell resolution across 4.6M and 593K cells in two cohorts.
