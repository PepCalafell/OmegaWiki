## Definition

PLCγ is a phospholipase activated downstream of receptor tyrosine kinases (including TAM/MERTK) and immune receptors, hydrolyzing PIP2 to drive Ca2+/PKC and MAPK signaling.

## Intuition

The enzyme that converts PS-receptor engagement into sustained MAPK and a pro-IL-1β priming signal.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

PLCγ sustains MAPK and pro-Il1b upregulation during efferoptosis; the PLC inhibitor U-73122 abolishes IL-1β without blocking caspase-8/GSDMD cleavage.
