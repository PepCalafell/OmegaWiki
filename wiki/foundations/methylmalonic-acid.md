## Definition

Methylmalonic acid is a dicarboxylic acid metabolite generated by methylmalonyl-CoA mutase deficiency or vitamin B12 insufficiency. Its serum levels rise with age and in mitochondrial dysfunction, and it is a clinical biomarker of B12 status.

## Relevance to active research

- Mouse models show MMA promotes EMT, TGFβ/SOX4 activation, and metastasis in NSCLC.
- Candidate biomarker and intervention target for age-related cancer progression.
