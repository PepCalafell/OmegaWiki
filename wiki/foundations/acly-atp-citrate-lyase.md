## Definition

ATP-citrate lyase converts mitochondrially-exported citrate plus CoA + ATP into cytoplasmic acetyl-CoA and oxaloacetate, providing the substrate for fatty-acid and cholesterol biosynthesis and for protein/histone acetylation.

## Intuition

The cytoplasmic acetyl-CoA factory — controls both lipogenesis and the acetylation pool that gives epigenetic and PI3K-AKT effectors their building blocks.

## Key variants

- Pharmacological inhibitor: bempedoic acid (clinically approved for hypercholesterolemia).
- AKT phosphorylates and activates ACLY in B cells.

## Known limitations

- Substrate channelling vs cytoplasmic pool effects are difficult to disentangle in vivo.

## Open problems

- Whether ACLY targeting can selectively dampen B-cell maturation in autoimmunity without broader metabolic disruption.

## Relevance to active research

B-cell maturation, CD8 IFN-γ output, T-cell memory; bempedoic acid as a non-statin lipid-lowering agent.
