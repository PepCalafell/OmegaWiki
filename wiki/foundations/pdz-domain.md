## Definition

PDZ domains are common ~90-residue protein-interaction modules that bind short
C-terminal peptide motifs (PDZ-binding motifs, PBMs) of partner proteins. They
scaffold multiprotein complexes at membranes and junctions.

## Intuition

A molecular "docking clip" that recognizes the very last few residues of a partner's
C terminus, organizing signalling and adhesion complexes.

## Formal notation

PDZ–PBM affinity is quantified by Kd; many proteins carry multiple (tandem) PDZ
domains with distinct specificities.

## Key variants

Single vs tandem PDZ arrays; PDZ-containing scaffolds (e.g. DLG, MAGI, MPDZ, PATJ,
SHANK, MPP7).

## Known limitations

Short-motif recognition is degenerate, complicating specificity prediction; tandem
arrangements can be required for binding.

## Relevance to active research

The largest group of commensal effector–host interfaces involves bacterial C-terminal
PBMs binding human PDZ domains, a mode validated quantitatively by holdup assay.
