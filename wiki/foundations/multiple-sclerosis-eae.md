## Definition

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a chronic autoimmune disease of the CNS driven by myelin-reactive T cells, causing demyelination, axonal loss, and progressive neurological disability. EAE (experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis) is the most widely used animal model of MS. MS patients show reduced microbiome-derived AHR agonists; multiple AHR agonists suppress EAE through Treg/Tr1 induction and astrocyte/microglia AHR-NF-κB axes.
