## Definition

IKZF4 encodes Eos, a zinc-finger transcription factor of the Ikaros family that partners with FOXP3 and is critical for regulatory T (Treg) cell function and the maintenance of their suppressive program.

## Intuition

By coregulating Treg gene expression with FOXP3, IKZF4 supports immune tolerance; reduced IKZF4 may impair Treg suppressive capacity, with downstream effects on antigen-presenting cells and inflammatory cytokine output.

## Formal notation

Not applicable (gene/protein entity).

## Key variants

- IKZF4 locus variants (e.g. rs34415530) associated with circulating IL-12B and asthma.

## Known limitations

- Broadly expressed across immune cell types, complicating attribution of cell type–specific genetic effects.

## Open problems

- Mechanism by which Treg-specific IKZF4 dosage modulates DC IL-12B secretion.

## Relevance to active research

Headline mechanistic example in [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]]: the rs34415530 T allele reduces IKZF4 expression specifically in CD4 Treg-FOXP3 cells, with pleiotropic effects on circulating IL-12B and asthma risk.
