## Definition

Armstrong strain of lymphocytic choriomeningitis virus cleared within ~8 days by CD8+ T cells, generating memory precursor (MP), terminal effector (TE), TEM, TCM, and tissue-resident memory (TRM) populations. Canonical model for protective memory CD8+ T cell differentiation in C57BL/6 mice.

## Relevance to active research

- Used by Chung et al. 2025 to test TF perturbations for TRM cell formation in the small intestine and circulating TEM/TCM in the spleen.
- Pair with LCMV–Clone 13 to compare protective vs dysfunctional CD8+ T cell programmes under identical antigen specificity (P14).
