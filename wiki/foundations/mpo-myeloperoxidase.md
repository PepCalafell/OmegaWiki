## Definition

MPO is a heme-containing peroxidase stored in azurophilic granules of neutrophils (and to a lesser extent monocytes). It catalyses the formation of hypochlorous acid from hydrogen peroxide and chloride and is a defining marker of mature neutrophils. In tumour contexts, MPO release by tumour-associated neutrophils (TANs) is linked to extracellular-matrix degradation, tumour invasion, and progression.

## Intuition

In imaging mass cytometry / multiplexed-imaging panels, MPO is the canonical neutrophil marker; co-expression with HIF1A at the tumour-niche level marks the hypoxic + neutrophil-rich tumour state described in lung-cancer spatial studies.

## Relevance to active research

Marks tumour cluster C23 (alongside HIF1A) in the IMC LUAD cohort analysed by [[papers/cellcharter-reveals-spatial-cell-niches-associated]] — the cluster invariably surrounding neutrophil-enriched cluster C7 and associated with worse prognosis.
