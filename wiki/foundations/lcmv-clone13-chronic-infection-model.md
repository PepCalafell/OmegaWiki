## Definition

Persistently replicating clone (Clone 13) of lymphocytic choriomeningitis virus that causes chronic viraemia and CD8+ T cell exhaustion in C57BL/6 mice. Canonical model for studying T cell exhaustion (TEXprog → TEXeff → TEXterm) and PD1-axis checkpoint biology.

## Relevance to active research

- Used by Chung et al. 2025 as the chronic-infection backbone for in vivo Perturb-seq screening of TEXterm-driving TFs.
- Standard counterpart to acute LCMV–Armstrong infection, which generates memory CD8+ T cell states (TCM, TEM, TRM, MP, TE).
- TILs and chronic-LCMV TEXterm cells share transcriptional and TF-activity profiles, motivating the cross-context atlas.
