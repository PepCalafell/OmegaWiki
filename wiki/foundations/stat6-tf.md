## Definition

STAT family transcription factor activated by IL-4 and IL-13 via JAK1/JAK2/JAK3. Drives Th2 differentiation, alternative (M2) macrophage polarization, and repression of M1-polarization genes (Czimmerer 2018).

## Relevance to active research

- Master regulator of Th2 / M2 program; therapeutic target in atopic disease.
- Under homeostasis, STAT6 KO increases chromatin accessibility in macrophages (repressive role consistent with M1-enhancer repression) and produces strong epigenome effects ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- TOBIAS footprinting in STAT6-KO macrophages shows enriched NFKB2 motifs at increased-accessibility regions.
