## Definition

Activating mutations in EGFR (most commonly exon 19 deletions and L858R) drive a clinically distinct subset of lung adenocarcinoma. Prevalent in female never-smokers and Asian populations (40–60% in Asian cohorts; 10–15% in non-Asian). Sensitive to EGFR tyrosine-kinase inhibitors (gefitinib, erlotinib, osimertinib).

## Relevance to active research

- Defines an ICI-resistant, immune-excluded TME phenotype.
- Subject to acquired resistance via T790M, C797S, and bypass-track mutations.
- Smoker EGFR-mutant LUAD has even lower TMB and stromal-rich, doubly resistant TME.
