## Definition

Siglec-F is a sialic-acid-binding immunoglobulin-like lectin expressed at high levels on mature murine eosinophils (also on alveolar macrophages and a subset of neutrophils). In skin and blood, CD45⁺ CD11b⁺ Siglec-F⁺ defines eosinophils for flow cytometric and immunohistochemical identification. The human orthologue is Siglec-8.

## Relevance to active research

Siglec-F flow cytometry quantifies stress-induced eosinophil infiltration in MC903-induced AD-like skin in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]; magnetic Siglec-F microbead separation isolates blood eosinophils for bulk RNA-seq and Transwell migration assays.
