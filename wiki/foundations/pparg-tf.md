PPARγ is a nuclear receptor that heterodimerizes with RXR and acts as a master regulator of lipid metabolism, adipocyte differentiation, and macrophage identity. In macrophages it controls fatty-acid oxidation, cholesterol efflux, and tissue-niche identity programs (notably in alveolar macrophages, Kupffer cells, and red pulp macrophages). It is induced in alveolar macrophages by GM-CSF/CSF2 and TGFβ; its loss causes pulmonary alveolar proteinosis through defective surfactant clearance. PPARγ agonists (e.g. thiazolidinediones) and inverse-agonist pharmacology make it a recurrent therapeutic node in metabolic and pulmonary disease.
