## Definition

Fatty acid synthase is the multifunctional homodimer that condenses acetyl-CoA and malonyl-CoA via seven catalytic steps to synthesize palmitate (C16:0) de novo. SREBP1 is the principal transcriptional driver.

## Intuition

The "lipid factory" — turning ACC's malonyl-CoA into actual saturated FA backbone for membranes, signaling lipids, and storage.

## Key variants

- Inhibitors: orlistat, TVB-2640 (denifanstat), C75, cerulenin.
- Regulation by SREBP1 (mTORC1/2-driven) and feedback by palmitate.

## Known limitations

- FASN inhibitors have narrow therapeutic windows due to memory CD8 / neutrophil dependencies.

## Open problems

- Selective FASN inhibition for tumour MHC-I induction without compromising memory CD8 survival.

## Relevance to active research

Cancer metabolism, NAFLD/NASH therapeutics (denifanstat), immune-cell maturation programs.
