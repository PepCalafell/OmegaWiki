## Definition

The inactive AHR is cytosolic and bound to an HSP90 dimer, the co-chaperone p23, the FK506-binding protein homologue AIP/XAP2, and the c-Src kinase. This complex preserves the AHR ligand-binding conformation, blocks nuclear localisation signal exposure, and prevents AHR ubiquitination — ligand binding triggers conformational change, AIP dissociation, and nuclear translocation.
