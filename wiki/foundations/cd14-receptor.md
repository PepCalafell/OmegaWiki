## Definition

CD14 is a myeloid PRR/co-receptor for LPS that also participates in phosphatidylserine recognition and efferocytosis.

## Intuition

An LPS co-receptor that doubles as a PS-recognition helper.

## Formal notation

N/A — biological entity.

## Key variants

N/A

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

CD14 deficiency strongly reduces efferoptosis, supporting PS recognition as the initiating event.
