## Definition

IKBKE (IKKε) is a non-canonical IκB kinase and a paralog of TBK1. Together with TBK1 it phosphorylates IRF3 (and IRF7) downstream of pattern-recognition-receptor signaling, driving type I interferon (IFNβ) induction. It also participates in NF-κB and STAT signaling and has oncogenic roles in some cancers.

## Intuition

IKBKE is the close cousin of TBK1 in the IRF3-activating branch of innate immunity; because the two paralogs share substrates and inhibitor cross-reactivity, distinguishing their individual contributions to interferon induction is experimentally subtle.

## Formal notation

- Pathway: PRR/TLR → TBK1/IKBKE → IRF3 phosphorylation → Ifnb1 induction → type I IFN.
- Shares many small-molecule inhibitors with TBK1 due to active-site similarity.

## Key variants

- TBK1 — the paralogous kinase with overlapping substrate specificity.

## Known limitations

- Functional redundancy with TBK1 complicates loss-of-function interpretation.
- Inhibitor selectivity between IKBKE and TBK1 is often incomplete.

## Open problems

- Context-specific division of labor between IKBKE and TBK1 in interferon responses.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/integrative-epigenome-based-strategy-unbiased-functional]], Momelotinib was used at its kinobeads EC50 for IKBKE; its near-identical epigenomic effect to the TBK1-targeting Midostaurin, together with CETSA showing TBK1 stabilization only at higher Momelotinib concentrations, illustrates how the TBK1/IKBKE paralog pair confounds target attribution.
