## Definition

Small-molecule non-CDN STING agonist binding the cyclic-dinucleotide pocket. Suitable for systemic delivery.

## Intuition

The chemistry that opened the door to systemically delivered STING agonists — non-CDN compounds avoid the hydrolysis instability and poor oral PK of cyclic dinucleotides. Used as the payload in albumin-hitchhiking conjugates and bispecific STING-PD-L1 nanobody-drug platforms.

## Relevance to active research

Backbone of multiple clinical and preclinical agents: GSK3745417, MSA-2, and conjugates with albumin- or PD-L1-directed nanobodies. Active investigation of dosing schedules that preserve T-cell function while still activating tumor and myeloid STING.
