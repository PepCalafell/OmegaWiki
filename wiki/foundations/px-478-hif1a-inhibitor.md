## Definition

PX-478 is a small-molecule inhibitor of HIF-1α with potent in vivo and in vitro antitumor activity. Mechanistically, it reduces HIF-1α protein levels, transactivating activity, and inhibits HIF-1α deubiquitination. It also prevents hypoxia-induced VEGF synthesis across multiple cancer cell lines. In specific contexts it acts via the HIF-1α / lysyl oxidase-like 2 (LOXL2) signaling pathway to enhance immunotherapeutic effectiveness and reduce hypoxia-induced EMT phenotypes. In pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), PX-478 dramatically reduces granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF) and the incidence of perineural invasion (PNI). PX-478 is not yet FDA-approved; clinical development has been slower than for HIF-2α-PAS-B inhibitors.

## Intuition

PX-478 is to HIF-1α what Belzutifan is to HIF-2α: a small-molecule inhibitor of an isoform-specific α-subunit. The mechanisms differ — Belzutifan is a PAS-B allosteric dimerization blocker; PX-478 acts on protein levels and deubiquitination, reducing the pool of stable HIF-1α. PX-478 thus has broader effects on the HIF-1α target gene program, including the hypoxic VEGF axis. Its clinical translation has been slower than the HIF-2α inhibitors, but it remains a key research tool and a candidate drug for HIF-1α-dominant tumors (most non-ccRCC settings).

## Formal notation

Mechanism (Bai 2022 cites):
- Reduces HIF-1α protein levels.
- Reduces HIF-1α transactivating activity.
- Inhibits HIF-1α deubiquitination.
- Blocks hypoxia-induced VEGF synthesis.
- HIF-1α / LOXL2 signaling axis blockade → reduced hypoxia-induced EMT, enhanced immunotherapy.

Cancer-specific effects:
- PDAC: reduces GM-CSF and PNI incidence.
- Multiple solid tumors: direct anti-tumor activity.

Clinical trials:
- NCT00522652 — phase 1 in advanced solid tumors or lymphoma.

## Key variants

- PX-478 is structurally distinct from PAS-B HIF-2α inhibitors (Belzutifan, PT2385).
- Other HIF-1α-targeting strategies: ASOs (RO7070179/EZN-2968), Topo I inhibitors (camptothecin family), digoxin, melatonin (via SPHK1-ROS-HIF-1α axis), MBM-02/Tempol (dual HIF-1/2 antioxidant).

## Known limitations

- Not yet FDA-approved.
- Pharmacokinetics and toxicity profile less optimized than Belzutifan's.
- Clinical efficacy data from phase 1 are limited.
- Newer HIF-1α-targeting strategies (RO7070179 ASO, camptothecin nanoparticle conjugates) may have superior PK/PD.

## Open problems

- Whether PX-478's effect on deubiquitination is mediated by a specific DUB target.
- Synergy with checkpoint blockade in HIF-1α-dominant tumors.
- Comparative efficacy vs HIF-1α-targeting ASOs.

## Relevance to active research

PX-478 is the canonical small-molecule HIF-1α inhibitor used as a research tool to dissect HIF-1α-specific effects. In [[papers/nf-kb-tet2-promote-macrophage-reprogramming]] (Calafell 2024), PX-478 is used to dissect HIF-1α-dependent vs NF-κB-dependent contributions to hypoxic macrophage reprogramming. In [[papers/hypoxia-driven-crosstalk-between-tumor-tumor]] (Bai 2022), PX-478 is positioned as the leading clinical HIF-1α small-molecule candidate, with mechanistic emphasis on HIF-1α/LOXL2 in EMT and on PDAC GM-CSF/PNI reduction.
