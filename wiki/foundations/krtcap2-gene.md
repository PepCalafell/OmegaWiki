## Definition

A small, single-pass membrane protein that accessorises the oligosaccharyltransferase (OST) complex at the ER membrane and contributes to N-linked glycosylation of nascent polypeptides. Originally named for keratinocyte enrichment, KRTCAP2 is now appreciated as widely expressed across epithelial tumours.

## Intuition

Modulating OST throughput indirectly tunes presentation and stability of many cell-surface glycoproteins, giving KRTCAP2 a hub-like position with downstream effects on adhesion, signalling and immune recognition.

## Formal notation

- Encoded at 1q22 in human; small ORF (~125 aa).
- Associates with STT3A/STT3B-containing OST complexes.

## Key variants

- N/A — single annotated isoform of interest in cancer literature to date.

## Known limitations

- Mechanism of action is inferred from OST-complex membership; direct biochemistry on KRTCAP2-specific function is sparse.

## Open problems

- Whether KRTCAP2 loss differentially affects substrate glycosylation in a cancer-relevant way.

## Relevance to active research

KRTCAP2 surfaces as a recurrent pan-cancer prognostic gene in HCC, gastric, uveal melanoma, and PDAC, and is one of 13 hub genes in the [[papers/development-hypoxia-responsive-macrophage-prognostic-model]] hypoxia signature. Its expression correlates with immune exclusion and Treg enrichment across TCGA cancers.
