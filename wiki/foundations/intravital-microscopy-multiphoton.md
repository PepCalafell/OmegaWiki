## Definition
Intravital microscopy uses pulsed near-infrared lasers and non-linear (multiphoton) excitation to image fluorescent reporters within living tissue at cellular or subcellular resolution and millimetre-scale depth. Implanted imaging windows allow continuous longitudinal observation of the same tissue in the same animal over days to weeks.

## Relevance to active research
Allows direct 4D observation of cancer initiation niches, e.g. tracking ERK signalling dynamics in KrasG12D skin stem cells during SCC initiation. Limited by window-implantation depth for deep organs (lung, pancreas).
