## Definition

*GSDMC* encodes gasdermin C, a member of the gasdermin family of pore-forming proteins that execute pyroptosis. Gasdermin-mediated membrane permeabilization is an evolutionarily conserved antimicrobial defense mechanism.

## Intuition

As a pyroptosis effector involved in antimicrobial defense, GSDMC links innate cell death programs to cytokine release, making methylation at GSDMC-proximal CpGs a plausible modulator of trained-immunity cytokine output.

## Formal notation

Gene located near *TMEM75* on chromosome 8.

## Key variants

Gasdermin family paralogs: GSDMA–E and PJVK, with distinct triggers and tissue expression.

## Known limitations

GSDMC's specific role in human trained immunity is inferred from genomic proximity and known antimicrobial function rather than direct assays here.

## Open problems

Whether GSDMC-dependent pyroptosis contributes mechanistically to BCG-trained IFN-γ responses.

## Relevance to active research

A mediating locus (CpG cg25926804 near TMEM75/GSDMC) linking genetic variant rs6991078 to IFN-γ trained immunity.
