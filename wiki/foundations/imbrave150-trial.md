## Definition

A phase III randomised controlled trial (NCT03434379, n=501 enrolled; n=358 in the atezolizumab+bevacizumab arm) of atezolizumab (anti-PD-L1) plus bevacizumab (anti-VEGF) versus sorafenib in unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma. Established the atezo+bev combination as standard-of-care first-line systemic therapy for unresectable HCC.

## Intuition

The first phase III trial demonstrating that combination ICB + anti-angiogenic therapy improves OS over the prior tyrosine-kinase-inhibitor standard (sorafenib) in unresectable HCC. The trial's bulk RNA-seq companion data has become a widely used external cohort for testing HCC ICB-response biomarkers.

## Key variants

- Atezo+bev arm (n=358, bulk RNA-seq companion) — used by Hamon 2025 for TREM2-program OS stratification
- Sorafenib control arm

## Known limitations

- Bulk RNA-seq cannot distinguish cell-type composition from cell-intrinsic expression.
- Patient selection: only unresectable HCC; does not generalise to neoadjuvant or early-stage HCC.

## Open problems

- Optimal biomarker for atezo+bev response remains debated (PD-L1 IHC, TREM2-mac signature, T-cell signatures, VEGF axis).
