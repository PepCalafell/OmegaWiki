## Definition

Serine/threonine kinase recruited to ligand-activated STING at the ERGIC. TBK1 autophosphorylates, phosphorylates STING (creating a docking site for IRF3), then phosphorylates IRF3 to license its nuclear translocation. Functionally redundant with IKKε for STING-induced NF-κB in some myeloid contexts.

## Intuition

Obligate intermediate between STING activation and IRF3-driven type I IFN. Also contributes to non-canonical NF-κB. Mutant p53 binds TBK1 directly and prevents STING-TBK1-IRF3 trimerization — a tumor immune-evasion mechanism.

## Relevance to active research

TBK1 inhibitors block downstream canonical STING signaling. The CIN-driven non-canonical NF-κB output is STING-dependent but TBK1-independent — TBK1 is therefore a potential lever to bias STING signaling toward canonical IRF3 over non-canonical NF-κB.
