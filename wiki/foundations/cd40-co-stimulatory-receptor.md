## Definition

Member of the TNF receptor superfamily expressed on B cells, dendritic cells, macrophages, and endothelial cells. Engagement by CD40L (CD154) or agonistic antibodies licenses APC activation, IL-12 production, and T-cell priming.

## Intuition

Agonistic anti-CD40 antibodies activate TAMs and dendritic cells, driving tumor regression in preclinical models. Clinical example: 2141-V11 (CD40 agonist with enhanced FcγRIIB binding) — 2/12 metastatic solid-tumor patients with complete responses (NCT04059588).

## Relevance to active research

Core myeloid-activator target. FcγR engineering (Fc-silent vs FcγRIIB-enhanced) is an active design axis.
