## Definition

HOXD13 is a homeodomain transcription factor encoded at the 5' end of the HOXD cluster. It binds A/T-rich DNA via a 60-aa homeodomain and is required for distal-limb (digit) patterning, GI tract patterning, and genitalia formation. Mutations cause synpolydactyly (poly-Ala expansions) and brachydactyly. Used in Khetan 2025 as a benchmark homeodomain TF with rich PBM/HT-SELEX/ChIP-seq data and a clinically relevant noncoding variant (rs606231230, pathogenic for preaxial polydactyly).

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]], HOXD13 PADIT-seq detected 1,780 active 8-mers and revealed that ChIP-seq peaks are enriched for 6 consecutive overlapping active 8-mers. The pathogenic SNP rs606231230 (limb-specific HOXD13 enhancer) was shown to create multiple overlapping HOXD13 binding sites simultaneously — illustrating how the overlapping-binding-sites model predicts noncoding-variant effects that PWM/MotifBreakR miss.
