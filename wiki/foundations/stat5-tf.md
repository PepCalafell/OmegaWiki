## Definition

Two highly homologous STAT family transcription factors (STAT5A and STAT5B) activated by IL-2 family cytokines (IL-2, IL-7, IL-15), prolactin, growth hormone, and erythropoietin via JAK kinases. Drives lymphocyte survival/proliferation and lineage commitment. Germline double-KO is lethal; conditional Vav-iCre KO is used for hematopoietic studies. The N642H gain-of-function mutation in STAT5B is a driver of T-cell leukemia/lymphoma.

## Relevance to active research

- Canonical mediator of IL-2 → STAT5 axis in T cells.
- Knockout under homeostasis produces strong transcriptional effects and increased chromatin accessibility in macrophages (repressive role) ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- STAT5BN642H acts as a neomorphic TF with cancer-associated target rewiring rather than simply hyperactive STAT5 (see [[stat5b-n642h-neomorphic-oncogenic-driver]]).
- IRF9 may partner with STAT5 to regulate non-ISG targets under homeostasis (correlated KO transcriptomes in macrophages).
