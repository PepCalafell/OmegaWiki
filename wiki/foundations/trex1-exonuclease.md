## Definition

3′→5′ DNA exonuclease that degrades cytosolic dsDNA, preventing cGAS activation and cGAMP synthesis. ER-tethered TREX1 localizes to micronuclei in chromosomally unstable cells.

## Intuition

The brake on cGAS-STING. TREX1 is induced downstream of STING-IFN signaling itself — an adaptive negative-feedback loop. Tumors upregulate TREX1 to dampen STING-driven antitumor immunity, especially in the context of radiation-induced DNA damage.

## Key variants

TREX1 loss-of-function causes Aicardi-Goutières syndrome (autoimmune cGAS-driven interferonopathy). Cancer cells exploit the opposite extreme — upregulation — for immune evasion. Tumor-intrinsic vs systemic TREX1 inactivation produce qualitatively different therapeutic windows.

## Known limitations

Systemic chronic TREX1 inhibition risks autoimmunity. Pulsatile dosing and combination with tumor-targeted DNA-damaging payloads (radioligand therapy, ADCs) are proposed to widen the therapeutic window.

## Relevance to active research

Re-classified by Cordova, Barbie, and others as an "innate immune checkpoint" — a tumor-restricted brake whose pharmacological release unleashes STING-IFN antitumor immunity. TREX1 inhibitors are in active preclinical development, with the first reports of systemic TREX1 inactivation enhancing T-cell-mediated tumor control while sparing T cells from STING-induced cytotoxicity.
