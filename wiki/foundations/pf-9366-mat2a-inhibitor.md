## Definition

PF-9366 is a selective allosteric inhibitor of methionine adenosyltransferase 2A (MAT2A), the ubiquitous enzyme that synthesizes S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) from methionine and ATP. It binds an allosteric site distinct from the substrate pocket, blocking SAM production and downstream cellular methylation reactions.

## Formal notation

- Target: MAT2A (allosteric site)
- IC50: ~80 nM (Quinlan 2017)
- Typical in vitro concentration: 1-10 μM
- Cellular readout: reduced SAM, reduced histone/DNA methylation
- Selectivity: ~100× selective vs MAT1A
- Used to probe methionine cycle contribution to methylation events

## Variants / related

- Newer MAT2A inhibitors (AG-270 / IDE397) in clinical development for MTAP-deleted cancers
- PF-9366 lacks oral bioavailability; in vitro use only

## Known limitations

- Off-target effects at >10 μM
- Cellular SAM also feeds RNA and protein methylation — DNA effect not isolated
- Methionine cycle recycling can produce SAM-independent methyl pools

## Open problems

- Whether MAT2A inhibition phenocopies DNMT3A KO precisely at specific gene promoters

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]] (Ampomah 2022 *Nat Metab*) as the principal pharmacological tool establishing MAT2A→SAM dependence of AC-induced Ptgs2/Tgfb1 induction. PF-9366 also blocks 13C-mC incorporation when combined with bafilomycin.
