## Definition

Leukocyte immunoglobulin-like receptor B2 (LILRB2 / ILT4 / LIR-2 / CD85d) — inhibitory MHC-class-I receptor expressed on monocytes, macrophages, and dendritic cells. Signals via cytoplasmic ITIMs to recruit SHP-1/2, restraining antigen presentation and pro-inflammatory output.

## Intuition

A "myeloid PD-1": LILRB2 engagement by HLA-G (and certain classical HLA-I) on tumor cells installs a tolerogenic state in TAMs. Clinically targetable: MK-4830 (anti-LILRB2) showed major pathological responses in 11/84 solid-tumor patients (NCT03564691).

## Relevance to active research

Innate immune checkpoint of growing interest as a complement to PD-1 blockade — restrains T-cell priming via the myeloid compartment.
