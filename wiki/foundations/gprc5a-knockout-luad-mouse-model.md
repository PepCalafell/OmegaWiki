## Definition

Gprc5a (retinoic acid-induced 3) knockout mice spontaneously develop lung adenomas and adenocarcinomas with somatic Kras mutations, particularly when exposed to tobacco-derived carcinogens such as [[foundations/nnk-tobacco-carcinogen]].

## Intuition

GPRC5A is a lung lineage-protective gene; its loss enables alveolar epithelium to acquire Kras mutations and progress through KAC-like intermediate states to invasive LUAD. The model uniquely permits study of precancerous niches because precursor lesions arise spontaneously.

## Relevance to active research

Used in Peng et al. 2026 ([[papers/multimodal-spatial-omics-reveal-co-evolution]]) to recapitulate human IL-1β/IL1R1 KAC niches in mouse, validate anti-IL-1β±anti-PD-1 interception, and generate AT2-derived KRT8⁺ organoids via Gprc5a−/−; Krt8CreER; RosatdT/+ lineage tracing.
