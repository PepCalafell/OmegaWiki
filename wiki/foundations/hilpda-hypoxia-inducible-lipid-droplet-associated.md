## Definition

HILPDA (HIG2) is a HIF-target, lipid-droplet–associated protein that promotes neutral-lipid storage, in part by inhibiting adipose triglyceride lipase (ATGL/PNPLA2) and thereby limiting lipolysis. It links hypoxic signaling to intracellular lipid accumulation.

## Intuition

Under hypoxia, HIF induces HILPDA, which protects triglycerides in lipid droplets from breakdown — favoring lipid storage. In hypoxic myeloid cells this contributes to the lipid-laden phenotype.

## Formal notation

- Gene: *HILPDA* / *Hilpda*. HIF-inducible; inhibits ATGL ([[foundations/atgl-adipose-triglyceride-lipase]]).

## Key variants

- Other lipid-storage regulators: PLIN2, PLIN3, SOAT1.

## Known limitations

- Context-dependent effects on lipid metabolism (storage vs. flux) across cell types.

## Open problems

- Whether HILPDA is required (not just induced) for the hypoxic lipid-droplet phenotype in microglia/macrophages.

## Relevance to active research

One of the hypoxia-upregulated lipid-storage genes identified in GAMs in [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]], connecting hypoxic HIF signaling to lipid-droplet accumulation in myeloid cells.
