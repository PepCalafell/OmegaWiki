## Definition

TNIK is a serine/threonine kinase that activates Wnt/β-catenin target-gene transcription and regulates cytoskeletal and cell-adhesion signalling. Its transcript is proline-rich, a feature associated with hypusine-eIF5A dependency for efficient translation.

## Intuition

TNIK links signalling to adhesion: through Wnt/β-catenin it supports macrophage adherence, and β-catenin-deficient macrophages lack adhesion capacity. Reduced TNIK translation therefore plausibly weakens the cell-adhesion program needed for tissue residency.
