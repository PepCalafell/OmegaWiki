## Definition

Muramyl dipeptide (MDP) is the minimal bioactive peptidoglycan motif of bacterial cell
walls and the canonical agonist of the cytosolic receptor NOD2, triggering NF-κB-driven
inflammatory responses.

## Intuition

A conserved bacterial "fragment" that the host senses intracellularly via NOD2 to
mount antibacterial immunity.

## Formal notation

MDP → NOD2 → RIPK2 → NF-κB / MAPK signalling.

## Key variants

Related peptidoglycan fragments sensed by NOD1 (iE-DAP).

## Known limitations

Cytosolic delivery and transporter dependence shape responsiveness; NOD2 variants alter
sensitivity.

## Relevance to active research

"Cellular response to muramyl dipeptide" is among the most enriched functions of human
proteins targeted by commensal T3SS effectors, tying effector activity to the
NOD2/Crohn's disease axis.
