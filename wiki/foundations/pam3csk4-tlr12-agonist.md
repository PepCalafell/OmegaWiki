## Definition

Pam3CSK4 is a synthetic triacylated lipopeptide that activates the Toll-like receptor
1/2 (TLR1/2) heterodimer, mimicking triacylated lipoproteins abundant in Gram-positive
bacteria, and driving NF-κB-dependent inflammatory cytokine production.

## Intuition

A defined "Gram-positive-like" stimulus used to probe how a cell's TLR1/2–NF-κB axis
responds, in contrast to flagellin (TLR5) or LPS (TLR4) stimuli.

## Formal notation

Pam3CSK4 → TLR1/2 → MyD88 → NF-κB; readout via secreted cytokines (e.g. IL-6, IL-8).

## Key variants

Pam2CSK4 (TLR2/6); flagellin (TLR5) and LPS (TLR4) as comparator agonists.

## Known limitations

A synthetic mimic; responses are cell-type and context dependent.

## Relevance to active research

Several commensal effectors potentiated Pam3CSK4-induced IL-6/IL-8 secretion (but not
TNF- or flagellin-induced), suggesting T3SS effectors can modulate host responses to
Gram-positive Bacteroidetes and influence interphyla competition.
