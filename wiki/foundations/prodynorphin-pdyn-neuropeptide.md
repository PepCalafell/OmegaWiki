## Definition

Prodynorphin (Pdyn) is the precursor protein of dynorphin opioid peptides that act through κ-opioid receptors (Oprk1). Beyond its classical CNS role, Pdyn marks a discrete subtype of paravertebral noradrenergic sympathetic neurons (large soma, TH⁺ VIP⁻ Npy⁻) that selectively innervate hairy skin and mediate stress-induced cutaneous inflammation via CCL11–CCR3 chemoattraction of eosinophils and Adrb2-mediated eosinophil activation.

## Relevance to active research

Defines a stress-responsive sympathetic circuit relevant to atopic dermatitis ([[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]); Pdyn-Cre + NetFlpo intersectional labelling exposes the only currently characterised dynorphin-marked peripheral sympathetic subtype with skin-targeted innervation.
