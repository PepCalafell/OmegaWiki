## Definition

GPCR for sphingosine-1-phosphate that controls lymphocyte egress from lymphoid tissues, Th1/Th17 differentiation, neutrophil bone-marrow trafficking, and macrophage M1→M2 polarization. Functionally antagonized by fingolimod (which induces receptor internalization).

## Intuition

The "exit-from-tissue" receptor for lymphocytes.

## Key variants

- S1PR2 — partially overlapping, distinct macrophage / neutrophil roles
- S1PR3-5 — less central; tissue-restricted

## Known limitations

- Receptor functional antagonism (e.g., fingolimod) overlaps with receptor agonism in early dosing windows.

## Open problems

- S1PR1-biased agonists for selective lymphocyte modulation without cardiac side effects.

## Relevance to active research

Multiple-sclerosis therapeutics (fingolimod, ozanimod, ponesimod); IBD; emerging interest in tumour-immune trafficking.
