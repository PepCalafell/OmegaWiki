## Definition

MIBI / imaging mass cytometry uses antibodies labelled with elemental mass tags and time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry to image tens of protein markers simultaneously in fixed tissue sections.

## Key variants

- MIBI-TOF (Ionpath).
- Hyperion imaging mass cytometry (Fluidigm/Standard BioTools).

## Known limitations

- Fixed tissue only.
- Acquisition speed slower than fluorescence-based multiplexing.
- Limited subcellular resolution compared to high-NA fluorescence.

## Relevance to active research

Identified novel breast cancer subgroups via 35-marker FFPE quantification and suggested CXCL13+ exhausted T cells as drivers of TLS formation in immune-infiltrated melanoma; directly applicable to native immune organoid sections.
