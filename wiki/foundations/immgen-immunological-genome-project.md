## Definition
A collaborative consortium that generated standardised, genome-wide expression (and later epigenomic) profiles of essentially all murine immune cell lineages under steady-state and defined activation conditions. ImmGen defined reference "core signatures" for murine tissue macrophages and dendritic cells.

## Intuition
ImmGen is the canonical mouse immune-cell transcriptomic atlas: a common reference against which new datasets are compared, and a source of curated lineage core-signature gene sets.

## Formal notation
- Core macrophage signature genes (Gautier et al. 2012)
- Core DC signature genes (Miller et al. 2012)

## Key variants
- ImmGen microarray phase (Affymetrix)
- ImmGen RNA-seq / ATAC-seq / open-chromatin phases

## Known limitations
- Murine-only; cross-species transfer to human requires ortholog mapping and signature refinement.
- Steady-state emphasis under-represents disease-context activation.

## Open problems
- How conserved murine core signatures are in human macrophages across tissues and activation states.

## Relevance to active research
[[papers/transcriptome-based-network-analysis-reveals-spectrum]] overlaid human macrophage transcriptomes onto ImmGen-defined murine macrophage/DC core signatures to propose a refined, activation-independent cross-species core macrophage signature.
