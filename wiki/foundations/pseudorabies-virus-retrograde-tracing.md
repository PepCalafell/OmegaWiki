## Definition

Pseudorabies virus (PRV) is an α-herpesvirus whose attenuated Bartha strain replicates transsynaptically in the retrograde direction (post- → pre-synaptic). Cre-dependent reporter-expressing PRV variants enable subtype-restricted multi-synaptic tracing from peripheral terminals back to brainstem and forebrain nuclei.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], intradermal injection of Cre-dependent PRV-Bartha into Pdyn^Cre mice traces skin-innervating Pdyn⁺ sympathetic neurons back through spinal cord and brainstem to forebrain stress nuclei (paraventricular nucleus; locus ceruleus), linking the peripheral subtype to central stress-processing circuitry.
