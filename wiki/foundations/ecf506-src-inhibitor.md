## Definition

eCF506 is a highly selective, orally bioavailable inhibitor of SRC-family kinases that stabilizes SRC in its inactive (closed) conformation. Unlike dasatinib or saracatinib it has a narrow kinase profile, making it a clean tool to probe SRC-specific signaling in vivo without broad off-target kinase inhibition.

## Intuition

eCF506 silences SRC's kinase activity specifically. In contexts where SRC phosphorylates a substrate to enable a downstream event, eCF506 removes that phosphorylation, letting researchers test the SRC dependence of a phenotype with high confidence.

## Formal notation

- Target: SRC-family kinases (high selectivity); stabilizes inactive conformation.
- Used in vitro (100 nM) and in vivo (40 mg/kg/day) in [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]].

## Key variants

- Less selective SRC inhibitors: dasatinib, saracatinib (AZD0530), bosutinib.

## Known limitations

- SRC-family redundancy can buffer single-kinase inhibition.

## Open problems

- Clinical translation of selective SRC inhibition for ischemic/hypoxic disease and VHL-mutant cancer.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/mitochondrial-vhl-rewires-cell-metabolism-hypoxia]], eCF506 blocked SRC-mediated VHL Y185 phosphorylation, dismantling the VHL–MCCC2 interaction; co-treatment converted WT kidneys to a mutant-like (BCAT-IN-2-responsive) state in renal ischemia-reperfusion. See [[concepts/src-vhl-y185-phosphorylation-mitochondrial-axis]] and [[foundations/src-kinase]].
