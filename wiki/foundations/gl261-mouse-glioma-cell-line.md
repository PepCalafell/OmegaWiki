## Definition

GL261 is a syngeneic (C57BL/6) mouse glioma cell line broadly used to model glioblastoma in immunocompetent hosts and in vitro. GFP-labeled GL261 enables co-culture and imaging studies of tumor–microglia interactions.

## Intuition

GL261 provides a tractable, immunocompetent glioma model in which tumor–myeloid crosstalk can be studied — in vivo as orthotopic tumors and in vitro as direct co-cultures with microglia/macrophages under defined oxygen conditions.

N/A — cell-line model.

## Key variants

- GL261-GFP (used for FACS separation in co-culture); other glioma models: CT-2A, patient-derived GBM lines.

## Known limitations

- High mutational burden and immunogenicity relative to human GBM; single-line model does not capture GBM heterogeneity.

## Open problems

- Generalizability of GL261-derived hypoxic-crosstalk findings to human glioblastoma.

## Relevance to active research

The glioma partner in the direct microglia–glioma co-culture model of [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]]; GL261 transcriptomes were also profiled to show hypoxic, microglia-dependent induction of pro-migratory programs.
