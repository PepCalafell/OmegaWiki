## Definition

Eosinophil peroxidase (Epx) is a basic, cationic haem-containing peroxidase stored in the secondary granules of eosinophils. Released upon eosinophil activation/degranulation, it generates reactive oxidants (HOBr, HOSCN) that damage parasites, host barrier cells, and recruited inflammatory cells. The Epx promoter is used to drive eosinophil-specific Cre recombinase (Epx^iCre) for conditional ablation (Epx^iCre-DTA), chemogenetic activation (Epx^iCre-hM3Dq), or eosinophil-restricted gene knockout (e.g. Epx^iCre-Adrb2^fl/fl).

## Relevance to active research

Serves both as a serum/tissue biomarker of eosinophil activation in atopic dermatitis severity and as the genetic handle for the eosinophil-specific perturbations central to [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]].
