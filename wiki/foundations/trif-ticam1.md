## Definition

TRIF (TIR-domain-containing adapter-inducing interferon-β) is the MyD88-independent adaptor of TLR4/TLR3 signaling. It can also nucleate a death-inducing complex (the TRIFosome) with RIPK1, ZBP1 and caspase-8.

## Intuition

The TLR adaptor that, besides type-I IFN, can wire receptors to a caspase-8 death platform.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Master switch of efferoptosis: TRIF deficiency protects macrophages in vitro and reduces caspase-8/GSDMD cleavage and lethality in vivo.
