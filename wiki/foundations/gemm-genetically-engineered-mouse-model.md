## Definition
GEMMs are mice harbouring germline or somatically introduced genetic alterations (oncogene activations or tumour-suppressor knockouts) that drive autochthonous tumorigenesis from native tissue cells, typically engineered with Cre-loxP, Dre-rox, or Flp-FRT conditional systems and tetracycline- or tamoxifen-inducible elements.

## Key variants
- Constitutive knockouts
- Conditional alleles (lox-stop-lox, floxed exon)
- Inducible alleles (CreER, rtTA-TRE)
- CRISPR-Cas9 in vivo somatic editing
- Multi-allele compound models (KP = KrasLSL-G12D;Trp53flox)

## Relevance to active research
Considered the gold standard for early-cancer research because tumorigenesis occurs within the native organ microenvironment with intact immune system and stromal architecture.
