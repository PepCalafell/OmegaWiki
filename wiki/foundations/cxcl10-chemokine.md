## Definition

CXCL10 is an IFN-γ-induced CXCR3-ligand chemokine, co-regulated with CXCL9 and CXCL11, that recruits CXCR3⁺ effector/memory T cells and NK cells into inflamed and tumour tissue.

## Intuition

CXCL10 travels with CXCL9 as part of the same IFN-γ readout; together they mark T-cell-engaging, immune-active microenvironments and have been proposed as early biomarkers of ICI response.

## Formal notation

IFN-γ → STAT1 → CXCL10 transcription; CXCL10–CXCR3 signalling drives lymphocyte chemotaxis.

## Key variants

- Co-regulated CXCR3 ligands CXCL9 and CXCL11.

## Known limitations

- As an IFN-γ readout, CXCL10 reflects cytokine exposure rather than a fixed cell identity.

## Open problems

- Separating the predictive value of CXCL10 from co-induced CXCL9 in ICI-response signatures.

## Relevance to active research

CXCL9 and CXCL10 secreted by CXCL9⁺ macrophages interact with ACKR1 on endothelial cells and are required for antitumour immune responses following immune checkpoint blockade.
