## Definition

Salmonella enterica subsp. enterica serovar Typhimurium is a Gram-negative enteric
pathogen and a canonical model organism for T3SS biology, encoding the SPI-1 and SPI-2
secretion systems and well-characterized effectors (e.g. SipA, SopA, SseJ, PipB2).

## Intuition

The textbook "T3SS workhorse": its robustly inducible secretion machinery makes it a
heterologous host for testing whether foreign candidate effectors can be injected.

## Formal notation

ΔsctV (T3SS apparatus knockout) serves as the T3SS-defective negative control;
SipA is a standard reference effector.

## Key variants

SPI-1 vs SPI-2 systems; isogenic secretion-defective mutants.

## Known limitations

A pathogen with its own chaperone repertoire, so heterologous expression of commensal
effectors may lack required cofactors (false negatives).

## Relevance to active research

Used as a heterologous delivery host to demonstrate T3SS-dependent injection of 32
commensal candidate effectors into HeLa cells.
