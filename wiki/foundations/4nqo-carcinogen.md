## Definition
4-nitroquinoline 1-oxide is a water-soluble chemical carcinogen administered in drinking water that induces hyperplasia, dysplasia and squamous cell carcinoma of the tongue, oral mucosa and oesophagus in mice. Its mutational signature mimics tobacco-associated head-and-neck and oesophageal SCC.

## Relevance to active research
Used to model carcinogen-driven oesophageal SCC initiation and to dissect clonal competition with mutant clones in the surrounding normal epithelium.
