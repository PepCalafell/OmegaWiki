## Definition

AHRR is a bHLH-PAS protein induced as an AHR target gene that competes with AHR for binding to ARNT, thereby limiting AHR transcriptional output. AHRR is a clinically interesting DNA-methylation biomarker (e.g., cg05575921 in smoking exposure) and a proposed tumour suppressor: decreased AHRR expression is detected in multiple human cancers.

## Relevance to active research

Negative-feedback regulator of AHR canonical signalling, discussed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]. Also relevant in epigenetic biomarker research (DNA-methylation EPIC arrays).
