## Definition

TMEM119 is a transmembrane protein widely used as a marker of brain-resident, homeostatic microglia that distinguishes them from peripheral monocyte/macrophage-derived myeloid cells. In glioma, TMEM119 marks Mg-GAMs versus Mo/Mφ-GAMs.

## Intuition

TMEM119 expression tracks the homeostatic microglial state and is downregulated upon activation, disease, and (as shown in glioma) hypoxic stress. Its protein-level loss in hypoxic GBM regions is detectable by multiplex imaging.

## Formal notation

- Gene: *TMEM119* (human) / *Tmem119* (mouse).
- Co-regulated with P2RY12 by microglial identity TFs; sensitive to HDAC/H3K27ac status (restored by panobinostat under hypoxia).

## Key variants

- Companion homeostatic markers: P2RY12, CX3CR1, OLFML3.

## Known limitations

- Low-level expression detectable in non-microglial myeloid cells; expression is environmentally modulated, limiting strict lineage specificity under stress.

## Open problems

- Whether TMEM119 loss in hypoxic tumor cores reflects depletion of microglia or reprogramming of resident microglia.

## Relevance to active research

Homeostatic microglial marker whose hypoxia-driven, HDAC-dependent downregulation — reversible by panobinostat — is a core finding of [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]], including protein-level confirmation by CODEX in human GBM.
