## Definition

Type I interferons (multiple IFNα paralogs, IFNβ, IFNε, IFNκ) signal through the heterodimeric IFNAR1/IFNAR2 receptor → JAK1/TYK2 → STAT1/STAT2 → IRF9 (ISGF3) to induce the canonical interferon-stimulated gene (ISG) signature.

## Intuition

In the Immune Dictionary, IFNα1 and IFNβ induce highly correlated, near-universal ISG programmes (GP27, GP33, GP34) across almost every cell type — the canonical "autonomous antiviral programme" — in contrast to IL-1α/β which induce highly cell-type-specific programmes.

## Relevance to active research

Reference signature for ISG response in any cell type. Distinguishes the "universal" cytokine class (type I IFN) from "pleiotropic, context-specific" cytokines (IL-1 family). Underpins ISG II vs ISG I dichotomy in cytokine biology.
