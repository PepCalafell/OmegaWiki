## Definition

Ribonucleotide reductase (RNR) is the rate-limiting enzyme of de novo dNTP synthesis, composed of catalytic RRM1 and oxygen-dependent radical subunit RRM2 (and its hypoxia-induced paralog RRM2B/p53R2). RRM2 contains a diferric-tyrosyl radical that requires O2 for assembly.

## Intuition

Severe-to-moderate hypoxia impairs RNR activity, depleting dNTP pools and stalling replication forks. The resulting replication stress activates ATR–CHK1 and drives γH2AX accumulation without overt DNA damage.

## Relevance to active research

Hypoxia-induced RNR inhibition is the upstream cause of nucleotide shortage that drives both replication stress and the ROS–ATM–MRE11 mutagenic axis (Suvac et al. 2025, Fig. 2).
