## Definition

BRCA2 is a tumor suppressor whose principal role is loading RAD51 onto resected ssDNA at DNA double-strand breaks to initiate homologous recombination repair.

## Intuition

Loss of BRCA2 yields the same HR-deficient phenotype as BRCA1 loss: PARP-inhibitor sensitivity, SBS3 mutational signature, copy-number signature CN17.

## Key variants

- Germline LOF (HBOC syndrome)
- Hypoxia-induced BRCA2 transcriptional and translational suppression — phenocopies HR deficiency without mutation

## Known limitations

Reversion mutations restore HR and confer PARPi resistance.

## Relevance to active research

Suvac, Ashton & Bristow (2025) cite chronic-hypoxia–induced BRCA2 and RAD51 downregulation as a primary driver of the HRD phenotype observed in hypoxic tumours absent germline DDR mutations.
