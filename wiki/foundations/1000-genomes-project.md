## Definition

The 1000 Genomes Project (completed 2015; Nature 526, 68–74) sequenced the genomes of thousands of individuals across diverse populations to build a deep, open reference catalogue of human genetic variation, including rarer variants than HapMap captured.

## Intuition

By profiling variation across many populations at sequence resolution, it expanded and refined the reference SNP/variant map underpinning genome-wide association studies and population genetics.

## Formal notation

Not applicable.

## Key variants

- Successive phases increasing sample size and population diversity.
- Builds on and extends the [[hapmap-project]].

## Known limitations

- Still incomplete coverage of all human populations and the rarest variants.

## Open problems

- Reference panels for under-represented and admixed populations.

## Relevance to active research

Cited in [[wealth-discovery-built-human-genome-project]] alongside HapMap as a cost-slashing variation catalogue that, combined with statistical advances, enabled [[gwas-genome-wide-association-study]].
