## Definition

HuRI is a systematic, proteome-scale map of binary human protein–protein interactions
generated by yeast two-hybrid screening of the human ORFeome, providing a reference
network of direct physical interactions.

## Intuition

A scaffold of the human "interactome wiring diagram," used to place new interactions
in network context and to define interaction neighbourhoods around proteins of interest.

## Formal notation

Network neighbourhoods can be defined by short random walks in HuRI, comparing visit
frequencies against degree-preserved randomly rewired control networks.

## Key variants

Built from the human ORFeome (e.g. ORFeome 9.1); related reference sets hsPRS-v2 and
hsRRS-v2 benchmark assay quality.

## Known limitations

Y2H-based, so context- and modification-dependent interactions can be under-sampled;
incomplete proteome coverage.

## Relevance to active research

Used to define host-protein interaction neighbourhoods and test them for enrichment of
GWAS disease variation targeted by commensal T3SS effectors.
