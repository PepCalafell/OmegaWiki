## Definition

Gasdermin D is the pore-forming executioner of pyroptosis. Cleavage liberates the N-terminal p30 fragment that oligomerizes into plasma-membrane pores, causing lytic death and release of IL-1β and LDH.

## Intuition

The membrane drill of pyroptosis; the p30 fragment is the lytic business end.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Required for efferoptosis (Gsdmd-/- macrophages are protected); p30 puncta mark dying splenic macrophages in vivo.
