## Definition

The Immune Dictionary is an in vivo perturbational single-cell transcriptomic atlas comprising 386,703 cells from mouse skin-draining lymph nodes profiled 4 h after subcutaneous injection of each of 86 carrier-free cytokines (3 biological replicates per cytokine, plus PBS controls), covering >17 immune cell types and >1,400 cytokine × cell-type combinations.

## Intuition

Functions as a reference dataset for cytokine-signature inference (via IREA) and as a hypothesis-generation resource for cytokine biology. Captures pleiotropy at single-cell resolution, distinguishes direct vs secondary cytokine effects, and defines 66 cytokine-driven polarization states.

## Key variants

- Cytokine × cell type DEG signatures (Supplementary Table 3)
- Gene programmes (GPs) of co-expressed genes (Supplementary Table 5)
- 66 cytokine-driven polarization states (Supplementary Tables 6–8)
- Cytokine production map (Supplementary Table 9)
- Cytokine–receptor expression map per cell type

## Relevance to active research

Reference scaffold for any project asking "which cytokines are active in my scRNA-seq dataset?" — applicable to ICB tumour datasets, infection cohorts, autoimmune lesions and developmental immune contexts. Cui & Hacohen 2024.
