## Definition

SNAG-domain zinc-finger transcriptional repressor central to hematopoiesis. Identified by Taiji TF activity analysis as a **multi-state TF active in both TEXterm and TRM** CD8+ T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- Cas9 KO of `Gfi1` in CD8+ T cells reduces TRM frequency and TRM-signature genes in small intestine, similar to PRDM1 KO.
- Highlighted alongside HIC1 as previously unrecognized multi-state regulators of CD8+ TEXterm and TRM differentiation.
