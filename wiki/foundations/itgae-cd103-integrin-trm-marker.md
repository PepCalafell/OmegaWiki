## Definition

ITGAE encodes the integrin αE subunit (CD103), which pairs with β7 to bind E-cadherin on epithelial cells. CD103, together with CD69, is a canonical surface marker of tissue-resident memory T (Trm) cells, anchoring them in epithelial tissues such as skin and gut.

## Intuition

CD103's binding to epithelial E-cadherin physically retains Trm cells at barrier surfaces, distinguishing non-circulating resident memory cells from recirculating central/effector memory subsets.

## Known limitations

- CD103 is not universal to all Trm; CD103-low/negative resident subsets exist (e.g. some CD4+ Trm), so it must be read alongside CD69 and residency transcriptional programs.

## Relevance to active research

A defining marker used to gate and transcriptionally define Trm clusters in skin and tumor scRNA-seq; central to the finding that disease-discriminating signatures of inflammatory skin disease localize to CD69+CD103+ Trm cells.
