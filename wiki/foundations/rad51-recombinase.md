## Definition

RAD51 is the eukaryotic homolog of bacterial RecA. It forms nucleoprotein filaments on resected ssDNA and catalyzes homology search and strand invasion during homologous recombination repair.

## Intuition

Chronic hypoxia transcriptionally and translationally downregulates RAD51 in cell lines and in situ in tumour hypoxic regions, producing a transient HR-deficient state that drives mutator phenotypes.

## Key variants

- RAD51 paralogs (RAD51B/C/D, XRCC2, XRCC3)
- Hypoxia-suppressed RAD51 (transient HR deficiency)

## Relevance to active research

In situ protein studies confirm RAD51 is decreased in hypoxic tumour areas (Suvac et al. 2025), validating the cell-line HR-deficiency model in human tissue.
