BACH1 is a bZIP transcriptional repressor that binds Maf-recognition elements at antioxidant-response and iron-metabolism genes (HMOX1, ferritin, SPI-C), competing with NRF2. Heme binding to BACH1 triggers its nuclear export and degradation, de-repressing the iron-recycling program. BACH1 is thus the heme-sensing switch that couples erythrophagocytosis to macrophage iron-handling gene induction.
