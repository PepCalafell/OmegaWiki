## Definition

A fully human IgG4 monoclonal antibody targeting PD-1 (PDCD1), developed by Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. FDA-approved for advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma, basal cell carcinoma, and NSCLC; off-label and trial use in many other indications including HCC.

## Intuition

A PD-1-blocking checkpoint inhibitor with binding/affinity profile broadly comparable to nivolumab and pembrolizumab; choice of cemiplimab in HCC neoadjuvant trials reflects Regeneron sponsorship rather than mechanistic distinction.

## Key variants

- Standard 350 mg IV every 3 weeks
- Neoadjuvant 2-cycle pre-resection (NCT03916627 HCC trials)
- Combination with SBRT (NCT03916627 Cohort B2)

## Known limitations

- Class-level toxicity profile: immune-related adverse events.
- Cross-trial comparability with other anti-PD-1 agents is imperfect.

## Open problems

- Whether biomarkers validated for cemiplimab (e.g., TREM2 program in HCC) generalise to other anti-PD-1 antibodies.
