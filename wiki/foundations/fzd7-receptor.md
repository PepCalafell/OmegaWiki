## Definition

Fzd7 is a seven-transmembrane receptor of the Frizzled family that binds canonical Wnt ligands (notably Wnt3) to activate β-catenin signaling. Fzd7 is highly expressed on Lgr5+ intestinal stem cells and is the dominant Frizzled mediating the Paneth-cell-derived Wnt3 signal required for crypt-base columnar stem-cell maintenance (Flanagan 2015).

## Known limitations

- Functional redundancy with Fzd5 and other Frizzled receptors.
- Inhibitor specificity is challenging across the Fzd family.

## Relevance to active research

NiCo (Agrawal 2024) identifies Fzd7 as the receptor on stem/TA cells correlating with Paneth Fa1 latent factor, matching the Wnt3 ligand on Paneth progenitors.
