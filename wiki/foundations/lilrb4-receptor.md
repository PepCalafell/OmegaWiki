## Definition

Leukocyte immunoglobulin-like receptor B4 — ITIM-bearing inhibitory receptor on monocytes, macrophages, plasmacytoid DCs, and (notably) monocytic AML blasts. Engages APOE on tumor or apoptotic cells to drive suppressive programs.

## Intuition

LILRB4 is particularly relevant in monocytic AML, where it marks leukemic blasts and supports their immune evasion. IO-202 (anti-LILRB4) showed responses in 6/7 leukemia patients who proceeded to allogeneic HCT (NCT04372433).

## Relevance to active research

Both a checkpoint target on TAMs and a tumor-cell marker in monocytic AML — dual-use innate checkpoint.
