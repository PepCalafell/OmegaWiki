## Definition

ADRA1A encodes the α1A-adrenergic receptor, a G-protein-coupled receptor involved in cell growth and proliferation and in smooth-muscle/vascular signalling. It is a heavily targeted pharmacological protein.

## Intuition

ADRA1A is the canonical example in [[wealth-discovery-built-human-genome-project]] of skewed drug-target attention: it is targeted by 99 distinct approved molecules — about 5% of all approved drugs — despite being the subject of only ~130 publications. The contrast illustrates that drug-target focus, like gene-study focus, is highly uneven.
