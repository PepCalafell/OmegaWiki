## Definition

Panc02 is a murine pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) cell line, derived chemically (methylcholanthrene) from C57BL/6 mice. Orthotopic implantation into syngeneic C57BL/6 hosts induces a mild cachexia phenotype (~3% body-weight loss) with characteristic muscle-atrophy gene-expression changes.

## Intuition

Panc02 represents a milder, slower-onset cachexia model than C26 — closer to indolent human PDAC progression. It complements the more aggressive 8025 PDAC model and the colon-carcinoma C26 in establishing pathway conservation across tumour entities.

## Formal notation

- Host: C57BL/6 (syngeneic).
- Implantation: orthotopic (pancreas).
- Cell suspension: typically in Matrigel for orthotopic protocols.
- Cachexia phenotype: ~3% BW loss; mild induction of muscle atrophy markers.

## Key variants

- Panc02 orthotopic vs subcutaneous.
- KPC mouse models (Kras/p53/Cre genetic PDAC) — distinct biology, separate foundation.

## Known limitations

- Mild cachexia magnitude; less suited for severe-cachexia rescue studies.
- C57BL/6 background means immune microenvironment differs from BALB/c-C26.

## Open problems

- Direct comparison of Panc02 vs 8025 cachexia trajectories (only partially covered in [[papers/multi-omics-profiling-cachexia-targeted-tissues]]).

## Relevance to active research

One of five additional cachexia models in [[papers/multi-omics-profiling-cachexia-targeted-tissues]] (alongside 8025, ApcMin, LLC, KPP) confirming conservation of one-carbon-metabolism activation and muscle glucose hypermetabolism across tumour entities.
