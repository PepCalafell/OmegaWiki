## Definition
HERC5 is a HECT-domain E3 ligase best characterised for catalysing ISG15 conjugation (ISGylation) onto newly translated proteins, particularly in antiviral responses. It also participates in canonical ubiquitin conjugation.

## Intuition
HERC5 couples interferon-induced ISG15 to nascent substrates ribosomally, making it both an innate-immunity effector and, in tumour contexts of elevated expression, a candidate handle for tumour-selective PROTAC-style degradation.

## Formal notation
- Type: HECT-domain E3 ligase
- Primary modification: ISGylation (ISG15 conjugation)
- Induction: type I IFN

## Key variants
- ISGylation activity (main)
- Ubiquitin conjugation activity (context-dependent)

## Known limitations
- ISGylation substrate breadth and specificity remain partly mapped.
- Tissue-selective small-molecule HERC5 recruiters do not exist.

## Open problems
- Demonstrating tumour-selective PROTAC-style degradation via HERC5 in esophageal cancer.

## Relevance to active research
Identified in TPCPA as an E3 ligase highly expressed in esophageal cancer, nominating it as a tumour-selective PROTAC E3 handle to expand the CRBN/VHL-dominated PROTAC toolkit.
