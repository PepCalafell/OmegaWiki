## Definition

SB297006 is a selective small-molecule antagonist of the CCR3 chemokine receptor that blocks ligand-induced (CCL11, CCL24, CCL26) eosinophil chemotaxis at sub-micromolar to low-micromolar concentrations. Used both in vitro (Transwell migration) and systemically in rodent models of allergic inflammation.

## Relevance to active research

Pharmacological proof-of-concept that CCR3 blockade is sufficient to abrogate stress-driven eosinophil recruitment to AD-like skin in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]; supports CCR3 as a candidate therapeutic target for stress-aggravated atopic dermatitis.
