## Definition

CCL11 (eotaxin-1) is a CC-family chemokine that selectively recruits eosinophils (and to a lesser degree basophils and Th2 cells) by engaging the chemokine receptor CCR3. It is constitutively and inducibly produced by epithelial cells, fibroblasts, and — as shown in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]] — by Pdyn⁺ skin-innervating sympathetic neurons during psychological stress.

## Relevance to active research

Primary chemoattractant in the sympathetic-eosinophil axis driving stress-exacerbated dermatitis; pharmacological CCR3 blockade with SB297006 abolishes CCL11-driven eosinophil chemotaxis to activated Pdyn⁺ sympathetic neurons in vitro and reduces stress-induced skin inflammation in vivo.
