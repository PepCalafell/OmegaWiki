## Definition

Zinc-finger and SCAN-domain-containing transcription factor 20. KRAB-less SCAN-domain TF with poorly characterized function. Identified in CD8+ T cell exhaustion atlas as a **TEXterm-selective single-state TF** with no previously known function in T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- Marker / driver of terminal exhaustion (TEXterm) in CD8+ T cells.
- Cas9 KO in mouse and human CD8+ T cells reduces inhibitory receptor expression (LAG3, PD1, TIM3), increases CCR7 stem-like marker, and improves effector cytokine production (IFNγ, TNF).
- Loss of `Zscan20` selectively impairs TEXterm differentiation without disrupting TRM cell formation.
- KO synergizes with anti-PD1 ICB to enhance tumour control in B16-GP33 melanoma.
