## Definition

RIPK1 is a serine/threonine kinase and scaffold central to TNFR1 signaling, controlling survival, apoptosis and necroptosis through kinase-dependent and scaffold (kinase-independent) functions.

## Intuition

A hub whose scaffolding role and kinase role can be separated; here the scaffold matters, not the kinase.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Scaffolding (not kinase) activity supports efferoptosis: Ripk1-D138N kinase-dead macrophages die normally, but RIPK1-deficient cells show delayed death.
