## Definition

ARNT2 is a paralog of ARNT/HIF1β, ~80% identical to ARNT in the N-terminal bHLH and PAS regions and more divergent in the C-terminus. It is dominantly expressed in kidney, central nervous system, and retinal epithelium. ARNT2 dimerises with AHR and HIFα family members but produces only minimal canonical AHR target gene induction.

## Intuition

A "second ARNT" that cells in renal/CNS/retinal tissues use as the dominant bHLH-PAS partner — but which actively *blunts* canonical AHR transcription rather than supporting it, providing a tissue-restricted brake on AHR signalling.

## Formal notation

- Murine Hepa-1 cells: ARNT2 dimerises with AHR equivalently to ARNT under TCDD; outcompetes ARNT when over-expressed; AHR-ARNT2 nuclear complexes are sub-detection threshold; ARNT2 expression reduces TCDD-mediated CYP1A1 induction by ~30%.
- Mouse tissue distribution: kidney, central nervous system, retinal epithelium.

## Key variants

- ARNT2 vs ARNT canonical: shared N-terminus; divergent C-terminus likely accounts for transactivation differences.

## Known limitations

- In-vivo evidence of ARNT2-driven AHR antagonism is mostly indirect.

## Open problems

- Does ARNT2 actively block productive AHR-XRE engagement, or merely sequester AHR in non-productive complexes?
- ARNT2-targeting strategies in renal/CNS tumours.

## Relevance to active research

A neglected node of AHR context specificity. Relevant to renal cell carcinoma, glioblastoma, and any analysis of AHR phenotypes in CNS/kidney. See [[concepts/ahr-arnt-paralogs-and-isoforms]].
