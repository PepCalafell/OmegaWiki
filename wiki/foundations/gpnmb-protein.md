## Definition

GPNMB is a type-I transmembrane glycoprotein expressed by lipid-handling / phagocytic macrophages (TREM2 mac, LAM, DAM), osteoclasts, melanocytes, and several tumor types. Functions include extracellular-matrix adhesion, negative regulation of T-cell activation (via Syndecan-4 binding), and modulation of lysosomal function in lipid-handling phagocytes.

## Intuition

A canonical marker of TREM2⁺ tumor-associated macrophages alongside APOE, SPP1, FABP5. Surface GPNMB has been investigated as an ADC target (glembatumumab vedotin).

## Key variants

- Membrane GPNMB
- Shed soluble GPNMB
- Tumor-cell-intrinsic GPNMB (cell-of-origin distinct from TAM expression)

## Known limitations

- Co-expression with multiple TREM2-mac markers; rarely diagnostic alone.

## Open problems

- Functional contribution of myeloid GPNMB to immunosuppression vs efferocytosis.
