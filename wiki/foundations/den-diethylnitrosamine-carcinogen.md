## Definition
Diethylnitrosamine is an alkylating nitrosamine carcinogen found in tobacco smoke and processed foods that induces hepatocellular carcinoma when injected and oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma when delivered orally. It produces field cancerization with mutational signatures resembling aged human epithelium.

## Relevance to active research
Workhorse carcinogen for modelling clonal competition and stepwise mutational evolution of oesophageal SCC; SCC penetrance scales with sustained DEN exposure and is amplified in p53-mutant backgrounds.
