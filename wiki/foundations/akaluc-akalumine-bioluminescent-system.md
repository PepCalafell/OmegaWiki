## Definition
Akaluc is an engineered firefly-luciferase variant paired with the synthetic substrate AkaLumine that produces near-infrared bioluminescence with markedly improved tissue penetration and brightness over native luciferin systems. Detection limits reach 10–1,000 cells in deep organs (lung, brain).

## Relevance to active research
Used as a knock-in reporter on milestone events (e.g. mutant p53 stabilization) to non-invasively monitor the emergence of rare precancerous cells in vivo without sacrificing the mouse.
