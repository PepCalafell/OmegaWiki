## Definition

TIM3 is a phosphatidylserine-binding receptor of the TIM family expressed on myeloid and T cells, mediating efferocytosis and immune regulation.

## Intuition

A PS receptor that is also a druggable handle on efferocytosis.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Therapeutic target in this work: αTIM3 antibody blocks efferocytosis, reduces splenic caspase-8 cleavage, and improves survival in TNF-induced SIRS.
