## Definition

IFIT1 is an interferon-stimulated gene encoding a tetratricopeptide-repeat protein that restricts viral replication by recognizing aberrant (e.g. non-self capped) RNAs; its expression is a canonical readout of type I interferon signaling.

## Intuition

IFIT1 induction marks cells that have sensed type I interferon, making it a robust marker of an IFN-I-experienced transcriptional state in immune and other cells.

## Formal notation

IFIT1 is downstream of the IFNAR–JAK–STAT–ISGF3 (STAT1/STAT2/IRF9) axis; its promoter carries ISRE elements driving strong induction upon IFN-I stimulation.

## Key variants

- IFIT family members IFIT2, IFIT3, IFIT5 with related antiviral roles.

## Known limitations

- As an ISG, IFIT1 reports interferon exposure rather than a specific functional program.

## Open problems

- Whether IFIT1+ states in tumors are pro- or anti-tumor depends on context (IFN-I duality).

## Relevance to active research

Defines IFIT1+ tumor-associated macrophages — a type I-interferon-induced TAM state distinct from IFN-γ-driven CXCL9+ TAMs — and anchors a cross-lineage IFN-I multicellular module in the tumor microenvironment.
