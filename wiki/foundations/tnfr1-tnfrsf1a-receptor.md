## Definition

TNFR1 is the principal pro-inflammatory/death-signaling receptor for TNF, assembling complex I (survival/NF-κB) and complex II (death).

## Intuition

The receptor that decides between TNF-driven survival and TNF-driven death.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

TNF signaling through TNFR1 is required to switch homeostatic efferocytosis into lethal efferoptosis, including in E. coli sepsis (Tnfr1-/- mice are protected).
