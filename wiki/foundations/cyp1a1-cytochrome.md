## Definition

CYP1A1 is a phase-I xenobiotic-metabolising P450 enzyme, the canonical AHR target gene, that hydroxylates polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons and other AHR ligands. It both detoxifies and bioactivates pro-carcinogens (e.g., benzo[a]pyrene → epoxides). Critically, CYP1A1 catalyses the clearance of endogenous AHR agonists (FICZ, indoles), constituting an AHR negative-feedback loop.

## Intuition

CYP1A1 is both the readout of AHR activity and an off-switch: AHR turns on CYP1A1 which then degrades the AHR ligand. Constitutive intestinal CYP1A1 over-expression depletes endogenous AHR agonists and abolishes intestinal AHR-dependent IL-22 / ILC3 responses.
