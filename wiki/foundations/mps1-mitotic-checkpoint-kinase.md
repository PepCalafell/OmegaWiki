## Definition

Serine/threonine kinase required for the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC). MPS1 phosphorylates kinetochore substrates to license chromosome bi-orientation and prevent mitotic exit until correct attachment is achieved.

## Intuition

Pulsed MPS1 inhibition (BAY 1217389, BAY 1161909, S81694) deliberately induces chromosome missegregation → micronuclei → cytosolic dsDNA → cGAS-STING activation. A "controlled CIN" therapeutic strategy.

## Relevance to active research

Phase 1 combinations with paclitaxel and (proposed) with epigenetic priming or DNA-damaging therapy. Pulsed MPS1 inhibition synergizes with decitabine in KRAS-LKB1 mutant lung cancer to restore STING-IFN-driven immunogenicity (Kitajima 2022, Cancer Cell).
