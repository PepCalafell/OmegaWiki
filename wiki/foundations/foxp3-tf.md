## Definition

FOXP3 is the lineage-defining transcription factor of CD4⁺CD25⁺ regulatory T cells (Tregs). It establishes and maintains the Treg suppressive programme; loss-of-function FOXP3 mutations cause IPEX syndrome. AHR activation in T cells (by TCDD/ITE plus TGFβ) stabilises FOXP3 expression via SMAD1 and Aiolos.

## Relevance to active research

Central to the AHR → Treg axis in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]; AHR is required for full Foxp3⁺ Treg differentiation and for the TIGIT⁺ regulatory Treg subset.
