IRG1 (ACOD1) decarboxylates cis-aconitate to itaconate, linking the TCA cycle to immune signalling. It is sharply induced by LPS and IFN signalling in M1 macrophages, dendritic cells, and microglia, where its product itaconate inhibits SDH, modifies KEAP1 to activate NRF2, and limits inflammation and lung fibrosis. IRG1 loss exacerbates fibrosis and inflammation; itaconate or 4-OI administration is preclinically therapeutic.
