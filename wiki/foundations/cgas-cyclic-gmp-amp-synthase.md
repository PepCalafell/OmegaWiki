## Definition

Cytosolic double-stranded DNA sensor. Upon binding dsDNA, cGAS dimerizes and synthesizes the second messenger 2′3′-cGAMP from ATP and GTP. cGAMP then activates STING.

## Intuition

The "alarm bell" for cytosolic DNA — whether from viral infection, mitochondrial leakage, or micronuclei generated by chromosomal instability. cGAS converts a DNA presence signal into a soluble cyclic dinucleotide that can also be exported to neighboring cells.

## Key variants

Epigenetic silencing via promoter DNA methylation (e.g., IDH1-mutant gliomas) is a common tumor immune-evasion route. Phase separation of cGAS at micronuclei can inhibit TREX1-mediated DNA degradation.

## Relevance to active research

Central node in cGAS-STING tumor immunology. Tumor cell cGAS expression is an emerging biomarker for precision STING-targeted immunotherapy.
