## Definition

NDRG1 (N-myc downstream regulated gene 1) is a stress-response gene with hypoxia-inducible expression (HIF1α-target in many tumour contexts) and pleiotropic roles in cell differentiation, vesicular trafficking, and epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT). In lung cancer it has been implicated in stem-like phenotypes and EMT.

## Intuition

In hypoxic tumour niches NDRG1 is consistently upregulated alongside other HIF-target genes (VEGFA, CA9). Its co-expression with chemokines (S100A8/9, CXCL1/2/3) marks neutrophil-recruiting hypoxic tumour-cell states.

## Known limitations

- Context-dependent: NDRG1 is reported both tumour-suppressive and tumour-promoting across cancers; lung-cancer studies favour pro-stemness / pro-EMT.

## Relevance to active research

NDRG1 marks the C0 hypoxic tumour-cell cluster in the LUAD CosMx dataset analysed by [[papers/cellcharter-reveals-spatial-cell-niches-associated]] and is part of the response-to-hypoxia signature spatially anticorrelated with cell-proliferation signatures in tumour niches surrounding tumour-associated neutrophils.
