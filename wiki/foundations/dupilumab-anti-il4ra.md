## Definition

Dupilumab is a fully human monoclonal antibody targeting IL-4 receptor alpha (IL-4Rα), blocking both IL-4 and IL-13 type-2 cytokine signalling. Approved for atopic dermatitis, asthma, and other allergic conditions.

## Relevance to active research

- First-in-human trial (n=6) added dupilumab to anti-PD-(L)1 in NSCLC, with evidence of reduced immunosuppressive monocytes and increased CD8⁺ infiltration.
- Candidate ICI adjuvant for type-2-skewed, myeloid-driven tumours.
