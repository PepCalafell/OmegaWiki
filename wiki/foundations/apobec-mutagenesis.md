## Definition

APOBEC3A/3B are cytidine deaminases that convert cytosine to uracil on single-stranded DNA. Their activity in cancer produces signatures SBS2 and SBS13 (C>T and C>G at TCN contexts) and is responsible for kataegis foci.

## Intuition

Cycling hypoxia and reoxygenation expose ssDNA at stalled replication forks, providing substrate for APOBEC editing. APOBEC signatures correlate with hypoxia score in human tumours.

## Relevance to active research

APOBEC-mediated mutation frequency correlates with elevated hypoxia signature scores in patient tumours and accumulates in cycling-hypoxia cell-line models (cited by Suvac et al. 2025).
