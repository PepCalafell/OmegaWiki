## Definition

Canakinumab is a high-affinity human monoclonal antibody that neutralizes human [[foundations/il-1-beta-cytokine]], preventing IL-1β engagement of IL-1R1.

## Relevance to active research

The CANTOS atherosclerosis trial reported reduced incident lung cancer with canakinumab. Subsequent NSCLC trials (CANOPY-1/2/A) failed to show benefit when added to first-line/post-progression/adjuvant chemo–ICI combinations. Peng et al. 2026 ([[papers/multimodal-spatial-omics-reveal-co-evolution]]) propose this clinical asymmetry reflects timing — IL-1β blockade is mechanistically active in *precancerous* phases (where IL1B-IL1R1 KAC niches dominate) but ineffective on established invasive LUAD, supported by their NNK-Gprc5a−/− mouse data and lack of effect in a syngeneic LUAD model.
