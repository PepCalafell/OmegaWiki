## Definition

Platelet factor 4 (Pf4 / CXCL4) is a CXC chemokine stored in platelet alpha-granules and expressed strongly by megakaryocytes and platelets. The Pf4 promoter is widely used to drive megakaryocyte-lineage-restricted Cre expression, and Pf4 transcript serves as a canonical megakaryocyte marker in single-cell haematopoiesis.

## Intuition

When Pf4 turns on along a differentiation trajectory, cells are committing to the megakaryocyte/platelet fate — making it a reliable readout of megakaryocyte lineage progression.

## Key variants

- Pf4-Cre transgenic driver for megakaryocyte/platelet-lineage labelling.
- Pf4 as a marker gene in scRNA-seq lineage analyses.

## Known limitations

- Pf4-Cre activity is not perfectly megakaryocyte-exclusive (reported activity in some other haematopoietic cells).

## Open problems

- Precise timing of Pf4 onset relative to megakaryocyte commitment in vivo.

## Relevance to active research

- Recovered as a canonical megakaryocyte regulator/marker by the drift-association test in [[papers/pseudodynamics-reconstructing-population-dynamics-time-resolved]] (Pf4 + Gata1 for the megakaryocyte lineage), and its rise tracks the inferred megakaryocyte-biased early haematopoiesis.
