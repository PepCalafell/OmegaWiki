## Definition

Caspase-8 is an apoptotic initiator cysteine-aspartate protease recruited to death-receptor complexes via FADD. Beyond apoptosis it can directly cleave gasdermin D (GSDMD) and pro-IL-1β, driving lytic, inflammasome-independent pyroptosis when prosurvival brakes (cFLIP) are removed.

## Intuition

The classic 'apoptotic' caspase that, under the right scaffold (TRIFosome) and loss of cFLIP, moonlights as a pyroptosis and IL-1β-maturation enzyme.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Central effector of efferoptosis: its noncleavable D387A mutant abolishes TNF/neutrophil-induced macrophage death and IL-1β maturation in this work.
