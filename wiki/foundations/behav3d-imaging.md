## Definition

BEHAV3D is a live 3D confocal imaging and transcriptomic-phenotyping platform for profiling the dynamic behaviour of immune cells (notably CAR-T cells) in organoid co-cultures. It clusters single-cell behaviours (motility, contact duration, lethality) and integrates them with single-cell transcriptomics to identify functional states.

## Key variants

- BEHAV3D with γδ CAR-T cells on breast cancer organoids.
- Adaptations to other adoptive cell therapies and tumour organoid systems.

## Known limitations

- Requires fluorescently tagged effector cells.
- Computationally intensive trajectory analysis.

## Relevance to active research

Revealed type I interferon signalling as a sensitizer of breast cancer organoids to γδ CAR-T cell killing — a paradigmatic functional readout for immune organoid platforms.
