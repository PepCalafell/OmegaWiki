## Definition

Class A scavenger receptor expressed on subsets of macrophages, including TAMs in many solid tumors. Mediates uptake of polyanionic ligands and tunes macrophage polarization toward an immunosuppressive state in cancer.

## Intuition

MARCO marks an immunosuppressive TAM population whose antibody-mediated blockade reprograms macrophages toward a pro-inflammatory state and reduces tumor growth in preclinical models. Considered an innate immune checkpoint.

## Relevance to active research

Investigational anti-MARCO antibodies in early development as TAM-reprogramming agents.
