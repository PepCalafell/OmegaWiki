## Definition

cFLIP is the catalytically inactive caspase-8 homolog encoded by CFLAR; as a labile prosurvival factor it restrains caspase-8 activation and death.

## Intuition

The brake on caspase-8; when its levels fall, caspase-8 is unleashed.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

TAK1 inhibition during efferocytosis lowers TNF-induced Cflar upregulation, removing the caspase-8 brake and promoting efferoptosis.
