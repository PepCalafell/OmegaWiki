## Definition

Brain-penetrant, dual mutant-IDH1/IDH2 small-molecule inhibitor designed for IDH-mutant glioma. Met its phase 3 endpoint with significant improvement of progression-free survival in residual or recurrent grade 2 mutant-IDH glioma (NCT04164901, INDIGO trial), leading to FDA approval following surgery.

## Intuition

Gliomas are sanctuary tumours behind the BBB; ivosidenib does not penetrate well, but vorasidenib does. The drug lowers 2HG in tumour and restores normal α-KG-dependent biology in glioma cells.

## Formal notation

Allosteric inhibition of both mIDH1 and mIDH2 active sites.

## Key variants

- Vorasidenib (dual mIDH1/2, CNS-penetrant).
- Ivosidenib (mIDH1, peripheral).
- Enasidenib (mIDH2, peripheral).

## Known limitations

- Hepatic enzyme elevations dose-limiting.
- Long-term durability and immune reprogramming effects in glioma still being characterised.

## Open problems

- Combination with anti-PD-1/anti-PD-L1 in IDH-mutant glioma.
- Sequencing relative to surgery and radiotherapy.

## Relevance to active research

- Recent FDA approval for IDH-mutant glioma.
- Proof of principle that BBB-penetrant metabolic inhibitors can deliver clinical benefit.
