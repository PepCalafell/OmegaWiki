## Definition

Clodronate liposomes are a macrophage-depletion reagent: phagocytes engulf the liposomes, release clodronate (a bisphosphonate) intracellularly, and undergo apoptosis. Route of delivery (intratracheal, intraperitoneal, intravenous) determines which macrophage compartment is depleted.

## Intuition

CL provides an acute "reset" of a macrophage niche, after which monocytes are recruited and differentiate to replenish the population — a clean assay for the monocyte-to-resident-macrophage transition and for testing whether replenishment requires a given gene.

## Key variants

- Intratracheal (alveolar macrophages), i.p. (peritoneal/serosal), i.v. (splenic/Kupffer).
- PBS liposomes as control.

## Known limitations

- Depletes all phagocytic cells in reach, not macrophages exclusively.
- Inflammatory effects of dying cells can confound.

## Relevance to active research

Used to show that DHPS-deficient monocytes enter depleted niches but fail to re-establish mature resident macrophages (e.g. Siglec-F+ alveolar macrophages), and that liver homeostasis fails after peritoneal CL in Dhps-ΔM mice.
