## Definition

UDP-GlcNAc is the nucleotide-sugar end product of the hexosamine biosynthetic pathway (HBP) and the obligate sugar donor for [[foundations/glcnacylation]] catalysed by [[foundations/ogt-glcnac-transferase]]. It is also a precursor for N-/O-linked glycosylation and GPI-anchor synthesis.

## Intuition

UDP-GlcNAc sits at a metabolic crossroads: its synthesis consumes glucose (→fructose-6-phosphate), glutamine (amino donor, via [[foundations/gfpt1-gfat1-glutamine-fructose-aminotransferase]], the HBP rate-limiting step), acetyl-CoA, and UTP. Its abundance therefore reflects integrated nutrient status, and changes in UDP-GlcNAc set the ceiling on O-GlcNAcylation flux.

## Formal notation

glucose → F6P → (GFAT1, +glutamine) → GlcN6P → ... → UDP-GlcNAc (donor for OGT).

## Key variants

- Pool size is responsive to glucose and glutamine availability and to flux through GFAT1.

## Known limitations

- Bulk UDP-GlcNAc measurements do not localise where the modification is being deposited.

## Open problems

- Whether UDP-GlcNAc enrichment in alternatively activated macrophages is cause or consequence of their metabolic state.

## Relevance to active research

UDP-GlcNAc is enriched in alternatively activated macrophages (AAMs); [[papers/glcnacylation-shapes-macrophage-tissue-residency-alternative]] builds on this to show its downstream modification (O-GlcNAcylation) is required for IL-4-driven AAM programmes and tissue residency.
