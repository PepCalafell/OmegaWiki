## Definition

Caspase-1 is the canonical inflammasome protease that matures IL-1β and IL-18 and cleaves GSDMD downstream of NLRP3 and related sensors.

## Intuition

The 'official' IL-1β-maturation enzyme — which efferoptosis bypasses.

N/A

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Used as the negative control for canonical inflammasome involvement: Casp1-/- macrophages undergo efferoptosis and IL-1β maturation normally, proving caspase-8 acts directly.
