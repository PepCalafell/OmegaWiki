## Definition

TAK1 is a MAP3K that activates NF-κB and MAPK pathways downstream of innate receptors and is a key survival checkpoint; its inhibition licenses RIPK1/caspase-8-dependent death.

## Intuition

The survival kinase whose loss flips cells from inflammation to death; its MAPK and NF-κB outputs can be uncoupled.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Efferocytosis of neutrophils inhibits TNF-induced TAK1 phosphorylation, collapsing NF-κB/cFLIP survival signaling and licensing efferoptosis.
