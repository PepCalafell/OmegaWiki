## Definition

A cytoplasmic pattern-recognition complex that assembles in response to PAMPs, DAMPs, ROS, and crystalline DAMPs (cholesterol, urate, silica). NLRP3 nucleates with ASC and procaspase-1; activation cleaves caspase-1, processing pro-IL-1β / pro-IL-18 into mature cytokines and triggering pyroptosis via gasdermin D.

## Intuition

The "intracellular alarm" for sterile damage signals; cholesterol crystals are a canonical activator.

## Key variants

- NLRP3 vs NLRC4 vs AIM2 inflammasomes
- MCC950 (canonical small-molecule inhibitor)
- Cholesterol crystal / lipid droplet activation route

## Known limitations

- MCC950 is preclinical; few clinical-stage NLRP3 inhibitors.

## Open problems

- Tissue- and stimulus-specific NLRP3 inhibition.

## Relevance to active research

Atherosclerosis (CANTOS trial, IL-1β), gout, type-2 diabetes, neuroinflammation.
