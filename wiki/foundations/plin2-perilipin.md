## Definition

PLIN2 (perilipin 2, adipophilin/ADRP) is a constitutive lipid-droplet coat protein of the perilipin family that stabilizes lipid droplets and regulates access of lipases to stored neutral lipids. It is a marker and effector of lipid storage in many cell types, including lipid-laden macrophages.

## Intuition

PLIN2 coats lipid droplets and protects their lipid cargo; its upregulation marks a shift toward lipid storage. In hypoxic myeloid cells, increased PLIN2 accompanies lipid-droplet accumulation.

## Formal notation

- Gene: *PLIN2* / *Plin2*. Perilipin family (PLIN1–5).

## Key variants

- PLIN3 (TIP47), PLIN1 (adipocytes); functionally related storage genes HILPDA, SOAT1.

## Known limitations

- Marks lipid droplets broadly; not specific to a particular metabolic program or cell state.

## Open problems

- Causal contribution of PLIN2 (versus correlation) to the hypoxic lipid-laden GAM phenotype.

## Relevance to active research

A hypoxia-upregulated lipid-storage gene in GAMs in [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]], part of the lipid-droplet program reversible by HDAC inhibition.
