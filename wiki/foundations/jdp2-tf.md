## Definition

Jun dimerization protein 2: basic leucine zipper (bZIP) TF of the AP-1 family that heterodimerizes with JUN/ATF and typically represses AP-1 target transcription. Identified as a **TEXterm-selective single-state TF** in CD8+ T cells with no previously known function in T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- Cas9 KO in mouse CD8+ T cells reduces TEXterm differentiation by ~43% in chronic LCMV and increases IFNγ/TNF cytokine production.
- KO in human PBMC-derived CD8+ T cells reduces inhibitory receptors (LAG3, PD1, TIM3) and increases polyfunctional cytokine output.
- KO synergizes with anti-PD1 ICB to reduce B16-GP33 melanoma growth.
- Does not impair TRM cell formation in acute LCMV–Armstrong infection.
