## Definition

Akkermansia muciniphila is a Gram-negative mucin-degrading bacterium of the Verrucomicrobia phylum, abundant in the healthy human gut. It is associated with intestinal barrier integrity, metabolic health, and immune homeostasis.

## Relevance to active research

- Enrichment in stool correlates with improved ICI response across multiple cancers, including NSCLC.
- Candidate live biotherapeutic for ICI augmentation.
