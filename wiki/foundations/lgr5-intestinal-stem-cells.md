## Definition

Lgr5+ intestinal stem cells (crypt-base columnar cells, CBCs) are actively cycling stem cells located between Paneth cells at the bottom of small-intestinal crypts. They self-renew and give rise to all intestinal epithelial lineages (enterocytes, goblet, Paneth, enteroendocrine, tuft) through transient-amplifying (TA) progenitors. Lgr5 is a Wnt-target receptor that potentiates canonical Wnt signaling via R-spondin co-stimulation.

## Intuition

Wnt signaling, especially Wnt3 from neighboring Paneth cells acting through Fzd7, is essential for Lgr5+ stem-cell survival and self-renewal. Markers Olfm4, Lgr5, Hopx co-mark the active stem state. NiCo (Agrawal 2024) detects covariation of stem/TA Fa1 (Olfm4/Lgr5/Hopx) with Paneth Fa1 (progenitor signature), supporting a model where nascent Paneth progenitors signal Wnt3 back to sister stem cells.

## Key markers

- Lgr5 (cardinal), Olfm4, Hopx, Ascl2, Smoc2.

## Known limitations

- Lgr5+ cells are heterogeneous; +4 reserve stem cells (Hopx+, Bmi1+) complement Lgr5+ active stem cells.
- Plasticity allows differentiated cells (e.g. Bhlha15+ secretory progenitors) to revert to Lgr5+ state upon injury.

## Relevance to active research

Reference stem cell population for intestinal regeneration, organoid biology, and colorectal cancer origin (Lgr5+ stem-cell-of-origin model).
