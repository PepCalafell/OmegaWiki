## Definition

IL4I1 is a secreted L-amino-acid oxidase that degrades L-tryptophan to indole-3-pyruvic acid (I3P), which yields the AHR agonists indole-3-aldehyde and kynurenic acid (KynA). Identified by Sadik et al. 2020 Cell as a metabolic immune checkpoint that activates AHR and promotes tumour progression independently of IDO1/IDO2/TDO2.

## Intuition

IL4I1 explains why some tumours show high AHR activity despite normal IDO1/TDO2 expression — they use an entirely different Trp catabolic branch (I3P/KynA rather than Kyn).

## Formal notation

- Substrate: L-Trp (and other aromatic L-amino acids); product: indole-3-pyruvic acid → indole-3-aldehyde, kynurenic acid.
- Highest baseline expression in testis; secreted.
- Pan-cancer: associates with worse prognosis in many tumour types; predicts poor response to ICI in some cohorts.

## Key variants

- Soluble IL4I1 in serum can be measured as a biomarker.

## Known limitations

- IL4I1 inhibitors are early-stage; selectivity over related LAAOs needs work.

## Open problems

- IL4I1-driven AHR-pathway therapies (e.g. combined IL4I1 inhibitor + AHR antagonist).
