## Definition

IL-17A and IL-17F are homodimeric or heterodimeric cytokines secreted by Th17 cells, γδ T cells, ILC3s and other innate lymphocytes; they bind IL-17RA/RC on epithelial and stromal cells to drive neutrophil recruitment, antimicrobial peptide expression, and tissue inflammation. They are the central effectors of plaque psoriasis (target of secukinumab, ixekizumab, brodalumab) and are suppressed by AHR agonists in T cells.

## Relevance to active research

Suppression of IL-17A/IL-17F expression in T cells is a key mechanism by which tapinarof and other AHR agonists treat psoriasis ([[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]).
