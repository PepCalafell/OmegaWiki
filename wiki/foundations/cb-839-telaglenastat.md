## Definition

Selective small-molecule inhibitor of kidney-type glutaminase (GLS1, KGA/GAC isoforms) that blocks the conversion of glutamine to glutamate, depriving cancer cells of anaplerotic TCA-cycle carbon.

## Intuition

CB-839 attacks the glutamine addiction of certain tumours (RCC, TNBC, NSCLC, AML), and its combination with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 has shown preclinical synergy. Clinical translation is mixed because tumours rewire metabolism (e.g., increase glucose uptake) to bypass GLS1 blockade.

## Formal notation

GLS1: glutamine → glutamate + NH3. CB-839 is an allosteric GLS1 inhibitor.

## Key variants

- CB-839 (telaglenastat) — clinical-stage.
- BPTES — early-generation allosteric GLS1 tool compound.

## Known limitations

- Tumour metabolic plasticity drives resistance.
- Off-target effects on immune-cell glutaminolysis (especially M2-like TAMs and Treg).

## Open problems

- Biomarkers identifying GLS1-dependent tumours.
- Optimal sequencing with ICB and dietary interventions.

## Relevance to active research

- Combination with ICB in melanoma, RCC, NSCLC (NCT02771626 nivolumab + CB-839).
- Asymmetric impact on M1 vs M2 macrophage polarisation.
