## Definition

BRCA1 is a tumor suppressor and core scaffold of homologous recombination (HR) repair of DNA double-strand breaks. Germline loss-of-function mutations strongly predispose to breast and ovarian cancer.

## Intuition

BRCA1-deficient cells cannot execute error-free HR and instead rely on error-prone repair pathways (NHEJ, MMEJ), accumulating SBS3 and ID6 mutational signatures. The same phenotype is mimicked transiently by hypoxia, which transcriptionally suppresses BRCA1/2 and RAD51.

## Key variants

- Germline LOF (familial BRCA1/2 syndrome)
- Somatic BRCA1 methylation
- Hypoxia-induced BRCA1 mRNA/protein downregulation (transient HR deficiency phenocopy)

## Known limitations

PARP-inhibitor sensitivity from "BRCAness" can revert via secondary BRCA1 reversion mutations.

## Relevance to active research

Core to the hypoxia-driven HR-deficiency model: chronic hypoxia transcriptionally and translationally suppresses BRCA1 and BRCA2, generating contextual HR deficiency in cells lacking germline mutations.
