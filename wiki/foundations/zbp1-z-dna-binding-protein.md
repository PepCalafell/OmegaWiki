## Definition

ZBP1 is a Z-nucleic-acid sensor that nucleates RIPK1/RIPK3-containing death complexes and can drive PANoptosis/pyroptosis.

## Intuition

A nucleic-acid sensor repurposed as a scaffold that brings RIPK1 and caspase-8 together.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Required for efferoptosis (Zbp1-/- macrophages protected); constitutively bound to RIPK1, binding enhanced by neutrophils+TNF.
