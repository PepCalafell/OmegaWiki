## Definition

Kynurenine is the first stable product of tryptophan catabolism by IDO1, IDO2 and TDO2. It is a low-to-moderate-affinity AHR agonist; in the tumour micro-environment IDO/TDO/IL4I1-expressing macrophages and cancer cells generate Kyn locally to activate AHR in T cells and myeloid cells, dampening anti-tumour immunity.

## Key variants

- Kyn (parent)
- Kynurenic acid (KA), 3-OH-Kyn, 3-OH-anthranilate, quinolinate (downstream pathway)
- Trace-extended aromatic condensation products (TEACOPs) — Kyn-derived, very potent AHR agonists

## Known limitations

- Kyn is a relatively weak AHR ligand on its own; trace condensation products may carry much of the activity attributed to Kyn
- Plasma Kyn levels reflect liver TDO2 as much as immune IDO1, complicating clinical interpretation
