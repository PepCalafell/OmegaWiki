## Definition

Catalytic subunit of Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2). Deposits H3K27me3 marks to repress transcription.

## Intuition

Major driver of tumor cell STING silencing — H3K27me3 deposition on the STING promoter epigenetically extinguishes the gene without mutating it. Combined with KDM5-mediated H3K4me3 removal and DNMT-mediated CpG hypermethylation in glioblastoma, ovarian, TNBC, melanoma, and lung tumors.

## Key variants

EZH1/EZH2 dual inhibitors (valemetostat). Selective EZH2 inhibitors (tazemetostat). Active in clinical trials in combination with pembrolizumab and with deruxtecan-based ADCs.

## Relevance to active research

Pharmacological EZH2 inhibition reinvigorates STING-IFN signaling and restores MHC-I antigen presentation in small-cell lung cancer (SCLC), KRAS-LKB1 lung cancer, melanoma, and TNBC — a "derepression" therapeutic strategy to be combined with STING agonists, DNA-damaging therapy, or ADCs.
