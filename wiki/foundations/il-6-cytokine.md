## Definition

IL-6 is a gp130-family cytokine signalling through the IL-6R/gp130 receptor complex via JAK/STAT3, driving acute-phase response (CRP, SAA), B cell differentiation and trans-signalling in stromal and endothelial cells.

## Intuition

IL-6 is the canonical biomarker of systemic inflammation and a cornerstone of cytokine storm scoring in sepsis and COVID-19. Despite its diagnostic value, in the pairwise-cytokine screen of Takahama et al. 2024 IL-6 alone or in combinations produced relatively limited organ-wide transcriptional change compared with TNF-centered pairs.

## Relevance to active research

Tocilizumab (anti-IL-6R) and siltuximab (anti-IL-6) have defined indications in CRS and inflammatory disease but show narrower efficacy than TNF blockade across organ systems.
