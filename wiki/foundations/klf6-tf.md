## Definition

Krüppel-like factor 6: Cys2-His2 zinc-finger TF with broad roles in differentiation, proliferation, and tumour suppression. Identified as a **TRM-selective single-state TF** in CD8+ T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- KLF6 overexpression in P14 CD8+ T cells transferred into acute LCMV–Armstrong mice produces ~15× enrichment in small intestine and ~42× more CD69+CD103+ TRM-like cells than empty-vector controls.
- KLF6 overexpression does not increase terminal exhaustion in chronic LCMV.
- Candidate enforced-expression TF for adoptive cell therapy "TF recipes" aimed at boosting tumour-resident memory programmes.
