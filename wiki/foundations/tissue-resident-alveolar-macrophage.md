## Definition

Tissue-resident alveolar macrophages (TR-AMs) are the long-lived, self-renewing macrophages of the alveolar lumen, seeded prenatally by fetal-liver monocytes and maintained by local proliferation. They are GM-CSF– and TGF-β–dependent, express the lineage-defining markers SiglecF, CD11c, CD64, Mertk, and PPARγ, and serve as the first phagocytic line against inhaled pathogens and particulates while clearing surfactant.

## Intuition

A specialised, niche-shaped macrophage: living in an O₂-rich, glucose-poor airspace makes oxidative phosphorylation more efficient than glycolysis. They are surfactant-handling, low-inflammatory at baseline, but pivotal in acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) outcomes — loss of TR-AMs correlates with worse influenza/SARS-CoV-2 disease.

## Formal notation

- Ontogeny: fetal-liver monocyte–derived; long-lived in situ self-renewal post-birth.
- Markers: SiglecF⁺ CD11c⁺ CD64⁺ Mertk⁺ F4/80^low PPARγ⁺.
- Niche cytokines: GM-CSF (CSF2), TGF-β.
- Metabolic baseline: very low glycolysis (low HK2, LDHA expression), high mitochondrial OXPHOS; non-glycolytic LPS response (cytokines persist).
- Sensitive to ETC inhibition (rotenone, antimycin A) — high baseline mitochondrial dependence.

## Key variants

- Recruited/monocyte-derived alveolar macrophages (post-injury) — distinct metabolic and inflammatory profile from resident TR-AMs.
- Interstitial lung macrophages — distinct niche, ontogenetically heterogeneous.

## Known limitations

- Hard to obtain in large numbers; bronchoalveolar lavage yields tens of thousands per mouse.
- In vitro culture rapidly drifts from in-vivo identity (GM-CSF supplementation partly mitigates).
- Often confounded with monocyte-derived alveolar macrophages in disease models without fate-mapping.

## Open problems

- Maintenance of TR-AM identity across human lifespan and in chronic disease (COPD, IPF).
- Mechanisms by which the alveolar niche enforces low-glycolytic phenotype on incoming monocytes ([[concepts/macrophage-ontogeny-resident-vs-monocyte-derived]]).

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]] (Woods et al., *Sci. Rep.* 2025), TR-AMs are the foil to BMDMs in dissecting HIF-1α's ontogeny-specific role: HIF-1α is dispensable for TR-AM steady-state metabolism and LPS-driven cytokine production, but essential for FG-4592-induced glycolytic adaptation against ETC inhibitors. This is consistent with prior reports that HIF-1α target genes are silenced during postnatal TR-AM maturation.
