## Definition

A stereoisomeric secondary bile-acid derivative of LCA that promotes Treg differentiation by inducing mitochondrial reactive oxygen species, raising Foxp3 expression, and increasing NR4A1 binding at the Foxp3 locus. Acts independently of the vitamin-D receptor and FXR CNS3 enhancer.

## Intuition

A microbiota-derived natural product that mimics aspects of the in-vivo Treg differentiation program — a small-molecule template for Treg therapeutics.

## Key variants

- Engineered consortia producing isoalloLCA / isoDCA
- Synthetic isoalloLCA analogues (preclinical)

## Known limitations

- Reproducibility at human physiological concentrations is uncertain.

## Open problems

- Receptor identity beyond NR4A1; full pharmacology not mapped.

## Relevance to active research

Gut-Treg engineering, IBD therapeutics, transplantation tolerance.
