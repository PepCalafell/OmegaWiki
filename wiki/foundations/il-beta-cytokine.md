## Definition

IL-1β is the prototypic pro-inflammatory cytokine of the IL-1 family. Synthesized as inactive pro-IL-1β, it is cleaved by caspase-1 within inflammasomes to its mature secreted form, signaling via IL-1R1/IL-1RAcP to activate MyD88–IRAK–NF-κB and MAPK pathways.

## Intuition

In the Immune Dictionary, IL-1β induces distinct gene programmes in almost every immune cell type, exemplifying the coordinated multicellular response paradigm — neutrophils upregulate Cd14, MigDCs upregulate Ccr7, Tregs induce Hif1a/Ctla4.

## Relevance to active research

Central cytokine driving the coordinated multicellular immune response model proposed by Cui & Hacohen 2024. Therapeutically targeted (anakinra, canakinumab) across autoinflammatory, cardiovascular and oncologic indications.
