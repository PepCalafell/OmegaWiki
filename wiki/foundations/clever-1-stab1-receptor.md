## Definition

Common lymphatic endothelial and vascular endothelial receptor 1 (CLEVER-1, encoded by STAB1) — scavenger/adhesion receptor expressed on subsets of TAMs and on lymphatic/sinusoidal endothelium. Restrains antigen presentation and T-cell priming, identifying it as an innate immune checkpoint.

## Intuition

Blocking CLEVER-1 reprograms TAMs toward an antigen-presenting, pro-inflammatory state. Clinical example: bexmarilimab (FP-1305) — 18/138 solid-tumor patients with stable disease and 1 partial response in MATINS phase 1/2 (NCT03733990).

## Relevance to active research

One of the few innate-checkpoint targets to advance into late phase clinical development.
