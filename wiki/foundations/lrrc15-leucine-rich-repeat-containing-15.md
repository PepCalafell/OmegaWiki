## Definition

LRRC15 is a leucine-rich-repeat transmembrane protein expressed on activated/myofibroblastic fibroblasts and myofibroblastic cancer-associated fibroblasts (myCAFs). It is a widely used marker of the activated, TGFβ-driven myofibroblast state and a candidate stromal therapeutic target.

## Intuition

LRRC15 surface expression flags a terminally activated, matrix-producing myofibroblast; its absence in non-inflamed skin and presence in inflamed/scarring stroma makes it a practical protein-level readout of myofibroblast burden.

## Formal notation

TGFβ → SMAD → LRRC15 induction on fibroblast surface.

## Key variants

LRRC15+ myCAF vs other CAF subsets.

## Known limitations

Not entirely fibroblast-specific; expression overlaps multiple activated stromal states.

## Open problems

Whether LRRC15+ myofibroblasts are a fixed endpoint or retain plasticity.

## Relevance to active research

Profiled at the protein level (immunofluorescence) to validate myofibroblast presence in inflamed hidradenitis suppurativa skin but not in non-inflamed or low-scarring-risk skin.
