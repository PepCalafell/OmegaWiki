## Definition

BMDMs are an in-vitro macrophage population obtained by differentiating mouse bone-marrow progenitor cells with M-CSF (CSF-1) or L929-conditioned medium over 6–8 days. They are the de-facto experimental surrogate for monocyte-derived / recruited macrophages and the dominant cell system in macrophage immunology because they are abundant, near-100% pure, and genetically tractable in any mouse line.

## Intuition

The "stem-cell-derived inflammatory macrophage" of the field: glycolysis-prone at baseline, strongly LPS-responsive (TNFα/IL-6/IL-1β), HIF-1α-stabilizing under inflammatory stimulus, and broadly representative of monocyte-derived macrophages that infiltrate inflamed tissue.

## Formal notation

- Source: mouse bone marrow flush (femur/tibia).
- Differentiation: 6–8 days in M-CSF (20–50 ng/mL) or L929 conditioned medium.
- Markers: F4/80⁺, CD11b⁺, MerTK⁺ (post-differentiation).
- Functional baseline: high basal glycolysis, intermediate OXPHOS, robust LPS-induced TNFα/IL-6/IL-1β.

## Key variants

- L929-medium BMDMs vs recombinant M-CSF BMDMs — slight differences in M2 polarisability.
- GM-CSF-derived bone-marrow macrophages (often called "BMDC-like") — distinct phenotype, not equivalent to BMDM.
- Human MDM (monocyte-derived macrophage) — the human analogue, differentiated from PBMC-CD14⁺ monocytes with M-CSF.

## Known limitations

- In vitro only — does not capture tissue-imposed phenotypic shifts.
- M-CSF-driven differentiation imposes a specific metabolic baseline that may not match every in-vivo monocyte-derived macrophage niche.
- Comparison to tissue-resident macrophages (e.g. [[foundations/tissue-resident-alveolar-macrophage]]) requires caution — they differ in ontogeny (definitive haematopoiesis vs yolk-sac/fetal liver) and metabolic baseline.

## Open problems

- How closely BMDMs match in-vivo recruited macrophages in disease-specific niches (tumor, infarct, infection).
- Standardization of differentiation conditions across labs.

## Relevance to active research

The default workhorse in immunometabolism. In [[papers/hif-regulates-mitochondrial-function-bone-marrow]], BMDMs are contrasted with tissue-resident alveolar macrophages to dissect HIF-1α's metabolic role — BMDMs require HIF-1α for baseline glycolysis and inflammatory cytokine production, but HIF-1α loss enhances mitochondrial OXPHOS via Myc derepression. In [[papers/nf-kb-tet2-promote-macrophage-reprogramming]] BMDMs are the system used to study NF-κB/TET2 chromatin co-binding under hypoxia.
