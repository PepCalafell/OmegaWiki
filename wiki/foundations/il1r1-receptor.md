## Definition

IL1R1 is the signaling receptor for [[foundations/il-1-beta-cytokine]] and IL-1α. Together with the co-receptor IL-1RAcP, it triggers MyD88–IRAK–[[foundations/nf-kb-p65-rela]] and MAPK pathways. IL-1Ra binds IL1R1 antagonistically without recruiting IL-1RAcP.

## Intuition

IL1R1 expression — not ligand availability — often limits IL-1β signaling, because IL-1β is broadly present whereas IL1R1 is cell-type-restricted. In LUAD, IL1R1 expression marks KACs and precursor cells specifically, with little expression on normal AT1/AT2 ([[papers/multimodal-spatial-omics-reveal-co-evolution]]).

## Relevance to active research

Genetic Il1r1 knockout in CC-LR (Kras G12D lung epithelium) mice significantly reduces lung tumor development and LAMP3⁺/KRT8⁺ alveolar transformation (Peng et al. 2026), validating IL1R1 as a precancer interception target distinct from established LUAD.
