## Definition

STAT family transcription factor activated by IL-6 family cytokines (IL-6, IL-10, IL-22, OSM, LIF) and other receptors. Mediates acute-phase response, Th17 differentiation, and many oncogenic programs. Germline STAT3 KO is embryonically lethal — typically studied via conditional (e.g. Vav-iCre hematopoietic) knockouts.

## Relevance to active research

- Central node in cytokine-driven inflammation, autoimmunity, and tumour signaling.
- Cooperates with ISGF3 members in macrophage homeostasis: STAT3 KO downregulates a subset of core ISGs ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- IRF9 KO transcriptomes correlate strongly with STAT3 KO macrophages — STAT3 is a candidate non-ISGF3 IRF9 partner.
