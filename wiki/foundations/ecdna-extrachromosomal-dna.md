## Definition
ecDNAs are large circular extrachromosomal DNA elements (often megabase-scale) that carry amplified oncogenes (e.g. MYC, ERBB2, KRAS) and immunomodulatory genes (CIITA, SOCS1). Lacking centromeres, they segregate unevenly during mitosis, accelerating focal copy-number heterogeneity and positive selection.

## Relevance to active research
Detection of ecDNAs in Barrett's oesophagus biopsies is strongly associated with progression to oesophageal adenocarcinoma, marking ecDNA as a candidate driver of precancer-to-cancer transition.
