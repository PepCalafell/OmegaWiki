## Definition

Imiquimod is a synthetic imidazoquinoline TLR7 (and weak TLR8) agonist used clinically for viral skin infections and as the standard preclinical model of psoriasis-like dermatitis when applied topically to mouse skin (van der Fits IMQ model). AHR-deficient mice show exacerbated IMQ-induced skin inflammation; FICZ ameliorates it.
