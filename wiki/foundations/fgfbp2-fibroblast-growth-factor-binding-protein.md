## Definition

FGFBP2 (also KSP37) is a secreted protein expressed by terminally differentiated cytotoxic lymphocytes — effector and effector-memory CD8 T cells and CD56dim NK cells. It is a robust marker of cytotoxic effector differentiation and is co-expressed with granzymes and perforin.

## Intuition

FGFBP2 marks the most differentiated, cytotoxic end of the CD8/NK spectrum; its presence flags circulating terminal effector cells.

## Formal notation

Secreted glycoprotein; used transcriptionally as an effector/effector-memory CD8 and NK marker.

## Key variants

- Co-marker with GZMB, GNLY, CX3CR1 in terminal effector states.

## Known limitations

- Marks differentiation state rather than a specific function.
- Shared across cytotoxic CD8 and NK lineages.

## Open problems

- Functional role of secreted FGFBP2 in inflammation versus its value as a marker.

## Relevance to active research

Identified (with [[gzmb-granzyme]]) as upregulated in effector-memory CD8 T-cell subsets in barrier IMIDs, marking activation of circulating cytotoxic effectors that may precede tissue infiltration; recently described as a marker of CD8 T cells at sites of epithelial damage.
