## Definition

CPT1 catalyzes the rate-limiting transfer of long-chain acyl groups from acyl-CoA onto carnitine at the outer mitochondrial membrane, enabling fatty-acid entry into the mitochondrial matrix for β-oxidation.

## Intuition

The bottleneck of FAO — open it and cells can burn fat; close it and they can't.

## Key variants

- CPT1A (liver / lymphoid / kidney)
- CPT1B (muscle / heart)
- CPT1C (brain)
- Pharmacological inhibitor: etomoxir (with known off-target ANT effects at high dose)

## Known limitations

- Etomoxir off-target activities have complicated CPT1-loss interpretation in immune cells.

## Open problems

- Selective CPT1A inhibitors that preserve other isoforms.

## Relevance to active research

Memory T-cell engineering, atherosclerosis macrophage biology, NAFLD/NASH therapy.
