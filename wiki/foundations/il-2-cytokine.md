## Definition

IL-2 is a 15.5 kDa four-α-helix-bundle cytokine produced mainly by activated CD4⁺ T cells. It signals through trimeric IL-2Rαβγ (high-affinity, on Tregs and activated T cells) or dimeric IL-2Rβγ (intermediate-affinity, on resting CD8 T cells, NK cells, memory T cells). Downstream signalling goes through JAK1/3 → STAT5 to drive proliferation, effector differentiation and survival.

## Intuition

IL-2 is the prototypical T-cell growth factor. Whether it drives effector immunity or tolerance depends on which receptor it engages: IL-2Rα-biased binding expands Tregs and limits effector responses (low-dose IL-2 strategies for autoimmunity); IL-2Rβ-biased binding (engineered "superkines" / no-α variants) preferentially expands CD8⁺ T and NK cells (cancer immunotherapy).

## Key variants

- Recombinant IL-2 (aldesleukin / Proleukin) — FDA-approved for metastatic melanoma / RCC; limited by capillary leak syndrome and severe toxicity
- "Not-α" IL-2 variants and pegylated forms (e.g., NKTR-214/bempegaldesleukin) — biased to IL-2Rβγ
- IL-2 "superkine" (H9 SK, Levin et al. 2012 Nature) — engineered to bind IL-2Rβ with high affinity, decoupling activation from IL-2Rα
- IL-2/anti-IL-2 antibody complexes — receptor-biased delivery
- Antibody-IL-2 immunocytokines (ICKs) — target-restricted delivery (anti-PD-L1-IL-15, anti-TIM3-IL-2, MiTEs, etc.)
- Masked/protease-activated IL-2 prodrugs — receptor-domain or peptide masking cleaved in TME

## Known limitations

- Severe systemic toxicity (cytokine storm, capillary leak, hepatotoxicity) at therapeutic doses
- Treg expansion can paradoxically suppress antitumor immunity
- Short half-life of native IL-2 requires repeated dosing or fusion-protein engineering
- Anti-drug antibody (ADA) formation against engineered variants

## Relevance to active research

Central to a growing class of cytokine-immunotherapy designs (immunocytokines, pro-cytokines, IL-2 muteins, IL-2/anti-IL-2 complexes). Key node in the [[macrophage-targeted-immunocytokine-leverages-myeloid-nk]] MiTE design (TAM-protease-activated IL-2 superkine prodrug).
