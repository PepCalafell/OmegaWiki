## Definition

HMG-CoA reductase catalyzes the conversion of HMG-CoA to mevalonate, the rate-limiting step of cholesterol biosynthesis and the upstream supply of isoprenoid intermediates (FPP, GGPP). Pharmacologically inhibited by statins.

## Intuition

The "thermostat" of cholesterol biosynthesis — statins clamp it down and affect everything downstream including immune-cell prenylation and lipid-raft cholesterol.

## Key variants

- Statins: atorvastatin, simvastatin, lovastatin, rosuvastatin
- Sterol-feedback regulation via SREBP2

## Known limitations

- Statin pleiotropic effects on immune cells (Treg suppression, Th1 enhancement).

## Open problems

- Tissue-selective HMGCR inhibition.

## Relevance to active research

Cardiovascular standard of care; emerging combination strategies with cancer immunotherapy.
