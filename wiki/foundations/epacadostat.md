## Definition

Highly selective oral IDO1 inhibitor developed by Incyte. Phase 1/2 data combined with pembrolizumab in advanced melanoma were promising, but the pivotal phase 3 ECHO-301 trial failed to improve PFS/OS in metastatic melanoma, cooling IDO1-targeted oncology development.

## Intuition

The combination's failure is the canonical cautionary tale for translating immunometabolism: strong mechanism plus clean phase-2 data did not translate to phase 3, likely due to inadequate biomarker selection and pathway redundancy with TDO/IDO2.

## Formal notation

Hydroxyamidine inhibitor of the IDO1 active site.

## Key variants

- Epacadostat (INCB024360).
- BMS-986205 (linrodostat, IDO1).
- Indoximod (D-1MT, broader pathway modulator).

## Known limitations

- Phase 3 ECHO-301 melanoma failure with pembrolizumab.
- No clear pharmacodynamic biomarker translated patient selection.

## Open problems

- Biomarker-stratified resurrection of IDO1 inhibition.
- Combination with non-PD-1 ICB or vaccines.

## Relevance to active research

- Reference case for clinical-translation failure of immunometabolic drugs.
