GATA6 is a zinc-finger transcription factor induced by retinoic acid (from omental milky spots) that serves as the lineage-determining factor of large peritoneal macrophages (LPMs). GATA6 deficiency reduces LPM numbers, drives alternative activation, increases OCR, and lowers aspartoacylase, which links GATA6 to acetyl-CoA generation and lipid synthesis. GATA6 also controls peritoneal wound-healing responses.
