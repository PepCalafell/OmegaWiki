## Definition

CH-223191 is a selective small-molecule AHR antagonist widely used as a research tool to block AHR activation in vitro and in vivo. It is used to dissect AHR-dependent versus independent effects of agonists and is the canonical antagonist control in mechanistic studies.

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] to demonstrate that pharmacologic AHR inhibition aggravates psoriasis-like skin inflammation, suppresses ZIKV and SARS-CoV-2 replication, and upregulates type I IFN responses.
