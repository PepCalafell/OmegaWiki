## Definition

IFNγ is the sole type II interferon, produced predominantly by NK, NKT, CD4 Th1 and CD8 T cells. It signals via IFNGR1/IFNGR2–JAK1/JAK2–STAT1 to induce class II IFN-stimulated genes (ISG II), driving macrophage M1 polarization, MHC-I/II upregulation and antimicrobial defence.

## Intuition

Drives macrophage Mac-b state (Cxcl9/Cxcl10 high, M1-like) in the Immune Dictionary. Required for productive anti-PD-1 ICB response; IREA detects strong IFNγ–Mac-b enrichment in tumours after anti-PD-1 therapy.

## Relevance to active research

Canonical biomarker and effector of ICB response; central to the cytokine network revealed by IREA in anti-PD-1-treated tumours (Cui & Hacohen 2024).
