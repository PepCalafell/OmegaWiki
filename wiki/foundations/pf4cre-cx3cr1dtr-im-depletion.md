## Definition

A dual-allele mouse model that achieves preferential diphtheria-toxin (DT)-inducible ablation of CD206hi (FOLR2⁺) lung interstitial macrophages. Pf4Cre activity is restricted to mature immune cells transcriptionally expressing Pf4 (megakaryocytes, peritoneal macrophages, IMs), but only IMs coexpress Cx3cr1; the Cx3cr1^LSL-DTR allele therefore restricts the diphtheria-toxin receptor to Pf4⁺Cx3cr1⁺ IMs.

## Intuition

Because no single genetic driver cleanly marks one chemokine-defined IM subset, the model exploits the intersection of two lineage markers (Pf4 AND Cx3cr1) so that DT injection deletes the high-Pf4/Cx3cr1 CD206hi IM compartment while sparing CD206lo IMs, recMacs and other cells.

## Key variants

- Cx3cr1^DTR (control, no Pf4Cre) used as the DT-treated comparator.
- Combined with bone-marrow chimera + AgerCreERT2KP autochthonous lung adenocarcinoma to restrict depletion to the hematopoietic compartment under spontaneous tumorigenesis.

## Known limitations

- Depletion is preferential, not absolute, to the CD206hi IM subset and also hits CD206hi IM subsets expressing Ccl6/Ccl8/Ccl9/Ccl24.
- CD206hi IMs are maximally depleted by day 7 post-DT and recover by ~day 15, so the window is transient.
- Does not resolve individual chemokine-defined IM subsets (e.g. Cxcl13⁺ vs Cxcl9⁺) from one another.

## Open problems

- New intersectional or subset-specific drivers (beyond Ccl24Cre) are needed to interrogate single chemokine-defined IM subsets.

## Relevance to active research

In [[chemokine-defined-macrophage-niches-establish-spatial]], this model demonstrated that CD206hi IMs are required for tertiary lymphoid structure formation, lymphocyte recruitment, CXCL9/CXCL10/CXCL13 production and tumor control across melanoma, transplanted and spontaneous lung adenocarcinoma models.
