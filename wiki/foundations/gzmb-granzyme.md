## Definition

GZMB encodes granzyme B, a serine protease stored in the cytotoxic granules of CD8 T cells and NK cells. Delivered into target cells via perforin, it activates caspases and induces apoptosis. GZMB expression is a hallmark of cytotoxic effector and effector-memory lymphocyte states.

## Intuition

Granzyme B is the "payload" of cytotoxic killing: high GZMB marks terminally differentiated, cytotoxically armed effector cells. Its level tracks effector-memory CD8 T-cell and NK activation.

## Formal notation

Aspase (Asp-ase) serine protease cleaving substrates after aspartate residues, activating the caspase cascade and BID.

## Key variants

- Co-expression with perforin, FGFBP2, GNLY in terminal effector CD8/NK states.

## Known limitations

- Expression marks cytotoxic potential, not necessarily active killing.
- Shared across CD8 T and NK lineages, complicating cell-type attribution.

## Open problems

- Whether circulating GZMB-high effector CD8 states predate tissue infiltration in IMIDs.

## Relevance to active research

Identified (with [[fgfbp2-fibroblast-growth-factor-binding-protein]]) as upregulated within specific effector-memory CD8 T-cell subsets (EM CX3CR1, Eff HOBIT, Activated) in barrier IMIDs, strongest in ulcerative colitis — suggesting activation of circulating cytotoxic effectors before tissue infiltration.
