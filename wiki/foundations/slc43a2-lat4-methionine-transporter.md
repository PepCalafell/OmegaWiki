## Definition

Sodium-, chloride-, and pH-independent transporter of L-isomers of neutral branched-chain and aromatic amino acids, prominently methionine, leucine, phenylalanine, and valine. Tumour overexpression depletes TME methionine, impairing T-cell SAM and downstream histone methylation.

## Intuition

T cells need methionine to keep H3K79me2 high, which is required for STAT5 expression and TCR signalling. When tumours overexpress SLC43A2, T cells starve for methionine, lose H3K79me2, and become functionally impaired even in the presence of antigen.

## Formal notation

Sodium-independent facilitated diffusion of neutral L-amino acids (Met > Leu > Phe > Val).

## Key variants

- LAT4 (SLC43A2) — neutral amino acid efflux/facilitated transport.
- LAT3 (SLC43A1) — paralog with overlapping substrate spectrum.

## Known limitations

- No selective small-molecule inhibitor approved.
- Knockout/knockdown studies confined to mouse models.

## Open problems

- Whether systemic methionine supplementation can mimic SLC43A2 blockade without feeding tumour growth.
- Biomarkers of SLC43A2-driven methionine competition in patients.

## Relevance to active research

- Mechanistic link between tumour amino-acid uptake and T-cell epigenome (H3K79me2/DOT1L axis).
- Synergy with ICB in preclinical models when methionine is restored to T cells.
