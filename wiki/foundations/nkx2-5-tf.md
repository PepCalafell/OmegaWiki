## Definition

NKX2.5 is a cardiac-restricted NK-family homeodomain TF essential for heart specification, cardiomyocyte differentiation, and conduction-system maturation. Heterozygous mutations cause congenital heart defects (ASD, AV block). Co-binds with TBX5 and GATA4 at cardiac enhancers. Used in Khetan 2025 as a homeodomain TF paralog comparator (vs HOXD13) with human cardiomyocyte ChIP-seq.

## Relevance to active research

[[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]] reports 1,132 active NKX2.5 10-mers and ChIP-seq peaks significantly enriched for 5 consecutive overlapping active 8-mers — consistent with the overlapping binding sites model in a cardiac-restricted TF.
