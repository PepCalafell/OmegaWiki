## Definition

PADI2 encodes a peptidylarginine deiminase that catalyzes protein citrullination (conversion of arginine to citrulline). Citrullinated proteins are key autoantigens in rheumatoid arthritis (RA), implicating PADI2 in RA pathogenesis.

## Intuition

Elevated PADI2 in myeloid cells increases citrullination, generating neoepitopes that drive anti-citrullinated protein antibody responses characteristic of RA.

## Formal notation

Not applicable (gene/protein entity).

## Key variants

- rs2235922 (chr1:17089967), whose A allele up-regulates PADI2 in classical monocytes.

## Known limitations

- Citrullination is context-dependent; the genetic effect is monocyte-restricted despite broader myeloid expression.

## Open problems

- Causal chain from monocyte PADI2 dosage to synovial autoimmunity.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/chinese-immune-multi-omics-atlas]], the rs2235922 A allele increases PADI2 expression specifically in cMono-CD14, refining a previous whole-blood eQTL to a monocyte-specific effect linked to RA risk.
