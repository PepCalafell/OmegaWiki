## Definition

UCHL3 is a deubiquitylase (and NEDD8 hydrolase) of the UCH family that cleaves ubiquitin from substrate proteins, stabilising them against proteasomal degradation. Ouyang et al. 2020 identified AHR as a UCHL3 substrate in non-small-cell lung cancer stem-like cells, where UCHL3-mediated AHR stabilisation drives cancer stem-cell properties.

## Intuition

Adds a layer of AHR protein stability control distinct from ARNT-dependent canonical ubiquitination and TIPARP-driven ADP-ribosylation: UCHL3 reverses the degradation signal.

## Formal notation

- Substrates: ubiquitin-conjugated proteins (broad), with AHR identified as a cancer-relevant substrate.
- NSCLC: UCHL3 knockdown destabilises AHR and reduces self-renewal markers.

## Key variants

- UCHL3 vs UCHL1: distinct substrate preferences and tissue distribution.

## Known limitations

- UCHL3 inhibitors are early-stage.

## Open problems

- UCHL3 selectivity for AHR vs other substrates in NSCLC?
- Pan-cancer relevance of UCHL3-driven AHR stabilisation.

## Relevance to active research

Cancer stem cell biology; AHR-pathway therapeutics. See [[claims/uchl3-deubiquitinates-stabilises-ahr-nsclc-stemness]].
