## Definition

Psoriasis is a chronic, immune-mediated skin disease characterised by hyperproliferation of keratinocytes and infiltration of neutrophils and Th17 cells, driven by the IL-23 / IL-17 axis. The most common form, plaque psoriasis, presents as well-demarcated erythematous scaly plaques. Tapinarof cream became the first FDA-approved AHR-targeting therapy for adult plaque psoriasis (2022).
