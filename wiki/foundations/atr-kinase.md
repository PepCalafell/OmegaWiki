## Definition

ATR is the master kinase of the replication-stress response. Activated by RPA-coated ssDNA at stalled forks, it phosphorylates CHK1 to enforce S/G2 arrest and stabilize replication forks.

## Intuition

Hypoxia activates ATR–CHK1 signaling and induces γH2AX in the absence of overt DNA damage. ATR/CHK1 loss under hypoxia precipitates apoptosis on reoxygenation, indicating ATR protects hypoxic cells.

## Relevance to active research

Hypoxia-induced ATR-dependent CHK1 phosphorylation underlies the S-phase arrest seen in chronically hypoxic cells (Suvac et al. 2025).
