## Definition

KLF4 is a zinc-finger transcription factor with context-dependent pro- and anti-inflammatory roles, also one of the four Yamanaka reprogramming factors. In tumour-associated macrophages, AHR drives KLF4 expression, which suppresses NF-κB and induces CD39, contributing to immunosuppression in glioblastoma. KLF4 is also a major Tr1 cell co-factor downstream of AHR.
