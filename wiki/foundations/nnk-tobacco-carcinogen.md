## Definition

NNK is a tobacco-specific nitrosamine that is metabolically activated to DNA-alkylating species, causing G→A transitions and Kras mutations preferentially in lung alveolar epithelium.

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[foundations/gprc5a-knockout-luad-mouse-model]] to accelerate lung adenoma/LUAD development for studying early precursor niches; Peng et al. 2026 ([[papers/multimodal-spatial-omics-reveal-co-evolution]]) use exposure followed by analysis at end-of-exposure (EOE), 3 months, and 7 months to capture precursor and invasive stages.
