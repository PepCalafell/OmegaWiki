Itaconate is an unsaturated dicarboxylic acid produced from cis-aconitate by the enzyme IRG1/ACOD1 during M1 macrophage activation. It is among the most highly upregulated metabolites in inflammatory macrophages and acts as a non-competitive inhibitor of succinate dehydrogenase (SDH; complex II), producing the second TCA-cycle break and succinate accumulation that stabilizes HIF-1α. Itaconate also electrophilically modifies KEAP1 cysteines to activate NRF2, modulates cytokine output independently of SDH, and is anti-fibrotic in lung-fibrosis models. Cell-permeable derivatives (4-octyl itaconate, dimethyl itaconate) enable pharmacological use; see [[foundations/4-octyl-itaconate-tet2-inhibitor]].
