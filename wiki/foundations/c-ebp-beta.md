C/EBPβ is a bZIP transcription factor that controls lipid-related and inflammatory genes in macrophages and adipocytes. In large peritoneal and alveolar macrophages it is required for differentiation, survival, and tissue identity; C/EBPβ loss derepresses LXRα/PPARγ programs and disrupts lipid handling. It also participates in acute-phase responses and IL-6/STAT3-driven inflammation.
