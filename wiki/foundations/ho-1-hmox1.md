HO-1 (HMOX1) is the rate-limiting enzyme of heme degradation, producing biliverdin, free iron (Fe²⁺), and carbon monoxide using O₂ and NADPH. Strongly induced by NRF2/SPI-C in red pulp and Kupffer macrophages, HO-1 is essential for erythrocyte-iron recycling. HMOX1 loss-of-function collapses macrophage populations in spleen, liver, and bone marrow and causes hemolytic anemia.
