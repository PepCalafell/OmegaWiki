## Definition

STAT family transcription factor activated downstream of type-I and type-III interferon receptors. Forms the ISGF3 heterotrimer (STAT1-STAT2-IRF9) binding ISRE elements to drive ISG transcription. Can also form STAT2-IRF9 heterodimers independent of STAT1 (Platanitis 2019).

## Relevance to active research

- Major effector of type-I IFN signaling and antiviral defense (Park 2000).
- Together with IRF9, identified as a keystone of **baseline / tonic JAK-STAT signaling** in homeostatic immune cells — STAT2 KO produces some of the strongest transcriptional effects on unstimulated mouse spleen cells ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- STAT2 KO transcriptomes only weakly correlate with IRF9 KO, suggesting STAT2 and IRF9 partially diverge in their homeostatic target gene sets.
