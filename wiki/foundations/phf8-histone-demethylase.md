## Definition

A Jumonji-C, PHD-finger histone demethylase (KDM7 family) that removes H3K9me1/me2 and H4K20me1, acting as a transcriptional activator. Like PHF2 it is α-ketoglutarate- and Fe(II)-dependent.

## Intuition

PHF8 supports genome stability through the DNA-damage response: it promotes TOPBP1 demethylation that drives ATR activation, preserving genomic integrity. It interacts with DDR proteins and shows possible oncogenic properties in some settings.

## Key variants

Implicated as oncogenic in several cancers, yet shares with PHF2 a genome-stabilizing function — so reducing it can paradoxically increase DNA damage and innate sensing.

## Known limitations

Functional redundancy/partnership with PHF2 means single-gene perturbations give inconsistent phenotypes; combined modulation is more informative.

## Relevance to active research

Together with PHF2, a candidate target whose knockdown increases cytosolic DNA, IFNB1 induction, and cell death via cGAS — a potential adjuvant strategy for tumor immunotherapy.
