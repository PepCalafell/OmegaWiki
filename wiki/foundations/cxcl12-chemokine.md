## Definition

CXCL12 is a CXC-class chemokine constitutively expressed by stromal cells (perivascular fibroblasts, mesenchymal stromal cells, FRCs) that signals through CXCR4 (and ACKR3/CXCR7) to retain naive lymphocytes, hematopoietic stem cells, plasma cells and bone-marrow progenitors in stromal niches.

## Intuition

CXCL12 is the canonical "stay here" signal of stromal niches: bone-marrow HSC retention, lymph-node T-zone residence, perivascular skin residence, and tumour CAF-mediated immune-cell trapping all share the CXCL12-CXCR4 axis.

## Key variants

- CXCL12 isoforms α/β/γ from alternative splicing.

## Known limitations

- ACKR3/CXCR7 acts as a scavenging receptor and modulates effective CXCL12 gradients.

## Relevance to active research

- Top stromal-to-immune L-R pair in PERIVASC I skin neighborhood ([[papers/single-cell-spatial-transcriptomic-analysis-human]]).
- CXCL12-CXCR4 inhibition (plerixafor, balixafortide) explored across solid tumours and HSC mobilisation.
