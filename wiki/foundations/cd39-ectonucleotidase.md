## Definition

CD39 is a cell-surface ectoenzyme that hydrolyses extracellular ATP (eATP) → AMP, which is further degraded by CD73 → adenosine. The CD39–CD73 axis converts pro-inflammatory eATP into immunosuppressive adenosine and is a defining feature of Tregs, Tr1 cells, and AHR-activated tumour-associated macrophages.

## Relevance to active research

AHR-driven CD39 expression on T cells and TAMs is a major immunosuppressive effector arm reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] (Tr1 cell function, glioblastoma TAM immunosuppression via KLF4 → CD39).
