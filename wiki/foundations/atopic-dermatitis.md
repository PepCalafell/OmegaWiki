## Definition

Atopic dermatitis is a chronic, relapsing inflammatory skin disease characterised by epidermal barrier defects (filaggrin, loricrin, involucrin, hornerin loss), Th2 inflammation (IL-4, IL-13, IL-31), and microbial dysbiosis. Tapinarof cream demonstrated efficacy in phase III ADORING 1/2/3 trials; an sNDA for atopic dermatitis was submitted to the FDA in 2024.

## Relevance to active research

A primary clinical indication being developed for AHR agonists ([[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]]).
