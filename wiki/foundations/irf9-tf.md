## Definition

IRF family transcription factor that completes the ISGF3 heterotrimer (STAT1-STAT2-IRF9) binding ISRE elements to drive ISG transcription downstream of type-I IFN. Can also form STAT2-IRF9 dimers and other non-canonical complexes.

## Relevance to active research

- Required for type-I IFN antiviral responses (Kimura 1996); KO mice are viral-susceptible.
- A keystone of baseline JAK-STAT signaling under homeostasis: IRF9 KO produces some of the strongest transcriptional effects in unstimulated mouse spleen ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- Regulates targets independent of ISGF3 (Rdh14, Tprkb, Usb1) and may partner with STAT3/STAT5 in macrophages (see [[isgf3-independent-irf9-function]]).
