## Definition

IL11 is an IL-6-family cytokine signaling through the IL11RA/gp130 receptor complex and the JAK-STAT3 pathway. It is increasingly recognized as a pro-fibrotic and stromal-activation cytokine produced by activated fibroblasts.

## Intuition

IL11 marks a fibroblast that has shifted toward an inflammatory, tissue-remodeling program; autocrine/paracrine IL11 signaling reinforces myofibroblast activation and fibrosis.

## Formal notation

IL11 → IL11RA + gp130 → JAK1/2 → STAT3 transcriptional program.

## Key variants

IL11 vs IL6 (shared gp130 but distinct receptor alpha chains and tissue effects).

## Known limitations

Roles are context-dependent (regenerative vs fibrotic); therapeutic targeting is still emerging.

## Open problems

Whether IL11 is a driver or a marker of the inflammatory myofibroblast state in human skin.

## Relevance to active research

A defining marker of the F6 inflammatory myofibroblast (IL11+MMP1+CXCL8+IL7R+) identified across human skin wounds, inflammatory disease and cancer.
