## Definition

IL-10 is the prototypical anti-inflammatory cytokine produced by Tr1 cells, Tregs, B regulatory cells, and macrophages; it suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokine production by antigen-presenting cells and dampens T effector responses. AHR cooperates with c-Maf to drive IL-10 expression in Tr1 cells and induces IL-10 in tolerogenic DCs.
