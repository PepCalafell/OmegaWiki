## Definition
Red2Onco is a multicolour Cre-dependent reporter mouse line that stochastically marks single epithelial clones with distinct fluorescent proteins while coupling colour to oncogenic-driver expression. It enables side-by-side analysis of mutant and wild-type clones within the same intestine to assess clonal competition.

## Relevance to active research
Showed that oncogenic intestinal clones disrupt homeostasis of neighbouring wild-type tissue through paracrine signalling, fueling field cancerization. Related multicolour systems (Crainbow variants, Confetti) are used analogously across organs.
