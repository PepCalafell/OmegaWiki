## Definition

CCR3 is a Gi-coupled chemokine receptor expressed on eosinophils, basophils, mast cells, and Th2 cells. Its principal ligands include CCL11 (eotaxin-1), CCL24 (eotaxin-2), CCL26 (eotaxin-3), CCL5 (RANTES) and CCL13. CCR3 signalling mediates eosinophil chemotaxis to allergic and atopic tissue.

## Relevance to active research

Selective small-molecule CCR3 antagonism (SB297006) and anti-CCR3 monoclonal antibodies block stress-induced eosinophil recruitment to inflamed skin in murine atopic dermatitis ([[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]). Candidate therapeutic target for stress-aggravated AD.
