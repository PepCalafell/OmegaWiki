## Definition

Histone deacetylases remove acetyl groups from lysines on histones and on many non-histone substrates (NF-κB p65, p70-S6K, Foxp3, p53), repressing transcription and modulating signaling. Inhibited by short-chain fatty acids (butyrate, propionate) and by drugs (vorinostat, romidepsin).

## Intuition

A "transcriptional volume knob" — HDAC inhibition raises acetylation broadly, often shifting cells toward activated/anti-inflammatory states in immune contexts.

## Key variants

- Class I: HDAC1, 2, 3, 8 (nuclear, broad gene repression)
- Class IIa: HDAC4, 5, 7, 9 (tissue-restricted; nucleocytoplasmic shuttling)
- Class IIb: HDAC6, 10 (cytoplasmic, tubulin/Hsp90 substrates)
- Class IV: HDAC11

## Known limitations

- Pan-HDAC inhibitors are pleiotropic — clinical use limited to specific cancers.

## Open problems

- Isoform-selective HDAC inhibitors for immune disorders.

## Relevance to active research

Critical node in SCFA-immune regulation, cancer epigenetics, Treg stability (Foxp3 acetylation), and viral latency reversal.
