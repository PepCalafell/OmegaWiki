## Definition

CXCL9 is an IFN-γ-induced CXCR3-ligand chemokine that recruits effector and memory T cells and NK cells, marking T-cell-engaging, immune-active microenvironments.

## Intuition

CXCL9 production by myeloid cells signals an IFN-γ-experienced, T-cell-recruiting state; in tumors it marks antitumorigenic, ICI-favorable macrophage polarity opposite to SPP1+ TAMs.

## Formal notation

IFN-γ → STAT1 → CXCL9 transcription; CXCL9–CXCR3 signaling drives chemotaxis of CXCR3+ lymphocytes.

## Key variants

- Co-regulated CXCR3 ligands CXCL10 and CXCL11.

## Known limitations

- As an IFN-γ readout, CXCL9 reports cytokine exposure rather than a fixed cell identity.

## Open problems

- Disentangling CXCL9+ TAM induction from broader inflammatory milieux in vivo.

## Relevance to active research

Defines IFN-γ-driven CXCL9+ tumor-associated macrophages, contrasted with IFN-I-driven IFIT1+ TAMs; central to the CXCL9:SPP1 TAM-polarity biomarker of ICI response.
