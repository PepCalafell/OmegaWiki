UCP2 is an inner-mitochondrial-membrane carrier that dissipates the proton gradient, lowering ΔΨm and limiting ROS production. In macrophages, UCP2 is required for continuous efferocytosis: lowering ΔΨm after each apoptotic-cell uptake licenses the engulfment of subsequent corpses. UCP2 deficiency stalls efferocytosis and impairs anti-inflammatory cytokine output.
