## Definition
GFPT1 (also GFAT1) catalyses the first and rate-limiting step of the hexosamine biosynthetic pathway (HBP), converting fructose-6-phosphate plus glutamine into glucosamine-6-phosphate. Its paralog GFPT2/GFAT2 plays a similar role in different tissue contexts. HBP output drives N- and O-linked glycosylation of cellular and extracellular proteins.

## Intuition
GFPT1 sits at the intersection of carbon and nitrogen metabolism, controlling protein glycosylation and O-GlcNAcylation. Cancer cells with high glycolytic flux often upregulate GFPT1, producing UDP-GlcNAc for surface and secreted glycoproteins.

## Formal notation
- EC 2.6.1.16
- Substrate: F6P + Gln → GlcN-6P + Glu
- Downstream: UDP-GlcNAc → glycosyltransferase / O-GlcNAc transferase substrates

## Key variants
- GFPT1 (ubiquitous)
- GFPT2 (tissue-restricted, especially mesenchymal)

## Known limitations
- Dual roles in different tissue contexts complicate pharmacological inhibition.

## Open problems
- Selective GFPT1 vs GFPT2 inhibitors with tumour-specific activity.

## Relevance to active research
Identified as a hub protein of TPCPA WGCNA module 5 (colon cancer); links N/O-glycosylation to β-catenin biology and has been demonstrated as a targetable liability in pancreatic and lung cancer mouse models.
