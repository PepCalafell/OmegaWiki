## Definition

STAT family transcription factor activated by type-I and type-II interferon receptors via JAK kinases. Forms STAT1-STAT1 homodimers (binding GAS elements, downstream of IFNγ/IFNGR) and the STAT1-STAT2-IRF9 heterotrimer (ISGF3, binding ISRE elements, downstream of type-I IFN receptors). Two splice isoforms — STAT1α (full-length, transactivation-competent) and STAT1β (truncated).

## Relevance to active research

- Canonical effector of type-I and type-II IFN responses; ISG signature anchor.
- Loss of STAT1 in mice compromises innate immunity to viruses (Durbin 1996).
- Beyond acute signaling, STAT1 maintains a chromatin-regulatory role in homeostatic spleen macrophages and T cells, with effects on the epigenome exceeding its transcriptional footprint ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- Isoform-specific effects between STAT1α and STAT1β are non-redundant and partially opposing under homeostasis.
