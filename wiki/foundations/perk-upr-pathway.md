## Definition

PERK is one of three ER stress sensors of the unfolded protein response. Activated by BIP release under ER stress (including severe hypoxia), PERK phosphorylates eIF2α to globally attenuate cap-dependent translation while selectively upregulating ATF4, CHOP and stress-adaptive genes.

## Intuition

Severe hypoxia activates PERK and arrests global translation while permitting expression of survival genes (CA9, ATF6, IRE1, ATF4). The PERK–ATF4 arm also induces SETX, which resolves R-loops to protect transcription-replication conflicts under hypoxia.

## Relevance to active research

Suvac et al. (2025) frame UPR–PERK as one of two principal O2-sensing pathways alongside HIFs that govern hypoxic adaptation, replication stress and genomic instability.
