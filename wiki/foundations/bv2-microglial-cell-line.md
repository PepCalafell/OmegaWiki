## Definition

BV2 is an immortalized murine microglial cell line (v-raf/v-myc transformed) widely used as an in vitro surrogate for primary microglia. It retains many microglial functional responses (phagocytosis, cytokine production, activation) and is amenable to genomics and co-culture assays.

## Intuition

BV2 provides a scalable, manipulable microglia model where genome-wide transcriptomic and epigenomic responses to defined stimuli (e.g. controlled hypoxia, glioma contact) can be profiled without the yield limits of primary microglia.

N/A — cell-line model.

## Key variants

- Primary microglia, iPSC-derived microglia; macrophage lines RAW264.7, and primary BMDM ([[foundations/bone-marrow-derived-macrophage-bmdm]]) as comparators.

## Known limitations

- Immortalization and proliferation alter the transcriptome relative to resting primary microglia; not all primary-microglia behaviors are recapitulated.

## Open problems

- How faithfully BV2 chromatin responses to hypoxia mirror those of primary or in vivo microglia.

## Relevance to active research

Primary in vitro model in [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]] for RNA-seq, ATAC-seq and CUT&RUN profiling of hypoxia- and glioma-driven microglial reprogramming, with key findings validated in primary BMDM and human GBM.
