## Definition

*SLC12A3* encodes the thiazide-sensitive Na–Cl cotransporter (NCC), classically studied in the renal distal convoluted tubule. It has additionally been implicated in IL-18 signaling and IL-18-induced cytokine production (including IFN-γ, IL-6, CCL2).

## Intuition

Beyond electrolyte transport, SLC12-family cotransporters intersect inflammatory cytokine signaling, providing a plausible link between an SLC12A3-proximal CpG site and IFN-γ production capacity.

## Formal notation

Gene on chromosome 16; loss-of-function mutations cause Gitelman syndrome.

## Key variants

WNK3-regulated SLC12 cotransporter variants with similar functional effects.

## Known limitations

Its immune role is inferred largely from association/mediation analyses rather than direct mechanism.

## Open problems

How methylation at *SLC12A3*-proximal CpGs (e.g. cg21375332) couples genetic variation to IFN-γ trained immunity.

## Relevance to active research

A mediating locus linking genetic variants (rs604639) to BCG-induced IFN-γ changes via DNA methylation.
