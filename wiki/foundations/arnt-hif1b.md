## Definition

ARNT is the obligate bHLH-PAS dimer partner of both AHR (forming the AHR–ARNT complex on XRE/DRE elements) and HIF-1α (forming HIF-1 on hypoxia response elements). Cellular ARNT availability is therefore a node of cross-talk between hypoxia signalling and AHR signalling: HIF-1α can compete with AHR for ARNT, limiting AHR transcriptional output.

## Relevance to active research

Reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]] as a key constraint on AHR transcriptional activity and as the connection to [[hif1a]] hypoxia biology — a competition mechanism relevant to tumour-associated macrophages and Tr1 differentiation in hypoxic niches.
