## Definition
RNF5 (also RMA1) is a RING-domain E3 ubiquitin ligase that localises to the endoplasmic reticulum and outer mitochondrial membrane and contributes to ER-associated degradation (ERAD) by ubiquitylating misfolded membrane proteins. It also regulates autophagy and ER stress responses.

## Intuition
RNF5 marks misfolded or surplus membrane proteins for proteasomal disposal at the ER, with downstream effects on autophagy and unfolded-protein response signalling.

## Formal notation
- Type: RING-domain E3 ligase
- Localisation: ER membrane, outer mitochondrial membrane
- Pathway: ERAD, ER-stress, autophagy regulation

## Key variants
- RNF5 vs the related RNF185
- Tissue-restricted expression patterns

## Known limitations
- Substrate scope is partially mapped.
- No clinical PROTAC chemistry against RNF5 yet.

## Open problems
- Tumour-selective PROTAC-style degradation via RNF5 in liver cancer.

## Relevance to active research
Identified in TPCPA as an E3 ligase highly expressed in liver cancer, nominating it as a candidate tissue-selective recruiter for PROTAC-style targeted protein degradation.
