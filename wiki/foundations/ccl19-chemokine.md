## Definition

CCL19 is a CC-class chemokine that, together with CCL21, signals through CCR7 to direct lymphocyte and dendritic-cell homing to T-zones of secondary lymphoid organs. In peripheral tissues CCL19 is produced by fibroblastic reticular cells (FRCs) of lymph nodes and by FRC-like perivascular fibroblasts in non-lymphoid organs, where it sustains immune-cell residence and motility.

## Intuition

CCL19 is the molecular signature of a lymphoid-tissue-like stromal niche. Wherever CCL19+ fibroblasts are found outside lymph nodes — perivascular skin, tumour-associated tertiary lymphoid structures, inflamed gut — there is an FRC-style organising hub that recruits and retains adaptive immune cells.

## Key variants

- CCL19 vs CCL21: both bind CCR7 but CCL19 recruits β-arrestin and desensitises CCR7 faster; CCL21 is a more sustained chemoattractant.

## Known limitations

- CCL19 expression alone does not establish bona fide tertiary lymphoid structure; histological confirmation of B/T compartments is required.
