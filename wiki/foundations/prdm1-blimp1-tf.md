## Definition

PR-SET-domain zinc-finger transcriptional repressor; master regulator of terminal differentiation in plasma cells and effector/exhausted CD8+ T cells. Identified as a **multi-state TF** controlling both TEXterm and TRM cell differentiation.

## Relevance to active research

- Positive-control TF in the Chung et al. Perturb-seq library: `Prdm1` KO reduces TEXterm differentiation by ~90% and increases TEXprog frequency.
- `Prdm1` KO also reduces TRM cell formation and increases TCM cell formation, confirming its dual role.
