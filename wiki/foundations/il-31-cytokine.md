## Definition

IL-31 is a Th2 cytokine produced by CD4⁺ T cells, mast cells, and activated eosinophils. It signals through the heterodimer IL-31RA/OSMR, expressed prominently on sensory neurons of the dorsal root ganglion, where it triggers itch. IL-31 is a central pruritogen in atopic dermatitis and is the target of the approved monoclonal antibody nemolizumab.

## Relevance to active research

Activated eosinophils release IL-31 alongside Epx and MBP in stress-aggravated AD-like skin, contributing to the itch–scratch cycle exacerbated by Pdyn⁺ sympathetic neuron activation ([[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]]).
