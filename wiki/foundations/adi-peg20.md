## Definition

PEGylated form of Mycoplasma arginine deiminase. Hydrolyses circulating L-arginine to L-citrulline and ammonia, starving arginine-auxotrophic tumours (often ASS1-deficient, e.g. mesothelioma, hepatocellular carcinoma, melanoma).

## Intuition

Like ASNase for asparagine but for arginine: tumours that have epigenetically silenced ASS1 cannot resynthesise arginine and die when serum arginine is removed. In hepatocellular carcinoma, however, ADI-PEG20 monotherapy has not improved overall survival, partly because it also strips arginine from tumour-infiltrating T cells.

## Formal notation

L-arginine → L-citrulline + NH3 (arginine deiminase).

## Key variants

- ADI-PEG20 (clinical lead).
- Arginase-based depletion (BCT-100, recombinant human arginase 1).

## Known limitations

- Phase 3 mesothelioma trial positive for OS; hepatocellular carcinoma trial negative.
- Co-depletion of T-cell arginine impairs antitumour immunity.

## Open problems

- Combination with ICB after ASS1 stratification.
- Local arginine restoration strategies in TILs.

## Relevance to active research

- Paradigm of "amino-acid auxotrophy" therapy and its immunometabolic trade-offs.
