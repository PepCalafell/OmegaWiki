## Definition

Xanthone derivative originally developed as a vascular-disrupting agent; later identified as a partial agonist of murine STING. Does not appreciably activate human STING.

## Intuition

The cautionary tale of murine-to-human STING agonist translation. DMXAA produced potent antitumor responses in mouse syngeneic models but failed phase 3 trials in advanced NSCLC because it cannot activate human STING.

## Known limitations

Species selectivity (mouse-only) makes it useless as a human therapeutic but still useful as a tool compound in mechanistic mouse work.

## Relevance to active research

The DMXAA failure crystallized the need for clinically translatable, human-STING-active agonists and motivated the development of CDN analogs (ADU-S100) and non-CDN small molecules (diABZI, MSA-2).
