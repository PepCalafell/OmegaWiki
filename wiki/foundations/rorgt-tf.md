## Definition

RORγt is the lineage-defining transcription factor of IL-17-producing T helper 17 (Th17) cells and group 3 innate lymphoid cells (ILC3s). AHR cooperates with RORγt at the *Il22* promoter to drive IL-22 production in Th17, Th22, and ILC3 cells, and AHR with STAT3 drives the Th17 transcription factor Aiolos.
