DRP1 (DNM1L) is a dynamin-family GTPase that drives mitochondrial fission by oligomerizing on the outer membrane and constricting it. In macrophages, DRP1-mediated fission is required for continuous efferocytosis: post-engulfment fission redistributes mitochondrial mass and supports anti-inflammatory metabolic rewiring. DRP1 loss in myeloid cells impairs corpse clearance and worsens inflammation.
