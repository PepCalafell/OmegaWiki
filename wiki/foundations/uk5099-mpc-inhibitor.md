## Definition

Tool-compound inhibitor of the mitochondrial pyruvate carrier complex (MPC1/MPC2). Blocks pyruvate import into mitochondria, forcing cells to use alternative fuels (glutamine, fatty acids) for TCA-cycle flux. Widely used ex vivo to push T cells toward memory differentiation.

## Intuition

In the CAR-T context, blocking MPC during expansion produces cells with stronger mitochondrial OxPhos, more histone acetylation, and longer in-vivo persistence.

## Formal notation

Inhibition of MPC1/MPC2-dependent pyruvate transport across the inner mitochondrial membrane.

## Key variants

- UK-5099 (canonical tool compound).
- MSDC-0160 / MSDC-0602 (clinical MPC modulators, indirect).

## Known limitations

- Toxicity profile limits in-vivo use.
- Long-term mitochondrial side effects when chronic.

## Open problems

- Translation of ex-vivo CAR-T conditioning into GMP manufacturing.
- Combination with metformin or LDH inhibition for synergistic memory induction.

## Relevance to active research

- Ex vivo conditioning of CD8+ T cells and CAR-T cells for memory phenotype.
- Reference inhibitor for studies of mitochondrial pyruvate flux in immune cells.
