## Definition
APC (adenomatous polyposis coli) encodes a multi-domain scaffold protein that promotes β-catenin degradation in the canonical WNT pathway. Biallelic APC loss is the gatekeeper event in colorectal adenoma formation; germline APC mutation causes familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP).

## Intuition
Loss of APC permits unchecked WNT signalling, driving clonal expansion of intestinal stem cells and the formation of adenomas — the principal precancerous lesion for colorectal cancer.

## Relevance to active research
Apc-floxed alleles paired with intestinal Cre drivers (Lgr5-CreER, Villin-CreER) are the workhorse genetic background for studying colon-cancer initiation and adenoma-to-carcinoma progression in autochthonous and organoid models.
