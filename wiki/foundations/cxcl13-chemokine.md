## Definition

CXCL13 is a homeostatic CXC chemokine that signals via CXCR5 and organises B-cell follicles in secondary lymphoid organs. In tumours, CXCL13 expression by exhausted T cells and stromal cells drives lymphocyte recruitment and tertiary lymphoid structure formation.

## Relevance to active research

- Tumour CXCL13 signature is a robust correlate of TLS and ICI response.
- Activated by anti-PD-1 + anti-CTLA4 + chemotherapy combinations in NSCLC.
