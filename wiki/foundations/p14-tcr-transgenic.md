## Definition

CD8+ T cell receptor transgenic mouse model expressing a TCR specific for the LCMV-derived peptide GP33–41 presented by H-2Db. P14 cells can be adoptively transferred into recipient mice for clonal monitoring of antigen-specific CD8+ T cell responses.

## Relevance to active research

- Donor cell population for in vivo Perturb-seq in Chung et al. 2025 — Cas9+ P14 cells transduced with TF-targeting retroviral gRNAs are transferred into LCMV-infected recipients.
- Standard system to compare acute (Armstrong) vs chronic (Clone 13) CD8+ T cell differentiation, and tumour control in B16-GP33 melanoma adoptive cell transfer.
