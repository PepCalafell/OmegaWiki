## Definition

IL-18 is an IL-1 family cytokine that signals through IL-18R1/IL-18RAP, activating MyD88–NF-κB and MAPK pathways. It synergizes with IL-12 to drive IFNγ production in T and NK cells.

## Intuition

In the Immune Dictionary, IL-18 induces a unique polyfunctional NK cell state ("NK-f") with >1,000 upregulated genes — an order of magnitude more than IL-2/IL-12/IL-15 — encompassing biosynthesis (Myc), cytotoxicity (Gzmb), DC recruitment (Xcl1), myeloid maturation (Csf2/GM-CSF) and differentiation (Kit, Batf).

## Relevance to active research

IL-18 has clinical promise in cancer immunotherapy, partially limited by the soluble decoy IL-18BP. The polyfunctional NK-f state described by Cui & Hacohen 2024 reframes IL-18's mechanism beyond classical "IFNγ-inducing factor".
