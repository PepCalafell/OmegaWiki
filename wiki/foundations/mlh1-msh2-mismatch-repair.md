## Definition

The mismatch repair (MMR) pathway, anchored by MutSα (MSH2/MSH6) recognition and MutLα (MLH1/PMS2) endonuclease activity, corrects base-base mismatches and insertion-deletion loops generated during DNA replication. Loss of MMR causes microsatellite instability (MSI) and the SBS6/SBS21/ID2 mutational signatures.

## Intuition

Hypoxia epigenetically silences MLH1, MSH2 and MSH6 promoters in a HIF1α-dependent manner, producing transient MSI without germline Lynch mutations. MLH1 repression involves direct binding of hypoxia-inducible repressors, reduced MYC occupancy, and CpG hypermethylation.

## Relevance to active research

Hypoxic suppression of MMR is one of the two cardinal mechanisms (alongside HR suppression) by which the hypoxic TME generates DDR-deficient mutational signatures in tumours that are genotypically MMR-wild-type (Suvac et al. 2025).
