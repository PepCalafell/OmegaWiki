## Definition

IvyGAP is a public anatomic transcriptional atlas of human glioblastoma, profiling gene expression in laser-microdissected samples from spatially defined tumor regions — leading edge (LE), infiltrating tumor (IT), cellular tumor (CT), perinecrotic zone (CTpnz), pseudopalisading cells around necrosis (CTpan), and microvascular/hyperplastic vascular regions.

## Intuition

By tying expression to defined anatomical structures (especially hypoxic perinecrotic/pseudopalisading zones), IvyGAP lets researchers ask how genes behave across the spatial oxygen and structural gradients of GBM without single-cell resolution.

## Formal notation

N/A — bulk regional transcriptomic resource.

## Key variants

- Complementary GBM scRNA-seq resources (e.g. GSE163120, Antunes et al.); spatial transcriptomics atlases.

## Known limitations

- Bulk regional expression conflates cell-type composition with per-cell expression changes; limited donor numbers.

## Open problems

- Deconvolving cell-type abundance shifts from intrinsic per-cell reprogramming in hypoxic regions.

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[papers/hypoxic-stress-dysregulates-functions-glioma-associated]] to show that LGALS3 peaks in hypoxic perinecrotic/pseudopalisading regions while TMEM119/P2RY12 are lowest there — corroborating in vitro marker dysregulation in human tumor space.
