## Definition

Sphingosine-1-phosphate is a bioactive sphingolipid metabolite produced by sphingosine kinases (Sphk1, Sphk2) that signals through five GPCRs (S1PR1–5). It is the canonical lymphocyte-egress signal, an M1→M2 macrophage modulator, and a mast-cell degranulation co-factor.

## Intuition

A small lipid gradient — high in blood/lymph, low in tissues — that immune cells navigate via S1PR1.

## Key variants

- S1P (extracellular signalling pool)
- Sphingosine (precursor)
- Ceramide (upstream)
- S1P lyase / phosphatase (degradation arms)

## Known limitations

- Receptor-isoform selectivity of S1P difficult to dissect pharmacologically.

## Open problems

- Tissue-by-tissue S1P concentration mapping in disease.

## Relevance to active research

Fingolimod (S1PR1 functional antagonist) clinical use in multiple sclerosis; emerging interest in S1PR2 macrophage polarization.
