## Definition

PCAWG is the ICGC/TCGA Pan-Cancer Analysis of Whole Genomes consortium, providing harmonized WGS calls (SNVs, structural variants, mutational signatures, copy number) across thousands of tumours spanning 38 tumour types.

## Intuition

PCAWG enables population-scale tests of microenvironment-genome interactions: in particular, the link between mRNA-based hypoxia signatures and DDR-deficiency mutational signatures was first demonstrated in PCAWG (Bhandari et al. 2019; Suvac et al. 2025 review).

## Relevance to active research

Suvac et al. (2025) cite PCAWG analysis of 1,188 tumours across 27 cancer types as the principal human-tumour evidence that high hypoxia tracks with increased SBS6/ID6 (HR-deficient) and SBS21/ID2 (MMR-deficient) signatures in the absence of germline DDR mutations.
