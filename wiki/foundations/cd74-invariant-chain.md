## Definition

CD74 is the MHC class II-associated invariant chain that chaperones MHC-II assembly and antigen-presentation, and also acts as a receptor for MIF. On non-professional antigen-presenting cells such as fibroblastic reticular cells, CD74/MHC-II expression marks an immune-interacting, antigen-presenting-capable stromal phenotype.

## Intuition

CD74 on a fibroblast signals that the cell participates in antigen presentation and immune-cell instruction — a hallmark of FRC-like immunomodulatory fibroblasts.

## Formal notation

CD74 + HLA-DRA/HLA-DRB1 = MHC-II antigen-presentation module.

## Key variants

CD74 as MHC-II chaperone vs CD74 as MIF receptor.

## Known limitations

Expression alone does not prove functional antigen presentation by stromal cells.

## Open problems

Functional significance of fibroblast antigen presentation in peripheral tissues.

## Relevance to active research

Core marker (CCL19+CD74+HLA-DRA+) of the F3 FRC-like fibroblast predicted to maintain the superficial perivascular immune niche.
