## Definition

Hydrolysis-resistant phosphorothioate analog of 2′3′-cyclic di-AMP. Binds all known human STING allelic variants. Delivered intratumorally.

## Intuition

The poster child of the first-generation STING-agonist era — strong preclinical activity (local and abscopal tumor regression in mouse syngeneic models) that failed to translate in humans. Phase 1 monotherapy and PD-1 combination trials (NCT03172936) showed only sporadic partial responses; the trial was terminated for lack of efficacy.

## Known limitations

Rapid clearance after intratumoral injection, T-cell-cytotoxic effects, and pharmacokinetic limitations of CDNs more broadly.

## Relevance to active research

ADU-S100's clinical failure motivated next-generation strategies: hydrogel-sustained release, extracellular-vesicle delivery (CDK-002/exoSTING), STING-agonist ADCs (XMT-2056, TAK-500), and indirect activation via ENPP1/TREX1 inhibitors.
