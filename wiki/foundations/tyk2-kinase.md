## Definition

Member of the Janus kinase (JAK) family that associates with IFNAR1, IL-10R2, IL-12Rβ1 and IL-13Rα1 receptor chains. Phosphorylates STAT1, STAT3, STAT4, STAT5 and STAT6 in receptor-specific contexts. The K923E substitution renders TYK2 catalytically inactive (kinase-dead) without abolishing scaffold function.

## Relevance to active research

- Allosteric TYK2 inhibitors (e.g. deucravacitinib) are clinically approved for plaque psoriasis.
- TYK2 has both catalytic-dependent and catalytic-independent regulatory effects on the transcriptome and chromatin of homeostatic immune cells — comparison of Tyk2-KO vs Tyk2-K923E (kinase-dead) reveals scaffold-mediated functions ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- Loss of TYK2 (full or kinase-dead) downregulates core ISGs under homeostasis, evidence that baseline JAK-STAT requires both ISGF3 components and TYK2 (see [[kinase-independent-functions-tyk2]]).
