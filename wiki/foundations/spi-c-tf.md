SPI-C is an ETS-family transcription factor induced in red pulp macrophages (RPMs) and erythroid island macrophages (EIMs) by intracellular heme (which sequesters the repressor BACH1) and in Kupffer cells by Notch ligands from liver sinusoidal endothelial cells. SPI-C drives the iron-recycling transcriptional program (HMOX1, ferroportin, ferritin, CD163, transferrin handling). SPI-C loss collapses RPM and EIM numbers and impairs erythrophagocytic clearance, but spares Kupffer cells.
