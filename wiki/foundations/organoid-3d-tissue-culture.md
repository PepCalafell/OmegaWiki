## Definition
Organoids are self-organized 3D mini-organs grown from pluripotent, fetal or adult stem cells that recapitulate key functional, structural and biological complexity of the source tissue. Cancer organoids can be initiated by genetic manipulation, carcinogen exposure or oncogenic viral infection, then transplanted or cocultured to model early carcinogenesis ex vivo.

## Key variants
- iPSC-derived organoids
- Adult-stem-cell-derived organoids
- Patient-derived precancerous organoids
- Mutant-organoid orthotopic grafts
- Optogenetics-integrated mini-colons (Opto-Cre)
- Air-liquid interface (ALI) tumour-immune organoids

## Relevance to active research
Provide the only route to track human carcinogenesis end-to-end in a tissue-like setting, enable longitudinal sampling, and complement GEMMs for early-cancer mechanistic studies.
