## Definition

MC903 is a low-calcaemic vitamin D3 analogue that, when applied topically to murine ear or dorsal skin, induces a robust TSLP-dependent atopic-dermatitis-like phenotype: epidermal hyperplasia, dermal thickening, elevated transepidermal water loss, eosinophil/mast-cell/Th2 infiltration, and spontaneous scratching. Standard protocol: daily topical application for ~10 days; lesion peaks around day 10.

## Relevance to active research

Workhorse model for atopic dermatitis mechanism studies, including the stress-aggravated AD paradigm in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], where MC903-treated mice are subjected to repeated high-platform stress to expose sympathetic-eosinophil exacerbation.
