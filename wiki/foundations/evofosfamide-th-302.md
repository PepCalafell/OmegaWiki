## Definition

Evofosfamide (TH-302) is a 2-nitroimidazole-linked prodrug of the DNA alkylator bromo-isophosphoramide mustard (Br-IPM). Under hypoxia, one-electron reduction of the 2-nitroimidazole produces a nitro-radical that fragments to release the cytotoxic Br-IPM warhead, which alkylates DNA and induces interstrand crosslinks. Activation is selective for tumor hypoxic regions (pO₂ < 10 mmHg).

## Intuition

TH-302 is the prototypical modern hypoxia-activated prodrug ([[concepts/hypoxia-activated-prodrugs-haps]]). Preclinical activity is robust across solid tumors and combined favorably with chemotherapy and radiotherapy. Phase III trials in advanced soft-tissue sarcoma (TH-CR-406, NCT01440088) and pancreatic adenocarcinoma (MAESTRO, NCT01746979) failed to meet OS endpoints — likely due to absent biomarker-stratified patient selection rather than mechanism failure.

## Formal notation

- Chemical: 1-methyl-2-nitro-1H-imidazol-5-yl)methyl N,N'-bis(2-bromoethyl)phosphorodiamidate
- Activation pO₂ threshold: <10 mmHg (clinically tumor-selective)
- Cytotoxic species released: Br-IPM (bromo-isophosphoramide mustard)
- DNA crosslinker, primarily interstrand crosslinks

## Key variants

- TH-302 (evofosfamide) — clinical
- Tarloxotinib — same 2-nitroimidazole strategy applied to an EGFR/HER2 inhibitor
- TH-4000 — earlier analog

## Known limitations

- Failed phase III in sarcoma and pancreatic cancer (no OS benefit) without hypoxia biomarker stratification.
- Toxicity: mucositis, fatigue, hematologic.
- Antagonism with vascular normalization or hyperoxia approaches.

## Open problems

- Whether re-trialing with FMISO-PET / mRNA hypoxia stratification rescues efficacy.
- Whether combining with ICB or PARPi exploits hypoxia-induced HRD.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/hypoxic-microenvironment-cancer-molecular-mechanisms-therapeutic]], TH-302 (and tarloxotinib) is featured as a prototype hypoxia-activated prodrug with mixed clinical outcomes; the review notes its capacity to *eliminate* hypoxic cells (rather than reverse hypoxia) and thereby promote T-cell infiltration. For thesis-relevant therapeutic combinations, TH-302 is the canonical example of "exploit-the-hypoxic-niche" therapy contrasted against vascular-normalization or nano-O₂ approaches.
