## Definition

DREADDs are engineered muscarinic GPCRs that no longer bind acetylcholine but respond selectively to inert designer ligands (clozapine-N-oxide, CNO; or deschloroclozapine, DCZ). hM3Dq couples to Gq and depolarises/activates neurons or non-neuronal cells. hM4Di couples to Gi/o and silences/hyperpolarises. Cre-dependent expression cassettes (loxP-stop-loxP-hM3Dq-mCitrine; loxP-stop-loxP-hM4Di-mCherry) provide cell-type-specific chemogenetic control.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], Epx^iCre-hM3Dq mice acutely activate eosinophils (CNO) to test sufficiency for itch; Pdyn^Net-hM4Di mice acutely silence the Pdyn⁺ sympathetic subtype during stress to test temporal necessity beyond ablation-based loss-of-function.
