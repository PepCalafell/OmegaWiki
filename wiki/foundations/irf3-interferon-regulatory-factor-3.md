## Definition

Transcription factor phosphorylated by TBK1 downstream of activated STING. Phospho-IRF3 dimerizes and translocates to the nucleus, where it drives transcription of type I IFN genes and pro-inflammatory ISGs including chemoattractants such as CXCL10.

## Intuition

The "canonical" output arm of STING signaling — when IRF3 fires, you get a robust antiviral/anti-tumor type I IFN programme. When STING signaling is biased away from IRF3 (e.g., chronic CIN, NF-κB activation in CD8 T cells, mutant-p53 sequestration of TBK1), cells get inflammation without protective IFN.

## Relevance to active research

Recent work shows MTAP deficiency confers STING-agonist resistance by destabilizing IRF3 and co-deletion of type I IFN genes on chromosome 9p — a candidate predictive biomarker for STING-pathway-directed therapy.
