## Definition

U0126 is a cell-permeable, ATP-noncompetitive inhibitor of MEK1 and MEK2 — the upstream activators of ERK1/2. It blocks ERK phosphorylation and thereby ERK-dependent gene expression. Widely used as a tool compound to establish ERK dependence of biological phenotypes in vitro.

## Formal notation

- Target: MEK1 / MEK2 (non-ATP-competitive, allosteric)
- IC50: ~70 nM (MEK1), ~60 nM (MEK2)
- Typical in vitro concentration: 5-20 μM
- Selectivity: clean vs other MAPKKs; some off-target at high doses (HSF1, PI3K)
- Readout: loss of p-ERK1/2 (Thr202/Tyr204) on Western blot, flow

## Variants / related

- PD98059 — earlier MEK inhibitor with weaker potency
- Trametinib, selumetinib — clinical-grade MEK inhibitors
- Sorafenib — Raf inhibitor (upstream of MEK)

## Known limitations

- Off-target at >20 μM
- Light-sensitive; stock solution degrades
- Does not distinguish MEK1 from MEK2

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[papers/macrophages-use-apoptotic-cell-derived-methionine]] (Ampomah 2022 *Nat Metab*) to establish ERK1/2 dependence of AC-induced Ptgs2/Tgfb1/TGF-β1; combined with siMapk1+siMapk3 for orthogonal validation.
