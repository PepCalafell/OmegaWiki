## Definition

Tumor suppressor kinase upstream of AMPK and mTOR. Germline loss causes Peutz-Jeghers syndrome. Somatic co-mutation with KRAS in ~20-30% of KRAS-mutant lung adenocarcinoma defines the KRAS-LKB1 (KL) molecular subset.

## Intuition

KRAS-LKB1 mutant NSCLC is robustly resistant to PD-1/PD-L1 immune checkpoint blockade and to radiotherapy. The proposed mechanism is impaired mitophagy → mitochondrial DNA leakage → chronic STING engagement → adaptive EZH2/DNMT1-mediated STING epigenetic silencing → loss of MHC-I expression and immune cell trafficking.

## Relevance to active research

EZH2/DNMT inhibition + MPS1 inhibition restores STING-IFN signaling in KRAS-LKB1 mutant lung cancer preclinically (Kitajima 2019, 2022). STK11 inactivation is a candidate biomarker for STING-pathway-directed combination therapy.
