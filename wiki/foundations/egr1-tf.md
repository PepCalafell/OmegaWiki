## Definition

EGR1 is a 3-zinc-finger (C2H2) immediate-early transcription factor binding the consensus GCG(T/G)GGGCG. It is broadly expressed across cell types and is rapidly induced by mitogens, neuronal activity, stress, and hypoxia. Functions span memory formation, T cell activation, vascular biology, and tumor suppression. Used in Khetan 2025 as the largest-family benchmark TF (zinc fingers) and provides ChIP-seq data in mouse frontal cortex plus MPRA data confirming variant-driven expression effects in vivo.

## Relevance to active research

[[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]] uses EGR1 PADIT-seq (554 active 10-mers) and shows that variants altering multiple overlapping EGR1 active k-mers have large effects on expression in MPRA data, while single-site variants have modest but significant effects — the dose–response signature predicted by the overlapping-binding-sites model.
