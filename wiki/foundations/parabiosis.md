## Definition

Parabiosis surgically joins two mice so they share a common blood circulation. With congenic markers (e.g. CD45.1/CD45.2), it quantifies the blood-borne (circulating progenitor) contribution to a tissue cell population versus local self-maintenance.

## Intuition

If a resident cell pool is fed by circulating precursors, the parabiont's congenically marked cells appear in the partner's tissue; if the pool self-renews locally, the partner contributes little. It is the classic test of monocyte contribution to resident-macrophage niches.

## Key variants

## Known limitations

- Surgical stress and incomplete chimerism.
- Slow equilibration; tissue access varies.
