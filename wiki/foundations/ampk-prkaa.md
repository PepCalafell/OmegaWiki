AMPK is a heterotrimeric Ser/Thr kinase activated by an elevated AMP/ATP ratio that promotes catabolic, energy-producing pathways (FAO, mitochondrial biogenesis, autophagy) and suppresses anabolic ones (FAS, mTORC1, gluconeogenesis). In macrophages, AMPKα1 activation is essential for the anti-inflammatory phenotype during tissue regeneration and supports OXPHOS in the resolution phase. Pharmacologic AMPK activators (metformin, AICAR) have been tested in macrophage-implicated diseases including pulmonary fibrosis.
