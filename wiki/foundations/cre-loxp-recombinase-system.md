## Definition
Cre is a site-specific recombinase from bacteriophage P1 that excises or inverts DNA flanked by 34-bp loxP sites. Tissue-specific promoters control where Cre is expressed; fusion with mutated oestrogen-receptor domains (CreER, CreERT2) makes recombination tamoxifen-inducible. Combined with lox-stop-lox (LSL) alleles, Cre conditionally activates oncogenes or deletes tumour suppressors.

## Key variants
- Cre / CreER / CreERT2
- Dre–rox (orthogonal recombinase)
- Flp–FRT (orthogonal recombinase)
- Inducible compound systems (sequential / reversible)

## Relevance to active research
Backbone of every conditional GEMM cited for early-cancer modelling; combined with multicolour reporters (Confetti, Crainbow, Red2Onco) it enables single-clone lineage tracing.
