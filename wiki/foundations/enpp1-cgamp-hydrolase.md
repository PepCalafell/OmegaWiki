## Definition

Cell-surface phosphodiesterase. Dominant extracellular hydrolase that degrades cGAMP, generating AMP + GMP and the immunosuppressive metabolite adenosine via downstream CD73.

## Intuition

The brake on the extracellular STING-IFN signaling axis. By degrading the cGAMP "immunotransmitter" produced by tumor cells, ENPP1 prevents paracrine STING activation of dendritic cells, macrophages, NK cells, and endothelial cells in the TME. Acts as a dual checkpoint: removes a pro-inflammatory signal and creates an immunosuppressive one.

## Relevance to active research

ENPP1 inhibitors (RBS3418, SR-8541A, ISM5939, TXN10128) are in phase 1/2 trials. Preclinically, ENPP1 inhibition synergizes with ionizing radiation and slows breast cancer growth/metastasis. Tumor ENPP1 expression is a candidate predictive biomarker for STING-pathway-directed therapy.
