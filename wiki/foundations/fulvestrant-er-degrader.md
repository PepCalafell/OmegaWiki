## Definition

Fulvestrant is a selective oestrogen receptor degrader (SERD) approved for hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. It binds the oestrogen receptor and induces its proteasomal degradation, blocking both genomic and non-genomic ER signalling.

## Relevance to active research

- Reverses ICI resistance driven by oestrogen-induced immunosuppressive TAM polarization in mouse melanoma.
- Candidate combination partner for anti-PD-(L)1 in oestrogen-modulated tumours.
