## Definition

IL-22 is an IL-10-family cytokine produced by Th17, Th22, γδ T cells, NKT cells, and ILC3 cells; it acts on non-immune epithelial cells expressing IL-22R1/IL-10R2 to induce antimicrobial peptides, mucin production, tight-junction repair, and regenerative responses in skin, gut, and lung. AHR drives *Il22* transcription via cooperation with RORγt.
