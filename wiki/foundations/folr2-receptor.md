## Definition

FOLR2 (folate receptor β / FRβ) is a GPI-anchored folate-binding glycoprotein expressed predominantly on tissue-resident-like macrophages and activated myeloid cells. It marks a distinct macrophage state across multiple tissues (including liver, breast, lung, decidua) and is widely used as a defining marker of FOLR2⁺ TAMs in cancer scRNA-seq atlases.

## Intuition

Marker of a "scavenger / homeostatic / tissue-resident-like" macrophage state across tissues. Functional output is highly tissue-context-dependent (non-responder-associated in HCC ICB; T-cell-infiltration-favourable in breast cancer).

## Key variants

- Membrane GPI-anchored FOLR2
- Tumor-associated FOLR2⁺ TAM (multiple cancers)
- Onco-fetal FOLR2 mac (Li et al., HCC)

## Known limitations

- Functional role is highly tissue-context-dependent.
- Not a strict ontogeny marker — expressed by both resident and monocyte-derived populations.

## Open problems

- Whether FOLR2 itself contributes functionally (e.g., folate scavenging, immune modulation) or is purely a marker.
