## Definition

Ubiquitously expressed cell-surface glycoprotein that engages SIRPα on macrophages to deliver the canonical "don't eat me" anti-phagocytic signal. Overexpressed on many cancer cell types as a mechanism of immune evasion.

## Intuition

CD47-SIRPα blockade was the prototypical "innate immune checkpoint" intervention. First-generation antagonists (Hu5F9-G4 / magrolimab, evorpacept) showed mixed results — some clinical activity in lymphoma (11/22 NHL responses with Hu5F9 + rituximab) but several solid-tumor and AML programs failed. Failures are attributed to ligand redundancy (CD24-Siglec-10, MHC-I-LILRB1) and to ADCP triggering an anti-inflammatory pro-angiogenic program. Next-generation strategies use masked antibodies (ADG153), bispecifics (TG-1801), and CRISPR-screen-derived combinations (e.g., APMAP KO).

## Relevance to active research

Remains an active TAM-effectorization target with iterative design refinements (masking, bispecifics, combination with eat-me agonists).
