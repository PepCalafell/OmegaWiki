## Definition
KRAS encodes a small GTPase that cycles between GDP- and GTP-bound states to relay receptor-tyrosine-kinase signals to the RAF–MEK–ERK and PI3K–AKT pathways. Activating point mutations (G12D, G12V, G12C, G13D) lock KRAS in the GTP-bound state, producing constitutive downstream signalling.

## Intuition
KRAS mutations are among the most prevalent oncogenic drivers in pancreatic, colorectal and lung adenocarcinoma. In autochthonous mouse models, conditional KrasLSL-G12D alleles activated by tissue-specific Cre recombinases initiate precancerous lesions (e.g., PanINs) and, with additional Trp53 or Cdkn2a loss, progress to full-blown cancer.

## Key variants
- G12D, G12V, G12C, G13D point mutations
- Lox-stop-lox conditional alleles (KrasLSL-G12D)

## Relevance to active research
Used as the canonical oncogenic driver in essentially every autochthonous early-cancer model discussed in the review.
