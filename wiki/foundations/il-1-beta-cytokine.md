## Definition

IL-1β is an IL-1 family pro-inflammatory cytokine cleaved from pro-IL-1β by caspase-1 downstream of inflammasome activation. It signals through IL-1R1/IL-1RAcP, driving MyD88–IRAK–NF-κB and MAPK pathways across most tissues.

## Intuition

IL-1β is a "danger" amplifier — small absolute amounts produce large transcriptional effects, partly because IL-1R is broadly expressed. In sepsis, IL-1β cooperates with TNF to produce synergistic and antagonistic gene programs across organs.

## Relevance to active research

Anakinra (IL-1Ra) and canakinumab (anti-IL-1β) define a therapeutic arsenal that complements anti-TNF in inflammatory diseases. In the TNF-centered cytokine hierarchy of sepsis (Takahama et al. 2024), TNF+IL-1β yields dose-dependent body-temperature drop and lethality.
