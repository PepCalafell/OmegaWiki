## Definition

SPP1 (secreted phosphoprotein 1), also known as osteopontin, is a secreted glycoprotein produced by macrophages, osteoblasts, and tumour cells. It binds integrins and CD44 and drives ECM remodelling, fibrosis, and chemotaxis. In tumour biology, SPP1⁺ TAMs are a well-defined immune-excluding macrophage state across solid tumours.

## Relevance to active research

- Marker of a fibrosis-coupled TAM polarity associated with ICI resistance.
- Component of the CXCL9:SPP1 macrophage biomarker axis.
- Component of the COL11A1–SPP1 stromal exclusion axis in NSCLC.
