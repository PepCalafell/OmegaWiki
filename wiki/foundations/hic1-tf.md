## Definition

POZ/BTB zinc-finger transcriptional repressor originally identified as a tumour-suppressor gene silenced by hypermethylation. Identified as a **multi-state TF active in both TEXterm and TRM** CD8+ T cells.

## Relevance to active research

- Cas9 KO in mouse CD8+ T cells profoundly reduces TEXterm differentiation (~90%) in chronic LCMV.
- HIC1 KO **also** reduces TRM cell frequency and TRM-signature gene expression in small intestine — distinguishing it from TEXterm single-state TFs.
- HIC1 KO improves tumour control in B16-GP33 less robustly than ZSCAN20 KO because of compromised TRM-like effector states.
- Highlights the trade-off of targeting multi-state vs single-state TFs in T-cell engineering.
