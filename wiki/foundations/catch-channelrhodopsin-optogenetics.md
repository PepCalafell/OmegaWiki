## Definition

CatCh is a high-Ca²⁺-permeability variant of channelrhodopsin-2 (carrying the L132C mutation) that depolarises target neurons with greater efficacy than wild-type ChR2 in response to blue (~473 nm) light. It is widely used as a Cre-dependent (loxP-stop-loxP) reporter for subtype-selective optogenetic activation in peripheral and central neurons.

## Relevance to active research

Pdyn^Net-CatCh mice enable local blue-light activation of skin-innervating Pdyn⁺ sympathetic fibres in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]], establishing that selective firing of this neuronal subtype is sufficient to drive eosinophil recruitment and exacerbate AD-like dermatitis.
