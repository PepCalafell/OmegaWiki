## Definition

Pho4 is a basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factor in Saccharomyces cerevisiae that drives the PHO regulon in response to low phosphate. It binds the E-box CACGTG and competes with the constitutive bHLH Cbf1 (centromere-binding factor) at shared sites; under phosphate starvation Pho4 translocates to the nucleus and displaces Cbf1 at a subset of sites. Used in Khetan 2025 as one of two paralogous yeast bHLH TFs in a controlled paralog-competition system with ChIP-nexus and BET-seq data.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]], Pho4 has 224 active 8-mers and ChIP-nexus peaks enriched for 4 consecutive overlapping sites; pairing Pho4 vs Cbf1 PADIT-seq activities provides the cleanest in vivo evidence that paralog competition is determined by differential numbers of overlapping low-affinity binding sites flanking the shared CACGTG core (r=0.948).
