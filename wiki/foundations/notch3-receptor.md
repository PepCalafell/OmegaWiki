## Definition

NOTCH3 is one of four mammalian Notch receptors, expressed primarily in vascular smooth muscle, pericytes and certain stromal cell populations. It mediates juxtacrine signalling that controls vascular development and stromal cell identity, and germline NOTCH3 mutations cause CADASIL.

## Relevance to active research

- Driver of CAF–pericyte–endothelial crosstalk linked to NSCLC invasion and poor prognosis.
- Candidate selective Notch target distinct from tumour-intrinsic Notch1/4 signalling.
