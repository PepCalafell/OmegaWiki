## Definition

6-Hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA) is a hydroxylated dopamine analogue selectively taken up by catecholamine transporters (DAT, NET) on noradrenergic and dopaminergic neurons, where it generates ROS and triggers terminal degeneration. Systemic peripheral injection in rodents ablates peripheral tyrosine-hydroxylase⁺ sympathetic fibres while sparing CNS catecholaminergic neurons (which 6-OHDA cannot cross the BBB to reach).

## Relevance to active research

Used in [[papers/sympathetic-eosinophil-axis-orchestrates-psychological-stress]] to demonstrate that peripheral noradrenergic sympathetic neurons — and not the HPA axis — mediate stress-induced worsening of skin inflammation and eosinophil accumulation.
