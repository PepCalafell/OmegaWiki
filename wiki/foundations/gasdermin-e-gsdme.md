## Definition

Gasdermin E is a pore-forming gasdermin cleaved by caspase-3, converting apoptosis into secondary pyroptosis in some contexts.

## Intuition

The caspase-3-activated cousin of GSDMD; a contrast case here.

## Known limitations

Context-dependent function; roles vary by cell type, stimulus, and species (most evidence here is from mouse macrophages with a human THP-1 confirmation).

## Open problems

Whether the functions described here generalize to chronic, physiological inflammatory settings beyond bolus TNF / high-dose bacterial models.

## Relevance to active research

Efferoptosis is GSDME-independent (Gsdme-/- macrophages die normally) despite caspase-3 activation, distinguishing it from TAK1-inhibition death.
