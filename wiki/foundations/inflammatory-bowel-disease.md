## Definition

Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) comprises Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis: chronic, relapsing immune-mediated intestinal inflammations driven by aberrant immune responses to microbiota, with barrier disruption and Th17/Th1 dysregulation. AHR signalling, fuelled by microbial tryptophan-derived ligands, supports IL-22/ILC3/Treg-driven barrier homeostasis; CARD9 IBD mutations decrease microbial AHR-ligand production. Indigo naturalis (Qing-Dai) has shown clinical efficacy in UC.
