## Definition

ATM is a PIKK-family kinase activated by DNA double-strand breaks. It phosphorylates CHK2, p53 and H2AX to enforce G1/S and G2/M checkpoints.

## Intuition

Under cycling hypoxia, ROS activate the ATM–MRE11 complex during active DNA replication, degrading nascent DNA and promoting use of error-prone polymerases — a key mutagenic step in the hypoxic mutator phenotype.

## Relevance to active research

The ROS–ATM–MRE11 axis is one of the central mechanisms by which hypoxia generates point mutations during replication (Suvac et al. 2025).
