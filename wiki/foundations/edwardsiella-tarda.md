## Definition

Edwardsiella tarda is a Gram-negative enterobacterium of the family Hafniaceae, found
among gut commensals, that encodes a functional type III secretion system.

## Intuition

A gut Pseudomonadota strain whose native T3SS can be activated in the lab, used to show
that commensal (not just pathogen) machinery can inject effectors into human cells.

## Formal notation

Strain abbreviation Eta in the study; effectors labelled Eta_1..Eta_n.

## Key variants

Includes opportunistic-pathogen lineages; lifestyle is strain-dependent.

## Known limitations

Some commensal strains were untestable for native secretion due to antibiotic
resistance or transformation failure.

## Relevance to active research

Reproducibly and significantly injected 3 of 4 tested effectors into HeLa cells via its
native T3SS, demonstrating functional secretion in a gut commensal.
