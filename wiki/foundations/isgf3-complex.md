## Definition

Heterotrimeric transcription factor complex composed of phosphorylated STAT1, STAT2 and IRF9 that assembles downstream of type-I IFN receptor (IFNAR1/IFNAR2) activation. Binds ISRE (interferon-stimulated response element) DNA sequences (consensus AGTTTCNNTTTCNC/T) to drive transcription of interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs).

## Relevance to active research

- Canonical transducer of type-I IFN signaling; central to antiviral defense and ISG signatures across tissues.
- Maintains tonic low-level ISG expression under homeostatic conditions ("baseline JAK-STAT signaling"), shown by Fortelny et al. 2024: knockout of any ISGF3 member (STAT1, STAT2, IRF9) or of TYK2 abolishes the core-ISG module under homeostasis ([[jak-stat-signaling-maintains-homeostasis-cells]]).
- ISGF3-independent activities of STAT2 and IRF9 (e.g. STAT2-IRF9 heterodimer, IRF9 partnership with STAT3/STAT5) account for many baseline effects that cannot be assigned to canonical ISGF3.
