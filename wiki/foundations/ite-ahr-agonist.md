## Definition

ITE is a non-toxic, endogenous-like AHR agonist used preclinically to evaluate AHR activation for tolerance induction. ITE activates AHR with kinetics distinct from TCDD (less persistent) and reduces IL-12 and IL-6 production in DCs, expands Foxp3⁺ Tregs and IL-10⁺ Tr1 cells in EAE, and induces tolerogenic DC programmes.

## Relevance to active research

Prototypical "tolerogenic" AHR agonist in EAE, IBD, and Treg-induction studies reviewed in [[papers/aryl-hydrocarbon-receptor-rehabilitated-target-therapeutic]].
