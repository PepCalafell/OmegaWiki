## Definition

c-Maf is a bZIP transcription factor that cooperates with AHR to drive expression of IL-10, IL-21, and CD39 in Tr1 cells, and that contributes to multiple regulatory T-cell programmes. c-Maf is downstream of IL-27 / STAT3 signalling and is a defining transcription factor of the Tr1 lineage.
