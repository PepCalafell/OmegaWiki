## Definition

Cbf1 is a constitutively expressed basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factor in S. cerevisiae that binds the E-box CACGTG. It supports chromosome stability (centromere binding), methionine prototrophy, and the MET regulon. Cbf1 is the obligate paralog/competitor of Pho4 at shared E-box sites and is displaced when Pho4 nuclear concentration rises under low-phosphate conditions. Used in Khetan 2025 alongside Pho4 to dissect the molecular basis of paralog competition.

## Relevance to active research

In [[papers/multiple-overlapping-binding-sites-determine-transcription]], Cbf1 has 280 active 8-mers and ChIP-nexus peaks enriched for 3 consecutive overlapping sites; with each added overlapping site Cbf1 ChIP-nexus footprint grows 28→29→30 bp by 1 bp at a time — the molecular signature of an additional independent TF–DNA contact predicted by the overlapping-binding-sites model.
