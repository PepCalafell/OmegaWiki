## Definition
DMBA is a polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon carcinogen that induces HrasQ61L mutations in skin keratinocytes when applied topically. Combined with TPA promotion or used alone, it drives sequential papilloma–carcinoma progression and is the canonical model of cutaneous SCC.

## Relevance to active research
DMBA-induced models reveal convergence of high-plasticity and proliferative stem-cell states in Lgr6+ cells during early SCC, and provide a tractable system for lineage tracing of cancer-initiating clones in stratified epithelium.
