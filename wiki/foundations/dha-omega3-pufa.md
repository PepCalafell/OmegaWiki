## Definition

n-3 long-chain polyunsaturated fatty acids, primarily DHA (22:6) and EPA (20:5), obtained from marine sources or synthesized from α-linolenic acid. They integrate into immune-cell membranes, alter MHC-II / costimulatory molecule expression, and serve as precursors of specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs: resolvins, protectins, maresins).

## Intuition

Dietary "anti-inflammatory" PUFAs — membrane-integrated regulators with effects on TCR / BCR / FcεRI proximal signaling and on lipid mediator class switching.

## Key variants

- DHA (CD4 suppression dominant)
- EPA (SPM precursor dominant; antiallergic)
- ALA (plant-derived n-3 precursor; weak conversion to EPA/DHA in humans)

## Known limitations

- Mixed clinical-trial outcomes despite preclinical promise.

## Open problems

- Whether n-3:n-6 ratio biomarkers predict ICI response or autoimmune-disease activity.

## Relevance to active research

Allergy, dietary immunology, cardiovascular trials (REDUCE-IT), inflammation resolution.
