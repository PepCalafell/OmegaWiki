## Definition
Barrett's oesophagus is the replacement of the normal stratified squamous oesophageal epithelium by intestinal-type columnar epithelium at the gastro-oesophageal junction, typically secondary to chronic gastro-oesophageal reflux. It is the canonical precancerous lesion for oesophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC).

## Relevance to active research
A unique human setting in which longitudinal endoscopic sampling of precancer-to-cancer transition is routine. Recent single-cell and deep-genomic studies in Barrett's tissue revealed ecDNA harbouring ERBB2/MYC/KRAS as a strong predictor of EAC progression.
