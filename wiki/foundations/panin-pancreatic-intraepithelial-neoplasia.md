## Definition
PanIN is a microscopic intraductal proliferative lesion graded 1-3 that constitutes the canonical precursor to pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Sporadic PanIN-like lesions are now detectable in single-cell atlases of healthy donor pancreases, complicating their interpretation as obligate precancers.

## Relevance to active research
KrasLSL-G12D-driven GEMMs reproducibly develop PanINs, and these are the principal experimental setting in which milestone-reporter lineage tracing, single-cell omics, and pre-CAF crosstalk are dissected during early pancreatic tumorigenesis.
